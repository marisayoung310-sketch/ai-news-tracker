# Darktable

darktable is an open source photography workflow application and raw developer. A virtual lighttable and darkroom for photographers. It manages your digital negatives in a database, lets you view them through a zoomable lighttable and enables you to develop raw images and enhance them. Have a look at our current features and how to install it on your system. And if you're new to darktable, the FAQ will answer many of your questions.

Darktable 是一款开源的摄影工作流应用及 RAW 格式图像处理软件。它为摄影师提供了一个虚拟的“灯箱”和“暗房”。它通过数据库管理你的数字底片，让你能够通过可缩放的灯箱查看照片，并支持 RAW 格式图像的开发与增强。你可以查看我们当前的功能介绍以及如何在你的系统上进行安装。如果你是 Darktable 的新用户，常见问题解答（FAQ）将为你解答许多疑惑。

**by photographers**
darktable is created for photographers, by photographers. Having developers that are also avid photographers as part of the target audience is good for understanding the real world problems, challenges, and workflows.

**由摄影师打造**
Darktable 是由摄影师为摄影师所创造的。开发人员本身也是热衷于摄影的用户，这有助于他们深入理解现实世界中的问题、挑战以及工作流程。

**powerful**
darktable edits your images non-destructively all the way through its pipeline. Your original image is never modified! Professional color management features ensure fidelity all the way to print and GPU accelerated processing means you can speed up your workflow using your video card.

**功能强大**
Darktable 在整个处理流程中对图像进行非破坏性编辑。你的原始图像永远不会被修改！专业的色彩管理功能确保了从编辑到打印的全程保真度，而 GPU 加速处理则意味着你可以利用显卡来提升工作效率。

**community**
You do not need to write code to be involved in the darktable community! You can help other users by providing your input on the mailing list or the forum. If you'd like to show others what darktable is capable of, contribute to the Flickr page. If you can write code, check out the source code!

**社区**
你无需编写代码也能参与到 Darktable 社区中！你可以通过在邮件列表或论坛上提供建议来帮助其他用户。如果你想向他人展示 Darktable 的强大功能，欢迎向我们的 Flickr 页面投稿。如果你擅长编程，欢迎查看我们的源代码！
