### A Quick Dyson Vacuum Rundown / 戴森吸尘器快速概览

Dyson's naming convention is fun, and the numbers usually represent which models are more powerful or offer better accessories, but not always. Most of its vacuums have the V name, which, to my best guess, simply stands for “vacuum.” The numbers aren't always in order of release and usually reflect a vacuum's power level and capabilities. The V10 and V8 have the same power level but different heads, for example, while the V16 is more powerful than the V15. But not all vacuums are named the same; the Gen5Detect has a different name entirely, and falls in between the V15 and V16 when it comes to suction power and price tag.

戴森的命名规则很有趣，数字通常代表机型的功率大小或配件的优劣，但并非总是如此。大多数吸尘器都带有“V”字头，我猜测这仅仅代表“Vacuum”（吸尘器）。数字并不总是按发布顺序排列，通常反映了吸尘器的功率水平和功能。例如，V10 和 V8 的功率水平相同，但吸头不同；而 V16 的功率则比 V15 更强。并非所有吸尘器的命名方式都一样；Gen5Detect 的命名完全不同，其吸力和价格都介于 V15 和 V16 之间。

As the models increase in number (both in the name and in the price tag), the vacuum usually gains more accessories or cleaner heads, more air watts of suction power, and sometimes the option to add the Submarine wet cleaner head on for an additional price, or future compatibility with Dyson's Auto-empty Dok station that's coming out soon.

这让你能很直观地看到每款机型的配置。随着型号数字（名称和价格）的增加，吸尘器通常会配备更多的配件或吸头，拥有更高的吸力（AW），有时还可以选择额外付费购买 Submarine 湿拖头，或者兼容即将推出的戴森自动清空底座。

The Digital Motorbar and All Floor Cones cleaner heads are both compatible with soft and hard floors, which is why sometimes it's the only head included. More expensive Dyson models will also include the Fluffy Optic cleaner head, which is designed specifically for hard floors and has an illuminated light to help you spot dust and debris. The All Floor Cones cleaner head includes a light too, so you're able to get that feature on both the V16 and V10 without switching heads.

Digital Motorbar 和 All Floor Cones 吸头均适用于软硬地面，这就是为什么有时它们是唯一随附的吸头。更昂贵的戴森机型还会包含 Fluffy Optic 吸头，它是专为硬质地面设计的，并配有照明灯，可以帮你发现灰尘和碎屑。All Floor Cones 吸头也带有照明功能，因此你在 V16 和 V10 上无需更换吸头即可获得该功能。
