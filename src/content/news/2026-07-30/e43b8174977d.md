# Festival Must-Haves, Field-Tested at Lost Lands and Electric Forest
# 音乐节必备装备：Lost Lands 与 Electric Forest 实地测评

The best festival gear, of course, depends on which festival you’re attending, the weather, your sleeping arrangements, and a ton of other factors. But as a longtime festival veteran, I’ve field-tested enough gear (and made enough mistakes) to have solid recommendations for preventing blisters, dehydration, a dead phone, and other common pitfalls in most situations. You'll find my top picks below.

### Best Electrolytes: Liquid I.V. Hydration Multiplier
### 最佳电解质：Liquid I.V. Hydration Multiplier

These electrolytes are my favorite in general, and I especially like them for festivals because the ingredient concentrations are high enough that they actually help with efficient hydration when you're hot and sweaty. And the flavors are tasty, too, so I can pass them out to my crew and know they’ll actually drink them. Hydration is of utmost importance when it comes to preventing hangovers and “wook flu,” and this mixture can help. Plus, the packets don’t take up much space in your bag or pocket.

这些电解质是我最喜欢的，尤其适合在音乐节使用，因为它们的成分浓度很高，在炎热出汗时确实能帮助高效补水。而且口味很好，我可以分发给同伴，并确信他们真的会喝下去。对于预防宿醉和“音乐节流感”（wook flu）来说，补水至关重要，而这种冲剂很有帮助。此外，小包装不占包包或口袋空间。

### Best Outdoor Blanket: Rumpl Backcountry Puffy Blanket
### 最佳户外毯：Rumpl Backcountry Puffy Blanket

I have yet to try a blanket from Rumpl that I dislike. They pack up and unfurl easily, and most of them have enough space for seating multiple people. They also have little hooks to convert the blanket into a cape, which is nice if it gets chilly at camp but you aren’t ready to go to bed yet. They’re also easy to clean, whether they’re covered in dust or grass blades or spilled White Claw. For just one or two people, this smaller blanket is perfect. It’s thin, so it’s best for summer or as an added layer.

我还没试过哪款 Rumpl 的毯子是不喜欢的。它们收纳和展开都很方便，大多数毯子都有足够的空间供多人坐下。它们还有小挂钩，可以将毯子变成斗篷，如果营地变冷但你还没准备睡觉，这非常实用。它们也很容易清洁，无论是沾满灰尘、草屑还是洒了酒，都能轻松打理。对于一两个人来说，这款小毯子非常完美。它很薄，所以最适合夏天使用，或者作为额外的保暖层。

### My Favorite Power Bank: Anker 737 Power Bank
### 我最喜欢的充电宝：Anker 737 Power Bank

I’m not sure what it is about festivals that seems to make my phone die twice as fast, but it’s practically a law of the universe at this point. (It’s likely that the lack of cell service means my phone is constantly searching for a signal.) Or maybe it's just overheating in my backpack. In any case, a power-bank phone charger with a large capacity is really handy. This Anker power bank isn’t the slimmest or the lightest, but it charges up quickly and can rapidly top off multiple devices per charge. Added bonus: You won’t be overpaying for the luxury of a charging locker.

我不知道为什么在音乐节上手机掉电速度总是快两倍，但这几乎成了宇宙定律。（很可能是因为缺乏信号，导致手机一直在搜索信号。）又或者是因为它在背包里过热了。无论如何，一个大容量的充电宝非常实用。这款 Anker 充电宝虽然不是最轻薄的，但充电速度很快，且单次充电能为多台设备快速补电。额外的好处是：你不需要花冤枉钱去租昂贵的充电柜。

### A Dedicated Friend Finder: Totem Labs Compass
### 专属寻友神器：Totem Labs Compass

The Totem Compass was designed with festivals in mind. It uses satellites and its own proprietary mesh network to let you find your friends without any cell service or Wi-Fi. No more manually time-stamping your text messages or waving your phone around in the air trying to get a signal. Once these trackers are set up, you'll be able to navigate to your friend group with ease, just by looking down and walking toward their corresponding light color.

Totem Compass 是专为音乐节设计的。它利用卫星和专有的网状网络，让你在没有手机信号或 Wi-Fi 的情况下也能找到朋友。再也不用手动给短信打时间戳，也不用在空中挥舞手机试图寻找信号了。一旦设置好这些追踪器，你只需低头查看并朝着对应颜色的灯光走去，就能轻松找到你的朋友圈。

### My Go-To Earplugs: Loop Experience 2
### 我的首选耳塞：Loop Experience 2

The Loop Experience 2 are my favorite earplugs overall. They’re part of my daily carry, which is made easier by the convenient case. They protect your hearing without muffling the sound, and they look visually striking which also helps my friends know I’m wearing them so they don't attempt talking to me on the dance floor. I like how the case flips open, so I'm not jostling to unscrew a metal case in a crowd.

Loop Experience 2 是我最喜欢的耳塞。它们是我日常携带的物品，便捷的收纳盒让携带变得更简单。它们在保护听力的同时不会让声音变得沉闷，而且外观醒目，这也能让朋友们知道我戴着耳塞，从而不会在舞池里试图跟我说话。我喜欢这种翻盖式的盒子，这样我就不必在人群中费力地拧开金属盒了。

### A Lighted Mirror: Fancii Abigail
### 带灯化妆镜：Fancii Abigail

If you aren’t staying in a hotel (or if you are but your room is cramped), a lighted makeup mirror is invaluable for getting ready. I take the Fancii Abigail with me to every festival. Having a base station helps a lot with elaborate makeup looks or braided hairstyles, and the light is bright enough that you'll be able to see what you're doing, even if you're getting ready in a tent. It's large enough to give you the full picture.

如果你不住酒店（或者即使住酒店但房间很挤），一个带灯的化妆镜对于准备妆容来说是无价之宝。我每次去音乐节都会带上 Fancii Abigail。底座设计对化精致妆容或编发非常有帮助，而且灯光足够明亮，即使在帐篷里准备，你也能看清自己在做什么。它的尺寸足够大，能让你看清整体效果。
