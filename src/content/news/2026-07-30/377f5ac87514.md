# Superlogical

We are building the multiplexer for all work. local development. remote access. coding agents. background jobs. production applications. live debugging. sandboxes. shared terminals. incident response. humans and machines. operational history. multiplayer work. all work.
我们正在为所有工作构建一个多路复用器（Multiplexer）。涵盖本地开发、远程访问、编码代理、后台任务、生产应用、实时调试、沙盒环境、共享终端、事故响应、人机协作、操作历史、多人协作等所有工作。

Building and operating software today spans local machines, remote hosts, sandboxes, services, and production systems. It has many modes of operation: interactively with a human developer, automatically through CI and background processes, and increasingly through agents working in parallel. This work is all related, yet today's tools divide it into separate systems. Interactive tools assume a person at an interface. Automatic work disappears into jobs and logs. And as the work moves to production it lives behind separate systems and controls.
如今，软件的构建与运维跨越了本地机器、远程主机、沙盒、服务和生产系统。它拥有多种操作模式：与人类开发者交互、通过 CI 和后台进程自动运行，以及日益增多的并行工作的 AI 代理。这些工作息息相关，但现有的工具却将其割裂在不同的系统中。交互式工具假设有人在界面前操作；自动化工作则消失在任务和日志中；而当工作进入生产环境时，又被隔离在独立的系统和控制之下。

AI makes this fragmentation more visible and costly, but it did not create it. System administration, continuous integration, remote development and collaboration have strained the same boundaries for decades. We believe the missing layer is a durable session around the work itself: one that can span applications and environments, provide relevant context by default, expose structured data and actions, preserve history, and be driven by software while remaining visible and controllable by people.
AI 让这种碎片化变得更加明显且代价高昂，但它并非始作俑者。系统管理、持续集成、远程开发和协作几十年来一直受困于同样的边界。我们认为，缺失的一层是围绕工作本身构建的“持久会话”：它能够跨越应用程序和环境，默认提供相关上下文，暴露结构化数据和操作，保留历史记录，并能由软件驱动，同时保持对人类可见且可控。

### What we're building
### 我们正在构建什么

This is our plan to build a multiplexer for all work:
这是我们构建全能工作多路复用器的计划：

1. Build an incredible multiplexer.
2. Make everything in it composable.
3. Make it safe and operable in production.
1. 构建一个卓越的多路复用器。
2. 让其中的一切皆可组合。
3. 确保其在生产环境中的安全与可操作性。

A multiplexer brings multiple independent streams together through a common interface. For us, that means interactive work, automatic work, and production work would share one well-crafted underlying system instead of living in separate tools. We'll begin with a terminal multiplexer. It keeps multiple terminal blocks organized inside a long-lived session, so you can close the application, reconnect from another device, and pick up exactly where you left off.
多路复用器通过统一的接口将多个独立的流汇聚在一起。对我们而言，这意味着交互式工作、自动化工作和生产工作将共享一个精心设计的底层系统，而不是分散在不同的工具中。我们将从终端多路复用器开始。它将多个终端区块组织在一个长效会话中，因此你可以关闭应用程序，从另一台设备重新连接，并从上次中断的地方继续工作。

If you're already familiar with terminal multiplexers, you'll feel right at home, but we're bringing a more modern touch. Sessions can be accessed through the web and native macOS/iOS applications, and sharing a live session with other people is built in from the start. We're also addressing the most common papercuts of existing tools, such as making scrollback, selection, and scrolling all work natively.
如果你已经熟悉终端多路复用器，你会感到非常亲切，但我们带来了更现代的体验。会话可以通过 Web 和原生 macOS/iOS 应用程序访问，并且从一开始就内置了与他人共享实时会话的功能。我们还解决了现有工具中最常见的“小毛病”，例如让回滚、选择和滚动操作都能原生顺畅地运行。

A terminal multiplexer may sound like a narrow place to start a company. Our vision is much larger, but terminals connect developers, agents, tools, and infrastructure so it is the right foundation for everything that follows. We will build a high-quality terminal multiplexer that remains excellent at that job, even as it grows to support the second and third parts of the plan. We'll have more to say about those later.
终端多路复用器听起来可能是一个狭窄的创业切入点。我们的愿景远不止于此，但终端连接着开发者、代理、工具和基础设施，因此它是后续一切工作的坚实基础。我们将构建一个高质量的终端多路复用器，即使在扩展以支持计划的第二和第三部分时，它依然会保持卓越的性能。关于后续计划，我们稍后再详述。
