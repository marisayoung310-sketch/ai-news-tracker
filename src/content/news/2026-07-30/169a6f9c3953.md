# Mark Zuckerberg is planning a big push into personal AI agents
# 马克·扎克伯格计划在个人 AI 智能体领域发力

Meta is all-in on AI, and sometime soon, the company is going to make a big push into personal AI agents that can do things on your behalf.
Meta 正全力押注人工智能，不久之后，该公司将大力推动个人 AI 智能体的发展，这些智能体将能够代表用户执行各种任务。

On Wednesday’s Q2 2026 earnings call, CEO Mark Zuckerberg previewed a high-level vision of how the company is thinking about personal agents and what it will do to make them viable for users — and how it will convince less technical people to give them a shot:
在周三举行的 2026 年第二季度财报电话会议上，首席执行官马克·扎克伯格勾勒了公司对个人智能体的宏观愿景，阐述了如何使其对用户具有实用价值，以及如何说服非技术背景的用户尝试使用：

"Soon we will have agents that can work 24/7 on your behalf to help you achieve your goals and improve your life, your health, your relationships, your finances, whatever you want. The first domain that agents have really taken off in is coding. But engineers are more technical and willing to spend time making those agents work, so to build great personal agents, this needs to be a great consumer product that just works out of the box and is easy enough for billions of people to adopt and use."
“不久之后，我们将拥有能够全天候为您工作的智能体，帮助您实现目标，改善您的生活、健康、人际关系、财务状况，无论您想要什么。智能体真正起飞的第一个领域是编程。但工程师们更具技术背景，也更愿意花时间去调试这些智能体；因此，要打造出色的个人智能体，它必须是一款开箱即用、简单易用，且足以让数十亿人采纳和使用的优秀消费级产品。”

Zuckerberg seems to be trying to differentiate his vision from that of companies like Anthropic and OpenAI, which have invested heavily in agents focused on coding and enterprise work. Google has also made a push into personal AI agents with its Gemini Spark tool, which I found to be very good but to an almost creepy degree, and it’s unclear right now how Meta’s potential agents might compare.
扎克伯格似乎正试图将其愿景与 Anthropic 和 OpenAI 等公司区分开来，后者在专注于编程和企业工作的智能体上投入了巨资。谷歌也通过其 Gemini Spark 工具进军个人 AI 智能体领域，我发现该工具非常好用，甚至达到了令人毛骨悚然的程度。目前尚不清楚 Meta 未来的智能体将如何与之竞争。

However, Meta could have an uphill battle in personal agents. Its AI efforts still lag behind the bigger AI labs. Unlike Google or Microsoft, it doesn’t have ecosystem access to people’s email or documents in the way Google or Microsoft do. And Meta is dealing with a different creep problem right now with smart glasses, and general distrust toward Meta might make people unwilling to use its agents.
然而，Meta 在个人智能体领域可能面临一场硬仗。其 AI 研发进度仍落后于大型 AI 实验室。与谷歌或微软不同，Meta 无法像它们那样通过生态系统获取用户的电子邮件或文档。此外，Meta 目前正因智能眼镜面临隐私方面的质疑，公众对 Meta 的普遍不信任感可能会导致人们不愿使用其智能体。

The company will have more to share about its personal agents “soon,” Zuckerberg said. Earlier in the call, he said that personal agents will be “the foundation for our next wave of products and revenue lines in the months and years ahead.”
扎克伯格表示，公司“很快”会分享更多关于个人智能体的消息。在电话会议早些时候，他提到个人智能体将成为“未来数月乃至数年内我们下一波产品和收入来源的基础”。

Zuckerberg also highlighted Meta’s business agents that are available on WhatsApp and Messenger, and he said that more than 1 million businesses are using them every week. The business agents are being rolled out to Instagram as well, Zuckerberg said.
扎克伯格还重点介绍了 Meta 在 WhatsApp 和 Messenger 上提供的商业智能体，并表示每周有超过 100 万家企业在使用它们。扎克伯格称，这些商业智能体也正在向 Instagram 推广。

Meta’s big move into agents follows the launch of its Muse Spark AI model last quarter, which it recently updated with a 1.1 update that offers better coding capabilities. The company has restructured itself to focus heavily on AI, reportedly moving 7,000 staffers to work on new AI initiatives, though it also laid off about 8,000 people in May. The company is also investing heavily in compute, noting in today’s earnings that it expects to spend $130 to $145 billion in capital expenditures in 2026. Yesterday, it announced that it’s building a 1 gigawatt data center campus in partnership with BlackRock.
Meta 在智能体领域的重大举措紧随其上季度推出的 Muse Spark AI 模型之后，该模型最近更新至 1.1 版本，提供了更强的编程能力。公司已进行重组以专注于 AI，据报道已调动 7,000 名员工从事新的 AI 项目，尽管该公司在 5 月份也裁减了约 8,000 名员工。公司还在计算领域投入巨资，在今天的财报中指出，预计 2026 年的资本支出将达到 1,300 亿至 1,450 亿美元。昨天，该公司宣布将与贝莱德（BlackRock）合作建设一个 1 吉瓦（GW）的数据中心园区。

During the Q2 2026 earnings call, Zuckerberg also said that Instagram now has more than 2 billion daily active users.
在 2026 年第二季度财报电话会议上，扎克伯格还透露，Instagram 目前的日活跃用户已超过 20 亿。
