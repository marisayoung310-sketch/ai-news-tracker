### We’re launching Lyria 3.5 in Google Flow Music, with advances across musicality, lyrics, vocals, and creative control

**We’re launching Lyria 3.5 in Google Flow Music, with advances across musicality, lyrics, vocals, and creative control. Jul 29, 2026 Our newest music generation model, Lyria 3.5, delivers significant advancements across musicality, lyrics, and vocal quality, empowering you to craft richer tracks. We’re rolling it out today in Google Flow Music, where we want to help you create songs you love, with creative control.**

我们正在 Google Flow Music 中推出 Lyria 3.5，在音乐性、歌词、人声和创作控制方面均有显著提升。2026 年 7 月 29 日，我们最新的音乐生成模型 Lyria 3.5 在音乐性、歌词和人声质量方面带来了重大进步，助您创作出更丰富的曲目。我们于今日在 Google Flow Music 中正式上线该模型，旨在帮助您通过更强的创作控制力，打造出您心仪的歌曲。

**Here's what’s new:**

以下是本次更新的内容：

**Improved musicality: Create richer, more complex melodic structures that sound more natural.**

音乐性提升：创作出更丰富、更复杂且听感更自然的旋律结构。

**Enhanced lyrics: Generate higher quality lyrics with improved prompt adherence and structural awareness.**

歌词增强：生成更高质量的歌词，并提升了对提示词的遵循能力和结构感知力。

**Improved vocals: Bring more expression and emotion to your songs with more realistic and emotionally nuanced vocals, plus improved pronunciation.**

人声优化：通过更逼真、更具情感细腻度的人声，以及更准确的发音，为您的歌曲注入更多的表现力和情感。

**Creative control: More easily control the tempo and duration of your outputs.**

创作控制：更轻松地控制输出内容的节奏和时长。

**Try it today in Flow Music.**

立即在 Flow Music 中体验吧。
