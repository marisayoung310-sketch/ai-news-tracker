# Gaming and IRL worlds collide in Jumanji: Open World trailer
# 游戏与现实世界碰撞：《勇敢者游戏：开放世界》预告片发布

You’d think all the trailers for upcoming major films would have been released during San Diego Comic-Con last weekend. But Sony Pictures decided to wait until today to drop the trailer for *Jumanji: Open World*, director Jake Kasdan’s sequel to his 2019 hit, *Jumanji: The Next Level*. (Some spoilers for prior films in the franchise below but no major reveals.)

你可能以为所有即将上映的大片预告片都会在上周末的圣地亚哥国际动漫展（SDCC）期间发布。但索尼影业决定等到今天才放出《勇敢者游戏：开放世界》（Jumanji: Open World）的预告片，这是导演杰克·卡斯丹（Jake Kasdan）继2019年热门影片《勇敢者游戏2：再战巅峰》（Jumanji: The Next Level）之后的续作。（下文包含该系列前作的部分剧透，但无重大情节泄露。）

The original *Jumanji* film, released in 1995 and starring the late Robin Williams, was based on a 1981 children’s book by Chris Van Allsburg. Critics were kinda meh on the movie, but audiences loved it, and it has since become something of a classic. The 2005 film *Zathura* was a standalone spinoff based on another van Allsburg book. While it didn’t do nearly as well at the box office, it has held up well over the last 20 years and has its own small cadre of fans. There was also a forgettable animated TV series.

1995年上映、由已故影星罗宾·威廉姆斯（Robin Williams）主演的原版《勇敢者游戏》改编自克里斯·范·奥尔斯伯格（Chris Van Allsburg）1981年的同名儿童读物。当时影评人对该片的评价平平，但观众却非常喜爱，它也因此成为了一部经典之作。2005年的电影《勇敢者游戏2：太空飞行棋》（Zathura）是基于范·奥尔斯伯格另一部作品的独立衍生片。虽然其票房表现远不如前作，但在过去20年里经受住了时间的考验，并拥有了一小批忠实粉丝。此外，还有一个容易被遗忘的动画剧集。

Kasdan gave the franchise a welcome major overhaul with the release of 2017’s *Jumanji: Welcome to the Jungle*, updating the original board game into a vintage video game and giving us a hugely entertaining action-adventure. There were plenty of clever nods to gaming tropes and terrific comic performances by the cast. I mean, who could forget Bethany’s (Madison Iseman) delight upon being able to pee standing up as her male avatar, Professor Sheldon “Smolder” Oberon (Jack Black)? Or the dance fighting and Ruby Roundhouse’s (Karen Gillan)/Martha’s (Morgan Turner) hilariously inept attempts at flirting?

卡斯丹在2017年推出的《勇敢者游戏：决战丛林》（Jumanji: Welcome to the Jungle）中对该系列进行了令人欣喜的重大革新，将原版的棋盘游戏更新为复古电子游戏，为我们带来了一部极具娱乐性的动作冒险片。片中充满了对游戏梗的巧妙致敬，演员们的喜剧表演也十分精彩。谁能忘记贝瑟尼（麦迪逊·伊瑟曼饰）在化身为男性角色谢尔顿·奥伯伦教授（杰克·布莱克饰）后，发现自己能站着小便时的那种喜悦？又或是片中的舞蹈格斗，以及露比·圆屋（凯伦·吉兰饰）与玛莎（摩根·特纳饰）那些笨拙又搞笑的调情尝试？

Small wonder *Welcome to the Jungle* racked up over $960 million at the global box office and spawned the equally successful 2019 sequel *Jumanji: The Next Level*. A third film was always in the cards: *The Next Level* featured a tantalizing mid-credits scene in which a heater repairman (Lamorne Morris) visits Spencer’s house, sees the broken video game console, and inadvertently triggers the game. The result: a herd of ostriches suddenly shows up outside Nora’s (Bebe Neuwirth) diner—shades of the original film, in which the in-game jungle invaded the real world until the players finished.

难怪《决战丛林》在全球斩获了超过9.6亿美元的票房，并催生了同样成功的2019年续集《再战巅峰》。第三部电影的制作早已在计划之中：《再战巅峰》片尾彩蛋中，一名暖气修理工（拉莫恩·莫里斯饰）来到斯宾塞家，看到了那台坏掉的游戏机并无意中启动了游戏。结果：一群鸵鸟突然出现在诺拉（碧碧·诺维尔饰）的餐馆外——这让人联想到原版电影，当时游戏中的丛林入侵了现实世界，直到玩家通关游戏才停止。

The COVID-19 pandemic paused the third film’s development, but things picked up again in 2024, and here we are. We don’t know many details about *Open World*, although star Dwayne Johnson—who played in-game avatar Dr. Xander “Smolder” Bravestone—let slip in 2019 that *The Next Level*‘s in-game villain, Jurgen the Brutal (罗里·麦肯饰), was actually the avatar of another unknown real-world player. In the new trailer, the avatars have also entered the real world, but they are stuck in demo mode. It’s only a matter of time before everyone gets sucked back into the game to set things right, with Spencer’s mom, Janice (Marin Hinkle), taking on Bravestone’s avatar.

新冠疫情曾导致第三部电影的开发停滞，但项目在2024年重新启动，现在我们终于等到了。关于《开放世界》的细节我们知之甚少，不过主演道恩·强森（饰演游戏角色布雷夫斯通博士）曾在2019年透露，《再战巅峰》中的反派“残暴者尤尔根”（罗里·麦肯饰）实际上是另一位现实世界玩家的化身。在新的预告片中，游戏角色也进入了现实世界，但他们被困在了“演示模式”中。大家被吸回游戏以纠正错误只是时间问题，而斯宾塞的母亲珍妮丝（马琳·辛科饰）将接手布雷夫斯通的化身。

The entire main cast is returning: Johnson, Black, Gillan, Neuwirth (who also appeared in the 1995 film), Turner, Hinkle, and Iseman; Kevin Hart as Franklin “Mouse” Finbar; Nick Jonas as Jefferson “Seaplane” McDonough; Awkwafina as Ming Flatfoot; Alex Wolff as Spencer Gilpin; Ser’Darius Blain as Fridge Johnson; Rhys Darby as Nigel Billingsley; and Danny DeVito as Spencer’s grandfather, Eddie. In addition, Dan Hildebrand plays Donatas, and Jack Jewkes plays Tyson. Burn Gorman, Nasim Pedrad, and Brittany O’Grady will also appear in as-yet-undisclosed roles.

主要演员阵容悉数回归：强森、布莱克、吉兰、诺维尔（她也出演了1995年的原版电影）、特纳、辛科和伊瑟曼；凯文·哈特饰演“鼠标”芬巴；尼克·乔纳斯饰演“水上飞机”麦克多诺；奥卡菲娜饰演明·平足；亚历克斯·沃尔夫饰演斯宾塞；瑟达留斯·布兰饰演弗里奇；瑞斯·达比饰演奈杰尔；丹尼·德维托饰演斯宾塞的祖父埃迪。此外，丹·希尔德布兰饰演多纳塔斯，杰克·朱克斯饰演泰森。伯恩·戈曼、纳西姆·帕杜雷德和布兰妮·奥格雷迪也将出演尚未公开的角色。

*Jumanji: Open World* hits theaters on December 25, 2026.

《勇敢者游戏：开放世界》将于2026年12月25日登陆院线。
