# French musician Kavinsky found dead
# 法国音乐人 Kavinsky 去世

A key figure of the French touch house scene, Kavinsky wrote "Nightcall", the track made famous by the film 'Drive', performed at the Paris 2024 Olympic closing ceremony alongside Angèle and Phoenix. French DJ Kavinsky has been found dead at his Paris home, according to local authorities. The body of the 50-year-old, whose real name was Vincent Belorgey, was discovered on Tuesday evening.

作为法国浩室音乐（French touch）场景的关键人物，Kavinsky 创作了因电影《亡命驾驶》（Drive）而闻名的曲目《Nightcall》，并曾与 Angèle 和 Phoenix 一同在巴黎 2024 奥运会闭幕式上表演。据当地有关部门消息，法国 DJ Kavinsky 被发现死于其巴黎家中。这位现年 50 岁的音乐人（原名 Vincent Belorgey）的遗体于周二晚间被发现。

Kavinsky was a leading figure of the house music movement, 'French touch', alongside Daft Punk, Justice, Cassius and DJ Mehdi. The Paris prosecutor's office said that "an investigation to establish the causes of death" had been opened, noting that no suspect was found at the scene on Tuesday evening.

Kavinsky 与 Daft Punk、Justice、Cassius 和 DJ Mehdi 等人并列，是“法国浩室”（French touch）音乐运动的领军人物。巴黎检察官办公室表示，已针对其死因展开调查，并指出周二晚间在现场并未发现任何嫌疑人。

"France has lost one of its most distinctive voices", French Culture Minister Catherine Pégard said in a message on social media network X. "Both danceable and nostalgic, his music will go on crossing generations and borders."

法国文化部长 Catherine Pégard 在社交媒体 X 上发文称：“法国失去了一位最具辨识度的声音。他的音乐既适合舞动又充满怀旧感，将继续跨越世代与国界流传下去。”

One of his most popular tracks was Nightcall, the dark, slow-tempo synthwave hit included on the soundtrack of Drive (2011), Nicolas Winding Refn's Palme d'or winning neon-noir thriller starring Ryan Gosling. The track enjoyed a new lease of life during the Paris 2024 Olympic Games.

他最受欢迎的作品之一是《Nightcall》，这首黑暗且节奏缓慢的合成器流行（synthwave）金曲被收录在尼古拉斯·温丁·雷弗恩执导、瑞恩·高斯林主演的戛纳金棕榈获奖电影《亡命驾驶》（2011）原声带中。这首曲目在巴黎 2024 奥运会期间焕发了新的生命力。

The DJ performed at the Stade de France alongside Belgian singer Angèle and pop-rock band Phoenix, who reworked Nightcall for the occasion during the Olympic closing ceremony.

在奥运会闭幕式上，这位 DJ 与比利时歌手 Angèle 以及流行摇滚乐队 Phoenix 在法兰西体育场同台演出，后者为此次盛会重新编排了《Nightcall》。

A self-taught pianist born in Seine-Saint-Denis, Kavinsky launched his career in the early 2000s, opening in particular for Daft Punk and sharing the stage with other emerging names on the electronic scene, such as SebastiAn. Kavinsky would have turned 51 on 31 July.

Kavinsky 出生于塞纳-圣但尼省，是一位自学成才的钢琴家。他在 21 世纪初开启了自己的职业生涯，曾为 Daft Punk 担任开场嘉宾，并与 SebastiAn 等电子音乐界的新星同台演出。Kavinsky 本将于 7 月 31 日迎来 51 岁生日。
