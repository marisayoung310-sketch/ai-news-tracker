### Instant Lap Pool / 即时游泳道
**iGarden Swim Jet X Pro**

What, you’re still swimming in a still-water pool? Get with the ’20s and add an artificial wave machine that uses jets of water to create a strong current that you can swim against.
什么，你还在静水泳池里游泳吗？跟上 20 年代的潮流，加装一台人工造浪机吧！它利用喷水流创造出强劲的水流，让你能够逆流而游。

iGarden’s Swim Jet X (also available in a few cheaper, lower-power versions) is a massive pump that clips onto the edge of your pool deck, cycling up to 1,100 gallons of water per minute through its 31-pound chassis, creating its powerful stream. At higher settings, this current is downright extreme, able to blast an unprepared swimmer halfway across the pool. The result is a very effective way to exercise without swimming long laps in a traditional lap pool. And if the kids just want to splash around a bit for fun, you can lower the Swim Jet’s intensity level to create a lighter, surface-level disturbance.
iGarden 的 Swim Jet X（也有几款更便宜、功率更低的版本）是一个巨大的泵，可以夹在泳池甲板边缘。它每分钟循环高达 1,100 加仑的水，通过其 31 磅重的机身产生强劲水流。在高档位下，这种水流非常强劲，足以将毫无准备的游泳者冲到泳池另一端。这是一种非常有效的锻炼方式，无需在传统的长泳道中来回游动。如果孩子们只是想玩水，你也可以调低 Swim Jet 的强度，制造出更轻柔的表面波动。

The Swim Jet isn’t perfect, though. The workouts are challenging and also adjustable, thanks to the machine’s six power modes. Even so, swimming in place may not provide quite as vigorous an exercise session as traditional laps. Battery life is another consideration: You’ll get 10 hours of running time on the slowest, lowest setting, but its efficiency plummets as you ratchet up the output. On the higher power modes, the system is good for about two hours of wave-blasting operation.
不过，Swim Jet 并非完美。得益于机器的六种功率模式，锻炼既具有挑战性又可调节。即便如此，原地游泳可能无法提供像传统长距离游泳那样剧烈的锻炼效果。电池续航是另一个需要考虑的因素：在最慢、最低档位下，你可以获得 10 小时的运行时间，但随着输出功率的增加，效率会急剧下降。在高功率模式下，该系统大约能持续运行两小时的造浪作业。

It’s also very expensive, with the top-tier version running $4,320 at some sellers. That’s astronomical in price, but what pool product isn’t? And compared to the cost of a membership at a gym that has a pool, it might make more sense than it seems.
它也非常昂贵，顶级版本在某些卖家处售价高达 4,320 美元。这个价格确实高得离谱，但哪种泳池产品不贵呢？与拥有泳池的健身房会员费相比，它可能比看起来更划算。

### The Best Pool Alarm / 最佳泳池警报器
**Lifebuoy Bcone Floating Pool Alarm**

If you (or your neighbors) have small children, investing in a pool alarm may very well be an invaluable insurance policy. Lifebuoy’s Bcone is the blue-chip alarm option on the market right now. It’s a bright red, double-ended cone that floats in your pool (or hot tub) and sounds a siren if something ends up in the water that isn’t supposed to be there.
如果你（或你的邻居）有小孩，投资一个泳池警报器可能是一份无价的保险。Lifebuoy 的 Bcone 是目前市场上最顶级的警报器选择。它是一个亮红色的双头锥体，漂浮在你的泳池（或热水浴缸）中。如果有什么不该出现在水里的东西落入水中，它就会发出警报声。

The system works by detecting disturbances to the water’s surface, analyzing wave patterns to distinguish between the splash of a person (or pet) falling in and ripples caused by the wind. If the Bcone detects an event, it fires off its internal Klaxon and relays the alarm to a Wi-Fi bridge that remains inside the home (plus the Bcone app). The bridge and app both let you easily disable the alarm when people are swimming—and can even turn the system back on automatically when the water is clear, so “I forgot” doesn’t become a deadly excuse. —Christopher Null
该系统通过检测水面扰动来工作，分析波浪模式以区分人（或宠物）落水溅起的水花和风引起的涟漪。如果 Bcone 检测到异常，它会触发内部警报器，并将警报传送到室内的 Wi-Fi 网桥（以及 Bcone 应用程序）。网桥和应用程序都能让你在有人游泳时轻松关闭警报，甚至能在水面恢复平静时自动重新开启系统，这样“我忘了”就不会成为致命的借口。——Christopher Null
