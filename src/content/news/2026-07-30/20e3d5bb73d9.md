# Customize Ars your way with an Ars Pro subscription
# 通过 Ars Pro 订阅，打造属于你的 Ars 阅读体验

Having it your way is one of the benefits of being an Ars Pro subscriber. You get all of the great content you come to Ars for every day, but you get to choose how you parse it. Some readers want the classic blog-style homepage. Others want as many headlines as possible packed onto one screen. Some want a wider text column; others would happily never see another story about a topic they don’t follow. We’ve got you covered.

按照自己的喜好阅读是 Ars Pro 订阅用户的福利之一。你依然可以每天获取 Ars 提供的优质内容，但你可以选择以何种方式来阅读它们。有些读者喜欢经典的博客式主页，另一些读者则希望在屏幕上尽可能多地展示标题。有人想要更宽的文本栏，也有人希望彻底屏蔽掉自己不感兴趣的话题。无论你的需求是什么，我们都能满足。

Both Grid and List views are available for all readers, but subscribing to Ars unlocks two additional layout options. Our old-school Classic view reprises the original site layout from 1998, the year the Ars Orbiting HQ launched. Turn the clock back with Classic View.

所有读者都可以使用“网格”和“列表”视图，但订阅 Ars 后，你将解锁另外两种布局选项。我们复古的“经典”视图重现了 1998 年（Ars 总部成立之年）的原始网站布局。通过“经典”视图，带你重回过去。

But if you really, really love information density (or like to read Hacker News style), you’ll love the Neutron Star layout, the ultimate in minimalism. No pictures, just words. And with keyboard navigation, you never need to reach for your mouse.

如果你非常追求信息密度（或者喜欢 Hacker News 的阅读风格），那么你一定会爱上“中子星”布局，这是极致简约的体现。没有图片，只有文字。配合键盘导航，你甚至无需触碰鼠标。

An Ars Pro subscription lets you tweak the site to optimize the way you read. Choose your favorite view, filter your favorite topics, adjust the reading layout, and make Ars feel less like a website designed for everyone and more like one designed for you.

Ars Pro 订阅允许你微调网站设置，以优化你的阅读方式。选择你最喜欢的视图，过滤你感兴趣的话题，调整阅读布局，让 Ars 不再是一个面向大众的通用网站，而是一个为你量身定制的阅读空间。

Beyond changing the look and feel of Ars to your tastes, subscribing also unlocks all of the other Ars Pro features, including:
* No ads
* No trackers
* Optimized page layout
* Full-text RSS feeds
* Access to subscriber-only forums
* And a special user badge for Ars Pro++ subscribers

除了根据你的品味改变 Ars 的外观和感受外，订阅还可以解锁所有其他 Ars Pro 功能，包括：
* 无广告
* 无追踪器
* 优化的页面布局
* 全文 RSS 订阅源
* 访问订阅者专属论坛
* 以及为 Ars Pro++ 订阅者提供的特殊用户徽章

Most importantly, you’re directly supporting Ars Technica’s journalistic mission at a critical time. And you get all of this for just $25 for an Ars Pro membership and $50 for Ars Pro++.

最重要的是，你在关键时刻直接支持了 Ars Technica 的新闻使命。你只需支付 25 美元即可获得 Ars Pro 会员资格，或支付 50 美元获得 Ars Pro++ 会员资格。

And while we’re on the topic of journalism, there is one small thing you could do for Ars, regardless of whether you subscribe. To absolutely no one’s surprise, Google’s search-result AI summaries have led to a drop in traffic for everyone, Ars included. Google does allow users to designate specific websites as a preferred source, which makes their stories more likely to appear on the “Top Stories” section of Google searches.

在谈到新闻业时，无论你是否订阅，有一件小事可以帮到 Ars。不出所料，谷歌搜索结果中的 AI 摘要导致了包括 Ars 在内的所有网站流量下滑。谷歌允许用户将特定网站指定为“首选来源”，这会使这些网站的文章更有可能出现在谷歌搜索的“头条新闻”板块中。

If you use Google and are willing to set Ars as a Preferred Source, simply click on the icon that looks like a black box with a white star. From there, you can enter Ars Technica (and any other site you love and want to support) in the text box and select it as a preferred source.

如果你使用谷歌并愿意将 Ars 设置为首选来源，只需点击那个看起来像带有白色星星的黑色方框图标。然后，你可以在文本框中输入 Ars Technica（以及任何你喜爱并希望支持的网站），并将其选为首选来源。

Please support Ars Technica and subscribe today!

请支持 Ars Technica 并立即订阅！
