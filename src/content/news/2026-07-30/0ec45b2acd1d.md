It’s well worth the $20 price of admission to check out Night City’s updated world and story.

Over the last few years, CD Projekt Red put a ton of work into fixing Cyberpunk 2077, squashing bugs, polishing content, and even launching new DLC in 2023, Phantom Liberty. As a result, this game has gone from a source for funny, glitch-fueled videos to a world I’ve returned to a number of times to live out my futuristic fantasies. A big discount happening across almost all platforms makes it a lot more accessible.
在过去的几年里，CD Projekt Red 在修复《赛博朋克 2077》上投入了大量心血，不仅修复了漏洞、优化了内容，还在 2023 年推出了全新的 DLC《往日之影》（Phantom Liberty）。因此，这款游戏已经从当初那个充满搞笑 Bug 的视频素材库，蜕变成了一个让我多次回归、沉浸其中的未来幻想世界。如今，几乎所有平台都在进行大幅折扣，使得这款游戏变得更加触手可及。

It’s generous to say that the ambitious RPG was only a bit buggy at launch. In fact, its performance issues were glaring enough to cause both Sony and Microsoft to offer extended refunds. After all the bug fixes and improvements, the game is now a commonly-used performance benchmark for PC and console gaming. While its most advanced ray tracing modes are reserved for few platforms, it’s a visually-stunning game no matter where you play it. Plus, continued updates have given players more options for getting the most out of their time in Night City.
