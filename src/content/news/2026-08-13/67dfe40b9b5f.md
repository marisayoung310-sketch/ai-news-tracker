# Honor’s Robot Phone Has a Gimbal-Powered Camera for Vloggers
# 荣耀推出“机器人手机”，配备专为视频博主设计的云台相机

You hear it often: Smartphones have plateaued. A small boost to battery life here, a minor camera upgrade there—our glass slabs don't seem to change all that much from year to year.
你经常会听到这样的说法：智能手机的发展已经进入了瓶颈期。电池续航提升一点，相机升级一点——这些玻璃板设备似乎每年都没有太大的变化。

That's exactly the kind of sentiment Honor wants to counter with the Robot Phone, which has a motorized camera that pops up out of the top. Honor has been teasing the phone since late 2025.
这正是荣耀希望通过“机器人手机”（Robot Phone）来反驳的观点，这款手机顶部配有一个可以弹出的电动相机。荣耀自2025年底以来一直在预热这款产品。

On Wednesday, the company officially made the Robot Phone available to buyers in China. Honor was originally a subsidiary of Huawei, but it's now a wholly independent company. While its Android phones are available in regions like the UK, Europe, Africa, and Latin America, it has no presence in the US, and the Robot Phone remains a China-exclusive for now. It costs 9,999 RMB (around $1,482).
周三，该公司正式在中国市场发售了这款机器人手机。荣耀最初是华为的子公司，但现在已是一家完全独立的公司。虽然其安卓手机在英国、欧洲、非洲和拉丁美洲等地区有售，但在美国市场并无业务，且这款机器人手机目前仅限中国市场销售。售价为9,999元人民币（约合1,482美元）。

Honor calls the Robot Phone a “new species of smartphone,” one designed to cater to the needs of the modern mobile content creator. That's the thinking behind its nifty trick of a gimbal-powered camera embedded in the phone, which makes it a potentially more enticing option for filming videos compared to dedicated gimbal cameras like the DJI Osmo Pocket or the Insta360 Luna Ultra.
荣耀将这款机器人手机称为“智能手机新物种”，旨在满足现代移动内容创作者的需求。这就是其内置云台相机这一巧妙设计的初衷，与大疆Osmo Pocket或Insta360 Luna Ultra等专用云台相机相比，它在视频拍摄方面可能更具吸引力。

Building that roving eye was no small feat. Honor says it had to develop the micro-motor for the gimbal itself because off-the-shelf components were too large, reducing the volume by 65 percent compared to traditional gimbals on the market. Honor claims the titanium-alloy gimbal motor weighs just 2.6 grams. It's embedded in a 6.3-inch smartphone that's only 9.59 mm thick (for reference, the iPhone 17 Pro is 8.75 mm).
打造这只“灵动之眼”并非易事。荣耀表示，由于市面上的现成组件体积太大，他们不得不自行研发云台微型电机，将体积比传统云台减少了65%。荣耀声称，这款钛合金云台电机仅重2.6克。它被嵌入在一款厚度仅为9.59毫米的6.3英寸智能手机中（作为参考，iPhone 17 Pro的厚度为8.75毫米）。

The gimbal-powered 200-megapixel camera isn't the only camera, either. It's joined by a 200-megapixel telephoto camera and a 50-megapixel ultrawide, not to mention the 50-megapixel selfie shooter. The gimbal has 4 degrees of freedom, and you can activate it in the camera app or by making a palm motion on the selfie camera. And since it can rotate 300 degrees, you can use it as a selfie camera too.
这款云台驱动的2亿像素相机并非唯一镜头。它还配备了一颗2亿像素的长焦镜头和一颗5000万像素的超广角镜头，此外还有一颗5000万像素的前置自拍镜头。该云台拥有4个自由度，你可以通过相机应用或在自拍镜头前做出手势来激活它。由于它可以旋转300度，你也可以将其用作自拍相机。

The phone is shockingly light at 248 grams (the aforementioned iPhone 17 Pro is 206 grams). I wholly expected the Robot Phone to be top-heavy, with all those camera components at the top and the massive 7,060-mAh silicon-carbon battery inside powering the whole system. But in fact it’s decently balanced.
这款手机重量仅为248克，令人惊讶（前述iPhone 17 Pro为206克）。我原以为这款手机会头重脚轻，毕竟顶部集成了所有相机组件，内部还搭载了巨大的7,060mAh硅碳电池。但事实上，它的平衡感做得相当不错。

A glass cover protects the gimbal camera, and you can slide it to the right when you're ready to activate the gimbal. Once the camera is out, you can manually control the gimbal for ultra-smooth video capture through the camera app, or use preset motions to give your clips a cinematic look. There's even voice control with natural language, so you can say “turn around for 5 seconds.”
云台相机由玻璃盖保护，准备激活时只需将其向右滑动即可。相机弹出后，你可以通过相机应用手动控制云台以获得超平滑的视频拍摄效果，或者使用预设动作让视频更具电影感。它甚至支持自然语言语音控制，你可以直接说“转过去5秒钟”。

The motorized stabilization in the camera makes action shots—which can typically look shaky on traditional smartphones—look amazingly smooth. There are tracking modes as well, such as a subject tracker that keeps the selected person in the frame even as they move around. You can imagine how helpful that can be for solo travelers vlogging their way through a trip.
相机中的电动防抖功能让动作拍摄画面看起来非常平滑，而传统智能手机拍摄此类画面通常会显得抖动。此外还有追踪模式，例如主体追踪功能，即使被选中的人在移动，也能将其保持在画面中心。你可以想象这对独自旅行并拍摄Vlog的博主来说有多么实用。

### Specs / 规格参数
*   **Display:** 6.31-inch, 120 Hz, OLED / **屏幕：** 6.31英寸，120Hz，OLED
*   **Processor, RAM, and Storage:** Snapdragon 8 Elite Gen 5, 12 or 16 GB RAM, 512 GB or 1 TB storage / **处理器、内存及存储：** 骁龙8 Elite Gen 5，12GB或16GB内存，512GB或1TB存储
*   **Cameras:** 200-MP gimbal main, 200-MP telephoto, 50-MP ultrawide, 50-MP selfie camera / **相机：** 2亿像素云台主摄，2亿像素长焦，5000万像素超广角，5000万像素自拍镜头
*   **Battery and Charging:** 7,060 mAh, 120-watt wired, 50-watt wireless / **电池与充电：** 7,060mAh，120W有线充电，50W无线充电
*   **Size and Weight:** 151.43 x 72.86 x 9.59 mm, 248 grams / **尺寸与重量：** 151.43 x 72.86 x 9.59毫米，248克
*   **Colors:** Silver, Gray / **颜色：** 银色，灰色
*   **Water Resistance:** IP54 / **防水等级：** IP54

Some of these features extend past content creation. For example, the subject-tracking mode works in video calls, meaning you can set the phone up on a table (Honor includes a case and a magnetic stand) and the camera can follow you around. I also took a group selfie, and instead of taking a normal picture, the gimbal took three photos—left, center, and right—and stitched them like a panorama for a wide shot of everyone. Quick and seamless.
其中一些功能不仅限于内容创作。例如，主体追踪模式在视频通话中同样有效，这意味着你可以将手机放在桌子上（荣耀附赠了保护壳和磁吸支架），相机就能自动跟随你。我还尝试了集体自拍，云台没有拍摄普通照片，而是自动拍摄了左、中、右三张照片，并像全景图一样拼接在一起，拍出了所有人的广角合影。过程快速且无缝。

There's a less effective and quite gimmicky integration with Honor's Yoyo virtual AI assistant, where the gimbal camera acts like an enigmatic robot with a pair of eyes on the phone's screen. Ask it to dance to music, and the gimbal camera will move to the beat. It can pretend to be happy or angry, making the appropriate movements to indicate its mood. The camera also serves as the AI's eyes; ask it to describe the scene in front of you, and it'll take a peek.
荣耀的Yoyo虚拟AI助手集成了一些效果一般且略显花哨的功能，云台相机会像一个神秘的机器人，屏幕上还会显示出一双眼睛。让它随音乐跳舞，云台相机会跟着节奏摆动。它还能假装开心或生气，做出相应的动作来表达情绪。相机同时充当了AI的眼睛；让它描述你面前的场景，它就会“看”一眼。

That last bit could be useful, say, if you propped the phone up and asked the assistant to watch you assemble some furniture and point out any steps you might have missed. However, Yoyo doesn't have this kind of capability yet. Honor uses Google's Gemini assistant in markets outside of China, and if the Robot Phone rolled out elsewhere, that could be a very real integration with Gemini Live.
最后一点功能可能会很有用，比如当你架起手机，让助手观察你组装家具，并指出你可能遗漏的步骤。不过，Yoyo目前还不具备这种能力。荣耀在中国以外的市场使用谷歌的Gemini助手，如果这款机器人手机在其他地区推出，这可能会成为与Gemini Live非常实用的集成功能。

You're probably wondering just how durable this mechanical gimbal resting on top of the phone really is. Unlike with folding phones, which are measured in lab tests to withstand more than 500,000 folds, Honor doesn't say how much strain the gimbal's motor can endure. Instead, a company spokesperson tells WIRED the “gimbal mechanism has undergone rigorous reliability testing for repeated deployment and retraction. Its durability is designed to meet everyday usage needs throughout the expected service life of the device.” There's a built-in software feature that safeguards the gimbal from "inadvertent handling or abnormal resistance," too.
你可能想知道手机顶部这个机械云台到底有多耐用。与折叠屏手机通过实验室测试证明能承受超过50万次折叠不同，荣耀并未透露云台电机能承受多大的压力。相反，公司发言人告诉《连线》杂志：“云台机构已经过严格的可靠性测试，以确保反复弹出和收回的稳定性。其耐用性旨在满足设备整个预期使用寿命内的日常需求。”此外，内置的软件功能还能保护云台免受“意外操作或异常阻力”的影响。

It's not the first time a phone maker has toyed with a mechanical camera. OnePlus famously had a mechanical pop-up selfie camera hidden in its OnePlus 7 Pro, offering a seamless display experience with no notch or floating pinhole camera, and its motor was rated for 300,000 cycles.
这并非手机制造商首次尝试机械相机。一加手机曾以其OnePlus 7 Pro中隐藏的机械弹出式自拍相机而闻名，实现了无刘海、无挖孔的无缝显示体验，其电机的额定寿命为30万次。

The design does sacrifice the phone’s overall water and dust resistance, given that the Robot Phone is only rated at IP54 when the camera is stowed.
这种设计确实牺牲了手机的整体防水防尘性能，因为机器人手机在相机收起时仅达到IP54防护等级。
