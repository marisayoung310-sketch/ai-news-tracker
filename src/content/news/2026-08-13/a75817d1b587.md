# Human versus Computer Vision / 人类视觉与计算机视觉

**Computer Science > Computer Vision and Pattern Recognition**
**计算机科学 > 计算机视觉与模式识别**

**arXiv:2608.10181 (cs) [Submitted on 10 Aug 2026]**
**arXiv:2608.10181 (cs) [提交于 2026 年 8 月 10 日]**

**Title: Human versus Computer Vision**
**标题：人类视觉与计算机视觉**

**Authors: Elena Sirotkina**
**作者：Elena Sirotkina**

**Abstract:**
**摘要：**

Computer vision saliency models predict where people will look, one map per image, and a billion-dollar predicted-attention industry sells those maps in place of measuring real viewers.
计算机视觉显著性模型旨在预测人们的注视点（每张图像生成一张热力图），目前已形成一个价值数十亿美元的“预测注意力”产业，通过销售这些热力图来替代对真实观众的测量。

I test the leading models from the audience side, against 11.4 million webcam gaze points from 3,023 US adults recruited to national quotas, viewing circulating news photographs.
我从观众的角度对领先的模型进行了测试，对比了 3,023 名美国成年人（按国家配额招募）在观看新闻照片时产生的 1,140 万个网络摄像头注视点数据。

I show that an untrained central marker outperforms every trained network, because the content the networks add on top of the center falls where these audiences never look.
研究表明，一个未经训练的“中心标记法”表现优于所有经过训练的神经网络，因为这些网络在图像中心之外添加的内容，恰恰是观众从不关注的区域。

What accuracy remains is systematically biased, favoring younger, White, and moderate viewers over older, Black, and ideologically extreme ones.
模型剩余的准确性存在系统性偏差，相比于年长者、黑人及意识形态极端者，模型对年轻、白人及立场温和的观众表现出明显的偏向性。

I propose a way forward and build on what a group's own gaze reveals about whether a model can learn that group at all, and I apply it across every demographic axis this sample supports.
我提出了一种改进方向，基于特定群体自身的注视点数据来评估模型是否能够真正“学习”该群体，并将此方法应用于样本所涵盖的所有人口统计维度。

Ultimately, I show how systems that decide what people see can learn to see everyone, and this study supplies the standard by which such a claim should be judged.
最终，我展示了决定人们“看到什么”的系统如何才能学会“看到每一个人”，本研究为评估此类系统提供了衡量标准。
