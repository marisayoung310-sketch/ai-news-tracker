# Finding the Signal in the Spam: Jointly Learning Rewards and Worker Reliability from Pairwise Comparisons
# 在垃圾信息中寻找信号：从成对比较中联合学习奖励与工作者可靠性

**Abstract:** The problem of learning from pairwise comparisons has been widely studied across many domains such as recommendation systems, social choice, and more recently, fine-tuning large language models. In this problem, the goal is to learn item rewards based on pairwise comparisons between them.
**摘要：** 从成对比较中进行学习的问题已在推荐系统、社会选择以及最近的大语言模型微调等多个领域得到广泛研究。在该问题中，目标是基于项目之间的成对比较来学习项目奖励。

In many scenarios, these comparisons are elicited from crowdworkers using platforms such as Amazon Mechanical Turk, Scale AI, etc. However, crowdworkers are often unreliable due to limited domain knowledge or revenue-maximizing (spamming) behavior.
在许多场景中，这些比较数据是通过 Amazon Mechanical Turk、Scale AI 等平台从众包工作者那里获取的。然而，由于领域知识有限或为了追求收益最大化（发送垃圾信息），众包工作者往往不可靠。

In this work, our goal is to understand whether worker reliability (competency) can be learned jointly with item rewards. To this end, we adopt the Boltzmann-rational model for pairwise comparisons, which extends the Bradley-Terry-Luce model by incorporating worker competencies.
在这项工作中，我们的目标是探究是否可以在学习项目奖励的同时联合学习工作者的可靠性（能力）。为此，我们采用了用于成对比较的 Boltzmann-rational 模型，该模型通过引入工作者能力对 Bradley-Terry-Luce 模型进行了扩展。

We derive an EM-based algorithm for learning under this model by introducing Polya-Gamma latent variables to transform the logistic likelihood into a conditionally Gaussian form, enabling tractable optimization and leading to a simplified $Q$ function in the E-step of the algorithm.
我们推导了一种基于 EM 的算法，通过引入 Polya-Gamma 潜变量将逻辑似然函数转换为条件高斯形式，从而实现了可处理的优化，并使算法 E 步中的 $Q$ 函数得到简化。

This technique allows us to reduce our formulation to a matrix sensing problem, using which we establish theoretical convergence guarantees for our algorithm. We conduct extensive experiments on real-world and synthetic datasets.
该技术使我们能够将公式简化为矩阵感知问题，并据此为我们的算法建立了理论收敛保证。我们在真实世界和合成数据集上进行了广泛的实验。

These experiments demonstrate the advantages of using our algorithm over several baselines and confirm its strong robustness to both spammers and adversarial workers, highlighting its practical effectiveness in realistic crowdsourcing and reward learning settings. The code and data is publicly available at this [URL](https://github.com/kaustubhshejole/Joint-Learning-Rewards-Worker-Reliability).
这些实验证明了我们的算法相较于多个基准方法的优势，并证实了其对垃圾信息发送者和对抗性工作者的强大鲁棒性，突显了其在现实众包和奖励学习环境中的实际有效性。代码和数据已在 [URL](https://github.com/kaustubhshejole/Joint-Learning-Rewards-Worker-Reliability) 公开。
