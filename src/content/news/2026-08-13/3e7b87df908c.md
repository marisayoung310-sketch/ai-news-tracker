# How Google’s new Pixel 11 phones compare to last year’s models
# 谷歌新款 Pixel 11 手机与去年机型对比

From new AI and camera features to a faster chip, here’s what separates the Pixel 11 and 11 Pro from their predecessors.
从全新的 AI 和相机功能到更快的芯片，以下是 Pixel 11 和 11 Pro 与其前代产品的区别。

Google just added four new phones to the Pixel family: the Pixel 11, Pixel 11 Pro, Pixel 11 Pro XL, and the Pixel 11 Pro Fold. They’re slightly more expensive than their predecessors, but they come with some notable upgrades, including improved cameras and a new Tensor G6 chip that should deliver faster performance. The phones also have new photography tools, like Magic Capture, along with improvements to Gemini Intelligence, expanded Live Translate capabilities, and the ability to use Circle to Search directly through the camera.
谷歌刚刚为 Pixel 家族增添了四款新手机：Pixel 11、Pixel 11 Pro、Pixel 11 Pro XL 和 Pixel 11 Pro Fold。它们的价格比前代产品略高，但带来了一些显著的升级，包括改进的摄像头和能够提供更快性能的全新 Tensor G6 芯片。这些手机还配备了新的摄影工具（如 Magic Capture）、改进的 Gemini Intelligence、扩展的实时翻译功能，以及直接通过相机使用“搜索圈”（Circle to Search）的能力。

To make deciding whether you’ll upgrade or hold onto your last-gen phone a little simpler, we’re focusing on comparing the specs of the 6.3-inch Pixel 11 and Pixel 10 models, as well as the 6.8-inch Pro XL models. If you’re curious about how the Pixel 11 Pro Fold compares to its predecessor (and to the Samsung Galaxy Z Fold 8 series foldables), we’ve got a spec comparison right here.
为了让你更轻松地决定是升级还是继续使用上一代手机，我们将重点对比 6.3 英寸的 Pixel 11 与 Pixel 10 机型，以及 6.8 英寸的 Pro XL 机型。如果你好奇 Pixel 11 Pro Fold 与其前代产品（以及三星 Galaxy Z Fold 8 系列折叠屏手机）的对比情况，我们这里也提供了规格对比。

### They’re more expensive, but you get more storage
### 价格更高，但存储空间更大

The most obvious difference is that, like almost everything these days, they’re more expensive than before. The Pixel 11 starts at $899, $100 more than its predecessor, but Google has doubled the base storage to 256GB with 12GB of RAM. The Pixel 11 Pro starts at $1,099 — $100 more than the Pixel 10 Pro’s launch price — with the same 256GB of storage and 12GB of RAM, and is available with up to 1TB of storage and 16GB of RAM. Meanwhile, Google’s larger 6.8-inch Pixel 11 Pro XL starts at $1,299 and offers the same storage and RAM configurations as the Pro.
最明显的区别在于，像如今的几乎所有产品一样，它们的价格比以前更高了。Pixel 11 起售价为 899 美元，比前代产品贵了 100 美元，但谷歌将基础存储空间翻倍至 256GB，并配备 12GB 内存。Pixel 11 Pro 起售价为 1099 美元（比 Pixel 10 Pro 的首发价高出 100 美元），同样提供 256GB 存储和 12GB 内存，最高可选 1TB 存储和 16GB 内存。与此同时，谷歌更大的 6.8 英寸 Pixel 11 Pro XL 起售价为 1299 美元，提供与 Pro 版相同的存储和内存配置。

### Tensor G6 allows for even more AI features
### Tensor G6 带来更多 AI 功能

Both the Pixel 11 and Pro phones run on Google’s new Tensor G6 chip, which the company claims should improve performance by up to 25 percent compared to its predecessor. The new chip also powers a host of AI upgrades, including improvements to Gemini Intelligence, which Google says can now pull more context from supported apps to proactively surface useful information. There are also new voice-to-text tools that remove filler words and clean up formatting, while writing assistance can use what’s on your screen to help draft, rewrite, and refine messages. Live Translate is also available on videos, podcasts, and voice messages, and Circle to Search now works directly through the camera, letting you identify objects, translate text, or search your surroundings without snapping a photo first.
Pixel 11 和 Pro 系列手机均搭载谷歌新款 Tensor G6 芯片，据称其性能比前代产品提升了高达 25%。这款新芯片还支持一系列 AI 升级，包括对 Gemini Intelligence 的改进——谷歌表示，它现在可以从支持的应用程序中提取更多上下文，主动呈现有用的信息。此外，还有新的语音转文字工具可以去除填充词并优化格式，而写作助手可以利用屏幕上的内容来帮助起草、重写和润色信息。实时翻译功能现在也支持视频、播客和语音消息，“搜索圈”功能现在可以直接通过相机使用，让你无需先拍照即可识别物体、翻译文本或搜索周围环境。

### The cameras get some notable upgrades inside and out
### 相机内外均获得显著升级

There are a number of camera improvements, too, along with new photography and video features. On the hardware side, the Pixel 11 has a slimmer camera bar that sits flush when paired with one of Google’s phone cases. It can also zoom in farther than its predecessor, reaching up to 30x for photos, while the Pixel 11 Pro series gets an updated 1/1.3-inch 50-megapixel sensor for its main camera and a redesigned telephoto sensor that Google says captures 32 percent more light. The Pro can also zoom up to 120x and features a 42-megapixel selfie camera. The Pro should be quicker at shooting in the dark, too, with Google claiming its Instant Night Sight can capture photos up to 4.5 times faster.
相机方面也有多项改进，并增加了新的摄影和视频功能。在硬件方面，Pixel 11 拥有更纤薄的相机条，搭配谷歌手机壳时可以保持平齐。它的变焦能力也比前代更强，照片变焦最高可达 30 倍；而 Pixel 11 Pro 系列的主摄升级为 1/1.3 英寸 5000 万像素传感器，并配备了重新设计的长焦传感器，谷歌称其进光量增加了 32%。Pro 系列最高支持 120 倍变焦，并配备 4200 万像素自拍镜头。Pro 系列在暗光拍摄时也更快，谷歌声称其“即时夜视”（Instant Night Sight）功能捕捉照片的速度最高可提升 4.5 倍。

Google has made several software improvements as well. The new Magic Capture mode in the camera app automatically captures photos and videos to make them ready to share with a tap. Camera Looks let you create custom styles for photos, while the Pixel 11 Pro series can record stabilized video at up to 8K using Pro Stable Video with Video Boost. A new Creator Suite also features tools for trimming and rearranging clips, using a teleprompter while recording, and improving speech clarity, and it automatically saves clips to a designated project album.
谷歌还进行了一些软件改进。相机应用中新增的“魔法捕捉”（Magic Capture）模式可自动拍摄照片和视频，让你一键即可分享。“相机外观”（Camera Looks）功能允许你为照片创建自定义风格，而 Pixel 11 Pro 系列可以使用“Pro Stable Video”配合“Video Boost”录制最高 8K 的稳定视频。全新的“创作者套件”（Creator Suite）还提供了剪辑和重排片段、录制时使用提词器以及提高语音清晰度的工具，并能自动将片段保存到指定的项目相册中。

### How the Pixel 10 and Pixel 11 compare
### Pixel 10 与 Pixel 11 对比

Those are some of the biggest changes, but they’re far from the only differences between the two lineups. For example, both the Pixel 11 and Pixel 11 Pro offer an Extreme Charging Mode that prioritizes charging over other tasks for a faster boost when you’re short on time. The Pixel 11 Pro and Pro XL also gets HiLight, colored LEDs around the camera flash that can alert you to things, like calls when your phone is face down or when Gemini is activated.
以上是一些最大的变化，但这远非两个系列之间的全部区别。例如，Pixel 11 和 Pixel 11 Pro 都提供了“极限充电模式”（Extreme Charging Mode），在时间紧迫时，该模式会优先处理充电任务以实现更快的补电。Pixel 11 Pro 和 Pro XL 还配备了 HiLight，即相机闪光灯周围的彩色 LED 灯，可以在手机屏幕朝下时提醒来电，或在 Gemini 被激活时发出提示。
