# Sheaf-Based Federated Representation Learning

**Title:** Sheaf-Based Federated Representation Learning
**标题：** 基于层（Sheaf）的联邦表征学习

**Abstract:** Heterogeneous federated systems require agents to learn and exchange informative representations despite differences in data distributions, sensing modalities, model architectures, latent dimensionalities, and local learning objectives.
**摘要：** 异构联邦系统要求智能体在数据分布、感知模态、模型架构、潜在维度和局部学习目标存在差异的情况下，仍能学习并交换具有信息量的表征。

To address this challenge, we propose Sheaf-based Federated Representation Learning (SFRL), a general framework that jointly optimizes local objectives with a manifold-constrained geometric alignment regularizer based on learnable sheaf restriction maps.
为了应对这一挑战，我们提出了基于层（Sheaf）的联邦表征学习（SFRL）。这是一个通用框架，它通过基于可学习层限制映射（learnable sheaf restriction maps）的流形约束几何对齐正则化器，联合优化局部目标。

Unlike most existing approaches, SFRL does not assume a shared global latent space. Instead, global consistency emerges from the alignment of neighboring latent representations through orthogonal transformations and isometric embeddings.
与大多数现有方法不同，SFRL 不假设存在共享的全局潜在空间。相反，全局一致性是通过正交变换和等距嵌入对相邻潜在表征进行对齐而产生的。

This alignment is enforced by a quadratic gluing regularizer induced by the sheaf Laplacian, whose learnable restriction maps adapt the geometry to the observed data. The penalty is evaluated on a small set of shared pilot samples, ensuring scalability and communication efficiency.
这种对齐由层拉普拉斯算子（sheaf Laplacian）诱导的二次粘合正则化器强制执行，其可学习的限制映射使几何结构能够适应观测数据。该惩罚项在一小部分共享的试点样本上进行评估，从而确保了可扩展性和通信效率。

We develop a decentralized algorithm for solving SFRL, termed Sheaf-FRL, which alternates between gradient updates of the local models and closed-form Procrustes updates of the edge-wise restriction maps.
我们开发了一种用于求解 SFRL 的去中心化算法，称为 Sheaf-FRL，该算法在局部模型的梯度更新与边级限制映射的闭式 Procrustes 更新之间交替进行。

We further establish convergence of Sheaf-FRL to first-order stationary points in both deterministic and stochastic settings. As an application, we consider a cooperative classification task in the context of semantic communication, under model and data heterogeneity.
我们进一步证明了 Sheaf-FRL 在确定性和随机设置下均能收敛到一阶平稳点。作为应用，我们考虑了在模型和数据异构背景下，语义通信中的协作分类任务。

Our results show that Sheaf-FRL outperforms baseline approaches in terms of local and post-communication classification accuracy across different levels of local distribution shift and exhibits greater robustness to latent-space dimensionality compression.
研究结果表明，在不同程度的局部分布偏移下，Sheaf-FRL 在局部和通信后的分类准确率方面均优于基准方法，并对潜在空间维度压缩表现出更强的鲁棒性。
