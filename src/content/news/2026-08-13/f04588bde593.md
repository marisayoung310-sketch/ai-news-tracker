# Tesla wants to build a $10B solar factory in Texas
# 特斯拉计划在德克萨斯州建造一座价值 100 亿美元的太阳能工厂

Tesla intends to build a massive solar panel factory 45 minutes southwest of Houston, according to documents filed with the state of Texas. The new factory, called Project Crystal Sun, could cost as much as $10.1 billion.
根据提交给德克萨斯州的文件显示，特斯拉计划在休斯顿西南方向 45 分钟车程处建造一座大型太阳能电池板工厂。这座名为“水晶太阳”（Project Crystal Sun）的新工厂耗资可能高达 101 亿美元。

Tesla has applied for tax incentives to partially offset the cost, saying that it is exploring other sites “across multiple U.S. states.” The factory would create about 9,700 full-time jobs, Tesla said.
特斯拉已申请税收优惠以部分抵消成本，并表示目前正在“美国多个州”考察其他选址。特斯拉称，该工厂将创造约 9,700 个全职工作岗位。

Without incentives, Tesla’s accountants estimated that the property tax liability for the project would be about $1.1 billion over 37 years.
据特斯拉的会计师估算，若没有税收优惠，该项目在 37 年内的房产税负债将约为 11 亿美元。

The project aims to break ground this year and be completed by 2028, and Tesla said the first solar panels would roll off the line in 2029. The company hasn’t indicated whether the panels would be destined for terrestrial installations or satellites.
该项目计划于今年动工，并于 2028 年完工。特斯拉表示，首批太阳能电池板将于 2029 年下线。该公司尚未说明这些电池板是用于地面安装还是卫星用途。

However, Tesla CEO Elon Musk, who also runs SpaceX, is famously bullish on orbital data centers. In the filings, Tesla did not publicly disclose the factory’s annual output, though it has said that it plans to build 100 gigawatts’ worth of manufacturing capacity in the U.S. by 2028.
不过，同时执掌 SpaceX 的特斯拉首席执行官埃隆·马斯克（Elon Musk）一直非常看好轨道数据中心。在提交的文件中，特斯拉并未公开该工厂的年产量，但此前曾表示计划到 2028 年在美国建立 100 吉瓦（GW）的制造产能。
