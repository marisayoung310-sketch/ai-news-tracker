# CurveFP: Rational-Radix Logarithmic Datatypes with Closed Products for Language Models
# CurveFP：用于语言模型的具有闭合乘积的有理基数对数数据类型

**Abstract:** Low-precision datatypes reduce language-model cost, but most formats optimize scalar fidelity while leaving the arithmetic induced by their products unchanged. We introduce CurveFP, a closed-product codebook family that distributes quantized magnitudes across interleaved logarithmic curves under compact block scales.

**摘要：** 低精度数据类型降低了语言模型的成本，但大多数格式在优化标量保真度的同时，并未改变其乘积所诱导的算术运算。我们引入了 CurveFP，这是一个闭合乘积码本系列，它在紧凑的块尺度下，将量化后的量级分布在交错的对数曲线上。

A rational radix tunes dynamic range against local resolution, while uniform curve indices make every nonzero product algebraically closed. Product formation becomes an exact sign XOR and integer-index update, and a derived finite phase count determines the accumulation schedule.

有理基数（Rational radix）在动态范围与局部分辨率之间进行权衡，而统一的曲线索引使得每一个非零乘积在代数上都是闭合的。乘积的形成简化为精确的符号异或（XOR）和整数索引更新，导出的有限相位计数则决定了累加调度。

We instantiate this algebra as CurveFP eight E4C3/E5C2 for training and CurveFP seven E3C3 for compact deployment. In evaluation, CurveFP seven beats tensor-wise FP8 perplexity on four 7B--9B models with one fewer element bit and stays within 1.32% of native quality.

我们将这种代数实例化为用于训练的 CurveFP eight (E4C3/E5C2) 和用于紧凑部署的 CurveFP seven (E3C3)。在评估中，CurveFP seven 在四个 7B-9B 模型上超越了张量级 FP8 的困惑度（perplexity），且每个元素减少了一位，同时保持在原生质量的 1.32% 以内。

CurveFP eight lowers operand NMSE in all 36 paired forward and backward GEMM comparisons. Across three matched 128.3M-parameter triplets, every mode completes 3B-token pretraining per seed; CurveFP eight reaches mean BF16-inference perplexity 22.5366 versus 22.5407 for FP8 and incurs a lower format-induced penalty in all three seeds.

在所有 36 组配对的前向和反向 GEMM 比较中，CurveFP eight 降低了操作数的归一化均方误差（NMSE）。在三组匹配的 1.283 亿参数三元组中，每种模式均完成了每个种子 3B token 的预训练；CurveFP eight 的平均 BF16 推理困惑度达到 22.5366（FP8 为 22.5407），并且在所有三个种子中产生的格式诱导损失更低。

A 36-cell downstream matrix finds lower WikiText-103 perplexity for the CurveFP eight-trained checkpoints in all 12 seed-format comparisons, with mixed PG-19 and task deltas. Together, these results establish CurveFP as an arithmetic co-design that combines FP8-class numerical behavior, seven-bit inference, and a substantially simpler product path.

在 12 组种子格式比较中，36 单元下游矩阵显示 CurveFP eight 训练的检查点在 WikiText-103 上具有更低的困惑度，PG-19 和其他任务的差异则互有胜负。总之，这些结果确立了 CurveFP 作为一种算术协同设计方案的地位，它结合了 FP8 级别的数值表现、七位推理能力以及显著简化的乘积路径。
