# Signpost Watermarking: Joint Optimization for Visual Watermark Coexistence
# 路标水印：视觉水印共存的联合优化

**Abstract:** We present a method for training imperceptible visual watermarks to coexist with other such watermarks. Recent work has shown that independently trained image watermarking models can coexist with surprisingly limited interference, enabling watermark ensembling. However, this coexistence is a serendipitous property rather than an explicit optimization objective, leaving interference uncontrolled and potentially reducing decoding robustness or visual quality.

**摘要：** 我们提出了一种训练不可见视觉水印的方法，使其能够与其他同类水印共存。近期的研究表明，独立训练的图像水印模型可以在干扰极小的情况下实现共存，从而支持水印集成。然而，这种共存性更多是一种偶然的特性，而非明确的优化目标，这导致干扰无法得到有效控制，并可能降低解码的鲁棒性或视觉质量。

We first show empirically that the same coexistence property extends to video watermarking. We then show that both image and video watermarks can be trained with a decoder-aware objective to improve coexistence. Our results suggest a practical path to signpost watermarks that indicate the presence of independently deployed provenance watermarking systems, supporting layered provenance signaling for content authenticity and rights.

我们首先通过实验证明，这种共存特性同样适用于视频水印。随后，我们展示了图像和视频水印均可以通过“解码器感知”（decoder-aware）的目标函数进行训练，以提升其共存能力。我们的研究结果为“路标水印”（signpost watermarks）提供了一条切实可行的路径，这种水印能够指示独立部署的溯源水印系统的存在，从而支持针对内容真实性和版权的分层溯源信号传输。
