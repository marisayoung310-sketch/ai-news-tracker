# Google Pixel 11 系列、Pixel Watch 5、Pixel Tag：规格、功能、价格与发布日期

Google is dialing things up to 11 this year with its Pixel phones. Ahead of its annual Made by Google event in New York City, the company unwrapped the Pixel 11 smartphone family—which includes the Pixel 11, Pixel 11 Pro, Pixel 11 Pro XL, and Pixel 11 Pro Fold—alongside the Pixel Watch 5 smartwatch. There's also a new hardware addition: the Pixel Tag, which seems designed to be the AirTag of the Android world.

今年，Google 将其 Pixel 手机产品线提升到了“11”的新高度。在纽约举行的年度“Made by Google”发布会前夕，该公司正式揭晓了 Pixel 11 智能手机家族——包括 Pixel 11、Pixel 11 Pro、Pixel 11 Pro XL 和 Pixel 11 Pro Fold——以及 Pixel Watch 5 智能手表。此外，还有一款全新的硬件产品：Pixel Tag，它似乎旨在成为 Android 世界的 AirTag。

Here's everything you need to know about the hardware changes and software upgrades on Google’s latest devices. Preorders open today, and the phones and watches go on sale August 20.

以下是关于 Google 最新设备在硬件变更和软件升级方面你需要了解的一切。预购于今日开启，手机和手表将于 8 月 20 日正式发售。

### The Pixel 11 and Pixel 11 Pro Fold
### Pixel 11 与 Pixel 11 Pro Fold

It was inevitable, but prices are up across the board for Google's Pixel 11 series. The Pixel 11 now starts at $899, and on the highest end of the spectrum, the Pixel 11 Pro Fold is now $1,900. This means both devices got $100 price bumps. There's some good news: Google has bumped the base storage up to 256 GB on every device, a move matching Samsung's and Apple's latest handsets.

尽管早有预料，但 Google Pixel 11 系列的全线价格确实有所上涨。Pixel 11 起售价现为 899 美元，而高端旗舰 Pixel 11 Pro Fold 则高达 1,900 美元。这意味着两款设备均涨价了 100 美元。不过也有好消息：Google 将所有设备的起步存储空间提升至 256 GB，这一举措与三星和苹果的最新机型保持一致。

The Pixel 11 series is powered by Google's Tensor G6 chipset, which the company says is 20 percent more power efficient, 25 percent faster for browsing, and 15 percent faster when loading apps. Many of the camera improvements in the new devices are thanks to an improved imaging signal processor on the chip and a 50 percent more powerful tensor processing unit.

Pixel 11 系列搭载了 Google 的 Tensor G6 芯片组。据公司称，该芯片能效提升了 20%，网页浏览速度提升了 25%，应用加载速度提升了 15%。新设备在相机方面的多项改进，得益于芯片上升级的图像信号处理器以及性能提升 50% 的张量处理单元（TPU）。

One big design change across the entire Pixel 11 series is on the oval camera bar on the back—it's now an all-glass surface, as opposed to a mix of metal and glass on its predecessors. Google says the camera bar on the Pixel 11 is more than 40 percent thinner, and when you pop on Google's case, the camera bar disappears and is completely flush. The Pixel 11 has the most striking color of the lot—Hibiscus—joined by more muted Pistachio, Frost, and Obsidian.

Pixel 11 系列在设计上的一个重大变化在于背部的椭圆形相机栏——它现在采用了全玻璃表面，而不再是前代产品中金属与玻璃的混合材质。Google 表示，Pixel 11 的相机栏厚度减少了 40% 以上，当装上 Google 原厂手机壳时，相机栏会完全平齐，几乎隐形。Pixel 11 拥有该系列中最引人注目的颜色——木槿红（Hibiscus），此外还有更为低调的开心果绿（Pistachio）、霜白（Frost）和曜石黑（Obsidian）。

The Pixel 11 and Pixel 11 Pro Fold share more in common than you'd expect. They now have the same primary 48-megapixel camera, upgraded this year with a larger sensor that can capture up to 56 percent more light for brighter low-light images. The 10.8-megapixel telephoto camera, which has a 5X optical zoom, can now digitally zoom up to 30X. At these higher digital zoom levels, Google's machine learning algorithm puts in the work to clean up the digital noise and deliver an enhanced photo.

Pixel 11 和 Pixel 11 Pro Fold 的共同点比你预想的要多。它们现在配备了相同的 4800 万像素主摄像头，今年升级了更大的传感器，进光量提升了 56%，从而在弱光环境下拍摄出更明亮的照片。1080 万像素的长焦镜头支持 5 倍光学变焦，现在最高可实现 30 倍数字变焦。在这些高倍率数字变焦下，Google 的机器学习算法会介入处理，清除数字噪点并提供增强后的成像效果。

Google's folding phone is now 10 percent lighter and 1 mm thinner than the Pixel 10 Pro Fold, with slimmer bezels around the screen, though the phone is still heftier and thicker than Samsung's new Galaxy Z Fold8 Ultra. It maintains its IP68 dust- and water-resistance rating, but there's a new glass-fiber composite back cover that Google says is “nearly impossible to crack” and an upgraded hinge that better protects the inner display from accidental drops. All of this, Google claims, makes the device three times more durable than its predecessor. Bold words.

Google 的折叠屏手机现在比 Pixel 10 Pro Fold 轻了 10%，薄了 1 毫米，屏幕边框也更窄，尽管它仍然比三星新款 Galaxy Z Fold8 Ultra 更重、更厚。它保持了 IP68 级的防尘防水性能，但采用了全新的玻璃纤维复合材料后盖，Google 称其“几乎不可能碎裂”，并升级了铰链以更好地保护内屏免受意外跌落的影响。Google 声称，所有这些改进使该设备的耐用性达到了前代产品的三倍。这确实是大胆的宣称。

The Fold features HiLight, a glowing LED on the back (excluded on the Pixel 11 but available on the Pixel 11 Pro phones). It lights up when you talk to Gemini, much like the glowing lights on the new Google Home Speaker. You can set it to light up in specific colors when you receive phone calls from your favorite contacts. Google says it's exploring the same capability for text messages, but didn't comment on whether it will enable HiLight for other notifications.

Fold 配备了 HiLight，这是背部的一枚发光 LED 灯（Pixel 11 不具备，但 Pixel 11 Pro 系列有）。当你与 Gemini 对话时，它会亮起，就像新款 Google Home 智能音箱上的呼吸灯一样。你可以设置它在收到特定联系人的来电时显示特定颜色。Google 表示正在探索将其应用于短信提醒，但未评论是否会为其他通知启用 HiLight 功能。

Both phones have been upgraded to 25-watt wireless charging speeds for faster Qi2 charging.

两款手机的无线充电速度均已升级至 25 瓦，以实现更快的 Qi2 充电体验。

### The Pixel 11 Pro and Pro XL
### Pixel 11 Pro 与 Pro XL

Even if the Pixel 11 Pro Fold has “Pro” in its name, a few extra upgrades in the candybar Pixel 11 Pro and Pixel 11 Pro XL elevate them to be the best hardware and software from Google. Both share the same specs outside of screen size, battery size, and charging speeds. They also are seeing $100 price bumps to $1,100 and $1,300, respectively.

尽管 Pixel 11 Pro Fold 的名字里带有“Pro”，但直板机型 Pixel 11 Pro 和 Pixel 11 Pro XL 的一些额外升级，使其成为了 Google 硬件和软件的巅峰之作。除了屏幕尺寸、电池容量和充电速度外，两者的规格基本一致。它们的价格也分别上涨了 100 美元，达到 1,100 美元和 1,300 美元。

The new colors this year are Canyon, Olive, and Fog, but the standard Obsidian (aka black) now has an all-matte finish. The same HiLight glowing LED as on the Fold is on the all-glass camera bar (Google is also bringing back the “Glowbar” on its upcoming Googlebook laptops later this year, so these glowing lights are a big theme for 2026). There's a new anti-scratch display coating that purportedly delivers more than double the scratch resistance of the Pixel 10 Pro.

今年推出的新配色包括峡谷色（Canyon）、橄榄色（Olive）和雾灰色（Fog），而标准的曜石黑（Obsidian）现在采用了全哑光饰面。与 Fold 一样，全玻璃相机栏上也配备了 HiLight 发光 LED 灯（Google 还将在今年晚些时候推出的 Googlebook 笔记本电脑上重新引入“Glowbar”，因此这些发光灯带是 2026 年的一大主题）。此外，新设备采用了全新的防刮屏幕涂层，据称其抗刮擦能力是 Pixel 10 Pro 的两倍以上。
