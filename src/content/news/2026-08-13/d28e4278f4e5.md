# Why tiny JPEGs look different in Chrome
# 为什么微小的 JPEG 图片在 Chrome 中显示效果不同

What looked like a rendering bug turned out to be a clever JPEG decoding optimization in Chrome.
原本以为是一个渲染错误，结果发现是 Chrome 中一种巧妙的 JPEG 解码优化。

### This icon looks better on my colleague’s computer
### 这个图标在我同事的电脑上看起来更好

A while back, when chatting with a colleague over their computer, I noticed that a logo did not look exactly the same as it did on mine. It looked thinner on theirs and more faithful to the original image. It was rendered at 15px; here is an upscaled version. Note: this was not the original image. It happened a while ago, so I made a new image to demonstrate the issue.
前段时间，在和同事交流时，我注意到一个 Logo 在他们电脑上的显示效果与我的不完全一样。在他们的屏幕上，Logo 看起来更细，也更忠实于原始图像。该图标渲染尺寸为 15px；以下是放大后的版本。注意：这不是原始图像。由于事情过去了一段时间，我制作了一张新图来演示这个问题。

On the left, Firefox; on the right, Chrome. If you squint, or take a step back, the one from Chrome looks thicker. A bit weird, but swapping the image for an SVG fixed it. Still, I was curious: why was it rendering like this in the first place? I did some digging and found a nifty optimization that Chrome uses when rendering JPEGs at small scales.
左侧是 Firefox，右侧是 Chrome。如果你眯起眼睛或退后一步，会发现 Chrome 渲染的图标看起来更粗。这有点奇怪，但将图片换成 SVG 后问题就解决了。不过我仍然很好奇：为什么它最初会渲染成这样？我深入研究后发现，Chrome 在以小尺寸渲染 JPEG 时使用了一种巧妙的优化手段。

### Scaling down images can be wasteful
### 缩小图片可能会造成浪费

The intuitive way to render a small image from a JPEG is to fully decompress it in memory and then scale it down. But that is not always efficient. Imagine a 2000 × 2000 JPEG that needs to be displayed at 20 × 20. Once uncompressed, the image takes far more memory than the final result. A bitmap of the full image uses roughly 12 MB, while the final 20 × 20 image needs only about 1.2 KB. Most of the information in the large version is lost when scaling down.
从 JPEG 渲染小图的直观方法是先在内存中完全解压，然后再进行缩放。但这并不总是高效的。想象一张需要以 20 × 20 显示的 2000 × 2000 JPEG 图片。一旦解压，该图像占用的内存远超最终结果。完整图像的位图大约占用 12 MB，而最终的 20 × 20 图像仅需约 1.2 KB。在缩放过程中，大版本中的大部分信息都被丢弃了。

### What information is lost when scaling down?
### 缩放时丢失了什么信息？

An interesting insight is that the information lost is not random. When an image is scaled down heavily, the information that disappears is mostly the high-frequency detail. This is easy to see intuitively. Think of a tree with lots of leaves and rough bark: those fine details change quickly from pixel to pixel, so they count as high-frequency information. If you scale that tree down to something tiny, like 20 × 10, you end up with just a green blob at the top for the foliage and a brown stick at the bottom for the trunk. The scaled-down version has thrown away the fine detail, the high-frequency information.
一个有趣的发现是，丢失的信息并非随机的。当图像被大幅缩小时，消失的信息主要是高频细节。这一点很容易直观地理解。想象一棵长满叶子、树皮粗糙的树：那些精细的细节在像素间变化很快，因此它们属于高频信息。如果你将这棵树缩小到 20 × 10 这样微小的尺寸，你最终只会得到顶部的一团绿色（树叶）和底部的一根棕色线条（树干）。缩小后的版本丢弃了精细的细节，即高频信息。

*Illustration of a tree being scaled down*
*树木缩小的示意图*

Some of that high-frequency information still survives to an extent, because the details get mixed together.
部分高频信息在一定程度上得以保留，因为细节被混合在了一起。

### How JPEG stores image data
### JPEG 如何存储图像数据

I will keep this explanation light on jargon and math, but I will still mention a few technical terms that can be good starting points if you want to dig deeper. I will also skip a fair chunk of the full JPEG transformation, because it is not needed here.
我将尽量减少术语和数学公式，但仍会提到一些技术术语，如果你想深入研究，这些术语可以作为切入点。我也会跳过完整的 JPEG 转换过程中的大部分内容，因为这里并不需要。

During JPEG compression, images are split into 8 × 8 blocks that are converted into the frequency domain. This operation is called a DCT (Discrete Cosine Transform). In an 8 × 8 block, the lowest possible frequency is a flat color. Strictly speaking, it is not really a frequency because nothing changes; it is the constant component. At the opposite end, the highest frequency looks like a checkerboard, where the value changes as much as possible. Everything in between represents the rest of the frequency domain. These are called basis functions.
在 JPEG 压缩过程中，图像被分割成 8 × 8 的块，并转换为频域。此操作称为 DCT（离散余弦变换）。在 8 × 8 的块中，最低频率是纯色。严格来说，它并不是真正的频率，因为没有任何变化；它是直流分量（constant component）。在另一端，最高频率看起来像棋盘，数值变化最为剧烈。介于两者之间的所有内容代表了频域的其余部分。这些被称为基函数（basis functions）。

*The basis functions: you can see the flat color in the top-left, and the checkerboard in the bottom-right.*
*基函数：你可以看到左上角的纯色和右下角的棋盘格。*

So converting an 8 × 8 block into the frequency domain is basically asking: how much of each pattern is present in this block? Those amounts are called coefficients. JPEG compression has a few more steps after that to store those coefficients efficiently, and that is where the lossy compression happens. But that part is not important for what we are discussing here.
因此，将 8 × 8 的块转换为频域，本质上是在问：这个块中包含了多少种模式？这些数量被称为系数。在此之后，JPEG 压缩还有几个步骤来高效存储这些系数，这正是产生有损压缩的地方。但这一部分对于我们讨论的内容并不重要。

### Putting it together: rendering a JPEG at 1/8 scale
### 总结：以 1/8 比例渲染 JPEG

Now let’s say you want to shrink an image by a factor of 8. Those 8 × 8 blocks I mentioned earlier can now be represented by a single pixel in the downscaled image. At that size, the image mostly needs low-frequency information because, as in the tree example, the high-frequency details mostly disappear during scaling. So instead of decompressing the whole JPEG, we can skip the coefficients for the high-frequency parts and use only the ones needed for the coarse version of the image. That gives a scaled-down result without fully expanding the original image first.
现在假设你想将图像缩小 8 倍。我之前提到的那些 8 × 8 的块现在可以用缩小后图像中的单个像素来表示。在这种尺寸下，图像主要需要低频信息，因为正如树的例子所示，高频细节在缩放过程中大多会消失。因此，我们无需解压整个 JPEG，只需跳过高频部分的系数，仅使用生成粗略图像所需的系数即可。这样无需先完全展开原始图像，就能得到缩小后的结果。

The decoded image takes less space and is faster to uncompress, since we are skipping a good chunk of the coefficients. This can be extended to other ratios, as long as they are fractions with a denominator of 8. The technical name for this is partial IDCT scaling*. See jpegclub.org (if you read a bit on this, you will see that this technique can also be used to upscale images!).
解码后的图像占用空间更小，解压速度也更快，因为我们跳过了很大一部分系数。这种方法可以扩展到其他比例，只要它们是分母为 8 的分数即可。其技术名称为部分 IDCT 缩放（partial IDCT scaling）*。参考 jpegclub.org（如果你阅读相关内容，会发现该技术也可用于放大图像！）。

*\* Inverse discrete cosine transform: taking the frequency domain back to the image domain.*
*\* 逆离散余弦变换：将频域转换回图像域。*

### How Chrome fits in
### Chrome 的角色

Chrome delegates image decoding and rendering to Skia. For JPEGs, Skia uses libjpeg-turbo, which implements partial IDCT scaling. That lets it decode only the lower-frequency data when the target size is small enough. In other words, Chrome/Skia does not always decompress the full image and scale it afterward. It computes the closest fraction with a denominator of 8 and decodes the image at that scale. It then scales the image further using a more traditional downsampling algorithm until it reaches the desired size.
Chrome 将图像解码和渲染委托给 Skia。对于 JPEG，Skia 使用 libjpeg-turbo，它实现了部分 IDCT 缩放。当目标尺寸足够小时，这使得它只需解码低频数据。换句话说，Chrome/Skia 并不总是先解压完整图像再进行缩放。它会计算出分母为 8 的最接近分数，并按该比例解码图像。然后，它会使用更传统的下采样算法进一步缩放图像，直到达到所需尺寸。

That is why the image looked thicker on my machine. Because it was rendered so small, it was decoded at one-eighth scale using partial IDCT scaling. So the only data from the frequency representation that remained was the constant component; all the edge softening and gradients were not used.
这就是为什么图像在我的机器上看起来更粗的原因。因为它渲染得太小，所以它是通过部分 IDCT 缩放以 1/8 的比例解码的。因此，频域表示中唯一保留的数据就是直流分量；所有的边缘平滑和渐变都没有被使用。

Really, the moral here is that you should not use JPEG for icons and the like. The format and its optimizations are designed around our perception of photos. After all, it is in the name: Joint Photographic Experts Group.
总之，这里的教训是：你不应该将 JPEG 用于图标等内容。该格式及其优化是围绕我们对照片的感知而设计的。毕竟，它的名字就说明了一切：联合图像专家组（Joint Photographic Experts Group）。
