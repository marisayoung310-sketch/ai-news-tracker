# Google’s Pixel 11 phone preorders come with up to $350 in gift cards
# Google Pixel 11 手机预购可获最高 350 美元礼品卡

Looking to get your hands on the latest Pixel devices? After weeks of leaks and rumors, Google has officially announced its next generation of Pixel phones and watches.
想要入手最新的 Pixel 设备吗？经过数周的泄露和传闻，Google 已正式发布了其下一代 Pixel 手机和手表。

The base Pixel 11 starts at $899, the Pro and Pro XL are $1,099 and $1,299 respectively, and the Pro Fold starts at $1,899. The watch comes in two sizes, with the smaller 41mm model starting at $399, and the larger 45mm model starting at $429. The phones are available for preorder now, and will ship on August 20th, 2026.
Pixel 11 标准版起售价为 899 美元，Pro 和 Pro XL 分别为 1,099 美元和 1,299 美元，Pro Fold 起售价为 1,899 美元。手表提供两种尺寸，较小的 41mm 型号起售价为 399 美元，较大的 45mm 型号起售价为 429 美元。手机现已开启预购，并将于 2026 年 8 月 20 日发货。

If you’re looking to preorder an unlocked phone on its own, your main options are Best Buy, Amazon, and directly from the Google Store. All three are running a similar introductory deal, where you’ll receive a gift card for a future purchase at that retailer, with the value depending on which model you buy. The Pixel 11 includes a $100 gift card, the Pixel 11 Pro and Pro XL include a $200 gift card, and the Pixel 11 Pro Fold includes a $350 gift card. Only Google lists an end date for the promotion, which is August 27th, 2026.
如果您打算单独预购无锁版手机，主要渠道包括百思买 (Best Buy)、亚马逊 (Amazon) 以及 Google 官方商店。这三家平台都推出了类似的促销活动：您将获得一张可在该零售商处用于未来消费的礼品卡，金额取决于您购买的机型。购买 Pixel 11 可获 100 美元礼品卡，Pixel 11 Pro 和 Pro XL 可获 200 美元礼品卡，而 Pixel 11 Pro Fold 则可获 350 美元礼品卡。只有 Google 明确标注了促销截止日期，即 2026 年 8 月 27 日。

If you’d also like a Pixel Watch 5, Google and Amazon will give you additional credit towards that purchase instead of the gift card. The Pixel 11 and 41mm watch bundle is $1,168.99, effectively a $130 discount if you bought the two devices separately, but only in obsidian. The Pixel 11 Pro and Pro XL with a 41mm watch as a bundle is discounted by $250. The Amazon bundles include only the Wi-Fi versions of the Pixel Watch 5 and only in limited color combinations. The Google store will let you apply the credit to other in-stock variants. If you buy a Pixel 11 Pro Fold, you can opt for a 41mm Wi-Fi watch for free, with Amazon offering obsidian only and Google letting you choose your colors.
如果您还想要 Pixel Watch 5，Google 和亚马逊将为您提供额外的购买抵扣额度，而不是礼品卡。Pixel 11 与 41mm 手表的套装价格为 1,168.99 美元，相当于比单独购买两款设备优惠了 130 美元，但仅限曜石黑配色。Pixel 11 Pro 和 Pro XL 与 41mm 手表的套装优惠力度为 250 美元。亚马逊的套装仅包含 Wi-Fi 版 Pixel Watch 5，且颜色选择有限。Google 商店则允许您将抵扣额度应用于其他现货版本。如果您购买 Pixel 11 Pro Fold，则可以免费获得一块 41mm Wi-Fi 手表，亚马逊仅提供曜石黑配色，而 Google 允许您自由选择颜色。

Each phone is powered by the new Tensor G6 processor, which Google says is capable of 25 percent faster web browsing and 15 percent improvement to app loading speeds over the Tensor G5. The camera and video features are a focus of the new Pixel 11 series phones. The new Magic Capture feature lets you forget about snapping a great shot, and instead stitches together a series of clips and photos from a longer video. All four phones also featuring upgraded batteries and fast charging, with wireless charging support for Qi2.2 up to 25W.
每款手机均搭载全新的 Tensor G6 处理器。据 Google 称，与 Tensor G5 相比，其网页浏览速度提升了 25%，应用加载速度提升了 15%。相机和视频功能是新款 Pixel 11 系列手机的重点。全新的“魔法捕捉”(Magic Capture) 功能让您无需费心抓拍，它能从较长的视频中自动拼接出一系列精彩片段和照片。四款手机均配备了升级后的电池和快充功能，并支持最高 25W 的 Qi2.2 无线充电。

### Where to preorder the Pixel 11 Pro and Pro XL
### Pixel 11 Pro 和 Pro XL 预购信息

The Pixel 11 Pro starts at $1,099 for the base model with 256GB of storage, and goes up to $1,219 for the 512GB model, or $1,449 for the 1TB model. The larger Pixel 11 Pro XL starts at $1,299 for the 256GB configuration, with the 512GB costing $1,419 and the 1TB model priced at $1,649. For both phones, the 256GB version has 12GB of memory, while the larger capacity models have 16GB of memory.
Pixel 11 Pro 256GB 基础版起售价为 1,099 美元，512GB 版本为 1,219 美元，1TB 版本为 1,449 美元。更大的 Pixel 11 Pro XL 256GB 配置起售价为 1,299 美元，512GB 版本为 1,419 美元，1TB 版本为 1,649 美元。两款手机的 256GB 版本均配备 12GB 内存，而更大容量版本则配备 16GB 内存。

Apart from the screen size, resolution, and battery capacity, the two phones are otherwise identical to each other. They feature updated cameras, including a 50MP wide angle, 48MP telephoto with up to 5x optical zoom, a 48MP ultrawide sensor, and a 42MP selfie camera with a 103 degree field of view. The phones are available in four colors: canyon, fog, olive, and obsidian.
除了屏幕尺寸、分辨率和电池容量外，这两款手机在其他方面完全相同。它们配备了升级后的摄像头系统，包括 5000 万像素广角镜头、支持最高 5 倍光学变焦的 4800 万像素长焦镜头、4800 万像素超广角传感器，以及拥有 103 度视角的 4200 万像素自拍镜头。手机提供四种颜色：峡谷色、雾灰色、橄榄色和曜石黑。

### Where to preorder the Pixel 11
### Pixel 11 预购信息

The Pixel 11 starts at $899 for the model with 256GB of storage, and the price rising to $1,019 for the 512GB model, both of which have 12GB of memory. The 6.3-inch display has a 1080 x 2424 resolution and 60-120Hz refresh rate, with HDR support up to 2000 nits, or 3000 nit brightness with HDR off. For cameras, the Pixel 11 sports a 48MP sensor wide camera, a 13MP ultrawide, a 10.8 MP telephoto with 5x optical zoom, and a 10.5MP selfie camera with a 95 degree field of view. The phone is available in four colors: frost, pistachio, hibiscus, and obsidian.
Pixel 11 256GB 版本起售价为 899 美元，512GB 版本价格为 1,019 美元，两者均配备 12GB 内存。其 6.3 英寸显示屏分辨率为 1080 x 2424，支持 60-120Hz 刷新率，HDR 模式下亮度最高可达 2000 尼特，关闭 HDR 时最高亮度为 3000 尼特。相机方面，Pixel 11 配备 4800 万像素广角镜头、1300 万像素超广角镜头、支持 5 倍光学变焦的 1080 万像素长焦镜头，以及拥有 95 度视角的 1050 万像素自拍镜头。手机提供四种颜色：霜白色、开心果绿、木槿红和曜石黑。

### Where to preorder the Pixel 11 Pro Fold
### Pixel 11 Pro Fold 预购信息

The Pixel 11 Pro Fold starts at $1,899 for the base model with 256GB and 16GB of RAM, with a 512GB model for $2,019, and a 1TB option for $2,249 available as well. It’s available in two colors: olive and obsidian.
Pixel 11 Pro Fold 256GB/16GB 内存基础版起售价为 1,899 美元，512GB 版本售价 2,019 美元，另有 1TB 版本售价 2,249 美元。提供橄榄色和曜石黑两种颜色。

The only foldable phone in Google’s Pixel lineup, the 11 Pro Fold features a 6.5-inch external display, and a 8-inch internal display when opened. Both screens feature refresh rates up to 120Hz, and HDR support. The internal and front selfie cameras are 10MP sensors, and the main camera block features a 10.5MP ultrawide, 10.8MP telephoto with up to 5X optical zoom, and a new 48MP wide camera with upgraded light sensitivity.
作为 Google Pixel 产品线中唯一的折叠屏手机，11 Pro Fold 配备 6.5 英寸外屏，展开后为 8 英寸内屏。两块屏幕均支持最高 120Hz 刷新率和 HDR。内屏和前置自拍镜头均为 1000 万像素传感器，主摄模组包含 1050 万像素超广角镜头、支持最高 5 倍光学变焦的 1080 万像素长焦镜头，以及一颗感光能力升级的全新 4800 万像素广角镜头。
