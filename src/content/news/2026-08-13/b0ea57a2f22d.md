# P3CA: Encoder-Agnostic Interpretation of Vision Foundation Model Embeddings via Spatial Probing
# P3CA：通过空间探测实现视觉基础模型嵌入的编码器无关解释

**Abstract:** Vision foundation models are increasingly used as reusable encoders in medical image computing, yet their high-dimensional spatial embeddings are difficult to inspect beyond downstream task performance or global dimensionality reduction.
**摘要：** 视觉基础模型正越来越多地被用作医学图像计算中的可重用编码器，然而，除了下游任务性能或全局降维之外，其高维空间嵌入很难进行深入检查。

We propose position-prompted PCA (P3CA), an encoder-agnostic method for local probing of channel-rich spatial tensors. Given a user-selected spatial prompt, P3CA estimates the feature normalization and dominant covariance directions within that region, then applies the resulting projection to the full tensor to visualize where locally informative directions are expressed.
我们提出了位置提示主成分分析（P3CA），这是一种用于对富含通道的空间张量进行局部探测的编码器无关方法。给定用户选择的空间提示，P3CA 会估计该区域内的特征归一化和主协方差方向，然后将所得投影应用于整个张量，以可视化局部信息方向的表达位置。

This produces a region-conditioned representation lens without modifying the encoder, retraining, or requiring task-specific labels. We implement P3CA in EmbedVision, an interactive 3D Slicer-based workflow, and evaluate it across natural images, colorectal pathology foundation-model embeddings, and spatial transcriptomic tensors.
这产生了一种区域条件化的表征透镜，无需修改编码器、重新训练或需要特定任务的标签。我们在基于 3D Slicer 的交互式工作流 EmbedVision 中实现了 P3CA，并在自然图像、结直肠病理学基础模型嵌入以及空间转录组张量上对其进行了评估。

Across these settings, prompted projections reveal local structure suppressed by global PCA, improve prompt-matched pathology discrimination from frozen three-dimensional projections, and support comparison between learned and measured spatial representations.
在这些设置中，提示投影揭示了被全局 PCA 所抑制的局部结构，改善了从冻结三维投影中进行的提示匹配病理判别，并支持了学习到的空间表征与测量到的空间表征之间的比较。
