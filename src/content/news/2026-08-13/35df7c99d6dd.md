# ICE wants to give agents electrified gloves that shock people into compliance
# 美国移民及海关执法局（ICE）计划为特工配备电击手套，以强制实现合规

Immigration and Customs Enforcement (ICE) is aiming to spend up to $20 million on equipping officers and agents with specialized gloves that deliver painful electric shocks. These plans were outlined in a notice published by the Department of Homeland Security on Monday, with an unspecified quantity of the devices set to be delivered by March 2027.

美国移民及海关执法局（ICE）计划斥资高达 2000 万美元，为官员和特工配备能够产生剧烈电击的特制手套。国土安全部周一发布的一份公告概述了这些计划，并指出该批设备将于 2027 年 3 月前交付，具体数量未明。

The electrified handwear outlined in the contract is the CTG 5 GLOVE (Generated Low Output Voltage Emitter), which manufacturer Compliant Technologies refers to as a CD3 (Conductive Distraction and De-escalation) device. Compliant Technologies describes it as “a great, humane, low-optics, de-escalation solution” that is “usually effective in bringing individuals into compliance in less than three seconds.”

合同中提到的这种带电手套名为 CTG 5 GLOVE（低压输出发射器），制造商 Compliant Technologies 将其称为 CD3（传导式干扰与降级冲突）设备。Compliant Technologies 将其描述为“一种出色、人道、低视觉冲击的冲突降级解决方案”，并称其“通常能在三秒内有效使目标对象服从”。

The shock feature can be activated in a second using a switch located on the glove, which delivers a maximum voltage of up to 380V upon skin contact — closer to a dog’s shock collar than a taser device. Still, this “inhibits/ distracts the subject from performing coordinated muscle movement” according to the glove’s user guide. The same guide includes several warnings: Don’t use more than one pair on a single individual, don’t shock people for more than 15 seconds, and don’t use it against high-risk populations such as children, or people who are elderly, pregnant, or disabled.

手套上的开关可在瞬间激活电击功能，接触皮肤时最高可产生 380 伏电压——这更接近于狗用的电击项圈，而非泰瑟枪。尽管如此，根据手套的用户指南，这种电击仍能“抑制/干扰目标对象进行协调的肌肉运动”。指南中还包含多项警告：不得对同一人使用超过一副手套，电击时间不得超过 15 秒，且不得用于儿童、老人、孕妇或残疾人等高风险群体。

Because the CD3 gloves are not immediately identifiable as a weapon, Compliant Technologies describes them as an “invisible partner” that can be used to enhance “established tactics, techniques and procedures,” and calls them “The New Protocol in Peace Keeping.” Similar gloves from the same company have already been used by select police departments and jails in recent years, and two corrections officers were charged in 2022 for beating and shocking an inmate in two separate attacks.

由于 CD3 手套无法被一眼识别为武器，Compliant Technologies 将其描述为“隐形伙伴”，可用于增强“既定的战术、技术和程序”，并称其为“维和新协议”。近年来，部分警察部门和监狱已经使用了该公司生产的类似手套；2022 年，两名惩教人员因在两次独立袭击中殴打并电击一名囚犯而被起诉。

ICE has demonstrated it can wield power with little oversight or accountability, fueled by an administration that encourages rough tactics. It has shown up at people’s doors to intimidate them, used mass surveillance, and killed people in broad daylight. The agency’s new shocking gloves will just be a new way to hurt people.

在鼓励粗暴手段的政府支持下，ICE 已证明其可以在几乎没有监督或问责的情况下行使权力。该机构曾上门恐吓民众、实施大规模监控，甚至在光天化日之下杀人。该机构新配备的电击手套，只不过是伤害民众的新工具罢了。

The Associated Press reports that the Department of Homeland Security is working on a response to its press inquiries, but offered “no immediate comment” on the purchase plans. In a statement to the publication, Jenn Rolnick Borchetta, deputy project director on policing at the American Civil Liberties Union, questioned why the devices would be necessary for civil immigration enforcement:

美联社报道称，国土安全部正在准备回应媒体的质询，但对采购计划“暂无评论”。美国公民自由联盟（ACLU）警务副项目主任 Jenn Rolnick Borchetta 在一份声明中质疑，为何民事移民执法需要此类设备：

“ICE spent the last year showing this country they are too quick to use force. Now they will be able to deploy electric shocks with the slight push of a button that maybe nobody else can see them do. Introducing gloves that can so easily be used to deliver terrible pain in encounters is a recipe for harm to the public.”

“ICE 在过去一年里向全国展示了他们滥用武力的倾向。现在，他们只需轻轻按下一个可能无人察觉的按钮，就能实施电击。引入这种在冲突中极易造成剧烈痛苦的手套，无异于为公众伤害埋下祸根。”
