# LEGO: Leveled Language Gaussian Splatting

**Abstract:** We introduce LEGO for advanced open-vocabulary scene understanding. Beyond basic concept recognition, its core innovation lies in capturing the intrinsic semantic hierarchies within the scene, such as the "flowerpot -> bouquet -> bud -> petal" lineage.

**摘要：** 我们引入了 LEGO，用于实现先进的开放词汇场景理解。除了基础的概念识别外，其核心创新在于能够捕捉场景内部固有的语义层级，例如“花盆 -> 花束 -> 花蕾 -> 花瓣”这样的谱系关系。

While foundation models like SAM can identify multi-granular structures in 2D, their partitions are strictly perspective-bound and lack cross-view consensus. LEGO self-adaptively re-grades volatile multi-view SAM granularities into a unified, 3D-consistent hierarchy.

虽然像 SAM 这样的基础模型可以在二维图像中识别多粒度结构，但它们的分割结果严格受限于视角，且缺乏跨视角的共识。LEGO 能够自适应地将不稳定的多视角 SAM 粒度重新分级，转化为统一且具有三维一致性的层级结构。

This provides precise supervision for the structurally coherent, multi-level segmentation of 3D scenes. By grounding these segments with CLIP embeddings, LEGO recovers open-vocabulary semantic logic across hierarchical levels.

这为三维场景的结构连贯、多层级分割提供了精确的监督。通过利用 CLIP 嵌入对这些分割区域进行定位（Grounding），LEGO 能够在各个层级上恢复开放词汇的语义逻辑。

Furthermore, by incorporating spatial relationships, we elevate these segments into level-wise language scene graphs, effectively empowering Large Language Models to perform complex, context-aware spatial reasoning and precise visual grounding.

此外，通过整合空间关系，我们将这些分割区域提升为分层级的语言场景图，从而有效地赋能大语言模型，使其能够执行复杂的、具备上下文感知能力的空间推理和精确的视觉定位。

Experimental results demonstrate that LEGO establishes new state-of-the-art performance across both promptable and open-vocabulary 3D segmentation benchmarks, exhibiting advanced hierarchical scene decomposition and context-aware spatial reasoning.

实验结果表明，LEGO 在可提示（promptable）和开放词汇三维分割基准测试中均确立了新的行业领先水平（SOTA），展现了先进的层级化场景分解能力和上下文感知空间推理能力。
