# California SB 1001: What the Bot Disclosure Law Actually Requires
# 加州 SB 1001 法案：机器人披露法案的实际要求是什么

California’s Bolstering Online Transparency Act — SB 1001 of 2018, operative from 1 July 2019 — is usually described as requiring chatbots in California to identify themselves. It does not. It prohibits a specific kind of deception and offers disclosure as the way out of liability, and the difference decides whether the statute applies to your product at all.
加州的《加强在线透明度法案》（2018 年 SB 1001 法案，自 2019 年 7 月 1 日起生效）通常被描述为要求加州的聊天机器人必须进行自我标识。事实并非如此。该法案禁止的是一种特定的欺骗行为，并提供“披露”作为规避法律责任的途径。这一区别决定了该法规是否适用于你的产品。

The operative provision The Act is codified at Cal. Bus. & Prof. Code §§ 17940–17943; the text is published by the California Legislative Counsel at leginfo.legislature.ca.gov. The prohibition in § 17941(a) is built from three stacked requirements, all of which must be present: a person uses a bot to communicate or interact with another person in California online; with the intent to mislead the other person about its artificial identity; for the purpose of knowingly deceiving the person about the content of the communication, in order either to incentivise a purchase or sale of goods or services in a commercial transaction, or to influence a vote in an election.
该法案的生效条款编纂在《加州商业与职业法典》第 17940–17943 条中；全文由加州立法顾问办公室发布在 leginfo.legislature.ca.gov 上。第 17941(a) 条中的禁令由三个叠加的要求构成，必须同时满足：某人在线上使用机器人与加州的另一人进行交流或互动；意图误导对方关于其人工智能身份的认知；且目的是为了在交流内容上蓄意欺骗对方，从而促成商业交易中的商品或服务买卖，或影响选举投票。

Two separate mental states, then, plus a purpose limitation. A support bot that never pretends to be human, and whose operator has no intention that anyone be misled about anything, is outside § 17941(a) even if it never says “I am a bot”. That is the opposite of how the statute is usually summarised. This describes what the section says; it is not legal advice, and intent is a fact question decided on evidence about your organisation, not on the text of a disclosure. The safe practice — disclosing — is cheap and is discussed below.
因此，这涉及两种不同的心理状态，外加一个目的限制。一个从不假装是人类、且其运营者无意误导任何人的支持类机器人，即使从未说过“我是机器人”，也不在第 17941(a) 条的管辖范围内。这与该法规通常的总结恰恰相反。以上内容仅描述了该条款的含义，并非法律建议；“意图”是一个基于你所在组织证据的事实问题，而非仅凭披露文本就能决定。最稳妥的做法——即进行披露——成本很低，下文将对此进行讨论。

What counts as a bot § 17940 defines a bot as an automated online account where all or substantially all of the actions or posts of that account are not the result of a person. Three observations follow. First, the definition is account-shaped. It was drafted with social media in mind, and applying it to a chat widget embedded in a website, a voice agent on a phone line, or an API-driven assistant inside somebody else’s product requires some construction. Nothing in the section says an “account” must be on a social network, but the fit is imperfect and no California appellate decision has settled it.
什么是机器人？第 17940 条将机器人定义为一种自动化的在线账户，其所有或绝大部分操作或发布内容并非由人类产生。这里有三点观察：首先，该定义是基于“账户”形态的。它是针对社交媒体起草的，将其应用于嵌入网站的聊天插件、电话语音代理或他人产品内部的 API 驱动助手时，需要进行一定的解读。虽然该条款并未规定“账户”必须存在于社交网络上，但这种定义并不完全契合，且目前尚无加州上诉法院的判决对此做出定论。

Second, “all or substantially all” means a hybrid does not escape by having a human occasionally intervene, but it also means an agent that drafts messages a human reviews and sends is arguably not a bot at all. Where the line is has not been litigated. Third, “online” is defined broadly in § 17940 as appearing on any public-facing internet website, web application or digital application, including a social network or publication. It is not restricted to social media, and it is not restricted by size.
其次，“所有或绝大部分”意味着混合型系统不能通过偶尔的人工干预来规避法律，但也意味着由人类审核并发送的草拟消息的代理，可以说根本不是机器人。界限在哪里尚未经过诉讼裁定。第三，“在线”在第 17940 条中被广泛定义为出现在任何面向公众的互联网网站、Web 应用或数字应用上，包括社交网络或出版物。它不限于社交媒体，也不受规模限制。

Two purposes, and nothing else The purpose limitation is the boundary of the statute and it is startlingly narrow given how the law is discussed. The deception must be aimed at incentivising a purchase or sale of goods or services in a commercial transaction, or at influencing a vote in an election. That leaves a great deal outside. A bot that manufactures the appearance of grassroots support for a policy, that impersonates a human in a customer service context without any transaction in view, that runs an emotional-companionship product, or that generates political content outside an election context, is not covered by § 17941(a) on its face. Those harms are real; this is not the statute that addresses them. Some are reached by the general unfair-competition law, some by newer California statutes, and some by nothing at all.
仅限两个目的：目的限制是该法规的边界，考虑到人们对该法律的讨论方式，这个边界窄得惊人。欺骗行为必须旨在促成商业交易中的商品或服务买卖，或影响选举投票。这意味着很多情况被排除在外。一个制造政策草根支持假象的机器人、一个在没有交易背景下冒充人类进行客户服务的机器人、一个运行情感陪伴产品的机器人，或一个在选举背景之外生成政治内容的机器人，从字面上看并不在第 17941(a) 条的管辖范围内。这些危害是真实存在的，但该法规并非为此而设。有些危害受一般反不正当竞争法管辖，有些受加州较新的法规管辖，而有些则完全不受监管。

§ 17941(b) then supplies the safe harbour: a person does not violate the section if the person discloses that the communication is from a bot. The disclosure must be clear, conspicuous and reasonably designed to inform the person that they are communicating with a bot. There is no prescribed form, no prescribed timing beyond that, and no requirement to repeat it — but “reasonably designed to inform” is an objective test, so a disclosure buried in a terms page is not one. Because the safe harbour is cheap and the intent analysis is expensive, disclosing is the sensible default even where the prohibition plainly does not apply.
第 17941(b) 条提供了避风港：如果某人披露了交流来自机器人，则不违反该条款。披露必须清晰、显眼，并经过合理设计以告知对方其正在与机器人交流。法律没有规定具体形式、时间要求，也没有要求重复披露——但“合理设计以告知”是一个客观测试，因此埋在条款页面中的披露是不合格的。由于避风港成本低廉，而意图分析成本高昂，即使在禁令明显不适用的情况下，进行披露也是明智的默认做法。

The ten-million-visitor misreading A very large number of summaries state that SB 1001 only applies to platforms with at least ten million unique monthly United States visitors. That figure is in the Act, and it is not in the prohibition. § 17940 defines “online platform” using the ten-million-visitor test, and “online platform” is the term used in § 17942, which provides that the chapter does not impose a duty on service providers of online platforms — including a duty to affirmatively review, monitor or enforce compliance. The threshold is therefore part of an immunity for large platforms, not a scope limit on the duty imposed on the person deploying the bot. The prohibition in § 17941 uses “online”, which has no size test. Practically: a two-person company running a bot on its own website has no ten-million-visitor exemption. A large platform hosting other people’s bots has no policing duty. Those are different propositions and the summaries collapse them.
关于“一千万访客”的误读：大量摘要称 SB 1001 仅适用于每月在美国拥有至少一千万独立访客的平台。该数字确实出现在法案中，但并不在禁令条款里。第 17940 条使用“一千万访客”测试来定义“在线平台”，而“在线平台”一词用于第 17942 条，该条规定本章不对在线平台的服务提供商施加义务——包括主动审查、监控或强制合规的义务。因此，该门槛是大型平台豁免权的一部分，而不是对部署机器人的个人所施加义务的范围限制。第 17941 条中的禁令使用的是“在线”，没有任何规模测试。实际上：一家在自己网站上运行机器人的两人公司并没有“一千万访客”的豁免权。托管他人机器人的大型平台也没有监管义务。这些是不同的命题，但摘要将它们混为一谈了。

Enforcement, and what has replaced it The Act contains no enforcement provision of its own: no agency is named, no penalty is set, and no private right of action is created in terms. Whether a violation can be pursued through California’s Unfair Competition Law, Bus. & Prof. Code § 17200, as an unlawful business practice is an open question that has not been answered by a published California decision, and a UCL plaintiff would still need to establish standing through injury in fact and lost money or property. The honest summary is that SB 1001 has been enforced rarely if at all, and that its practical force has been as a drafting reference and a compliance baseline rather than as a source of litigation.
