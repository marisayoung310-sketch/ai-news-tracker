### Why Recompute the Same Gradients?
### 为什么要重复计算相同的梯度？

We calculated the gradient for $w_1$ using the chain rule.
我们使用链式法则计算了 $w_1$ 的梯度。

\[ \frac{\partial L}{\partial w_1} = \frac{\partial L}{\partial \hat{y}} \cdot \frac{\partial \hat{y}}{\partial a_1} \cdot \frac{\partial a_1}{\partial z_1} \cdot \frac{\partial z_1}{\partial w_1} \]

We got the same equation that we had previously derived using classical differentiation in Part 1.
我们得到了与第一部分中使用经典微分法推导出的相同方程。

\[ \frac{\partial L}{\partial w_1} = -2(y-\hat{y}) \cdot w_3 \cdot \mathrm{ReLU}'(w_1x+b_1) \cdot x \]

We then came to understand just how important the chain rule is. Now, what about the gradients for the other parameters? $b_1,\; w_2,\; b_2,\; w_3,\; w_4,\; b_3$. We’ve already seen the entire process for $w_1$. So, we generally think of repeating the same steps which is using the chain rule for the remaining parameters. If we have a look at all the chain rule equations for all parameters, we can observe that many of the partial derivatives appear more than once.
随后，我们理解了链式法则的重要性。那么，其他参数的梯度该如何计算呢？即 $b_1,\; w_2,\; b_2,\; w_3,\; w_4,\; b_3$。我们已经了解了 $w_1$ 的完整计算过程，因此通常会想到对剩余参数重复相同的步骤，即使用链式法则。如果我们观察所有参数的链式法则方程，会发现许多偏导数出现了不止一次。

\[ \frac{\partial L}{\partial w_1} = \frac{\partial L}{\partial \hat{y}} \cdot \frac{\partial \hat{y}}{\partial a_1} \cdot \frac{\partial a_1}{\partial z_1} \cdot \frac{\partial z_1}{\partial w_1} \]
\[ \frac{\partial L}{\partial b_1} = \frac{\partial L}{\partial \hat{y}} \cdot \frac{\partial \hat{y}}{\partial a_1} \cdot \frac{\partial a_1}{\partial z_1} \cdot \frac{\partial z_1}{\partial b_1} \]
\[ \frac{\partial L}{\partial w_2} = \frac{\partial L}{\partial \hat{y}} \cdot \frac{\partial \hat{y}}{\partial a_2} \cdot \frac{\partial a_2}{\partial z_2} \cdot \frac{\partial z_2}{\partial w_2} \]
\[ \frac{\partial L}{\partial b_2} = \frac{\partial L}{\partial \hat{y}} \cdot \frac{\partial \hat{y}}{\partial a_2} \cdot \frac{\partial a_2}{\partial z_2} \cdot \frac{\partial z_2}{\partial b_2} \]
\[ \frac{\partial L}{\partial w_3} = \frac{\partial L}{\partial \hat{y}} \cdot \frac{\partial \hat{y}}{\partial w_3} \]
\[ \frac{\partial L}{\partial w_4} = \frac{\partial L}{\partial \hat{y}} \cdot \frac{\partial \hat{y}}{\partial w_4} \]
\[ \frac{\partial L}{\partial b_3} = \frac{\partial L}{\partial \hat{y}} \cdot \frac{\partial \hat{y}}{\partial b_3} \]

Let’s consider $b_1$. The chain rule equation for this parameter is:
让我们考虑 $b_1$。该参数的链式法则方程为：

\[ \frac{\partial L}{\partial b_1} = \frac{\partial L}{\partial \hat{y}} \cdot \frac{\partial \hat{y}}{\partial a_1} \cdot \frac{\partial a_1}{\partial z_1} \cdot \frac{\partial z_1}{\partial b_1} \]

Now, let’s compare this with chain rule equation of $w_1$.
现在，让我们将其与 $w_1$ 的链式法则方程进行比较。

\[ \frac{\partial L}{\partial w_1} = \frac{\partial L}{\partial \hat{y}} \cdot \frac{\partial \hat{y}}{\partial a_1} \cdot \frac{\partial a_1}{\partial z_1} \cdot \frac{\partial z_1}{\partial w_1} \]

We can observe that most of the chain is actually identical. Both equations contain $\frac{\partial L}{\partial \hat{y}}$. They also contain $\frac{\partial \hat{y}}{\partial a_1}$ and $\frac{\partial a_1}{\partial z_1}$. The only difference is the final partial derivative. For $w_1$, the final term is $\frac{\partial z_1}{\partial w_1}$ whereas for $b_1$, the final term is $\frac{\partial z_1}{\partial b_1}$.
我们可以观察到，大部分链式结构实际上是相同的。两个方程都包含 $\frac{\partial L}{\partial \hat{y}}$，也都包含 $\frac{\partial \hat{y}}{\partial a_1}$ 和 $\frac{\partial a_1}{\partial z_1}$。唯一的区别在于最后的偏导数。对于 $w_1$，最后一项是 $\frac{\partial z_1}{\partial w_1}$，而对于 $b_1$，最后一项是 $\frac{\partial z_1}{\partial b_1}$。

Now, if we calculate the gradient for $b_1$, then first three partial derivatives are computed twice, even though their values are exactly the same. The same pattern appears when we compute the gradients for $w_2,\; b_2,\; w_3,\; w_4,\; b_3$. In each case, many of the intermediate partial derivatives appear more than once.
现在，如果我们计算 $b_1$ 的梯度，前三个偏导数就会被计算两次，尽管它们的值完全相同。当我们计算 $w_2,\; b_2,\; w_3,\; w_4,\; b_3$ 的梯度时，也会出现同样的模式。在每种情况下，许多中间偏导数都会出现不止一次。

At this point, we generally have a question. Why are we recomputing the same intermediate partial derivatives which we’ve already computed once? If we continue this way, then we end up doing many unnecessary calculations. For our small neural network, this may not look like a big issue, but in real world we have neural networks with millions of parameters. If we proceed in the same way for huge neural networks, then it results in increasing the time and computational resources required to train the model, making the training process much more expensive.
此时，我们通常会产生一个疑问：为什么要重复计算那些已经计算过的中间偏导数呢？如果我们继续这样做，最终会进行许多不必要的计算。对于我们这个小型神经网络来说，这可能看起来不是什么大问题，但在现实世界中，神经网络拥有数百万个参数。如果我们对庞大的神经网络采用同样的方法，会导致训练模型所需的时间和计算资源增加，从而使训练过程变得极其昂贵。

What can we do here? The one idea that comes to our mind is, instead of recomputing the same values, why don’t we reuse those values whenever we need them. This is exactly the idea behind backpropagation. It doesn’t use a different way for computing gradients but relies entirely on the chain rule.
我们该怎么办呢？我们想到的一个办法是：与其重复计算相同的值，为什么不在需要时直接复用这些值呢？这正是反向传播背后的核心思想。它并没有使用不同的梯度计算方法，而是完全依赖于链式法则。

Now we know the idea behind backpropagation. Then we might think, when we compute the gradient for $w_1$, we store the intermediate partial derivatives $\frac{\partial L}{\partial \hat{y}}, \frac{\partial L}{\partial a_1}, \frac{\partial L}{\partial z_1}$. Next, while computing the gradient of $\frac{\partial L}{\partial b_1}$ we can simply reuse the previously computed values of $\frac{\partial L}{\partial \hat{y}}, \frac{\partial L}{\partial a_1}, \frac{\partial L}{\partial z_1}$. Similarly, we continue computing the gradients of the remaining parameters, storing intermediate partial derivatives whenever possible and reusing them whenever they are needed. What do you think about this approach?
现在我们了解了反向传播背后的思想。那么我们可能会想，当我们计算 $w_1$ 的梯度时，我们将中间偏导数 $\frac{\partial L}{\partial \hat{y}}, \frac{\partial L}{\partial a_1}, \frac{\partial L}{\partial z_1}$ 存储起来。接下来，在计算 $\frac{\partial L}{\partial b_1}$ 的梯度时，我们可以直接复用之前计算出的 $\frac{\partial L}{\partial \hat{y}}, \frac{\partial L}{\partial a_1}, \frac{\partial L}{\partial z_1}$ 的值。同样地，我们继续计算剩余参数的梯度，尽可能存储中间偏导数，并在需要时复用它们。你觉得这种方法怎么样？
