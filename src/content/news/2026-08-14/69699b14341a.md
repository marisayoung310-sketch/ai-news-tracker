### How to Enable Qi2 Charging on Your Samsung Galaxy S26
### 如何在三星 Galaxy S26 上启用 Qi2 充电

Frustratingly, there's no native Qi2 support on the Samsung Galaxy S26 lineup, so a magnetic phone case is the only way you can take advantage of Qi2 charging speeds and other benefits. I recommend several magnetic options in this guide.
令人沮丧的是，三星 Galaxy S26 系列并不原生支持 Qi2 标准，因此磁吸手机壳是你体验 Qi2 充电速度及其他优势的唯一途径。我在本指南中推荐了几款磁吸式手机壳。

### A Note on Magnetic Accessories
### 关于磁吸配件的注意事项

It's important to note that due to Samsung's camera bump design, some MagSafe or otherwise magnetic accessories do not play well with the S26 lineup. I didn't have trouble with a PopSockets grip, but I did struggle with most magnetic wallets. Power banks are also hit or miss. Anything square or rectangular might give you some issues due to the camera bump design. This is a frustrating carryover from the Samsung S25 line.
需要注意的是，由于三星的摄像头凸起设计，一些 MagSafe 或其他磁吸配件可能无法与 S26 系列完美兼容。我使用 PopSockets 气囊支架时没有遇到问题，但在使用大多数磁吸钱包时却遇到了困难。充电宝的表现也参差不齐。任何方形或长方形的配件都可能因为摄像头凸起设计而产生兼容性问题。这是从三星 S25 系列延续下来的一个令人沮丧的问题。

### How We Test Cases
### 我们如何测试手机壳

WIRED historically has not performed drop tests because we don't have infinite test units. Drop tests are notoriously difficult to control outside of a lab environment, and “dummy” phones aren't usually accurate representations of their working counterparts. However, I am working on standardizing a similar protocol. Remember that while a case or screen protector can offer some protection, neither can guarantee your screen won't shatter.
WIRED 过去并未进行跌落测试，因为我们没有无限的测试机。在实验室环境之外，跌落测试很难控制，且“模型机”通常无法准确代表真实手机的抗摔表现。不过，我正在努力制定一套类似的标准化测试流程。请记住，虽然手机壳或屏幕保护膜可以提供一定的保护，但两者都无法保证你的屏幕绝对不会碎裂。

For now, I'm testing phone cases by assessing fit, comfort, overall look and feel, and checking out the precision and responsiveness of any buttons or additional features.

### Best Clear Case: Spigen Ultra Hybrid MagFit
### 最佳透明手机壳：Spigen Ultra Hybrid MagFit

This clear case from Spigen is affordable and looks dope. There are 10 different styles to choose from, but I like the classic semi-clear teardown look best. The case is thinner than it is protective, but the printed-on graphics give you a little bit of texture to prevent your phone from feeling as slippery as it would without a case. My only minor complaint is that the volume buttons are a bit difficult to press—not at all impossible, but just not quite as readily responsive as the other cases I tried.
Spigen 的这款透明手机壳价格实惠，外观也很酷。它有 10 种不同款式可供选择，但我最喜欢经典的半透明拆解风格。这款手机壳的保护性一般，但胜在轻薄，且印花图案提供了一定的纹理感，防止手机像裸机那样滑手。我唯一的小抱怨是音量键稍微有点难按——虽然不是按不动，但确实不如我测试的其他手机壳那样灵敏。
