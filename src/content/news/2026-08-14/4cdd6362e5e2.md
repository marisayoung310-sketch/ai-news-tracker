# Introducing Gemini 3.7 Flash
# 隆重推出 Gemini 3.7 Flash

Today, we’re building on the progress of our widely used Flash series by introducing Gemini 3.7 Flash, our most intelligent workhorse model yet for coding and agents. This release comes just three weeks after Gemini 3.6 Flash, and is a direct result of developer feedback and algorithmic innovations that we look forward to bringing to future models. 3.7 Flash delivers substantial improvements across software engineering, knowledge work, and web development workflows — with an introductory price of half the original 3.6 Flash cost per million tokens.

今天，我们在广受欢迎的 Flash 系列基础上，隆重推出 Gemini 3.7 Flash。这是我们迄今为止在编程和智能体（Agent）领域最智能的“主力”模型。此次发布距离 Gemini 3.6 Flash 仅过去三周，它是开发者反馈与算法创新的直接成果，我们期待将这些成果应用到未来的模型中。3.7 Flash 在软件工程、知识工作和 Web 开发工作流方面实现了显著提升，且每百万 token 的入门价格仅为 3.6 Flash 的一半。

### Better intelligence for complex workflows
### 为复杂工作流提供更强的智能

3.7 Flash shows strong gains over 3.6 Flash in coding tasks like debugging and issue resolution. It also achieves higher first-pass code accuracy and has improved performance in generating production-ready code as seen in FrontierCode 1.1 Main (43.6% vs 34.4%) and DeepSWE v1.1 (65.3% vs 49.0%).

在调试和问题解决等编程任务中，3.7 Flash 相比 3.6 Flash 表现出显著优势。它实现了更高的首轮代码准确率，并在生成生产级代码方面表现更佳，在 FrontierCode 1.1 Main（43.6% 对 34.4%）和 DeepSWE v1.1（65.3% 对 49.0%）测试中均有体现。

In web development, 3.7 Flash generates more functional layouts and feature-complete apps in fewer prompts. For UI generation, the model shows high design adherence and parity based on a reference input, whether it’s a screenshot, an image, or a full design system. It outperforms 3.6 Flash on Arena.ai’s WebDev Arena with an Elo score of 1588 vs 1538.

在 Web 开发方面，3.7 Flash 能通过更少的提示词生成功能更完善的布局和应用。在 UI 生成方面，无论参考输入是截图、图像还是完整的视觉设计系统，该模型都能展现出极高的设计遵循度和还原度。在 Arena.ai 的 WebDev Arena 测试中，它以 1588 的 Elo 分数超越了 3.6 Flash（1538 分）。

For knowledge-dense fields like finance, law, and biosciences, 3.7 Flash delivers improved reasoning and accuracy. It significantly outperforms 3.6 Flash on the GDP.pdf benchmark (34.0% vs 22.0%), an eval for testing a model’s ability to process complex documents. It also surpasses 3.6 Flash in AutomationBench, demonstrating it can more effectively complete real-world business workflows (30.4% vs 17.0%).

在金融、法律和生物科学等知识密集型领域，3.7 Flash 提供了更强的推理能力和准确性。在用于测试模型处理复杂文档能力的 GDP.pdf 基准测试中，它显著优于 3.6 Flash（34.0% 对 22.0%）。它在 AutomationBench 测试中也超越了 3.6 Flash，证明其能更有效地完成现实世界的业务工作流（30.4% 对 17.0%）。

### Better developer experience and price
### 更佳的开发者体验与价格

Gemini 3.7 Flash delivers a noticeably improved developer experience over 3.6 Flash. It better adapts to roadblocks, clarifies intent when needed, and follows instructions with greater fidelity. It thinks more diligently, putting in more effort into multi-step planning and tool calls. A more disciplined execution means less manual oversight and fewer retries across engineering workflows.

相比 3.6 Flash，Gemini 3.7 Flash 为开发者带来了显著提升的体验。它能更好地应对障碍，在需要时澄清意图，并以更高的保真度执行指令。它思考得更加严谨，在多步规划和工具调用上投入了更多精力。更严谨的执行意味着在工程工作流中需要的人工监督更少，重试次数也更少。

3.7 Flash is available through the end of the year at an introductory price of $0.75/1M input tokens and $3.75/1M output tokens. This price combined with the enhanced model performance enables developers and customers to scale production-ready agents cost effectively. Early customer feedback is highlighting 3.7 Flash’s performance and precision, achieving results that are significantly better than 3.6 Flash at a low cost.

3.7 Flash 在今年年底前提供入门优惠价：输入 token 每百万 0.75 美元，输出 token 每百万 3.75 美元。这一价格结合增强的模型性能，使开发者和客户能够以经济高效的方式扩展生产级智能体。早期的客户反馈强调了 3.7 Flash 的性能和精度，以低成本实现了远超 3.6 Flash 的成果。

### Improving Gemini Spark with 3.7 Flash
### 用 3.7 Flash 升级 Gemini Spark

Gemini Spark, available to Google AI Pro and Ultra subscribers in over 160 countries, will be using Gemini 3.7 Flash starting today. We launched Spark at I/O as your personal AI agent that runs 24/7, taking action on your behalf while under your direction. This model update makes Spark more efficient for knowledge work with improved tool use for Google Workspace apps, delivering improved accuracy and output quality for complex, multi-skill workflows. With 3.7 Flash, Gemini Spark can turn ideas into action more efficiently by consolidating files, drafting emails, and updating status documents.

面向 160 多个国家/地区的 Google AI Pro 和 Ultra 订阅用户提供的 Gemini Spark，从今天起将使用 Gemini 3.7 Flash。我们在 I/O 大会上推出了 Spark，作为您的 24/7 全天候个人 AI 智能体，在您的指导下为您执行任务。此次模型更新使 Spark 在知识工作方面更高效，改进了对 Google Workspace 应用的工具使用，为复杂的多技能工作流提供了更高的准确性和输出质量。借助 3.7 Flash，Gemini Spark 可以通过整合文件、起草电子邮件和更新状态文档，更高效地将想法转化为行动。

### Built with safety in mind
### 以安全为核心构建

We continually work to improve the coverage and robustness of Frontier Safety safeguards. Gemini 3.7 Flash is shipping with updated safeguards against misuse in the domains of Chemical, Biological, Radiological, and Nuclear (CBRN) and cyber offense, while enabling beneficial use cases, in accordance with our approach to bioresilience and our cyber program.

我们持续致力于提高“前沿安全”（Frontier Safety）防护措施的覆盖范围和稳健性。Gemini 3.7 Flash 随附了针对化学、生物、放射性和核（CBRN）领域以及网络攻击滥用的更新防护措施，同时根据我们的生物韧性方法和网络安全计划，确保其在有益场景下的使用。
