# FarSky: Task-Aware Latent-Space Coupling for Generative Intra-Hour Solar Forecasting
# FarSky：用于生成式小时内太阳能预测的任务感知潜在空间耦合技术

**Abstract:** Accurate solar irradiance forecasting is essential for the reliable integration of photovoltaic power into modern electricity grids. All-sky imagers (ASI) provide high-resolution observations of clouds, making them well suited for intra-hour forecasting. Recent deep learning approaches have substantially improved forecast accuracy but are often limited by deterministic predictions and a reduced capability to anticipate ramp events.

**摘要：** 精确的太阳辐照度预测对于将光伏电力可靠地整合到现代电网中至关重要。全天空成像仪（ASI）提供了云层的高分辨率观测数据，使其非常适合小时内的短期预测。近期的深度学习方法虽然显著提高了预测精度，但往往受限于确定性预测，且在预测功率爬坡事件（ramp events）方面的能力较弱。

This work proposes FarSky, a generative forecasting framework that leverages latent-space coupling to learn task-aware representations of sky images. A multi-task autoencoder first learns a shared latent representation for image reconstruction and irradiance estimation. A latent diffusion model then generates future latent states conditioned on recent observations, from which irradiance forecasts are directly decoded. Probabilistic forecasts are inherently obtained through stochastic sampling.

本研究提出了 FarSky，这是一个利用潜在空间耦合来学习天空图像任务感知表示的生成式预测框架。首先，一个多任务自动编码器学习用于图像重建和辐照度估计的共享潜在表示。随后，一个潜在扩散模型根据近期观测结果生成未来的潜在状态，并从中直接解码出辐照度预测。通过随机采样，可以自然地获得概率预测结果。

The framework is developed using a multi-year ASI dataset acquired at the Plataforma Solar de Almería, Spain, and evaluated on two independent test datasets against persistence, state-of-the-art end-to-end, and generative forecasting approaches. FarSky achieves the best overall deterministic and probabilistic forecasting performance, improving forecast skill by up to 11 percentage points. Furthermore, it substantially improves ramp event detection over existing methods, achieving F1-scores above 60%. These results demonstrate the potential of combining generative models with task-aware latent-space coupling for solar forecasting.

该框架使用在西班牙阿尔梅里亚太阳能平台（Plataforma Solar de Almería）采集的多年 ASI 数据集进行开发，并在两个独立测试数据集上与持久性预测、最先进的端到端预测以及其他生成式预测方法进行了对比评估。FarSky 实现了最佳的整体确定性和概率预测性能，将预测技巧（forecast skill）提高了多达 11 个百分点。此外，它在爬坡事件检测方面较现有方法有显著提升，F1 分数超过了 60%。这些结果证明了将生成模型与任务感知潜在空间耦合相结合在太阳能预测领域的潜力。
