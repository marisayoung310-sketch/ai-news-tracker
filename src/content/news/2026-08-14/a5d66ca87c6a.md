# Terminal Symmetry as a Decision Resource: Statewise Refinement for Anytime Verified Construction
# 终端对称性作为决策资源：用于随时验证构建的状态级细化

**Abstract:** Many sequential construction tasks exhibit exact symmetry at completion while their execution remains directed and history-dependent. We develop a decision-resource view of terminal symmetry: process evidence supplies directionality, terminal correspondence transports that structure across equivalent outcomes, realized-state evidence refines its current decision relevance after transitions, and a fixed verifier certifies execution. This decomposition yields transport--refine--certify.

**摘要：** 许多序列构建任务在完成时表现出精确的对称性，但其执行过程仍然是有向且依赖于历史的。我们提出了一种关于终端对称性的决策资源视角：过程证据提供方向性，终端对应关系在等价结果之间传递该结构，已实现状态的证据在转换后细化其当前的决策相关性，并由固定的验证器对执行结果进行认证。这种分解产生了“传递—细化—认证”的流程。

\method{} instantiates the principle with an episode-fixed transported process structure, its state-restricted process rank, a state-dependent residual rank refreshed after accepted transitions, and an ordinal rank meet whose top-$k$ set is exactly the union of the two proposal prefixes. The meet provides a completion guarantee under prefix coverage and attains the tight worst-case verifier-query bound under the corresponding prefix information model; a two-state construction predicts a strict post-transition dynamic--static separation.

\method{} 通过一种情境固定的传递过程结构、其状态受限的过程秩、在接受转换后刷新的状态依赖残差秩，以及一个其 top-$k$ 集合恰好是两个提议前缀并集的序秩交集，实例化了这一原则。该交集在前缀覆盖下提供了完成保证，并在相应的前缀信息模型下达到了最紧的最坏情况验证器查询界限；一个双状态构建预测了转换后动态与静态之间的严格分离。

Across CAD assembly, Mini-Programs, and exact-fill packing, statewise refresh improves anytime AUC by up to $6.77$, $21.75$, and $8.68$ points, respectively. On 1,135 target-removal episodes from the official GRN OOD scenes, \method{} attains the lowest mean capped verifier cost at all three scales among the compared GRN and CDGS-style planners. The statewise signal also transfers across aggregation and scheduler organizations. Terminal symmetry thereby becomes a reusable decision resource for directed construction.

在 CAD 装配、小程序和精确填充打包任务中，状态级刷新将随时 AUC 分别提高了最多 $6.77$、$21.75$ 和 $8.68$ 个点。在来自官方 GRN OOD 场景的 1,135 个目标移除情境中，\method{} 在所有三个尺度上均达到了相比 GRN 和 CDGS 类规划器最低的平均上限验证器成本。这种状态级信号还可以在不同的聚合和调度组织之间进行迁移。因此，终端对称性成为了一种可用于有向构建的可重用决策资源。
