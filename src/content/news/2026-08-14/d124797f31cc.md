# Dual-Domain Cross-Modal Decoding for Clinical Text-Guided Medical Image Segmentation
# 用于临床文本引导医学图像分割的双域跨模态解码

**Abstract:** Clinical text can narrow down what to segment, but recent text-guided designs emphasize spatial alignment while overlooking frequency content that governs texture and boundaries. We propose Dual-Domain Cross-Modal Decoding (DD-CMD) for clinical text-guided pulmonary infection segmentation, integrating two complementary forms of language guidance during decoding.

**摘要：** 临床文本可以缩小分割目标的范围，但近期的文本引导设计多强调空间对齐，却忽视了决定纹理和边界的频率内容。我们提出了用于临床文本引导肺部感染分割的“双域跨模态解码”（DD-CMD），在解码过程中集成了两种互补的语言引导形式。

In the spatial domain, Text-Guided Spatial Cross-Attention (TGSA) aligns multi-scale visual tokens with text semantics and updates features through gated residual fusion. In the frequency domain, Spectral-Text Adaptive Modulation (STAM) applies a 2D DCT to compute learnable band-energy statistics and predicts text-conditioned FiLM parameters to recalibrate decoder channels for frequency-aware decoding.

在空间域中，文本引导空间交叉注意力（TGSA）将多尺度视觉标记与文本语义对齐，并通过门控残差融合更新特征。在频域中，谱-文本自适应调制（STAM）应用二维离散余弦变换（2D DCT）来计算可学习的频带能量统计信息，并预测文本条件下的 FiLM 参数，从而重新校准解码器通道，实现频率感知解码。

DD-CMD embeds TGSA and STAM into a coarse-to-fine decoder (7x7 to 56x56) and restores full-resolution masks using a lightweight two-stage refinement module. Experiments on QaTa-COV19 and MosMedData+ show that DD-CMD achieves 91.46% Dice / 84.26% mIoU and 81.95% Dice / 69.42% mIoU, respectively, with average gains of +1.96 Dice and +2.67 mIoU over the strongest prior baselines.

DD-CMD 将 TGSA 和 STAM 嵌入到一个从粗到细的解码器（7x7 到 56x56）中，并使用轻量级的两阶段细化模块恢复全分辨率掩码。在 QaTa-COV19 和 MosMedData+ 数据集上的实验表明，DD-CMD 分别达到了 91.46% Dice / 84.26% mIoU 和 81.95% Dice / 69.42% mIoU 的性能，相较于目前最强的基准模型，平均提升了 +1.96 Dice 和 +2.67 mIoU。
