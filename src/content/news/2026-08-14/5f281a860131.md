# The new Instagram logo is the perfect embodiment of AI slop
# Instagram 的新 Logo 是 AI 垃圾内容的完美化身

Today, Instagram unveiled a refresh of its wordmark, accompanied by the usual bland corporate platitudes these kinds of announcements are always packaged with. Head of Instagram Adam Mosseri called it “leaner and more modern, with references to the original and the simplicity and craft that’s always made it Instagram.”
今天，Instagram 发布了其焕新后的品牌标识（wordmark），并附带了此类公告中常见的平淡官话。Instagram 负责人 Adam Mosseri 称其“更精简、更现代，参考了最初的设计，并体现了 Instagram 一贯的简洁与匠心。”

Sure, Adam. Simplicity and craft are definitely what everyone thinks of when Instagram comes to mind. I might be more inclined to think ruthless algorithmic capture, relentless influencer hype cycles, and don’t forget mining every last gram of your personal data to be packaged and repackaged and sold. Potato, potahto.
得了吧，Adam。当人们想到 Instagram 时，想到的绝对不是什么“简洁与匠心”。我更倾向于想到无情的算法捕获、没完没了的网红炒作周期，别忘了还有挖掘你每一丁点个人数据，将其打包、重组后再转手卖掉。半斤八两罢了。

Still, it’s been 10 years since the wordmark was updated, and in the current fruit fly life cycle of frenzied tech rebirths, that’s almost staid. This kind of housekeeping for a brand isn’t something I would normally remark on. But I had two thoughts when I looked at the new text. The first is the one we probably all had, “Why does it say Instagzam?” The second was “Huh, it looks just like AI slop!”
不过，距离上一次更新品牌标识已经过去 10 年了。在如今科技界疯狂迭代、生命周期短如果蝇的背景下，这简直算得上“老派”。通常情况下，我不会对这种品牌层面的修修补补发表评论。但当我看到新字体时，脑海中闪过两个念头。第一个念头大概也是大家共有的：“为什么它看起来像 Instagzam？”第二个念头则是：“哈，这看起来简直就像 AI 生成的垃圾内容！”

I don’t mean I think the design team at Instagram used AI to make the logo. I doubt it, honestly; this is something machines are still terrible at. I mean, it literally looks like the almost-but-not-quite-real words that generative AI often outputs in images—the weird cutoff letters and spacing that come out of the noise.
我并不是说 Instagram 的设计团队用了 AI 来制作这个 Logo。老实说，我对此表示怀疑；这正是机器目前依然非常不擅长的地方。我的意思是，它看起来确实就像生成式 AI 在图像中经常输出的那种“似是而非”的文字——那种从噪点中产生的、奇怪的断字和间距。

The ‘s’ is supposed to be a callback to the old logo’s cursive, but it feels like the random number generator grabbed a different typeface. The slightly unsettling kerning around the ‘g.’ But mostly the whole “I am a robot, and I don’t know what an ‘r’ looks like” action. My guy, that’s a ‘z.’
那个“s”本意是呼应旧 Logo 的连笔字，但感觉就像是随机数生成器抓取了另一种字体。围绕“g”的字距调整让人感到些许不安。但最主要的是那种“我是机器人，我不知道‘r’长什么样”的既视感。老兄，那是个“z”啊。

As a functional piece of text, I don’t think the new wordmark does a great job. It has legibility and identity problems. But as a symbol of our new AI slop era? It might actually be unintentional genius.
作为功能性文字，我认为这个新标识做得并不好。它存在易读性和辨识度方面的问题。但作为我们这个 AI 垃圾内容时代的一个象征？它或许真算得上是无心插柳的杰作。
