# Bring your spreadsheet data to life with Sheets canvas
# 用 Sheets canvas 让你的电子表格数据“活”起来

A new Google Sheets feature, Sheets canvas transforms rows and columns into interactive dashboards, custom study trackers, seating charts, and more, all with a simple prompt.
Google 表格推出了一项名为“Sheets canvas”的新功能，它能通过简单的提示词，将枯燥的行和列转换为交互式仪表板、自定义学习追踪器、座位表等。

Google Sheets now features Sheets canvas, a tool that uses artificial intelligence to turn your data into interactive, visual mini-apps without any coding. You can simply describe your needs to Gemini, and it will build custom layouts like study trackers or seating charts that sync automatically with your original spreadsheet. To get started, open a sheet and click the Gemini icon to select "create canvas."
Google 表格现已加入 Sheets canvas 功能，这是一款利用人工智能将数据转化为交互式可视化“微型应用”的工具，无需任何编程知识。你只需向 Gemini 描述你的需求，它就能构建出如学习追踪器或座位表等自定义布局，并与原始电子表格自动同步。要开始使用，请打开一个表格，点击 Gemini 图标并选择“创建画布”（create canvas）。

Spreadsheets are a powerful tool for organizing and understanding data, but staring at hundreds of rows and columns can sometimes feel a bit overwhelming. Whether you’re planning a wedding, tracking school assignments, managing a fantasy football roster, or comparing side hustle income, you want to see what you need to know from your data quickly. To help get you there, we’re introducing Sheets canvas, a new feature built with Gemini that turns your Google Sheets spreadsheets into custom, interactive "mini-apps." With a simple natural language prompt, you can transform your data into a visual experience that’s easy to understand and even easier to use.
电子表格是组织和理解数据的强大工具，但盯着成百上千行的数据有时会让人感到不知所措。无论你是正在筹备婚礼、追踪学校作业、管理梦幻足球阵容，还是对比副业收入，你都希望快速从数据中获取关键信息。为了实现这一目标，我们推出了由 Gemini 驱动的 Sheets canvas，它能将你的 Google 表格转换为自定义的交互式“微型应用”。通过简单的自然语言提示，你可以将数据转化为易于理解且更易于使用的可视化体验。

**Visualize your data in new ways**
**以全新方式可视化你的数据**

Sheets canvas is a dynamic, read-write layer that sits directly on top of your spreadsheet data. This means that you can visually organize, edit, and navigate your data in whatever layout you imagine. Here is what makes it so powerful.
Sheets canvas 是一个直接覆盖在电子表格数据之上的动态读写层。这意味着你可以按照你想象的任何布局，直观地组织、编辑和浏览数据。以下是它如此强大的原因：

*   **No coding required:** No formulas or programming required. Just describe what you want — like "create a visual study tracker" — and Gemini builds it for you.
*   **无需编程：** 无需公式或编程。只需描述你的需求，例如“创建一个可视化学习追踪器”，Gemini 就会为你构建出来。

*   **Always in sync:** Whether you make changes to your canvas or to your sheet, updates are synced between the canvas and the original sheet in real time.
*   **始终同步：** 无论你是在画布上还是在表格中进行更改，画布与原始表格之间都会实时同步更新。

*   **Easy to adjust:** You can continue to polish and refine your Sheets canvas by prompting Gemini to make edits to layout, design, functionality, and more.
*   **易于调整：** 你可以通过提示 Gemini 修改布局、设计、功能等，持续打磨和优化你的 Sheets canvas。

*   **Fully collaborative:** Since it lives as a tab in Google Sheets, your canvas can be shared with anyone, just like a regular sheet.
*   **全面协作：** 由于它以标签页的形式存在于 Google 表格中，你的画布可以像普通表格一样与任何人共享。

**实际应用示例**

We designed Sheets canvas to be as flexible as your imagination. Here are a few examples to show how you can start using it today.
我们将 Sheets canvas 设计得像你的想象力一样灵活。以下是一些示例，展示了你今天就可以如何开始使用它：

1. **Stay on top of the school year:** For students, managing multiple subjects and deadlines is a full-time job. With Sheets canvas, you can turn a long list of assignments into an interactive study aid. Track progress, set reminders, and visualize the week ahead so you never miss a deadline.
1. **掌控学业：** 对于学生来说，管理多门课程和截止日期是一项繁重的工作。利用 Sheets canvas，你可以将长长的作业清单变成交互式学习助手。追踪进度、设置提醒并可视化未来一周的安排，确保你永远不会错过截止日期。

2. **Dominate your fantasy football league:** Take your fantasy team to the next level by using Sheets canvas to build the ultimate command center for your roster. Create a custom dashboard to track player performance, compare stats side by side, and clearly visualize your path to the championship — all powered by the data in your sheet.
2. **称霸梦幻足球联赛：** 使用 Sheets canvas 为你的阵容构建终极指挥中心，将你的梦幻球队提升到新高度。创建一个自定义仪表板来追踪球员表现、并排对比数据，并清晰地规划你的夺冠之路——所有这些都由你表格中的数据驱动。
