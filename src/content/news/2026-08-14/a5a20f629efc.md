### Roundtables: Inside the “Censorship-Industrial Complex” Idea Shaping US Policy
### 圆桌会议：深入探讨正在塑造美国政策的“审查-工业复合体”概念

The “censorship-industrial complex” is an idea that a network of government, tech, and research groups is collaborating to suppress conservative online speech. This was fodder for the right-wing information sphere for years—then it began making its way into US policy.
“审查-工业复合体”这一概念认为，政府、科技公司和研究机构组成的网络正在协同压制保守派的在线言论。多年来，这一直是右翼信息领域的热门话题，随后它开始进入美国政策制定的视野。

Watch a conversation exploring how it started, where it’s going, and what it means for the future of democracy and the internet. Speakers: Amy Nordrum, Executive Editor, Operations, and Eileen Guo, Senior Reporter, Features & Investigations. Recorded on August 13, 2026.
观看这场对话，探讨该概念的起源、发展方向，以及它对民主和互联网未来的意义。演讲嘉宾：运营执行编辑 Amy Nordrum 和专题与调查高级记者 Eileen Guo。录制于 2026 年 8 月 13 日。
