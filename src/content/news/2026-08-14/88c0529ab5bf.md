# EuroZig

**Hello Europe! Announcing EuroZig! EuroZig is a community of Zig enthusiasts. Our mission is to grow and support the European Zig community.**

你好，欧洲！隆重介绍 EuroZig！EuroZig 是一个 Zig 爱好者社区。我们的使命是发展并支持欧洲的 Zig 社区。

**Hosting Conferences and Workshops: We organize conferences, workshops, and Zig Days, and support others in organizing them. Our first conference will be Zigtoberfest 2027.**

举办会议与研讨会：我们负责组织各类会议、研讨会和“Zig 日”活动，并支持他人举办此类活动。我们的首场会议将是 2027 年的 Zigtoberfest。

**Supporting the Zig Ecosystem: We aim to support important projects in the Zig ecosystem by applying for grants.**

支持 Zig 生态系统：我们旨在通过申请资助，为 Zig 生态系统中的重要项目提供支持。

**Join the Mailing list! We’re currently registering as a German non-profit (e.V.) and are not yet ready to accept members. If you want to be amongst the first to join, you can attend our founding meeting or sign up to the mailing list here: [EuroZig](https://eurozig.org/)**

加入邮件列表！我们目前正在注册成为德国非营利组织（e.V.），尚未开放会员注册。如果您想成为首批加入的成员，可以参加我们的成立大会，或点击此处订阅邮件列表：[EuroZig](https://eurozig.org/)

**By signing up to the list, you are agreeing to us storing your name and e-mail address.**

订阅此列表即表示您同意我们存储您的姓名和电子邮件地址。
