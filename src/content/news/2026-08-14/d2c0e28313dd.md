# GeoUniPR: A Geometry-Consistent Unified Framework for Cross-Modal Place Recognition

**GeoUniPR：一种用于跨模态地点识别的几何一致性统一框架**

***

Cross-modal place recognition (CMPR) aims to identify the same location across heterogeneous sensing modalities, such as vision and LiDAR. Existing methods commonly bridge the modality gap using complex alignment modules, multi-stage training, or full fine-tuning of pretrained backbones.

跨模态地点识别（CMPR）旨在识别不同传感模态（如视觉和激光雷达）下的同一地点。现有的方法通常使用复杂的对齐模块、多阶段训练或对预训练骨干网络进行全量微调来弥合模态差异。

In this work, we revisit CMPR from the perspective of geometric consistency and propose GeoUniPR, a unified and concise geometry-consistent framework. GeoUniPR reduces cross-modal discrepancy at the representation level by projecting LiDAR point clouds into the camera perspective to construct Geometry-Consistent depth image views (DIV), which establish direct RGB-LiDAR correspondence.

在这项工作中，我们从几何一致性的角度重新审视了CMPR，并提出了GeoUniPR，这是一个统一且简洁的几何一致性框架。GeoUniPR通过将激光雷达点云投影到相机视角以构建几何一致的深度图像视图（DIV），从而在表征层面减少了跨模态差异，并建立了RGB与激光雷达之间的直接对应关系。

We further augment DIV with native LiDAR cues, including intensity and surface-normal information, yielding a multi-channel geometric representation that improves structural consistency. Based on this representation, GeoUniPR learns a unified embedding space using two modality-specific ViT-based encoders with identical architectures, trained through parameter-efficient adaptation without auxiliary alignment modules, multi-stage training, or full backbone fine-tuning.

我们进一步利用激光雷达的原始线索（包括强度和表面法线信息）增强了DIV，从而产生了一种能够提高结构一致性的多通道几何表征。基于这种表征，GeoUniPR使用两个架构相同的特定模态ViT编码器来学习统一的嵌入空间，并通过参数高效的适配进行训练，无需辅助对齐模块、多阶段训练或全量骨干网络微调。

In addition, we introduce Spatially-Consistent InfoNCE (SC-InfoNCE), a CMPR-specific contrastive objective that suppresses distance-induced false negatives under spatial continuity. Extensive experiments on KITTI and KITTI-360 demonstrate that GeoUniPR achieves state-of-the-art (SOTA) performance in both same-modal and cross-modal place recognition, with strong cross-dataset generalization.

此外，我们引入了空间一致性InfoNCE（SC-InfoNCE），这是一种针对CMPR的对比目标函数，旨在抑制空间连续性下由距离引起的假阴性。在KITTI和KITTI-360上的大量实验表明，GeoUniPR在同模态和跨模态地点识别中均达到了最先进（SOTA）的性能，并具有强大的跨数据集泛化能力。
