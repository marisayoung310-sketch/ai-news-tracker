# Federated Learning for Distributed CNC Tool Wear Prediction
# 用于分布式数控机床刀具磨损预测的联邦学习

**Abstract:** Tool wear prediction is an important task in CNC machining, where accurate monitoring of tool condition supports product quality and process reliability. Machine learning methods have shown potential for this task, but their use in industrial environments is limited by the distributed nature of machining data and by restrictions on data sharing between machines, sites, or organizations.

**摘要：** 刀具磨损预测是数控（CNC）加工中的一项重要任务，准确监测刀具状态有助于保障产品质量和工艺可靠性。机器学习方法在这一领域展现出了潜力，但其在工业环境中的应用受到加工数据分布式特性以及机器、站点或组织间数据共享限制的制约。

Federated learning offers a suitable framework for this setting by enabling collaborative model training without transferring raw operational data. This paper investigates federated learning for CNC tool wear prediction. Tool trajectories are distributed across simulated clients to represent a federated learning scenario.

联邦学习为这种环境提供了一个合适的框架，它能够在不传输原始操作数据的情况下实现协作式模型训练。本文研究了联邦学习在数控刀具磨损预测中的应用。研究通过将刀具轨迹分布在模拟客户端中，构建了一个联邦学习场景。

The federated models are compared against centralized references and local client baselines. Results show that federated learning achieves performance close to centralized learning and improves significantly over local client models. These findings indicate that federated learning can support collaborative tool wear prediction in distributed CNC manufacturing environments.

研究将联邦模型与集中式参考模型及本地客户端基准模型进行了对比。结果表明，联邦学习的性能接近于集中式学习，且显著优于本地客户端模型。这些发现表明，联邦学习能够支持分布式数控制造环境下的协作式刀具磨损预测。
