# OpenAI is losing its second executive this week
# OpenAI 本周流失第二位高管

OpenAI is losing its second executive this week. Chief revenue officer Denise Dresser announced she is leaving OpenAI. Two days ago, Brad Lightcap also said he’d be leaving.
OpenAI 本周流失了第二位高管。首席营收官 Denise Dresser 宣布她将离开 OpenAI。两天前，Brad Lightcap 也表示他将离职。

Another OpenAI executive is departing. Denise Dresser, who joined OpenAI as its chief revenue officer in December after serving as CEO of Slack, will be leaving in the “coming weeks” to “pursue other opportunities,” she said in a team note posted to LinkedIn. Dali Rajic, president and COO of Wiz, will be taking over the CRO role, OpenAI says.
又一位 OpenAI 高管即将离职。Denise Dresser 在担任 Slack 首席执行官后，于去年 12 月加入 OpenAI 担任首席营收官。她在 LinkedIn 上发布的团队备忘录中表示，她将在“未来几周内”离职，以“寻求其他机会”。OpenAI 表示，Wiz 的总裁兼首席运营官 Dali Rajic 将接任首席营收官（CRO）一职。

Dresser’s departure is just the latest in a string of major exits. Earlier this week, special projects lead and former COO Brad Lightcap announced he would be leaving. (Dresser had taken over some of Lightcap’s work when he moved to the special projects role.) Former AGI chief Fidji Simo and former CMO Kate Rouch also recently stepped down.
Dresser 的离职是近期一系列高管变动中的最新一起。本周早些时候，特别项目负责人兼前首席运营官 Brad Lightcap 宣布离职。（当 Lightcap 转任特别项目负责人时，Dresser 曾接手了他部分的工作。）前 AGI 负责人 Fidji Simo 和前首席营销官 Kate Rouch 最近也已卸任。

“We’re now at an inflection point: the next generation of models will change not just how work gets done but how companies are built and run,” OpenAI says. “Dali will build the revenue operating system needed to scale for this next phase.”
OpenAI 表示：“我们正处于一个转折点：下一代模型不仅将改变工作方式，还将改变公司的构建和运营方式。Dali 将构建下一阶段规模化发展所需的营收操作系统。”

Dresser is also leaving ahead of OpenAI’s IPO; the company announced in June that it had submitted a confidential S-1 filing to the Securities and Exchange Commission.
Dresser 的离职正值 OpenAI 即将进行首次公开募股（IPO）之际；该公司在 6 月份宣布，已向美国证券交易委员会提交了机密的 S-1 注册文件。
