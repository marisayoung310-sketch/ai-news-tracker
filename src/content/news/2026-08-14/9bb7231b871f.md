# SegPAR: Class-Centric Decision-Based Sparse Attack for Semantic Segmentation
# SegPAR：面向语义分割的以类别为中心的决策型稀疏攻击

**Abstract:** Despite the practical relevance of sparse decision-based black-box threats, they have received limited attention in semantic segmentation. To bridge this gap, we adapt the most representative decision-based black-box sparse attacks from the classification domain to serve as baselines, establishing a rigorous benchmark for this underexplored setting.

**摘要：** 尽管基于决策的稀疏黑盒威胁具有实际意义，但它们在语义分割领域受到的关注却十分有限。为了弥补这一差距，我们将分类领域中最具代表性的基于决策的黑盒稀疏攻击方法进行适配，作为基准模型，从而为这一尚未被充分探索的领域建立了一个严谨的基准。

In this context, we demonstrate that one of the existing methods suffers from severe query inefficiency due to its image-centric pixel accumulation, which rapidly exhausts query budgets across the vast image space. To overcome this, we propose SegPAR, a novel decision-based framework that shifts to a class-centric exploration paradigm.

在此背景下，我们证明了现有的一种方法由于采用以图像为中心的像素累积方式，导致查询效率极低，在广阔的图像空间中迅速耗尽了查询预算。为了克服这一问题，我们提出了 SegPAR，这是一个全新的基于决策的框架，它将探索范式转向了以类别为中心。

Furthermore, to eliminate the misleading feedback generated by standard decision rewards during pixel accumulation, we introduce a novel discrepancy reward. Extensive experiments show that SegPAR significantly outperforms black-box baselines in sparsity efficiency and MIoU reduction, while remaining competitive with white-box sparse attacks. Code is available at [this https URL](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.11285).

此外，为了消除在像素累积过程中由标准决策奖励产生的误导性反馈，我们引入了一种新颖的差异奖励（discrepancy reward）。大量实验表明，SegPAR 在稀疏效率和 MIoU（平均交并比）降低方面显著优于黑盒基准模型，同时在与白盒稀疏攻击的对比中也保持了竞争力。代码已在 [此处](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.11285) 提供。
