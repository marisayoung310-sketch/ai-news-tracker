# Weightless Fine-Tuning: Personalizing LLMs via Logit-Space Transport
# 无权重微调：通过 Logit 空间迁移实现大语言模型个性化

Supervised fine-tuning (SFT) is a standard approach for adapting LLMs to a target distribution, but in settings such as personalization, where each author requires separate weight access, optimization, storage, and retraining, its costs become prohibitive.
监督微调（SFT）是将大语言模型（LLM）适配到目标分布的标准方法。然而，在个性化等场景中，每个作者都需要独立的权重访问、优化、存储和再训练，这使得其成本变得极其高昂。

We propose Weightless Fine-Tuning (WFT), a training-free decoding-time method that approximates the distributional effect of SFT without weight updates.
我们提出了“无权重微调”（Weightless Fine-Tuning, WFT），这是一种无需训练的解码时方法，它能够在不更新权重的情况下近似实现 SFT 的分布效果。

WFT computes supervised residuals on an author's training sequence and transports them to the current prompt through a cross-prefix transport operator estimated from dropout-induced cross-covariance.
WFT 通过计算作者训练序列上的监督残差，并利用从 Dropout 诱导的交叉协方差中估计出的“跨前缀迁移算子”（cross-prefix transport operator），将这些残差迁移到当前的提示词（prompt）中。

The operator captures how a perturbation at one context propagates to predictions at another, replacing gradient-based parameter updates with logit-space corrections.
该算子能够捕捉一个上下文中的扰动如何传播到另一个上下文的预测中，从而用 Logit 空间的修正替代了基于梯度的参数更新。

On three LaMP personalization benchmarks, WFT achieves the best average performance across datasets, matches or exceeds SFT on individual tasks, and outperforms other lightweight baselines on average.
在三个 LaMP 个性化基准测试中，WFT 在所有数据集上实现了最佳的平均性能，在单个任务上达到或超过了 SFT 的表现，并且在平均水平上优于其他轻量级基准方法。

In a budget-controlled comparison, WFT approaches SFT performance using less than 7% of the effective computation.
在预算受控的对比实验中，WFT 仅使用了不到 7% 的有效计算量，就达到了接近 SFT 的性能。

Logit-level analysis shows a cosine similarity of 0.875 between the logit shifts induced by WFT and SFT over 95% of the next-token probability mass, suggesting that WFT captures the distributional effect of supervised adaptation without modifying model weights.
Logit 级别的分析显示，在 95% 的下一个 Token 概率质量上，WFT 和 SFT 诱导的 Logit 偏移之间的余弦相似度达到 0.875，这表明 WFT 在不修改模型权重的情况下，成功捕捉到了监督适配的分布效应。
