# 6 Best Dehumidifiers to Fight Mold and Muggy Summers (2026)
# 2026年抗击霉菌与潮湿夏季的6款最佳除湿机

Mold is no joke. Neither is a mid-Atlantic summer. Whether you live in humid-heat New York or humid-winter Oregon, the best dehumidifiers can bring your humidity to livable levels and help mitigate the most obvious ill effects of damp climates.
霉菌绝非儿戏，大西洋中部的夏季也同样令人难受。无论你住在湿热的纽约还是潮湿多雨的俄勒冈，最好的除湿机都能将室内湿度调节到宜居水平，并帮助减轻潮湿气候带来的最明显的负面影响。

What ill effects? In hot weather, humid climates can make it difficult for your body (or your air conditioner) to cool down appropriately and attain homeostasis, potentially worsening cardiac issues. Closer to normal room temperature, oh dear lord, there's the mold—the ancient foe of asthmatics, hypochondriacs, record collectors, and clotheshorses.
会有什么负面影响？在炎热天气下，潮湿的气候会使你的身体（或空调）难以有效降温并维持内稳态，这可能会加重心脏问题。而在接近常温的环境下，天哪，还有霉菌——它是哮喘患者、疑病症患者、唱片收藏家和衣物爱好者的宿敌。

WIRED reviewer Lisa Wood Shapiro discovered the salutary effects of dehumidifiers after life in a damp Maine cabin where her air quality monitors often registered 80 percent humidity, not to mention a stint on dehumidifier TikTok. Reviewer Matthew Korfhage lives in Portland, Oregon, where black mold is a genre of small talk.
《连线》（WIRED）评测员丽莎·伍德·夏皮罗（Lisa Wood Shapiro）在缅因州一间潮湿的小木屋生活后，发现了除湿机的益处，当时她的空气质量监测仪经常显示湿度高达80%，更不用说她还曾沉迷于TikTok上的除湿机内容。另一位评测员马修·科夫哈格（Matthew Korfhage）住在俄勒冈州的波特兰，在那里，黑霉菌是人们茶余饭后的谈资。

After testing, these are the best models out of the many we tested, from our top-pick Midea 20-Pint Cube ($190) to our best portable version for travel or small spaces, the Eva-Dry Renewable Mini Dehumidifier E-333 ($25).
经过测试，以下是我们从众多机型中挑选出的最佳产品，包括我们的首选机型——美的（Midea）20品脱Cube除湿机（190美元），以及最适合旅行或小空间的便携式机型——Eva-Dry可再生迷你除湿机E-333（25美元）。

### 综合最佳除湿机：美的（Midea）20品脱Cube除湿机

The Midea Cube 20 is a powerful dehumidifier that combines a great number of desirable attributes held in balance. It has a large 3-gallon tank without being overly large, and its bucket can nest for easy storage when you're not using it. And it's quiet, noted WIRED tester Lisa Wood Shapiro, humming along at a near-imperceptible 20 decibels on its lowest fan setting and at around 40 decibels at its highest. This is as quiet as the quietest space heaters.
美的Cube 20是一款功能强大的除湿机，它在众多理想特性之间取得了极佳的平衡。它拥有一个3加仑的大水箱，但机身并不臃肿，且水桶可以嵌套收纳，方便在不使用时存放。根据我们的体验，它配备了直观的控制面板和易于配对的智能应用程序（尽管网上也有用户反馈遇到过问题）。《连线》评测员丽莎·伍德·夏皮罗指出，它非常安静，在最低风速档位下，运行声音仅为几乎无法察觉的20分贝，最高档位约为40分贝。这与最安静的取暖器一样安静。

大多数浴室都很小，甚至有些局促，空间太小以至于无法舒适地放置一台笨重的除湿机。然而，浴室每天会产生大量湿气，使其成为霉菌的聚集地。这款Breezome除湿机是一个出色且极具个性的解决方案。

But for a dorm, a bathroom, or a closet, it's an excellent and unobtrusive solution that uses very little energy compared to larger devices.
