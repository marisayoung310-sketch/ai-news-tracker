# Impact of Dataset Composition on Embedded Real-Time UAV Wildfire Detection Using Compact YOLO Models
# 数据集构成对基于紧凑型 YOLO 模型的嵌入式实时无人机森林火灾检测的影响

**Abstract:** The development of vision-based wildfire detection systems for unmanned aerial vehicles is constrained by the limited availability of diverse real-world training images. This paper investigates the impact of dataset composition on embedded real-time UAV wildfire detection using compact YOLO models as a controlled validation family.

**摘要：** 基于视觉的无人机（UAV）森林火灾检测系统的开发，受到多样化真实世界训练图像资源匮乏的限制。本文以紧凑型 YOLO 模型作为受控验证系列，研究了数据集构成对嵌入式实时无人机森林火灾检测的影响。

Four training configurations were evaluated: real non-augmented, real augmented, hybrid non-augmented, and hybrid augmented, where the hybrid sets combine real wildfire images with AI-generated samples. The objective is to determine whether synthetic data mixing and image augmentation improve practical detection performance under resource-constrained deployment conditions.

研究评估了四种训练配置：真实非增强、真实增强、混合非增强以及混合增强，其中混合数据集将真实火灾图像与人工智能生成的样本相结合。研究旨在确定在资源受限的部署条件下，合成数据混合和图像增强是否能提升实际检测性能。

Experimental results show that the best overall operating point was obtained with the real non-augmented dataset, which achieved the strongest balance between recall and mean average precision for UAV-based wildfire detection. The results also show that neither hybridization with synthetic data nor augmentation produced a better final deployment choice.

实验结果表明，使用真实非增强数据集获得了最佳的整体运行点，该配置在基于无人机的火灾检测中实现了召回率与平均精度均值（mAP）之间的最佳平衡。结果还显示，无论是与合成数据进行混合，还是进行图像增强，都未能产生更好的最终部署选择。

These findings suggest that, for embedded UAV wildfire detection, dataset realism and domain alignment are more valuable than increasing training set size through synthetic expansion.

这些发现表明，对于嵌入式无人机火灾检测而言，数据集的真实性和领域对齐比通过合成扩展来增加训练集规模更具价值。
