# Neural Operators for Immersed-Boundary Soft Swimmers Locomotion
# 用于浸入边界法软体游动生物运动的神经算子

**Abstract:** High-fidelity immersed-boundary simulation resolves the coupled motion of a deforming swimmer and its surrounding flow, but the resulting cost limits repeated evaluations for engineering design, parameter studies, and control. 

**摘要：** 高保真浸入边界模拟（Immersed-boundary simulation）能够解析变形游动生物与其周围流体之间的耦合运动，但由此产生的计算成本限制了其在工程设计、参数研究和控制方面的重复评估应用。

We develop neural-operator surrogates for temporal prediction of the hydrodynamic fields generated by planar and volumetric eel swimmers. The surrogates are trained on regular-grid fields exported from adaptive fluid--structure simulations and are conditioned on swimmer geometry and Reynolds number. 

我们开发了神经算子代理模型，用于对平面和三维鳗鱼状游动生物产生的流体动力场进行时间预测。这些代理模型基于从自适应流固耦合模拟中导出的规则网格场进行训练，并以游动生物的几何形状和雷诺数作为条件输入。

The planar model jointly predicts two velocity components, scalar vorticity, and pressure. On five held-out high-Reynolds-number trajectories, its full-domain global relative L^2 error is 3.51 %. 

平面模型联合预测了两个速度分量、标量涡量和压力。在五个未参与训练的高雷诺数轨迹测试中，其全域全局相对 L^2 误差为 3.51%。

The volumetric formulation uses three target-specific models with a common multichannel input: one model predicts three-dimensional velocity, one predicts vorticity, and one predicts pressure. Their full-domain global relative L^2 errors on five held-out within-range trajectories are 3.44 %, 5.58 %, and 19.2 %. 

三维模型采用了三个针对特定目标的模型，并共享一个多通道输入：其中一个模型预测三维速度，一个预测涡量，另一个预测压力。在五个未参与训练的范围内轨迹测试中，它们的全域全局相对 L^2 误差分别为 3.44%、5.58% 和 19.2%。

Together, the results demonstrate the feasibility of field-resolved neural surrogates for moving-boundary swimmer flows while identifying pressure accuracy and physical consistency as priorities for further development.

总之，这些结果证明了使用场解析神经代理模型处理移动边界游动生物流的可行性，同时也指出压力精度和物理一致性是未来研究的重点方向。
