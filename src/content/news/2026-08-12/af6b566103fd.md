# AMIE, our research medical AI system, demonstrates real-time clinical video consultation capabilities in a first-of-its-kind study.
# AMIE，我们的研究型医疗 AI 系统，在首个同类研究中展示了实时临床视频咨询能力。

AMIE, our research medical AI system, demonstrates real-time clinical video consultation capabilities in a first-of-its-kind study. Anil Palepu Research Lead Aug 11, 2026.
AMIE，我们的研究型医疗 AI 系统，在首个同类研究中展示了实时临床视频咨询能力。Anil Palepu，研究负责人，2026 年 8 月 11 日。

When you visit a doctor, a consultation extends far beyond words — a physician notices a cough, observes gait, or registers visible signs of discomfort. Today, Google Research and Google DeepMind are advancing AMIE, our research medical AI system, toward real-time clinical video consultations, with a first-of-its-kind demonstration of expert-level AI capabilities in this setting.
当你去看医生时，问诊的内容远不止言语交流——医生会注意到咳嗽、观察步态，或捕捉到身体不适的可见迹象。今天，Google Research 和 Google DeepMind 正在推动我们的研究型医疗 AI 系统 AMIE 向实时临床视频咨询迈进，并首次展示了该场景下专家级的 AI 能力。

Built on Gemini and Project Astra using a multi-agent architecture, AMIE now interprets visual and auditory cues, guides virtual physical exams, and reasons diagnostically in real time.
基于 Gemini 和 Project Astra 并采用多智能体架构，AMIE 现在能够解读视觉和听觉线索，引导虚拟体格检查，并进行实时的诊断推理。

In a randomized study using simulated consultations with patient actors and a group of primary care physicians, clinical evaluators assessed AMIE favorably across core clinical competencies, including history-taking thoroughness, diagnostic accuracy, management appropriateness and communication quality. Patient actors also preferred the video experience over text chat.
在一项使用模拟患者和一组初级保健医生进行模拟问诊的随机研究中，临床评估人员对 AMIE 在核心临床能力方面的表现给予了积极评价，包括病史采集的详尽程度、诊断准确性、治疗方案的适当性以及沟通质量。模拟患者也表示，相比文字聊天，他们更倾向于视频问诊体验。

AMIE remains a research system and more research is needed before responsible real-world clinical deployment, but it offers an exciting glimpse into the future of health AI. Read more on the Google Research blog.
AMIE 目前仍处于研究阶段，在进行负责任的现实临床部署之前还需要更多的研究，但它为我们提供了一个令人兴奋的视角，让我们得以一窥医疗 AI 的未来。阅读更多内容请访问 Google Research 博客。
