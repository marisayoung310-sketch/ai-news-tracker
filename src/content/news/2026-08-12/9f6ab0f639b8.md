# Evolving Safety Landscape of Multi-modal Large Language Models: A Survey of Emerging Threats and Safeguards
# 多模态大语言模型不断演变的安全格局：新兴威胁与防御机制综述

**Abstract:** Multi-modal large language models (MLLMs) integrate heterogeneous modalities through modality alignment and fusion, enabling stronger understanding and reasoning. However, this architectural shift reshapes the safety landscape of machine learning. Increased model complexity and cross-modal interactions give rise to novel threats, including compromised modality integration, modality misalignment, and fused safety risks, reflecting shifts in threat modeling beyond uni-modal assumptions.

**摘要：** 多模态大语言模型（MLLMs）通过模态对齐与融合技术整合了异构模态，从而具备了更强的理解与推理能力。然而，这种架构上的转变重塑了机器学习的安全格局。模型复杂度的提升以及跨模态交互引发了诸多新型威胁，包括模态整合受损、模态对齐偏差以及融合安全风险，这些都反映出威胁建模已超越了单一模态的假设范畴。

These shifts, in turn, impose new constraints on safety solutions not captured by existing frameworks rooted in uni-modal learning. Motivated by these challenges, this survey provides a systematic analysis of the evolving safety landscape of MLLMs. We first propose a multimodal grounded taxonomy of safety threats and analyze shifts in threat models, covering adversarial attacks, data poisoning, jailbreaks, and hallucinations.

这些转变反过来对安全解决方案提出了新的约束，而这些约束是现有基于单模态学习的框架所无法涵盖的。受这些挑战的驱动，本综述对 MLLMs 不断演变的安全格局进行了系统性分析。我们首先提出了一个基于多模态基础的安全威胁分类体系，并分析了威胁模型中的变化，涵盖了对抗性攻击、数据投毒、越狱攻击以及幻觉问题。

We then summarize updated safety assumptions and organize recent advances in MLLM safety strategies accordingly. Finally, we discuss open challenges and future directions to inform the development of more principled and scalable safety mechanisms for multimodal systems.

随后，我们总结了更新后的安全假设，并据此梳理了 MLLM 安全策略的最新进展。最后，我们探讨了当前面临的挑战与未来研究方向，旨在为多模态系统开发更具原则性且可扩展的安全机制提供参考。
