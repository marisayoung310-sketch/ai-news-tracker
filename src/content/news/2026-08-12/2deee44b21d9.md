# Accel closes oversubscribed $550M India fund within weeks, 19 months after its last
# Accel 在上一支基金募集 19 个月后，于数周内完成 5.5 亿美元超额认购的印度基金募集

Accel has closed a new $550 million India fund, less than two years after raising its previous India-focused vehicle, as part of a coordinated $3.5 billion global fundraising effort. The new India fund was oversubscribed and closed within weeks, people familiar with the matter told TechCrunch.
Accel 已完成一支新的 5.5 亿美元印度基金募集。这是其 35 亿美元全球募资计划的一部分，距离其上一支专注于印度的基金募集还不到两年。据知情人士向 TechCrunch 透露，这支新的印度基金获得了超额认购，并在数周内即告完成。

Accel still has more than 55% of its previous $650 million India fund available for investment, the people said, underlining that the latest raise came despite ample capital remaining in its earlier vehicle. The fundraising comes as Accel bets that India’s next startup wave will be driven not only by AI, but also by consumer internet, fintech, and advanced manufacturing.
知情人士表示，Accel 此前 6.5 亿美元的印度基金仍有超过 55% 的资金可供投资，这凸显了尽管其早期基金仍有充足资本，但公司依然进行了最新一轮募资。此次募资表明，Accel 看好印度下一波创业浪潮，认为其驱动力不仅来自人工智能，还将涵盖消费互联网、金融科技和先进制造业。

The firm believes that artificial intelligence is becoming a horizontal technology that underpins each of those sectors rather than a standalone investment category. “There is a significant amount of money available in the market for early-stage investing in the categories we have always invested in — AI, consumer, fintech, and now advanced manufacturing, and deep tech,” Shekhar Kirani, a partner at Accel, told TechCrunch.
该公司认为，人工智能正成为支撑上述各行业的横向技术，而非一个独立的投资类别。Accel 合伙人 Shekhar Kirani 对 TechCrunch 表示：“市场上仍有大量资金可用于我们一直投资的领域——人工智能、消费、金融科技，以及现在的先进制造业和深科技。”

“We will continue to invest, looking for the best of the best local winners, where we can make them into global successes.” Accel is expected to begin deploying capital from the new fund in 2027, Kirani said. Until then, the firm will continue investing from its previous India fund, he added while declining to disclose how much remains.
“我们将继续投资，寻找最优秀的本土赢家，并将他们打造为全球性的成功企业。”Kirani 表示，Accel 预计将于 2027 年开始从这支新基金中部署资本。他补充说，在此之前，公司将继续使用上一支印度基金进行投资，但拒绝透露剩余金额。

Accel’s renewed commitment comes as global investors debate whether India can produce globally competitive AI startups after the country largely missed the first wave of foundation model companies. Accel sees India’s opportunity specifically in building AI applications, infrastructure, and software aimed at enterprise and consumer use cases.
在印度基本错过了第一波基础模型公司浪潮后，全球投资者正在争论印度是否能产生具有全球竞争力的 AI 初创公司，而 Accel 的再次加码正是在这一背景下进行的。Accel 特别看好印度在构建面向企业和消费者用例的 AI 应用、基础设施和软件方面的机会。

“The early movers have been on the LLM [large language model] side… but there is a significant opportunity in the application layer,” Prayank Swaroop, a partner at Accel, said. Accel expects Indian startups to build AI-powered applications and enterprise software on top of existing models rather than competing with OpenAI or Anthropic.
Accel 合伙人 Prayank Swaroop 表示：“早期的先行者主要集中在 LLM（大语言模型）领域……但在应用层存在巨大的机会。”Accel 预计印度初创公司将基于现有模型构建 AI 驱动的应用和企业软件，而不是与 OpenAI 或 Anthropic 直接竞争。

Swaroop told TechCrunch that Indian startups are increasingly combining AI with the country’s existing engineering talent and services expertise to solve enterprise problems, particularly in sectors where human oversight remains critical. Kirani echoed Swaroop and mentioned RapidClaims, an Accel-backed startup that automates medical coding for U.S. healthcare providers, as an example.
Swaroop 向 TechCrunch 表示，印度初创公司正越来越多地将 AI 与该国现有的工程人才和专业服务经验相结合，以解决企业问题，特别是在仍需人工监督的关键领域。Kirani 对此表示赞同，并以 Accel 支持的初创公司 RapidClaims 为例，该公司为美国医疗服务提供商提供医疗编码自动化服务。

The startup combines AI with domain expertise to deliver coding accuracy of about 95%, targeting a market that has traditionally relied on outsourced human labor in India and the Philippines. Barath Shankar Subramanian, a partner at Accel, said the firm’s optimism is also being driven by the rapid adoption of AI among Indian consumers and businesses, creating a growing domestic market for AI-native products alongside globally focused software companies.
该初创公司将 AI 与领域专业知识相结合，实现了约 95% 的编码准确率，其目标市场传统上依赖印度和菲律宾的外包劳动力。Accel 合伙人 Barath Shankar Subramanian 表示，该公司的乐观态度也源于印度消费者和企业对 AI 的快速采用，这在面向全球的软件公司之外，为 AI 原生产品创造了一个不断增长的国内市场。

The trend is already visible across leading AI companies. OpenAI and Anthropic have both identified India as their largest market outside the U.S., while AI coding platform Cursor recently said India has become one of its fastest-growing developer markets and its largest market for power users.
这一趋势在领先的 AI 公司中已经显现。OpenAI 和 Anthropic 都已将印度视为其在美国以外最大的市场，而 AI 编程平台 Cursor 最近也表示，印度已成为其增长最快的开发者市场之一，也是其超级用户最多的市场。

Accel’s fundraising comes as several global venture firms are renewing their focus on India despite a broader slowdown in venture capital. Peak XV Partners, the former Sequoia Capital India business, recently raised $1.3 billion across new India and Southeast Asia-focused funds, while General Catalyst has committed to deploying $5 billion in India over the next five years. Lightspeed Venture Partners is also said to be exploring a new $300–$350 million India-focused fund.
尽管风险投资整体放缓，但多家全球风投机构正重新聚焦印度，Accel 的此次募资正是在此背景下进行的。前红杉印度业务 Peak XV Partners 最近为其新的印度和东南亚基金募集了 13 亿美元，而 General Catalyst 已承诺在未来五年内向印度投入 50 亿美元。据称，Lightspeed Venture Partners 也在探索一支 3 亿至 3.5 亿美元的新印度基金。

Kirani said the renewed interest reflects a shift in the quality and ambition of Indian entrepreneurs. “Compared to several years back,” he said, “the quality of ideas and quality of founders are significantly better than what we have ever seen.”
Kirani 表示，这种重新燃起的兴趣反映了印度企业家在素质和抱负上的转变。“与几年前相比，”他说，“创意和创始人的素质都比我们以往见过的要好得多。”

The new India fund was one of four funds Accel raised simultaneously for the first time, alongside dedicated U.S. and Europe funds and a $1.35 billion growth vehicle. The growth fund, Accel said, can back breakout companies emerging from any of its regional funds, including India, allowing the firm to continue investing from inception through IPO and beyond.
这支新的印度基金是 Accel 首次同时募集的四支基金之一，其他三支分别是专门针对美国和欧洲的基金，以及一支 13.5 亿美元的成长型基金。Accel 表示，成长型基金可以支持从其任何区域基金（包括印度）中涌现出的突破性公司，使公司能够从初创阶段一直投资到 IPO 及以后。

Kirani told TechCrunch that the coordinated fundraising was driven by investor preference to evaluate Accel’s global platform in a single process rather than through separate regional fundraises. Accel’s investment philosophy, Kirani said, remains rooted in backing founders early rather than chasing later-stage trends.
Kirani 向 TechCrunch 解释说，这种协同募资是出于投资者的偏好，他们希望通过单一流程评估 Accel 的全球平台，而不是通过分散的区域募资。Kirani 表示，Accel 的投资理念依然植根于尽早支持创始人，而不是追逐后期趋势。

Accel writes the first institutional check in roughly 80% of the companies it backs, a strategy that has helped it invest early in companies including Flipkart, Swiggy, Freshworks, and Zetwerk.
Accel 在其支持的约 80% 的公司中担任首家机构投资者，这一策略帮助其早期投资了 Flipkart、Swiggy、Freshworks 和 Zetwerk 等公司。
