# Data-Driven Fire-Zone Segmentation for Improved Short-Term Wildfire Prediction
# 基于数据驱动的火灾区域分割技术：提升短期森林火灾预测精度

**Abstract:** Wildfire prediction models typically discretize study areas into uniform grids, ignoring the heterogeneous spatial distribution of ignitions. We challenge this paradigm by showing that how data is discretized matters more than which model is used.
**摘要：** 森林火灾预测模型通常将研究区域离散化为均匀的网格，却忽略了火源分布的空间异质性。我们对这一范式提出了挑战，并指出：数据离散化的方式比所使用的模型本身更为重要。

We propose an unsupervised fire-zone segmentation algorithm combining watershed detection with K-means clustering to define prediction units directly from historical fire patterns.
我们提出了一种无监督的火灾区域分割算法，该算法结合了分水岭检测（watershed detection）与 K-means 聚类，能够直接根据历史火灾模式来定义预测单元。

Experiments across six French departments and six forecasting models show that fire-zone segmentation consistently outperforms grid-based approaches, with mean IoU improvements of +3--6% depending on spatial scale.
在法国六个省份及六种预测模型上的实验表明，火灾区域分割方法始终优于基于网格的方法，根据空间尺度的不同，平均交并比（IoU）提升了 3% 至 6%。

The method is computationally lightweight (<10s per configuration) and fully parallelizable. Our results demonstrate that optimizing spatial discretization yields significant, reproducible performance gains for short-term wildfire forecasting.
该方法计算开销极小（每种配置耗时小于 10 秒）且完全可并行化。我们的研究结果证明，优化空间离散化能够为短期森林火灾预测带来显著且可复现的性能提升。
