# NeuroPilot: An Agent-Driven Smart Pipeline for Processing, Quality Control, and Managing Neuroimages
# NeuroPilot：一种用于神经影像处理、质量控制与管理的智能代理驱动流水线

**Abstract:** Transforming raw neuroimage archives into analysis-ready derivatives relies on three brittle stages: data standardization, modality-specific preprocessing, and quality control (QC). While individual neuroimaging tools are well developed, their orchestration requires project-specific scripts, environment-adaptive tuning, and labor-intensive manual QC. 

**摘要：** 将原始神经影像档案转化为可分析的衍生数据，依赖于三个脆弱的阶段：数据标准化、特定模态的预处理以及质量控制（QC）。虽然现有的神经影像工具已相当成熟，但它们的协同工作需要针对特定项目的脚本、环境自适应调整以及劳动密集型的人工质量控制。

To address this, we introduce NeuroPilot, a multi-agent system that digitalizes the expertise of neuroimage processing, QC, and data management into three LLM-invocable skills: dcm2bids-skill, neuroimage-pre-skill, and qc-agent-skill. The LLM-driven agent autonomously orchestrates workflows, generalizing various infrastructure settings into a single configuration to achieve the highest scalability. 

为了解决这些问题，我们推出了 NeuroPilot，这是一个多代理系统，它将神经影像处理、质量控制和数据管理的专业知识数字化为三种可由大语言模型（LLM）调用的技能：dcm2bids-skill、neuroimage-pre-skill 和 qc-agent-skill。该大模型驱动的代理能够自主编排工作流，将各种基础设施设置概括为单一配置，从而实现最高的可扩展性。

Demonstrating the system's generalizability, we deployed NeuroPilot across 17 cohorts (>123,000 subjects) spanning infant to aging populations and multiple MRI modalities (structural, diffusion, functional). In practice, after standardizing data via the dcm2bids-skill, the agent dynamically routes datasets to the optimal neuroimage-pre-skill based on available modalities and cohort traits (e.g., dispatching T1w and fMRI data to fMRIPrep, or selecting specialized pipelines for infant cohorts). 

为了展示该系统的通用性，我们将 NeuroPilot 部署在涵盖婴儿到老年人群以及多种 MRI 模态（结构、弥散、功能）的 17 个队列（超过 123,000 名受试者）中。在实践中，通过 dcm2bids-skill 标准化数据后，代理会根据可用模态和队列特征，动态地将数据集路由到最优的 neuroimage-pre-skill（例如，将 T1w 和 fMRI 数据分发给 fMRIPrep，或为婴儿队列选择专门的流水线）。

The qc-agent-skill then drives an evidence-based, semi-automated QC via a 3-D browser dashboard, utilizing a multi-tiered verification system to optimize failed cases and escalate complex issues for supervisor inspection. Quantitatively, our QC agent screened 558 production subjects, validating its automated flags against FreeSurfer's topology-defect metrics. The infant processing pipeline achieved a 100% (201/201) completion rate on QC-validated inputs. 

随后，qc-agent-skill 通过 3D 浏览器仪表板驱动基于证据的半自动化质量控制，利用多级验证系统来优化失败案例，并将复杂问题升级以供主管检查。在定量分析方面，我们的质量控制代理筛选了 558 名生产环境受试者，并根据 FreeSurfer 的拓扑缺陷指标验证了其自动标记的准确性。婴儿处理流水线在经质量控制验证的输入上实现了 100% (201/201) 的完成率。

Importantly, NeuroPilot compresses the traditional 2--3 month timeline for training staff and processing complete datasets into a single week. NeuroPilot is deployed in this [https URL](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2608.07541).

重要的是，NeuroPilot 将传统上需要 2-3 个月的人员培训和完整数据集处理周期压缩到了一周之内。NeuroPilot 已部署于此 [链接](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2608.07541)。
