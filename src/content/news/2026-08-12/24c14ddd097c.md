# PhysAttNet: Enhancing Predictive Performance in Industrial and Astrophysical Time Series via Physics-Informed Attention
# PhysAttNet：通过物理信息注意力机制提升工业与天体物理时间序列的预测性能

**Abstract:** Accurate and robust time series forecasting is essential in many applications involving physical processes, such as manufacturing monitoring and astrophysical event detection. In these settings, predictive models must remain reliable under noise, variability, and measurement uncertainty while capturing temporally localized structures corresponding to physically meaningful events.

**摘要：** 在涉及物理过程的许多应用中（如制造监控和天体物理事件检测），准确且稳健的时间序列预测至关重要。在这些场景下，预测模型必须在面对噪声、变异性和测量不确定性时保持可靠，同时能够捕捉到与物理意义事件相对应的局部时间结构。

Convolutional neural networks (CNNs) are widely used for such tasks due to their computational efficiency and strong representational capacity. However, their learned temporal representations often exhibit unstable or physically inconsistent attention patterns, reducing robustness, generalization, and interpretability.

卷积神经网络（CNN）因其计算效率高和强大的表征能力，被广泛应用于此类任务。然而，它们所学习到的时间表征往往表现出不稳定或物理上不一致的注意力模式，从而降低了模型的稳健性、泛化能力和可解释性。

This paper introduces PhysAttNet, a physics-informed attention framework for time series forecasting. PhysAttNet augments a lightweight CNN forecaster with an attention head guided by domain-informed regularization reflecting the structural properties of physical signals.

本文介绍了 PhysAttNet，这是一个用于时间序列预测的物理信息注意力框架。PhysAttNet 通过一个注意力头增强了轻量级 CNN 预测器，该注意力头由反映物理信号结构特性的领域知识正则化所引导。

Specifically, three complementary constraints are imposed during training: an alignment regularization that encourages attention to follow smooth, peak-centered temporal structures derived from the input signal, a smoothness regularization that enforces continuous temporal evolution, and a sparsity regularization that promotes selective focus on informative intervals.

具体而言，训练过程中施加了三个互补的约束：一种对齐正则化，鼓励注意力机制遵循从输入信号中导出的平滑、以峰值为中心的时间结构；一种平滑正则化，强制实现连续的时间演化；以及一种稀疏正则化，促进对信息丰富区间的选择性关注。

These differentiable regularization terms introduce physics-guided inductive bias without requiring annotated explanations or manual supervision. Experiments on two distinct applications, namely predicting cutting forces during milling and forecasting flares in blazar time series, demonstrate that PhysAttNet improves forecasting accuracy, generalization, and prediction performance on structurally important events.

这些可微分的正则化项引入了物理引导的归纳偏置，而无需标注解释或人工监督。在两个不同应用场景（即铣削过程中的切削力预测和耀变体时间序列中的耀斑预测）上的实验表明，PhysAttNet 提高了预测准确性、泛化能力以及对结构重要事件的预测性能。
