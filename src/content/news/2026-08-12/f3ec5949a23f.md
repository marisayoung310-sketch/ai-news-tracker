# Made by Google 2026: all the Pixel news and announcements
# Made by Google 2026：所有 Pixel 新品发布与资讯汇总

Google is gearing up to reveal a bunch of new Pixel devices on August 12th. A series of leaks leading up to the event suggest that the Pixel 11 lineup will come in an array of colors, with signs pointing to a built-in light coming to Pro models. Teasers from Google also indicate that it will show off a next-gen Pixel Fold alongside a Pixel Watch 5 in four different finishes. There might be a new Pixel Tag tracker as well.

谷歌正准备在 8 月 12 日发布一系列全新的 Pixel 设备。活动前夕的一系列泄露消息显示，Pixel 11 系列将提供多种配色，且有迹象表明 Pro 机型将内置灯光功能。谷歌的预热信息还显示，公司将展示新一代 Pixel Fold 折叠屏手机，以及拥有四种不同外观的 Pixel Watch 5。此外，可能还会有一款全新的 Pixel Tag 追踪器亮相。

Google may take the wraps off the new devices well ahead of its Made by Google livestream. On Google’s website, the countdown for preorders ends on August 12th at 10AM ET — eight hours before its event at 6PM ET. Comedian Trevor Noah will host this year’s show, featuring notable guests like NBA star Stephen Curry, podcaster Alex Cooper, Indian cricketer Shubman Gill, and Japanese actress Ayami Nakajo. We’ll be live blogging the event, so stay tuned!

谷歌可能会在“Made by Google”直播活动开始前就提前揭晓这些新设备。在谷歌官网上，预购倒计时将于美国东部时间 8 月 12 日上午 10 点结束，这比下午 6 点的发布会提前了八个小时。今年的活动将由喜剧演员特雷弗·诺亚（Trevor Noah）主持，嘉宾阵容包括 NBA 球星斯蒂芬·库里（Stephen Curry）、播客主亚历克斯·库珀（Alex Cooper）、印度板球运动员舒布曼·吉尔（Shubman Gill）以及日本女演员中条彩未（Ayami Nakajo）。我们将对活动进行实时图文直播，敬请关注！

Last year’s Made by Google event for the Pixel 10 resembled a late night talk show, complete with Jimmy Fallon as host, and it aired an hour after the phones were revealed. Spec dives and executive appearances were replaced with celebrity cameos and awkward skits, to the confusion of many diehard Pixel fans. But it served its purpose in reaching a broader audience: Last year’s stream racked up 8.4 million viewers on YouTube; the launch the year before only managed 1.4 million.

去年的 Pixel 10 “Made by Google”发布会更像是一场深夜脱口秀，由吉米·法伦（Jimmy Fallon）担任主持，且在手机正式发布一小时后才播出。深度规格解析和高管演讲被明星客串和尴尬的短剧所取代，这让许多 Pixel 的忠实粉丝感到困惑。但这种做法确实达到了扩大受众的目的：去年发布会的 YouTube 直播观看量达到了 840 万次，而前一年的发布会仅有 140 万次。

This year, Google has only escalated matters. For tomorrow evening’s livestream, Fallon has been replaced by Trevor Noah, who will lead a “stacked” lineup of celebrities including basketball star Stephen Curry, podcaster Alex Cooper, and DJ Peggy Gou. What the event won’t have is many surprises. While the launch event kicks off at 6PM ET, the Google Store’s preorder countdown is set to end eight hours earlier, presumably when the phones will be revealed in full.

今年，谷歌进一步加大了这种投入。在明晚的直播中，主持人换成了特雷弗·诺亚，他将带领包括篮球巨星斯蒂芬·库里、播客主亚历克斯·库珀和 DJ Peggy Gou 在内的“豪华”明星阵容。不过，这场活动可能不会有太多惊喜。虽然发布会定于美国东部时间下午 6 点开始，但谷歌商店的预购倒计时提前了八个小时结束，届时手机的详细信息很可能已经全部公开。

It’s that time of year: On Wednesday, Google is set to host its annual Made by Google hardware launch event for Pixel gadgets. Google itself has already teased new slab-style and foldable Pixel smartphones and an updated watch, but leaks also indicate that the company could announce a new color for familiar earbuds and perhaps a brand new AirTag-like item tracker.

一年一度的时刻又到了：周三，谷歌将举办年度“Made by Google”硬件发布会，推出 Pixel 系列产品。谷歌官方已经预热了新款直板和折叠屏 Pixel 手机以及更新后的手表，但泄露消息显示，该公司还可能宣布为现有的耳机产品增加新配色，甚至可能推出一款类似 AirTag 的全新物品追踪器。

However, unusually, Google’s event is taking place in the evening: It kicks off on Wednesday at 6PM ET / 3PM PT instead of in the early afternoon or mid-morning, like many other tech events. And this time it has a celebrity host in Trevor Noah.

然而，不同寻常的是，谷歌的活动安排在晚上进行：活动将于周三美国东部时间下午 6 点（太平洋时间下午 3 点）开始，而不是像许多其他科技活动那样安排在清晨或午后。而且这一次，活动还邀请了特雷弗·诺亚担任明星主持。
