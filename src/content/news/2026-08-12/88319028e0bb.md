# CODS: Iterative Bellman-Residual Data Selection for Reusable Offline Reinforcement Learning
# CODS：用于可重用离线强化学习的迭代贝尔曼残差数据选择

**Abstract:** Offline reinforcement learning repeatedly trains policies from a fixed transition pool, making redundant data costly across seeds and hyperparameters, while naive subsampling can remove rare transitions needed for long-horizon credit assignment. 

**摘要：** 离线强化学习通常需要从固定的转换池中反复训练策略，这使得冗余数据在不同的随机种子和超参数设置下变得昂贵；而简单的子采样可能会移除长程信用分配（long-horizon credit assignment）所需的稀有转换数据。

We introduce CODS, a critic-guided selector that alternates between fitting an algorithm-matched critic and acquiring high-residual transitions before freezing a reusable subset. Unlike prioritized replay, CODS produces a static artifact; unlike one-shot residual selection, it refreshes scores as the critic changes. 

我们引入了 CODS，这是一种由评论家（critic）引导的选择器。它在拟合算法匹配的评论家与获取高残差转换之间交替进行，最终冻结一个可重用的子集。与优先经验回放（prioritized replay）不同，CODS 生成的是一个静态工件；与一次性残差选择不同，它会随着评论家的变化不断刷新评分。

At a 10% budget, CODS retains 96.6% of eligible-pool performance across 20 valid D4RL task--algorithm cells. It exceeds ReDOR and OPER on 19/20 cells and every other subset baseline on 20/20; all six subset advantages remain significant under predeclared hierarchical inference with Holm correction. 

在 10% 的预算下，CODS 在 20 个有效的 D4RL 任务-算法单元中保留了 96.6% 的原始池性能。它在 19/20 的单元中表现优于 ReDOR 和 OPER，并在 20/20 的单元中超过了所有其他子集基准；在采用 Holm 校正的预声明分层推理下，所有六种子集优势均保持显著。

Holding total selector updates fixed, five acquisition rounds improve four representative cells by 11.23 points over one round and saturate thereafter. Equal-pass and equal-hour evaluations clarify that reuse, rather than a single-run speedup, creates the compute advantage. 

在保持总选择器更新次数不变的情况下，五轮获取过程比一轮获取过程在四个代表性单元上提升了 11.23 个点，之后趋于饱和。等次（equal-pass）和等时（equal-hour）评估表明，计算优势源于数据的可重用性，而非单次运行的加速。

Mechanism and corruption interventions expose both useful sparse-reward enrichment and sensitivity to outliers. Finally, a whole-trace extension retains 95.4% of pooled ALFWorld success and 96.5% of pooled GSM8K exact match. CODS is therefore a reusable selection procedure, not a formal coreset guarantee.

机制和损坏干预实验揭示了该方法在稀疏奖励增强方面的有效性，同时也暴露了其对异常值的敏感性。最后，全轨迹扩展版本保留了 95.4% 的 ALFWorld 池化成功率和 96.5% 的 GSM8K 池化精确匹配率。因此，CODS 是一种可重用的选择程序，而非正式的核心集（coreset）保证。
