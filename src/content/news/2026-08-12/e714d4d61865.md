# Auditing Medical Vision-Language Models on Chest Radiographs: Estimating Reference Agreement Across Institutions
# 审计胸部X光片医学视觉语言模型：跨机构参考一致性评估

**Abstract:** Vision-language models return structured chest-radiograph findings through interfaces exposing no confidence score, so a receiving institution cannot read off how far to trust an individual judgment. Whether agreement with an institution's reference standard transfers across sites, findings, prediction directions and question formats is largely unmeasured.

**摘要：** 视觉语言模型通过不提供置信度分数的接口返回结构化的胸部X光片检查结果，因此接收机构无法直接判断在多大程度上可以信任某项具体的判断。目前，模型与机构参考标准之间的一致性是否能在不同站点、检查结果、预测方向和问题格式之间迁移，在很大程度上尚未得到衡量。

We evaluated three generative vision-language models on three institutional chest-radiograph corpora and six findings under two elicitation protocols, comprising more than 345,000 finding-level predictions, and estimated finding-by-direction reference agreement at a receiving institution from a small budget of local labels. Estimation strategies were then stress-tested under repeated strict institution-held-out evaluation.

我们评估了三种生成式视觉语言模型在三个机构胸部X光片语料库和六种检查结果上的表现，采用了两种诱导协议，涵盖超过345,000个检查结果级别的预测，并根据少量的本地标签估算了接收机构的“检查结果-预测方向”参考一致性。随后，在重复且严格的“机构留出”（institution-held-out）评估下，对这些估算策略进行了压力测试。

Under evaluation excluding the receiving institution from development entirely, adaptive selection among the seven estimators that design admits did not improve on simple fixed alternatives: it achieved a mean Brier score of 0.1083, against 0.0853 for always using a Beta-Binomial empirical-Bayes estimator and 0.0855 for a target-only logistic model. Those two differ by 0.0003, less than this family's own sensitivity to a change of solver version, and each leads in about half the settings, so no default can be recommended.

在完全排除接收机构参与开发的情况下，从设计允许的七种估算器中进行自适应选择，并未比简单的固定替代方案有所改进：其平均 Brier 分数为 0.1083，而始终使用 Beta-二项式经验贝叶斯估算器的分数为 0.0853，仅使用目标数据的逻辑回归模型分数为 0.0855。这两者相差 0.0003，小于该模型族对求解器版本变更的敏感度，且两者在约一半的设置中表现领先，因此无法推荐任何默认方案。

Their advantage over estimators pooling across institutions was concentrated at one site and not confirmatory once clustered by institution, and a plug-in empirical-Bayes posterior-predictive count interval at a nominal 95% level covered 87.0%, less at the hardest institution. Reference agreement therefore has to be re-evaluated per site and per interface; these results concern agreement with institutional labels, not clinical correctness.

它们相对于跨机构汇总估算器的优势仅集中在一个站点，且在按机构聚类后无法得到证实。此外，名义水平为 95% 的插件式经验贝叶斯后验预测计数区间仅覆盖了 87.0% 的数据，在最困难的机构中覆盖率更低。因此，参考一致性必须针对每个站点和每个接口进行重新评估；这些结果关注的是与机构标签的一致性，而非临床准确性。
