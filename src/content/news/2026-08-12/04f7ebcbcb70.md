# Grok Bot Just Dropped and I Had to Try It
# Grok Bot 刚刚发布，我必须得试一试

Grok Bot just dropped and I had to try it. It's basically a team of bots on your computer that do the stuff you'd normally hand to a teammate — LinkedIn, GitHub, email, the lot. In this video I spin up a coding bot on my Playwright movies repo, create a LinkedIn bot (and yes… it actually posted for me), close some old GitHub issues, poke through the plugins, and add email + X bots. I'm properly blown away.

Grok Bot 刚刚发布，我必须得试一试。它本质上是你电脑上的一支机器人团队，能够处理你通常会交给同事去做的那些琐事——比如 LinkedIn、GitHub、电子邮件等等。在视频中，我为我的 Playwright 电影仓库启动了一个编程机器人，创建了一个 LinkedIn 机器人（没错……它真的帮我发布了内容），关闭了一些旧的 GitHub Issue，探索了各种插件，并添加了电子邮件和 X 机器人。我真的被震撼到了。

If you've been drowning in context switching between apps, this is worth a look. Since the video I have read some emails and sent replies. I hate emails so this feels great for me. I also set up a content creating workflow so writing this blog post here — which uses my add-content skill — goes ahead and produces a post on my site, then adds it to Dev.to with a canonical URL, then creates a LinkedIn post and an X post. At least it should do. This is the start of it. See you at the end of the workflow....

如果你一直深陷于在不同应用程序之间切换的泥潭中，那么这个工具非常值得一看。自录制视频以来，我已经用它阅读并回复了一些邮件。我讨厌处理邮件，所以这对我来说感觉太棒了。我还设置了一个内容创作工作流，这样我写这篇博客文章时——它会调用我的“添加内容”技能——就能自动在我的网站上生成文章，然后将其发布到 Dev.to 并附上规范链接（canonical URL），接着还会自动创建 LinkedIn 和 X 的帖子。至少它应该能做到这些。这仅仅是个开始，工作流结束时再见……

In the meantime, seriously, this can do so much and this was just me on the free trial, yet I am already sold. I think it can take so much off my plate, meaning I can do more and then easily share more cool stuff with the rest of the world. I now have a team of bots who work for me.

与此同时，说真的，它的功能非常强大，而且我目前只是在用免费试用版，就已经被彻底折服了。我认为它能帮我分担大量工作，这意味着我可以做更多事情，并轻松地与世界分享更多酷炫的内容。我现在拥有了一支为我工作的机器人团队。

The crazy part is how easy it is to onboard and connect with other providers. With one word, like just naming the bot, it gives me a list of things I might want to do and I just click along. So if I don't really know what I want to do, it guides me, and that is cool. Best user experience ever. It's gonna change how we do things and that is exciting. Look forward to hearing other views on it.

最疯狂的部分在于，它的上手难度极低，且能轻松连接其他服务商。只需一个词，比如给机器人起个名字，它就会列出我可能想做的事情，我只需点击即可。所以，即使我不知道自己想做什么，它也会引导我，这真的很酷。这是我体验过最好的用户体验。它将改变我们的工作方式，这令人兴奋。期待听到大家对它的看法。
