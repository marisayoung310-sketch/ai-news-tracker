# Should I Raise Venture Capital or Bootstrap?
# 我应该寻求风险投资还是自力更生（Bootstrap）？

The framing that causes the most damage is treating this as a financing decision. It is not. Taking venture money is choosing a category of business: one that must attempt to become very large very quickly, and produce an exit that returns a fund. Everything else follows from that.
最容易造成误导的观点，是把这仅仅看作一个融资决策。其实不然。接受风险投资意味着你选择了一种特定的商业模式：这种模式要求企业必须尝试在极短时间内迅速做大，并实现能够回馈基金的退出（上市或被收购）。其他一切决策都源于这一前提。

What venture capital actually requires: A venture fund needs a small number of investments to return the entire fund. That structure means a company growing steadily and profitably at twenty percent a year is a failure in a venture portfolio, even though it is an excellent business by any other measure. Once you take the money, that expectation becomes your operating constraint. Decisions that would be obviously correct for a durable business, such as slowing hiring to protect margin, become hard to defend.
风险投资的真实要求是：风险投资基金需要通过少数几个投资项目来收回整个基金的成本。这种结构意味着，一家每年以 20% 速度稳健盈利增长的公司，在风险投资组合中会被视为失败，尽管从任何其他标准来看，它都是一家优秀的企业。一旦你拿了钱，这种预期就成了你的经营约束。对于一家追求长久发展的企业来说，诸如“放缓招聘以保护利润率”这类显而易见的正确决策，在风投背景下将变得难以辩护。

Questions that actually decide it: Can this business plausibly become very large? Not "could it grow", but could it realistically reach a scale where a meaningful ownership stake is worth a large multiple of the money invested. Most good businesses cannot, and that is not a criticism of them.
真正决定决策的问题是：这家企业是否真的有可能变得非常大？不是问“它能否增长”，而是问它能否现实地达到这样一种规模：即持有的股份价值能达到投资额的数倍。大多数优秀的企业都做不到这一点，但这并不是对它们的批评。

Does speed determine who wins here? In some markets the first company to reach scale takes most of the value, usually where network effects or heavy switching costs exist. In those markets, refusing capital while a competitor takes it is a decision to lose. In most markets this dynamic does not apply, and speed bought with dilution buys nothing durable.
速度是否决定了胜负？在某些市场中，率先达到规模效应的公司会占据大部分价值，这通常存在于具有网络效应或高转换成本的领域。在这些市场中，当竞争对手接受融资时，你拒绝融资就等于选择了失败。但在大多数市场中，这种动态并不适用，通过稀释股权换来的速度，并不能带来持久的竞争优势。

Do you want to run this for a decade and then sell it? Venture capital has an implicit ending. The fund needs liquidity. If you want to own a profitable business indefinitely, you want a fundamentally different structure and should say so before, not after.
你想经营十年然后卖掉它吗？风险投资有一个隐含的终点。基金需要流动性。如果你想无限期地拥有一家盈利的企业，你需要的是一种根本不同的结构，并且应该在融资前（而不是融资后）就明确这一点。

Is capital genuinely your constraint? Founders often raise to solve problems money does not solve. If you have not found product-market fit, capital lets you be wrong more expensively and for longer. If distribution is unproven, funding scales an unproven motion.
资本真的是你的瓶颈吗？创始人往往通过融资来解决那些钱无法解决的问题。如果你还没有找到产品与市场的契合点（Product-Market Fit），资本只会让你以更昂贵的代价、更长的时间去犯错。如果分销渠道尚未验证，融资只会放大一个未经证实的商业动作。

### The honest tradeoff / 坦诚的权衡

| | Venture backed / 风投支持 | Bootstrapped / 自力更生 |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Speed** | Buy time and headcount immediately | Constrained by cash generated |
| **速度** | 立即购买时间和人力 | 受限于现金流 |
| **Ownership** | Diluted, and the dilution compounds each round | Retained |
| **所有权** | 被稀释，且每轮融资稀释叠加 | 保留所有权 |
| **Control** | Shared, with board seats and preferences | Yours |
| **控制权** | 共享（包含董事会席位和优先权） | 完全属于你 |
| **Acceptable outcome** | Very large or nothing | Wide range, including modest and excellent |
| **可接受的结果** | 要么做大，要么归零 | 范围广泛，包括小而美或卓越 |
| **Failure mode** | Scaling something that does not work | Being outrun in a market where speed wins |
| **失败模式** | 盲目扩张无效的业务 | 在讲究速度的市场中被超越 |

The middle path most people forget: The choice is often presented as binary and is not. Revenue-based financing, customer prepayment, a bank facility, a strategic partner, or simply raising a smaller amount from angels who do not need fund-returning outcomes all exist. They are less discussed because they generate no press, not because they are worse.
大多数人忽略的中间道路：人们常把选择呈现为二元对立，但事实并非如此。基于收入的融资、客户预付款、银行信贷、战略合作伙伴，或者仅仅是从不需要“回馈基金”回报的天使投资人那里筹集少量资金，这些都是可行的路径。它们讨论得少，是因为它们无法制造新闻噱头，而不是因为它们更差。

The clarifying question is this: if you knew you could never raise, what would you build? If the answer is a business you would be glad to own, you have learned that venture capital is optional for you, which is a much stronger position from which to consider it.
最能理清思路的问题是：如果你知道自己永远无法融资，你会建立什么样的公司？如果答案是你乐于拥有的业务，那么你已经明白风险投资对你而言是可选的，而这正是你考虑是否融资时所处的最强有力的位置。
