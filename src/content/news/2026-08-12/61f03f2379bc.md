# Google’s Gemini app surges to 1 billion users
# Google Gemini 应用用户量激增至 10 亿

In a significant milestone for Google, CEO Sundar Pichai announced via X that the Gemini app has become one of the company’s fastest-growing products, recently surpassing 1 billion monthly active users.
对于 Google 而言，这是一个重要的里程碑。首席执行官桑达尔·皮查伊（Sundar Pichai）通过 X 平台宣布，Gemini 应用已成为该公司增长最快的产品之一，近期月活跃用户数已突破 10 亿大关。

Pichai also noted it was the 14th Google product to hit the 1 billion mark. With this rapid growth, Gemini is keeping pace with OpenAI’s ChatGPT, which hit 1 billion monthly active users back in June.
皮查伊还指出，这是 Google 第 14 款达到 10 亿用户里程碑的产品。凭借这种快速增长，Gemini 正与 OpenAI 的 ChatGPT 并驾齐驱，后者早在今年 6 月就达到了 10 亿月活跃用户。

The company has been steadily integrating Gemini across its products, from Search and Workspace to Android and its standalone app. Google’s AI Mode in Search, in particular, has achieved a lot of success, with over 1 billion monthly active users globally. But today’s figure refers specifically to the Gemini app, and does not include AI users from other channels.
该公司一直在稳步将 Gemini 集成到其各项产品中，从搜索（Search）和办公套件（Workspace）到 Android 系统及其独立应用。特别是 Google 搜索中的 AI 模式取得了巨大成功，全球月活跃用户超过 10 亿。但今天公布的数据专门指代 Gemini 应用，并不包含来自其他渠道的 AI 用户。

The company also continued rolling out new models and features, including Gemini 3.5 Flash, which Google says is designed to improve coding and autonomous AI-agent tasks. Google also shared numbers of how people are actually using the chatbot, with 63% of Gemini users talking directly to the assistant using the voice feature.
该公司还持续推出新的模型和功能，包括 Gemini 3.5 Flash。Google 表示，该模型旨在提升编程能力和自主 AI 代理任务的处理效率。Google 还分享了用户实际使用该聊天机器人的数据：63% 的 Gemini 用户通过语音功能直接与助手进行交流。

Plus, Gemini now generates more than 150 million images every day, according to Google. And Gemini’s growth isn’t limited to Google’s own products. The company says it now has more than 100 million active users on iOS.
此外，据 Google 称，Gemini 现在每天生成超过 1.5 亿张图像。Gemini 的增长并不局限于 Google 自家的产品，该公司表示，目前其在 iOS 平台上的活跃用户已超过 1 亿。

The milestone comes right after its Q2 2026 earnings call, where the company celebrated having over 950 million monthly users, with daily active users tripling this past year. The announcement also comes ahead of its Made by Google event, where it’s expected to have more Gemini-powered features launching across Pixel devices.
这一里程碑是在 2026 年第二季度财报电话会议之后达成的，当时该公司庆祝其月活跃用户超过 9.5 亿，且日活跃用户在过去一年中翻了三倍。此次公告发布于“Made by Google”活动之前，预计届时将有更多由 Gemini 驱动的功能在 Pixel 设备上推出。
