# MVMD: A Multi-View Approach for Enhanced Mirror Detection
# MVMD：一种用于增强镜面检测的多视图方法

**Abstract:** In 3D reconstruction, mirrors introduce significant challenges by creating distorted and fragmented spaces, resulting in inaccurate and unreliable 3D models. As 3D reconstruction typically relies on multi-view images to capture different perspectives of a scene, detecting and labeling mirrors in multi-view images before reconstruction can effectively address this issue.
**摘要：** 在三维重建中，镜面会产生扭曲和碎片化的空间，从而导致三维模型不准确且不可靠，这带来了巨大的挑战。由于三维重建通常依赖多视图图像来捕捉场景的不同视角，因此在重建前对多视图图像中的镜面进行检测和标注，可以有效解决这一问题。

However, existing methods focus solely on single-image detection, overlooking the rich information provided by multi-view setups. To overcome this limitation, we propose MVMD, a novel Multi-View Mirror Detection method, along with the first database specifically designed for mirror detection in multi-view scenes.
然而，现有的方法仅专注于单图像检测，忽略了多视图设置所提供的丰富信息。为了克服这一局限性，我们提出了 MVMD，这是一种新颖的多视图镜面检测方法，并发布了首个专门为多视图场景下的镜面检测而设计的数据库。

The design of MVMD is grounded in the inherent associations between objects seen from different views and those reflected inside and outside of mirrors. These relationships are learned through cross- and self-attention mechanisms.
MVMD 的设计基于从不同视角观察到的物体与镜面内外反射物体之间的内在联系。这些关系通过交叉注意力和自注意力机制进行学习。

MVMD consists of three key blocks: the Inter-Views Block tracks the shifts of objects within mirrors caused by changes in viewpoint; the Intra-View Block detects object reflections inside mirrors; and the Refinement Block sharpens mirror boundaries and enhances detected details.
MVMD 由三个关键模块组成：视图间模块（Inter-Views Block）用于追踪因视角变化而导致的镜面内物体位移；视图内模块（Intra-View Block）用于检测镜面内的物体反射；细化模块（Refinement Block）则用于锐化镜面边界并增强检测细节。

Experimental results show that our method improves accuracy by up to 2.6% and IoU by up to 11.1%, compared to single-image mirror detection techniques. This substantial improvement makes MVMD particularly effective for computer vision tasks, especially in enhancing the accuracy of 3D reconstruction in mirror-dense environments.
实验结果表明，与单图像镜面检测技术相比，我们的方法将准确率提高了 2.6%，IoU（交并比）提高了 11.1%。这一显著提升使得 MVMD 在计算机视觉任务中表现尤为出色，特别是在提高镜面密集环境下的三维重建精度方面效果显著。
