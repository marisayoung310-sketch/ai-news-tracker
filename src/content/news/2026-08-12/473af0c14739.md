# Threads has a VR app now
# Threads 现在有了 VR 应用

Doomscrolling, but in VR.
在虚拟现实中“末日刷屏”。

Meta has launched a Threads app for Meta Quest VR headsets, the company announced on Tuesday. The launch follows Meta bringing the app to its Ray-Ban Display AR glasses last month and the recent news that the platform has crossed 500 million monthly active users.
Meta 周二宣布，已为 Meta Quest VR 头显推出了 Threads 应用。在此之前，Meta 上个月刚将该应用引入了其 Ray-Ban 智能眼镜，且近期有消息称该平台的月活跃用户已突破 5 亿。

It seems like a pretty full-featured app. Meta says that with the Threads VR app, you can do things like browse feeds, make posts, like and reply to posts, visit profiles, search, and watch videos — you know, Threads stuff.
这看起来是一款功能相当齐全的应用。Meta 表示，通过 Threads VR 应用，你可以浏览信息流、发布帖子、点赞和回复、访问个人资料、搜索以及观看视频——总之，就是你在 Threads 上能做的那些事。

“We designed the new Threads app for Meta Quest to make using VR more social than ever,” Meta says. “Whether you want to keep up with conversations during a game, riff on the latest TV show or hit album with friends, or simply do fast check-ins between daily tasks, Threads can enhance your VR experience with a dedicated native app.”
“我们为 Meta Quest 设计了全新的 Threads 应用，旨在让 VR 的使用体验比以往任何时候都更具社交性，”Meta 表示，“无论你是想在游戏过程中跟进对话、与朋友讨论最新的电视节目或热门专辑，还是仅仅想在日常任务间隙快速查看动态，Threads 都能通过这款专属的原生应用提升你的 VR 体验。”

You can download the app from Meta’s store.
你可以从 Meta 的应用商店下载该应用。
