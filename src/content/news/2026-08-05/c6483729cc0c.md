# Noise-Robust Conditional Flow Matching: Generating Clean Samples from Noisy Datasets
# 噪声鲁棒条件流匹配：从噪声数据集中生成干净样本

**Abstract:** Generative models learn the statistical properties of their training data, so high-quality generation depends on clean and representative datasets. In scientific imaging, acquisition often yields noisy measurements, while collecting clean references can be costly, impractical or even unattainable. Training directly on these measurements results in a model that reproduces the corrupted data. This can be circumvented by learning the clean population distribution directly from the noisy data.

**摘要：** 生成模型通过学习训练数据的统计特性来工作，因此高质量的生成效果依赖于干净且具有代表性的数据集。在科学成像领域，数据采集往往会产生噪声测量值，而收集干净的参考数据可能成本高昂、不切实际，甚至无法实现。直接在这些测量值上进行训练会导致模型复现出受损的数据。这一问题可以通过直接从噪声数据中学习干净的总体分布来规避。

Conditional flow matching (CFM) combines a simple regression objective with stable training, efficient sampling, and strong image-generation performance, making it a natural framework for this setting. We introduce Noise-Robust Conditional Flow Matching (NR-CFM), an unconditional generator that learns from one corrupted observation per image. NR-CFM provides a closed-form clean endpoint correction for additive white Gaussian noise and learns a data-driven correction for general Gaussian corruptions with more complex covariance structure.

条件流匹配（CFM）将简单的回归目标与稳定的训练、高效的采样以及强大的图像生成性能相结合，使其成为该场景下的天然框架。我们引入了噪声鲁棒条件流匹配（NR-CFM），这是一种无条件生成器，它通过每张图像的一个受损观测值进行学习。NR-CFM 为加性高斯白噪声提供了闭式（closed-form）的干净端点校正，并针对具有更复杂协方差结构的一般高斯噪声，学习了一种数据驱动的校正方法。

Across the evaluated corruption settings, NR-CFM outperforms NR-GAN in most cases and remains competitive with Ambient Diffusion in the high-noise regime. We further evaluate NR-CFM on scientific data at signal-to-noise ratios as low as $0.001$, where it generates plausible particle images from severely corrupted measurements.

在所评估的各种噪声设置中，NR-CFM 在大多数情况下优于 NR-GAN，并在高噪声环境下保持了与 Ambient Diffusion 相当的竞争力。我们进一步在信噪比低至 $0.001$ 的科学数据上评估了 NR-CFM，结果显示它能够从严重受损的测量值中生成合理的粒子图像。
