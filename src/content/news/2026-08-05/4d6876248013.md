# Mariano-Florentino (Tino) Cuéllar to join Anthropic as Chief Global Affairs Officer
# 马里亚诺-弗洛伦蒂诺 (Tino) Cuéllar 将加入 Anthropic 担任首席全球事务官

Aug 4, 2026
2026年8月4日

Mariano-Florentino (Tino) Cuéllar will join Anthropic as its first Chief Global Affairs Officer, leading the company’s work on policy, strategic international engagement, and government relationships worldwide.
马里亚诺-弗洛伦蒂诺 (Tino) Cuéllar 将加入 Anthropic，担任公司首位首席全球事务官，负责领导公司在全球范围内的政策制定、战略性国际参与以及政府关系工作。

Tino’s career spans law, technology, international security, and public institutions at the international, national, and state levels. He recently stepped down as President of the Carnegie Endowment for International Peace, a leading independent global policy research institution with scholars in 20 countries.
Tino 的职业生涯横跨法律、技术、国际安全以及国际、国家和州各级的公共机构。他近期卸任了卡内基国际和平基金会（Carnegie Endowment for International Peace）主席一职，该基金会是一家领先的独立全球政策研究机构，在20个国家拥有学者。

Prior to his role at Carnegie, Tino was a Justice of the Supreme Court of California, where his opinions addressed technology and privacy, international agreements, and the separation of powers, among other issues. He was previously director of Stanford's Freeman Spogli Institute for International Studies, co-director of the university’s Center for International Security and Cooperation, and director of the Stanford Cyber Initiative.
在加入卡内基之前，Tino 曾担任加利福尼亚州最高法院大法官，其司法意见涉及技术与隐私、国际协议以及权力分立等议题。他此前还曾担任斯坦福大学弗里曼·斯波格利国际问题研究所（Freeman Spogli Institute for International Studies）所长、该校国际安全与合作中心联席主任，以及斯坦福网络倡议（Stanford Cyber Initiative）主任。

He has served on the President's Intelligence Advisory Board and the US Department of State's Foreign Affairs Policy Board, and worked in the White House and federal agencies in three presidential administrations. The National Academy of Sciences appointed him to its Committee on Responsible Computing Research.
他曾任职于总统情报咨询委员会和美国国务院外交政策委员会，并在三届总统政府任期内于白宫及联邦机构工作。美国国家科学院还任命他为负责任计算研究委员会成员。

In recent years, he also co-chaired the bipartisan Task Force on Nuclear Proliferation and American Security, co-led California’s Frontier AI Working Group, and served as board chair and later director of the Center for Advanced Study in the Behavioral Sciences. Currently, he is the Cameron Schrier Family Professor at Stanford Law School, where he started his teaching career before serving in the judiciary and began organizing classes on artificial intelligence nearly a decade ago. He also serves as Senior Fellow at Stanford’s Institute for Human-Centered Artificial Intelligence.
近年来，他还共同主持了跨党派的核扩散与美国安全特别工作组，共同领导了加州前沿人工智能工作组，并担任过行为科学高级研究中心（Center for Advanced Study in the Behavioral Sciences）的董事会主席及后来的主任。目前，他是斯坦福法学院的 Cameron Schrier 家庭教授，他在进入司法界之前便在此开启了教学生涯，并于近十年前开始组织人工智能相关课程。他同时担任斯坦福以人为本人工智能研究所（Institute for Human-Centered Artificial Intelligence）的高级研究员。

Tino has served as a Trustee of Anthropic's Long-Term Benefit Trust since January 2026. He has stepped down from the Trust to join the company. The Trust will select a successor under its normal process.
自2026年1月起，Tino 一直担任 Anthropic 长期利益信托（Long-Term Benefit Trust）的受托人。为了加入公司，他已辞去信托职务。信托将按照正常程序选出继任者。

“Policymakers in the US and around the world are increasingly realizing that we are at a critical inflection point when it comes to how we govern and develop artificial intelligence. The choices we make today will determine whether humanity can harness extraordinary possibilities to advance science and improve lives across the world or face enormous risk and growing inequality,” said Cuéllar. “Democracies must set the terms on which this technology advances, and there is no more consequential place to be shaping that work right now than Anthropic.”
“美国及全球的决策者们正日益意识到，在如何治理和发展人工智能方面，我们正处于一个关键的转折点。我们今天所做的选择，将决定人类是能够利用非凡的可能性来推动科学进步并改善全球生活，还是会面临巨大的风险和日益加剧的不平等，” Cuéllar 表示。“民主国家必须为这项技术的发展设定准则，而目前没有比 Anthropic 更具影响力的平台来塑造这项工作了。”

“Tino has spent his career helping public institutions respond to times of change with thoughtfulness, pragmatism, and deep commitment to the common good,” said Daniela Amodei. “At all levels of government, the law, and academia, Tino has served with sound judgment and civic-mindedness, and we’re looking forward to him putting these principles to work at Anthropic. I can't think of anyone better prepared to partner with governments, civil society, and community groups as they engage with both the risks and opportunities presented by advanced AI.”
“Tino 的职业生涯一直致力于帮助公共机构以深思熟虑、务实且对公共利益高度负责的态度应对变革，” Daniela Amodei 说道。“无论是在政府、法律还是学术界的各个层面，Tino 都展现出了卓越的判断力和公民意识，我们期待他将这些原则带到 Anthropic。我想不出还有谁比他更适合与政府、公民社会和社区团体合作，共同应对先进人工智能带来的风险与机遇。”

Tino arrives at a pivotal moment for Anthropic's work with governments around the world. The questions AI raises for economies, for security, and for communities absorbing rapid change are being debated by leaders everywhere. Ensuring AI’s trajectory is shaped by democratic societies and its benefits reach people broadly is a critical priority. Tino will help steer this work while finding common cause with heads of state and policy leaders on the questions and possibilities AI is raising for communities everywhere.
Tino 的加入正值 Anthropic 与全球政府合作的关键时刻。人工智能对经济、安全以及正在经历快速变革的社区所提出的问题，正受到各地领导人的热议。确保人工智能的发展轨迹由民主社会塑造，并使其惠及广大民众，是一项至关重要的优先事项。Tino 将协助引导这项工作，同时就人工智能为各地社区带来的问题与可能性，与各国元首及政策领袖寻求共识。
