# Meet Wrinkles, an app that uncovers the hidden stories of the places around you
# 认识 Wrinkles：一款为你揭开身边地点隐藏故事的应用程序

A new app called Wrinkles uses your location to automatically surface stories about the places around you. Wrinkles, available on both iOS and Android, essentially acts as an AI-powered audio tour guide that reveals hidden history and local stories. The idea behind the app is to allow you to move through the world while hearing the places around you come to life without having to stare at your phone. As you explore, Wrinkles can tell you the secrets behind a building, the history of a street, and other local lore.

一款名为 Wrinkles 的新应用利用你的地理位置，自动为你呈现周边地点的故事。Wrinkles 目前已在 iOS 和 Android 平台上线，它本质上是一个由人工智能驱动的音频导游，旨在揭示隐藏的历史和当地轶事。这款应用的设计初衷是让你在行走世界时，无需盯着手机屏幕，就能听到周围地点“活”过来的声音。当你探索时，Wrinkles 可以告诉你建筑背后的秘密、街道的历史以及其他当地传说。

Wrinkles was founded by David Stemler, a longtime advertising executive, and Ryan Hansan, who previously founded ScratchDC, a meal delivery service, and TasteLab, a shared commercial kitchen that helped independent food businesses launch and grow. The pair have known each other since middle school and say travel has always been a defining part of their friendship. They began taking trips abroad together as teenagers and continue to travel together today, now with their families. Over the years, many of their business ideas have grown out of long conversations while traveling and questioning how the experiences around them could be improved. Wrinkles was born from that same curiosity.

Wrinkles 由资深广告高管 David Stemler 和 Ryan Hansan 共同创立。Hansan 此前曾创立过餐饮配送服务 ScratchDC，以及帮助独立食品企业起步和发展的共享商业厨房 TasteLab。两人从中学时代就相识，他们表示旅行一直是他们友谊中至关重要的一部分。他们从青少年时期就开始结伴出国旅行，如今即便各自成家，依然保持着共同出游的习惯。多年来，他们的许多商业构想都源于旅行途中的长谈，以及对如何改善周边体验的思考。Wrinkles 正是诞生于这种好奇心。

Stemler came up with the idea for Wrinkles while visiting the British Museum in London and using its official app for a self-guided tour. Although he believed the app was well designed, Stemler found the experience frustrating. “I spent the entire visit heads-down, matching numbers on the walls to lists on my phone instead of taking in my surroundings while learning about them,” Stemler told TechCrunch in an email. At the end of his trip, he called Hansan from the airport and asked, ”If the phone in my pocket knows exactly where I am, shouldn’t the places in front of me be able speak?”

Stemler 产生开发 Wrinkles 的想法，是在伦敦大英博物馆参观并使用其官方应用进行自助导览时。尽管他认为该应用设计得不错，但体验却让他感到沮丧。“我整个参观过程都在低头看手机，忙着把墙上的编号和手机里的列表进行匹配，而不是在了解周围环境的同时去欣赏它们，”Stemler 在给 TechCrunch 的邮件中写道。旅程结束时，他在机场给 Hansan 打了电话并问道：“如果我口袋里的手机能精准定位我的位置，那么我眼前的这些地方难道不应该能‘开口说话’吗？”

The startup has built a global foundation of 1.3 million Wrinkles, or points of interest, across 177 countries. Beyond this foundational content, historians, museums, creators, and brands can add their own stories and experiences to the map. Users can discover these stories as they move through the world or explore them remotely by searching and browsing the map. To make the experience feel like a traditional tour, users can ask questions as they explore. For example, while learning about how the Trevi Fountain was built, you could ask a question like, “Where does this water actually come from?”

这家初创公司已经在全球 177 个国家建立了 130 万个“Wrinkles”（即兴趣点）的基础数据库。除了这些基础内容外，历史学家、博物馆、创作者和品牌方也可以将他们自己的故事和体验添加到地图中。用户可以在行走世界时发现这些故事，也可以通过搜索和浏览地图进行远程探索。为了让体验更像传统的导览，用户在探索时还可以进行提问。例如，在了解特雷维喷泉（Trevi Fountain）的建造过程时，你可以问：“这些水到底是从哪里来的？”

There’s also a social aspect to Wrinkles, as users can follow friends, family, creators, and organizations, then save and share Wrinkles or organize them into custom maps and guides. Users can personalize their Wrinkles, too, by attaching family memories, photos, videos, and recordings to specific places. Families can collaborate on these histories to preserve stories tied to places like a childhood home, a first date location, or a hometown.

Wrinkles 还具备社交属性，用户可以关注朋友、家人、创作者和组织，然后保存并分享 Wrinkles，或者将其整理成自定义地图和指南。用户还可以通过在特定地点添加家庭回忆、照片、视频和录音来个性化自己的 Wrinkles。家庭成员可以共同协作，记录并保存与童年故居、初次约会地点或家乡等地方相关的珍贵故事。

Hansan and Stemler see Wrinkles as more than a travel app. While it can help spontaneous travelers discover the stories behind new places, they believe it’s also designed for people who want to learn more about the buildings they pass every day or rediscover their own hometown. “The common thread isn’t travel — it’s curiosity,” Hansan said. “A travel app gets used on one trip a year. Wrinkles gets used on your commute, at the botanical garden, at the zoo with your kids, leaving a permanent legacy for a loved one, and on the trip to Spain.”

Hansan 和 Stemler 认为 Wrinkles 不仅仅是一款旅行应用。虽然它可以帮助随性的旅行者发现新地点背后的故事，但他们认为它同样适合那些想要了解每天路过的建筑，或重新发现自己家乡的人。“共同点不是旅行，而是好奇心，”Hansan 说，“旅行应用可能一年只用一次，但 Wrinkles 可以用在你的通勤路上、植物园里、带孩子去动物园时、为亲人留下永久的纪念，以及去西班牙旅行时。”

While the duo has experimented with a freemium model, they say they’re committed to keeping the app free for users. Wrinkle’s revenue primarily comes from the supply side, with museums, tourism boards, universities, and hotels using Wrinkles to create location-based experiences for visitors without having to build their own apps. Creators and media partners can build sponsored place-based guides, while businesses can promote Wrinkles connected to their locations. Partners can also add links for users to book tours, reserve tables, buy tickets, or visit businesses, with Wrinkles earning a share of those transactions.

尽管两人曾尝试过免费增值模式，但他们表示致力于让用户免费使用该应用。Wrinkles 的收入主要来自供应端，博物馆、旅游局、大学和酒店可以使用 Wrinkles 为游客创建基于位置的体验，而无需自行开发应用。创作者和媒体合作伙伴可以制作赞助性的地点指南，企业也可以推广与其实体位置相关的 Wrinkles。合作伙伴还可以添加链接，方便用户预订导览、预订餐位、购买门票或访问商家，Wrinkles 则会从这些交易中抽取分成。

As for the company’s long-term vision, the duo sees Wrinkles becoming a normal part of how people navigate places, whether that means discovering the history of a college campus, learning more about their own city, exploring a map while planning a trip, or getting a deeper understanding of a city they’re visiting for the first time.

关于公司的长期愿景，两人希望 Wrinkles 能成为人们探索地点时的常态化工具，无论是发现大学校园的历史、深入了解自己所在的城市、在规划旅行时探索地图，还是在初次造访某座城市时获得更深层次的认知。
