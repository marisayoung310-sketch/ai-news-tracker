# All of Winona Police Department's Flock cameras cut down and stolen
# 威诺纳警察局所有 Flock 监控摄像头被锯断并盗走

WINONA, Minn. (Valley News Live) - Every Flock license plate reader camera operated by the Winona Police Department has been sawed off and stolen in what investigators believe was a coordinated theft.
明尼苏达州威诺纳（Valley News Live 报道）——威诺纳警察局运营的所有 Flock 车牌识别摄像头均被锯断并盗走，调查人员认为这是一起有组织的盗窃案。

All eight cameras were taken Aug. 1, according to the Winona Police Department. A patrol officer first noticed the cameras had not sent any alerts in 24 hours. When officers checked the locations, they found the cameras had been cut from their poles and taken. The poles were left behind.
据威诺纳警察局称，所有八台摄像头均于 8 月 1 日被盗。一名巡警最先发现这些摄像头在 24 小时内未发送任何警报。当警员前往现场查看时，发现摄像头已从杆子上被锯断并带走，而杆子则被留在了原地。

Two additional Flock cameras on the Mississippi River Bridge, owned by Buffalo County, were also stolen in the same manner.
位于密西西比河大桥上、归布法罗县所有的另外两台 Flock 摄像头也以同样的方式被盗。

Each camera is valued at approximately $3,000, putting the total loss at roughly $24,000. That cost falls directly on the Winona Police Department’s budget.
每台摄像头的价值约为 3,000 美元，总损失约为 24,000 美元。这笔费用将直接由威诺纳警察局的预算承担。

The eight WPD cameras were positioned at key highway entry and exit points throughout the city: near Highway 61 and Bundy Boulevard, Highway 43 and Homer Road, Highway 14 and Knapp Valley Drive, and Highway 61 and Highway 14. Two cameras were stationed at each location.
这八台威诺纳警察局的摄像头分布在全市各主要公路的出入口：包括 61 号公路与邦迪大道（Bundy Boulevard）交汇处、43 号公路与荷马路（Homer Road）交汇处、14 号公路与纳普谷大道（Knapp Valley Drive）交汇处，以及 61 号公路与 14 号公路交汇处。每个地点各安装有两台摄像头。

Flock Safety cameras are automated license plate readers that capture images of plates along with a vehicle’s make, model and color. Police use the technology to investigate crimes such as hit-and-runs and to locate missing persons. Camera data is owned by the police department and permanently deleted after 30 days.
Flock Safety 摄像头是一种自动车牌识别系统，可以拍摄车牌图像，并记录车辆的品牌、型号和颜色。警方利用该技术调查肇事逃逸等犯罪案件，并寻找失踪人员。摄像头数据归警察局所有，并在 30 天后永久删除。

The thefts are part of a broader national trend. Flock cameras have been vandalized and cut down in communities across the country. The technology has drawn criticism from civil liberties advocates who argue the cameras expand government surveillance and raise concerns about data being shared with third-party companies or federal agencies.
此次盗窃案是全国范围内更广泛趋势的一部分。全美各地的社区都曾发生过 Flock 摄像头被破坏和锯断的事件。该技术也招致了民权倡导者的批评，他们认为这些摄像头扩大了政府的监控范围，并对数据被共享给第三方公司或联邦机构表示担忧。

No suspects have been identified. The investigation is ongoing. Anyone with information is asked to contact the Winona Police Department.
目前尚未确定嫌疑人。调查仍在进行中。任何有相关信息的人请联系威诺纳警察局。
