# PhysAgent: A Multi-Agent Framework for Reliable Remote Heart Rate Estimation
# PhysAgent：用于可靠远程心率估计的多智能体框架

**Abstract:** Remote photoplethysmography (rPPG) enables non-contact heart-rate estimation from facial videos, but its weak physiological signal is easily corrupted by motion, illumination changes, occlusion, skin-appearance variation, and device noise. 
**摘要：** 远程光电容积脉搏波描记法（rPPG）能够通过面部视频进行非接触式心率估计，但其微弱的生理信号极易受到运动、光照变化、遮挡、皮肤外观差异以及设备噪声的干扰。

Existing rPPG methods typically rely on a single model to directly predict heart rate or recover pulse waveforms, while different strong estimators may produce conflicting yet individually plausible candidates for the same video. 
现有的 rPPG 方法通常依赖单一模型直接预测心率或恢复脉搏波形，然而，不同的强估计器针对同一视频可能会产生相互冲突但各自看似合理的候选结果。

To resolve these conflicts, we propose PhysAgent, an inference-time multi-agent candidate-verification framework. Unlike direct prediction approaches, PhysAgent neither trains a new base rPPG model nor asks Multimodal Large Language Models (MLLMs) to output heart rate directly. 
为了解决这些冲突，我们提出了 PhysAgent，这是一个推理阶段的多智能体候选验证框架。与直接预测方法不同，PhysAgent 既不需要训练新的基础 rPPG 模型，也不要求多模态大语言模型（MLLM）直接输出心率。

In contrast, it treats outputs from multiple base estimators as physiological hypotheses to be verified and uses a lightweight 4B MLLM, Qwen3-VL-4B, to drive multi-agent reasoning over video conditions, signal reliability, and candidate disagreement. 
相反，它将多个基础估计器的输出视为待验证的生理假设，并利用轻量级的 4B 参数 MLLM（Qwen3-VL-4B）来驱动针对视频条件、信号可靠性和候选结果分歧的多智能体推理。

A deterministic physiological verifier checks the fusion proposal, and a reproducible numerical fusion process produces the final heart rate. 
一个确定性的生理验证器会对融合方案进行检查，随后通过可复现的数值融合过程得出最终心率。

Experimental results on multiple public rPPG benchmarks show that PhysAgent improves fusion stability and reliability across different datasets and source-domain settings, while avoiding the irreproducibility and physiological inconsistency of direct MLLM prediction or unconstrained ensemble fusion. The code will be released soon.
在多个公开 rPPG 基准测试上的实验结果表明，PhysAgent 在不同数据集和源域设置下提高了融合的稳定性和可靠性，同时避免了直接使用 MLLM 预测或无约束集成融合所带来的不可复现性和生理不一致性问题。相关代码即将发布。
