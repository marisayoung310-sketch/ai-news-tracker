# The latest AI news we announced in July 2026
# 2026年7月发布的最新AI新闻

Here’s a recap of some of our biggest AI updates from July, including three new Gemini models for building AI agents at scale and Gemini Robotics ER 2 to help robots reason, collaborate, and solve real-world tasks.
以下是我们7月份一些重大AI更新的回顾，包括用于大规模构建AI智能体的三款全新Gemini模型，以及旨在帮助机器人进行推理、协作并解决现实世界任务的Gemini Robotics ER 2。

Google’s July updates introduce faster Gemini models, advanced robotics, and new creative tools for video and music production. You can now use Gemini Spark to automate web errands, link your favorite apps to Search, and easily transfer data to new Android devices. We are also expanding our impact through new wildfire detection satellites and a partnership to support the skilled trades workforce.
谷歌7月份的更新引入了更快的Gemini模型、先进的机器人技术以及用于视频和音乐制作的全新创意工具。现在，您可以使用Gemini Spark来自动化处理网页琐事、将您喜爱的应用程序关联到搜索，并轻松将数据传输到新的Android设备。我们还通过新的野火探测卫星以及支持技术工种劳动力的合作伙伴关系，进一步扩大了我们的影响力。

For more than 20 years, we’ve invested in machine learning and AI research, tools, and infrastructure to build products that make everyday life better for more people. Teams across Google are working on ways to unlock AI’s benefits in fields as wide-ranging as healthcare, crisis response, and education.
二十多年来，我们一直致力于机器学习和AI研究、工具及基础设施的投入，旨在打造让更多人的日常生活变得更美好的产品。谷歌各地的团队正致力于在医疗保健、危机应对和教育等广泛领域释放AI的潜力。

July brought a wave of AI advances to support developers, simplify everyday life, and protect local communities. We gave developers faster, more efficient Gemini models for scaling production agents and advancing robotics. We also expanded our creative suite with next-generation music and video tools, and made Search and Gemini Spark even more connected to your go-to daily apps.
7月带来了一波AI技术进步，旨在支持开发者、简化日常生活并保护当地社区。我们为开发者提供了更快、更高效的Gemini模型，用于扩展生产级智能体并推动机器人技术的发展。我们还通过下一代音乐和视频工具扩展了创意套件，并使搜索和Gemini Spark与您常用的日常应用程序连接得更加紧密。

Check out three new Gemini models. Developers and customers building production AI agents need higher token efficiency, lower latency, and more reliable performance. Gemini 3.6 Flash, 3.5 Flash-Lite, and 3.5 Flash Cyber are built to meet the sweet spot of efficiency and quality to enable scaling agentic workflows.
了解三款全新的Gemini模型。构建生产级AI智能体的开发者和客户需要更高的Token效率、更低的延迟以及更可靠的性能。Gemini 3.6 Flash、3.5 Flash-Lite和3.5 Flash Cyber旨在实现效率与质量的最佳平衡，从而支持智能体工作流的扩展。

See how robots gear up with Gemini Robotics ER 2. We launched Gemini Robotics ER 2, our most capable "embodied reasoning" model yet. Built for developers crafting genuinely helpful machines, ER 2 bridges the gap between digital smarts and the physical world. It lets systems chat naturally with people, make sense of their surroundings, and work through complex, multi-step tasks.
看看机器人如何通过Gemini Robotics ER 2实现升级。我们发布了迄今为止功能最强大的“具身推理”模型——Gemini Robotics ER 2。ER 2专为打造真正实用机器的开发者而设计，它架起了数字智能与物理世界之间的桥梁。它使系统能够与人类自然交流、理解周围环境，并处理复杂的多步骤任务。

Get started with Gemini Intelligence on the latest devices from Galaxy Unpacked 2026. With the launch of the Samsung Galaxy Z Fold8 Ultra, Fold8, and Flip8, we’re making it easier than ever to get started on Android while also rolling out the first capabilities of Gemini Intelligence.
在Galaxy Unpacked 2026发布的最新设备上体验Gemini Intelligence。随着三星Galaxy Z Fold8 Ultra、Fold8和Flip8的发布，我们让Android系统的上手变得前所未有的简单，同时也推出了Gemini Intelligence的首批功能。

Switch to Android even more easily and bring your data with you. With the launch of Samsung’s new Z Series at Galaxy Unpacked, we introduced a new native migration experience built directly into Android 17 that lets you transfer even more data types from an iPhone wirelessly — without downloading a separate app.
更轻松地切换到Android并迁移您的数据。随着三星在Galaxy Unpacked上发布新款Z系列手机，我们引入了一种直接内置于Android 17中的原生迁移体验，让您可以无线传输更多类型的数据——无需下载任何单独的应用程序。

Streamline your research with Gemini Notebook. Gemini Notebook is the same standalone product as the hugely popular NotebookLM, only now it's doing more across the Google ecosystem — including inside the Gemini app and Google Search — and it’s updated with a secure cloud computer.
使用Gemini Notebook简化您的研究。Gemini Notebook与广受欢迎的NotebookLM是同一款独立产品，但现在它在整个谷歌生态系统中发挥着更大的作用——包括在Gemini应用和谷歌搜索内部——并且它还更新了安全云计算机功能。

Explore the first iteration of the AI & Economy ATLAS. Google’s ATLAS is an expansive look at how people are using AI at work and in day-to-day life. This ongoing, large-scale, de-identified study is designed to offer an early view of a quickly moving landscape: AI’s capabilities are advancing, its use is evolving, and tools for observing its impact on the economy are still a work-in-progress.
探索AI与经济ATLAS的首个版本。谷歌的ATLAS是对人们如何在工作和日常生活中使用AI的广泛观察。这项持续进行的大规模去标识化研究旨在提供对快速变化格局的早期洞察：AI的能力正在进步，其用途正在演变，而观察其对经济影响的工具仍在开发完善中。

Meet the Alliance for America’s Skilled Trades. To help meet the growing demand for skilled trades workers, we’re joining forces with BlackRock, Carhartt, and Ford to launch the Alliance for America’s Skilled Trades. Together, we’ll build a stronger pipeline for the skilled trades, scale evidence-based training approaches, and expand opportunities nationwide through cross-industry partnerships.
了解“美国技术工种联盟”。为了满足对技术工种劳动力日益增长的需求，我们正与贝莱德（BlackRock）、卡哈特（Carhartt）和福特（Ford）携手成立“美国技术工种联盟”。我们将共同为技术工种建立更强大的人才输送渠道，推广基于证据的培训方法，并通过跨行业合作在全国范围内扩大就业机会。

See how AlphaEvolve can help Cloud customers solve their hardest problems. Finding the most efficient algorithm can be challenging. To help, we made AlphaEvolve, our Gemini-powered AI code-optimization agent, generally available to all Google Cloud customers on Gemini Enterprise Agent Platform. AlphaEvolve acts as an evolutionary collaborator: You provide a baseline algorithm and your goals, and it automatically searches for better solutions, returning human-readable, optimized code.
看看AlphaEvolve如何帮助云客户解决最棘手的问题。寻找最高效的算法往往充满挑战。为此，我们将由Gemini驱动的AI代码优化智能体AlphaEvolve向所有Gemini企业智能体平台上的谷歌云客户全面开放。AlphaEvolve就像一位进化协作伙伴：您提供基准算法和目标，它会自动搜索更优的解决方案，并返回人类可读的优化代码。

Connect more of your apps to Search. You can already connect some of your favorite services to the Gemini app. To help you get even more done, we’re enabling you to securely link and interact with your go-to apps like YouTube Music directly in AI Mode. With Personal Intelligence and your connected apps, Search can give even more tailored responses.
将更多应用程序连接到搜索。您已经可以将一些喜爱的服务连接到Gemini应用。为了帮助您完成更多任务，我们现在支持您在AI模式下直接安全地关联并与YouTube Music等常用应用进行交互。借助个人智能和已连接的应用程序，搜索功能可以提供更加个性化的回复。
