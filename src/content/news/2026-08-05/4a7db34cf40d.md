# Empirical investigation of 3D CT Foundation Models and Unsupervised Adaptation for Head and Neck Cancer Recurrence Prediction
# 3D CT 基础模型及无监督适应在头颈癌复发预测中的实证研究

**Abstract:** The rapid emergence of 3D CT foundation models has opened new avenues for predictive modeling from CT imaging, offering a compelling alternative to traditional radiomics which is known to suffer from reproducibility issues and sensitivity to acquisition protocol variations. 

**摘要：** 3D CT 基础模型的迅速兴起为基于 CT 影像的预测建模开辟了新途径，为传统放射组学提供了一种极具吸引力的替代方案。众所周知，传统放射组学在可重复性方面存在问题，且对采集协议的变化较为敏感。

Yet, as these models grow in availability, a critical need arises to evaluate how well their learned representations generalize across diverse clinical settings and whether adaptation to specific downstream tasks is necessary to unlock their full potential. 

然而，随着这些模型可用性的增加，评估其学习到的表征在不同临床环境下的泛化能力，以及是否需要针对特定下游任务进行适应以充分发挥其潜力，已成为一项迫切需求。

To address these questions, we benchmarked several 3D CT foundation models for predicting recurrence-free survival in head and neck cancer across two public datasets totaling 3,644 patients, evaluating various adaptation strategies and modality fusion mechanisms. 

为了解决这些问题，我们对多个 3D CT 基础模型进行了基准测试，旨在预测头颈癌患者的无复发生存期。研究涵盖了两个总计 3,644 名患者的公共数据集，并评估了多种适应策略和模态融合机制。

Our findings reveal persistent difficulty in identifying features that generalize consistently across different imaging distributions, as evidenced by significant performance drops on external validation cohorts. 

研究结果表明，在不同影像分布之间识别具有一致泛化能力的特征仍然存在困难，这一点在外部验证队列中表现出的显著性能下降得到了证实。

Ultimately, the integration of imaging features with clinical data remains the most accurate approach for prognostic prediction, though achieving universal generalization across varied clinical contexts continues to represent a substantial challenge for the current generation of models.

最终，将影像特征与临床数据相结合仍然是预后预测最准确的方法，尽管在多样的临床背景下实现通用泛化对于当前一代模型而言，依然是一项重大挑战。
