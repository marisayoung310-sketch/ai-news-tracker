### Best Overall Gaming Mouse: Razer Cobra HyperSpeed
### 最佳综合游戏鼠标：Razer Cobra HyperSpeed

The Razer Cobra HyperSpeed (9/10, WIRED Recommends) is far more reasonable and practical than most of the ultra-light options today. Instead of shaving down features and mass to make the simplest mouse possible, the Cobra Hyperspeed finds an ideal middle ground, maintaining features like RGB lighting, Bluetooth connectivity, and options for wireless charging. Despite that, it still weighs below 70 grams, a number that was unbelievable for even the most premium wireless mice a decade ago.

Razer Cobra HyperSpeed（9/10，WIRED 推荐）比当今大多数超轻量化鼠标更合理、更实用。Cobra HyperSpeed 没有为了追求极致简约而削减功能和重量，它找到了一个理想的平衡点，保留了 RGB 灯效、蓝牙连接和无线充电等功能。尽管如此，它的重量仍低于 70 克，这在十年前即使是对最顶级的无线鼠标来说也是不可思议的数字。

Inside, it’s packing an optical sensor, optical switches, and an optical scroll wheel—all top-of-the-line parts that are some of the fastest and most reliable on the market. While the mouse is limited to 1,000-Hz polling with the included low-profile USB-A dongle (which I prefer for convenience), it can be upgraded to 8,000 Hz with the bulkier and less-convenient HyperPolling dongle, cutting out its only serious limitation.

内部配备了光学传感器、光学微动和光学滚轮——所有这些顶级组件都是市场上最快、最可靠的部件。虽然该鼠标在使用随附的低调 USB-A 接收器（我个人为了方便更喜欢这个）时限制在 1,000Hz 轮询率，但它可以通过更大、不太方便的 HyperPolling 接收器升级到 8,000Hz，从而消除了它唯一的严重局限性。

### Best Competitive Gaming Mouse: Razer Viper V4 Pro
### 最佳竞技游戏鼠标：Razer Viper V4 Pro

The Razer Viper V4 Pro (9/10, WIRED Recommends) is the latest installment in a long line of fantastic competitive mice, offering performance improvements across every metric. Along with top-of-the-line specs, optical sensors across the board, and a great symmetrical shape, the V4 also incorporates the same spherical dongle included with the Deathadder V4 Pro, which maintains its 8,000-Hz polling while adding LED indicators for information like connection quality, battery life, and DPI stage.

Razer Viper V4 Pro（9/10，WIRED 推荐）是这一系列出色竞技鼠标的最新力作，在各项指标上都提供了性能提升。除了顶级的规格、全系光学传感器和出色的对称外形外，V4 还采用了与 Deathadder V4 Pro 相同的球形接收器，在保持 8,000Hz 轮询率的同时，增加了 LED 指示灯，用于显示连接质量、电池寿命和 DPI 档位等信息。

The only downside of the Viper V4 Pro is how uncompromising it is— there's no Bluetooth, and the included 2.4-GHz dongle requires a USB-A to USB-C cable (included) that runs to your computer, with no option for a smaller USB-A dongle (last seen on the V2). These decisions were made to lighten the mouse and improve connection quality, but they can be inconvenient if you need a wireless gaming mouse to pair with a laptop.

Viper V4 Pro 唯一的缺点是它在设计上毫不妥协——没有蓝牙功能，且随附的 2.4GHz 接收器需要通过一根 USB-A 转 USB-C 线缆（随附）连接到电脑，没有更小的 USB-A 接收器选项（V2 上曾有）。这些设计决策是为了减轻鼠标重量并提高连接质量，但如果你需要一款与笔记本电脑搭配使用的无线游戏鼠标，这可能会很不方便。

### Best Cheap Gaming Mouse: SteelSeries Rival 3 Gen 2
### 最佳平价游戏鼠标：SteelSeries Rival 3 Gen 2

The Rival 3 (7/10, WIRED Recommends) is a simple, no-frills gaming mouse. It’s lightweight, robust, and pretty much hits all the marks for gaming: Easy-access side buttons, clicky main buttons, underside RGB lighting, and a grippy rubberized scroll wheel. This SteelSeries mouse doesn’t innovate much, but like a Toyota Camry, it’s simple and reliable enough to get nearly anything done.

Rival 3（7/10，WIRED 推荐）是一款简单、朴实的游戏鼠标。它轻便、坚固，几乎满足了游戏的所有需求：易于使用的侧键、清脆的主按键、底部 RGB 灯效以及防滑的橡胶滚轮。这款赛睿鼠标没有太多创新，但就像丰田凯美瑞一样，它简单且可靠，足以完成几乎所有任务。
