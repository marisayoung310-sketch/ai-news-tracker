TL;DR spoiler: You can get more statistical power out of a limited subject pool by using a within-subject design (each person does multiple conditions), have each person do multiple tasks/problems per condition, and treat outcomes as semi-independent data points with a crossed statistical correction to account for the dependency. The simulation helps you estimate how well this will work.
摘要剧透：你可以通过使用被试内设计（每个人完成多个实验条件）、让每个人在每个条件下完成多个任务/问题，并将结果视为半独立数据点，同时使用交叉统计校正来处理相关性，从而从有限的受试者群体中获得更高的统计功效。该模拟器可以帮助你评估这种方法的效果。

**Power analysis: why nobody wants to invite the statistician to the party**
**功效分析：为什么没人想邀请统计学家参加派对**

The person who does the power analysis is the guest nobody wants to invite to the party, but everyone knows you should. I was this bearer of bad news more than a decade ago for a multimillion-dollar government research program, which I will call Program Impossible. The program wanted a large, well-controlled evaluation with large groups, multiple nested conditions, and be able to detect small differences between them.
负责功效分析的人是那种没人想邀请参加派对，但每个人都知道必须邀请的客人。十多年前，我曾在一个耗资数百万美元的政府研究项目（我称之为“不可能的任务”项目）中担任这个“坏消息传递者”。该项目希望进行大规模、受控良好的评估，涉及大型群体、多个嵌套条件，并能够检测出它们之间的微小差异。

Doing this with a traditional between-subjects design made the power requirements (number of subjects needed) explode. Ten person groups meant that every 10 people counted as N=1, and many of these groups would simply be controls for the conditions we actually wanted to study. The complex design required many comparisons, and small effect size meant each was difficult to detect with any certainty. The program went in expecting to need hundreds of subjects but, if nothing changed, their power requirements was many thousands, a completely infeasible number.
使用传统的被试间设计，使得功效需求（所需的受试者数量）呈爆炸式增长。10人一组意味着每10个人才算作 N=1，而其中许多组仅仅是我们真正想研究的条件的对照组。复杂的设计需要大量的比较，而较小的效应量意味着每一个差异都难以确定地检测出来。该项目最初预计需要数百名受试者，但如果不做改变，其功效需求将达到数千人，这是一个完全不可行的数字。

It doesn’t take a complex group study design to make your statistician into the party’s pariah. Even simple designs are daunting if you are honest about requirements. In my current work, I am studying what happens when you take a traditional graphical user interface (GUI) and give the user an LLM powered chat guide along with it. I might like to set up a simple A/B test, with A being ‘GUI only’ and B being ‘GUI + Guide’.
不需要复杂的群体研究设计也能让你的统计学家成为派对上的“贱民”。如果你诚实地面对需求，即使是简单的设计也令人望而生畏。在我目前的工作中，我正在研究当你在传统的图形用户界面（GUI）中加入一个由大语言模型（LLM）驱动的聊天指南时会发生什么。我可能想设置一个简单的 A/B 测试，A 为“仅 GUI”，B 为“GUI + 指南”。

In planning the A/B study I would use a power analysis to ask the question, “how many participants do I need?” Or, more precisely, “If there is a ‘medium’ size difference between these conditions, how many participants to I need to have an 80% chance of detecting this difference?” I open the R pwr package and input this:
在规划 A/B 测试时，我会使用功效分析来问这样一个问题：“我需要多少参与者？”或者更准确地说：“如果这些条件之间存在‘中等’程度的差异，我需要多少参与者才能有 80% 的把握检测到这种差异？”我打开 R 语言的 pwr 包并输入：

`pwr.t.test(d = 0.5, sig.level = 0.05, power = 0.8, type = “two.sample”)`

And get back: `n = 63.76561`
得到结果：`n = 63.76561`

That means 63 people per condition. I need 126 total people to test whether my GUI guide is an improvement. For a small design study, 126 is often an impossibly high number, both in terms of recruiting and running that many sessions. What are my options?
这意味着每个条件需要 63 人。我总共需要 126 人来测试我的 GUI 指南是否是一种改进。对于一个小型的设计研究来说，126 往往是一个高得离谱的数字，无论是在招募还是在进行这么多场次实验方面。我有什么选择？

1. Run with however many people I can get and hope I have I either have a very large effect size, or just get lucky.
1. 尽我所能招募尽可能多的人，并希望要么效应量非常大，要么我只是运气好。

2. Design the study for discovery not hypothesis testing, so that I do not need that level control. (See Landauer and Nielsen, 1993, and many follow-up studies on power analysis for usability testing.)
2. 将研究设计为探索性而非假设检验，这样我就不需要那种程度的控制。（参见 Landauer 和 Nielsen, 1993，以及许多关于可用性测试功效分析的后续研究。）

3. Ask our intern to run the task 126 times. Ha, ha. But we can’t do that. Can we?
3. 让我们的实习生把任务跑 126 次。哈哈。但我们不能那样做。是吗？

**This last option was the line of thought our team took in Program Impossible.**
**最后这个选项正是我们团队在“不可能的任务”项目中采取的思路。**

Could we just have a smaller number of teams each do more problems? If, for example. 100 teams did 25 problems each, that gives us 2,500 data points instead of 100, closer to feasible. Is that legitimate? As stated, no, it is not. We would have violated the statistical assumption that the data points are independent of each other; groups of responses all came from the same team. A more traditional approach is to have each team do multiple problems but average them into one score, but for power purpose that puts us right back to N=100.
我们能不能让较少数量的团队每组完成更多的问题？例如，如果 100 个团队每组完成 25 个问题，那么我们就有 2,500 个数据点而不是 100 个，这更接近可行性。这合法吗？如前所述，不，它不合法。我们将违反数据点彼此独立的统计假设；因为一组响应都来自同一个团队。一种更传统的方法是让每个团队完成多个问题，但将它们平均为一个分数，但就功效而言，这又让我们回到了 N=100 的原点。

But what if, instead of averaging scores across problems, we treat the points as semi-independent, and use multilevel regression to control for the dependency? Could that work?
但如果不是对跨问题得分进行平均，而是将这些点视为半独立数据，并使用多层回归来控制这种相关性呢？这行得通吗？

**That can’t be right! Between-subjects designs do not work.**
**这不可能对！被试间设计行不通。**

My team quickly ran into two problems. One was the mathematics of between-subjects experiments. The second was a stubborn task lead, namely me, who did not want to believe the initial results.
我的团队很快遇到了两个问题。一个是关于被试间实验的数学原理。第二个是一个固执的任务负责人，也就是我，我不愿意相信最初的结果。

*Table 1. Adding problems (increasing M) does little to improve statistical power in between-subjects designs.*
*表 1. 在被试间设计中，增加问题数量（增加 M）对提高统计功效几乎没有帮助。*

My team member did a mathematical analysis showing that additional questions added very little in a between-subjects design, as shown in Table 1. This table shows observed statistical power (percentage of runs where a difference was detected) for N participants by M problems. I did not want to believe this outcome when I saw it. Intuitively, it seemed wrong. How could we have that much more data but not gain more information?
我的团队成员做了一项数学分析，表明在被试间设计中，增加额外的问题几乎没有作用，如表 1 所示。该表显示了 N 名参与者在 M 个问题下的观测统计功效（检测到差异的运行百分比）。当我看到这个结果时，我不愿相信。直觉上，这似乎是错的。我们怎么可能拥有这么多数据却没能获得更多信息呢？

So, we built analysis #2, a small Monte Carlo simulation, which is slower but more transparent. This is how the current simulation works; it generated synthetic populations of participants and problems, adds an ‘effect’ and some noise, tests whether the effect is statistically detectable a number of times and reports how many times this ‘true’ effect was found, with 80% being the target goal by convention. 因此，我们构建了第二个分析，一个小的蒙特卡洛模拟，它虽然较慢但更透明。这就是当前模拟器的工作原理：它生成参与者和问题的合成群体，添加一个“效应”和一些噪声，多次测试该效应在统计上是否可检测，并报告发现该“真实”效应的次数，按照惯例，80% 是目标。两种分析得出的结论完全一致。如果我们使用被试间设计，意味着每个人只处于一种条件下……
