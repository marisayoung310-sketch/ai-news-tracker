# The Best Cordless Vacuums (2026): My Brand-New Top Pick
# 2026年最佳无线吸尘器：我的全新首选推荐

It may sound hyperbolic to claim the best cordless vacuums can improve your life. But they do hold that power. In addition to providing you the freedom you didn't know you needed, they also leave your home clean and tidy in a matter of minutes with no cord unraveling or heavy vacuum dragging required.
说最好的无线吸尘器能改善你的生活，听起来可能有些夸张。但它们确实拥有这种力量。除了为你提供你意想不到的自由感之外，它们还能在几分钟内让你的家变得干净整洁，无需解开缠绕的电线，也不必拖拽沉重的吸尘器。

These machines are especially handy for me, as I live in a three-story home. Even the prospect of lugging a classic upright vacuum up and down the stairs leaves me unlikely to do the chore. And I can never seem to remember to take my robot vacuum to different floors to clean the whole house. A stick vacuum, though? Because the form is light and mobile, these vacuums are a breeze to bring to every level and use on my two sets of stairs—and I only expend a tiny bit of sweat in the process.
这些机器对我来说特别方便，因为我住的是三层楼的房子。一想到要扛着传统的立式吸尘器上下楼梯，我就不想打扫了。而且我似乎总记不住把扫地机器人搬到不同的楼层去清洁全屋。但手持式吸尘器呢？由于其轻便灵活的形态，将它们带到每一层楼并清理我的两段楼梯简直轻而易举——整个过程我几乎不费吹灰之力。

After testing nearly two dozen stick vacuums—including hard-floor–only models, powerful Dysons, and more—these are the best cordless vacuums I've tried. My new favorite is the Shark PowerDetect Speed Cordless Stick Vacuum ($430), which not only has impressive power but also bends to make vacuuming under furniture and low surfaces easier. It also has the Clean & Empty version ($500), which features a self-emptying base station. If you've got room to splurge, you can upgrade to the Dyson Gen5Detect ($800) for the best power I've seen in a cordless vacuum.
在测试了近二十款手持式吸尘器（包括仅适用于硬地板的型号、强劲的戴森产品等）之后，这些是我尝试过最好的无线吸尘器。我最新的最爱是 Shark PowerDetect Speed 无线手持吸尘器（430美元），它不仅动力强劲，而且机身可弯曲，让清理家具底部和低矮表面变得更加容易。它还有一个“Clean & Empty”版本（500美元），配备了自动清空基站。如果你预算充足，可以升级到 Dyson Gen5Detect（800美元），这是我在无线吸尘器中见过动力最强的产品。

Shark isn't the only brand with a growing vacuum lineup this year. Shark recently launched the Shark PowerDetect Speed, a new cordless vacuum in its beloved PowerDetect line. The Speed is cheaper than the main model, but it packs a ton of features I like, and it performed excellently in many of my testing rounds.
今年，Shark 并不是唯一一个不断扩充吸尘器产品线的品牌。Shark 最近推出了 Shark PowerDetect Speed，这是其广受欢迎的 PowerDetect 系列中的一款新无线吸尘器。Speed 比主打型号更便宜，但它包含了许多我喜欢的功能，并且在我的多轮测试中表现出色。

The PowerDetect Speed is supposed to be a lighter, more agile version of the PowerDetect vacuum. It weighs about a pound less, has 60 minutes of battery life, and has 350 watts of power to the regular PowerDetect's 380 watts. It also comes with the same two attachments as the regular model.
PowerDetect Speed 被定位为 PowerDetect 吸尘器更轻、更灵活的版本。它比原版轻了约一磅，拥有 60 分钟的续航时间，功率为 350 瓦（原版为 380 瓦）。它还配备了与原版型号相同的两个附件。

The Speed moves smoothly over carpets and rugs while still picking up a ton of debris; I used this vacuum on my carpeted bedrooms the day after vacuuming with the V16 Piston Animal, and it pulled up tons of dust and cat hair from my rug. The vacuum can bend, too, so you can get underneath furniture without hunching over.
Speed 在地毯和垫子上移动顺滑，同时能吸走大量碎屑；我在用 V16 Piston Animal 吸尘后的第二天，用这款吸尘器清理了铺设地毯的卧室，它从地毯里吸出了大量的灰尘和猫毛。这款吸尘器还可以弯曲，因此你无需弯腰就能清理家具底部。

My only frustration is that the head of the vacuum gets stuck upright frequently when you're using it in handheld mode. It also doesn't have the edge detection that the main model features. If you go for the Clean & Empty version, though, you’ll get a self-emptying charger base and prettier color options. Overall, either the Speed or the regular PowerDetect makes a great investment, but I prefer the lower prices of the former model.
我唯一感到沮丧的是，当你在手持模式下使用时，吸尘器的刷头经常会卡在直立位置。它也没有主打型号所具备的边缘检测功能。不过，如果你选择“Clean & Empty”版本，你将获得一个自动清空的充电基站和更好看的配色。总的来说，Speed 或普通版 PowerDetect 都是不错的投资，但我更倾向于价格较低的前者。
