# Inference-Time Policy Alignment for Fair Reinforcement Learning
# 推理时策略对齐：实现公平的强化学习

**Abstract:** Deep reinforcement learning (RL) agents achieve strong performance by optimizing scalar reward functions. However, once deployed, the policies of these RL agents are often rigid and costly to adapt to new performance criteria. For instance, an agent trained to maximize expected cumulative reward may not accommodate previously unknown stakeholder preferences.

**摘要：** 深度强化学习（RL）智能体通过优化标量奖励函数来实现强大的性能。然而，一旦部署，这些强化学习智能体的策略往往是僵化的，且难以适应新的性能标准。例如，一个旨在最大化预期累积奖励的智能体，可能无法满足先前未知的利益相关者偏好。

Existing approaches to achieve fairness, a type of preference, in RL typically assume that such preferences are known a priori and require complete retraining of the policy under a fairness-oriented metric. Inspired by inference-time alignment in large language models, we investigate the problem of steering a pretrained RL policy toward welfare-based fairness objectives at inference time without updating the base policy's parameters.

现有的在强化学习中实现公平性（一种偏好类型）的方法，通常假设这些偏好是先验已知的，并且需要根据公平性导向的指标对策略进行完全重训练。受大语言模型中“推理时对齐”技术的启发，我们研究了如何在推理阶段引导预训练的强化学习策略向基于福利的公平性目标靠拢，且无需更新基础策略的参数。

We formalize inference-time fairness alignment as a policy shaping problem and propose a multiplicative policy shaping framework that adjusts action probabilities using action-dependent welfare scores, thus requiring no modification to the base policy. Our framework is general and compatible with any deep RL agent.

我们将推理时的公平性对齐形式化为一个“策略塑造”（policy shaping）问题，并提出了一种乘法策略塑造框架。该框架利用与动作相关的福利评分来调整动作概率，从而无需对基础策略进行任何修改。我们的框架具有通用性，可与任何深度强化学习智能体兼容。

Through extensive experiments across multiple domains, we demonstrate that inference-time policy shaping substantially improves welfare-based fairness objectives while preserving core task performance.

通过在多个领域进行的广泛实验，我们证明了推理时策略塑造能够在保持核心任务性能的同时，显著提升基于福利的公平性目标。
