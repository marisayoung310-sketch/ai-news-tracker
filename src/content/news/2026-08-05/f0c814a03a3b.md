# Uncertainty-Aware Simulation-Based Inference for Operations Research with Large Language Models
# 面向运筹学的大语言模型：基于不确定性感知与模拟的推理框架

**Abstract:** Deploying large language models (LLMs) for operations research (OR) tasks remains challenging because correctness depends on a coherent modeling process, not merely a correct final answer. Standard autoregressive generation operates on a myopic policy, which sometimes fails to anticipate whether a partial formulation can be validly extended into a globally consistent optimization model. Consequently, locally plausible steps may propagate into catastrophic downstream formulation or solver code errors.

**摘要：** 将大语言模型（LLM）应用于运筹学（OR）任务仍然充满挑战，因为其正确性不仅取决于最终答案的准确性，更依赖于连贯的建模过程。标准的自回归生成采用的是一种“短视”策略，有时无法预判当前的局部公式是否能够有效地扩展为全局一致的优化模型。因此，局部看似合理的步骤可能会演变成下游建模或求解器代码的灾难性错误。

To address this, we propose an uncertainty-aware, training-free inference framework for OR mathematical modeling. Without updating model parameters, our method evaluates intermediate candidate steps using short lookahead simulations to quantify downstream predictive uncertainty or probability concentration. Candidates that demonstrate a higher likelihood of yielding coherent mathematical formulations are then dynamically selected via importance resampling.

为了解决这一问题，我们提出了一种面向运筹学数学建模的、无需训练的不确定性感知推理框架。该方法无需更新模型参数，而是通过短期的前瞻模拟来评估中间候选步骤，从而量化下游预测的不确定性或概率集中度。随后，通过重要性重采样（importance resampling）动态选择那些更有可能产生连贯数学公式的候选方案。

Empirical evaluations across multiple OR benchmarks (including NL4OPT, MAMO, and IndustryOR) demonstrate that our framework consistently outperforms both standard and low-temperature baselines, establishing an efficient, training-free paradigm for reliable OR formulation generation.

在多个运筹学基准测试（包括 NL4OPT、MAMO 和 IndustryOR）上的实证评估表明，我们的框架始终优于标准基准和低温度采样基准，为可靠的运筹学公式生成建立了一种高效、无需训练的新范式。
