# 4 Best Compression Boots: Therabody, Hyperice, and More (2026)
# 2026年四款最佳压缩恢复靴：Therabody、Hyperice 等品牌推荐

The appeal of compression boots is easy to understand: After an intense workout or a long run, these sleeves wrap around your legs and mimic the squeezing sensation of a massage. The sequential air pressure is meant to treat delayed onset muscle soreness (DOMS) and improve circulation, helping blood, oxygen, and nutrients through tired muscles.
压缩恢复靴的吸引力显而易见：在剧烈运动或长跑之后，这些套筒可以包裹住双腿，模拟按摩的挤压感。这种顺序气压旨在缓解延迟性肌肉酸痛（DOMS）并改善血液循环，从而帮助血液、氧气和营养物质输送到疲劳的肌肉中。

Unlike many wellness gadgets that make big promises with little evidence behind them, intermittent pneumatic compression has been used for decades in medical and rehabilitation settings. Today, brands like Hyperice and Therabody have created at-home systems designed for everyone from marathon runners to workers who spend long hours on their feet.
与许多夸大其词却缺乏证据支持的健康小工具不同，间歇性气动压缩技术在医疗和康复领域已经使用了几十年。如今，Hyperice 和 Therabody 等品牌已经开发出家用系统，专为从马拉松运动员到需要长时间站立工作的各类人群设计。

But with prices ranging from a few hundred to well over $1,000—like our top pick, the Therabody JetBoots Pro Plus—and add-ons like heat and red light therapy, it can be difficult to know which boots are worth the investment. I tested the best compression boots to find the models that deliver the ideal combination of comfort, performance, and customization.
然而，由于价格从几百美元到超过 1,000 美元不等（例如我们的首选产品 Therabody JetBoots Pro Plus），再加上热疗和红光疗法等附加功能，很难判断哪些产品值得投资。我测试了市面上最优秀的压缩恢复靴，旨在找出那些在舒适度、性能和定制化方面达到理想平衡的型号。

### Best Compression Boots Overall: Therabody JetBoots Pro Plus
### 综合最佳压缩恢复靴：Therabody JetBoots Pro Plus

The Therabody JetBoots Pro Plus are the most feature-packed recovery boots I tested, combining pneumatic compression, vibrations, and infrared LED light therapy in one wireless system.
Therabody JetBoots Pro Plus 是我测试过功能最全面的恢复靴，它将气动压缩、振动和红外 LED 光疗整合在一个无线系统中。

**Why WIRED recommends:** The compression and vibrating pairing is therapeutic, to say the least; it consistently left my legs feeling less stiff after workouts. Everything is controlled from an LCD screen built into the lead boot, so there’s no separate controller to keep track of. You can tailor every session to your preference, with durations ranging from 10 to 60 minutes in five-minute increments and manual controls to adjust both pressure—measured in millimeters of mercury (mmHg)—and vibration intensity. You can also choose from four compression modes and eight preset routines.
**WIRED 推荐理由：** 压缩与振动的结合堪称疗愈，它总能让我在运动后感到腿部僵硬感明显减轻。所有功能均可通过内置在主靴上的 LCD 屏幕进行控制，因此无需担心弄丢独立的控制器。你可以根据个人喜好定制每次疗程，时长从 10 到 60 分钟不等（以 5 分钟为增量），并可通过手动控制调节压力（以毫米汞柱 mmHg 为单位）和振动强度。此外，你还可以选择四种压缩模式和八种预设程序。

**The trade-offs:** Therabody doesn’t publish key specs about the LEDs, and I didn’t notice a significant difference in my post-workout recovery that I could attribute to the infrared light therapy. The boots also don’t connect to the Therabody app, which may disappoint some users, but I didn’t find it necessary. At $1,150, these are also among the priciest compression boots on the market.
**不足之处：** Therabody 没有公布关于 LED 灯的关键规格，我在运动后的恢复过程中也没有察觉到红外光疗带来的显著差异。此外，这款靴子无法连接 Therabody 应用程序，这可能会让一些用户感到失望，但我个人认为这并非必要。1,150 美元的价格也使其成为市场上最昂贵的压缩恢复靴之一。

### Best Entry-Level Compression Boots: Therabody JetBoots Prime
### 最佳入门级压缩恢复靴：Therabody JetBoots Prime

If all you want is effective pneumatic compression, the Therabody JetBoots Prime delivers the essentials at a more affordable price point.
如果你只需要有效的气动压缩功能，Therabody JetBoots Prime 以更实惠的价格提供了核心体验。

**Why WIRED recommends:** For half the cost of the JetBoots Pro Plus, the Prime skips the vibration and infrared LED therapies but still offers four compression levels (25, 50, 75, and 100 mmHg) and three session lengths (20, 40, or 60 minutes), all controlled from a built-in panel on the boot. They’re wireless compression boots, offer up to three hours of battery life, and can even be used while charging. Weighing about 6 pounds, they’re surprisingly portable and fold down into the included drawstring backpack.
**WIRED 推荐理由：** Prime 的价格仅为 JetBoots Pro Plus 的一半，虽然省去了振动和红外 LED 疗法，但仍提供四种压缩级别（25、50、75 和 100 mmHg）和三种疗程时长（20、40 或 60 分钟），所有功能均可通过靴子上的内置面板控制。它们是无线压缩靴，电池续航长达三小时，甚至可以在充电时使用。重量约为 6 磅（约 2.7 公斤），便携性出人意料，可以折叠放入附赠的抽绳背包中。

**The trade-offs:** Customization is limited compared to pricier models. While the boots are foldable, they’re still bulky enough that squeezing them into the drawstring bag can be a bit of a hassle.
**不足之处：** 与更昂贵的型号相比，定制化功能有限。虽然靴子可以折叠，但体积依然较大，塞进抽绳背包时可能会有些费劲。

### Best Portable Compression Boots: Hyperice Normatec Go
### 最佳便携式压缩恢复靴：Hyperice Normatec Go

If you want the recovery benefits of compression therapy without investing in full-length boots, the Normatec Go is the easiest system to live with.
如果你想获得压缩疗法的恢复效果，又不想投资全腿式恢复靴，那么 Normatec Go 是最易于使用的系统。

**Why WIRED recommends:** These calf sleeves ditch the hoses and external control unit in favor of a wearable design. You can recover while making dinner, working from home, or walking around the airport. Each sleeve delivers 360-degree dynamic air compression across three overlapping zones with seven intensity levels. They’re compact, TSA-approved, and light enough to pack in a carry-on or personal item. They’re also compatible with the Hyperice app.
**WIRED 推荐理由：** 这款小腿套筒摒弃了软管和外部控制单元，采用了可穿戴设计。你可以在做饭、居家办公或在机场行走时进行恢复。每个套筒可在三个重叠区域提供 360 度动态空气压缩，并具有七个强度级别。它们体积小巧，符合 TSA（美国运输安全管理局）规定，重量轻便，足以放入随身行李或个人物品中。此外，它们还兼容 Hyperice 应用程序。

**The trade-offs:** Since the Normatec Go only targets your calves, you won’t get the full leg compression that traditional boots provide.
**不足之处：** 由于 Normatec Go 仅针对小腿，你无法获得传统全腿式恢复靴所提供的完整腿部压缩效果。
