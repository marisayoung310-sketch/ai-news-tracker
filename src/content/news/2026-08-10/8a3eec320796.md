# How to Find the Beat of a Song (BPM + Key)
# 如何寻找歌曲的节拍（BPM + 音调）

Originally published on IFEELVOID. Play the song and count the pulse for 15 seconds. Multiply that number by four. If you counted 35 beats, the song is roughly 140 BPM. That is the fastest manual way to find the beat of a song.
本文最初发布于 IFEELVOID。播放歌曲并数出 15 秒内的脉冲次数，将该数字乘以四。如果你数出 35 次节拍，那么这首歌大约是 140 BPM。这是手动寻找歌曲节拍最快的方法。

It is also where the confusion starts. A trap record at 140 BPM can feel like 70. A drumless intro can hide the pulse completely. A sample can drift. And knowing the tempo still does not tell you the musical key you need for bass lines, vocal tuning, remixes, or harmonic mixing. This guide gives you the manual method, the DAW method, and the faster analysis workflow I use when a session cannot stop for guesswork.
这也是困惑的开始。一首 140 BPM 的 Trap 音乐听起来可能只有 70 BPM。没有鼓点的开头可能会完全掩盖脉冲。采样可能会出现漂移。此外，仅知道速度并不能告诉你制作低音线、人声调音、混音或和声混音所需的音乐调性。本指南将介绍手动方法、DAW（数字音频工作站）方法，以及我在无法停下来进行猜测的创作过程中所使用的快速分析工作流。

First: what does “beat” mean? People use “beat” to describe three different things: The pulse: the steady count you nod your head to. The BPM: how many pulses happen in one minute. The instrumental: the drums, melody, bass, and arrangement behind a vocal. If you need the tempo and key so you can work with the audio, keep going.
首先：什么是“节拍”（beat）？人们用“节拍”来描述三种不同的事物：脉冲（Pulse）：你点头时跟随的稳定节拍；BPM：一分钟内有多少次脉冲；伴奏（Instrumental）：人声背后的鼓点、旋律、低音和编曲。如果你需要速度和调性以便处理音频，请继续往下看。

### Method 1: count the BPM manually
### 方法 1：手动计算 BPM

Find the strongest repeating pulse. In most trap and hip-hop records, start with the snare or clap. Count along for 15 seconds, then multiply by four. Start the song at a section where the drums are clear. Tap your foot or nod to the main pulse. Count every pulse for exactly 15 seconds. Multiply the count by four. Repeat once to make sure your count is stable. Twenty beats in 15 seconds is 80 BPM. Thirty beats is 120 BPM. Thirty-five beats is 140 BPM.
找到最强烈的重复脉冲。在大多数 Trap 和嘻哈音乐中，从军鼓或拍手声开始。跟随节奏数 15 秒，然后乘以四。从鼓点清晰的部分开始播放歌曲。用脚打拍子或跟随主脉冲点头。准确数出 15 秒内的每一次脉冲。将计数值乘以四。重复一次以确保计数稳定。15 秒内 20 次节拍即为 80 BPM，30 次为 120 BPM，35 次为 140 BPM。

### Watch for half-time and double-time
### 注意半速（Half-time）和倍速（Double-time）

A beat can be represented at two mathematically correct tempos. A dark trap record may read as 70 BPM or 140 BPM depending on whether you count the slow backbeat or the faster production grid. Neither number is automatically wrong. Use the tempo that matches your purpose. Producers usually want the grid that makes drum placement and subdivisions easy. DJs may want the value that matches the rest of their library.
一个节拍可以用两个数学上都正确的速度来表示。一首暗黑风格的 Trap 音乐可能被读作 70 BPM 或 140 BPM，这取决于你数的是缓慢的后拍还是更快的制作网格。这两个数字都没有错。使用符合你目的的速度即可。制作人通常需要方便放置鼓点和细分的网格；而 DJ 可能需要与他们曲库中其他歌曲相匹配的数值。

### Method 2: use tap tempo
### 方法 2：使用 Tap Tempo（点击测速）

Most DAWs, DJ applications, and metronome tools include tap tempo. Play the loudest section and tap once per beat for at least eight bars. The longer you tap consistently, the less one bad hit affects the average. Tap tempo works well when the drums are clear, the track follows a stable grid, and you only need a fast estimate. It gets unreliable when a live drummer pushes and pulls, the track changes tempo, or the intro has no rhythmic anchor.
大多数 DAW、DJ 软件和节拍器工具都包含 Tap Tempo 功能。播放最响亮的部分，并按节拍点击至少八个小节。点击的时间越长且越稳定，单次失误对平均值的影响就越小。当鼓点清晰、轨道遵循稳定网格且你只需要快速估算时，Tap Tempo 非常有效。但当现场鼓手节奏不稳、轨道变速或开头没有节奏锚点时，它就不可靠了。

### Method 3: find the BPM inside your DAW
### 方法 3：在 DAW 中查找 BPM

Drag the song into your DAW and line up a clear downbeat with the grid. Move forward eight or sixteen bars. If the transients slowly drift away from the grid, adjust the project tempo until both the beginning and end line up. This method is slower, but it exposes details an automatic detector can miss: tempo changes between sections; loose live timing; samples that were not stretched cleanly; notes before the first downbeat; half-time versus double-time interpretation. If you are preparing a remix or recording over the track, this is the final check. A BPM number is useful. A grid that stays locked through the entire arrangement is better.
将歌曲拖入 DAW，并将一个清晰的强拍与网格对齐。向前移动八或十六个小节。如果瞬态信号（transients）慢慢偏离网格，调整项目速度直到开头和结尾都对齐。这种方法较慢，但能揭示自动检测器可能遗漏的细节：段落间的速度变化、松散的现场节奏、未处理干净的采样、第一拍之前的音符、半速与倍速的解读。如果你正在准备混音或在轨道上录音，这是最后的检查。BPM 数值很有用，但贯穿整个编曲始终锁定的网格更好。

### Verify the result
### 核实结果

Do not trust one number blindly. Turn on a metronome. The click should stay with the track for more than a few bars. Try half and double. If 75 feels wrong, test 150. Check a drum-heavy section. Intros and breakdowns can mislead detectors. Listen for drift. A correct starting point that slides away may indicate live timing or a tempo change. Confirm the key with an instrument. Play the detected root note under the chorus and listen for stability. The goal is not to worship the detector. The goal is to get close instantly, verify with your ears, and move on with the session.
不要盲目相信一个数字。打开节拍器。节拍声应与轨道保持同步超过几个小节。尝试半速和倍速。如果 75 感觉不对，测试 150。检查鼓点密集的部分。开头和间奏可能会误导检测器。听是否有漂移。一个正确的起点如果逐渐偏离，可能意味着现场节奏不稳或有速度变化。用乐器确认调性。在副歌下弹奏检测到的根音，听其是否稳定。目标不是盲从检测器，而是瞬间获得接近的结果，用耳朵核实，然后继续你的创作。

### Why BPM and key matter together
### 为什么 BPM 和调性同样重要

BPM tells you how fast the song moves. Key tells you which notes will feel stable against it. You need both when matching an 808 to a sample, building a remix, tuning vocals, choosing compatible loops, planning harmonic DJ transitions, or comparing reference tracks during a mix. Camelot notation translates musical keys into DJ-friendly codes. Compatible codes make it easier to find transitions that do not fight harmonically. It is not a replacement for listening. It is a faster map.
BPM 告诉你歌曲的速度，调性告诉你哪些音符与之配合会感觉稳定。在将 808 低音与采样匹配、制作混音、人声调音、选择兼容的 Loop、规划 DJ 和声转场或在混音时对比参考轨道时，两者缺一不可。Camelot 记谱法将音乐调性转换为 DJ 友好的代码。兼容的代码使寻找不会产生和声冲突的转场变得更容易。它不能替代聆听，但它是一张更快的地图。
