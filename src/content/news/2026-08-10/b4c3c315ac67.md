# No Dogs in Space is a music history podcast for true obsessives
# 《No Dogs in Space》：献给音乐狂热爱好者的音乐史播客

Sadly, there hasn’t been a new episode of *No Dogs in Space* since July of 2024. Part four of the podcast’s series on Can wrapped up an abbreviated season three, which focused on experimental rock. Don’t get me wrong, I loved seasons one and two, but the show seemed to be gearing up for a deep dive into Krautrock, and I was hype. Unfortunately, that episode ended with hosts Marcus Parks (*Last Podcast on the Left*) and Carolina Hidalgo declaring they were going to do another season on punk (the topic of season one), only for the feed to go quiet.

遗憾的是，自 2024 年 7 月以来，《No Dogs in Space》就再也没有更新过新剧集了。该播客关于德国实验摇滚乐队 Can 的第四部分内容，为聚焦实验摇滚的第三季画上了简短的句号。别误会，我很喜欢第一季和第二季，但当时节目似乎正准备深入探讨德国泡菜摇滚（Krautrock），我对此兴奋不已。不幸的是，那一集结束时，主持人 Marcus Parks（《Last Podcast on the Left》成员）和 Carolina Hidalgo 宣布他们将制作另一季关于朋克音乐的内容（第一季的主题），随后播客频道便陷入了沉寂。

If you’re a music obsessive, this is a must-listen podcast. Over the course of 73 episodes, the husband-and-wife duo digs into the careers of the Beastie Boys, The Velvet Underground, Joy Division, and more. Even if you think you know everything there is to know about iconic bands like The Stooges, I guarantee there will be new info here.

如果你是一个音乐狂热爱好者，这绝对是一档必听的播客。在 73 集的节目中，这对夫妻档深入挖掘了野兽男孩（Beastie Boys）、地下丝绒（The Velvet Underground）、快乐小分队（Joy Division）等乐队的职业生涯。即使你认为自己已经了解像丑角乐队（The Stooges）这样标志性乐队的一切，我敢保证，你依然能在这里发现新的知识点。

The show dedicates multiple hours over the course of several episodes to its subjects. Its two-part intro to Krautrock, which heavily focuses on Amon Düül II is over three hours long, and that’s on the shorter side. The Replacements series is nearly nine hours long, and Beastie Boys is even longer still.

该节目会用数集的时间，花费数小时来深度剖析其主题。他们关于德国泡菜摇滚的两部分入门介绍（重点关注了 Amon Düül II 乐队）时长超过三小时，而这在节目中还算短的。关于 The Replacements 乐队的系列节目时长近九小时，而关于野兽男孩的系列则更长。

Over these exceptionally long series, it never gets dull, though. The two balance scripted info-dense sections with more improvisational sidebars expertly. They also, as you’d expect from a married couple, have great chemistry on mic. They also make sure to share all their sources at the end of the episode in case you want to track down the primary source or do your own fact-checking.

尽管这些系列节目异常漫长，但它们从未让人感到枯燥。两位主持人巧妙地平衡了信息量密集的剧本内容与即兴的侧面讨论。正如你对一对夫妻所期待的那样，他们在麦克风前展现出了极佳的默契。此外，他们还会确保在每集结尾分享所有参考资料，以备你想追溯原始来源或进行事实核查。

The show isn’t officially over. As recently as a couple of months ago, Carolina was responding to Instagram comments saying she’d love to do more episodes. And the possibility of the show returning has come up multiple times on live streams. But as of this writing, we’re still waiting on that Punk Rock 2.0 series.

该节目并未正式完结。就在几个月前，Carolina 在回复 Instagram 评论时还表示，她很乐意制作更多剧集。在直播中，节目回归的可能性也多次被提及。但截至本文撰写之时，我们仍在等待那个“朋克摇滚 2.0”系列。

*No Dogs in Space* is available on most podcast platforms, including Apple Podcasts, Pocketcasts, and Spotify.

《No Dogs in Space》可在大多数播客平台收听，包括 Apple Podcasts、Pocketcasts 和 Spotify。
