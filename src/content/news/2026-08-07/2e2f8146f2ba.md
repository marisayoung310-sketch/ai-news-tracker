### Title: On non-rooted Android 17, ADB uninstall of system apps fails
### 标题：在非 Root 的 Android 17 上，通过 ADB 卸载系统应用将失效

**Description**
**描述**

The "Disable mode" ("Freeze" in AppManager) should be the default. "Uninstall" causes too much problems.
“禁用模式”（即 AppManager 中的“冻结”）应作为默认选项。“卸载”会导致太多问题。
