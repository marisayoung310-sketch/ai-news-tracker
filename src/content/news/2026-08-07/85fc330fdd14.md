# The Download: Google’s AI shake-up and Meta’s rogue model
# 每日下载：谷歌的 AI 大洗牌与 Meta 的“流氓”模型

This is today's edition of The Download, our weekday newsletter that provides a daily dose of what's going on in the world of technology. Google’s AI empire is being reshaped. Here’s what’s changed. After a wave of painful losses in the tech talent wars, delays to its next flagship model, and murmurings of poor morale, Google has announced a major shake-up to its AI operations. Here's a quick rundown of what’s happened and why it matters.
这是我们工作日时事通讯《每日下载》的今日版，为您提供每日科技界动态。谷歌的 AI 帝国正在重塑，以下是具体变化。在经历了科技人才争夺战中的惨痛损失、下一代旗舰模型的延期以及关于士气低落的传言后，谷歌宣布对其 AI 业务进行重大调整。以下是事件经过及其重要性的简要概述。

DeepMind CEO Demis Hassabis is stepping back from running the unit day-to-day. He’s becoming the unit’s chairman and Alphabet's chief scientist, a broader role. DeepMind will now be led by CTO Koray Kavukcuoglu, under the title of senior vice-president.
DeepMind 首席执行官 Demis Hassabis 将不再负责该部门的日常运营。他将担任该部门主席及 Alphabet 首席科学家，这是一个更广泛的职位。DeepMind 现在将由首席技术官 Koray Kavukcuoglu 领导，头衔为高级副总裁。

DeepMind may be absorbed into Google's wider business. Google is expected to tighten its control over the AI lab, which it acquired 12 years ago.
DeepMind 可能会被整合进谷歌更广泛的业务中。预计谷歌将加强对这个 12 年前收购的 AI 实验室的控制。

Jeff Dean is leaving to start a new AI company. After 27 years at Google, the company’s former chief scientist is launching Discovery Loop with three former colleagues. The startup’s goal is to fully automate the process of scientific research, and Google is one of its early investors.
Jeff Dean 即将离职创办一家新的 AI 公司。在谷歌工作 27 年后，这位前首席科学家将与三位前同事共同创立 Discovery Loop。该初创公司的目标是实现科学研究过程的全面自动化，谷歌是其早期投资者之一。

The changes come amid financial concerns at Google AI. In the latest quarter, the company turned cash flow negative for the first time on record. Google is now concentrating its AI leadership in California. The company is now shifting its AI science strategy. It’s moving from specialized tools, like DeepMind’s AlphaFold, toward agentic AI systems that can conduct research more autonomously. Find out more in our recent story.
这些变动是在谷歌 AI 面临财务担忧的背景下发生的。在最近一个季度，该公司有史以来首次出现现金流为负的情况。谷歌目前正将其 AI 领导层集中在加利福尼亚州。公司正在调整其 AI 科学战略，从 DeepMind 的 AlphaFold 等专业工具转向能够更自主地进行研究的代理式 AI 系统。更多详情请阅读我们近期的报道。

**How AI can help supercharge creativity**

Generative tools put out by companies like OpenAI and Google DeepMind can automate a striking range of creative tasks and offer near-instant gratification—but at what cost? Some artists and researchers fear that such technology could turn us into passive consumers of yet more AI slop. And so they are looking for ways to inject human creativity back into the process. The aim is to develop AI tools that augment our creativity rather than strip it from us—pushing us to be better at composing music, developing games, designing toys, and much more—and lay the groundwork for a future in which humans and machines create things together. Read how generative models could push artists to make things that couldn’t have been made before, and give all of us creative superpowers. —Will Douglas Heaven
