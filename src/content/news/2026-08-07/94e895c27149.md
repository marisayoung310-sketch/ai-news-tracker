# Jony Ive’s first OpenAI gadget is reportedly a hockey puck-sized smart speaker
# 乔尼·艾维（Jony Ive）的首款 OpenAI 设备据传为冰球大小的智能音箱

The “doughnut-shaped” device could cost more than $300 when it launches next year, according to Bloomberg.
据彭博社报道，这款“甜甜圈形状”的设备在明年推出时，价格可能超过 300 美元。

The AI device OpenAI is developing with former Apple designer Jony Ive is “essentially a smart speaker without a display” that’s battery-powered, doughnut-shaped and roughly the size of a hockey puck, according to Bloomberg reporter Mark Gurman.
据彭博社记者马克·古尔曼（Mark Gurman）报道，OpenAI 正在与前苹果设计师乔尼·艾维合作开发一款人工智能设备，它“本质上是一款没有显示屏的智能音箱”，采用电池供电，呈甜甜圈形状，大小约为一个冰球。

The device, expected to launch in 2027 for a price over $300, reportedly “will have a unique look, complete with moving parts,” including some that will move on their own to show when the device is responding or interacting with the user, along with lights, a camera system, and other sensors.
该设备预计将于 2027 年发布，售价超过 300 美元。据报道，它“将拥有独特的外观，并配有活动部件”，其中一些部件会自动移动，以显示设备何时正在响应或与用户交互，此外还配备了灯光、摄像头系统和其他传感器。

Gurman’s sources also say the “battery-powered” device is designed to be “easy to carry around the home with one hand,” similar to Amazon’s now-discontinued Tap speaker.
古尔曼的消息来源还称，这款“电池供电”的设备旨在“方便单手在家中携带”，类似于亚马逊现已停产的 Tap 音箱。

Gurman says it will “work similarly to the company’s ChatGPT voice mode on smartphone apps, but with more advanced models for humanlike interactivity” but look and feel nothing like an Apple product — despite their ongoing lawsuit.
古尔曼表示，它的“工作方式类似于该公司智能手机应用上的 ChatGPT 语音模式，但采用了更先进的模型以实现类人的交互”，尽管双方目前存在诉讼，但其外观和手感与苹果产品毫无相似之处。

The speaker / pocket personal assistant could be the first in a “family of devices” OpenAI has planned, but we’ll probably hear a lot more about all of them before it eventually arrives.
这款音箱/袖珍个人助理可能是 OpenAI 计划推出的“设备系列”中的第一款，但在它最终问世之前，我们可能会听到更多关于这些设备的消息。
