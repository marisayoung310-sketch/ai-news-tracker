# goauthentik / authentik

**What is authentik? authentik is an open-source Identity Provider (IdP) for modern SSO. It supports SAML, OAuth2/OIDC, LDAP, RADIUS, and more, designed for self-hosting from small labs to large production clusters. Our enterprise offering is available for organizations to securely replace existing IdPs such as Okta, Auth0, Entra ID, and Ping Identity for robust, large-scale identity management.**

什么是 authentik？authentik 是一个用于现代单点登录（SSO）的开源身份提供商（IdP）。它支持 SAML、OAuth2/OIDC、LDAP、RADIUS 等多种协议，专为从小型实验室到大型生产集群的自托管需求而设计。我们还提供企业级版本，供组织安全地替换现有的 IdP（如 Okta、Auth0、Entra ID 和 Ping Identity），以实现稳健的大规模身份管理。
