# GEB-Bench: Abstract Structures Told in Many Voices
# GEB-Bench：以多种形式呈现的抽象结构

Can a model look at a river delta and a lightning bolt and see that they share a structure? We introduce GEB-Bench, a benchmark whose unit is an abstract structural motif--self-reference, a strange loop, a Mobius twist--in the spirit of Godel, Escher, Bach. 

模型能否在观察河流三角洲和闪电时，识别出它们共享某种结构？我们引入了 GEB-Bench，这是一个以抽象结构母题（如自指、怪圈、莫比乌斯环）为基本单元的基准测试，其灵感源自《哥德尔、埃舍尔、巴赫》（GEB）。

Each motif is told in several voices: a natural scene whose composition is the structure, a folk story whose telling enacts it through a mechanically checkable form device, a mathematical theorem, and a programmatic skeleton; surface parameters are declared nuisance variables and never scored. 

每个母题都通过多种“声音”来呈现：一种是构图体现该结构的自然场景，一种是通过机械可校验形式装置来演绎结构的民间故事，一种是数学定理，还有一种是程序框架；表面参数被视为干扰变量，不计入评分。

Motifs, voices, and the structural changes between them form a small cross-modal category, and GEB-Bench's tasks are its questions. Evaluating twelve open and proprietary models, we find that abstraction failure is lawful. 

母题、声音以及它们之间的结构转换构成了一个小型跨模态范畴，而 GEB-Bench 的任务正是针对该范畴提出的问题。通过评估十二个开源及闭源模型，我们发现抽象能力的失效是有规律可循的。

The central finding is a gap between recognition and cross-voice mapping: models identify a structure within one voice far better than they carry it across voices; every model pays this tax, and mapping strong enough to narrow it appears only at the frontier tier. 

核心发现是识别能力与跨声音映射能力之间存在差距：模型在单一声音内识别结构的能力远强于跨声音迁移结构的能力；每个模型都为此付出了代价，只有处于前沿梯队的模型才具备足够强大的映射能力来缩小这一差距。

Two patterns support it. Errors align more strongly with the designed formal geometry than with measured perceptual geometries, and frontier models from different vendors converge on the same wrong answers; and surface complexity taxes every model that reads structure, with capacity buying headroom rather than immunity. 

两个模式支持了这一结论。首先，错误更倾向于与设计的形式几何结构对齐，而非感知的几何结构，且来自不同厂商的前沿模型往往会得出相同的错误答案；其次，表面复杂性对所有读取结构的模型都造成了负担，模型容量的提升带来的是处理空间的增加，而非对复杂性的免疫。

GEB-Bench is fully generative and released with its pipeline. 

GEB-Bench 是完全生成式的，并已随其处理流水线一同发布。
