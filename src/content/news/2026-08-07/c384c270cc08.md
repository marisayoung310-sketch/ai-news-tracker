# Perception Before Reasoning: Dynamic Latent Reasoning for Video Understanding and Question Answering

**Title:** Perception Before Reasoning: Dynamic Latent Reasoning for Video Understanding and Question Answering
**标题：** 先感知，后推理：用于视频理解与问答的动态潜在推理 (Dynamic Latent Reasoning)

**Abstract:** Video question answering requires models to ground language queries in visual evidence and, when necessary, reason over that evidence across time. Existing methods typically rely on long textual chain-of-thought rationales, even though many questions can be answered as soon as the relevant object, action, or frame is localized.
**摘要：** 视频问答要求模型将语言查询锚定在视觉证据上，并在必要时跨时间对这些证据进行推理。现有的方法通常依赖于冗长的文本思维链（Chain-of-Thought），尽管许多问题在定位到相关的物体、动作或帧时即可回答。

We propose Dynamic Latent Reasoning (DyLaR), which first grounds a question in a short block of perception latents (continuous hidden states that encode query-relevant visual evidence), and then adaptively decides whether to append reasoning latents (continuous thoughts that reason over this evidence in latent space) before answering.
我们提出了动态潜在推理（DyLaR），它首先将问题锚定在一小块感知潜在向量（编码查询相关视觉证据的连续隐藏状态）中，然后自适应地决定在回答前是否需要附加推理潜在向量（在潜在空间中对这些证据进行推理的连续思维）。

DyLaR learns this behavior by grounding perception latents in verified visual evidence and distilling verified rationales into reasoning latents, followed by reinforcement learning that further refines when to reason.
DyLaR 通过将感知潜在向量锚定在经过验证的视觉证据中，并将经过验证的推理逻辑提炼为推理潜在向量来学习这种行为，随后通过强化学习进一步优化推理的时机。

Across nine video benchmarks and four multimodal language model backbones, DyLaR improves average accuracy over same-backbone baselines while generating fewer than 20 tokens per query. On Qwen3-VL-4B, for example, DyLaR improves average accuracy over Qwen3-VL-4B-Thinking from 54.0 to 58.2 while reducing response length from 1,220.7 to 18.5 tokens per query.
在九个视频基准测试和四个多模态语言模型主干网络上，DyLaR 在提升平均准确率的同时，每个查询生成的 Token 数少于 20 个。以 Qwen3-VL-4B 为例，DyLaR 将 Qwen3-VL-4B-Thinking 的平均准确率从 54.0 提升至 58.2，同时将每个查询的响应长度从 1,220.7 个 Token 缩减至 18.5 个。

Ablations further show that grounded perception latents, rationale-supervised reasoning latents, and adaptive routing each improve accuracy.
消融实验进一步表明，锚定的感知潜在向量、基于推理逻辑监督的推理潜在向量以及自适应路由机制均能有效提升准确率。
