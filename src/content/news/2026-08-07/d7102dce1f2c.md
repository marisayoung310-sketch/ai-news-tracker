# LoRetta: A Foundation Model and Extensive Dataset for Global-Scale Remote Sensing Dense Image Matching
# LoRetta：用于全球尺度遥感密集图像匹配的基础模型与大规模数据集

**Abstract:** Dense image matching establishes pixel-wise correspondences and underpins broad applications in computer vision and photogrammetry. However, extending dense matching to global-scale remote sensing remains challenging because image pairs may differ in acquisition time, season, viewpoint, spatial resolution, and land-cover state. The resulting large geometric offsets, partial overlap, and intrinsically unmatchable regions make direct dense correspondence prediction unreliable and inefficient.

**摘要：** 密集图像匹配旨在建立像素级的对应关系，是计算机视觉和摄影测量领域广泛应用的基础。然而，将密集匹配扩展到全球尺度的遥感领域仍然充满挑战，因为图像对在获取时间、季节、视角、空间分辨率和地表覆盖状态等方面可能存在巨大差异。由此产生的巨大几何偏移、部分重叠以及本质上无法匹配的区域，使得直接进行密集对应关系预测变得不可靠且效率低下。

We thus reformulate dense matching as localization-and-registration: first localizing the matchable overlap and affine geometry, then refining dense residuals within the aligned frame. Based on this formulation, we propose LoRetta, a foundation model coupling matchability-aware affine localization with guided dense registration.

因此，我们将密集匹配重新定义为“定位与配准”过程：首先定位可匹配的重叠区域和仿射几何，然后在对齐的框架内细化密集残差。基于这一构想，我们提出了 LoRetta，这是一个将“可匹配性感知仿射定位”与“引导式密集配准”相结合的基础模型。

We also introduce LEVIR-GM, a global-scale multi-temporal optical matching benchmark with dataset-native matchability labels (103K aligned, 827K augmented pairs, six continents, five years, 0.5-1024 m resolution). We further establish a unified evaluation protocol for sparse, semi-dense, and dense matchers.

我们还引入了 LEVIR-GM，这是一个全球尺度的多时相光学匹配基准，包含数据集原生的可匹配性标签（涵盖六大洲、五年跨度、0.5-1024米分辨率，包含 10.3 万对对齐图像和 82.7 万对增强图像）。此外，我们还为稀疏、半密集和密集匹配器建立了一套统一的评估协议。

On LEVIR-GM, LoRetta achieves an area under the curve (AUC) of 83.3%, outperforming the strongest baseline RoMa v2 by 1.6 points, with larger percentage of correct keypoints (PCK) gains of 6.5 and 8.2 points at 1 and 2 pixels, while reducing inference latency by 47.8%. Astronaut-to-satellite and unmanned aerial vehicle (UAV)-to-satellite geolocalization experiments further demonstrate its transferability as a reusable geometric aligner.

在 LEVIR-GM 基准测试中，LoRetta 的曲线下面积（AUC）达到了 83.3%，比最强的基准模型 RoMa v2 高出 1.6 个百分点；在 1 像素和 2 像素误差阈值下的正确关键点百分比（PCK）分别提升了 6.5 和 8.2 个百分点，同时推理延迟降低了 47.8%。宇航员视角到卫星视角以及无人机（UAV）到卫星的地理定位实验，进一步证明了其作为可复用几何对齐器的迁移能力。
