# Suno shares plans to combat spammy AI music
# Suno 分享打击垃圾 AI 音乐的计划

Suno announced plans to implement a new watermarking technology and download policy to limit the spread of spammy AI tracks and increase transparency. In a lengthy blog post, CEO and co-founder Mikey Shulman laid out the company’s principles and the next steps for the company as it seeks legitimacy.

Suno 宣布计划实施一项新的水印技术和下载政策，以限制垃圾 AI 音轨的传播并提高透明度。在一篇长篇博文中，首席执行官兼联合创始人 Mikey Shulman 阐述了该公司的原则以及在寻求合法化过程中将采取的后续步骤。

The company is rolling out new transparency tools, along with new watermarking and fingerprinting tech. Shulman says it aligns with “emerging industry standards” that will make it easier to identify Suno-generated content. He also says the company is aiming to partner with “distribution platforms on combatting fraud and misuse.”

该公司正在推出新的透明度工具，以及新的水印和指纹识别技术。Shulman 表示，这符合“新兴行业标准”，将使识别 Suno 生成的内容变得更加容易。他还表示，公司旨在与“分发平台合作，共同打击欺诈和滥用行为”。

Last year, the company also announced it would change its download policy following a settlement with Warner Music Group (WMG), and in the wake of a similar settlement between WMG and Udio that ended downloads of outputs from that platform entirely. The company hasn’t offered specifics yet, but at the time it announced the plan last year, it said that downloads would be limited to paying subscribers, and users would be limited to a certain number of downloads each month.

去年，在与华纳音乐集团 (WMG) 达成和解后，该公司还宣布将更改其下载政策。此前，WMG 与 Udio 达成了类似的协议，完全终止了该平台输出内容的下载。该公司尚未提供具体细节，但在去年宣布该计划时，曾表示下载将仅限于付费订阅用户，且用户每月的下载次数将受到限制。

Shulman’s post repeatedly mentions the company’s dedication to transparency, respect for rights holders, and the importance of human-made art. But he’s also careful to say it’s not Suno’s place to “pass judgment” on the value of individual pieces of art, and that it’s ultimately up to artists and platforms to decide whether they want to disclose the use of AI tools.

Shulman 的文章反复提到公司对透明度的承诺、对权利持有人的尊重以及人类创作艺术的重要性。但他同时也谨慎地表示，Suno 无权对单件艺术品的价值“做出评判”，最终应由艺术家和平台自行决定是否披露 AI 工具的使用情况。

Suno didn’t immediately respond to a request for specifics about the new policies and technology.

Suno 没有立即回应关于新政策和技术细节的置评请求。
