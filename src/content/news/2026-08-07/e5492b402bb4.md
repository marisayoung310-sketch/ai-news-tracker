# Trevor Noah is hosting Google’s Pixel 11 launch event
# 特雷弗·诺亚（Trevor Noah）将主持谷歌 Pixel 11 发布会

Google is set to host its next live Made by Google hardware launch event on August 12th, and the company says in a new video that comedian Trevor Noah will be hosting the show.
谷歌定于 8 月 12 日举办下一场“Made by Google”硬件发布会。公司在一段新视频中宣布，喜剧演员特雷弗·诺亚（Trevor Noah）将担任此次活动的主持人。

The video indicates that the event will feature other celebrities and influencers as well, including Call Her Daddy host Alex Cooper (who was at the 2025 event), Indian cricketer Shubman Gill (who has starred in Pixel ads), Japanese actress Ayami Nakajo (who has also starred in Pixel ads), and NBA star / Google Performance Advisor Stephen Curry. (Cooper and Noah are also bringing shows to YouTube.)
视频显示，此次活动还将邀请其他名人和网红出席，包括《Call Her Daddy》主持人亚历克斯·库珀（Alex Cooper，曾出席 2025 年发布会）、印度板球运动员舒布曼·吉尔（Shubman Gill，曾出演 Pixel 广告）、日本女演员中条彩未（Ayami Nakajo，也曾出演 Pixel 广告）以及 NBA 球星兼谷歌性能顾问斯蒂芬·库里（Stephen Curry）。（库珀和诺亚也正在将他们的节目引入 YouTube。）

Jimmy Fallon hosted the show last year, an event that my colleague Victoria Song described as more like “a live taping of The Tonight Show with Jimmy Fallon” than a traditional tech launch event. Given the celebrity faces set to appear at this year’s event, it seems that Google is once again aiming to reach a more mainstream audience. It’s also airing right at the end of the ET workday: the event, which takes place in New York City, starts at 6 PM ET.
去年吉米·法伦（Jimmy Fallon）主持了该发布会，我的同事维多利亚·宋（Victoria Song）形容那场活动与其说是传统的科技发布会，不如说是“吉米·法伦今夜秀的现场录制”。鉴于今年将有众多明星面孔出席，谷歌似乎再次旨在触达更广泛的主流受众。此外，活动时间定在美东时间工作日结束时：发布会将于美东时间下午 6 点在纽约市举行。

As for the gadgets you can expect, Google’s event teaser video features brief glimpses of a new phone and smartwatch, and the company has previously shown teases of the Pixel 11 Pro and Pixel 11 Pro Fold. There are a bunch of leaked images and specs of the upcoming hardware floating around, too. However, if you’re thinking about picking up one of Google’s new gadgets, you should be prepared for a higher price tag.
至于大家可以期待的设备，谷歌的预告视频中简短展示了一款新手机和智能手表，此前该公司也已经预告过 Pixel 11 Pro 和 Pixel 11 Pro Fold。目前网上也流传着大量关于这些即将发布硬件的泄露图片和规格信息。不过，如果你打算入手谷歌的新设备，最好做好迎接更高价格的心理准备。
