# 5 Best AI Notetakers (2026), Tested and Reviewed
# 2026年五款最佳AI笔记设备：测试与评测

Whether sitting in a class, meeting, or interview, I’ve never been fond of taking notes, and I’m far from alone. Not only does the process of scribbling something down cause me to miss what was said immediately after, but I also suffer from awful handwriting, meaning that I can rarely read the notes anyway. Recording interviews has long been a solution, but transcribing interviews is another step (with extra cost) that can leave you with thousands of words of material to sift through, much of it irrelevant.

无论是在课堂、会议还是采访中，我一直都不喜欢做笔记，而且我绝非个例。手写记录的过程不仅会导致我错过随后的谈话内容，而且我的字迹非常潦草，这意味着我事后往往根本看不懂自己写了什么。录音采访长期以来是一个解决方案，但转录采访是另一个步骤（且需要额外成本），这会让你面对数千字的材料需要筛选，其中大部分内容往往无关紧要。

AI notetakers—massively popular at CES 2026—have emerged to offer a new way of making IRL note-taking easier and faster, putting the power of AI into (or at least adjacent to) a portable device that evokes the microcassette recorder of yesteryear. As with all things AI, the jury’s out on whether AI notetakers are actually useful or if bypassing the intellectual rigor of writing down what people say is going to make us even stupider, but at the very least I’ve found them to be a convenient way to capture the main points of a conversation—and helpful at keeping a record of discussions and meetings I might otherwise have promptly forgotten.

AI笔记设备在2026年国际消费电子展（CES 2026）上大受欢迎，它们提供了一种让现实生活中的笔记记录变得更简单、更快捷的新方式。这些设备将AI的能力植入（或至少是结合）到便携式硬件中，让人联想到过去的老式微型磁带录音机。和所有AI产品一样，人们对于AI笔记设备是否真的有用，或者绕过记录谈话内容的智力锻炼是否会让我们变得更笨，目前尚无定论。但至少我发现，它们是捕捉对话要点的便捷方式，也有助于保存那些我本可能很快就会遗忘的讨论和会议记录。

For this guide, I evaluated several AI notetaker products, using them in person and online for meetings, interviews, and conference calls. In addition to “daily life” use cases, I tested each one by recording the same prerecorded presentation to use as a control. (I won’t identify it by name to mitigate the risk of companies attempting to tune their AI models for that specific dialog.) Throughout all of this testing, I considered the accuracy of transcriptions (and translations, where appropriate), the quality of insights generated, overall speed, additional features, and value for the money.

为了撰写本指南，我评估了几款AI笔记产品，并在面对面及线上的会议、采访和电话会议中进行了使用。除了“日常生活”的使用场景外，我还通过录制同一段预先录制好的演示文稿作为对照组来测试每一款设备。（为了降低厂商针对特定对话调整AI模型的风险，我不会透露该演示文稿的名称。）在整个测试过程中，我考量了转录（以及适当情况下的翻译）的准确性、生成见解的质量、整体速度、附加功能以及性价比。

### How Do AI Notetakers Work?
### AI笔记设备是如何工作的？

The concept behind most AI notetakers is simple: Drop the device on the table between you and your interview subject or in earshot of your professor, and fire it up with the touch of a button. An onboard microphone records the discussion, transcribing all that is said on the fly, then beams it to a companion app on your smartphone. When the discussion is finished, the transcription is uploaded to the cloud for AI processing, where it is crunched and then turned into outline form, often featuring AI-selected quotes, action items, and other takeaways designed to make sense out of lengthy, meandering meetings.

大多数AI笔记设备背后的概念很简单：将设备放在你和采访对象之间的桌子上，或者放在教授的听力范围内，按下一个按钮即可启动。内置麦克风会实时录制讨论内容并进行转录，然后将其发送到你智能手机上的配套应用程序中。讨论结束后，转录内容会被上传到云端进行AI处理，经过分析后转化为大纲形式，通常包含AI筛选出的引语、行动项以及其他旨在理清冗长、发散性会议内容的要点。

### Can I Just Use an App?
### 我可以直接使用应用程序吗？

You don’t need a physical device to AI-ify your notes. Services like Bluedot and Otter.ai, ubiquitous on Zoom calls these days, can do the job from your phone or computer. On Pixel phones, Google's Recorder app has been offering transcription and summarizations for several years, and Apple's Voice Memos app can do the same if you have an iPhone with Apple Intelligence.

你不需要物理设备也能实现笔记的AI化。像Bluedot和Otter.ai这类在Zoom通话中随处可见的服务，通过手机或电脑就能完成这项工作。在Pixel手机上，谷歌的Recorder应用多年来一直提供转录和摘要功能；如果你拥有支持Apple Intelligence的iPhone，苹果的语音备忘录（Voice Memos）应用也能做到同样的事情。

But physical gadgets are handy for complicated in-person situations where the speaker is far away or difficult to hear, or where you want to use your phone for something else besides its voice recorder function. Just drop a notetaker where it’s convenient, and it'll take care of the rest. You don’t even need to stick around for the discussion—and some notetakers can even translate foreign languages. More advanced systems like the $1,600 Vibe Bot are also available for conference room settings, working as a sort of permanent stenographer for business meetings.

但物理设备在复杂的面对面场景中非常方便，比如当演讲者距离较远、听不清，或者你想用手机做录音以外的其他事情时。只需将笔记设备放在方便的地方，剩下的交给它就行了。你甚至不需要留在现场参与讨论——而且一些笔记设备甚至可以翻译外语。对于会议室环境，还有像价值1600美元的Vibe Bot这样更先进的系统，它们可以作为商务会议的“常驻速记员”。

### What's the Deal With Subscription Plans?
### 订阅计划是怎么回事？

Nearly all AI notetakers push a paid subscription plan, and while most offer a stripped-down, no-cost tier, the utility of these plans varies greatly. Expect to pay $15 to $30 per month for unlimited AI insights on top of the hardware price.

几乎所有的AI笔记设备都推行付费订阅计划。虽然大多数设备提供精简的免费层级，但这些计划的实用性差异很大。除了硬件价格外，预计每月还需支付15到30美元以获取无限的AI见解功能。

### Best Overall AI Notetaker: Comulytic Note Pro
### 最佳整体AI笔记设备：Comulytic Note Pro

I had low expectations for the rather generic Comulytic Note Pro, but it surprised me as not only the most useful all-around notetaker I tested, but also the cheapest after you consider the cost of a premium subscription.

我对外观平平无奇的Comulytic Note Pro期望值很低，但它却给了我惊喜。它不仅是我测试过最实用的全能型笔记设备，而且在考虑了高级订阅费用后，它也是最便宜的一款。

The slim device, at 28 grams, is small enough to fit in a wallet or attach unobtrusively with the included magnetic ring to the back of your phone (note: it requires a special USB dongle to charge). The 64 GB of storage space and a 45-hour battery life aren’t massive, but both should be more than enough to handle a full week of interviews—all processed through OpenAI's GPT-5 and Google's Gemini—without offloading or recharging. The small LCD display is helpful (and rare in this market), indicating when you’re recording and showing the recording duration. This makes it a lot more foolproof than other notetakers, which offer nothing more than a colored LED light to tell you if they're recording.

这款纤薄的设备仅重28克，小到可以放进钱包，或者通过附带的磁吸环不显眼地贴在手机背面（注意：它需要一个特殊的USB适配器来充电）。64GB的存储空间和45小时的续航时间虽然不算巨大，但足以应付整整一周的采访——所有内容都通过OpenAI的GPT-5和Google的Gemini进行处理——无需导出数据或充电。其小巧的LCD显示屏非常实用（在市场上很少见），可以显示录音状态和录音时长。这使得它比其他仅靠彩色LED灯来提示录音状态的设备更不容易出错。
