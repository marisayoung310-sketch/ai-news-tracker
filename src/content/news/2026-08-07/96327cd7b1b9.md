# Loop Engineering for Cross-References: When RAG Answers ‘see Section 7.2’ Instead of the Actual Answer
# 交叉引用的循环工程：当 RAG 回答“见第 7.2 节”而非实际答案时

**Enterprise Document Intelligence [Vol.1 #11] – When the first answer points elsewhere in the document, the pipeline loops back to fetch the linked context**
**企业文档智能 [第 1 卷 #11] —— 当第一个答案指向文档中的其他位置时，流水线会循环回溯以获取链接的上下文**

A RAG pipeline gives you this answer: “the applicable sublimit is defined in Section 7.2 of the policy.” Now read it as the user. Yes… and? What does Section 7.2 say? What is the actual number? The answer is not wrong, it is unfinished: the retrieved passage points at the answer instead of containing it, and nothing in the pipeline went to get it. Contracts, standards, and papers are full of these internal pointers; the question is how to follow them systematically, rather than hoping the right page was fetched on the first try.
一个 RAG 流水线给出了这样的回答：“适用的子限额定义在保单的第 7.2 节中。” 现在以用户的视角读一下。是的……然后呢？第 7.2 节说了什么？具体的数字是多少？这个答案并没有错，但它是不完整的：检索到的段落指向了答案，而不是包含了答案，且流水线中没有任何环节去获取它。合同、标准和论文中充满了这类内部指针；问题在于如何系统性地追踪它们，而不是寄希望于第一次尝试就抓取到了正确的页面。

This article is part of Part III of Enterprise Document Intelligence, a series that builds an enterprise RAG system from four bricks: document parsing, question parsing, retrieval, and generation. It builds that systematic way: the parser flags references cheaply, generation reports the unresolved ones, and the orchestrator loops back for the target.
本文是《企业文档智能》第三部分的内容，该系列旨在通过四个模块构建企业级 RAG 系统：文档解析、问题解析、检索和生成。它构建了这种系统性方法：解析器以低成本标记引用，生成环节报告未解决的引用，而编排器则循环回溯以获取目标内容。

### 1. Where cross-references live in each brick
### 1. 交叉引用在各个模块中的位置

The reference happens at parse time (the document author writes it), at retrieval time (a chunk containing the reference matches the query), and at generation time (the model writes “see Section X” instead of fetching Section X). The pointer could be resolved at any of these three moments, and is silently dropped at each. The fix is a feedback field on the generation output that flags an unresolved reference, plus an orchestrator that catches the signal, resolves the reference against the parsing brick’s relational tables, re-retrieves the linked region, and re-runs generation with the linked context now in hand.
引用发生在解析时（文档作者编写时）、检索时（包含引用的块匹配查询时）以及生成时（模型写出“见第 X 节”而不是去获取第 X 节时）。指针可以在这三个时刻中的任何一个被解析，但在每个环节中它都可能被静默丢弃。解决方案是在生成输出中增加一个反馈字段来标记未解决的引用，并配合一个编排器来捕获该信号，根据解析模块的关系表解析引用，重新检索链接区域，并在获取到链接上下文后重新运行生成。

The architectural point is that the orchestrator loops on signals from the structured output, not on confidence scores. The same machinery used in Article 10 for parsing quality is used here for references. Article 12 applies it to a third dimension (listing completeness); Article 13 (the RAG workflow) names the pattern explicitly and shows how the patterns compose.
架构上的关键点在于，编排器是基于结构化输出的信号进行循环，而不是基于置信度分数。第 10 篇中用于解析质量的机制同样适用于此处的引用处理。第 12 篇将其应用于第三个维度（列表完整性）；第 13 篇（RAG 工作流）明确命名了这种模式，并展示了这些模式是如何组合的。

Parsing is where references are initiated, but cheaply. Native PDF links (the kind you click on in a PDF viewer) come for free from the parser and go into the cross_ref_df table at zero cost. Anything beyond that, regex detection of “see Section 4.2”, glossary tagging, conditional-clause flagging, is not done upfront. The vocabulary is too varied (“cf.”, “see”, “per”, “as defined in”) and the volume too high to extract everything before knowing what will be needed.
解析是引用发起的地方，但成本很低。原生 PDF 链接（即你在 PDF 阅读器中点击的那种）可以从解析器中免费获得，并以零成本进入 `cross_ref_df` 表。除此之外的任何内容，如通过正则表达式检测“见第 4.2 节”、术语表标记、条件从句标记等，都不会预先完成。因为词汇太多样化（“cf.”、“见”、“根据”、“定义于”等），且数量太大，在不知道需要什么之前无法提取所有内容。

Question parsing does not change. The user’s question is parsed into the usual question_df plus satellites (Article 6). References do not require a new question shape. Retrieval does not change on the first pass. It runs the standard retrieval from Article 9 (TOC pages plus keyword pages, merged). The references that exist in the candidate passages are not yet resolved.
问题解析保持不变。用户的问题被解析为常规的 `question_df` 加上卫星数据（第 6 篇）。引用不需要新的问题格式。检索在第一轮中也不变。它运行第 9 篇中的标准检索（目录页加上关键词页，合并后）。候选段落中存在的引用尚未被解析。

Generation is where the new contract appears. The Pydantic schema adds two fields: a list of pending_references (references the LLM detected in the candidates that look like they would change the answer if resolved) and an answer_completeness flag (complete, references_unresolved, or partial). These two fields are the feedback signal. The orchestrator (the decide.py from Article 13) reads the structured output and decides whether the answer is shippable or whether a second pass is needed.
生成环节是新契约出现的地方。Pydantic 模式增加了两个字段：`pending_references` 列表（LLM 在候选内容中检测到的、如果解析可能会改变答案的引用）和 `answer_completeness` 标志（完整、引用未解决或部分）。这两个字段就是反馈信号。编排器（第 13 篇中的 `decide.py`）读取结构化输出，并决定答案是否可以交付，或者是否需要进行第二轮处理。

### 2. The running example: “see Table 3 row (E)”
### 2. 运行示例：“见表 3 第 (E) 行”

The example runs on the Attention Is All You Need paper. The question: “Do learned positional embeddings give similar results to sinusoidal ones in the Transformer paper?” The prose on page 6 (Section 3.5 Positional Encoding) gives a qualitative answer (“the two versions produced nearly identical results”) and adds “see Table 3 row (E)”. The actual numbers are on page 9. With a top-1 retrieval policy, the first pass fetches page 6 and the table is missed.
该示例基于《Attention Is All You Need》论文。问题是：“在 Transformer 论文中，学习到的位置嵌入与正弦位置嵌入的结果相似吗？” 第 6 页（3.5 节位置编码）的文字给出了定性回答（“两个版本产生的结果几乎相同”），并补充了“见表 3 第 (E) 行”。实际数字在第 9 页。使用 Top-1 检索策略时，第一轮只抓取了第 6 页，因此错过了表格。

### 3. First pass: the pipeline runs once
### 3. 第一轮：流水线运行一次

3.1 Parsing initiates references cheaply. The parser from Article 5B (the relational data model) runs on the PDF and produces the usual tables: line_df, page_df, toc_df, plus cross_ref_df. Article 5 introduces cross_ref_df (one row per body-text mention of a named object); this article extends it with a source column that records what produced each row, and fills the regex and LLM rows on demand.
3.1 解析以低成本发起引用。来自第 5B 篇（关系数据模型）的解析器在 PDF 上运行，并生成常规表格：`line_df`、`page_df`、`toc_df` 以及 `cross_ref_df`。第 5 篇介绍了 `cross_ref_df`（正文中提到的每个命名对象占一行）；本文对其进行了扩展，增加了一个 `source` 列，记录每行的来源，并按需填充正则表达式和 LLM 识别出的行。
