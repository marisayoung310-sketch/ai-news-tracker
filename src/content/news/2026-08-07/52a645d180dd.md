# Radar4D-VLM: Proposal-Grounded Temporal 4D Radar Reasoning Across Frozen Language Models

**Abstract:** Vision-language models for autonomous driving primarily rely on cameras and LiDAR, leaving 4D radar largely unexplored as a standalone perceptual modality despite its robustness to adverse visibility and direct measurement of radial velocity. 

**摘要：** 用于自动驾驶的视觉-语言模型主要依赖于摄像头和激光雷达（LiDAR），尽管 4D 雷达在恶劣能见度条件下具有鲁棒性，且能直接测量径向速度，但其作为独立感知模态的研究仍处于探索阶段。

We introduce Radar4D-VLM, a radar-only temporal vision-language model that reasons from ten consecutive 4D-radar point-cloud sweeps without camera or LiDAR input. 

我们推出了 Radar4D-VLM，这是一种仅基于雷达的时序视觉-语言模型，它无需摄像头或激光雷达输入，即可根据连续十帧的 4D 雷达点云扫描进行推理。

Radar4D-VLM extracts geometrically grounded object proposals and organizes radar evidence into a compact hierarchy of object, scene, and kinematic tokens. 

Radar4D-VLM 提取几何基础的目标建议（object proposals），并将雷达证据组织成目标、场景和运动学标记（kinematic tokens）的紧凑层级结构。

A parameter-efficient projector maps these tokens into frozen language backbones, while auditable prediction heads jointly model object count, spatial distribution, motion state, collision risk, semantic category, and radial velocity. 

一个参数高效的投影器将这些标记映射到冻结的语言模型主干中，同时可审计的预测头共同建模目标数量、空间分布、运动状态、碰撞风险、语义类别和径向速度。

Radar4D-VLM combines proposal-grounded temporal object tokenization, global scene context, and explicit kinematic tokens within a unified frozen-backbone interface. 

Radar4D-VLM 在统一的冻结主干接口内，结合了基于建议的时序目标标记化、全局场景上下文和显式运动学标记。

On sequence-isolated K-Radar development validation, its Top-64 proposal recall reaches 98.13% at 4 m, exceeding fixed-lattice and uniform-random controls by 6.40 and 22.83 percentage points, respectively. 

在序列隔离的 K-Radar 开发验证中，其 Top-64 建议召回率在 4 米处达到 98.13%，分别比固定网格（fixed-lattice）和均匀随机（uniform-random）对照组高出 6.40 和 22.83 个百分点。

We further evaluate 24 matched runs spanning eight frozen Qwen, Phi, Mistral, Llama, and Gemma backbones under an identical adaptation budget. 

我们进一步评估了在相同适配预算下，涵盖 Qwen、Phi、Mistral、Llama 和 Gemma 八种冻结主干模型的 24 次匹配运行。

The radar-token interface remains compatible across all five language-model families, while matched aligned, permuted, and no-language controls show sensor dependence but no stable direct-head gain from aligned language supervision. 

雷达标记接口在所有五个语言模型系列中均保持兼容，而匹配对齐、置换和无语言对照组显示出对传感器的依赖性，但并未从对齐的语言监督中获得稳定的直接预测头增益。

These results establish a reproducible foundation for radar-only multimodal scene and motion reasoning while separating interface compatibility from the benefit of language supervision.

这些结果为仅基于雷达的多模态场景和运动推理建立了一个可复现的基础，同时将接口兼容性与语言监督带来的收益区分开来。
