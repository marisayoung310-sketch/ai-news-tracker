# Best Android Charger: Wireless, Portable, Cable (2026)
# 2026年最佳安卓充电器：无线、便携与线缆指南

The best Android charger might seem obvious, but it can depend heavily on your priorities. Do you want to charge your phone from zero to 100 as quickly as possible? Are you charging at home in bed overnight, or on the go in a hotel? WIRED Reviews staffers are constantly testing Android chargers, and below you'll find our top recommendations for every situation.
选择最好的安卓充电器看似简单，但实际上很大程度上取决于你的需求。你是想以最快速度将手机从 0% 充到 100% 吗？还是在家里睡前充电，亦或是在酒店出差时使用？WIRED 评测团队一直在测试各类安卓充电器，以下是我们针对各种场景的最佳推荐。

There was once a time when different phones required different cords, like Lightning cables for iPhone or Micro-USB for Android. But now, the majority of devices don't have special ports and just use a USB-C cable. Modern smartphones from Apple, Google, and Samsung use the Power Delivery charging standard, a fast-charging protocol for USB-C that supports higher voltage and wattage to charge phones faster. All the chargers below support USB-C PD and will work with any smartphone ecosystem, including iPhone.
曾几何时，不同的手机需要不同的线缆，比如 iPhone 的 Lightning 线或安卓的 Micro-USB 线。但现在，大多数设备不再使用专用接口，而是统一采用 USB-C 线缆。苹果、谷歌和三星的现代智能手机均采用 Power Delivery (PD) 充电标准，这是一种支持更高电压和功率的 USB-C 快充协议，能让手机充电更快。以下所有充电器均支持 USB-C PD，适用于包括 iPhone 在内的任何智能手机生态系统。

### How Fast Can My Android Phone Charge? / 我的安卓手机充电有多快？
Charging speeds depend on the equipment you use. If you aren't using cables or wireless chargers that can handle these speeds, your experience may vary. The same goes for your phone usage—if you're using your phone while it's charging, or while it's hot, or both, your speeds might diminish.
充电速度取决于你使用的设备。如果你使用的线缆或无线充电器无法达到相应功率，充电体验可能会打折扣。手机的使用状态也是如此——如果你在充电时使用手机，或者手机处于发热状态（或两者兼有），充电速度可能会下降。

### What Wattage Do I Need? / 我需要多少瓦的充电功率？
Truly, at the end of the day, it doesn't matter that much. A 15-watt charger is the bare minimum we'd recommend, and it will work just fine to charge your phone in a couple of hours, which is better for long-term battery health and perfect for charging overnight or while you're chilling on the couch. If you need as much juice as fast as possible, a 40-watt charger will do the trick and can get most newer smartphones to 80 percent charged in about half an hour.
说实话，归根结底，功率大小并没有那么重要。我们建议至少使用 15 瓦的充电器，它能在几小时内充满手机，这对电池的长期健康更有利，非常适合夜间充电或在沙发上休息时使用。如果你需要尽可能快地补充电量，40 瓦的充电器就能搞定，可以在半小时内将大多数新款智能手机充至 80%。
