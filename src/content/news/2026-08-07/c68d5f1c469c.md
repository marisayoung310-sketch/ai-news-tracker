# OpenAI is giving ChatGPT free users unlimited text chats
# OpenAI 为 ChatGPT 免费用户提供无限文本对话

OpenAI is making a big change for ChatGPT users on its free and Go tiers: Starting next week, users on those tiers will be able to have unlimited text chats with the chatbot, according to OpenAI. Right now, you may run into rate limits if you do too many text chats on those tiers, but those limits, just for text, will be going away soon. Messages that include things like file uploads and images will continue to have limits.

OpenAI 正在为 ChatGPT 的免费版和 Go 版用户带来重大调整：据 OpenAI 称，从下周开始，这些层级的用户将能够与聊天机器人进行无限次的文本对话。目前，如果你在这些层级进行过多的文本对话，可能会遇到频率限制，但这些针对纯文本的限制很快就会取消。包含文件上传和图片等内容的对话仍将受到限制。

Next week, OpenAI is also adding a “Think” button for free and Go users that they can use to “access higher reasoning for harder questions.” This week, for those users, the company is additionally upgrading ChatGPT’s default AI model to the recently released GPT-5.6 Luna.

下周，OpenAI 还将为免费版和 Go 版用户增加一个“思考”（Think）按钮，用户可以使用该功能来“针对更难的问题获取更高阶的推理能力”。本周，该公司还将这些用户的 ChatGPT 默认 AI 模型升级为近期发布的 GPT-5.6 Luna。

For Plus and Pro subscribers, GPT-5.6 Sol in ChatGPT is being updated to be “more reliable with facts,” OpenAI says. “The new GPT‑5.6 Sol is designed to make fewer mistakes — especially when answers depend on dates, numbers, sources, rules, or assumptions — by better using the sources it finds to answer your question.” The updated GPT-5.6 Sol will also give you “more direct responses” with “tighter formatting,” and OpenAI says ChatGPT will “avoid extra detail when it does not help.”

对于 Plus 和 Pro 订阅用户，OpenAI 表示，ChatGPT 中的 GPT-5.6 Sol 模型正在进行更新，以使其“在事实方面更加可靠”。“全新的 GPT-5.6 Sol 旨在减少错误——特别是在答案依赖于日期、数字、来源、规则或假设时——通过更好地利用其找到的来源来回答你的问题。”更新后的 GPT-5.6 Sol 还将提供“更直接的回答”和“更紧凑的格式”，OpenAI 表示，ChatGPT 将“在无助于回答的情况下避免提供多余的细节”。

Plus and Pro subscribers can also use a new slider to decide “how much thought ChatGPT puts into an answer.” Both the updated GPT-5.6 Sol and the slider will be available starting Thursday.

Plus 和 Pro 订阅用户还可以使用一个新的滑块来决定“ChatGPT 在回答问题时投入多少思考”。更新后的 GPT-5.6 Sol 和该滑块功能都将于周四起正式上线。
