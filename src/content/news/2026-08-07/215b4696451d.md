# TikTok blames ‘moderator error’ on slow response to Perez Hilton livestream
# TikTok 将 Perez Hilton 直播响应缓慢归咎于“审核员失误”

TikTok says a “moderator error” delayed the removal of a livestream that appeared to show blogger Perez Hilton harming himself, as reported earlier by Wired. Jamie Favazza, a spokesperson for TikTok US, tells The Verge that the platform notified law enforcement about the situation and banned Hilton’s account for violating its community guidelines.
据《连线》（Wired）早先报道，TikTok 表示，由于“审核员失误”，导致一段疑似博主 Perez Hilton 自残的直播未能及时被移除。TikTok 美国发言人 Jamie Favazza 向《The Verge》表示，平台已就此事通知了执法部门，并因 Hilton 违反社区准则而封禁了他的账号。

Hilton, or Mario Armando Lavandeira Jr., is known for the celebrity gossip blog he started in 2004, where he often posted photos of stars featuring his own often obscene or cruel drawings layered on top.
Hilton（原名 Mario Armando Lavandeira Jr.）以其 2004 年创办的名人八卦博客而闻名，他经常在博客中发布明星照片，并在照片上叠加自己创作的、通常带有猥亵或残忍意味的涂鸦。

Though some users claimed Perez’s livestream aired for more than 30 minutes, Favazza disputes this, saying it lasted around 15 minutes. Favazza adds that TikTok’s automated content moderation team flagged the video “within minutes” and routed it to US moderation teams. TikTok also took down the subsequent livestreams started by Hilton within 90 seconds. Screenshots and clips from the livestream are still floating around on some platforms like X.
尽管一些用户声称 Perez 的直播持续了超过 30 分钟，但 Favazza 对此予以否认，称直播时长约为 15 分钟。Favazza 补充说，TikTok 的自动化内容审核团队在“几分钟内”就标记了该视频，并将其转交给美国审核团队处理。此后，TikTok 在 90 秒内下架了 Hilton 发起的后续直播。目前，该直播的截图和片段仍在 X 等部分平台上流传。

The livestream aired on Tuesday night, with the Miami-Dade Sheriff’s Office responding to Hilton’s home after saying they received “multiple calls regarding an individual who was livestreaming acts of self-harm on social media,” according to CNN. The authorities transported Hilton to a local hospital.
据 CNN 报道，该直播发生在周二晚上。迈阿密-戴德县治安官办公室表示，他们在接到“多起关于某人在社交媒体上直播自残行为的报警电话”后，前往了 Hilton 的住所。随后，当局将 Hilton 送往当地医院。

The slow response to Hilton’s livestream has raised concerns about moderation on TikTok, which some creators have found to be unevenly applied. TikTok’s policies strictly prohibit content that “shows, promotes, or provides instructions for suicide or self-harm,” along with “extremely graphic, violent, or disturbing content.” On Wednesday, The New York Times reported that TikTok US is laying off 250 employees and shutting down its Nashville office, where part of its content moderation team is based.
对 Hilton 直播响应缓慢的问题引发了人们对 TikTok 审核机制的担忧，一些创作者认为该平台的审核标准执行不一。TikTok 的政策严格禁止“展示、宣传或提供自杀或自残指南”的内容，以及“极其血腥、暴力或令人不安的内容”。周三，《纽约时报》报道称，TikTok 美国公司正在裁员 250 人，并关闭其位于纳什维尔的办公室，该办公室正是其部分内容审核团队的所在地。
