# On Hamming-Lipschitz Type Stability of the Subdominant (Minmax) Ultrametric: Theory and Simple Proofs
# 关于次主导（极小极大）超度量的 Hamming-Lipschitz 型稳定性：理论与简易证明

**Abstract:** The subdominant (minmax) ultrametric is a canonical tree-structured summary of a dissimilarity matrix, arising equivalently as the ultrametric induced by single-linkage clustering. While its classical stability theory is usually formulated in $\ell_\infty$ or Gromov--Hausdorff terms, such bounds are poorly suited to sparse perturbations that alter only a few pairwise distances.

**摘要：** 次主导（极小极大）超度量是相异度矩阵的一种规范化树状摘要，等价于由单链接聚类（single-linkage clustering）诱导出的超度量。虽然其经典稳定性理论通常以 $\ell_\infty$ 或 Gromov--Hausdorff 范数来表述，但这些界限并不适用于仅改变少数成对距离的稀疏扰动。

We develop an $\ell_0$-type stability theory for this operator. Our analysis shows that sparse edits propagate only through the minimum spanning tree (MST): a pairwise ultrametric value can change only if its tree path crosses an edited edge or a cut newly exposed by an edited off-tree edge.

我们为该算子开发了一种 $\ell_0$ 型稳定性理论。我们的分析表明，稀疏编辑仅通过最小生成树（MST）传播：只有当成对超度量值的树路径穿过被编辑的边，或穿过由非树边编辑所新暴露出的割集时，该值才会发生改变。

This yields a sharp per-edit exposed-cut score and a tree-only global envelope, leading to Hamming--Lipschitz bounds on the number of ultrametric entries that can change. We also prove sharpness results showing that this dependence on tree geometry is unavoidable: under strict cut separation the tree-edge bound is attained exactly, and for off-tree edits there are explicit families in which one edited distance changes $\Theta(n^2)$ ultrametric entries.

这产生了一个精确的单次编辑暴露割集评分和一个仅基于树的全局包络，从而导出了超度量条目变化数量的 Hamming--Lipschitz 界限。我们还证明了尖锐性结果，表明这种对树几何结构的依赖是不可避免的：在严格的割集分离下，树边界限可以被精确达到；对于非树边编辑，存在明确的族群，其中一个距离的编辑会改变 $\Theta(n^2)$ 个超度量条目。

In addition, we prove a conditional near-additivity principle for multiple edits under certified large per-edit changed regions and negligible aggregate overlap. Experiments on deep-embedding graphs show that the resulting structural scores provide useful vulnerability diagnostics for hierarchical representations.

此外，我们证明了在经过认证的单次编辑大变化区域且总重叠可忽略不计的情况下，多次编辑的条件近加性原理。在深度嵌入图上的实验表明，由此产生的结构评分可为层次化表示提供有用的脆弱性诊断。
