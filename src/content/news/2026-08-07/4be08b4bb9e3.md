# Tactus: Open-Vocabulary Object Recognition from Low-Cost Pressure Arrays
# Tactus：基于低成本压力阵列的开放词汇物体识别

**Abstract:** Resistive pressure arrays are the cheapest and most widely shipped tactile sensors, yet tactile representation learning has concentrated on optical sensors that image a deforming gel. We present Tactus, an open model that answers text queries from pressure data alone: on the STAG benchmark (27 objects, held-out recordings), it reaches 0.771 +/- 0.062 top-1 over four runs (top-3 0.935), matching, and at best exceeding, the dataset's supervised closed-set CNN at 0.76, with no trained classifier head.

**摘要：** 电阻式压力阵列是目前最廉价且出货量最大的触觉传感器，然而触觉表征学习的研究重心一直集中在通过成像变形凝胶来实现的视觉传感器上。我们提出了 Tactus，这是一个仅通过压力数据即可回答文本查询的开放模型：在 STAG 基准测试（27 个物体，保留记录）中，该模型在四次运行中达到了 0.771 +/- 0.062 的 Top-1 准确率（Top-3 为 0.935），在没有训练分类器头的情况下，其表现与该数据集的监督式闭集 CNN（0.76）持平，甚至在最佳情况下有所超越。

The recipe is small-data: 187 training recordings, masked-autoencoder pretraining on 144k unlabeled same-sensor frames, and the sensor's own calibration affine, which recovered more accuracy than every architecture change combined. The released model's errors concentrate in a few contact-ambiguous classes, are uncorrelated with text-target geometry (Spearman rho <= 0.05 over 702 class pairs), and survive paraphrased and even bare-name queries within one point; two diverse frames recover 89% of eight-frame accuracy.

该方案基于小数据量：仅使用 187 条训练记录，在 14.4 万帧无标签的同类传感器数据上进行掩码自编码器（masked-autoencoder）预训练，并利用传感器自身的仿射校准，其带来的精度提升超过了所有架构改进的总和。发布模型的错误集中在少数接触模糊的类别中，且与文本目标几何形状无关（702 个类别对的 Spearman 相关系数 <= 0.05），在改写查询甚至仅使用名称查询时，准确率波动均在 1 个百分点以内；仅需两帧不同的数据即可恢复八帧准确率的 89%。

Failures are reported with equal precision: cross-sensor pretraining pooling gave no gain, vision co-training degraded touch, and a mis-normalized input pipeline silently discarded 97% of the sensor's dynamic range while producing plausible intermediate results. Weights, code, and the memory layer the model plugs into are released openly.

研究同样精确地报告了失败的尝试：跨传感器预训练池化（pooling）没有带来增益，视觉协同训练反而降低了触觉性能，而一个归一化错误的输入流水线在产生看似合理的中间结果的同时，悄无声息地丢弃了传感器 97% 的动态范围。该模型的权重、代码以及其所接入的记忆层均已开源。
