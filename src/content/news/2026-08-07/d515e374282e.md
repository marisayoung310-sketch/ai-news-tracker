# The Zelda movie’s Ganondorf casting hints at more movies
# 《塞尔达传说》电影中加农多夫的选角暗示了后续作品的可能

Deadline reported Thursday that the upcoming The Legend of Zelda movie will feature Australian actor Uli Latukefu as the villain Ganondorf, and the publication notes that “We hear Latukefu inked a multi-picture deal.”
据《Deadline》周四报道，即将上映的《塞尔达传说》真人电影将由澳大利亚演员乌利·拉图凯夫（Uli Latukefu）饰演反派加农多夫（Ganondorf）。该媒体指出：“我们获悉拉图凯夫已经签署了一份多部电影的合约。”

Nintendo or Sony haven’t formally confirmed that there’s more than one movie in the works, though it’s been rumored that Nintendo and Sony might be planning a trilogy. Still, it’s not exactly unexpected that more Zelda movies might be on the way, especially given the huge success of the recent Super Mario films at the box office.
任天堂和索尼尚未正式确认是否正在筹备多部电影，尽管此前有传言称双方可能在计划拍摄三部曲。不过，考虑到近期《超级马力欧》系列电影在票房上的巨大成功，未来会有更多《塞尔达传说》电影问世并不令人意外。

Nintendo initially announced the live-action Zelda movie, which it’s making with Sony Pictures Entertainment, back in 2023, with Wes Ball directing with Shigeru Miyamoto and Avi Arad producing. Last year, Nintendo announced that Bo Bragason and Benjamin Evan Ainsworth had been cast as Princess Zelda and Link, respectively. The movie is set to release on April 30th, 2027, and filming has officially wrapped.
任天堂最初于2023年宣布了这部与索尼影视娱乐公司合作的《塞尔达传说》真人电影，由韦斯·鲍尔（Wes Ball）执导，宫本茂与阿维·阿拉德（Avi Arad）担任制片人。去年，任天堂宣布博·布拉加森（Bo Bragason）和本杰明·埃文·安斯沃思（Benjamin Evan Ainsworth）将分别饰演塞尔达公主和林克。该片定于2027年4月30日上映，目前拍摄工作已正式杀青。

In a separate report published later on Thursday, Deadline says that Dichen Lachman, Yvonne Strahovski, and the late Sam Neill will be in the Zelda movie as well. Neill’s role is unclear, but the article says Lachman plays Impa while Strahovski has been cast as “a queen character.”
在周四晚些时候发布的另一篇报道中，《Deadline》称迪辰·拉克曼（Dichen Lachman）、伊冯娜·斯特拉霍夫斯基（Yvonne Strahovski）以及已故演员山姆·尼尔（Sam Neill）也将参演该片。尼尔的角色尚不明确，但文章指出拉克曼将饰演英帕（Impa），而斯特拉霍夫斯基则被选定饰演“一位女王角色”。

Nintendo is also preparing a big new Zelda game: the company has announced a Switch 2 remake of Ocarina of Time that’s set to launch sometime in 2026.
任天堂也在筹备一款重磅的《塞尔达传说》新游戏：该公司已宣布将推出《时之笛》的 Switch 2 重制版，预计于2026年内发布。
