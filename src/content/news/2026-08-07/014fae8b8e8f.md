# TRNet: Topography-Guided Frequency Rectification and Structure-Aware Decoding for Multimodal Paddy Rice Segmentation
# TRNet：用于多模态水稻分割的地形引导频率校正与结构感知解码

**Abstract:** Mapping paddy rice from very-high-resolution imagery in mountainous and hilly regions is difficult because terrain alters optical appearance and increases confusion with visually similar vegetation. 

**摘要：** 在山区和丘陵地区，利用超高分辨率影像进行水稻制图十分困难，因为地形会改变光学外观，并增加与视觉相似植被之间的混淆。

We present TRNet for 0.5-m GaoJing-1 red--green--blue (RGB) imagery, a 5-m TanDEM-X digital elevation model (DEM), and derived slope. Separate visual and terrain encoders preserve modality-specific features. 

我们提出了 TRNet，该模型适用于 0.5 米分辨率的高景一号（GaoJing-1）红绿蓝（RGB）影像、5 米分辨率的 TanDEM-X 数字高程模型（DEM）及其导出的坡度数据。独立的视觉编码器和地形编码器能够保留各自模态的特定特征。

At an early encoder stage, Topographic Energy-Spectral Rectification applies terrain-conditioned low-frequency modulation and asymmetric high-frequency regulation to suppress steep-slope clutter and conditionally enhance compatible low-slope rice cues. 

在编码器的早期阶段，地形能量谱校正（Topographic Energy-Spectral Rectification）应用了基于地形条件的低频调制和非对称高频调节，以抑制陡坡杂波，并有条件地增强与低坡度相符的水稻特征。

The Topography-guided Paddy Structure Decoder combines semantic, rice--background boundary, and interior cues, using coarse terrain as context. 

地形引导的水稻结构解码器（Topography-guided Paddy Structure Decoder）结合了语义、水稻与背景边界以及内部特征，并将粗略的地形信息作为上下文参考。

Experiments used an Area A internal test set and held-out Area B, which had steeper terrain and lower rice prevalence. TRNet achieved rice intersection-over-union (IoU) values of 85.10% and 80.68%, exceeding the original Dual-Encoder U-Net by 9.15 and 18.83 percentage points, respectively. 

实验使用了 A 区内部测试集和 B 区留出测试集，其中 B 区地形更陡峭且水稻分布较少。TRNet 在水稻分割任务中实现了 85.10% 和 80.68% 的交并比（IoU），分别比原始的双编码器 U-Net（Dual-Encoder U-Net）高出 9.15 和 18.83 个百分点。

Ablation and slope-stratified results linked these gains to frequency rectification, structure learning, and fewer steep-terrain false positives. The results support coarse topography as a contextual prior for very-high-resolution paddy rice mapping.

消融实验和坡度分层结果表明，这些性能提升归功于频率校正、结构学习以及陡峭地形中误报的减少。研究结果证实，粗略的地形信息可作为超高分辨率水稻制图的上下文先验知识。
