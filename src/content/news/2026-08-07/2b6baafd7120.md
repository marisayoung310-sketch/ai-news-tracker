# Lindblad-Inspired Multi-Timescale Reservoir Computing with Separable Rotation and Dissipation
# 基于 Lindblad 启发的旋转与耗散可分离多时间尺度储备池计算

**Abstract:** Echo-state networks enable efficient temporal learning by fixing the recurrent dynamics and training only a linear readout. However, conventional reservoirs typically accommodate signal mixing, memory retention, and stability within a single random recurrent matrix. Existing structured designs improve topology, norm preservation, leakage, or depth, but generally do not provide separate modal control of reversible mixing and irreversible forgetting together with a direct global stability guarantee.

**摘要：** 回声状态网络（Echo-state networks）通过固定循环动力学并仅训练线性读出层，实现了高效的时间序列学习。然而，传统的储备池通常将信号混合、记忆保持和稳定性全部容纳在一个随机循环矩阵中。现有的结构化设计虽然改善了拓扑结构、范数保持、泄漏或深度，但通常无法在提供直接全局稳定性保证的同时，实现可逆混合与不可逆遗忘的独立模态控制。

We introduce a classical Lindblad-inspired multi-timescale reservoir that bridges open-system dynamical principles with structured state-space modeling. The recurrent operator is assembled from exactly discretized damped rotational modes, so rotation and decay become independent design variables governing phase mixing and memory loss. Orthogonal mode mixing preserves normality, while the decay spectrum directly determines the echo-state stability margin without post-hoc spectral-radius rescaling.

我们引入了一种受经典 Lindblad 启发的、跨越开放系统动力学原理与结构化状态空间建模的多时间尺度储备池。该循环算子由精确离散化的阻尼旋转模态组装而成，因此旋转和衰减成为控制相位混合与记忆丢失的独立设计变量。正交模态混合保持了正规性，而衰减谱直接决定了回声状态的稳定性边界，无需事后进行谱半径重缩放。

We evaluate the method over ten aligned seeds against standard, leaky, deep, orthogonal, cycle, and next-generation reservoirs, together with a compact trained gated recurrent unit, across linear memory, nonlinear recurrence, chaotic forecasting, delayed logic, and real sensor calibration. Across the benchmark suite, the proposed reservoir achieves the best fixed-reservoir performance on bounded NARMA-20 and the lowest mean error on Lorenz-63, matches the strongest linear-memory result, and remains broadly competitive across broad range of benchmarks.

我们在十个对齐种子（aligned seeds）上，将该方法与标准、泄漏、深度、正交、循环和下一代储备池，以及紧凑型训练门控循环单元（GRU）进行了对比评估，涵盖了线性记忆、非线性循环、混沌预测、延迟逻辑和真实传感器校准等任务。在基准测试套件中，该储备池在有界 NARMA-20 上实现了最佳的固定储备池性能，在 Lorenz-63 上实现了最低的平均误差，匹配了最强的线性记忆结果，并在广泛的基准测试中保持了强大的竞争力。

Ablation studies show that rotation increases state diversity, whereas dissipation provides controlled forgetting and improves predictive conditioning. The resulting framework offers an interpretable recurrent architecture in which mixing, memory, and stability are explicit and independently tunable design variables.

消融研究表明，旋转增加了状态多样性，而耗散提供了受控的遗忘机制并改善了预测条件。由此产生的框架提供了一种可解释的循环架构，其中混合、记忆和稳定性成为明确且可独立调节的设计变量。
