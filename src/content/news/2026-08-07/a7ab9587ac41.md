# Blue Origin narrowing in on root cause of catastrophic rocket accident
# 蓝色起源公司正逐步锁定火箭灾难性事故的根本原因

Blue Origin provided its first substantive update this week on the cause of the catastrophic loss of the New Glenn rocket and most of its launch pad infrastructure at Cape Canaveral, Florida, in May. The space company’s chief executive, Dave Limp, said investigative work is continuing into the anomaly that happened during a hot-fire test of the rocket during the evening of May 28, 2026.

本周，蓝色起源公司（Blue Origin）针对今年5月在佛罗里达州卡纳维拉尔角发生的“新格伦”（New Glenn）火箭及其大部分发射台基础设施损毁的灾难性事故，提供了首次实质性进展更新。该公司首席执行官戴夫·林普（Dave Limp）表示，针对2026年5月28日晚间火箭热试车期间发生的异常情况，调查工作仍在持续进行中。

He confirmed, for the first time, widespread speculation that the problem started in one of the rocket’s seven BE-4 engines that power the vehicle’s first stage. “The anomaly originated at the main oxygen valve on one of the BE-4 engines, which was later confirmed by hardware recovery and inspections,” he said. “Extensive component-level and engine hotfire tests have been conducted to understand the failure mode better and inform mitigations.”

他首次证实了外界的广泛猜测：问题确实源于为该火箭第一级提供动力的七台BE-4发动机中的其中一台。“异常情况起源于其中一台BE-4发动机的主氧阀，这一点已通过硬件回收和检查得到证实，”他说道，“我们已经进行了广泛的组件级和发动机热试车测试，以更好地了解故障模式并制定缓解措施。”

No root cause found yet. Limp said the company’s path forward is “clear” as it works toward returning the New Glenn rocket to flight before the end of this year. “We’re making small modifications to the valve that can be quickly retrofitted to existing engines,” he said. “We’re manufacturing the updated hardware, which will be ready by the end of this month. In parallel, we’re working through the remaining fault tree analysis.”

尚未找到根本原因。林普表示，公司在努力争取让“新格伦”火箭在今年年底前恢复飞行，前方的道路是“清晰的”。“我们正在对阀门进行微小改动，这些改动可以快速改装到现有发动机上，”他说，“我们正在制造更新后的硬件，预计本月底即可准备就绪。与此同时，我们正在完成剩余的故障树分析工作。”

This is a positive update, and the transparency is appreciated. However, it also seems clear from Limp’s statement that the company has not yet identified the root cause of the issue. The update was published 10 weeks after the hot fire test anomaly, and in parallel Blue Origin has rapidly been working to clear debris around the sprawling Launch Complex 36-A site in Florida and starting to rebuild a launch tower. Multiple sources have said the company is operating with a sense of urgency and sparing little expense in its efforts to resume launching the heavy lift rocket.

这是一个积极的更新，其透明度值得赞赏。然而，从林普的声明中也可以清楚地看出，该公司尚未确定问题的根本原因。此次更新是在热试车异常发生10周后发布的；与此同时，蓝色起源一直在迅速清理佛罗里达州庞大的36-A发射场周围的碎片，并开始重建发射塔。多位消息人士称，该公司正以一种紧迫感开展工作，并不惜代价地努力恢复这款重型运载火箭的发射。

Combining urgency and funding, Blue Origin founder Jeff Bezos has made it clear that he wants to see New Glenn flying before the end of the year. Tory Bruno, the president of national security for Blue Origin, indicated on the social media site X that the next flight of New Glenn could carry the company’s “Blue Ring” spacecraft. “She’s in final build now for a flight this year,” Bruno said Wednesday of the spacecraft.

在紧迫感与资金的双重支持下，蓝色起源创始人杰夫·贝索斯（Jeff Bezos）已明确表示，他希望在今年年底前看到“新格伦”火箭升空。蓝色起源国家安全部门总裁托里·布鲁诺（Tory Bruno）在社交媒体平台X上暗示，“新格伦”的下一次飞行可能会搭载该公司的“蓝环”（Blue Ring）航天器。布鲁诺周三在谈到该航天器时表示：“它目前正处于最终建造阶段，以备今年飞行。”

Despite the progress in both understanding the cause of the failure and rebuilding the launch site, the end-of-the-year timeline still seems to be extremely ambitious. However, it is clear that Blue Origin, which historically has appeared sluggish and unfocused in its various efforts across the space ecosystem, is now laser-focused on getting New Glenn back into service. This is a new era for Blue Origin and suggests that Limp’s hire to lead the company has led to a significant transformation.

尽管在查明故障原因和重建发射场方面取得了进展，但年底的时间表似乎仍然极其雄心勃勃。然而显而易见的是，蓝色起源这家在航天领域各项工作中历来显得迟缓且缺乏专注的公司，现在正全神贯注于让“新格伦”重返服务。这对蓝色起源来说是一个新时代，也表明聘请林普领导公司已经带来了显著的转变。

In other words, the long-slumbering giant in the US space industry may finally be waking up. And just in time. New Glenn is central to the company’s future success, of course. But beyond this, it is essential to NASA’s lunar ambitions to establish a Moon base and for the broader commercial space industry that is grappling with the difficulty of finding launch alternatives to the Falcon 9 rocket, which is increasingly difficult to procure for non-SpaceX missions.

换句话说，这位美国航天工业中长期沉睡的巨人可能终于苏醒了。而且正是时候。“新格伦”火箭不仅对该公司未来的成功至关重要，更重要的是，它对于美国国家航空航天局（NASA）建立月球基地的雄心，以及对于正苦于寻找“猎鹰9号”火箭替代方案的更广泛商业航天业来说，都是必不可少的——毕竟，非SpaceX任务想要预订“猎鹰9号”正变得越来越困难。
