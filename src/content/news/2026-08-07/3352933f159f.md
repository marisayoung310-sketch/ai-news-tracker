# Robust and Personalized Federated Learning for Aircraft-Engine Prognostics under Benign and Adversarial Client Heterogeneity
# 面向航空发动机预测的鲁棒与个性化联邦学习：应对良性与对抗性客户端异构性

**Abstract:** Federated learning (FL) enables aircraft fleet operators to jointly train remaining-useful-life (RUL) models from engine sensor telemetry without sharing raw data. This study examines two complementary challenges: benign heterogeneity, where honest operators observe different operating conditions and fault modes, and adversarial heterogeneity, where compromised operators submit poisoned updates.

**摘要：** 联邦学习（FL）使飞机机队运营商能够在不共享原始数据的情况下，利用发动机传感器遥测数据共同训练剩余使用寿命（RUL）模型。本研究探讨了两个互补的挑战：良性异构性（即诚实的运营商观察到不同的运行条件和故障模式）以及对抗性异构性（即受损的运营商提交中毒更新）。

We conduct a controlled, safety-oriented evaluation using a multi-task one-dimensional convolutional neural network and a structurally non-IID partition of the Commercial Modular Aero-Propulsion System Simulation (C-MAPSS) benchmark. We compare four remedies for benign heterogeneity and evaluate five attacks against four aggregation methods, including a physically motivated sensor-value backdoor designed to mask engine degradation.

我们使用多任务一维卷积神经网络，并基于商业模块化航空推进系统仿真（C-MAPSS）基准测试的结构化非独立同分布（non-IID）分区，进行了受控的安全导向评估。我们比较了四种针对良性异构性的补救措施，并评估了针对四种聚合方法的五种攻击，其中包括一种旨在掩盖发动机退化的、基于物理原理的传感器数值后门攻击。

Shared-representation personalization closes approximately 70% of the local-to-centralized root-mean-square-error gap, compared with 21% for proximal regularization and 10% for server-side reweighting. The backdoor achieves a 94.9% attack success rate against standard averaging while leaving clean accuracy statistically unchanged, demonstrating that accuracy alone cannot certify model safety and that attack success must be evaluated explicitly.

共享表示个性化方法缩小了约 70% 的本地模型与集中式模型之间的均方根误差差距，相比之下，近端正则化方法缩小了 21%，服务器端重加权方法缩小了 10%。该后门攻击在标准平均聚合下达到了 94.9% 的攻击成功率，同时保持了干净数据的准确性基本不变，这表明仅凭准确性无法证明模型的安全性，必须对攻击成功率进行明确评估。

Krum reduces attack success by an order of magnitude and is the only evaluated aggregator that withstands coordinated attackers, whereas personalization alone provides no protection. Combining personalization with robust aggregation restores robustness (2.8% attack success) with only a small accuracy cost, revealing a trade-off between robust update selection and collaborative representation learning. Results remain consistent across client counts and on a harder six-condition dataset. Code and data partitions are released for reproducibility.

Krum 算法将攻击成功率降低了一个数量级，是唯一能够抵御协同攻击者的评估聚合器，而单纯的个性化方法无法提供保护。将个性化与鲁棒聚合相结合，在仅付出少量准确性代价的情况下恢复了鲁棒性（攻击成功率降至 2.8%），揭示了鲁棒更新选择与协作表示学习之间的权衡。研究结果在不同客户端数量及更困难的六条件数据集上均保持一致。代码和数据分区已发布，以供复现。
