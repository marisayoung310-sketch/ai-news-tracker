# Ford needs another Taurus, and the $30K Fathom EV pickup isn’t it
# 福特需要另一款“金牛座”，但 3 万美元的 Fathom 电动皮卡并非答案

Few automotive companies are as dependent on breakout hits as Ford. It’s almost as though it’s baked into the company’s DNA: From the Model T and the Taurus to the F-150 and the Explorer, Ford has lurched from one success to another. Recently, though, the automaker has been coming up empty handed. While its U.S. vehicle sales rose 6% year-over-year to 2.08 million in 2025, they’re today less than half their peak of 4.48 million in 2000. Of the new vehicles that Ford has introduced in recent years, none have become a sensation.
很少有汽车公司像福特这样依赖爆款产品。这几乎已经融入了公司的基因：从 T 型车（Model T）和金牛座（Taurus）到 F-150 和探险者（Explorer），福特从一个成功走向另一个成功。然而，这家汽车制造商最近却一无所获。尽管其 2025 年美国汽车销量同比增长 6%，达到 208 万辆，但如今的销量还不到 2000 年 448 万辆峰值的一半。在福特近年来推出的新车型中，没有一款成为现象级产品。

Ford CEO Jim Farley is betting its forthcoming EV pickup, Fathom, will change its fortunes, calling the introduction of the truck and its Universal EV platform a “Model T moment” for the company. Ford needs a product that can compete with Chinese EVs, which have been taking the world by storm. Yet, for reasons that are both within and beyond Ford’s control, the Fathom is unlikely to be another Taurus or Explorer, two hits that drove Ford sales in the 1990s.
福特首席执行官吉姆·法利（Jim Farley）寄希望于即将推出的电动皮卡 Fathom 能扭转乾坤，他称这款皮卡及其通用电动汽车平台（Universal EV platform）的推出是公司的“T 型车时刻”。福特需要一款能够与风靡全球的中国电动汽车竞争的产品。然而，由于福特可控及不可控的多种原因，Fathom 不太可能成为像 90 年代推动福特销量增长的“金牛座”或“探险者”那样的爆款。

Now, Fathom could be a minor success. Any way you spin it, the base model will be a good deal. On Thursday, Ford said the Fathom would start at $29,945, including the destination fee. That’s significantly below the $50,000 average for new vehicles and the $56,000 average for EVs. It’s also about $5,000 lower than the Keating Line — the average listing price of the largest price segment available at dealers. But consumers don’t decide to buy a car based on price alone. Here’s where Ford could run into some challenges.
当然，Fathom 可能会取得小范围的成功。无论怎么看，基础款都非常划算。周四，福特宣布 Fathom 的起售价为 29,945 美元（含运费）。这远低于新车 5 万美元和电动汽车 5.6 万美元的平均价格。它也比“基廷线”（Keating Line，即经销商处最大价格区间的平均挂牌价）低约 5,000 美元。但消费者买车并非只看价格，这正是福特可能面临挑战的地方。

The Fathom will be a compact pickup truck, a form factor that the market largely ignored until Ford started selling the Maverick in 2021. The Fathom is entering an under-exploited niche, but it doesn’t appear to be one with enormous potential either. Sales of the Maverick offer a clue: they’ve been brisk, but at 155,000 last year, the small truck is not a blockbuster, either. The Taurus sold more than twice as many at its peak in 1992.
Fathom 将是一款紧凑型皮卡，这种车型在福特 2021 年开始销售 Maverick 之前基本被市场忽视。Fathom 进入的是一个尚未被充分开发的细分市场，但它似乎也没有巨大的潜力。Maverick 的销量提供了一些线索：虽然销售火爆，但去年 15.5 万辆的销量也算不上超级大片。相比之下，金牛座在 1992 年的巅峰时期销量是这个数字的两倍多。

Now, the Fathom appears to have a few advantages over the Maverick. For one, passengers should enjoy a bit more interior space, according to Ford. The EV will also have a frunk, which could sway some buyers of traditional cars who have overlooked pickup trucks for lack of closed storage. But the Fathom is still a pickup, and not everyone loves that look. The majority of the market today is dominated by SUVs, with just over 70% of prospective buyers looking for an SUV, compared with 33% for pickups, according to Cox Automotive.
目前来看，Fathom 相比 Maverick 有一些优势。据福特称，乘客将享受到更大的内部空间。这款电动车还将配备前备箱（frunk），这可能会吸引一些因皮卡缺乏封闭式储物空间而将其排除在外的传统汽车买家。但 Fathom 终究还是皮卡，并非所有人都喜欢这种外观。根据考克斯汽车（Cox Automotive）的数据，当今市场以 SUV 为主，超过 70% 的潜在买家倾向于购买 SUV，而考虑皮卡的仅占 33%。

Even among pickup fans, the Fathom may struggle to draw buyers. For many, trucks are a vibe. They want them to look ready to tackle the Rubicon trail, even if they never make it beyond the city limits. Every automaker is aware of this. GM, in a recent briefing I attended, made a point to mention how it is delivering the “lifted trucks” that its customers are demanding. The Fathom, from the little we’ve seen so far, doesn’t scream “rugged.”
即使在皮卡爱好者中，Fathom 可能也难以吸引买家。对许多人来说，皮卡代表一种氛围。他们希望车看起来随时能去挑战卢比肯小道（Rubicon trail），即便他们从未开出过城市边界。每家汽车制造商都意识到了这一点。在最近我参加的一次简报会上，通用汽车特意提到他们是如何交付客户所要求的“升高版皮卡”的。而从目前有限的信息来看，Fathom 并没有给人一种“硬派”的感觉。

Then there’s the EV thing. Most people who buy an EV, myself included, would never buy an internal combustion vehicle again, but a lot of people don’t love change. They’re content with gas-powered cars and trucks, and many dealers are happy to keep them in one. The Fathom’s success won’t be dictated entirely by the whims of the market, though. Ford can spend on marketing and dealer training to overcome the EV hurdle, and may even convince people that a truck with a frunk is an ideal replacement for a sedan.
此外还有电动化的问题。大多数购买电动汽车的人（包括我自己）都不会再买燃油车了，但很多人并不喜欢改变。他们对燃油车感到满足，许多经销商也乐于维持现状。不过，Fathom 的成功不会完全由市场情绪决定。福特可以通过营销和经销商培训来克服电动汽车的门槛，甚至可能说服人们相信，带前备箱的皮卡是轿车的理想替代品。

And it’s almost a guarantee that Ford will introduce a ruggedized version of the Fathom. Will that be enough to make the Fathom a breakout hit? Probably not. But maybe Ford doesn’t need it to be. Maybe the Fathom is a test of its new production process, a toe in the water at a part of the beach that hasn’t been overrun. Compact trucks aren’t setting sales records, but the market for them is still growing. Ford can use that demand to soak up every Fathom it makes while it refines its new platform and process.
福特几乎肯定会推出 Fathom 的硬派版本。但这足以让 Fathom 成为爆款吗？可能不会。但也许福特并不需要它成为爆款。也许 Fathom 只是对其新生产流程的一次测试，是在尚未被占领的市场海滩上的一次试水。紧凑型皮卡虽然没有创下销售纪录，但市场仍在增长。福特可以利用这种需求来消化其生产的每一辆 Fathom，同时不断完善其新平台和新流程。

Then, once it’s confident it has the kinks sorted out, it can expand it to other segments, including SUVs. The Fathom may not be Ford’s swing for the fences. Maybe the company’s merely hoping to get on base and lay the groundwork for the next model rolling off the Universal EV platform — the one it really needs to be a home run.
一旦福特确信解决了所有问题，就可以将其扩展到其他领域，包括 SUV。Fathom 可能不是福特的“全垒打”之作。也许公司只是希望先“上垒”，为下一款基于通用电动平台推出的车型打下基础——那才是它真正需要打出的全垒打。
