# A Trust-region Framework for Moment Estimation
# 矩估计的信赖域框架

**Abstract:** In this paper, we develop a trust-region framework for understanding the behavior of adaptive moment estimation mechanisms, such as \textsc{Adam}, in stochastic gradient optimization. Specifically, in this framework, the magnitude of the update step for each individual weight is constrained within a trust-region governed by a moment constraint of order $p\in[2,4]$.

**摘要：** 在本文中，我们开发了一个信赖域框架，用于理解随机梯度优化中自适应矩估计机制（如 Adam）的行为。具体而言，在该框架中，每个权重更新步长的大小被限制在一个由 $p\in[2,4]$ 阶矩约束所控制的信赖域内。

The resulting derivation then leads to a family of learning-rate mechanisms based on second-moment estimation and a normalized $p$-th moment estimation. When $p=4$, this involves kurtosis-like estimation. The general mechanism, referred to as \textsc{Gmake}, provides a unified interpretation of normalization by moment estimation, learning-rate scheduling, spectral lowpass filtering as momentum, and operator-level spectral normalization within a common trust-region framework.

由此推导出一系列基于二阶矩估计和归一化 $p$ 阶矩估计的学习率机制。当 $p=4$ 时，这涉及类似峰度的估计。这种被称为 \textsc{Gmake} 的通用机制，在统一的信赖域框架内，为矩估计归一化、学习率调度、作为动量的谱低通滤波以及算子级谱归一化提供了一种统一的解释。

Experiments on GPT2-124M trained on FineWeb-Edu and TinyStories suggest that the fourth-moment realization provides its greatest benefit when trust-region constraints are weak. As progressively stronger trust-region controls are introduced, the second-moment realization becomes increasingly competitive, often achieving slightly lower validation loss than its corresponding fourth-moment realization.

在 FineWeb-Edu 和 TinyStories 数据集上对 GPT2-124M 进行的实验表明，当信赖域约束较弱时，四阶矩实现能带来最大的收益。随着信赖域控制逐渐增强，二阶矩实现变得越来越具有竞争力，通常能比对应的四阶矩实现获得略低的验证损失。
