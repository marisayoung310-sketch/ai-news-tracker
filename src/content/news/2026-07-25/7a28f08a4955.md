# Qualcomm is about to raise prices and that’s bad news for everyone
# 高通即将涨价，这对所有人来说都是坏消息

Qualcomm sent a letter to customers on Friday warning of plans to increase its prices by “a percentage in the double digits,” Bloomberg reports. The price hikes will go into effect starting with products shipped after September 1st.
据彭博社报道，高通周五致信客户，警告称计划将其产品价格提高“两位数的百分比”。此次涨价将从 9 月 1 日之后发货的产品开始生效。

Qualcomm claims it has “exhausted its ability to absorb higher costs from suppliers,” as ongoing component shortages continue to drive up prices. The company also said it attempted to source components from alternate suppliers prior to announcing the price hikes.
高通声称，由于持续的零部件短缺不断推高成本，公司已“耗尽了消化供应商涨价的能力”。该公司还表示，在宣布涨价之前，曾尝试从其他供应商处采购零部件。

As Bloomberg notes, Qualcomm’s price increases will likely have a ripple effect, particularly for smartphones. Qualcomm’s chips power everything from Samsung’s new foldables to upcoming smartglasses. Earlier this year, Qualcomm even promised one of its new chip platforms would deliver $300 Windows PCs. Those devices could get pricier if device manufacturers pass the brunt of Qualcomm’s price hikes onto their customers.
正如彭博社所指出的，高通的涨价可能会产生连锁反应，尤其是在智能手机领域。从三星的新款折叠屏手机到即将推出的智能眼镜，高通芯片驱动着各种设备。今年早些时候，高通甚至承诺其新的芯片平台将助力打造 300 美元的 Windows PC。如果设备制造商将高通涨价带来的压力转嫁给消费者，这些设备的价格可能会随之上涨。

Qualcomm’s far from the only company bumping up its prices. Seemingly everything is getting more expensive as a result of component shortages, including Xbox consoles, Raspberry Pi boards, most Apple devices, and Samsung and Google’s smartphones. Some devices are even getting downgraded specs alongside higher prices, while other devices are getting canceled or delayed.
高通远非唯一一家提高价格的公司。受零部件短缺影响，似乎所有东西都在变贵，包括 Xbox 游戏机、树莓派（Raspberry Pi）开发板、大多数苹果设备，以及三星和谷歌的智能手机。一些设备甚至在涨价的同时还出现了配置缩水的情况，而另一些设备则面临被取消或推迟发布的命运。

Qualcomm did not immediately respond to a request for comment.
高通未立即回应置评请求。
