# Blade Runner 2099’s moody dystopia streams on Amazon in November
# 《银翼杀手 2099》：忧郁的反乌托邦世界将于 11 月登陆亚马逊流媒体

The series stars Michelle Yeoh and Hunter Schafer, and you can watch the first trailer now.
该剧由杨紫琼（Michelle Yeoh）和亨特·莎弗（Hunter Schafer）主演，首支预告片现已发布。

After teasing the series with some first-look images yesterday, Amazon is finally properly unveiling its Blade Runner streaming series. Called Blade Runner 2099, the show hits Prime Video on November 25th with all eight episodes dropping at once. We also got the first trailer for the series, which is full of Blade Runner staples: a bleak futuristic LA, dramatic narration, and some really good coats.
在昨天发布了一些先导剧照进行预热后，亚马逊终于正式揭开了其《银翼杀手》流媒体剧集的面纱。该剧定名为《银翼杀手 2099》，将于 11 月 25 日在 Prime Video 上线，全季八集将一次性放出。我们还看到了该剧的首支预告片，其中充满了《银翼杀手》的经典元素：阴郁的未来洛杉矶、充满戏剧性的旁白，以及非常帅气的风衣。

The show is helmed by Silka Luisa, previously showrunner on Apple TV’s adaptation of Shining Girls, while original Blade Runner director Ridley Scott is signed on as an executive producer. 2099 stars Michelle Yeoh and Hunter Schafer and, as the name implies, takes place a few decades after Denis Villeneuve’s film Blade Runner 2049. Here’s the official premise:
该剧由曾担任 Apple TV+ 剧集《闪亮女孩》（Shining Girls）制片人的西尔卡·路易莎（Silka Luisa）执导，原版《银翼杀手》导演雷德利·斯科特（Ridley Scott）担任执行制片人。《银翼杀手 2099》由杨紫琼和亨特·莎弗主演，正如剧名所示，故事发生在丹尼斯·维伦纽瓦（Denis Villeneuve）执导的电影《银翼杀手 2049》几十年之后。以下是官方剧情简介：

Fifty years after the events of Blade Runner 2049, Los Angeles has been reborn, just not by humanity. Cora, a fugitive in a final bid to stop running, takes on one last identity: a Blade Runner. Forced to partner with Olwen, a Replicant days from dying, she hunts down a runaway hiding a truth that could collapse their fragile city.
在《银翼杀手 2049》事件发生五十年后，洛杉矶重获新生，但并非由人类主导。科拉（Cora）是一名逃亡者，为了结束逃亡生涯，她接受了最后一个身份：银翼杀手。她被迫与一名生命垂危的复制人奥尔文（Olwen）搭档，追捕一名隐藏着足以摧毁这座脆弱城市真相的逃亡者。

It’s been a long time coming for Blade Runner 2099, as Amazon first revealed it was working on the series back in 2022. But it’s coming at what could be a good time for cyberpunk television, as Apple is also working up an adaptation of William Gibson’s Neuromancer.
《银翼杀手 2099》的制作历程漫长，亚马逊早在 2022 年就首次透露了该剧的开发计划。但该剧的推出正值赛博朋克题材电视剧的黄金时期，因为苹果公司目前也正在筹备威廉·吉布森（William Gibson）名作《神经漫游者》（Neuromancer）的改编剧集。
