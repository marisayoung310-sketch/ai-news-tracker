# U-CFR: Uncertainty-Guided Cascade Forward Refinement for Interactive Segmentation
# U-CFR：用于交互式分割的不确定性引导级联前向细化

**Abstract:** Interactive image segmentation is critical for efficient image annotation; however, existing methods often require many corrective clicks or rely on passive refinement schemes that converge slowly.
**摘要：** 交互式图像分割对于高效的图像标注至关重要；然而，现有方法通常需要大量的修正点击，或者依赖于收敛缓慢的被动细化方案。

We propose Uncertainty-Guided Cascade Forward Refinement (U-CFR), a novel inference-time framework that enables models to autonomously self-correct after each user interaction.
我们提出了不确定性引导级联前向细化（U-CFR），这是一种新颖的推理时框架，使模型能够在每次用户交互后自主进行自我修正。

U-CFR introduces a boundary-aware uncertainty score that fuses segmentation uncertainty, contour gradients, and explicit edge predictions to guide the placement of internal pseudo-clicks.
U-CFR 引入了一种边界感知不确定性评分，该评分融合了分割不确定性、轮廓梯度和显式边缘预测，以引导内部伪点击（pseudo-clicks）的放置。

These self-generated clicks target the most ambiguous boundary regions, providing strong corrective signals without additional manual input.
这些自生成的点击针对最模糊的边界区域，在无需额外人工输入的情况下提供强有力的修正信号。

To support this process, we design a dual-head network with a shared encoder-decoder backbone: a segmentation head ensures region consistency, while an edge head sharpens boundary alignment.
为了支持这一过程，我们设计了一个具有共享编码器-解码器主干的双头网络：分割头确保区域一致性，而边缘头则强化边界对齐。

In inference, U-CFR launches a cascade of refinement steps, where each stage leverages the uncertainty-driven pseudo-clicks to refine the mask progressively.
在推理过程中，U-CFR 启动了一系列级联细化步骤，每个阶段都利用不确定性驱动的伪点击来逐步细化掩码。

Experiments on standard benchmark datasets demonstrate that the proposed U-CFR improves click efficiency, initial mask quality, and boundary accuracy.
在标准基准数据集上的实验表明，所提出的 U-CFR 提高了点击效率、初始掩码质量和边界准确性。

It reduces the required clicks by over 10% on challenging datasets like Berkeley and offers a more intelligent and efficient interactive annotation.
它在 Berkeley 等具有挑战性的数据集上将所需点击次数减少了 10% 以上，并提供了一种更智能、更高效的交互式标注方式。
