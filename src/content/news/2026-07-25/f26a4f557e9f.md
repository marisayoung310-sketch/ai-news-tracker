# Tabular LLMs: An Introduction to the Foundation Models That Predict Your Spreadsheet
# 表格大模型：预测电子表格的基础模型简介

A 28M-parameter model you don’t train beats tuned XGBoost. An introduction to tabular foundation models, with an independent reproduction.
一个无需训练、仅有 2800 万参数的模型击败了经过调优的 XGBoost。本文将介绍表格基础模型，并提供独立的复现结果。

TabICLv2’s three-stage pipeline. It reads a whole table in one forward pass and predicts each test row’s missing target — like a learned k-NN, with no fitting to your data. (1) A set transformer embeds each cell into a 128-d vector, per column and target-aware. (2) For each row, 4 CLS tokens attend across its features and concatenate into one 512-d token (RoPE). (3) Twelve in-context blocks let each test row attend over the labelled training rows only, with QASSMax keeping attention sharp as training rows grow. A classification or 999-quantile regression head emits one distribution per test row.
TabICLv2 的三阶段流水线：它通过一次前向传播读取整个表格，并预测每个测试行的缺失目标值——就像一个“习得的 k-NN（最近邻算法）”，无需针对你的数据进行拟合。(1) 一个集合 Transformer 将每个单元格嵌入为 128 维向量，并兼顾列信息与目标感知。(2) 对于每一行，4 个 CLS token 在其特征间进行注意力计算，并拼接成一个 512 维的 token (RoPE)。(3) 十二个上下文块（in-context blocks）让每个测试行仅关注已标记的训练行，QASSMax 机制确保随着训练行数的增加，注意力依然保持精准。分类或 999 分位数回归头会为每个测试行输出一个分布。

A tabular foundation model predicts the missing column of any table, zero-shot, the way a language model completes text. On the main community benchmark, every single-model entry above the best tuned gradient-boosted tree is now one of these. This post explains what they are, verifies the strongest one with unrestricted open weights on my own hardware, and maps where the trees still win.
表格基础模型能够像语言模型补全文本一样，零样本（zero-shot）预测任何表格中的缺失列。在主流社区基准测试中，所有表现优于最佳调优梯度提升树（GBDT）的单模型条目，现在都是这类模型。本文将解释它们是什么，在我的硬件上验证其中权重完全开放的最强模型，并梳理树模型依然占据优势的领域。

TabICLv2 (blue) sits on the cheap-and-accurate frontier among models anyone can download and check; TabFM (hollow) tops the board but ships no paper, and the tree family (squares) sits 150+ Elo lower at comparable serving cost.
图 1：TabArena（官方排行榜，2026 年 7 月 15 日快照）上的准确率与服务成本对比。TabICLv2（蓝色）处于“低成本且高准确率”的前沿，且任何人都可以下载并验证；TabFM（空心）位居榜首但未发布论文，而树模型家族（方形）在相似的服务成本下，Elo 分数低了 150 多分。

There is a fairly new class of model for tables: pretrained transformers that predict on any spreadsheet zero-shot, loosely called “tabular LLMs”. On the TabArena leaderboard, every single-model entry above the best tuned, ensembled GBDT configuration is one of them; the only entries above it that aren’t are AutoGluon’s 4-hour ensemble pipelines. That inverts the default answer of the last decade, which was to fit a tuned gradient-boosted tree.
表格领域出现了一类相当新的模型：预训练 Transformer，它们能对任何电子表格进行零样本预测，被笼统地称为“表格大模型（tabular LLMs）”。在 TabArena 排行榜上，所有表现优于最佳调优集成 GBDT 配置的单模型条目都是此类模型；唯一表现更好但非此类模型的，是 AutoGluon 的 4 小时集成流水线。这颠覆了过去十年间的默认方案——即拟合一个调优后的梯度提升树。

Leaderboards have been wrong before, in ways that only show up when an outsider re-runs them (that was my experience with time-series models), so before believing this one I audited it. I recomputed the board’s scoring from its published artefacts, took TabICLv2, the strongest model on it with unrestricted open weights, and re-ran it from scratch on hardware I controlled. My independent run covered all 51 datasets on the benchmark’s official Lite protocol (one train/test split per dataset, the same split indices the leaderboard uses) on a single AWS A10G.
排行榜过去曾出现过错误，只有当外部人员重新运行测试时才会显现（我在时间序列模型中就有过这种经历），所以在相信这个排行榜之前，我对其进行了审计。我根据其发布的产物重新计算了分数，选取了其中权重完全开放的最强模型 TabICLv2，并在我控制的硬件上从头开始运行。我的独立运行覆盖了基准测试官方 Lite 协议中的所有 51 个数据集（每个数据集一个训练/测试集划分，使用与排行榜相同的划分索引），运行在单台 AWS A10G 上。

Scored against the official artefact rows on those identical splits, my TabICLv2 lands at Elo 1559 against the official 1575, adjacent ranks and well inside the ±60–86 bootstrap intervals. Per task, 16 of the 51 metric values came back identical to four decimal places, the median relative difference was 0.08%, and the worst dataset differed by 3.5%, the size of gap you’d expect from GPU nondeterminism rather than a methodology difference. The whole sweep, environment setup included, fitted in a 2.1-hour GPU session that cost just over $2.
在相同的划分下，对比官方产物行进行评分，我的 TabICLv2 获得了 1559 的 Elo 分数，而官方为 1575，两者排名相邻，且完全处于 ±60–86 的自助法（bootstrap）置信区间内。在每个任务中，51 个指标值中有 16 个在小数点后四位完全一致，中位数相对差异为 0.08%，最差的数据集差异为 3.5%，这属于 GPU 非确定性带来的预期误差范围，而非方法论差异。整个测试过程（包括环境配置）在 2.1 小时的 GPU 会话中完成，成本仅略高于 2 美元。

What a tabular foundation model is: A tabular foundation model is a single pretrained model that predicts on any table, zero-shot. You hand it the rows whose labels you know as context, the way you would paste worked examples into an LLM prompt, and it predicts the labels of the held-out rows in one forward pass. Nothing gradient-trains on your data and there is no hyperparameter search; the step every other tabular method calls “training” becomes inference.
什么是表格基础模型：表格基础模型是一个单一的预训练模型，可以对任何表格进行零样本预测。你将已知标签的行作为上下文提供给它，就像将示例粘贴到 LLM 提示词中一样，它通过一次前向传播即可预测出保留行的标签。你的数据无需进行梯度训练，也没有超参数搜索；其他表格方法中所谓的“训练”步骤，在这里变成了推理。

The literature calls this in-context learning, and the TabPFN papers established that it works for tables. It behaves like a learned k-nearest-neighbours: to label a held-out row, the model attends over the rows whose labels it can see and reads off an answer weighted by how similar they are. The difference from ordinary k-NN is that the similarity, and the way neighbours are combined, come from pretraining rather than being fixed in advance.
文献称之为“上下文学习（in-context learning）”，TabPFN 的论文证实了它在表格上的有效性。它的行为类似于一个“习得的 k-最近邻算法”：为了给保留行打标签，模型会对可见标签的行进行注意力计算，并根据相似度加权得出答案。与普通 k-NN 的区别在于，相似度计算以及邻居的组合方式源于预训练，而不是预先固定的。

The family has grown fast since: TabPFN’s successors (Prior Labs), TabICL and TabICLv2 (Inria’s SODA team), TabDPT (Layer 6), and, two weeks before this post, Google Research’s TabFM. All of them appear in the standings below.
此后，该家族发展迅速：TabPFN 的继任者（Prior Labs）、TabICL 和 TabICLv2（Inria 的 SODA 团队）、TabDPT（Layer 6），以及在本文发布前两周出现的 Google Research 的 TabFM。它们都出现在下方的排名中。

The “LLM” in “tabular LLM” is loose. These models are transformers, but they do not model language, and they differ from a text LLM in every ingredient. A text LLM’s token is a word-piece from a fixed vocabulary; a tabular model’s raw material is the cells, columns, and rows of a table, numbers and categories with no fixed vocabulary at all, since your table’s columns have never been seen before and the model has to cope anyway.
“表格大模型”中的“大模型（LLM）”一词用得比较宽泛。这些模型虽然是 Transformer，但它们并不建模语言，且在每一个组成部分上都与文本大模型不同。文本大模型的 token 是来自固定词表的词片段；而表格模型的原材料是表格的单元格、列和行，即数字和类别，根本没有固定词表，因为你的表格列是模型从未见过的，但它必须能够处理。

A text LLM pretrains on internet text with next-token prediction; a tabular foundation model pretrains on millions of small synthetic tables generated by random structural causal models (random cause-and-effect graphs wired up to emit plausible fake datasets), and its objective is to predict the held-out cells and labels of those tables. Pretraining instils a general procedure for reading a fresh table and inferring how its features relate to its target, rather than knowledge about the world.
文本大模型通过预测下一个 token 在互联网文本上进行预训练；而表格基础模型通过数百万个由随机结构因果模型（随机因果图，用于生成合理的虚构数据集）生成的小型合成表格进行预训练，其目标是预测这些表格中被保留的单元格和标签。预训练灌输的是一种读取新表格并推断特征与目标之间关系的通用程序，而不是关于世界的知识。

One consequence is size. Tables carry less surface complexity than language, so tens of millions of parameters are enough: TabICLv2 is about 28M, against t0-alpha’s 102M and a text LLM’s billions. The contrast with the time-series foundation models from the t0 post comes down to order. A time series has one: t0, Chronos, and TimesFM apply causal attention along time, only ever looking at the past, and forecasting means continuing the sequence beyond its end. A table has no row order. Shuffle the rows and it is the same table, so the architecture needs the opposite property: predictions must not depend on how the spreadsheet happened to be sorted.
一个结果就是模型规模。表格所承载的表面复杂性低于语言，因此几千万个参数就足够了：TabICLv2 约为 2800 万参数，相比之下 t0-alpha 为 1.02 亿，而文本大模型则有数十亿。与 t0 文章中提到的时间序列基础模型的对比，归根结底在于顺序。时间序列是有顺序的：t0、Chronos 和 TimesFM 沿时间轴应用因果注意力，只关注过去，预测意味着在序列末尾进行延伸。而表格没有行顺序。打乱行顺序，表格依然是同一个表格，因此架构需要相反的特性：预测结果不能依赖于电子表格当前的排序方式。
