# Fluree AI

Fluree is an enterprise AI data platform and verifiable knowledge graph database that combines immutability, security, and semantic graph capabilities. It operates as a unified intelligence layer, helping organizations connect fragmented information into a single "source of truth" to power zero-hallucination AI agents, analytics, and decentralized data sharing.

Fluree 是一个企业级 AI 数据平台和可验证知识图谱数据库，结合了不可篡改性、安全性和语义图谱功能。它作为一个统一的智能层运行，帮助企业将碎片化的信息连接成单一的“事实来源”，从而为零幻觉 AI 智能体、分析和去中心化数据共享提供支持。

***

Fluree AI gives every app and AI agent the same trusted context from your company data. Ask questions and get cited, verifiable answers from one live data layer, with permissions checked on every request.

Fluree AI 为每个应用程序和 AI 智能体提供来自公司数据的、统一且可信的上下文。用户可以提出问题，并从单一的实时数据层获取带有引用来源、可验证的答案，且每次请求都会进行权限校验。

***

Instead of rebuilding prompts or relying on RAG guesses, Fluree queries structured data directly and connects to MCP-ready agents, dashboards, and apps in minutes.

Fluree 无需重新构建提示词或依赖 RAG（检索增强生成）的猜测，而是直接查询结构化数据，并在几分钟内连接到支持 MCP（模型上下文协议）的智能体、仪表板和应用程序。
