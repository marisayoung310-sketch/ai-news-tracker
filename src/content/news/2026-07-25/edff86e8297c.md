### The Future of Money Movement: Stablecoins, Instant Payments & What’s Next
### 资金流动的未来：稳定币、即时支付及后续发展

Stablecoins and instant payments are changing how money moves around the world. This session looks at how these new payment systems compare to traditional banking infrastructure, where FedNow and private networks fit in, and how regulatory and market changes could shape the future of payments. Hear where these new systems are gaining traction, and which challenges remain. With Nikhil Chandhok, Chief Product & Technology Officer, Circle; Rodney Robinson, Co-founder and CEO, TabaPay, Inc.; and Lotti Siniscalco, General Partner, Emergence.

稳定币和即时支付正在改变全球资金的流动方式。本场会议将探讨这些新型支付系统与传统银行基础设施的对比，FedNow 和私有网络在其中的定位，以及监管和市场变化如何塑造支付的未来。听听这些新系统在哪些领域获得了增长，以及目前仍面临哪些挑战。嘉宾包括：Circle 首席产品与技术官 Nikhil Chandhok；TabaPay, Inc. 联合创始人兼 CEO Rodney Robinson；以及 Emergence 普通合伙人 Lotti Siniscalco。

### Winning the Modern Financial Consumer
### 赢得现代金融消费者

The way people pay, invest, and manage money is changing fast. Robinhood, currently boasting a market cap of more than $90 billion, has evolved from a trading app into a financial platform spanning investing, banking, credit, crypto, and prediction markets. Head of Product Abhishek Fatehpuria will share how technology and changing consumer expectations are reshaping financial services, and what it takes to build trusted products that hold up under massive growth. With Abhishek Fatehpuria, Head of Product, Robinhood.

人们支付、投资和管理金钱的方式正在迅速改变。Robinhood 目前市值已超过 900 亿美元，它已从一个交易应用程序演变为涵盖投资、银行、信贷、加密货币和预测市场的综合金融平台。产品负责人 Abhishek Fatehpuria 将分享技术和不断变化的消费者期望如何重塑金融服务，以及在高速增长下构建值得信赖的产品需要具备什么。嘉宾：Robinhood 产品负责人 Abhishek Fatehpuria。

### AI, Trust & Verification in Financial Services
### 金融服务中的人工智能、信任与验证

As AI moves beyond generating content and begins taking action, financial companies are rethinking trust, oversight, and security. Our panelists will explore how AI agents are changing financial workflows, why transparency and human judgment still matter, and how companies are right now approaching privacy, fraud prevention, and identity verification in an AI-powered world. With Hannah Bozian, VP, Agentic Partnerships & Strategy, American Express; Pedro Sanzovo, Head of Fraud and Identity, Plaid; and Victoria Zuo, Partner, QED Investors.

随着人工智能从生成内容转向采取行动，金融公司正在重新思考信任、监管和安全问题。我们的嘉宾将探讨 AI 智能体如何改变金融工作流程，为什么透明度和人类判断依然重要，以及在人工智能驱动的世界中，公司目前如何处理隐私、欺诈预防和身份验证。嘉宾包括：美国运通智能体合作与战略副总裁 Hannah Bozian；Plaid 欺诈与身份部门负责人 Pedro Sanzovo；以及 QED Investors 合伙人 Victoria Zuo。

### Building the Infrastructure for Global Commerce
### 构建全球商业基础设施

Traditional financial systems weren’t built for today’s global businesses. Airwallex, now valued at $11 billion by its investors, is building an AI native financial operating system, helping companies move money across borders, manage global finances, and embed financial products into their own platforms. Founder and CEO Jack Zhang will give you a look at how AI is reshaping payments, and what it takes to build regulated financial infrastructure that powers millions of businesses. With Jack Zhang, Founder & CEO, Airwallex.

传统的金融系统并非为当今的全球化企业而建。Airwallex（空中云汇）目前估值已达 110 亿美元，正在构建一个 AI 原生金融操作系统，帮助企业进行跨境资金流动、管理全球财务，并将金融产品嵌入其自身平台。创始人兼 CEO Jack Zhang 将带你了解 AI 如何重塑支付，以及构建支撑数百万企业的受监管金融基础设施需要什么。嘉宾：Airwallex 创始人兼 CEO Jack Zhang。
