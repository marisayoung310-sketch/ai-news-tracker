# DS@GT ARC at ImageCLEFmed GANs 2026: Geometric Filtering for Privacy-Preserving CT Slice Generation
# DS@GT ARC 在 ImageCLEFmed GANs 2026 竞赛中的表现：用于隐私保护 CT 切片生成的几何过滤技术

**Abstract:** We present a privacy-preserving framework for synthetic lung CT slice generation developed for the Image-CLEFmed GANs 2026 challenge. The approach combines Optimal Transport Conditional Flow Matching with privacy-oriented training and a post-generation "Supervisor" pipeline that filters generated candidates in learned geometric latent spaces using autoencoder embeddings, Determinantal Point Processes, and Stein Kernel Thinning.

**摘要：** 我们提出了一种为 Image-CLEFmed GANs 2026 挑战赛开发的合成肺部 CT 切片生成隐私保护框架。该方法结合了最优传输条件流匹配（Optimal Transport Conditional Flow Matching）与面向隐私的训练，并引入了一个生成后的“监督者”（Supervisor）流水线。该流水线利用自动编码器嵌入、行列式点过程（Determinantal Point Processes）和 Stein 核稀疏化（Stein Kernel Thinning），在学习到的几何潜在空间中对生成的候选图像进行过滤。

Official results show a strong realism-privacy trade-off, with the best-performing model achieving a Privacy Preservation Score of 0.549 and competitive visual fidelity with an FID of 0.3290. While the proposed geometric filtering substantially reduces nearest-neighbor memorization and membership-inference leakage, persistent patient re-identification scores indicate that preventing direct image copying is not sufficient to remove deeper patient-specific anatomical identity, highlighting an important frontier for future privacy-preserving medical image generation.

官方结果显示，该模型在真实感与隐私保护之间取得了良好的平衡，表现最好的模型实现了 0.549 的隐私保护评分，并以 0.3290 的 FID（弗雷歇起始距离）达到了具有竞争力的视觉保真度。尽管所提出的几何过滤方法显著降低了最近邻记忆和成员推理泄露的风险，但持续存在的患者重新识别评分表明，仅防止直接的图像复制不足以消除更深层次的患者特定解剖特征，这凸显了未来隐私保护医学图像生成领域的一个重要研究前沿。
