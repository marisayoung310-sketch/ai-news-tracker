### OVEarth-Bench: Evaluating Category Breadth and Query Diversity for Open-Vocabulary Earth Observation
### OVEarth-Bench：评估开放词汇对地观测的类别广度与查询多样性

**Abstract:** Open-vocabulary Earth observation (EO) aims to localize geospatial concepts specified in natural language rather than a fixed label set. Existing benchmarks, however, usually cover narrow category vocabularies or limited query forms. To fill this gap, we introduce OVEarth-Bench, which extends existing evaluation in two directions: category breadth, through broad hierarchical category coverage with positive and negative expressions, and query diversity, through vocabulary, referring, and reasoning queries.

**摘要：** 开放词汇对地观测（EO）旨在定位自然语言描述的地理空间概念，而非局限于固定的标签集。然而，现有的基准测试通常涵盖的类别词汇较窄或查询形式有限。为了填补这一空白，我们引入了 OVEarth-Bench，它从两个方向扩展了现有的评估体系：一是通过包含正向和负向表达的广泛层级类别覆盖来提升“类别广度”；二是通过词汇查询、指代查询和推理查询来提升“查询多样性”。

The benchmark supports mask and box localization under a unified zero-shot protocol. We evaluate a broad set of general and EO-specific methods. The evaluation reveals that: (1) the performance of current methods remains limited, while broader category coverage yields more stable model rankings; (2) MLLM-based methods achieve the strongest overall performance; and (3) EO-specific methods generally underperform general models and rarely match the strongest methods. These findings provide guidance for future open-vocabulary EO method design and highlight the importance of developing more realistic, diverse, high-quality, and large-scale benchmarks for reliable evaluation. Our data and evaluation package are released at this https URL.

该基准测试支持在统一的零样本（zero-shot）协议下进行掩码（mask）和边界框（box）定位。我们评估了一系列通用方法和对地观测专用方法。评估结果显示：（1）当前方法的性能仍然有限，但更广泛的类别覆盖能带来更稳定的模型排名；（2）基于多模态大语言模型（MLLM）的方法实现了最强的整体性能；（3）对地观测专用方法通常表现不如通用模型，且很少能达到最强方法的水平。这些发现为未来开放词汇对地观测方法的设计提供了指导，并强调了开发更真实、更多样、高质量且大规模的基准测试对于可靠评估的重要性。我们的数据和评估包已在指定链接发布。
