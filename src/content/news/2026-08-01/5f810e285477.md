# IGME: Efficient Chained Method Ensemble for Transferable Semantic Segmentation Attacks
# IGME：用于可迁移语义分割攻击的高效链式方法集成

**Abstract:** Semantic segmentation models are vulnerable to transferable adversarial perturbations, yet evaluating transfer attacks on dense prediction models can be computationally expensive. Existing ensemble attacks often rely on multiple surrogate models, increasing the computation cost, even harder for segmentation. 

**摘要：** 语义分割模型容易受到可迁移对抗扰动的影响，然而在密集预测模型上评估迁移攻击的计算成本可能非常高昂。现有的集成攻击通常依赖于多个代理模型，这增加了计算成本，对于语义分割任务而言更是如此。

This paper studies an efficient single-source alternative for transferable attacks on semantic segmentation. We formulate transferable attack composition as a chained computation over differentiable attack components, allowing the expensive source-model gradient computation to be shared. 

本文研究了一种用于语义分割可迁移攻击的高效单源替代方案。我们将可迁移攻击的组合形式化为在可微攻击组件上的链式计算，从而使得昂贵的源模型梯度计算能够被共享。

To reduce the update instability introduced by chained composition, we further use an integrated-gradient-style path-averaged direction as an empirical stabilization heuristic. Experiments on Pascal VOC and Cityscapes evaluate the resulting transferability efficiency trade-off across CNN- and transformer-based segmentation models. 

为了减少由链式组合引入的更新不稳定性，我们进一步采用了一种集成梯度风格的路径平均方向作为经验稳定启发式方法。在 Pascal VOC 和 Cityscapes 数据集上的实验评估了该方法在基于 CNN 和 Transformer 的分割模型中，在迁移能力与效率之间取得的平衡。

IGME achieves competitive transferability compared with single-source baselines and favorable runtime compared with model-ensemble attacks, while requiring access to only one source model.

IGME 在保持与单源基线相当的迁移能力的同时，相比模型集成攻击具有更优的运行时间，且仅需访问一个源模型。
