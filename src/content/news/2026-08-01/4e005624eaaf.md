# Beyond KV Reconstruction: Functional Reconstruction for MLA Draft Models in Speculative Decoding
# 超越 KV 重构：推测解码中 MLA 草稿模型的函数重构

Multi-head latent attention (MLA) is increasingly important for long-context LLM inference because compact latent states replace the growing key-value (KV) cache and reduce decoding memory traffic. Yet most capable open checkpoints use multi-head or grouped-query attention (MHA/GQA), so conversion is needed to obtain MLA's cache efficiency without retraining from scratch.

多头潜在注意力（MLA）对于长上下文大语言模型（LLM）推理日益重要，因为它利用紧凑的潜在状态取代了不断增长的键值（KV）缓存，从而减少了解码过程中的内存流量。然而，目前大多数高性能的开源模型检查点仍使用多头注意力（MHA）或分组查询注意力（GQA），因此需要进行转换，以便在无需从头训练的情况下获得 MLA 的缓存效率。

Speculative decoding offers complementary acceleration, but its speedup depends on agreement between draft proposals and target verification. We find that direct MHA/GQA-to-MLA conversion can sharply reduce this agreement: low-rank factorization and RoPE handling introduce attention-function errors that may be tolerable for standalone generation but substantially lower draft-token acceptance.

推测解码提供了互补的加速方案，但其加速效果取决于草稿建议与目标验证之间的一致性。我们发现，直接将 MHA/GQA 转换为 MLA 会显著降低这种一致性：低秩分解和旋转位置编码（RoPE）的处理引入了注意力函数误差，这些误差在独立生成任务中或许可以容忍，但会大幅降低草稿 Token 的接受率。

We therefore formulate MLA draft construction as functional reconstruction rather than cache compression. Our end-to-end (E2E) method optimizes each converted MLA attention module to reproduce the post-output-projection response of its original MHA/GQA counterpart on calibration hidden states. This converter-agnostic post-conversion procedure preserves the converted cache and inference graph and requires neither verifier logits nor verifier supervision.

因此，我们将 MLA 草稿构建定义为“函数重构”而非“缓存压缩”。我们的端到端（E2E）方法通过优化每个转换后的 MLA 注意力模块，使其在校准隐藏状态下能够复现原始 MHA/GQA 模块在输出投影后的响应。这种与转换器无关的后处理过程保留了转换后的缓存和推理图，且既不需要验证器的 Logits，也不需要验证器的监督。

We evaluate 192 model-converter-backend-method-task configurations spanning four Llama/Qwen draft-target pairs, TransMLA and MHA2MLA, HF and vLLM, and four 200-prompt tasks. With a 0.5-percentage-point reporting tolerance, Functional Reconstruction materially improves acceptance in 37 of 64 matched task cells, leaves 26 practically unchanged, and materially decreases one. Code and evaluation artifacts are available at this https URL.

我们评估了 192 种模型-转换器-后端-方法-任务配置，涵盖了四对 Llama/Qwen 草稿-目标模型、TransMLA 和 MHA2MLA、HF 和 vLLM 后端，以及四个包含 200 个提示词的任务。在 0.5 个百分点的报告容差下，函数重构方法在 64 个匹配的任务单元中，有 37 个显著提高了接受率，26 个基本保持不变，仅 1 个出现显著下降。代码和评估产物可在该链接获取。
