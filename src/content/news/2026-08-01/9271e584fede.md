# PlatformBid: An Auto-Bidding Benchmark from a Unified Advertising Platform's Perspective
# PlatformBid：从统一广告平台视角出发的自动出价基准

**Abstract:** Real-time bidding is central to computational advertising, comprising three elements: Supply Side Platform (SSP) selling ad impressions, Demand Side Platform (DSP) bidding for advertisers, and Ad Exchange conducting auctions between them. 

**摘要：** 实时竞价是计算广告的核心，包含三个要素：销售广告展示的供应方平台（SSP）、为广告主进行竞价的需求方平台（DSP），以及在两者之间进行拍卖的广告交易平台（Ad Exchange）。

Traditional auto-bidding algorithms focus solely on the DSP side, maximizing advertiser conversions by adjusting bids against competitors. However, current big ad platforms, such as social media and e-commerce companies, now integrate SSP, DSP, and Ad Exchange functions internally. 

传统的自动出价算法仅关注 DSP 端，通过调整出价以应对竞争对手，从而最大化广告主的转化率。然而，当前的各大广告平台（如社交媒体和电商公司）现在已在内部集成了 SSP、DSP 和广告交易平台的功能。

From such ad platforms' perspective, the goal of the auto-bidding algorithms is not only to maximize the advertisers' conversions, but also the total revenue of the platform. Given the lack of platform-centric evaluation frameworks and the pressing need to advance auto-bidding research, we propose PlatformBid - the first comprehensive benchmark designed from a unified ad platform's perspective. 

从这些广告平台的视角来看，自动出价算法的目标不仅是最大化广告主的转化，还要最大化平台的总收入。鉴于目前缺乏以平台为中心的评估框架，且推进自动出价研究的需求迫在眉睫，我们提出了 PlatformBid——这是首个从统一广告平台视角设计的综合基准。

To accurately reflect the real-world auto-bidding scenarios, we define three representative settings: (1) homogeneous competition with identical algorithms across advertisers, (2) heterogeneous competition with diverse algorithmic strategies, and (3) promotional competition where some advertisers surge budgets for boosting sales during promotional events like Black Friday. 

为了准确反映现实世界的自动出价场景，我们定义了三种代表性设置：（1）所有广告主使用相同算法的同质竞争；（2）采用多样化算法策略的异质竞争；以及（3）促销竞争，即部分广告主在“黑色星期五”等促销活动期间增加预算以提升销量。

We systematically evaluate a broad spectrum of existing auto-bidding methods across these settings, encompassing classical control methods, RL-based methods, and recent generative methods. Besides these methods, we further propose a novel auto-bidding method based on flow-matching, termed BidFlow, which leverages the flow-matching method's expressive policy representation to effectively handle dynamic competitive environments. 

我们针对这些设置系统地评估了广泛的现有自动出价方法，涵盖了经典控制方法、基于强化学习（RL）的方法以及近期的生成式方法。除了这些方法，我们还提出了一种基于流匹配（flow-matching）的新型自动出价方法，称为 BidFlow。该方法利用流匹配技术强大的策略表示能力，能够有效应对动态的竞争环境。

Online experiments on Kuaishou further show a +0.68% improvement in target cost, providing deployment evidence for the offline-online consistency of PlatformBid.

在快手进行的在线实验进一步显示，目标成本（target cost）提升了 +0.68%，为 PlatformBid 的离线与在线一致性提供了部署证据。
