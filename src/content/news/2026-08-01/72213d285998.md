# Betting Ban Takes Effect: No More Gambling Sponsors in the Premier League

**Gambling Sponsorship Ban Implemented:** The front-of-shirt gambling sponsorship ban officially took effect for the 2026-27 Premier League season, completely eliminating betting companies from the front of all 20 club jerseys for the first time in many seasons.

**博彩赞助禁令正式实施：** 英超联赛 2026-27 赛季正式实施球衣胸前广告博彩赞助禁令，这是多年来首次在所有 20 家俱乐部的球衣胸前彻底清除了博彩公司广告。

**Shift in Commercial Landscape:** The ban has dramatically changed the commercial sponsorship landscape, with finance becoming the new dominant sector, and three clubs (Sunderland, Nottingham Forest, and Chelsea) currently without a front-of-shirt sponsor as a direct or indirect consequence.

**商业格局的转变：** 该禁令极大地改变了商业赞助格局，金融业成为新的主导行业。受此直接或间接影响，目前有三家俱乐部（桑德兰、诺丁汉森林和切尔西）尚未找到胸前广告赞助商。

**Alternative Sponsorship Avenues Remain:** Despite the front-of-shirt prohibition, gambling brands are still permitted to maintain a visual presence in the Premier League through shirt sleeve sponsorships or training kit branding, as demonstrated by Betano's new deals.

**其他赞助渠道依然存在：** 尽管胸前广告被禁，但博彩品牌仍被允许通过球衣袖口赞助或训练服品牌展示来维持在英超联赛的曝光度，Betano 的新合作协议便是一个例证。

**From Dominance to Complete Elimination:** For 2026-27, every single one of those front-of-shirt betting logos has completely evaporated as the official prohibition comes into full force. Looking back at the 2025-26 campaign, gambling companies held absolute dominance over the league, featuring as the primary sponsor for 11 out of the 20 teams.

**从垄断到彻底消失：** 随着官方禁令全面生效，2026-27 赛季所有球衣胸前的博彩标志已彻底消失。回顾 2025-26 赛季，博彩公司在联赛中占据绝对主导地位，曾是 20 支球队中 11 支球队的主要赞助商。

**Commercial Breakdown:** With betting operators pushed off the front of shirts, the commercial breakdown across the Premier League now shows a much more balanced distribution across a variety of global industries: Finance (5 Teams) takes over as the dominant sector; Airline (3 Teams) holds the second-largest presence; other sectors include Technology, Food & Beverage, Human Resources, Software, Insurance, Telecommunications, and Tourism.

**商业分布概况：** 随着博彩运营商退出球衣胸前位置，英超联赛的商业分布呈现出更加均衡的态势，涵盖了全球多个行业：金融业（5 支球队）成为主导行业；航空业（3 支球队）位居第二；其他行业还包括科技、食品饮料、人力资源、软件、保险、电信和旅游业。

**Three Clubs Left Without Sponsors:** Despite the market opening up, three Premier League teams currently find themselves without a front-of-shirt sponsor as the season approaches: Sunderland, Nottingham Forest, and Chelsea. Sunderland and Nottingham Forest are suffering the direct aftermath of the new regulation, while Chelsea continues its peculiar ongoing sponsorship saga, entering a fourth consecutive season struggling to secure a stable, long-term partner.

**三家俱乐部尚无赞助商：** 尽管市场空间已经打开，但随着新赛季临近，仍有三支英超球队没有胸前赞助商：桑德兰、诺丁汉森林和切尔西。桑德兰和诺丁汉森林正承受着新规带来的直接后果，而切尔西则继续其奇特的赞助困境，这已是他们连续第四个赛季难以敲定稳定的长期合作伙伴。

**Pivoting to Sleeves and Training Wear:** While front-of-shirt real estate is now strictly off-limits, gambling brands are not disappearing from the Premier League entirely. Betting operator Betano serves as a prime example of this pivot, adapting to the new rules by taking over as Aston Villa's new sleeve sponsor while simultaneously becoming the official training wear sponsor for Tottenham Hotspur.

**转向袖口与训练服：** 虽然球衣胸前位置现已严格禁止，但博彩品牌并未完全从英超联赛中消失。博彩运营商 Betano 就是这一转型的主要代表，他们通过成为阿斯顿维拉的新袖口赞助商，同时担任托特纳姆热刺的官方训练服赞助商，从而适应了新规则。
