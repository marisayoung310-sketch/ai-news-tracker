### The Best Dyson Vacuum: Dyson Gen5Detect
### 最佳戴森吸尘器：Dyson Gen5Detect

Dyson's most powerful stick vacuum is the Dyson Gen5Detect. It's been our top pick for pet hair since it does a fantastic job deep cleaning and has a HEPA filter that Dyson says traps 99.99 percent of particles down to 0.1 microns. After side-by-side testing against Dyson's new vacuums and our longtime favorite, the V15 Detect, the Gen5Detect came out with the best score.

戴森最强大的手持吸尘器是 Dyson Gen5Detect。它一直是我们清理宠物毛发的首选，因为它在深度清洁方面表现出色，并配备了 HEPA 滤网，戴森称其能捕捉 99.99% 小至 0.1 微米的颗粒物。在与戴森新款吸尘器以及我们长期以来的最爱 V15 Detect 进行对比测试后，Gen5Detect 获得了最高分。

The Gen5Detect scored the highest out of all five Dyson vacuums I tested it with (the V16 Piston Animal, V15 Detect, V10 Konical, and V8 Cyclone) in vacuuming up Cheerios on carpet, cat litter on hard floors, and general vacuuming in both vacuum mode and handheld mode. It didn't do as well vacuuming sand on a thin rug, but when I moved to a thicker rug, it got all of the sand in a single sweep. You can really feel the suction in action on this vacuum—it packs 262 air watts (AW) and has a motor that reaches 35,000 revolutions per minute (rpm).

在清理地毯上的麦圈、硬地板上的猫砂，以及在吸尘模式和手持模式下的日常清洁测试中，Gen5Detect 在我测试的五款戴森吸尘器（V16 Piston Animal、V15 Detect、V10 Konical 和 V8 Cyclone）中得分最高。虽然它在清理薄地毯上的沙子时表现稍逊，但换到较厚的地毯上时，它一次扫过就清理干净了所有沙子。你能真切感受到这款吸尘器的吸力——它拥有 262 空气瓦特（AW）的吸力，电机转速高达每分钟 35,000 转（rpm）。

### The Best Affordable Dyson: Dyson V10 Konical
### 最佳高性价比戴森：Dyson V10 Konical

If you want to spend less but still get a great Dyson vacuum, the V10 Konical did much better than I expected. It has 150 air watts of power, three cleaning modes, a HEPA filter, and a new cleaner head called the All Floor Cones head. It's similar to the new head you'll find on the V16 Piston Animal, though without the sensing technology. Still, it's designed for both hard floors and soft carpet, and uses dual rotating conical brush bars to keep hair from getting tangled in it as it vacuums.

如果你想少花钱但仍想拥有一台出色的戴森吸尘器，V10 Konical 的表现远超我的预期。它拥有 150 空气瓦特的功率、三种清洁模式、HEPA 滤网，以及一个名为“All Floor Cones”的新型吸头。它与 V16 Piston Animal 上配备的新吸头类似，只是没有感应技术。尽管如此，它专为硬地板和软地毯设计，并使用双旋转锥形刷条，防止在吸尘过程中毛发缠绕。

The V10 Konical did a good job in my overall testing of Dyson vacuums. It did slightly better than the more powerful V15 Detect when vacuuming sand on a rug, and it did a good job picking up Cheerios. It felt similar on rugs and carpets to the V15 Detect, though it has a lower power level and cheaper price point. Overall, if you're set on a Dyson but don't want to spend close to a grand, this is a great option.

在我的戴森吸尘器综合测试中，V10 Konical 表现良好。在清理地毯上的沙子时，它比功率更大的 V15 Detect 表现稍好，清理麦圈的效果也很不错。虽然它的功率较低且价格更便宜，但在地毯上的使用感受与 V15 Detect 相似。总的来说，如果你认准了戴森，但又不想花费近千美元，这是一个绝佳的选择。
