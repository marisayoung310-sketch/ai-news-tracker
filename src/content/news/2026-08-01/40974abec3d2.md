# Apple’s new AirTags are back down to their best price
# 苹果新款 AirTag 价格回落至历史最低点

The latest AirTags offer more precise tracking, a louder speaker, and longer range than before.
最新款 AirTag 提供了比以往更精准的追踪功能、更响亮的扬声器以及更远的信号覆盖范围。

We spotted a great deal on Tile trackers earlier this week that’s still live, but if you’re an iPhone owner, we ultimately recommend Apple’s latest AirTag. Right now, you can pick up a four-pack for $89 ($10 off) at Amazon and Target, matching the bundle’s all-time low price. If you’re a member, Costco also has a deal that throws in a fifth AirTag for free.
本周早些时候我们发现 Tile 追踪器有不错的折扣，目前该优惠依然有效，但如果您是 iPhone 用户，我们最终还是推荐苹果最新的 AirTag。目前，您可以在亚马逊（Amazon）和塔吉特（Target）以 89 美元的价格购入四件装（优惠 10 美元），这已达到该套装的历史最低价。如果您是 Costco 会员，他们还提供额外赠送第五个 AirTag 的优惠活动。

A growing number of Bluetooth trackers can tap into Apple’s Find My network these days, but none combine that with Apple’s latest ultra wideband (UWB) chip, which powers even better Precision Finding than before. Precision Finding uses on-screen arrows and distance indicators in the Find My app to guide you directly to a misplaced item, while the second-generation AirTag’s upgraded chip and extended Bluetooth range make it faster and more reliable to locate tags, even in multistory homes. You can also use Precision Finding to find lost items on an Apple Watch, provided you have a Series 9 或 later. And if all else fails, the built-in speaker is noticeably louder than before, making it easier to track down an AirTag by sound.
如今，越来越多的蓝牙追踪器可以接入苹果的“查找”（Find My）网络，但没有一款能像苹果自家的 AirTag 那样，结合最新的超宽带（UWB）芯片，从而实现比以往更出色的“精确查找”功能。“精确查找”功能通过“查找”应用中的屏幕箭头和距离指示器，引导您直接找到丢失的物品。同时，第二代 AirTag 升级后的芯片和更广的蓝牙覆盖范围，使其定位速度更快、更可靠，即使在多层住宅中也能轻松定位。如果您拥有 Apple Watch Series 9 或更新机型，也可以使用“精确查找”功能来寻找丢失物品。如果以上方法都失效，内置扬声器的音量也比以往明显增大，让您更容易通过声音找到 AirTag。

Plus, like its predecessor, the second-generation AirTag comes with a handful of features that are especially useful if you’re traveling this summer. AirTag sharing lets up to five people keep track of the same item, while Apple’s Share Item Location feature allows you to temporarily share an AirTag’s location with participating airlines, including United, Delta, and American Airlines. It’s still a shame Apple doesn’t include a built-in lanyard hole, meaning you’ll need to buy a separate loop or holder, but the tracker continues to use a replaceable CR2032 battery, so you won’t have to replace the entire device when it eventually runs out of power. And at $89 for a four-pack, it’s also a little easier on your wallet today.
此外，与上一代产品一样，第二代 AirTag 具备多项功能，在今年夏天出行时尤为实用。AirTag 共享功能允许最多五个人同时追踪同一物品，而苹果的“共享物品位置”功能则允许您将 AirTag 的位置临时分享给参与合作的航空公司，包括美联航、达美航空和美国航空。遗憾的是，苹果依然没有为其设计内置挂绳孔，这意味着您需要额外购买挂环或保护套。不过，该追踪器继续使用可更换的 CR2032 电池，因此当电量耗尽时，您无需更换整个设备。目前四件装仅需 89 美元，性价比也更高。
