# Regularizing modality contribution drift in multimodal continual learning
# 正则化多模态持续学习中的模态贡献漂移

**Abstract:** Multimodal continual learning (MMCL) aims to learn emerging knowledge from multimodal data while preserving knowledge. To mitigate forgetting, current MMCL methods usually focus on cross-modal representation alignment or semantic similarity, but they overlook whether the relative contributions of individual modalities and their interactions remain stable across incremental tasks.

**摘要：** 多模态持续学习（MMCL）旨在从多模态数据中学习新知识，同时保留已有知识。为了缓解遗忘，当前的 MMCL 方法通常侧重于跨模态表示对齐或语义相似性，但它们忽略了各个模态及其交互的相对贡献在增量任务中是否保持稳定。

We term this decision-level shift Modality Contribution Drift (MCD) and quantify it with the MCD score, which combines contribution-strength and relative-reliance changes under controlled interventions on modality subsets. Theoretical and empirical analyses further explain why current MMCL methods cannot reliably mitigate this drift.

我们将这种决策层面的偏移称为“模态贡献漂移”（Modality Contribution Drift, MCD），并利用 MCD 分数对其进行量化。该分数结合了在对模态子集进行受控干预下，贡献强度和相对依赖性的变化。理论和实证分析进一步解释了为何当前的 MMCL 方法无法可靠地缓解这种漂移。

To this end, we propose Continual Modality Contribution Drift Regularization (CMCDR), which preserves the modality contribution structure of previously learned tasks. Since MMCL settings differ in whether old exemplars are available, CMCDR includes both replay-based and replay-free versions.

为此，我们提出了“持续模态贡献漂移正则化”（CMCDR），旨在保留先前学习任务的模态贡献结构。由于 MMCL 设置在是否提供旧样本方面存在差异，CMCDR 包含了基于重放（replay-based）和无重放（replay-free）两个版本。

The replay-based version uses modality-subset interventions as diagnostic probes on stored old samples, compares their contribution profiles between the current model and a frozen previous model, and constrains changes in old-sample modality-specific and interaction contributions.

基于重放的版本使用模态子集干预作为诊断探针，对存储的旧样本进行分析，比较当前模型与冻结的先前模型之间的贡献分布，并约束旧样本在模态特定贡献和交互贡献上的变化。

The replay-free version uses current-task samples as probes and distills the frozen model's old-task contribution responses, thereby regularizing the observed contribution profile without exemplars. Experiments on multimodal class-incremental learning and continual visual question answering validate the generality and effectiveness of CMCDR.

无重放版本则使用当前任务的样本作为探针，提取冻结模型对旧任务的贡献响应，从而在没有旧样本的情况下正则化观察到的贡献分布。在多模态类增量学习和持续视觉问答任务上的实验验证了 CMCDR 的通用性和有效性。
