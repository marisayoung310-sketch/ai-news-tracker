# PanDent: Toward Comprehensive Tooth-Level Structure-Language Consistency in Dental Radiology
# PanDent：迈向牙科放射学中全面的牙齿级结构-语言一致性

**Abstract:** Accurate evaluation of multimodal large language models (MLLMs) in dental panoramic radiography (orthopantomogram, OPG) is limited by the lack of fine-grained, clinically reliable benchmarks that reflect expert interpretation. This work introduces PanDent, a large-scale, clinically grounded OPG benchmark built upon fine-grained, expert-validated tooth-level annotations.

**摘要：** 在牙科全景放射摄影（OPG）领域，多模态大语言模型（MLLM）的准确评估受到缺乏细粒度、临床可靠且能反映专家解读的基准测试的限制。本研究引入了 PanDent，这是一个基于细粒度、经专家验证的牙齿级标注构建的大规模临床基础 OPG 基准测试。

The dataset comprises 9,524 high-quality OPGs, each associated with comprehensive structured annotations produced by experienced dentists and further validated by an oral and maxillofacial radiologist, providing clinically reliable supervision for tooth-level diagnosis and reasoning.

该数据集包含 9,524 张高质量 OPG 图像，每张图像都关联了由经验丰富的牙医制作并经口腔颌面放射科医生进一步验证的全面结构化标注，为牙齿级的诊断和推理提供了临床可靠的监督。

Clinically consistent radiology reports are constructed from expert-validated findings using clinician-defined reporting logic, establishing explicit correspondence between structured clinical evidence and free-text descriptions. This design enables evaluation of whether MLLMs generate reports that are not only linguistically coherent but also clinically consistent with expert-validated tooth-level findings.

临床一致的放射学报告是根据临床医生定义的报告逻辑，从经专家验证的发现中构建出来的，从而在结构化的临床证据与自由文本描述之间建立了明确的对应关系。这种设计使得评估 MLLM 生成的报告不仅在语言上连贯，而且在临床上与经专家验证的牙齿级发现保持一致成为可能。

Experiments are conducted on diverse MLLMs, including state-of-the-art (SOTA) proprietary models, general-domain open-source models, and medical-specific models. Results show that current MLLMs can generate fluent reports, yet fail to produce clinically consistent descriptions, exhibiting substantial errors in fine-grained localization and tooth-level diagnosis.

实验在多种 MLLM 上进行，包括最先进（SOTA）的专有模型、通用领域开源模型以及医学专用模型。结果表明，当前的 MLLM 虽然能够生成流畅的报告，但在生成临床一致的描述方面表现不佳，在细粒度定位和牙齿级诊断方面存在重大错误。

Fine-tuning on PanDent significantly improves structure-language consistency, substantially enhancing visual localization accuracy and diagnostic correctness, and bringing model outputs closer to expert dental interpretation. These results establish PanDent as a rigorous benchmark for evaluating tooth-level clinical reasoning in MLLMs and a valuable resource for clinically grounded dental AI.

在 PanDent 上进行微调显著改善了结构-语言的一致性，大幅提高了视觉定位的准确性和诊断的正确性，使模型输出更接近专家的牙科解读。这些结果确立了 PanDent 作为评估 MLLM 中牙齿级临床推理的严格基准，并成为临床基础牙科人工智能的宝贵资源。
