# VETO: Towards Protecting Images From Frontier AI Editing
# VETO：旨在保护图像免受前沿 AI 编辑的影响

**Abstract:** The rise of powerful, accessible image-editing models such as FLUX.2 has brought high-fidelity editing within broad reach. Their capabilities now extend beyond localized modifications to extracting and recontextualizing objects and identities in entirely new scenes. By allowing prompt and generation tokens to attend directly to reference-image tokens, modern models blur the boundary between conventional editing and text-to-image synthesis. This expanded generative freedom also broadens the space of potential misuse, as harmful transformations are no longer confined to a predictable set of localized edits.

**摘要：** 诸如 FLUX.2 等功能强大且易于使用的图像编辑模型的兴起，使得高保真编辑变得触手可及。它们的能力现已超越了局部修改，能够提取物体和身份并将其重新置于全新的场景中。通过允许提示词（prompt）和生成标记（generation tokens）直接关注参考图像标记，现代模型模糊了传统编辑与文本生成图像之间的界限。这种扩展的生成自由也扩大了潜在的滥用空间，因为有害的转换不再局限于可预测的一系列局部编辑。

Existing anti-edit defenses are designed to disrupt the semantic bottleneck of the reference-image encoding in legacy diffusion pipelines. However, newer editors distill reference information through joint-attention blocks, thereby often circumventing these protections. We therefore introduce VETO, a subtle anti-edit cloak that disrupts this inner mechanism through which modern models read the source image.

现有的防编辑防御措施旨在破坏传统扩散流程中参考图像编码的语义瓶颈。然而，较新的编辑器通过联合注意力模块（joint-attention blocks）提取参考信息，从而经常绕过这些保护措施。因此，我们引入了 VETO，这是一种微妙的防编辑“斗篷”，它能够破坏现代模型读取源图像的内部机制。

Additionally, as existing editing benchmarks leave comprehensive recontextualizations largely untested, we introduce VetoBench, which evaluates defenses not on conventional localized edits but also on broader contextual shifts. Across two contemporary editing models and three benchmarks, VETO consistently outperforms existing defenses while providing a stronger protection-fidelity trade-off.

此外，由于现有的编辑基准测试在很大程度上未对全面的重新语境化（recontextualizations）进行测试，我们引入了 VetoBench。它不仅评估传统局部编辑下的防御效果，还评估更广泛的语境转换下的防御能力。在两个当代编辑模型和三个基准测试中，VETO 的表现始终优于现有的防御措施，同时在保护效果与图像保真度之间提供了更好的平衡。
