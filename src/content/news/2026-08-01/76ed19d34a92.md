# Theatre Chapbooks At Scale: A Statistical Comparative Analysis of Typography
# 大规模戏剧小册子：排版统计对比分析

**Abstract:** We propose a statistical methodology that quantifies the similarity of typefaces between printed historical books. This provides a tool that accelerates philological analysis. Using character prototypes derived from clustering and aligning automatically extracted character images, the method defines a typeface distance between any two books.

**摘要：** 我们提出了一种统计方法，用于量化历史印刷书籍之间字体的相似度。该方法提供了一种加速文献学分析的工具。通过使用从自动提取的字符图像中聚类并对齐得出的字符原型，该方法定义了任意两本书之间的字体距离。

To produce actionable outputs, we develop an a contrario statistical framework to interpret the significance of the computed typeface distances. We apply the method to the philological study of 17th-century Spanish printed theatre chapbooks in a quantity that exceeds the capabilities of systematic visual inspection by human experts.

为了产生可操作的成果，我们开发了一个“反向”（a contrario）统计框架，以解释所计算出的字体距离的显著性。我们将该方法应用于 17 世纪西班牙印刷戏剧小册子的文献学研究，其处理数量远超人类专家进行系统视觉检查的能力范围。

Our method enables the automatic comparison of Roman and Italic types extracted from different books. After validation by human experts, our method has led to new printer attributions being discovered, and former printer attributions being revised. This success strongly suggests that our method has the potential to enable digital bibliography on a larger scale than was previously possible.

我们的方法能够自动比较从不同书籍中提取的罗马体和斜体。经过人类专家的验证，该方法已成功发现了一些新的印刷者归属，并修正了以往的错误归属。这一成功有力地表明，我们的方法有潜力实现比以往更大规模的数字文献学研究。
