# Govee’s portable smart lamp is down to one of its best prices to date
# Govee 便携式智能台灯价格降至历史新低

It offers customizable color-changing lighting, Matter support, and up to 30 hours of battery life.
它提供可定制的变色照明、Matter 协议支持，以及长达 30 小时的电池续航。

Buying multiple lamps for different rooms can get expensive. Govee’s rechargeable Table Lamp Classic gives you one lamp you can use throughout your home instead of getting one for every room, and right now it’s down to $59 ($21 off) at Amazon. That’s just $4 shy of the all-time low set during Prime Day, and the smart lamp’s second-best price to date.
为不同房间购买多盏台灯可能会是一笔不小的开销。Govee 的可充电经典台灯（Table Lamp Classic）让你只需一盏灯就能在家中各处使用，无需为每个房间单独配置。目前，该产品在亚马逊上的售价已降至 59 美元（优惠 21 美元）。这仅比 Prime Day 期间创下的历史最低价高出 4 美元，是该智能台灯迄今为止的第二低价。

Because it’s cordless, the lamp is surprisingly versatile. You can use it to brighten your desk during the day, keep it by your bedside at night, and even carry it to the patio for dinner (just don’t forget to bring it back inside if it’s expected to rain, as the lamp isn’t weather-resistant). A built-in 4,800mAh battery powers the lamp for up to 30 hours with colored lighting enabled, or about five hours when using bright white light, so you don’t need to worry about staying near an outlet.
由于采用无线设计，这款台灯的用途非常广泛。你可以在白天用它照亮书桌，晚上放在床头，甚至可以带到露台享用晚餐（不过如果预计有雨，记得把它拿回室内，因为它不具备防水功能）。内置的 4,800mAh 电池在开启彩色照明模式下可续航长达 30 小时，而在使用明亮的白光时续航约为 5 小时，因此你无需担心必须靠近电源插座。

Despite being small and light enough to carry from room to room, the Table Lamp Classic offers many of the same smart lighting features found on Govee’s bigger lamps. It lets you tweak the color temperature, cycle through 72 preset scenes, and sync the lighting to your music, making it easy to match the mood whether you’re studying, unwinding, or enjoying a date night. If you have something more specific in mind, Govee’s app can even generate a custom effect from a short text prompt. Since it supports Matter, you won’t have to reach for the Govee app every time you move the lamp to a different room, either. Instead, you can control it with your voice through your favorite smart home platform.
尽管这款经典台灯小巧轻便，便于在不同房间之间移动，但它依然具备 Govee 大型灯具的许多智能照明功能。你可以调节色温、切换 72 种预设场景，并将灯光与音乐同步，无论是学习、放松还是享受约会之夜，都能轻松营造出合适的氛围。如果你有更具体的需求，Govee 的应用程序甚至可以通过简短的文字提示生成自定义灯光效果。此外，由于它支持 Matter 协议，你无需在每次移动台灯时都打开 Govee App，而是可以通过你喜爱的智能家居平台进行语音控制。
