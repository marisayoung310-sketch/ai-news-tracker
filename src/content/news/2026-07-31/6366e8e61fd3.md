# Shared SFT Lessons Across Alignment, Model Organisms, and Toy Models
# 对齐、模型生物与玩具模型之间共享的 SFT 经验

**Abstract:** Alignment training, model organisms, and toy models are usually treated as separate research areas. But projects in all three frequently use supervised fine-tuning (SFT) to pursue the same underlying goals. When projects share a goal, we should test whether lessons learned from one area transfer to the other areas.

**摘要：** 对齐训练、模型生物（model organisms）和玩具模型（toy models）通常被视为独立的研究领域。然而，这三个领域的研究项目经常使用监督微调（SFT）来追求相同的底层目标。当项目拥有共同目标时，我们应当测试从一个领域学到的经验是否可以迁移到其他领域。

We study three such transfers, each taking a lesson developed in one SFT setting and testing it in another. First, we port a lesson about behavior generalization from alignment training into toy models. Training on the reason for a behavior, as in Teaching Claude Why, can make the behavior generalize better than training on examples of the behavior alone.

我们研究了三种此类迁移，每一种都将一个 SFT 环境中总结出的经验应用到另一个环境中进行测试。首先，我们将关于行为泛化的经验从对齐训练迁移到玩具模型中。正如“教导 Claude 知其所以然”（Teaching Claude Why）所展示的那样，针对行为背后的原因进行训练，比仅针对行为示例进行训练能使行为具有更好的泛化能力。

Second, we port a lesson about capability preservation from model organisms into the Model-Spec Midtraining alignment setting. SFT on outputs written by a model other than the student (off-model outputs) can damage capabilities when trained on. Mixing in benign on-model (and on-policy) data into our training can prevent most of this damage while still embedding the target behavior.

其次，我们将关于能力保持的经验从模型生物迁移到 Model-Spec 中期训练（Midtraining）对齐环境中。使用非学生模型生成的输出（off-model outputs）进行 SFT 可能会在训练过程中损害模型能力。在训练中混合良性的、由模型自身生成的（且符合策略的）数据，可以在植入目标行为的同时，避免大部分此类损害。

Third, we port a lesson about robustness from model organisms into the same alignment setting. We find that follow-up benign SFT can erase the alignment behavior while preserving capabilities, showing that capability preservation alone does not ensure robustness to subsequent training.

第三，我们将关于鲁棒性的经验从模型生物迁移到相同的对齐环境中。我们发现，后续的良性 SFT 可能会在保持模型能力的同时抹除对齐行为，这表明仅靠能力保持并不能确保模型对后续训练的鲁棒性。

Our work illustrates how porting SFT lessons between different research fields can uplift them all, suggesting more researchers should borrow techniques from outside their own areas.

我们的工作展示了在不同研究领域之间迁移 SFT 经验如何能促进所有领域的发展，并建议更多的研究人员应当借鉴自身领域之外的技术。
