# Nvidia’s Open Source Alliance Is Missing Some Key Names: OpenAI and Anthropic
# 英伟达开源联盟缺席关键成员：OpenAI 与 Anthropic

This week on Uncanny Valley, the team discusses the launch of Nvidia’s splashy alliance to push for open-source AI security alongside companies like Microsoft, SpaceX, and Palantir. Notably absent? OpenAI and Anthropic. Brian Barrett, Zoë Schiffer, and Leah Feiger break down what the split means for the industry, and the Trump administration’s own shifting opinions on AI. Plus, we discuss how Claude conversations turned up in Google and Bing search results, and the massive feat of how four whales were rescued and transported from Canada to Chicago.

在本周的《Uncanny Valley》节目中，团队讨论了英伟达（Nvidia）联合微软、SpaceX 和 Palantir 等公司推出的重磅开源 AI 安全联盟。值得注意的是，OpenAI 和 Anthropic 缺席了该联盟。Brian Barrett、Zoë Schiffer 和 Leah Feiger 深入剖析了这一分歧对行业意味着什么，以及特朗普政府在 AI 问题上不断变化的立场。此外，我们还讨论了 Claude 对话内容为何会出现在谷歌和必应的搜索结果中，以及四头白鲸从加拿大被营救并运往芝加哥的壮举。
