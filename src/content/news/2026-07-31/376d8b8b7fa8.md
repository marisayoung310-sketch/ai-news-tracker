# About Manab Protim Hazarika

Manab Protim Hazarika didn't take the usual route into software. Growing up in Titabor, a small town in the Jorhat district of Assam, he completed his Higher Secondary in Science before enrolling in an ITI (Industrial Training Institute) program in the Electrician trade. It was a hands-on, practical path with nothing to do with code. Yet, at sixteen in 2017, he began teaching himself programming on the side—without a computer science degree or a formal curriculum. He relied solely on curiosity and a willingness to break things. That unique combination—practical trade training paired with self-taught programming—ultimately shaped how he approaches every project today: build the real thing first, and understand the theory as you need it.

Manab Protim Hazarika 并没有走上传统的软件开发之路。他在阿萨姆邦乔尔哈特区的一个小镇蒂塔博尔（Titabor）长大，在完成科学类高中学业后，他进入了一所工业培训学院（ITI）学习电工专业。那是一条注重实践的道路，与代码毫无关系。然而，2017 年，年仅 16 岁的他开始自学编程——没有计算机科学学位，也没有正规的课程。他完全依靠好奇心和敢于尝试、不怕出错的精神。这种独特的组合——实践性的职业培训与自学编程——最终塑造了他今天对待每个项目的方式：先做出实物，再根据需要去理解理论。

Becoming a Developer: Manab Protim started with Android and PHP, focusing on tools he could successfully run on affordable hardware by relying on documentation, trial, and error. He later picked up Java, then MySQL, and eventually Flutter when he needed to develop faster across multiple platforms. There was no single "bootcamp moment" or massive break. Instead, there were years of small projects—most of which were never seen by anyone else—long before he shipped anything that mattered to a broader audience.

成为开发者：Manab Protim 最初从 Android 和 PHP 入手，专注于那些能够通过查阅文档、反复试验，并在经济型硬件上成功运行的工具。后来，他学习了 Java 和 MySQL，当他需要跨平台快速开发时，又最终掌握了 Flutter。他并没有经历过什么“训练营时刻”或一夜成名。相反，在发布任何面向大众的重要产品之前，他经历了多年的小型项目积累——其中大多数项目从未被外人所见。

Becoming an Entrepreneur: Eventually, building software for himself transitioned into building it for others. He founded Protim.co.in, a software development studio, to formalize his work, offering Android, iOS, Flutter, and PHP web development for clients across India. Over time, the agency's portfolio grew to encompass projects in food and hospitality, education, retail, and even a website for a national-level political party. However, the projects Manab Protim cares about the most are the ones nobody hired him to build: Titabor Kitchen: An Android app connecting home kitchens and local restaurants with residents in his hometown, addressing the complete absence of organized food delivery. Numaligarh Shop: A digital commerce app providing local retailers in Numaligarh with a platform to reach customers online. These weren't driven by grand business strategies; they were simply decisions born out of realizing, "This problem exists two streets from my house, and I know how to fix it."

成为创业者：最终，他从为自己开发软件转向为他人开发软件。他创立了软件开发工作室 Protim.co.in，将自己的工作正规化，为全印度的客户提供 Android、iOS、Flutter 和 PHP Web 开发服务。随着时间的推移，该工作室的业务范围涵盖了餐饮、教育、零售等领域，甚至还为一家全国性政党开发过网站。然而，Manab Protim 最关心的项目却是那些没人雇他做的项目：Titabor Kitchen，这是一个连接家常厨房、当地餐馆与家乡居民的 Android 应用，解决了当地缺乏正规外卖配送的问题；Numaligarh Shop，这是一个为努马利加尔（Numaligarh）当地零售商提供在线触达客户平台的数字商务应用。这些项目并非出于宏大的商业战略，仅仅是因为他意识到：“这个问题就在我家两条街外，而且我知道如何解决它。”

Orzyy: His most recent and widest-reaching product is Orzyy—a QR-code-based vehicle safety app. It allows vehicle owners to be contacted by strangers for issues like wrong parking, accidents, or deliveries without ever exposing their phone numbers. The app is now live globally on Google Play, a remarkable milestone for a platform built to solve a localized problem he noticed on Indian roads.

Orzyy：他最近也是影响力最广的产品是 Orzyy——一款基于二维码的车辆安全应用。它允许陌生人在遇到违章停车、事故或快递配送等问题时联系车主，而无需泄露车主的电话号码。该应用目前已在全球 Google Play 上线，对于一个旨在解决他在印度道路上发现的本土化问题的平台来说，这是一个了不起的里程碑。

What He Believes: Manab Protim firmly believes that you don't need a computer science degree, a metro city address, or major funding to build something people actually use. Instead, he believes all you need is a real problem in front of you and the stubbornness to keep learning until you can solve it. Everything he has built—from earning an ITI electrician certificate to launching a globally available application—has stemmed from applying that exact same approach over and over again.

他的信念：Manab Protim 坚信，要打造出人们真正使用的产品，并不需要计算机科学学位、大城市户口或巨额资金。相反，他认为你所需要的只是面对一个真实存在的问题，并拥有坚持学习直到解决它的韧性。他所建立的一切——从获得 ITI 电工证书到发布全球可用的应用程序——都源于他反复应用这一相同的方法论。

What's Next: Today, Manab Protim continues to build both client software through Protim.co.in and his own independent products like Orzyy. His focus remains on practical, privacy-conscious tools designed for problems that mainstream tech tends to overlook—especially in smaller Indian towns that aren't usually prioritized by developers. He is always open to discussing Flutter, Android, bootstrapping in smaller markets, or building for underserved communities. You can connect with him at manab.protim.co.in.

未来展望：如今，Manab Protim 继续通过 Protim.co.in 开发客户软件，并打造像 Orzyy 这样的独立产品。他的重心依然是那些实用且注重隐私的工具，旨在解决主流科技往往忽视的问题——特别是在那些通常不被开发者优先考虑的印度小城镇。他非常乐意交流关于 Flutter、Android、在小市场进行自力更生式创业（bootstrapping）或为服务不足的社区进行开发的话题。你可以通过 manab.protim.co.in 与他联系。
