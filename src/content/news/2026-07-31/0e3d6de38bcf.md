# Samsung’s Galaxy Watch 9 is $40 off at Costco and comes with over $50 in freebies
# 三星 Galaxy Watch 9 在 Costco 现已开启预购，立减 40 美元并赠送价值超 50 美元的礼品

Costco members can grab the best deal we’ve seen yet for preordering Samsung’s upcoming watch.
Costco 会员现在可以享受到我们目前所见过的三星新款手表预购最优惠方案。

The Galaxy Watch 9 launches on August 7th, and not only does Costco have the best preorder incentives we’ve seen so far, the watch is also discounted. The deal includes the 40mm Watch 9 in cream or graphite, two wireless fast chargers for the watch, and a $50 Costco gift card, all for $339.99 — $40 less than the list price for the watch on its own directly from Samsung.
Galaxy Watch 9 将于 8 月 7 日正式发布。Costco 不仅提供了目前为止我们见过的最佳预购激励措施，而且手表本身还享有折扣。该优惠套餐包含一款 40 毫米的 Galaxy Watch 9（奶油色或石墨色）、两个手表无线快充充电器以及一张 50 美元的 Costco 礼品卡，总价仅为 339.99 美元——这比直接从三星官网购买单表的价格便宜了 40 美元。

The discounted price erases the $30 gen-to-gen price bump, and the extra chargers and gift card further sweeten the deal. If you aren’t a Costco member, a year of membership is only $65, which is less than the value of the free items in this purchase.
这一折扣价格抵消了该代产品 30 美元的涨价幅度，而额外赠送的充电器和礼品卡则让这笔交易更加划算。如果你还不是 Costco 会员，一年的会员费仅需 65 美元，这甚至低于此次购买中所赠送礼品的总价值。

There are some changes to the new watches from the Watch 8. The brand says the new chip, a Qualcomm Snapdragon Wear Elite, is built specifically for AI performance, and there are new health tracking features, plus better battery life, to boot. We got to spend some time with the Watch 9 and Watch 2 Ultra at Samsung’s July 2026 Unpacked event, so make sure to read our initial impressions of both watches if you’re curious about how the upgrades feel ahead of their early August launch.
新款手表相较于 Watch 8 有了一些变化。三星表示，新搭载的芯片——高通骁龙 Wear Elite——是专为 AI 性能打造的。此外，新手表还增加了健康追踪功能，并提升了电池续航能力。我们在 2026 年 7 月的三星 Unpacked 发布会上已经上手体验了 Watch 9 和 Watch 2 Ultra，如果你对这些升级在 8 月初发布前的实际感受感到好奇，请务必阅读我们对这两款手表的初步印象。
