# How to Decode the Temperature Parameter in LLMs
# 如何解读大语言模型中的 Temperature（温度）参数

Large Language Models: How statistical physics explains the transition from deterministic predictions to generative AI.
大语言模型：统计物理学如何解释从确定性预测到生成式 AI 的转变。

Bridging the gap: connecting classical statistical mechanics to the probabilistic nature of modern AI.
弥合鸿沟：连接经典统计力学与现代 AI 的概率本质。

### The Physics: Energy, Probability, and Temperature
### 物理学基础：能量、概率与温度

Imagine a physical system that can exist in several different states. Each state has an associated energy. Some states are cheap, requiring very little energy, while others are expensive. One fundamental question statistical physics asks is deceptively simple: Given all these possible states, how likely is the system to occupy each one?
想象一个可以存在于多种不同状态的物理系统。每个状态都有一个关联的能量值。有些状态是“廉价”的，只需要很少的能量；而另一些则是“昂贵”的。统计物理学提出了一个看似简单却意义深远的问题：在所有这些可能的状态中，系统占据每一个状态的概率是多少？

At first, it might seem tempting to use the energies themselves as probabilities. But energies aren’t probabilities. A state can have energy 5, or -3, or 127, none of which says anything about how likely it is. Probabilities obey rules that energies do not: they can never be negative, and together they must add up to exactly one.
起初，人们可能会倾向于直接将能量值当作概率。但能量并非概率。一个状态的能量可以是 5、-3 或 127，这些数值本身并不能说明其发生的可能性。概率遵循能量所不具备的规则：它们永远不能为负，且所有状态的概率之和必须精确等于 1。

The solution is to stop thinking about probabilities altogether. Instead, each state’s energy is first converted into a weight: $weight = e^{-E_{i}/kT}$. The exponential doesn’t produce probabilities. It produces relative importance. States with low energy receive large weights, while high-energy states receive exponentially smaller ones. At this stage, nothing has been normalized; the numbers simply express how strongly each state competes with the others.
解决方案是暂时抛开概率的概念。取而代之的是，将每个状态的能量转换为权重：$weight = e^{-E_{i}/kT}$。指数函数产生的不是概率，而是相对重要性。低能量状态获得较大的权重，而高能量状态获得的权重则呈指数级减小。在此阶段，数值尚未归一化；它们仅仅表达了各个状态之间竞争的激烈程度。

Turning those weights into probabilities is almost trivial. Divide each state’s weight by the sum of all weights: $P(state) = \frac{e^{-E_{i}/kT}}{\sum_{j}{e^{-E_{j}/kT}}}$. That’s the entire Boltzmann distribution. Despite its intimidating appearance, the equation performs only two operations. The exponential translates energy into relative preference. The denominator rescales those preferences so they form a valid probability distribution. What initially looks like a complicated formula is really just a weighting step followed by a normalization step.
将这些权重转化为概率非常简单：用每个状态的权重除以所有权重的总和：$P(state) = \frac{e^{-E_{i}/kT}}{\sum_{j}{e^{-E_{j}/kT}}}$。这就是玻尔兹曼分布（Boltzmann distribution）的全部内容。尽管它看起来很复杂，但该方程只执行了两个操作：指数函数将能量转化为相对偏好，分母则对这些偏好进行缩放，使其构成一个有效的概率分布。起初看起来复杂的公式，实际上只是一个加权步骤加上一个归一化步骤。

After this picture is clear for the reader, let’s turn in the temperature variable. Notice that temperature never changes the energies themselves. Instead, it changes how much those energy differences matter. Consider three possible states with energies 0, 1, and 2. At a very low temperature, say $T=0.1$, even these modest energy differences become enormous inside the exponential. The lowest-energy state captures almost all the probability, while the other two become practically impossible. Increase the temperature to $T=10$, and the opposite happens. The same energy differences are now heavily compressed, producing nearly identical weights and a distribution that’s close to uniform.
在读者理解了这一图景后，我们引入温度变量。请注意，温度永远不会改变能量本身，它改变的是这些能量差异的重要性。考虑三个能量分别为 0、1 和 2 的状态。在极低温度下（例如 $T=0.1$），即使是这些微小的能量差异在指数函数中也会变得巨大。最低能量状态几乎占据了所有概率，而其他两个状态几乎变得不可能发生。将温度提高到 $T=10$，情况则恰恰相反。同样的能量差异现在被大幅压缩，产生几乎相同的权重，分布也趋于均匀。

Nothing about the states changed. Their energies remained 0, 1, and 2 throughout. Only the importance of those differences changed. This is the role of temperature in the Boltzmann distribution. It doesn’t change the energies or introduce any new terms into the equation. It simply controls how aggressively the probability distribution favors low-energy states. Low temperatures amplify small energy differences into near certainty. High temperatures flatten those differences until every state becomes almost equally plausible.
状态本身没有任何改变，它们的能量始终是 0、1 和 2。改变的仅仅是这些差异的重要性。这就是温度在玻尔兹曼分布中的作用。它不会改变能量，也不会在方程中引入新项。它只是控制概率分布对低能状态的偏好程度。低温将微小的能量差异放大为近乎确定性；高温则抹平了这些差异，直到每个状态变得几乎同样可能。

### The LLM Equivalency: Vocabulary as States
### 大语言模型的等价性：词汇即状态

To see how this connects to Large Language Models, we only need to change the labels on our variables. In physics, our system might be a gas, and the “states” are the different energy levels its molecules can occupy. In an LLM, the system is the exact moment the model needs to predict the next word. The “states” are the entire vocabulary of the model—meaning tens of thousands of possible words (or tokens) competing to be chosen next.
要理解这与大语言模型的联系，我们只需更改变量的标签。在物理学中，系统可能是气体，“状态”是分子可以占据的不同能级。在 LLM 中，系统是模型需要预测下一个词的那一刻。“状态”是模型的整个词汇表——意味着成千上万个可能的词（或 Token）正在竞争被选中。

When an LLM processes your prompt, it doesn’t immediately output probabilities. Its final neural layer spits out raw, unnormalized numbers for every single word in its vocabulary. In Machine Learning, we call these raw scores logits ($z$). One word might get a logit of 12.5, another -3.2, and another 0.8. Here is the crucial conceptual bridge: Logits are the model’s energy, but inverted. Nature is inherently lazy and it tends to favor lower-energy states. Neural networks, on the other hand, are greedy; they generally prefer the predictions with the highest possible score ($+z$).
当 LLM 处理你的提示词时，它不会立即输出概率。其最终的神经网络层会为词汇表中的每一个词输出原始的、未归一化的数值。在机器学习中，我们将这些原始分数称为 Logits ($z$)。一个词的 Logit 可能是 12.5，另一个是 -3.2，还有一个是 0.8。这是关键的概念桥梁：Logits 就是模型的“能量”，但方向相反。自然界本质上是“懒惰”的，倾向于低能状态；而神经网络则是“贪婪”的，它们通常偏好分数最高（$+z$）的预测。

### Softmax is Boltzmann in Disguise
### Softmax 是伪装下的玻尔兹曼分布

To turn those raw, unbounded logits into a clean probability distribution that sums to one, ML engineers use the Softmax function. If we explicitly include the temperature parameter ($T$), the Softmax equation looks like this: $P(word_i) = \frac{e^{z_{i}/T}}{\sum_{j}{e^{z_{j}/T}}}$. Look familiar? It is the exact same mathematical trick. We replace the negative energy ($-E$) with our positive logits ($+z$). We exponentiate to turn scores into relative weights, and we divide by the sum of all weights to normalize them. Softmax is just the Boltzmann distribution wearing a computer science trench coat.
为了将这些原始的、无界的 Logits 转化为总和为 1 的清晰概率分布，机器学习工程师使用了 Softmax 函数。如果我们显式地包含温度参数 ($T$)，Softmax 方程看起来是这样的：$P(word_i) = \frac{e^{z_{i}/T}}{\sum_{j}{e^{z_{j}/T}}}$。看起来眼熟吗？这正是同样的数学技巧。我们将负能量 ($-E$) 替换为正的 Logits ($+z$)。我们通过指数运算将分数转化为相对权重，并除以权重总和进行归一化。Softmax 不过是披着计算机科学外衣的玻尔兹曼分布。
