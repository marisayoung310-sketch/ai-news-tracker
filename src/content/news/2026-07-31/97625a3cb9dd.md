# Synthetic-user startup Simile raises $200M at $2B valuation 5 months after $100M Series A
# 合成用户初创公司 Simile 在完成 1 亿美元 A 轮融资 5 个月后，以 20 亿美元估值完成 2 亿美元融资

Synthetic-user startup Simile raises $200M at $2B valuation 5 months after $100M Series A. Add another member to the fast-and-furious AI unicorn club: Simile. Just five months after emerging from stealth and announcing a $100 million Series A led by Index Ventures, it has closed a $200 million Series B at a $2 billion valuation, the startup says.
合成用户初创公司 Simile 在完成 1 亿美元 A 轮融资 5 个月后，以 20 亿美元估值完成 2 亿美元融资。AI 独角兽俱乐部又迎来了一位“极速”新成员：Simile。该公司表示，在走出隐身模式并宣布由 Index Ventures 领投的 1 亿美元 A 轮融资仅五个月后，他们又完成了 2 亿美元的 B 轮融资，估值达到 20 亿美元。

The B round was led by Greenoaks with participation from Index, Hanabi, Bain Capital Ventures, A*, Factory, Definition, and CVS Health Ventures. CVS is also one of Simile’s marquee customers.
本轮融资由 Greenoaks 领投，Index、Hanabi、Bain Capital Ventures、A*、Factory、Definition 和 CVS Health Ventures 跟投。CVS 同时也是 Simile 的重要客户之一。

The startup offers simulated users for areas like marketing and product research. It was founded by Stanford PhD graduate Joon Sung Park, whose dissertation involved a project called “Smallville” in which AI agents carried on simulated human lives, right down to holding parties.
该初创公司为市场营销和产品研究等领域提供模拟用户服务。公司由斯坦福大学博士毕业生 Joon Sung Park 创立，他的博士论文涉及一个名为“Smallville”的项目，在该项目中，AI 智能体能够模拟人类生活，甚至包括举办派对等细节。

The startup’s stated mission of simulating “all eight billion people on earth, accurately and honestly” is preposterous — the whole reason to conduct market research is because humans are unpredictable, guided, as we are, by both emotions and reason. However, simulating users for research is a promising area. It’s akin to vibe coding for product mock-ups.
该公司宣称的使命是“准确且真实地模拟地球上所有 80 亿人”，这听起来有些荒谬——进行市场研究的根本原因在于人类是不可预测的，我们的行为既受情感驱动，也受理性引导。然而，将模拟用户用于研究是一个很有前景的领域，这类似于产品原型的“氛围编程”（vibe coding）。

Other startups in this vein have also caught VC attention, such as Aaru, which raised a Series A in December, also at a hefty “headline” $1 billion valuation.
其他同类初创公司也吸引了风投的关注，例如 Aaru，该公司在 12 月完成了 A 轮融资，估值同样达到了惊人的 10 亿美元。
