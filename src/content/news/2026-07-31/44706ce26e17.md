### GCC 指导委员会宣布人工智能政策

**The GCC steering committee has announced that it has accepted an AI contributions policy recommended by the GCC AI policy working group.**
GCC 指导委员会宣布，已采纳由 GCC 人工智能政策工作组推荐的人工智能贡献政策。

**The policy, in part, states that the project will decline any "legally significant contributions which include LLM-generated content or are derived from LLM-generated content".**
该政策部分规定，项目将拒绝任何“包含大语言模型（LLM）生成内容或源自大语言模型生成内容的法律意义上的贡献”。

**It uses the definition of "legally significant" from the GNU Project maintainer guidelines, which holds that the threshold is "around 15 lines of code and/or text" to qualify as significant for copyright purposes.**
该政策采用了 GNU 项目维护者指南中对“法律意义（legally significant）”的定义，即在版权法意义上，达到“约 15 行代码和/或文本”的门槛即被视为具有法律意义。

**GCC maintainers may, however, choose to accept legally significant test cases that are generated by an LLM.**
不过，GCC 维护者可以选择接受由大语言模型生成的、具有法律意义的测试用例。

**The policy does not forbid use of LLMs for research, analysis, bug discovery and reporting, patch review, etc. as long as the output is not included in contributions.**
只要输出内容不被包含在贡献代码中，该政策并不禁止使用大语言模型进行研究、分析、漏洞发现与报告、补丁审查等工作。

**The committee says that it expects the policy will evolve and will be revisited periodically.**
委员会表示，预计该政策将会不断演进，并会定期进行重新审议。
