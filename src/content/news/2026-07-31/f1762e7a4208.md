# Dynamic Parameterization Is Not Dynamic Inference
# 动态参数化不等同于动态推理

Input-dependent controller coefficients are often treated as evidence of dynamic inference or computational savings. This interpretation conflates three properties: coefficient variation, dependence of a frozen model on how coefficients are assigned to inputs, and conditional execution.
输入依赖的控制器系数常被视为动态推理或计算节省的证据。这种解读混淆了三个属性：系数变化、冻结模型对系数如何分配给输入的依赖性，以及条件执行。

We focus on the second property and formulate a general principle of frozen-controller auditing. We provide one concrete implementation, Frozen-Controller Auditing (FCA), which caches the complete coefficient tensor along an unperturbed trajectory, disables the controller, and replays the frozen model with cross-input reassignment, token shuffling, and static profiles estimated from an independent calibration set.
我们聚焦于第二个属性，并制定了冻结控制器审计（Frozen-Controller Auditing, FCA）的一般原则。我们提供了一种具体的实现方式——FCA，它在未受干扰的轨迹上缓存完整的系数张量，禁用控制器，并通过跨输入重分配、Token 打乱以及从独立校准集估计的静态配置来重放冻结模型。

Because the coefficients are cached before any intervention, performance changes under replay measure assignment dependence without feedback from recomputing the controller on perturbed hidden states.
由于系数是在任何干预之前缓存的，重放过程中的性能变化衡量的是分配依赖性，而不会受到在受扰动隐藏状态下重新计算控制器所带来的反馈影响。

Across seven independently trained 76M FeatureGate Transformers and three 504M models, static layerwise profiles retain 98.70% and 99.43% of the Correct-to-GlobalMean performance gap, respectively. Layer identity explains 87% to 96% of the coefficient variance. FeatureGate nevertheless executes every Transformer block, and its measured inference is 30.8% slower than Dense.
在七个独立训练的 76M FeatureGate Transformer 和三个 504M 模型中，静态层级配置分别保留了 98.70% 和 99.43% 的“正确值至全局均值”性能差距。层标识解释了 87% 到 96% 的系数方差。尽管如此，FeatureGate 仍会执行每一个 Transformer 块，其测得的推理速度比 Dense 模型慢 30.8%。

On the public MUDDPythia-1.4B checkpoint, cross-input reassignment and token shuffling increase NLL by 1.9067 and 2.9637, respectively. These penalties show that the model depends strongly on content-conditioned cross-layer assignment. MUDDPythia also executes every Transformer block.
在公开的 MUDDPythia-1.4B 检查点上，跨输入重分配和 Token 打乱分别使 NLL（负对数似然）增加了 1.9067 和 2.9637。这些惩罚表明模型高度依赖于内容条件下的跨层分配。MUDDPythia 同样会执行每一个 Transformer 块。

The results show that dynamic parameterization alone does not establish dynamic inference and that functional dynamics do not establish computational savings. Claims about dynamic models should separately report coefficient variation, functional dependence of the frozen model, and actual execution.
研究结果表明，仅凭动态参数化并不能确立动态推理，功能动态性也不等同于计算节省。关于动态模型的声明应分别报告系数变化、冻结模型的功能依赖性以及实际执行情况。
