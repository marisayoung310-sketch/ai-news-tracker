# Judge says Trump admin still lacks evidence for Anthropic ‘supply-chain risk’ label
# 法官称特朗普政府仍缺乏证据将 Anthropic 列为“供应链风险”

During a Thursday hearing, a judge said the Trump administration hasn’t presented enough evidence to justify labeling Anthropic a supply-chain risk and banning the federal government from using the company’s technology. Bloomberg and Axios were among the first to report the news.
在周四的听证会上，一位法官表示，特朗普政府尚未提供足够的证据来证明将 Anthropic 列为“供应链风险”并禁止联邦政府使用该公司技术的合理性。彭博社和 Axios 最早报道了这一消息。

The dispute stems from stalled contract negotiations between Anthropic and the Department of Defense. Anthropic said it didn’t want its AI used for mass surveillance of Americans or for targeting or firing decisions involving lethal weapons, arguing the technology wasn’t ready. The Pentagon countered that a private company shouldn’t dictate how the military uses technologies, and said it would use the tools in “lawful” ways.
这场纠纷源于 Anthropic 与国防部之间陷入僵局的合同谈判。Anthropic 表示，不希望其人工智能被用于对美国民众进行大规模监控，也不希望用于涉及致命武器的瞄准或开火决策，并认为相关技术尚未成熟。五角大楼则反驳称，私营公司不应干涉军方如何使用技术，并表示将以“合法”方式使用这些工具。

The government has also argued that Anthropic’s public criticism of the DOD justifies the ban — logic that U.S. District Judge Rita Lin called “really troubling,” warning it could set a precedent of retaliating against federal contractors who disagree with the administration.
政府还辩称，Anthropic 对国防部的公开批评证明了禁令的合理性。美国地方法院法官丽塔·林（Rita Lin）称这种逻辑“非常令人不安”，并警告称这可能会开创一个先例，即对与政府意见不合的联邦承包商进行报复。

The DOD further claimed Anthropic could potentially disable or alter its AI models during warfighting operations — a claim that experts say lacks evidence. Lin agreed, saying she saw no proof Anthropic could alter a delivered model or “flip some kind of kill switch.”
国防部进一步声称，Anthropic 可能会在作战行动中禁用或篡改其人工智能模型——专家指出这一说法缺乏证据。林法官表示认同，称她没有看到任何证据表明 Anthropic 能够篡改已交付的模型或“触发某种终止开关”。

Thursday’s hearing was part of one of two lawsuits Anthropic filed against the DOD in March, challenging the ban and risk designation. The other is being heard in Washington. Lin, who temporarily blocked the ban in March, is now weighing whether to make that order permanent.
周四的听证会是 Anthropic 于 3 月份针对国防部提起的两起诉讼之一，旨在挑战该禁令和风险认定。另一起诉讼正在华盛顿审理。林法官曾在 3 月份暂时叫停了该禁令，目前正在权衡是否将该命令永久化。
