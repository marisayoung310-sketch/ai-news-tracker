# Weak-to-Strong On-Policy Distillation

**Abstract:** On-policy distillation (OPD), which aligns a student with the teacher's token-level distribution on the student's own rollouts, is an effective paradigm for transferring capabilities across LLMs. Prevailing approaches assume a teacher at least as capable as the student: they either distill a larger model into a smaller one, which fails at the frontier where no larger teacher exists, or consolidate multiple domain experts trained from a shared base, which requires costly training at the student's scale.

**摘要：** 在策略蒸馏（On-policy distillation, OPD）通过在学生模型自身的输出序列上使其与教师模型的词元级分布对齐，是跨大语言模型（LLM）迁移能力的有效范式。目前主流的方法通常假设教师模型至少与学生模型能力相当：它们要么将更大的模型蒸馏为较小的模型（这在不存在更大教师模型的前沿领域会失效），要么整合从共享基座训练出的多个领域专家（这需要在学生模型的规模上进行昂贵的训练）。

We introduce Weak-to-Strong On-Policy Distillation (W2S-OPD), a simple yet effective OPD framework that improves the strong student by distilling from multiple weak models. W2S-OPD constructs a proxy teacher in logit space from a contrast pair of a positive and a negative model, both smaller than the student and cheap to obtain. Their logit difference isolates the capability direction, which is added to the student's own base model, yielding a proxy teacher that couples this direction while staying distributionally adjacent to the student. The student then distills it by minimizing the per-token reverse KL on its own rollouts.

我们引入了“弱到强在策略蒸馏”（Weak-to-Strong On-Policy Distillation, W2S-OPD），这是一个简单且有效的 OPD 框架，通过从多个弱模型中进行蒸馏来提升强大的学生模型。W2S-OPD 在 Logit 空间中构建了一个代理教师，该代理教师由一对正向和负向模型对比得出，这两个模型都比学生模型小且易于获取。它们的 Logit 差值分离出了能力方向，并将其添加到学生模型自身的基座中，从而产生了一个既结合了该方向又在分布上与学生模型保持邻近的代理教师。随后，学生模型通过在自身输出序列上最小化逐词元的反向 KL 散度来进行蒸馏。

We instantiate the contrast pair as i) a post-RL expert against its pre-RL initialization, isolating the skill RL instills, ii) a larger against a smaller base model, isolating the capability from scale, and iii) a small base model with correct versus wrong hints, isolating the instance-level direction toward the solution. Across four math and three code benchmarks, W2S-OPD outperforms OPD, enables the student to surpass the domain teacher, and keeps improving the student even when every supervision source is weaker. Analysis shows different contrasts yield distinct signals: the post-RL and hint contrasts emphasize reasoning frameworks, while the scale contrast emphasizes the solving procedure.
