# Quasi-Monte Carlo Initialization for Meta-Reinforcement Learning
# 用于元强化学习的拟蒙特卡洛初始化

**Abstract:** This paper explores the efficacy of quasi-Monte Carlo (QMC) weight initialization for meta-reinforcement learning within modern benchmark environments. Various sampling methods are used to bound a population-based search and aggregate an optimal prior from a baseline set of tasks.

**摘要：** 本文探讨了拟蒙特卡洛（QMC）权重初始化在现代基准测试环境中应用于元强化学习的有效性。研究采用了多种采样方法来限定基于种群的搜索，并从一组基准任务中聚合出最优先验。

The QMC meta-priors show improvements in training convergence compared to modern orthogonal (SB3) defaults when extrapolated to similar unseen continuous control environments. In dissimilar tasks, the orthogonal orientation was globally superior for an unbiased search.

当外推到类似的未见连续控制环境时，QMC 元先验在训练收敛性方面表现出比现代正交（SB3）默认设置更好的改进。而在差异较大的任务中，正交初始化在无偏搜索方面表现出全局优势。
