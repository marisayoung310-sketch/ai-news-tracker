# What Happens to Accuracy When Photo Lineups Contain Non-Mated Rank-One Images From Large Galleries?
# 当照片队列包含来自大型图库的非匹配排名第一图像时，准确率会发生什么变化？

One-to-many facial identification is commonly used to match a probe image from surveillance video against a gallery of driver's licenses and/or booking photos. The algorithm's rank-one image from the gallery, or a human examiner's selection from the algorithm's top-ranked images, may then be placed in a photo lineup shown to a witness. Witness selection of the gallery image in the photo lineup may then lead directly to the person in the gallery image being arrested. This facial identification process is involved in at least 9 wrongful arrests.
一对多面部识别通常用于将监控视频中的探测图像与驾驶执照和/或存档照片库进行比对。算法从图库中选出的排名第一的图像，或人工审查员从算法排名靠前的图像中挑选出的图像，随后可能会被放入向证人展示的照片队列中。证人在照片队列中对图库图像的选择，可能直接导致图库图像中的当事人被捕。这一面部识别过程已涉及至少 9 起错误逮捕案件。

This work specifically examines whether the probability of a witness making an incorrect identification increases with the size of the gallery searched. We compare photo lineup accuracy when the "suspect" image is drawn from galleries of 500, 5,000, and 24,000 images. We find that larger galleries increase both the likelihood of a witness making an incorrect identification and their confidence in that (incorrect) identification.
本研究专门探讨了证人做出错误识别的概率是否会随着搜索图库规模的增大而增加。我们比较了当“嫌疑人”图像分别来自 500 张、5,000 张和 24,000 张图像的图库时，照片队列的准确率。我们发现，图库规模越大，证人做出错误识别的可能性及其对该（错误）识别的信心都会随之增加。

These results raise questions of whether an image resulting from such a facial identification process should be used in photo lineups and of whether results of a photo lineup alone should constitute probable cause for arrest.
这些结果引发了人们的质疑：通过此类面部识别过程得出的图像是否应该用于照片队列，以及仅凭照片队列的结果是否足以构成逮捕的合理依据。
