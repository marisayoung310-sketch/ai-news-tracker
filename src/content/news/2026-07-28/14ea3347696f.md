This discounted gaming keyboard features customizable analog switches and advanced features for competitive gamers.

Gaming keyboards have evolved over the years to add RGB LEDs, extra knobs, and buttons with screens, but one feature has remained fairly consistent: the mechanical switch. That’s slowly changing, with brands introducing adjustable optical switches that are more customizable and have a faster response time. Razer’s Huntsman V3 Pro TKL is a compact, wired option on sale at Amazon for $124.99 (usually $159.99).
多年来，游戏键盘不断进化，增加了 RGB 灯效、额外的旋钮以及带屏幕的按键，但有一个核心功能始终保持不变：机械轴。这种情况正在慢慢改变，各大品牌开始引入可调节的光轴，它们不仅可定制性更高，响应速度也更快。雷蛇的 Huntsman V3 Pro TKL 是一款紧凑型有线键盘，目前在亚马逊上的售价为 124.99 美元（原价 159.99 美元）。

Optical switches constantly read the height of each key, opening up a wealth of new features that traditional mechanical switches don’t support. For example, you can set the activation height for different keys, down to 0.1mm for the lightest touch, or up to 4mm for keys you don’t want to accidentally press at the wrong time, and they can reset without reaching the top height for faster repeated presses.
光轴能够实时读取每个按键的高度，从而解锁了传统机械轴所不支持的丰富新功能。例如，你可以为不同的按键设置触发高度，最轻触控可低至 0.1 毫米，而对于不希望误触的按键，则可设置为 4 毫米。此外，按键无需回弹至顶部即可重置，从而实现更快的连续敲击。

Like some other keyboards with optical switches, you can use a function Razer calls “Snap Tap” to prioritize specific key inputs over each other, letting you rock back and forth between your strafing keys in a shooter, for example. The feature is turned off by default, and is banned in some games like Counter-Strike 2, but can be switched on at the keyboard level or in the Razer Synapse app.
与其他配备光轴的键盘一样，你可以使用雷蛇称为“Snap Tap”的功能来优先处理特定的按键输入，例如在射击游戏中，这能让你在左右平移键之间快速切换。该功能默认处于关闭状态，且在《反恐精英 2》（Counter-Strike 2）等部分游戏中被禁止使用，但你可以通过键盘快捷键或 Razer Synapse 应用程序将其开启。
