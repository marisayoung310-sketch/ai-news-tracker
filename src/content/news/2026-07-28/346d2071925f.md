### Notate

**Launching today**
今天正式发布

**Annotate anything for humans and their agents**
为人类及其智能体（Agents）提供任何内容的标注功能

**Notate is for anything where pointing beats describing: design and motion review, product walkthroughs, code review, and handoffs.**
Notate 适用于任何“指点胜过描述”的场景：包括设计与动态效果评审、产品演示、代码审查以及工作交接。

**Unlike screenshot tools, it freezes open menus, hover and focus states before they disappear, or records a flow so you can pin the exact frame where an animation breaks.**
与普通的截图工具不同，它能够冻结在消失前的打开菜单、悬停和焦点状态，或者记录整个操作流程，让你能够精准定位动画出错的那一帧。

**Gesture-based annotation keeps the flow fast; smart crop and reopenable sessions preserve the image, comments, and context so feedback stays useful when it reaches a teammate or an agent.**
基于手势的标注功能让操作流程保持高效；智能裁剪和可重新打开的会话功能保留了图像、评论和上下文信息，确保反馈在传达给团队成员或智能体时依然具有实用价值。
