### Best Overall Handheld Vacuum: Dyson Car+Boat
### 最佳综合手持吸尘器：Dyson Car+Boat

The newest addition to Dyson's handheld lineup, this little number was designed specifically for sucking up dirt and debris from your car and boat, plus bedding, furniture, and other awkward spaces. It's small, but has a powerful suction, with a spin speed of 110,000-rpm (comparable to other bigger, more expensive Dyson models) and a crevice tool that made it easy to suck up crumbs in hard-to-reach places like in between seats and in upholstery crevices.

作为戴森手持系列的新成员，这款小巧的产品专为清理汽车、船只、床上用品、家具及其他难以触及的空间中的灰尘和碎屑而设计。它体积虽小，但吸力强劲，转速高达 110,000 转/分钟（可媲美其他更大、更昂贵的戴森型号）。其配备的缝隙吸嘴可以轻松吸走座椅缝隙和内饰褶皱中难以触及的碎屑。

However, unlike Dyson's standard vacuums, it only comes with two cleaning modes, and it doesn't have a digital display or power button. Instead, flip the switch between the two modes—standard and max—and press the trigger. This handheld also has a long battery life to get you through multiple cleans without having to remove it from your car or wherever you mostly use it.

然而，与戴森的标准吸尘器不同，它仅有两种清洁模式，且没有数字显示屏或电源按钮。你需要通过拨动开关在标准和强力模式之间切换，并按住扳机使用。这款手持吸尘器的电池续航时间也很长，足以支撑多次清洁，无需频繁将其从车内或主要使用地点拿去充电。

### Best Handheld Vacuum for Less Than $100: Ryobi 18V One+ Performance Hand Vacuum
### 100美元以下最佳手持吸尘器：Ryobi 18V One+ Performance 手持吸尘器

This is a powerful small vac that attaches to a rechargeable 18-volt Lithium-ion battery pack and comes with brush and crevice tool attachments. Although the vacuum is powerful on its own, the dust brush worked well for cleaning fabrics and effectively brushed up pet hair, and the crevice tool was small enough to fit between upholstery and hard-to-reach places in my car. For emptying, all you have to do is push the button to eject the dust cup. The vac has a dual filter system that, like many on the list, untwists from each other for even more filtration and should be cleaned regularly.

这是一款功能强大的小型吸尘器，使用可充电的 18 伏锂电池组，并配有毛刷和缝隙吸嘴。尽管吸尘器本身动力十足，但除尘刷在清洁织物和清理宠物毛发方面表现出色，缝隙吸嘴也足够小，可以深入汽车座椅缝隙和难以触及的地方。清理时，只需按下按钮即可弹出尘杯。该吸尘器拥有双重过滤系统，与清单上的许多产品一样，滤网可以拆卸以实现更深层的过滤，建议定期清洁。

It’s a steal at less than $100, it’s lightweight; I like that the battery pack on its own needs to be charged (helps conserve space) and easily snaps (and unsnaps) to the device with latches; and it's compatible with other Ryobi tools.

它售价不到100美元，性价比极高，而且重量轻。我喜欢它电池组独立充电的设计（有助于节省空间），并且可以通过卡扣轻松安装和拆卸；此外，它还兼容其他 Ryobi 工具。

### Best Handheld Vacuum for Pet Hair: Bissell Pet Hair Eraser
### 最佳宠物毛发手持吸尘器：Bissell Pet Hair Eraser

This lightweight, 3-pound hand vacuum is specially designed to target pet hair and does exactly that. A powerful motorized brush attachment lifts pet hair from a myriad of surfaces, including fur embedded in fabric and carpet; a super-skinny crevice tool sucks debris from in between cushions; and a flat, wide attachment works well for surface upholstery vacuuming. Like most other handheld vacs, you press a button on the top to disconnect the bin, and the filter sits on top to easily lift out and clean.

这款轻便的 3 磅重手持吸尘器专为清理宠物毛发而设计，且效果显著。强劲的电动刷头附件可以从各种表面（包括嵌入织物和地毯中的毛发）吸起宠物毛发；超细的缝隙吸嘴可以吸走垫子缝隙中的碎屑；扁平宽大的附件则非常适合清洁家具表面。与大多数其他手持吸尘器一样，你只需按下顶部的按钮即可拆卸尘盒，滤网位于顶部，可以轻松取出进行清洁。

In the first go, which lasted about 15 minutes before running out of juice, I was able to pick up over three canisters filled with just dust and hair. The only issue I have with this is that the crevice tool would often get stuck in the vacuum attachment connector, and it took a lot of muscle to pull it out to change attachments.

在第一次使用中，它在电量耗尽前持续工作了约 15 分钟，我清理出了满满三盒灰尘和毛发。我唯一的问题是，缝隙吸嘴经常会卡在吸尘器的连接口里，需要费很大力气才能拔出来更换其他附件。
