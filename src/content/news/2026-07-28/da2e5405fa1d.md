# opengeos / GeoLibre

GeoLibre is a free and open-source, lightweight, cloud-native GIS platform for visualizing, exploring, and analyzing geospatial data. It runs everywhere you do, in the web browser, on the desktop, on mobile, and inside Jupyter notebooks, all while keeping your data local and private.
GeoLibre 是一个免费、开源、轻量级且云原生的 GIS 平台，用于可视化、探索和分析地理空间数据。它可以在任何地方运行，包括网页浏览器、桌面端、移动端以及 Jupyter Notebook 中，同时确保您的数据保持本地化和私密性。

GeoLibre is built with Tauri v2, React, TypeScript, MapLibre GL JS, DuckDB-WASM Spatial, and deck.gl. The same workspace runs as a native desktop app, a native Android app, in any modern web browser, and adapts responsively to mobile and small screens.
GeoLibre 基于 Tauri v2、React、TypeScript、MapLibre GL JS、DuckDB-WASM Spatial 和 deck.gl 构建。同一个工作区可以作为原生桌面应用、原生 Android 应用或在任何现代网页浏览器中运行，并能自适应移动设备和小屏幕。

### Planetary basemaps
### 行星底图

GeoLibre is not limited to Earth. Planetary basemaps from OpenPlanetaryMap and USGS Astrogeology cover the Moon, Mars, Mercury, Venus, the Galilean moons (Io, Europa, Ganymede, Callisto), Titan, Pluto, and Charon, with a per-project ellipsoid so distance, area, and scale measurements match the body you are mapping. The deep-space starfield behind each globe comes from the Atmosphere Effects plugin.
GeoLibre 不局限于地球。来自 OpenPlanetaryMap 和 USGS Astrogeology 的行星底图涵盖了月球、火星、水星、金星、伽利略卫星（木卫一、木卫二、木卫三、木卫四）、土卫六、冥王星和卡戎星。每个项目都配有相应的椭球体，确保距离、面积和比例尺测量与您正在映射的天体相匹配。每个球体背后的深空星场均来自“大气效果 (Atmosphere Effects)”插件。
