# Oxygen-TryOn: Fashion-Native Foundation Model for Any-item Virtual Try-On
# Oxygen-TryOn：面向任意单品虚拟试穿的时尚原生基础模型

We present Oxygen-TryOn, a unified foundation model for any-item virtual try-on. Rather than repurposing a general-purpose image editor, Oxygen-TryOn is fashion-native, built for try-on through a dedicated data engine and try-on-specific training.
我们推出了 Oxygen-TryOn，这是一个用于任意单品虚拟试穿的统一基础模型。Oxygen-TryOn 并非简单地改造通用图像编辑器，而是“时尚原生”的，它通过专门的数据引擎和针对试穿任务的特定训练构建而成。

Given one or more reference items (clean product shots or in-the-wild worn-on photos) and a single target subject image, it synthesizes a photorealistic image of the subject wearing the items across virtually any fashion category. Prior systems handle a single garment category in a studio setting, and recent multi-reference methods remain garment-centric; in contrast, Oxygen-TryOn supports diverse items and scenarios, including full- and half-body views, a variable number of references, and free multi-item composition, while faithfully preserving both subject identity and item appearance.
给定一个或多个参考单品（纯净的产品图或实景穿戴照）以及一张目标人物图像，该模型能够合成出人物穿戴这些单品的照片级真实图像，且几乎涵盖所有时尚品类。以往的系统通常只能在摄影棚环境下处理单一服装类别，而近期的多参考方法也多以服装为中心；相比之下，Oxygen-TryOn 支持多种单品和场景，包括全身和半身视角、可变数量的参考图以及自由的多单品组合，同时能忠实地保留人物身份特征和单品外观。

Instead of mask-based inpainting, we reformulate try-on as a multi-reference, understanding-driven generation task. We build a data engine that collects, manufactures, annotates, and filters high-quality try-on data at scale, and design a three-stage recipe of continued pre-training (CPT), supervised fine-tuning (SFT), and reinforcement learning (RL).
我们不再使用基于掩码（mask）的图像修复技术，而是将试穿重新定义为一种基于多参考图、由理解驱动的生成任务。我们构建了一个数据引擎，用于大规模收集、制造、标注和筛选高质量的试穿数据，并设计了包含持续预训练（CPT）、监督微调（SFT）和强化学习（RL）的三个阶段训练方案。

The RL stage uses a hybrid reward combining an in-house try-on reward model with a proprietary, rubric-guided general-purpose model, jointly supervising fine-grained consistency and instruction-level quality. It also follows general editing instructions (e.g., pose changes) in the same pass.
强化学习阶段采用了一种混合奖励机制，结合了自研的试穿奖励模型与专有的、基于准则引导的通用模型，共同监督细粒度的一致性和指令级的生成质量。此外，它还能在同一次生成过程中遵循通用的编辑指令（例如姿势调整）。

Across public benchmarks and our in-house Oxygen-TryOn Bench, it achieves state-of-the-art consistency and realism on single-item try-on and leads on multi-item try-on, matching or surpassing both leading proprietary systems (Nano Banana Pro, GPT-Image-2, Seedream5 Lite) and open-source models (FLUX.2).
在公开基准测试和我们自建的 Oxygen-TryOn Bench 中，该模型在单品试穿方面实现了最先进的一致性和真实感，并在多品试穿方面处于领先地位，其表现持平或超越了主流的专有系统（如 Nano Banana Pro、GPT-Image-2、Seedream5 Lite）以及开源模型（如 FLUX.2）。
