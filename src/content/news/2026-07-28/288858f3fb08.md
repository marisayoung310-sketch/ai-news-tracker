# The Most Beautiful Statistic: The History and the Science of the Humble Mean
# 最美的统计量：平凡“平均值”背后的历史与科学

The mean keeps making its usefulness felt in all sorts of situations, often in truly non-obvious ways.
平均值在各种情境下不断展现其价值，且往往以一种极其隐晦的方式发挥作用。

Viking 1 launched aboard a Titan IIIE rocket August 20, 1975 and arrived at Mars on June 19, 1976. The first month was spent in orbit around the martian planet and on July 20, 1976 Viking Lander 1 separated from the Orbiter and touched down at Chryse Planitia.
“海盗1号”（Viking 1）于1975年8月20日搭乘泰坦IIIE火箭发射升空，并于1976年6月19日抵达火星。第一个月在火星轨道上运行，1976年7月20日，海盗1号着陆器与轨道飞行器分离，并在克律塞平原（Chryse Planitia）成功着陆。

On Saturday, June 19, 1976, the Viking 1 spacecraft entered Mars orbit. A month later, as a midsummer night stretched pleasantly ahead of them in Pasadena, California, dozens of NASA controllers helplessly watched a feed delayed by 19 minutes as the Viking 1 lander separated from the orbiter. Three hours later, the lander plunged into the Martian atmosphere at 10,000 miles per hour. Ten minutes later, following a fiendishly complex approach, and with zero help from JPL, the lander made a perfect touchdown on Mars.
1976年6月19日星期六，海盗1号航天器进入火星轨道。一个月后，在加利福尼亚州帕萨迪纳市一个惬意的仲夏夜，数十名NASA控制员无奈地看着延迟了19分钟的信号，目睹了海盗1号着陆器与轨道飞行器的分离。三小时后，着陆器以每小时1万英里的速度冲入火星大气层。十分钟后，在经历了一段极其复杂的进场过程，且没有任何来自喷气推进实验室（JPL）的实时协助下，着陆器在火星上实现了完美着陆。

For Mission Control, it hadn’t helped that, when Viking 1 entered Martian orbit and pointed its camera down at the surface, the originally selected landing site turned out to be “at least twice as rough as the Mars average” prompting a frantic search for a new site. Fortunately, a safer site was found which, in the scientists’ words, was still “rougher than the Martian average,” but “near the Martian average for elevations accessible to Viking” and “near the Mars average in reflectivity.” Even at 212 million miles from Earth, the humble mean turned out to be the statistical device guiding Viking 1 to a safe spot in an unfriendly world.
对于任务控制中心来说，当时的情况并不乐观：当海盗1号进入火星轨道并将摄像头对准表面时，最初选定的着陆点被发现“至少比火星平均水平粗糙两倍”，这迫使团队不得不紧急寻找新地点。幸运的是，他们最终找到了一个更安全的地点。用科学家的话说，虽然它仍然“比火星平均水平粗糙”，但在“海盗号可达的海拔高度范围内接近火星平均水平”，且“反射率接近火星平均水平”。即便远在距离地球2.12亿英里之外，平凡的“平均值”依然成为了引导海盗1号在这个陌生世界找到安全落脚点的统计工具。

The mean keeps making its usefulness felt in all sorts of situations, often in truly non-obvious ways. Consider, for instance, another happenstance. Five days after the Viking lander touched down on Mars, on July 25, 1976, the Viking orbiter transmitted to Earth, the following photograph.
平均值在各种情境下不断展现其价值，且往往以一种极其隐晦的方式发挥作用。例如，考虑另一个偶然事件。在海盗号着陆器登陆火星五天后，即1976年7月25日，海盗号轨道飞行器向地球传回了下面这张照片。

The photo was immediately dismissed as a mere play of light and shadow coupled with missing data (the dark “speckles” in the image). Nevertheless, it became one of the most famous examples of pareidolia: our tendency to detect patterns, especially faces, and ascribe meaning to them. And it is not just faces on Mars or the Moon. People have spotted animal shapes and UFOs amid clouds. Down on Earth, people have spotted cups and saucers, saucepans with handles, flagpoles, hammers, people hanging from poles, triangles, rectangles, and above all, skyrocketing trends in charts and plots that have been comfortably out on little more than a random walk.
这张照片随即被认定仅仅是光影效果与数据缺失（图像中的黑色“斑点”）共同作用的结果。尽管如此，它还是成为了“空想性错视”（pareidolia）最著名的例子之一：即人类倾向于在事物中寻找模式（尤其是人脸），并赋予其意义。这不仅限于火星或月球上的“人脸”，人们还曾在云层中看到过动物形状和不明飞行物。在地球上，人们也曾“看到”过杯碟、带柄的平底锅、旗杆、锤子、挂在杆子上的人、三角形、矩形，最重要的是，在那些本质上不过是随机游走的图表中，人们总能“看到”飙升的趋势。

It’s not that these patterns do not exist. Often, they do exist, if only lightly and ephemerally, and when they are there, our vision system has evolved to detect their presence almost every single time. Sadly, the patterns often never mean what we think they do. So, where in all this is the connection with the mean?
这并不是说这些模式不存在。通常它们确实存在，哪怕只是微弱且短暂的。当它们出现时，我们的视觉系统已经进化到几乎每次都能捕捉到它们。遗憾的是，这些模式往往并不代表我们所认为的含义。那么，这一切与“平均值”有什么联系呢？

### A Representative for Every Occasion
### 适用于各种场合的代表

The connection with the mean emerges from the counterfactual, i.e., from data in which you do not instantly see saucepans or people hanging from flag poles. In other words, data in which trends and patterns cannot be easily detected. Often, trends in data aren’t expressed in ways that give you an evolutionary advantage to detect them. So, your eyes fail to see them. Your brain fails to interpret them. That is when you sorely need devices like the mean, median, mode, and percentile scores. Such statistical measures as the mean can compress the noise and pull out the pattern in a measurable and actionable form, provided it exists.
与平均值的联系源于反事实，即那些你无法一眼看出平底锅或旗杆上挂着人的数据。换句话说，是指那些趋势和模式无法轻易被察觉的数据。通常，数据中的趋势并不会以一种让你具备进化优势的方式呈现，因此你的眼睛无法看到它们，大脑也无法解读它们。这时，你就非常需要平均值、中位数、众数和百分位数等工具。只要模式确实存在，像平均值这样的统计指标就能压缩噪声，并以可衡量、可操作的形式提取出这些模式。
