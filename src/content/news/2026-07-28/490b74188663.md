# YouTube Premium will include Peacock starting next year
# YouTube Premium 明年起将包含 Peacock 服务

YouTube’s ad-free Premium subscription is getting another perk: access to Peacock. In an announcement on Monday, NBCUniversal says the multi-year agreement will allow Premium subscribers to stream Peacock’s ad-supported shows, movies, and live sports directly through the YouTube app in 2027.

YouTube 的无广告 Premium 订阅服务即将迎来一项新福利：访问 Peacock。NBCUniversal 在周一的公告中表示，这项多年期协议将允许 Premium 订阅用户在 2027 年直接通过 YouTube 应用观看 Peacock 的广告支持版电视节目、电影和体育直播。

YouTube will bundle Peacock in its standard subscription, which increased to $15.99 / month in April, YouTube spokesperson Jessica Gibby confirmed to The Verge. That makes it cheaper than subscribing to both services individually, as Peacock currently starts at $10.99 / month for its ad-supported plan.

YouTube 发言人 Jessica Gibby 向 The Verge 证实，YouTube 将把 Peacock 整合进其标准订阅服务中（该服务价格已于 4 月涨至每月 15.99 美元）。这使得该方案比分别订阅两项服务更划算，因为 Peacock 目前的广告支持版套餐起价为每月 10.99 美元。

The news of the deal comes at a pivotal time for Peacock, which is separating from Comcast’s broadband business alongside NBCUniversal and Sky. Matt Strauss, the chairman of NBCUniversal’s media group, says the agreement will boost Peacock’s “next phase of growth by bringing the service to millions of YouTube Premium subscribers.”

这项交易的消息发布之际，正值 Peacock 的关键时期——它正与 NBCUniversal 和 Sky 一起从 Comcast 的宽带业务中剥离出来。NBCUniversal 媒体集团董事长 Matt Strauss 表示，该协议将通过把服务带给数百万 YouTube Premium 订阅用户，从而推动 Peacock 的“下一阶段增长”。

YouTube revealed that it had 125 million Premium and Music subscribers in 2025, while Comcast’s most recent earnings report revealed that Peacock has 48 million subscribers in the US. Peacock also turned a profit for the first time last quarter.

YouTube 透露，其在 2025 年拥有 1.25 亿 Premium 和 Music 订阅用户，而 Comcast 最新的财报显示，Peacock 在美国拥有 4800 万订阅用户。Peacock 也在上个季度首次实现了盈利。

As part of the deal, NBCUniversal will stream “a selection of live sporting events” on YouTube, and it’s also extending its distribution agreement with YouTube TV.

作为协议的一部分，NBCUniversal 将在 YouTube 上直播“精选体育赛事”，并延长了与 YouTube TV 的分发协议。
