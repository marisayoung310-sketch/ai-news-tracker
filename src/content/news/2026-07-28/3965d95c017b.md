# Be Consistent! Enhancing Robust Visual Reasoning in LVLMs with Consistency Constraints
# 保持一致性！通过一致性约束增强大型视觉语言模型（LVLM）的鲁棒视觉推理能力

**Abstract:** While Large Vision-Language Models (LVLMs) exhibit strong perceptual capabilities, they remain vulnerable in visual reasoning tasks. Existing benchmarks largely focus on symbolic mathematical or scientific problems and simple vision-centric tasks, offering limited assessment of complex visual reasoning and logical consistency, a critical requirement for reliable reasoning systems.

**摘要：** 尽管大型视觉语言模型（LVLM）展现出了强大的感知能力，但在视觉推理任务中仍然表现脆弱。现有的基准测试主要集中在符号数学、科学问题以及简单的视觉中心任务上，对于复杂视觉推理和逻辑一致性的评估非常有限，而这正是构建可靠推理系统的关键要求。

We introduce ConVBench, a complex vision-centric reasoning benchmark in which each image is paired with two logically equivalent questions across six categories: action and state, complex counting, spatial reasoning, causal and intent understanding, commonsense reasoning, and temporal perception.

我们引入了 ConVBench，这是一个以视觉为中心的复杂推理基准测试。在该基准中，每张图像都配有两个逻辑等价的问题，涵盖六大类别：动作与状态、复杂计数、空间推理、因果与意图理解、常识推理以及时间感知。

To complement this benchmark, we define two evaluation metrics, logical consistency and robust accuracy, that jointly assess both the correctness and consistency of model responses.

为了完善这一基准测试，我们定义了两个评估指标——逻辑一致性和鲁棒准确率，旨在共同评估模型回答的正确性与一致性。

We further present ConVLM, which improves LVLM reasoning through Group Relative Policy Optimization (GRPO)-based reinforcement learning with a novel consistency reward. This method leverages automatically generated logically equivalent question-answer pairs and a dual-reward design combining accuracy- and consistency-based signals, encouraging agreement between paired responses. The framework functions effectively with or without strict answer supervision.

此外，我们提出了 ConVLM，它通过基于组相对策略优化（GRPO）的强化学习以及一种新颖的一致性奖励机制，提升了 LVLM 的推理能力。该方法利用自动生成的逻辑等价问答对，并采用结合了准确率和一致性信号的双重奖励设计，从而鼓励模型在成对回答中保持一致。该框架在有或无严格答案监督的情况下均能有效运行。
