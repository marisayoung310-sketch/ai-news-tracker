# SDCC 2026’s best trailers are heavy on cyberpunk and sci-fi
# 2026年圣迭戈漫展（SDCC）最佳预告片：赛博朋克与科幻题材大放异彩

Get your first look at *Neuromancer*, *Blade Runner 2099*, and more.
抢先预览《神经漫游者》、《银翼杀手 2099》等重磅作品。

San Diego Comic-Con took place over the weekend, and as per usual it was full of tidbits of news across film, TV, and gaming. Marvel revealed the next Ghost Rider (Ryan Gosling) and Black Panther (David Jonsson), while we also got release dates for *God of War: Laufey* (February 16th) and the fourth and final season of *Silo* (summer 2027).
上周末，圣迭戈漫展（SDCC）如期举行，一如既往地带来了电影、电视和游戏领域的各类新闻。漫威公布了下一任“恶灵骑士”（瑞恩·高斯林饰）和“黑豹”（大卫·琼斯饰），同时我们也获悉了《战神：劳菲》（2月16日）以及《末日地堡》第四季（最终季，2027年夏季）的发布日期。

But the best part of SDCC each year, at least for those following along at home, are the trailers. And 2026 did not disappoint: We got first looks at highly anticipated cyberpunk shows *Blade Runner 2099* and *Neuromancer*, along with a new take on a Stephen King classic and the return of some animated favorites.
但对于在家关注展会的人来说，每年 SDCC 最精彩的部分莫过于预告片。2026 年的展会没有让人失望：我们抢先看到了备受期待的赛博朋克剧集《银翼杀手 2099》和《神经漫游者》，以及斯蒂芬·金经典作品的全新改编版和一些动画佳作的回归。

Here’s all of the best stuff in one handy place.
以下是所有精彩内容的汇总。

### Blade Runner 2099
### 《银翼杀手 2099》

Amazon first revealed that it was working on a series set in the *Blade Runner* universe in 2022, but it took a few years before we got a glimpse at it. 2099 is set 50 years after the last film and stars Michelle Yeoh and Hunter Schafer as a blade runner and replicant who are forced to work together. The show is a limited series — don’t expect a season 2 — and all episodes will be streaming on November 25th.
亚马逊早在 2022 年就透露正在开发一部设定在《银翼杀手》宇宙中的剧集，但直到几年后的今天我们才得以一窥真容。《2099》的故事设定在上一部电影的 50 年后，由杨紫琼和亨特·莎弗主演，讲述了一名银翼杀手与一名复制人被迫联手的故事。该剧为限定剧（不会有第二季），所有剧集将于 11 月 25 日上线。

### Carrie
### 《魔女嘉莉》

Mike Flanagan had a solid run at Netflix through series like *The Haunting of Hill House* and *The Fall of the House of Usher*, but Amazon poached him a few years back for its own streaming service. He’s starting out with a series based on Stephen King’s classic *Carrie*. This isn’t Flanagan’s first King adaptation — see also *Doctor Sleep* and *The Life of Chuck* — and it starts streaming on October 7th.
迈克·弗拉纳根曾凭借《鬼入侵》和《厄舍府的崩塌》等剧集在 Netflix 取得了巨大成功，但几年前他被亚马逊挖角到了自家的流媒体平台。他目前正在制作一部基于斯蒂芬·金经典小说《魔女嘉莉》的剧集。这并非弗拉纳根首次改编金的作品（此前还有《睡眠医生》和《查克的一生》），该剧将于 10 月 7 日上线。

### The Lord of the Rings: The Rings of Power
### 《指环王：力量之戒》

Elsewhere on Prime Video, the service’s big fantasy epic *The Rings of Power* is coming back for a third season on November 11th. Perhaps the biggest addition in season 3 is that Simon Pegg is joining the cast as the voice of a demonic Balrog creature.
在 Prime Video 的其他动态方面，该平台的奇幻史诗大作《力量之戒》将于 11 月 11 日回归第三季。第三季最大的看点或许是西蒙·佩吉的加盟，他将为剧中的恶魔炎魔配音。

### Neuromancer
### 《神经漫游者》

Apple TV has steadily become the preeminent home of streaming sci-fi, and so an adaptation of William Gibson’s seminal cyberpunk novel *Neuromancer* seems like a perfect fit. The new series stars Callum Turner as “a damaged, top-rung super-hacker,” and it starts streaming on January 22nd.
Apple TV 已稳步成为流媒体科幻剧集的首选平台，因此改编威廉·吉布森的赛博朋克开山之作《神经漫游者》显得再合适不过。这部新剧由卡勒姆·特纳主演，饰演一名“受过创伤的顶级超级黑客”，该剧将于 1 月 22 日上线。

### Dark Matter
### 《暗物质》

Speaking of sci-fi on Apple TV, season 2 of *Dark Matter* starts streaming on August 28th, and a new trailer reveals just how complex its story is becoming, with a family searching for safety amid a multiverse of infinite worlds.
说到 Apple TV 的科幻作品，《暗物质》第二季将于 8 月 28 日上线。新预告片揭示了其故事正变得愈发复杂，讲述了一个家庭在无限多元宇宙中寻求安全的故事。

### Futurama
### 《飞出个未来》

*Futurama*’s latest revival has been hit or miss, but the next season — we’re at 14, if you’re counting — at least looks jam-packed with everything from pop stars to vampires. It starts streaming on Disney Plus and Hulu on August 3rd.
《飞出个未来》最近的重启表现褒贬不一，但下一季（如果你在数的话，这已经是第 14 季了）看起来内容极其丰富，从流行歌星到吸血鬼应有尽有。该季将于 8 月 3 日在 Disney Plus 和 Hulu 上线。

### Common Side Effects
### 《常见副作用》

The first season of Adult Swim’s *Common Side Effects* was an excellent surprise, an animated series that both looked incredible and explored the real-world dangers of the US healthcare system. And season 2 looks like it’s going to continue that when it premieres in 2027.
Adult Swim 的《常见副作用》第一季是一个巨大的惊喜，这部动画剧集不仅视觉效果惊人，还探讨了美国医疗体系在现实世界中的弊端。第二季预计将于 2027 年首播，并将延续这一风格。

### Lanterns
### 《绿灯侠》

If you ever wondered what *True Detective* would be like if it featured space cops, the DC universe has you covered. HBO’s *Lanterns* follows a pair of intergalactic investigators — played by Aaron Pierre and Kyle Chandler — who become “drawn into a dark, earth-based mystery with cosmic ties” while looking into a murder. It premieres on August 16th.
如果你曾好奇如果《真探》的主角是太空警察会是什么样，DC 宇宙满足了你的想象。HBO 的《绿灯侠》讲述了一对星际调查员（由亚伦·皮埃尔和凯尔·钱德勒饰演）在调查一起谋杀案时，被“卷入了一场与宇宙有关的黑暗地球谜团”的故事。该剧将于 8 月 16 日首播。

### Onslaught
### 《猛攻》

A24 isn’t exactly known for action movies, but that could change with *Onslaught*, the latest from Adam Wingard, director of the last two *Godzilla vs. Kong* movies. It’s about a mother trying to protect her daughter from “a rogue squad of genetically engineered super soldiers,” and it hits theaters on September 4th.
A24 并不以动作片闻名，但这种情况可能会随着《猛攻》而改变。这是亚当·温加德（《哥斯拉大战金刚》系列导演）的最新力作，讲述了一位母亲试图保护女儿免受“一支基因改造超级士兵叛军”伤害的故事，该片将于 9 月 4 日在影院上映。

### Queen of the Damned
### 《吸血鬼女王》

The show formerly known as *Interview With the Vampire* (and *The Vampire Lestat*) is coming back for the next chapter of Anne Rice’s saga. *Queen of the Damned* introduces the vampire progenitor Akasha to the series, though there’s no word yet on when the new season will premiere.
这部曾被称为《夜访吸血鬼》（以及《吸血鬼莱斯特》）的剧集即将回归，开启安妮·赖斯传奇的下一个篇章。《吸血鬼女王》将引入吸血鬼始祖阿卡莎，不过目前尚未公布新一季的开播日期。

### Transformers: The Movie
### 《变形金刚大电影》

Okay, this one isn’t actually new, but the classic animated film is getting a 4K re-release for its 40th anniversary. It’ll be in theaters on September 17th.
好吧，这部作品其实并不算新，但为了纪念其上映 40 周年，这部经典动画电影将推出 4K 重制版，并于 9 月 17 日在影院上映。
