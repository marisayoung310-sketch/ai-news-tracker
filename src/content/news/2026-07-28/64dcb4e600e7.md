# Cloud-Native Evaluation-as-a-Service: A Microservices Architecture for Scalable AI Monitoring with Conformal Guarantees
# 云原生“评估即服务”：一种具有共形保证的可扩展 AI 监控微服务架构

**Abstract:** We present EaaS, a cloud-native reference architecture that operationalizes AI evaluation methods as six stateless Kubernetes microservices: conformal prediction with finite-sample-corrected Adaptive Prediction Sets, calibration assessment, drift detection via RFF-approximated Maximum Mean Discrepancy, fairness monitoring with bootstrap confidence intervals, a DAG-based pipeline orchestrator, and a result storage API.

**摘要：** 我们提出了 EaaS，这是一种云原生参考架构，它将 AI 评估方法操作化为六个无状态的 Kubernetes 微服务：包含有限样本校正自适应预测集的共形预测、校准评估、通过 RFF 近似最大均值差异（MMD）进行的漂移检测、使用自助法置信区间的公平性监控、基于 DAG 的流水线编排器以及结果存储 API。

We validate four key methodological concerns. First, empirical coverage is consistent with the marginal conformal guarantee across K=50 random calibration/test splits, with mean coverage within 1.4 percentage points of the nominal target.

我们验证了四个关键的方法论问题。首先，在 K=50 次随机校准/测试划分中，经验覆盖率与边缘共形保证一致，平均覆盖率与标称目标的偏差在 1.4 个百分点以内。

Second, all four MMLU answer tokens appear in the top-20 logprobs with 0% imputation needed, and simulated imputation at 10% produces less than 1.5% coverage impact.

其次，所有四个 MMLU 答案标记均出现在前 20 个对数概率（logprobs）中，无需进行任何插补；而在 10% 的模拟插补下，对覆盖率的影响小于 1.5%。

Third, RFF-MMD achieves 100% detection power for mild and severe drift at the median heuristic bandwidth, with Type I error between 5-8.5%.

第三，RFF-MMD 在中位数启发式带宽下对轻度和重度漂移实现了 100% 的检测能力，且第一类错误率在 5-8.5% 之间。

Fourth, fairness monitoring on the UCI Adult Income dataset reveals significant demographic parity disparities by race (DP gap=0.33) with stable alerts across sequential batches.

第四，在 UCI Adult Income 数据集上的公平性监控显示，按种族划分的人口统计学平价存在显著差异（DP 差距为 0.33），且在连续批次中警报保持稳定。

Conformal prediction and calibration services achieve sub-2ms p99 latency at batch size 100; RFF-MMD requires ~500ms suited for periodic batch monitoring. A comparison with four open-source tools suggests that, to the best of our knowledge, no current platform combines conformal-prediction-as-a-service, microservice decomposition, and DAG-based orchestration.

共形预测和校准服务在批处理大小为 100 时实现了低于 2ms 的 p99 延迟；RFF-MMD 需要约 500ms，适用于周期性的批处理监控。与四种开源工具的对比表明，据我们所知，目前尚无平台能够结合“共形预测即服务”、微服务解耦以及基于 DAG 的编排功能。
