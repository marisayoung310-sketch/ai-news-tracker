# SCALE: Self-Supervised Constraint-Aware Layout GEneration for Local P&R DRV Fixing at Advanced Nodes
# SCALE：面向先进工艺节点局部布局布线（P&R）DRV 修复的自监督约束感知布局生成技术

**Abstract:** As semiconductor manufacturing advances toward sub-2nm nodes, local place-and-route (P&R) design-rule violation (DRV) fixing is increasingly limited by complex rule interactions, dense multi-layer routing geometries, and foundry-specific constraints. 

**摘要：** 随着半导体制造工艺向 2nm 以下节点推进，局部布局布线（P&R）的设计规则违例（DRV）修复日益受到复杂规则交互、高密度多层布线几何结构以及代工厂特定约束的限制。

While Large Language Models (LLMs) have recently demonstrated strong capabilities in EDA scripting and documentation, their application to visual layout understanding remains largely unexplored: diagnosing DRC violations from layout imagery demands precise geometric reasoning and foundry-specific rule knowledge absent from general-purpose VLM training. 

尽管大语言模型（LLMs）近期在 EDA 脚本编写和文档处理方面展现出强大的能力，但其在视觉布局理解方面的应用仍处于探索阶段：从布局图像中诊断 DRC 违例需要精确的几何推理和代工厂特定的规则知识，而这些知识在通用视觉语言模型（VLM）的训练中通常是缺失的。

We propose SCALE, a framework with a self-supervised layout-generation stage for local DRV fixing at advanced nodes. Multi-layer layout geometry is serialized into structured text, and a fine-tuned language model learns to reconstruct randomly masked polygons from surrounding BEOL context alone without violation labels. 

我们提出了 SCALE，这是一个包含自监督布局生成阶段的框架，旨在解决先进工艺节点下的局部 DRV 修复问题。该框架将多层布局几何结构序列化为结构化文本，并利用微调后的语言模型，在没有违例标签的情况下，仅根据周围的后端（BEOL）上下文信息学习重建随机掩码的多边形。

At inference, natural-language rule constraints and high-temperature sampling steer generation toward diverse, violation-prone layout variants validated by an industrial signoff DRC checker, producing DRC-annotated layout--violation pairs used to fine-tune a domain-adapted DRC-VLM. 

在推理阶段，通过自然语言规则约束和高温采样（high-temperature sampling），引导生成多样化且易出现违例的布局变体，并由工业级签核（Signoff）DRC 检查器进行验证，从而生成带有 DRC 注释的“布局-违例”对，用于微调领域适配的 DRC-VLM 模型。

This VLM provides rule-aware geometric guidance for local DRV repair, boosting state-of-the-art agents' solve rates by +12--25% (up to 97%) on 100 real sub-2nm cases spanning enclosure, spacing, width, and color-spacing violations.

该 VLM 模型为局部 DRV 修复提供了具备规则感知能力的几何指导，在涵盖包围（enclosure）、间距（spacing）、宽度（width）和颜色间距（color-spacing）违例的 100 个真实 2nm 以下案例中，将现有最先进智能体的解决率提升了 12% 至 25%（最高可达 97%）。
