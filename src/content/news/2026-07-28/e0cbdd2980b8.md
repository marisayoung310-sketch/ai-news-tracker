# Threads users can now chat with Meta AI in their DMs
# Threads 用户现在可以在私信中与 Meta AI 聊天

Meta on Monday said it is rolling out its Meta AI chatbot within Threads’ DMs, giving users a way to chat with the AI assistant.
Meta 周一表示，正在 Threads 的私信（DM）功能中推出 Meta AI 聊天机器人，为用户提供了一种与该 AI 助手交流的新方式。

Although Threads users in select markets could already interact with Meta AI in public posts, like people can with Grok on X, this new integration lets users talk with the AI assistant privately.
尽管部分市场的 Threads 用户此前已经可以在公开帖子中与 Meta AI 互动（类似于用户在 X 上与 Grok 的互动），但此次新集成允许用户与 AI 助手进行私密对话。

By giving users an easier way to talk to an AI chatbot, Meta is looking to keep users within its ecosystem, with an eye toward discouraging them from using third-party assistants like OpenAI’s ChatGPT or Google Gemini.
通过为用户提供更便捷的 AI 聊天机器人交流方式，Meta 旨在将用户留在其生态系统内，并试图以此减少用户使用 OpenAI 的 ChatGPT 或 Google Gemini 等第三方助手。

Meta AI is already available within DMs on Meta’s other platforms, including Facebook, Instagram, and WhatsApp.
Meta AI 此前已在 Meta 的其他平台（包括 Facebook、Instagram 和 WhatsApp）的私信功能中上线。

The new integration lets users share Threads posts, images, links, and videos directly with Meta AI. You can ask follow-up questions and dive deeper into topics, too.
这一新集成允许用户直接将 Threads 的帖子、图片、链接和视频分享给 Meta AI。你还可以进行追问，并深入探讨相关话题。

The update will be rolled out globally starting Monday, the company says. Meta noted it’s continuing to test Meta AI in Threads’ public feeds in a handful of global markets and is considering feedback before expanding availability more broadly.
该公司表示，此次更新将从周一开始在全球范围内推出。Meta 指出，他们目前仍在少数全球市场测试 Threads 公开信息流中的 Meta AI 功能，并将在扩大覆盖范围前评估用户反馈。

Users who want to see fewer Meta AI replies in their feed can mute @meta.ai, use the “Not interested” option on any Meta AI post, or hide Meta AI replies that appear on their post.
希望在信息流中减少看到 Meta AI 回复的用户，可以选择屏蔽 @meta.ai，在任何 Meta AI 的帖子中使用“不感兴趣”选项，或者隐藏出现在自己帖子下的 Meta AI 回复。

By further integrating Meta AI into Threads, Meta is positioning its X rival as a place where you can get information and recommendations without having to leave the app.
通过将 Meta AI 进一步整合进 Threads，Meta 正将其这款 X 的竞争对手定位为一个无需离开应用即可获取信息和建议的平台。
