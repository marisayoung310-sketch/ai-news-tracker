# Multi-Horizon Consistency as Geometry: When Latent Dynamics Contract, and When They Do Not
# 多视界一致性作为几何：潜在动力学何时收缩，何时不收缩

**Abstract:** Multi-horizon latent consistency is a common training knob in video predictors and world models, but practitioners rarely know what it does to transition geometry. We treat lambda, the weight on multi-step latent agreement, as a diagnostic control and measure an empirical expansion proxy L20,q95 together with horizon-20 prediction error E20.

**摘要：** 多视界潜在一致性（Multi-horizon latent consistency）是视频预测器和世界模型中常见的训练调节参数，但从业者很少了解它对转换几何结构的影响。我们将多步潜在一致性的权重 lambda 视为一种诊断控制手段，并测量了经验扩张代理指标 L20,q95 以及 20 步视界预测误差 E20。

On Moving-MNIST (n=6 seeds at the critical pair), raising lambda from 0 to 0.8 cuts L20 from 4.96 +/- 2.01 to 1.01 +/- 0.06 (paired t p=0.005, Wilcoxon p=0.031) and halves E20 (0.365 to 0.177, paired t p=1.1e-13). Four of six seeds cross L<1 at lambda=0.8. The same loss does not produce population L<1 on action-conditioned Pendulum-v1 or CartPole-v1, nor on KTH Actions video, even when E20 improves.

在 Moving-MNIST 数据集上（关键对中 n=6 个种子），将 lambda 从 0 提高到 0.8，L20 从 4.96 +/- 2.01 降至 1.01 +/- 0.06（配对 t 检验 p=0.005，Wilcoxon 检验 p=0.031），并将 E20 减半（从 0.365 降至 0.177，配对 t 检验 p=1.1e-13）。在 lambda=0.8 时，六个种子中有四个达到了 L<1。同样的损失函数在动作条件下的 Pendulum-v1 或 CartPole-v1，以及 KTH Actions 视频上，即使 E20 得到改善，也无法产生总体 L<1 的结果。

An associational mediation analysis on MMNIST gives r-hat=0.94 (95% CI [0.88, 1.00], n=27, B=2000); lambda was not randomized. Defensive checks (architectural baselines, exogenous stress, WorldTest, MPC, scaling) mostly support a narrow claim: soft consistency can push passive video toward a near-contractive band, and that band is domain-limited.

对 MMNIST 进行的关联中介分析得出 r-hat=0.94（95% 置信区间 [0.88, 1.00], n=27, B=2000）；lambda 未进行随机化处理。防御性检查（架构基线、外源压力、WorldTest、MPC、缩放）大多支持一个狭义的结论：软一致性可以将被动视频推向近收缩带，且该带具有领域局限性。

A stochastic-forcing law L20 ~ 1.23 + 1.82 eta at lambda=0.8 (bootstrap slope CI [1.73, 1.92], R^2=0.96) unifies control domains on the same curve via calibrated eta_eff. Complete joint slices at lambda in {0.4, 1.2} (30/30 cells, 5 eta x 3 seeds) show comparable linear L20(eta) slopes (~1.69 and ~2.00); we do not fit a continuous (lambda, eta) surface. We do not report DreamerV3 or TD-MPC2 returns.

在 lambda=0.8 时，随机强迫定律 L20 ~ 1.23 + 1.82 eta（自助法斜率置信区间 [1.73, 1.92], R^2=0.96）通过校准后的 eta_eff 将控制领域统一在同一曲线上。在 lambda 为 {0.4, 1.2} 时的完整联合切片（30/30 单元，5 个 eta x 3 个种子）显示出相当的线性 L20(eta) 斜率（约 1.69 和 2.00）；我们没有拟合连续的 (lambda, eta) 曲面。我们未报告 DreamerV3 或 TD-MPC2 的回报结果。
