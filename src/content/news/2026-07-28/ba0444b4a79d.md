# Farmland Extent and Visible Boundary Mapping from 1 m NAIP Imagery Using Residual U-Net and Text-Prompted SAM 3 Refinement
# 基于残差 U-Net 与文本提示 SAM 3 优化的 1 米 NAIP 影像农田范围及可见边界测绘

**Abstract:** Agricultural field maps are often proprietary, incomplete, or outdated, yet they provide the spatial framework for crop monitoring, production accounting, and land-conversion analysis. This study presents a reproducible workflow for mapping farmland extent and visible boundaries from 1 m NAIP RGB imagery.
**摘要：** 农田地图通常具有专有性、不完整性或滞后性，但它们为作物监测、产量核算和土地转换分析提供了空间框架。本研究提出了一种可复现的工作流程，用于从 1 米分辨率的 NAIP RGB 影像中测绘农田范围及可见边界。

Thirty-seven scenes spanning open cropland, peri-urban interfaces, semi-irrigation geometries, and fragmented mosaics were annotated in CVAT and converted to binary masks. Non-overlapping 256 x 256 patches yielded 5,698 samples, split by source scene into 3,850 training, 770 validation, and 1,078 test patches.
研究选取了涵盖开放农田、城乡结合部、半干旱灌溉几何区域及破碎化镶嵌地块的 37 个场景，在 CVAT 中进行标注并转换为二值掩膜。通过非重叠的 256 x 256 切片处理，共获得 5,698 个样本，并按源场景划分为 3,850 个训练集、770 个验证集和 1,078 个测试集切片。

A residual U-Net (ResUNet) trained with a Dice-dominant loss, L = 2.5(1 - Dice) + BCE, achieved test accuracy 0.8808, IoU 0.8605, Dice 0.9234, precision 0.8766, and recall 0.9794. A frozen SAM 3 branch prompted with "agricultural farmland field" was fused with ResUNet by logical OR.
模型采用以 Dice 系数为主导的损失函数 L = 2.5(1 - Dice) + BCE 训练残差 U-Net (ResUNet)，测试集准确率达到 0.8808，IoU 为 0.8605，Dice 系数为 0.9234，精确率为 0.8766，召回率为 0.9794。此外，通过逻辑“或”运算，将一个以“agricultural farmland field”（农业农田）为提示词的冻结 SAM 3 分支与 ResUNet 进行了融合。

On selected difficult patches, Dice improved from 0.858 to 0.955 (orchard rows) and from 0.804 to 0.903 (fragmented parcels). Sliding-window stitching produced coherent regional masks (example tile Dice 0.898 and 0.919). The product is a semantic farmland-extent layer, not a cadastral parcel map, and supports agricultural monitoring where current field layers are unavailable.
在选定的困难切片上，Dice 系数分别从 0.858 提升至 0.955（果园行）和从 0.804 提升至 0.903（破碎地块）。通过滑动窗口拼接生成了连贯的区域掩膜（示例切片 Dice 分别为 0.898 和 0.919）。该成果是一种语义农田范围图层，而非地籍地块图，旨在为缺乏现有农田图层的地区提供农业监测支持。
