# Tariffs didn’t bring manufacturing jobs back to the US
# 关税并没有将制造业工作岗位带回美国

**Altana CEO Evan Smith on the new normal of global trade.**

我们上一次邀请 Evan 是在 2025 年初，当时我们讨论了特朗普政府最初几轮关税如何开始影响全球贸易，以及 Altana 在航运和贸易的宏观数据中观察到了哪些模式。那是一次既令人警醒又极具启发性的谈话；我和 Evan 几乎立刻就深入探讨了国际关系和经济学的核心本质。那是一次非常棒的对话。

You’ll hear Evan describe how AI is changing how they make software for a very demanding set of clients that manage billions in international trade — which is causing some big debates about how a software company even runs in 2026.

And of course, what they do also needs to change more or less daily. Trade policy is changing rapidly around the world but likely nowhere faster than here in the US. Between the Trump administration’s whack-a-mole tariff policies and war in Iran, it’s very hard to keep up with the current rules.
当然，他们的工作内容也需要几乎每天都在变化。全球贸易政策正在迅速更迭，但恐怕没有哪里比美国变化得更快。在特朗普政府“打地鼠”式的关税政策和伊朗战争的双重影响下，想要跟上当前的规则变得非常困难。

That’s where Altana comes in. All that data coming from all around the world gives Evan a really high-level view of what’s actually happening. All those tariffs, for example, were supposed to bring manufacturing jobs back to the United States. So I asked Evan directly: Is that actually happening? Spoiler alert — you won’t be surprised to hear him say “no.” And while it’s clear the world still relies on global supply chains, the pressure placed on economic chokepoints like the Strait of Hormuz is going to get higher than ever.
这就是 Altana 的用武之地。来自世界各地的海量数据让 Evan 能够从极高的视角洞察实际发生的情况。例如，所有那些关税本应将制造业工作岗位带回美国。所以我直接问了 Evan：这真的发生了吗？剧透一下——听到他说“没有”，你一定不会感到惊讶。虽然显而易见世界仍然依赖全球供应链，但霍尔木兹海峡等经济咽喉要道所承受的压力将比以往任何时候都大。

There’s a lot going on in this one — as you can tell, Evan and I really enjoy talking to each other. Okay: Altana CEO Evan Smith. Here we go. This interview has been lightly edited for length and clarity.
这次访谈内容非常丰富——正如你所感受到的，Evan 和我非常享受彼此的交流。好了：Altana 首席执行官 Evan Smith。我们开始吧。本次访谈为篇幅和清晰度经过了轻微编辑。

Evan Smith, you’re the co-founder and CEO of Altana. Welcome back to Decoder.

People can go back and watch, read, or listen to that interview. It’s very good. I really enjoyed it. The thing that strikes me about the past year and a half is that some of this stuff was theoretical the last time you were on the show. You founded Altana on the idea that globalization as we knew it — what you call, “globalization 1.0” — was going to come to an end, it was going to get more complicated, and the world needed software to solve it. The rubber has hit the road. It has gotten more complicated. It’s getting more complicated by the day. Even as we speak this week, it is getting more complicated. Even over the last year and a half it feels like it has gone from being a bet to being something very practical now. Is that how you see it?

Completely. One of our investors described Altana as, “an index bet on global dislocation.”
完全同意。我们的一位投资者将 Altana 描述为“对全球错位（dislocation）的指数级押注”。

That’s a lot. Can I just ask you maybe a philosophical, emotional question about that? I get a lot of feedback from our audience. 这信息量很大。我能问你一个关于此的哲学性、情感性的问题吗？我从听众那里收到了很多反馈。节目里常有首席执行官谈论这种押注方式，但在现实层面，情况却是一团糟。所以，我理解“对错位的指数级押注”。这在 CNBC（财经频道）上听起来很棒，对吧？

I understand exactly how that plays to that audience. 我完全明白这种说法对那个受众群体意味着什么。作为一家本质上只是为人们绘制地图的软件公司，你如何看待这种看待世界的方式，以及它对普通人意味着什么？我认为普通人现在真的在挣扎，他们感到非常困惑，也非常焦虑。

Well, trade touches virtually every part of or most of our lives. So, our mission is to fix globalization, but we’re not retreating from globalization. We’re leaning into it. But we believe that globalization needs to be fixed. It needs to be more trusted, more secure, more fair. That’s a recognition that trade will and should continue across borders. That’s the animating ethos in the company. We work with our customers who in most respects are on the front lines of all this stuff. We work with eight of the 10 biggest logistics providers. We work with the government agencies that have to scramble to enforce these laws. Then, we work with importers and their supply chains to comply with them. And the stakes are huge. Do medical devices get to the hospital? What’s the price of fuel and food?
贸易几乎触及我们生活的方方面面。因此，我们的使命是修复全球化，但我们并没有从全球化中退缩，我们是在拥抱它。但我们认为全球化需要被修复。它需要变得更值得信赖、更安全、更公平。这承认了贸易将会且应该继续跨越国界。这就是公司的核心精神。我们与客户合作，他们在大多数方面都处于这一切的前线。我们与全球十大物流供应商中的八家合作。我们与那些必须争分夺秒执行法律的政府机构合作。此外，我们还与进口商及其供应链合作，以确保合规。这关系重大。医疗设备能送到医院吗？燃料和食品的价格是多少？

This is how we feed ourselves, sustain ourselves, and protect ourselves. So, how do you have both at the same time? How do you have more enforcement and more fracturing as geopolitics play out, but have more trade, more growth, and more economic security? I think Altana is the answer to that question.
所以，我非常同意你的观点，即这种错位最终会对普通人产生最深远的影响，但这正是问题的关键，对吧？贸易是增长的命脉。这是我们养活自己、维持生存和保护自己的方式。那么，如何同时实现这两者呢？如何在地缘政治博弈中加强执法和应对碎片化的同时，又能拥有更多的贸易、更多的增长和更多的经济安全？我认为 Altana 就是这个问题的答案。
