# MotifRole-Diff: Risk-Optimal Role-Aware Corruption for Masked Molecular Graph Diffusion
# MotifRole-Diff：用于掩码分子图扩散的风险最优角色感知损坏机制

**Abstract:** Masked discrete diffusion for molecular graph generation typically applies a uniform corruption schedule to all tokens in a lossless graph-to-sequence representation, implicitly treating structurally heterogeneous molecular components as equally difficult and equally important to reconstruct.
**摘要：** 用于分子图生成的掩码离散扩散模型通常对无损图到序列表示中的所有标记应用统一的损坏调度，这隐含地将结构异构的分子组件视为具有相同的重构难度和重要性。

However, different molecular graph token roles exhibit substantial variation in denoising difficulty and their influence on the decoded molecule, motivating role-specific corruption strategies.
然而，不同的分子图标记角色在去噪难度及其对解码分子的影响方面表现出显著差异，这促使我们采用基于角色的损坏策略。

We introduce MotifRole-Diff, a role-aware corruption process that allocates masking rates according to empirically measured denoising difficulty and graph-level perturbation impact while preserving the model architecture, clean sequence space, and lossless molecular-graph decoder.
我们引入了 MotifRole-Diff，这是一种角色感知的损坏过程，它根据经验测量的去噪难度和图级扰动影响来分配掩码率，同时保持了模型架构、干净序列空间和无损分子图解码器不变。

We formulate schedule selection as the risk-optimal allocation of a fixed masking budget across token roles. Our theorem characterizes optimality for the modeled role-weighted residual risk, while downstream generation performance is evaluated empirically.
我们将调度选择表述为在不同标记角色间对固定掩码预算的风险最优分配。我们的定理刻画了建模的角色加权残差风险的最优性，同时通过实验评估了下游的生成性能。

Under matched architecture, training budget, and sampling compute, MotifRole-Diff improves validity on QM9 from 0.905 to 0.944 while reducing FCD from 1.701 to 1.609, and on MOSES improves validity from 0.920 to 0.938 while reducing FCD from 2.125 to 1.850.
在相同的架构、训练预算和采样计算量下，MotifRole-Diff 将 QM9 数据集上的有效性从 0.905 提高到 0.944，同时将 FCD 从 1.701 降低至 1.609；在 MOSES 数据集上，有效性从 0.920 提高到 0.938，同时将 FCD 从 2.125 降低至 1.850。

Role-wise diagnostics further show improved reconstruction across molecular graph token categories. Together, these matched-compute results indicate that structurally informed corruption is a more effective masking strategy than uniform schedules for serialized molecular graph diffusion.
基于角色的诊断进一步表明，各类分子图标记的重构效果均得到了改善。总之，这些在计算量匹配条件下的结果表明，对于序列化分子图扩散而言，结构感知损坏是一种比统一调度更有效的掩码策略。
