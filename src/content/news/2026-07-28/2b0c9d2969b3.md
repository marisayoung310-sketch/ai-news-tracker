# Cognizant and Anthropic expand their partnership to bring Claude to enterprise clients
# Cognizant 与 Anthropic 扩大合作伙伴关系，将 Claude 引入企业客户

We're expanding our partnership with Cognizant, one of the world's largest technology services companies.
我们正在扩大与全球最大的技术服务公司之一 Cognizant 的合作伙伴关系。

Cognizant uses Claude in the systems it builds and runs for clients across manufacturing, life sciences, insurance, and other industries. With the expansion of our partnership, it’s embedding Claude across its own business and engineering platforms, scaling a Claude-certified workforce as part of its new Frontier Certified workforce model, and becoming a Global Premier Partner in the Claude Partner Network.
Cognizant 在其为制造业、生命科学、保险及其他行业客户构建和运营的系统中使用了 Claude。随着双方合作的深化，Cognizant 正将其业务和工程平台全面嵌入 Claude，通过其全新的“前沿认证”（Frontier Certified）劳动力模型扩大 Claude 认证员工队伍，并成为 Claude 合作伙伴网络中的全球首要合作伙伴（Global Premier Partner）。

Successfully integrating AI into a large enterprise requires knowledge of the company's industry, the systems it already runs on, and the rules it operates under. Cognizant brings that domain context, along with the engineering depth and delivery scale to bring Claude to enterprises worldwide.
将人工智能成功集成到大型企业中，需要深入了解该公司的行业背景、现有运行系统及其运营规则。Cognizant 带来了这些领域知识，结合其工程深度和交付规模，将 Claude 带给全球企业。

### Cognizant builds with Claude
### Cognizant 利用 Claude 进行开发

Cognizant's engineers build with Claude every day, and more than 30,000 associates have completed Claude training.
Cognizant 的工程师每天都在使用 Claude 进行开发，目前已有超过 30,000 名员工完成了 Claude 的相关培训。

Cognizant is embedding Claude across several of its platforms, including Flowsource™, Neuro® AI Engineering, and Neuro® IT Ops. Flowsource, its full-stack engineering platform, now runs Claude Code alongside software engineers in its Spec-Driven Development module. Flowsource directs Claude Code using the specifications, coding standards, and architectural blueprints a project defines, then evaluates the output before production.
Cognizant 正在将其多个平台嵌入 Claude，包括 Flowsource™、Neuro® AI Engineering 和 Neuro® IT Ops。其全栈工程平台 Flowsource 现在在其“规范驱动开发”（Spec-Driven Development）模块中，让 Claude Code 与软件工程师协同工作。Flowsource 利用项目定义的规范、编码标准和架构蓝图来指导 Claude Code，并在投入生产前对输出结果进行评估。

### Cognizant puts Claude to work for clients
### Cognizant 为客户部署 Claude

The company uses what it learns internally to shape how it brings Claude to clients, and that work is already underway. Examples of what its teams built include:
该公司利用内部积累的经验来优化为客户提供 Claude 服务的方式，相关工作已经展开。其团队构建的案例包括：

*   A customer experience portal for a global manufacturer within six months of kickoff.
    为一家全球制造商在启动后六个月内构建了客户体验门户。
*   An agentic contract-intelligence system for a biopharmaceutical company that has helped cut contract review time by up to 40 percent while lifting extraction accuracy above 88 percent in that deployment.
    为一家生物制药公司开发了代理式合同智能系统，在实际部署中将合同审查时间缩短了高达 40%，同时将信息提取准确率提升至 88% 以上。
*   A risk-navigation tool that has helped underwriters evaluate accounts, which once took hours of manual research, in minutes—saving each person roughly eight hours a week in that deployment.
    开发了一款风险导航工具，帮助承保人评估账户。原本需要数小时人工研究的工作现在只需几分钟即可完成，在部署中平均每人每周节省了约八小时的工作时间。

"AI capability is rising faster than enterprises can absorb it, and that gap is the defining problem of this moment," said Ravi Kumar S, Chief Executive Officer of Cognizant. "Our role is to be the bridge. We bring the industry context, the engineering scale and the trust frameworks that use Claude to deliver production outcomes inside the most demanding enterprise environments. This partnership with Anthropic is about doing that for clients who need AI they can rely on, not just experiment with."
“人工智能能力的提升速度超过了企业的吸收速度，这种差距是当前面临的关键问题，”Cognizant 首席执行官 Ravi Kumar S 表示。“我们的角色是成为桥梁。我们带来了行业背景、工程规模和信任框架，利用 Claude 在要求最严苛的企业环境中交付生产成果。与 Anthropic 的这次合作，旨在为那些需要可靠 AI 而非仅仅是进行实验的客户提供支持。”

"Deepening our partnership with Cognizant will help more companies harness AI's growing capability and deploy it in real, practical ways for their businesses," said Daniela Amodei, Co-Founder and President of Anthropic. "From manufacturing to the life sciences, Cognizant is bringing Claude into the everyday work of some of the world's most demanding industries—the kinds of contexts where AI can demonstrate its greatest value for humanity."
“深化与 Cognizant 的合作将帮助更多公司利用 AI 不断增长的能力，并以切实可行的方式将其部署到业务中，”Anthropic 联合创始人兼总裁 Daniela Amodei 表示。“从制造业到生命科学，Cognizant 正在将 Claude 引入全球一些要求最严苛行业的日常工作中——这些场景正是 AI 能为人类展现最大价值的地方。”
