# Samsung’s 27-inch QD-OLED gaming monitor is priced right at $299.99
# 三星 27 英寸 QD-OLED 游戏显示器售价仅为 299.99 美元

The cost of QD-OLED gaming monitors is going down, even as many other PC components are still soaring above their normal prices. If your budget is locked at $300, Samsung’s 27-inch 1440p model costs exactly that much thanks to a recent $50 discount at Amazon. Finding a good QD-OLED monitor under $500 these days is pretty easy, but under $300 is pretty rare. Its G50SF debuted at $499.99, but has spent the past six months bouncing back and forth between $350 and $450.

尽管许多其他电脑组件的价格仍居高不下，但 QD-OLED 游戏显示器的成本正在下降。如果你的预算锁定在 300 美元，得益于亚马逊最近 50 美元的折扣，三星这款 27 英寸 1440p 显示器正好符合你的预算。如今，以低于 500 美元的价格找到一台不错的 QD-OLED 显示器很容易，但低于 300 美元却相当罕见。其 G50SF 型号首发价为 499.99 美元，但在过去六个月里一直在 350 美元到 450 美元之间波动。

Despite being cheaper than Alienware’s awesome $350 AW2726DM and some competitors, the Samsung G50SF doesn’t make a ton of compromises. It has a thin design, variable refresh rate support, and the same fast .03-millisecond response time we expect from these panels. It has a DisplayPort port plus HDMI 2.0 and a headphone jack (most competing monitors have two HDMI ports). Unless you’re keen on getting a monitor that has a 240Hz or faster refresh rate, you may not mind that this one tops out at 180Hz (via DisplayPort), or 144Hz via HDMI.

尽管比 Alienware 出色的 350 美元 AW2726DM 以及其他一些竞品更便宜，但三星 G50SF 并没有做出太多妥协。它拥有纤薄的设计、可变刷新率支持，以及我们对这类面板所期待的 0.03 毫秒快速响应时间。它配备了一个 DisplayPort 接口、一个 HDMI 2.0 接口和一个耳机插孔（大多数竞品显示器通常有两个 HDMI 接口）。除非你执着于 240Hz 或更高的刷新率，否则你可能不会介意它通过 DisplayPort 最高支持 180Hz，或通过 HDMI 支持 144Hz。
