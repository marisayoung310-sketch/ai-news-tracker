# The 3 Best USB Car Chargers for Phones (2026): Anker, DeWalt
# 2026年三款最佳手机车载充电器：Anker、DeWalt

A car charger is not a complicated device. The best USB car charger is the one that charges your phone quickly, does not overheat, allows multiple devices to charge, and remains compatible with whichever USB cable you (or your passengers) happen to have available. It is usually also a trusted brand, with certification to international standards—not a janky gas station charger that'll blow a fuse with an internal short or offer insufficient protection against dust and debris.

车载充电器并不是什么复杂的设备。最好的USB车载充电器应该具备以下特点：充电速度快、不会过热、支持多设备同时充电，并且能兼容你（或你的乘客）手头现有的任何USB线缆。它通常也来自值得信赖的品牌，并拥有国际标准认证，而不是那种在加油站买的劣质充电器——那种产品可能会因为内部短路而烧断保险丝，或者无法提供足够的防尘防杂物保护。

I put car chargers to the test with ammeters and phone apps, and on multi-hour road trips, to make sure each one lived up to its promises. The best car charger I've found is the three-port Anker 167.5W Nano ($38), which combines 100-watt fast-charging capability for auxiliary devices, plus dual-port USB-Cs, USB-A capability, and safety features that include overheat protection.

我通过电流表、手机应用程序以及长达数小时的公路旅行对这些车载充电器进行了测试，以确保每一款产品都能兑现其承诺。我发现最好的车载充电器是三接口的 Anker 167.5W Nano（售价38美元），它结合了为辅助设备提供的100瓦快充能力，外加双USB-C接口、USB-A接口，以及包括过热保护在内的多项安全功能。

If you shun dangling cords, I offer the Anker 75W Fast Charger ($30) with slightly lower top amperage but a retractable cable. And for families whose kids can't live without constant tablets in the back seat, there's the DeWalt 72W Front and Back Seat Charger ($45).

如果你讨厌垂落的线缆，我推荐 Anker 75W 快充充电器（售价30美元），它的最高电流稍低，但配有伸缩式线缆。而对于那些后座孩子离不开平板电脑的家庭，则可以选择 DeWalt 72W 前后座充电器（售价45美元）。

### The Best USB Car Charger: Anker 167.5W Nano
### 最佳USB车载充电器：Anker 167.5W Nano

This three-port charger from Anker offers pretty much everything I want from a USB car charger while remaining hearteningly compact and affordable. The 100W fast-charging USB-C port kicks out nearly 7 amps at top speeds, enough juice to charge my Macbook Air to 50 percent in about 25 minutes, before fast-charging slows down, and does the same for my iPhone. But especially, Anker's PowerIQ technology smartly assesses the differing voltage needs of various phones to avoid over- or under-voltage. I've never actually seen this Anker on sale on Amazon above $40.

这款来自 Anker 的三接口充电器几乎满足了我对车载充电器的所有需求，同时保持了令人欣慰的紧凑体积和实惠价格。其100W快充USB-C接口在最高速度下可输出近7安培电流，足以在约25分钟内将我的 Macbook Air 充电至50%（随后快充速度会放缓），对我的 iPhone 也能实现同样的效果。特别值得一提的是，Anker 的 PowerIQ 技术能智能评估不同手机的电压需求，从而避免过压或欠压。事实上，我从未在亚马逊上看到这款 Anker 充电器的售价超过40美元。

It also doesn't hurt that Anker makes some of WIRED's most trusted portable power technology—tech whose reliability we've verified over years of real-world testing. You don't overwhelmingly want a janky cheap charger that blows a fuse. Good insulation and a tiny built-in fan effectively lower overheating versus other fast-chargers, though you'll still notice some heat when charging at 100 watts or above. USB-A capability is welcome for versatility, though my testing showed somewhat lower power than advertised on that port.

此外，Anker 生产了一些 WIRED 最信赖的便携电源技术，这些技术的可靠性已通过我们多年的实际测试验证。你肯定不希望买到一个会烧断保险丝的劣质充电器。良好的绝缘性能和一个微型内置风扇能有效降低过热现象，尽管在100瓦或更高功率充电时，你仍会感觉到一些热量。USB-A接口增加了设备的通用性，尽管我的测试显示该接口的实际功率略低于广告宣传值。

### Best USB Car Charger With Retractable Cord: Anker 75W Fast Charger
### 最佳带伸缩线USB车载充电器：Anker 75W 快充充电器

A retractable charging cable has the distinct merit of keeping your car tidy, without dangling charging cords to get tangled between your foot and the gas pedal. It also obviates the need to remember a cable at all. This Anker model offers a 2-foot cable that retracts quite easily back into its base after a quick tug on the cable, neat as you'd like. The cable's 45W fast-charging capability will max out the charging capability of most iPhones.

伸缩式充电线有一个显著的优点，那就是能保持车内整洁，不会有垂落的充电线缠绕在你的脚和油门踏板之间。它还让你无需再专门携带充电线。这款 Anker 型号提供了一根2英尺长的线缆，只需轻轻一拉，就能轻松收回底座，非常利落。该线缆的45W快充能力足以达到大多数 iPhone 的充电上限。

Anker is a brand I trust for charging technology. But it's worth noting that retractable cables introduce a couple failure points that could lower longevity. The cable can't be swapped out if it gets worn, and the retraction mechanism itself could wear out. Tidiness has a price, it seems. This said, the charger is not overly expensive, usually retailing closer to $20.

Anker 是我在充电技术领域信赖的品牌。但值得注意的是，伸缩式线缆引入了几个可能降低使用寿命的故障点。如果线缆磨损，无法更换，且伸缩机构本身也可能损坏。看来，整洁是有代价的。话虽如此，这款充电器并不算太贵，零售价通常在20美元左右。
