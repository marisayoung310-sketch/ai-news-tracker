# Runlayer, Rippling drop lawsuits — but the brouhaha is still a cautionary tale for founders
# Runlayer 与 Rippling 撤销诉讼——但这起风波仍是创始人们的前车之鉴

On Wednesday night, Runlayer and Rippling dropped their respective lawsuits against each other. No settlement was made. No money changed hands. Not even lawyers’ fees, according to court documents seen by TechCrunch.
周三晚上，Runlayer 和 Rippling 分别撤销了针对对方的诉讼。双方未达成和解，没有发生任何资金往来。据 TechCrunch 查阅的法庭文件显示，甚至连律师费都没有支付。

Rippling celebrated by instantly releasing its MCP gateway, the product at the heart of the dueling lawsuits and the one that competes with Runlayer’s offering. This public fight is a cautionary tale to founders: In the age of AI, when building new software has become almost trivial, you never know who your next competitor will be. It might even be a prospective customer.
Rippling 随后以发布其 MCP 网关作为“庆祝”，该产品正是双方诉讼的核心，也是与 Runlayer 产品形成竞争的产品。这场公开的争斗给创始人们敲响了警钟：在人工智能时代，构建新软件已变得几乎轻而易举，你永远不知道你的下一个竞争对手会是谁。它甚至可能就是你的一位潜在客户。

To recap the short-lived legal brouhaha: Runlayer is an early-stage startup that launched out of stealth in November 2025 and has raised a total of $42 million from VCs like Khosla Ventures’ Keith Rabois and Felicis. It’s led by third-time founder Andrew Berman (previous companies include baby-monitor maker Nanit and an AI video conferencing tool, Vowel, that sold to Zapier in 2024).
回顾这场短暂的法律风波：Runlayer 是一家处于早期阶段的初创公司，于 2025 年 11 月结束隐身状态并正式发布，已从 Khosla Ventures 的 Keith Rabois 和 Felicis 等风投机构筹集了总计 4200 万美元的资金。该公司由三次创业的创始人 Andrew Berman 领导（他之前的公司包括婴儿监视器制造商 Nanit，以及在 2024 年被 Zapier 收购的 AI 视频会议工具 Vowel）。

After Rippling tested Runlayer’s MCP gateway for more than a year, with the two engineering teams working closely together, Rippling never signed on to become a customer, according to Runlayer’s lawsuit. Instead, Berman received a text from a Rippling employee that said his employer was building its own MCP gateway and planned to release it as a product. This employee described Rippling’s product as a clone of Runlayer’s. Runlayer sued, claiming that Rippling violated contractual agreements covering the tests of its products.
根据 Runlayer 的诉讼，在 Rippling 对 Runlayer 的 MCP 网关进行了超过一年的测试，且双方工程团队密切合作之后，Rippling 最终并未签约成为客户。相反，Berman 收到了一名 Rippling 员工的短信，称其雇主正在构建自己的 MCP 网关，并计划将其作为产品发布。该员工形容 Rippling 的产品是 Runlayer 产品的克隆版。Runlayer 随后提起诉讼，声称 Rippling 违反了涵盖其产品测试的合同协议。

An MCP gateway securely handles an enterprise’s AI agent requests for data from other software systems. So, for instance, when a hiring professional asks for details on the top five candidates for a job, including their emails, that data must be retrieved from the company’s recruitment system. The gateway handles the retrieval process, rather than granting agents direct access to the company’s software systems. It can then also layer on other features like employee role-based access control (managers getting different access than interns), observability (logs and usage trails), and so on.
MCP 网关可以安全地处理企业 AI 代理从其他软件系统获取数据的请求。例如，当招聘专员询问某职位的五位候选人详情（包括电子邮件）时，这些数据必须从公司的招聘系统中检索。网关负责处理检索过程，而不是让 AI 代理直接访问公司的软件系统。它还可以叠加其他功能，如基于员工角色的访问控制（经理与实习生拥有不同的访问权限）、可观测性（日志和使用轨迹）等。

Then Rippling countersued, alleging that Runlayer was violating some of its patents. The move was seen by Runlayer as a way to induce it to drop its suit while ratcheting up legal expenses. Runlayer dropped its suit after spending the last three weeks in discovery. Rippling dropped its own suit and didn’t collect a settlement either.
随后，Rippling 提起反诉，指控 Runlayer 侵犯了其部分专利。Runlayer 认为此举是为了诱使其撤诉，同时增加其法律支出。在经历了过去三周的证据开示程序后，Runlayer 撤回了诉讼。Rippling 也撤回了自己的诉讼，且并未获得任何和解金。

So, while the lawsuits didn’t lead to anything but a lot of public flaming, there is a deeper takeaway for founders. The AI landscape is changing so rapidly that the long-running technical shoot-outs that enterprises love to impose on startups need to be rethought. Between the time an AI startup enters into one and however many months later, an enterprise’s needs and desires may drastically change.
因此，虽然这些诉讼除了引发大量公开的口水战外没有产生任何结果，但对于创始人来说，有一个更深层次的启示。AI 领域变化如此之快，以至于企业喜欢强加给初创公司的长期技术“拉锯战”需要被重新审视。从 AI 初创公司进入这种合作关系到几个月后，企业的需求和愿望可能会发生剧烈变化。

In the meantime, in the span of weeks, Rippling, whose bread and butter has historically been payroll and benefits management, has now entered the AI gateway market with a tool that can route to different models while dashboarding token spend by employee. The product is competing with the likes of Stripe, Ramp, and Databricks. Now Rippling is also in the AI security business with this MCP gateway that ties AI access to employee roles. It competes with the likes of Runlayer, Docker, and Amazon Bedrock.
与此同时，在短短几周内，一直以薪资和福利管理为核心业务的 Rippling，现已进入 AI 网关市场，推出了一款既能路由到不同模型，又能按员工仪表盘显示 Token 消耗的工具。该产品正在与 Stripe、Ramp 和 Databricks 等公司竞争。现在，Rippling 还凭借这款将 AI 访问权限与员工角色挂钩的 MCP 网关进入了 AI 安全领域，与 Runlayer、Docker 和 Amazon Bedrock 等公司展开竞争。

As for Runlayer, its pitch is a broader bundle of agent security services tied to the gateway, ranging from agent creation to spotting shadow AI agents running in an enterprise unbeknownst to IT.
至于 Runlayer，其卖点是与网关绑定的更广泛的代理安全服务组合，范围涵盖从代理创建到识别企业内部 IT 部门不知情的“影子 AI 代理”。
