# Linkdaze’s smart calendar is built to run a household, not just track a schedule
# Linkdaze 智能日历：专为家庭管理打造，而不只是记录日程

With back-to-school season approaching (or already here in some places), keeping track of everyone’s schedules can get pretty chaotic. Between work, school, appointments, sports, chores, and everything else going on, a regular paper calendar just doesn’t cut it. That’s where Linkdaze’s smart digital calendar comes in — a touchscreen tablet built specifically to organize a household rather than a single person.

随着返校季的临近（或在某些地区已经开始），追踪每个人的日程安排可能会变得非常混乱。在工作、学校、预约、体育活动、家务以及其他各种琐事之间，普通的纸质日历显然已经不够用了。这就是 Linkdaze 智能数字日历的用武之地——它是一款专门为家庭管理而非个人使用而设计的触摸屏平板电脑。

One of Linkdaze’s biggest strengths is its calendar compatibility. The system can synchronize calendars from popular services, including Google, iCloud, Outlook, Yahoo, and Cozi, which is a dedicated family-organizing app. This is particularly useful for families where different members use different platforms. Instead of asking everyone to switch to a single calendar app, Linkdaze brings multiple schedules together and uses color coding to make individual family members easy to identify.

Linkdaze 的一大优势在于其日历兼容性。该系统可以同步来自主流服务的日历，包括 Google、iCloud、Outlook、Yahoo 以及专门的家庭组织应用 Cozi。这对于家庭成员使用不同平台的情况特别有用。Linkdaze 无需强求每个人都切换到同一个日历应用，而是将多个日程整合在一起，并通过颜色编码让用户轻松识别每位家庭成员的安排。

Launched last December, Linkdaze is available in 15.6-inch and 10.1-inch models, giving you some flexibility depending on how much wall space you have. Beyond calendars and appointments, you can use it for chores and rewards, meal planning, shopping lists, and other family organization. It can even double as a digital photo frame for displaying family photos.

Linkdaze 于去年 12 月推出，提供 15.6 英寸和 10.1 英寸两种型号，您可以根据墙面空间的大小灵活选择。除了日历和预约功能外，您还可以将其用于家务分配与奖励、膳食规划、购物清单以及其他家庭管理事务。它甚至可以作为数字相框，用来展示家庭照片。

The most interesting feature, however, is Linkdaze’s AI meal planner with “Snap-to-Sync.” Instead of manually entering everything into a meal-planning app, you can take a photo of a paper recipe or your kid’s school lunch menu. Linkdaze will turn that information into a digital meal plan and generate a shopping list from it. While not an entirely new idea, it’s a useful feature that helps Linkdaze stand out from a basic digital calendar.

然而，最有趣的功能是 Linkdaze 的 AI 膳食规划器，它配备了“拍照同步”（Snap-to-Sync）功能。您无需手动将所有内容输入到膳食规划应用中，只需拍一张纸质食谱或孩子学校午餐菜单的照片，Linkdaze 就能将这些信息转化为数字膳食计划，并据此生成购物清单。虽然这并非全新的概念，但这一实用功能确实让 Linkdaze 从基础的数字日历中脱颖而出。

Another big plus is that Linkdaze doesn’t require a monthly subscription for its main features. It’s an interesting choice in a category where recurring revenue has become the default. Skylight, a competing smart-calendar brand, offers additional features through its $79 per month subscription. For a hardware company entering a crowded smart-display market, that decision is either going to differentiate its product or look like a lost revenue stream. Linkdaze is also less expensive up front, with the 10.1-inch model priced at $119.99 compared with Skylight’s 10-inch model at $159.99.

另一个巨大的优势是，Linkdaze 的主要功能无需每月订阅。在循环收入已成为行业默认模式的领域，这是一个有趣的策略。竞争对手智能日历品牌 Skylight 通过每月 79 美元的订阅费提供额外功能。对于一家进入拥挤的智能显示器市场的硬件公司来说，这一决定要么能让其产品实现差异化，要么会被视为错失了收入来源。此外，Linkdaze 的前期购买成本也更低，10.1 英寸型号售价为 119.99 美元，而 Skylight 的 10 英寸型号售价为 159.99 美元。

Overall, this device could make a practical gift for busy parents who are trying to keep everyone’s schedules in one place. It could also be a great fit for college apartments, where roommates can use it to coordinate chores, study schedules, shared meals, and other household responsibilities. It’s also very helpful for those of us juggling juggling interviews, deadlines, meetings, and story assignments.

总的来说，对于那些试图将全家人日程集中管理的忙碌父母来说，这款设备是一份实用的礼物。它也非常适合大学宿舍，室友们可以用它来协调家务、学习计划、共享餐食以及其他生活责任。对于我们这些需要同时处理采访、截止日期、会议和稿件任务的人来说，它也非常有帮助。
