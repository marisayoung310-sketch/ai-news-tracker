# Xbox prices are increasing by up to €200 or £170
# Xbox 价格最高上涨 200 欧元或 170 英镑

When Microsoft announced its latest round of Xbox price bumps in June, it only gave US pricing. Now we know the pricing increases for the EU and UK, and they’re dramatic. Depending on the model, Xbox prices are increasing by up to €200 or £170. 
当微软在 6 月宣布最新一轮 Xbox 涨价时，仅公布了美国市场的价格。现在我们知道了欧盟和英国的涨价幅度，情况相当惊人。根据机型不同，Xbox 的价格最高上涨了 200 欧元或 170 英镑。

The 1TB Xbox Series X with a disc drive is going up by over 30 percent from £499.99 / €599.99 to £669.99 / €799.99. The 512GB Xbox Series S is getting a price increase of over 43 percent, jumping from £299.99 / €349.99 to £429.99 / €499.99.
配备光驱的 1TB Xbox Series X 价格上涨超过 30%，从 499.99 英镑 / 599.99 欧元涨至 669.99 英镑 / 799.99 欧元。512GB Xbox Series S 的涨幅则超过 43%，从 299.99 英镑 / 349.99 欧元跃升至 429.99 英镑 / 499.99 欧元。

While other consoles have received price bumps as RAM and storage prices skyrocket due to demand from the AI industry, none have been quite as dramatic as Microsoft’s. This is the third round of price increases since May of 2025, with the highest-end model now costing $300 more than its launch price of $499.99 in 2020. The 2TB model is also being retired in the face of the storage crunch. 
尽管由于人工智能行业的需求导致内存和存储价格飙升，其他游戏主机也进行了涨价，但没有哪一款像微软这样涨幅如此剧烈。这是自 2025 年 5 月以来的第三轮涨价，最高端机型的价格现已比 2020 年 499.99 美元的首发价高出 300 美元。面对存储芯片短缺，2TB 机型也将停产。
