# The Best Kids’ Backpacks for the 2026 School Year
# 2026学年最佳儿童书包推荐

As an adult gear tester, I have one major problem with looking for the best kids’ backpacks: Ultimately, the decision is not up to me. My 8-year-old and 10-year-old are the ones who have to carry their backpacks to school, camp, and beyond. The bag has to protect iPads and Chromebooks; fit lunch boxes and water bottles; and endure getting kicked around cars, bikes, and school gyms. Most importantly, it has to be comfortable. Less important but still crucial, it has to be a good fashion accessory. And you have to be able to find it quickly when you hang it on a hook next to 50 virtually identical backpacks.

作为一名成年装备测试员，我在寻找最佳儿童书包时面临一个主要问题：归根结底，决定权不在我。我的两个孩子（8岁和10岁）才是真正需要背着书包去学校、夏令营以及其他地方的人。书包必须能保护好iPad和Chromebook；能装下饭盒和水壶；还要经得起在汽车、自行车和学校体育馆里被踢来踢去。最重要的是，它必须背着舒适。虽然没那么重要但依然关键的是，它还得是一个时尚的配饰。而且，当它挂在挂钩上，旁边有50个几乎一模一样的书包时，你必须能一眼认出它来。

To find the best kids’ backpacks, children of WIRED’s Reviews team tested the following for weeks, if not years, by stuffing them into lockers and under plane seats. Don’t forget to check out the rest of our parenting coverage, which includes the Best Laptops for College Students, the Best Paper Planners, and the Best Subscription Boxes for Kids.

为了找到最好的儿童书包，WIRED评测团队的孩子们对以下产品进行了数周甚至数年的测试，包括将它们塞进储物柜和飞机座椅下方。别忘了查看我们其他的育儿专题报道，其中包括《大学生最佳笔记本电脑》、《最佳纸质日程本》以及《最佳儿童订阅礼盒》。

### Best Overall: L. L. Bean Deluxe Book Pack
### 最佳综合奖：L. L. Bean Deluxe Book Pack

My parents bought me a new JanSport backpack every fall, but I’ve taken a different approach with my daughter. She got three years out of her pint-sized Deuter and is now entering her second year with this L.L.Bean pack, in her favorite shade of teal, which is embroidered with her name.

我父母每年秋天都会给我买一个新的JanSport书包，但我对我女儿采取了不同的做法。她之前那个小巧的Deuter书包用了三年，现在她正背着这个L.L.Bean书包进入第二年，这是她最喜欢的蓝绿色，上面还绣着她的名字。

This is a large pack, able to hold more heavy stuff than a fourth grader has any business carrying. It’s got a mesh side pocket for a water bottle and a couple of flat zippered pockets to hold things you want to separate from the main compartment. What we both like about it (other than the embroidered name) is that it’s incredibly sturdy without looking overbuilt. After a trip through the washer to clean out the past year’s spilled yogurt and stains from capless markers, it’s basically back to its original condition. —Martin Cizmar

这是一个大容量书包，能装下的重物远超四年级学生所能承受的范围。它有一个侧面网兜用于放置水壶，还有几个扁平的拉链袋，可以存放你想与主隔层分开的物品。我们俩都喜欢它的一点（除了绣着名字之外）是，它非常结实，但看起来又不会显得过于笨重。经过洗衣机的清洗，去除了过去一年里洒出的酸奶和无盖马克笔留下的污渍后，它基本恢复到了最初的状态。——Martin Cizmar

### Best Upgrade: State Bags Kane Double Pocket Backpack
### 最佳升级奖：State Bags Kane Double Pocket Backpack

When my son found out that my daughter was going to get her own tester State backpack to match her State carry-on suitcase, he immediately demanded his own. State is known for its fantastical matching patterns that extend from luggage and backpacks to lunch bags ($48). Its products are pricey, but the kid bags are not worse in quality or fewer in features than the adult bags, which I appreciate. It makes sense, considering kids are often harder on their bags than adults are on theirs.

当我的儿子发现我女儿要得到一个State书包来搭配她的State登机箱时，他立刻要求也要一个。State以其梦幻般的配套图案而闻名，从行李箱、书包一直延伸到午餐包（48美元）。它的产品价格不菲，但我很欣赏的一点是，儿童书包在质量和功能上并不比成人款逊色。考虑到孩子们对待书包的方式通常比成年人粗鲁得多，这很有道理。

The Kane kids backpacks come in several sizes. Now that my kids are 8 and 10 years old, they each have the largest double-pocket backpack, which has two separate compartments so that they can separate their dirty clothes or muddy shoes from delicate electronics. Its 23.6-liter capacity can fit up to a 15-inch laptop and is more than capacious enough for a Chromebook, sweatshirt, Omie lunch box, and mini Nalgene water bottle.

Kane儿童书包有多种尺寸。现在我的孩子分别是8岁和10岁，他们每人都拥有最大的双口袋书包，它有两个独立的隔层，这样他们就可以把脏衣服或泥泞的鞋子与精密的电子产品隔开。其23.6升的容量可以容纳一台15英寸的笔记本电脑，对于Chromebook、运动衫、Omie午餐盒和迷你Nalgene水壶来说绰绰有余。

### Best Heavy-Duty: GoRuck KR1
### 最佳耐用奖：GoRuck KR1

I know what you’re thinking, a GoRuck for kids—how much is that going to set me back? Version 2.0 of GoRuck’s KR1 kids pack is actually not that expensive at $65. Yes, that’s more than some on this page, but this is also an extremely tough backpack made of 1000D cordura (except the part against the back, which 200D to be easier on clothes) just like the GoRuck packs for adults. My son has had version one of the KR1 for almost four years now and it looks like it did the day he got it.

我知道你在想什么：给孩子买个GoRuck书包，得花多少钱？GoRuck KR1儿童书包2.0版本其实并不贵，只要65美元。是的，这比本页上的其他一些书包要贵，但它也是一款极其坚固的书包，采用1000D Cordura面料制成（除了背部接触部分采用200D以保护衣物），就像GoRuck的成人书包一样。我儿子用第一代KR1已经快四年了，看起来和刚买那天一样新。

As with any GoRuck, the shoulder straps are extra padded and won’t cut into your kids’ shoulders when they load it up with books. There’s an internal sleeve to separate out a tablet, but the space there is a little small for a laptop. The top loop handle is sturdy and makes it easy to carry it around without throwing it over a shoulder, and there’s plenty of velcro for patches if your kid is into those. —Scott Gilbertson

和所有GoRuck产品一样，它的肩带经过加厚处理，当孩子装满书时，不会勒伤他们的肩膀。内部有一个隔层可以放置平板电脑，但空间对于笔记本电脑来说有点小。顶部的提手非常结实，方便手提，无需背在肩上。如果你的孩子喜欢贴章，上面还有足够的魔术贴空间。——Scott Gilbertson
