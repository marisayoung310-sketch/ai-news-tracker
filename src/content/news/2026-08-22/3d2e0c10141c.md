# The Types of Dimensions in a Star Schema, and How to Use Them
# 星型模型中的维度类型及其使用方法

Dimensions are one of the two main object types in dimensional modelling. But what are the different types of dimensions? And how can you use them?
维度是维度建模中两种主要对象类型之一。但维度有哪些不同的类型？你又该如何使用它们呢？

### A short Intro to Dimensional modelling
### 维度建模简介

To begin, here is a short introduction to what a Star Schema is and what dimensional modelling is. A Star schema can be best explained with the following diagram:
首先，简单介绍一下什么是星型模型（Star Schema）以及什么是维度建模。星型模型可以通过下图进行最好的解释：

[Figure 1 – The principle of a Star Schema and of Dimensional modelling]
（图 1 – 星型模型与维度建模的原理）

You can see at first sight why it is called “Star Schema”. A Star Schema consists of two object types:
你可以一眼看出它为什么被称为“星型模型”。星型模型由两种对象类型组成：

**The Fact table(s)**
Fact tables describe business processes. More precisely, they contain information and numbers that describe events in business processes. They can be: Sales Transactions, Bank Transactions, Stock levels, HR Data (like salaries or Headcounts), Data about events like the entrance of a person into a building, etc.
**事实表 (Fact tables)**
事实表描述业务流程。更准确地说，它们包含描述业务流程中事件的信息和数值。它们可以是：销售交易、银行交易、库存水平、人力资源数据（如工资或员工人数）、关于人员进入建筑物等事件的数据等。

**The Dimensions**
These tables contain descriptive data, like: The Date of the transaction, Information about the customer, Accounts, Products, The geographical information about the transaction, etc.
**维度 (Dimensions)**
这些表包含描述性数据，例如：交易日期、客户信息、账户、产品、关于交易的地理信息等。

Because there are multiple Dimensions, this modelling approach is also called Dimensional data modelling. But the term “Star Schema” is easier to understand, and it is used much more.
由于存在多个维度，这种建模方法也被称为维度数据建模。但“星型模型”这个术语更容易理解，使用也更为广泛。

This modelling approach has multiple benefits: Easy to understand and use by the business user, has a clear separation between transactional and descriptive data, can cover complex business scenarios, and is widely used when building reporting models. It can be considered the de facto standard for modelling approaches in data analysis and reporting scenarios.
这种建模方法具有多种优势：易于业务用户理解和使用，清晰地分离了交易数据和描述性数据，能够覆盖复杂的业务场景，并且在构建报表模型时被广泛使用。它可以被视为数据分析和报表场景中建模方法的实际标准。

One can say a Dimension is easy to define, as I did above. But dimensions come in multiple types. Let’s explore them.
正如我上面所说，维度很容易定义。但维度有多种类型，让我们来探讨一下。

### The main types of Dimensions
### 维度的主要类型

There are three types of dimensions:
维度主要有三种类型：

*   **Regular dimensions:** For example, customer, product or geography.
*   **Junk Dimensions:** These mix different entities into one dimension instead of having multiple small dimensions.
*   **Degenerate Dimensions:** These are pieces of information that change frequently, such as a transaction number.
*   **常规维度 (Regular dimensions)：** 例如客户、产品或地理位置。
*   **垃圾维度 (Junk Dimensions)：** 将不同的实体混合到一个维度中，而不是拥有多个小维度。
*   **退化维度 (Degenerate Dimensions)：** 指那些频繁变化的信息片段，例如交易编号。

### Regular Dimensions
### 常规维度

Regular Dimensions are the most common type of Dimensions. They contain information to describe a Business Entity. An Example of such an Entity is the Customer. A Regular Entity follows these rules: Each row explains one occurrence of an entity; Each row is uniquely identifiable; The Data is denormalised.
常规维度是最常见的维度类型。它们包含描述业务实体的信息。此类实体的一个例子是“客户”。常规实体遵循以下规则：每一行解释实体的一次出现；每一行都是唯一可标识的；数据是反规范化的。

Denormalised data contains duplication of information (As opposed to normalised data in the 3rd normal form). In addition, a regular dimension contains: Hierarchies, Additional Attributes, Information about the historization of the occurrence.
反规范化数据包含信息的重复（与第三范式中的规范化数据相反）。此外，常规维度还包含：层级、附加属性、关于该实体出现历史的信息。

### Junk Dimensions
### 垃圾维度

This is a special type of dimension. Imagine the following scenario: Your data model contains several business entities with only a small number of rows (fewer than 10 rows). You end up with many dimensions that can confuse users. You can group them into a smaller number of so-called “Junk Dimensions”.
这是一种特殊的维度类型。想象以下场景：你的数据模型包含几个只有少量行（少于 10 行）的业务实体。最终你会得到许多可能让用户感到困惑的维度。你可以将它们分组为数量较少的所谓“垃圾维度”。

Another example of a junk dimension is a Measure dimension. You can use such a dimension to let the user select which measure to display in the report. But be careful not to group smaller dimensions into the same junk dimension when both should be selectable to generate a result.
垃圾维度的另一个例子是度量维度。你可以使用此类维度让用户选择在报表中显示哪些度量。但要注意，当两个较小的维度都应该可被选择以生成结果时，不要将它们合并到同一个垃圾维度中。

### Degenerated Dimensions
### 退化维度

A degenerated dimension is a dimension that has a direct relation to each transaction in the fact table. A common example of a degenerated dimension is a transaction number. You can have millions of transactions, which wouldn’t make sense to store in a dimension table. Usually, such information is stored in the fact table alongside the transaction values.
退化维度是与事实表中的每笔交易有直接关系的维度。退化维度的常见例子是交易编号。你可能有数百万笔交易，将它们存储在维度表中是没有意义的。通常，此类信息与交易数值一起存储在事实表中。
