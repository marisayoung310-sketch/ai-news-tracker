# Kagi added a setting for removing paywalled links from search results

### August 21st, 2026 - A new Stocks widget and a better everyday Assistant experience
2026年8月21日 - 全新股票小组件与更出色的日常助手体验

#### Kagi Search: Bringing Stocks up to speed
Kagi 搜索：股票功能升级

We've revamped our Stocks widget. It should appear more often when you need it. It can now display information about exchange-traded funds in addition to stocks. Most importantly, it now features a price chart, with animations between time windows that instantly contextualize how big the price fluctuations you're seeing are compared to the wider story. As well, we've added a setting for removing paywalled links from search results automatically.
我们重构了股票小组件，它现在会在你需要时更频繁地出现。除了股票外，它现在还能显示交易所交易基金（ETF）的信息。最重要的是，它新增了价格走势图，并在不同时间窗口切换时提供动画效果，让你能直观地对比当前价格波动与整体趋势。此外，我们还增加了一项设置，可自动从搜索结果中移除付费墙链接。

#### Kagi Assistant: Everyday use just got smoother
Kagi 助手：日常使用更加流畅

**Richer messages:** User messages now render links, Markdown, and LaTeX.
**更丰富的信息：** 用户消息现在支持渲染链接、Markdown 和 LaTeX。

**More powerful search:** Search across all your threads, sort by recency or alphabetically, and start with `/` to filter by folder.
**更强大的搜索：** 支持搜索所有对话记录，按时间或字母顺序排序，并可通过 `/` 开头来按文件夹筛选。

**More control with calmer settings:** Now you can choose whether temporary threads stick around for 24h, 7 or 30 days. All within a calmer, easier-to-scan settings experience.
**更从容的设置控制：** 你现在可以选择临时对话保留 24 小时、7 天或 30 天。所有这些都在更简洁、更易于浏览的设置界面中完成。

### July 30th, 2026 - Kagi Assistant on the go and design refinements for Search
2026年7月30日 - Kagi 助手移动端发布及搜索设计优化

#### Announcing the official Kagi Assistant apps
发布 Kagi 助手官方应用

Kagi Assistant is now available as a native app for iOS and Android! Ask a question, explore the web, work with files, conduct in-depth research, or choose from leading AI models, all from your phone. Your threads and Custom Assistants stay with you, so you can pick up wherever you left off. These are the first steps towards delivering a fantastic Kagi Assistant experience on mobile, with much more to come.
Kagi 助手现已推出 iOS 和 Android 原生应用！你可以通过手机提问、浏览网页、处理文件、进行深度研究或选择领先的 AI 模型。你的对话记录和自定义助手会同步保存，让你随时随地从上次中断的地方继续。这是我们在移动端提供卓越 Kagi 助手体验的第一步，未来还会有更多功能。

#### Report responses directly from Kagi Assistant
直接在 Kagi 助手内举报回复

You can now report an assistant response without leaving the conversation. Hover over any assistant message and select the thumbs-down button to open the feedback form, where you can report issues for reasons ranging from UI bugs to harmful content. Note that when you submit a report, the full thread is shared with Kagi for review. The report and its associated copy of the thread are automatically deleted from Kagi’s review records after 30 days.
你现在无需离开对话即可举报助手的回复。将鼠标悬停在任何助手消息上并点击“踩”（thumbs-down）按钮即可打开反馈表单，你可以举报从 UI 错误到有害内容等各种问题。请注意，提交举报时，完整对话将共享给 Kagi 进行审核。举报内容及其关联的对话副本将在 30 天后自动从 Kagi 的审核记录中删除。

#### Export or delete all your threads
导出或删除所有对话

We've also added important controls, so you can now export all your threads or permanently delete them at once from Settings > General.
我们还增加了重要的控制选项，你现在可以在“设置 > 常规”中一次性导出所有对话或将其永久删除。

#### Kagi Search: A sharper search experience
Kagi 搜索：更清晰的搜索体验

We’ve polished the search results page to make its controls easier to find and understand. From the filter bar to domain-related options and menus, these updates bring greater clarity and ease of use to the features you rely on most.
我们优化了搜索结果页面，使控件更易于查找和理解。从筛选栏到域名相关选项和菜单，这些更新为你最常用的功能带来了更高的清晰度和易用性。

#### Exchange rates, right in your search results
搜索结果直接显示汇率

Next up in our broader effort to improve search widgets: currency conversion. Comes handy when you’re planning a trip, shopping abroad, or simply want to keep tabs on exchange rates.
作为我们改进搜索小组件计划的一部分，接下来是货币转换功能。无论是在规划旅行、海外购物，还是仅仅想关注汇率时，它都非常实用。
