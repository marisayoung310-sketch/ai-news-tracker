# A Plug-in Interpretation of Conditioning in Score-Based Diffusion Models
# 基于分数的扩散模型中条件机制的插件式解释

**Abstract:** We propose a conditioning mechanism for diffusion models based on multi-speed joint diffusion of the target and the condition. The mechanism learns an unconditional joint score network and enforces conditioning at inference via a plug-in correction term. The plug-in term separates the conditioning contribution from the learned unconditional dynamics, offering a transparent view of how the condition steers generation of the target distribution.

**摘要：** 我们提出了一种基于目标与条件多速联合扩散的扩散模型条件机制。该机制通过学习一个无条件的联合分数网络，并在推理阶段通过一个插件式修正项来强制执行条件约束。该插件项将条件贡献与学习到的无条件动力学分离开来，从而提供了一个透明的视角，展示了条件如何引导目标分布的生成。

Building on this, we derive explicit conditional reverse-time SDEs and approximate probability-flow ODEs, enabling principled and directly comparable conditional samplers. To reduce the induced ODE--SDE discrepancy, we introduce a log-Fokker--Planck residual regularization that improves ODE sampling quality.

在此基础上，我们推导出了显式的条件逆向时间随机微分方程（SDE）和近似概率流常微分方程（ODE），从而实现了有据可依且可直接比较的条件采样器。为了减少由此产生的 ODE-SDE 差异，我们引入了一种对数 Fokker-Planck 残差正则化方法，该方法提升了 ODE 的采样质量。

Experiments on conditional image generation tasks demonstrate competitive performance and support the effectiveness of the plug-in conditioning view. Additional ODE--SDE comparison experiments show that the log-Fokker--Planck residual regularization improves deterministic ODE sampling.

在条件图像生成任务上的实验证明了该方法具有竞争力的性能，并支持了插件式条件视角（plug-in conditioning view）的有效性。额外的 ODE-SDE 对比实验表明，对数 Fokker-Planck 残差正则化改善了确定性 ODE 的采样效果。
