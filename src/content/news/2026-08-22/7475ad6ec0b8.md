# OpenAI Rolls Out Flexible Codex Pricing for Business and Enterprise Teams
# OpenAI 为企业和团队推出灵活的 Codex 定价模式

OpenAI has formally rolled out a more flexible way for organizations to buy and use Codex. Eligible ChatGPT Business and Enterprise workspaces can add Codex-only seats on a pay-as-you-go basis, with usage billed through API-style token consumption rather than a fixed fee for each of those seats.
OpenAI 已正式推出一种更灵活的方式，供组织购买和使用 Codex。符合条件的 ChatGPT Business 和 Enterprise 工作区可以按“即用即付”的方式添加仅限 Codex 使用的席位，其费用通过 API 风格的 Token（令牌）消耗量来结算，而不是为每个席位支付固定费用。

The change matters because it separates AI coding access from a conventional per-user subscription model. A team can provision Codex-only members for development work while managing consumption through credits, rather than treating every user as a full ChatGPT seat.
这一变化意义重大，因为它将 AI 编程访问权限与传统的按用户订阅模式分离开来。团队可以为开发工作配置仅限 Codex 的成员，并通过积分管理消耗量，而不必将每个用户都视为完整的 ChatGPT 席位。

OpenAI also reduced the annual ChatGPT Business seat price from $25 to $20 and introduced promotional credits intended to lower the cost of onboarding new Codex users. OpenAI details the model in its official flexible Codex pricing announcement for teams. The initial announcement was made on April 2, 2026. A June 24, 2026 update clarified that existing pay-as-you-go seats remain in place, while the availability of new Business pay-as-you-go seats may change.
OpenAI 还将 ChatGPT Business 的年度席位价格从 25 美元下调至 20 美元，并推出了旨在降低新 Codex 用户入门成本的促销积分。OpenAI 在其官方的团队灵活 Codex 定价公告中详细介绍了该模式。最初的公告发布于 2026 年 4 月 2 日。2026 年 6 月 24 日的更新澄清称，现有的即用即付席位保持不变，但新的 Business 即用即付席位的可用性可能会发生变化。

### How OpenAI's Codex team pricing works
### OpenAI 的 Codex 团队定价模式如何运作

The central change is the availability of Codex-only seats within Business and Enterprise workspaces. These seats have no fixed seat fee. Instead, their Codex consumption is charged using API-style token rates. OpenAI's approach gives organizations a way to make Codex available to users whose primary need is AI-assisted coding, without requiring the same seat model used for standard ChatGPT access.
核心变化是在 Business 和 Enterprise 工作区内提供了仅限 Codex 的席位。这些席位没有固定费用，其 Codex 消耗量按 API 风格的 Token 费率计费。OpenAI 的这种方法使组织能够为主要需求是 AI 辅助编程的用户提供 Codex，而无需采用标准 ChatGPT 访问所使用的席位模式。

This is not a claim that coding costs are universally lower. A pay-as-you-go model makes expenditure more directly dependent on usage, so the financial effect will vary by team and workload. The documented advantage is billing flexibility: organizations can align Codex costs with token consumption and use credits across supported workspace features where their plan allows it.
这并不意味着编程成本在所有情况下都会降低。即用即付模式使支出更直接地取决于使用量，因此财务影响会因团队和工作负载而异。其记录在案的优势在于计费灵活性：组织可以将 Codex 成本与 Token 消耗量挂钩，并在其计划允许的情况下，在支持的工作区功能中通用积分。

| 入门促销 | 公告中未说明 | 每位新成员 100 美元 Codex 积分，每个团队上限 500 美元 |
| 工作区环境 | ChatGPT Business | 符合条件的 ChatGPT Business 和 Enterprise 工作区 |

### Shared credits change the purchasing model
### 共享积分改变了购买模式

OpenAI's pricing framework also links usage across supported workspace tools. Credits and usage for Codex, ChatGPT Work, ChatGPT for Excel, and related Workspace features can draw from a common pool when that capability is available on the plan. OpenAI Help Center guidance corroborates that credits can work across these supported features and that Codex usage follows API-style token pricing.
OpenAI 的定价框架还将各个受支持的工作区工具的使用情况关联起来。当计划支持该功能时，Codex、ChatGPT Work、ChatGPT for Excel 及相关工作区功能的积分和使用量可以从一个公共池中扣除。OpenAI 帮助中心的指南证实，积分可以在这些受支持的功能之间通用，且 Codex 的使用遵循 API 风格的 Token 定价。

For administrators, that creates a broader budgeting question than the cost of a single coding tool. The same credit pool can cover several AI-enabled workflows, which may simplify purchasing but also means usage in one feature can affect the credits available to another. Organizations will need to understand which features their plan supports and how they want to allocate credits across development and business use cases.
对于管理员来说，这带来了一个比单一编程工具成本更广泛的预算问题。同一个积分池可以覆盖多个 AI 工作流，这可能会简化购买流程，但也意味着在一个功能上的使用可能会影响另一个功能可用的积分。组织需要了解其计划支持哪些功能，以及他们希望如何在开发和业务用例之间分配积分。

### The rollout includes several practical elements:
### 此次发布包含几个实际要素：

*   Codex-only members can be added without fixed seat fees, with consumption billed by token usage.
    *   可以添加仅限 Codex 的成员，无需固定席位费，消耗量按 Token 使用量计费。
*   New Codex-only team members are eligible for $100 in promotional Codex credits, up to $500 per team.
    *   新的仅限 Codex 的团队成员可获得 100 美元的促销 Codex 积分，每个团队上限 500 美元。
*   ChatGPT Business annual seats have a documented price reduction from $25 to $20 per seat.
    *   ChatGPT Business 年度席位价格已明确从每席位 25 美元下调至 20 美元。
*   Supported workspace tools can use a shared credits model, rather than keeping credits isolated to Codex alone.
    *   受支持的工作区工具可以使用共享积分模式，而不是将积分仅局限于 Codex。
*   Existing pay-as-you-go seats remain unaffected by the June update, although new-seat availability for Business may change.
    *   现有的即用即付席位不受 6 月份更新的影响，尽管 Business 的新席位可用性可能会发生变化。

### What developers and enterprise teams should watch
### 开发人员和企业团队应关注什么

For developers, Codex-only seats could make it easier for a company to extend AI coding access to targeted groups. The operational trade-off is that usage-based billing requires closer attention to consumption than a simple fixed-seat model. Teams should account for token use as part of their engineering-tool budget rather than assuming a predictable monthly cost per user.
对于开发人员而言，仅限 Codex 的席位可以使公司更容易向特定群体扩展 AI 编程访问权限。运营方面的权衡是，基于使用的计费方式比简单的固定席位模式需要更密切地关注消耗量。团队应将 Token 使用量视为其工程工具预算的一部分，而不是假设每个用户每月都有可预测的成本。

For Business and Enterprise administrators, the shared-credit design is equally important. Codex spending cannot be viewed entirely in isolation if credits are also used for ChatGPT Work, Excel, or related features. The relevant question becomes how a workspace governs access and usage across its supported AI tools.
对于 Business 和 Enterprise 管理员来说，共享积分设计同样重要。如果积分也用于 ChatGPT Work、Excel 或相关功能，那么 Codex 的支出就不能完全孤立地看待。相关的问题变成了工作区如何管理其受支持的 AI 工具的访问和使用。

OpenAI's June 24 clarification also makes availability worth monitoring. The company states that existing pay-as-you-go seats are unaffected, but it notes that new pay-as-you-go seats for Business may be subject to changing availability. Organizations considering the model should therefore confirm the options visible in their own workspace before planning a broader rollout.
OpenAI 6 月 24 日的澄清也使得可用性值得关注。该公司表示，现有的即用即付席位不受影响，但指出 Business 的新即用即付席位可能会受到可用性变化的影响。因此，考虑该模式的组织在计划更广泛的推广之前，应先确认其工作区中可见的选项。
