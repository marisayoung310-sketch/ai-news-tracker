# Retrieve One Row from a Table, Not the Whole Table: Row-Level Chunks for RAG
# 从表格中检索单行而非整表：RAG 的行级分块策略

**Enterprise Document Intelligence [Vol.1 #7sexies]** – The unit of retrieval doesn’t have to be a page or a paragraph. When the corpus carries tables, each body row with its column headers is a chunk in its own right, and it’s often the one row the reader asked about.
**企业文档智能 [第1卷 #7sexies]** – 检索的单位不一定非得是页面或段落。当语料库中包含表格时，每一行正文及其对应的列标题本身就是一个独立的分块（Chunk），而且这往往就是读者想要查询的那一行。

A car-insurance contract prints its guarantees as a 40-row table: one row per covered event, with columns for the event, its cap, its deductible, and the eligibility condition. A user opens the search bar and asks “what is the cap for vehicle theft?”. The naive retrieval move indexes the whole table as one chunk. When the question matches, the generation model receives 39 rows of unrelated events on top of the one that answers, and has to guess which line the reader meant. Sending the whole table makes the model do the filtering the retriever should have done, and the unit that actually matches the question is one row, not the whole rectangle.
一份汽车保险合同将保障条款印成了一个 40 行的表格：每一行对应一个承保事件，列包括事件名称、赔付上限、免赔额和资格条件。当用户打开搜索栏询问“车辆盗窃的赔付上限是多少？”时，原始的检索方式会将整个表格作为一个分块进行索引。当问题匹配成功时，生成模型不仅会收到那一行答案，还会收到另外 39 行无关的事件，并不得不去猜测读者指的是哪一行。发送整个表格迫使模型去执行本应由检索器完成的过滤工作，而实际上与问题匹配的单位仅仅是一行，而不是整个矩形区域。

本文是《企业文档智能》第二部分的内容，该系列旨在通过四个模块构建企业级 RAG 系统。其中的检索模块将检索视为过滤而非单纯的向量搜索，并行运行关键词和嵌入信号，利用 LLM 仲裁器对最终候选结果进行排序，并通过目录引导长文档的检索。本文作为补充，处理了之前未覆盖的情况：当答案位于表格内部，且符合问题的单位仅为单行时的情况。

### 1. Table vs row: the unit of retrieval
### 1. 表格与行：检索的单位

A table on paper is a bounded region: a rectangle of cells with a header row on top and body rows below. A retrieval system that treats the whole rectangle as one chunk collapses that structure. The chunk either matches (and the generation model receives every row, most of them irrelevant to the question) or it does not (and the generation model receives nothing, because the one relevant row is buried under all the other rows the scorer had to average over).
纸面上的表格是一个有界的区域：一个由顶部标题行和下方正文行组成的矩形单元格区域。如果检索系统将整个矩形视为一个分块，就会破坏这种结构。要么分块匹配成功（导致生成模型收到所有行，其中大部分与问题无关），要么匹配失败（导致生成模型一无所获，因为唯一相关的行被评分器平均化处理后的其他行淹没了）。

The mismatch is between the document’s unit of information (one rectangle) and the reader’s unit of question (one row). A question like “cap for vehicle theft?” asks about one row. A question like “which events are covered?” asks about the whole rectangle. Both are legitimate. A retrieval brick that only offers the rectangle answers the second question and mishandles the first. The fix is not to pick one scale over the other, but to make both scales available and let the dispatcher pick.
这种不匹配源于文档的信息单位（一个矩形）与读者的提问单位（一行）之间的差异。像“车辆盗窃的赔付上限是多少？”这样的问题是在询问某一行；而“哪些事件在承保范围内？”这样的问题则是在询问整个矩形。两者都是合理的。如果检索模块只提供矩形，它能回答第二个问题，却无法处理第一个。解决方法不是二选一，而是同时提供两种尺度，并让调度器进行选择。

### 2. Building the row-level index
### 2. 构建行级索引

Two steps turn a parsed table into a retrievable index: read the shape the parser emits, then serialize each body row into its own chunk.
将解析后的表格转化为可检索索引分为两步：读取解析器输出的结构，然后将每一行正文序列化为独立的分块。

#### 2.1 What the parser already emits
#### 2.1 解析器已有的输出

Docling and Azure Document Intelligence both emit tables as markdown-pipe lines inside `line_df`. A table with 4 body rows becomes 6 lines: a header row, a separator row, and one body row per data row, in reading order. The pipe pattern in text is the signal. The pipe format is the parser’s contract for a table, and what the row-level serializer reads.
Docling 和 Azure Document Intelligence 都会将表格以 Markdown 管道符（pipe）格式输出在 `line_df` 中。一个包含 4 行正文的表格会变成 6 行：一个标题行、一个分隔行，以及按阅读顺序排列的 4 个数据行。文本中的管道符模式就是信号。管道格式是解析器对表格的约定，也是行级序列化器读取的内容。

#### 2.2 One chunk per table row
#### 2.2 每个表格行对应一个分块

The `serialize_table_rows` function turns each body row of every table into a retrievable chunk. A `pandas.DataFrame` in, a `pandas.DataFrame` out, the same shape Article 7B’s retriever uses, so the two bricks compose over the one `line_df`. The whole function is a small scan: group the pipe lines into tables, read the header off the row above the separator, and emit one output row per body line.
`serialize_table_rows` 函数将每个表格的每一行正文转化为一个可检索的分块。输入为 `pandas.DataFrame`，输出也为 `pandas.DataFrame`，其结构与第 7B 篇文章中的检索器一致，因此这两个模块可以在同一个 `line_df` 上组合使用。整个函数是一个简单的扫描过程：将管道行分组为表格，从分隔行上方读取标题，并为每一行正文输出一个结果行。
