# HiRA-CAM: Preserving Fine-Grained Spatial Relevance in Gradient-Based Visual Explanations
# HiRA-CAM：在基于梯度的视觉解释中保留细粒度空间相关性

**Abstract:** Deep Learning models can include billions of parameters or more, making it difficult to explain their internal transformations and outputs. However, explainability is increasing in importance due to the use of AI in crucial applications. 

**摘要：** 深度学习模型可能包含数十亿甚至更多的参数，这使得解释其内部转换和输出变得十分困难。然而，由于人工智能在关键应用中的广泛使用，可解释性正变得日益重要。

This paper focuses on the interpretability of convolutional neural networks (CNNs). Building on the popular gradient based method LayerCAM for extracting internal features in CNNs, we propose an improved method named HiRA-CAM, and show that it outperforms both LayerCAM and Grad-CAM on creating useful saliency maps for object classification. 

本文聚焦于卷积神经网络（CNN）的可解释性。在用于提取 CNN 内部特征的流行梯度方法 LayerCAM 的基础上，我们提出了一种名为 HiRA-CAM 的改进方法，并证明其在为对象分类创建有效的显著性图（saliency maps）方面，表现优于 LayerCAM 和 Grad-CAM。

The main feature of HiRA-CAM is its adaptive use of activation maps from all the layers of the CNN to arrive at a more focused saliency map.

HiRA-CAM 的主要特点是自适应地利用 CNN 所有层的激活图，从而生成更加聚焦的显著性图。
