# Estimating from No Data: Deriving a Continuous Score from Categories
# 从无数据中估算：从类别中推导连续评分

Algebra Estimating from No Data: Deriving a Continuous Score from Categories. A walkthrough of and the maths behind using low-capacity networks to acquire fine-grained scoring when only categorical labelling is available for training. Elod Pal Csirmaz, Aug 21, 2026.

从无数据中估算：从类别中推导连续评分。本文将深入探讨在仅有分类标签可用于训练的情况下，如何利用低容量神经网络获取细粒度评分的数学原理与实践过程。作者：Elod Pal Csirmaz，2026年8月21日。

Imagine that a country has collected data on the outcomes of patients who have acquired “Pathogen A” responsible for an infectious respiratory illness. Available are 8 features of each patient and the outcome: (a) treated at home and recovered, (b) hospitalized and recovered, or (c) died. It has proven trivial to train a neural net to predict one of the three outcomes from the 8 features with almost complete accuracy.

想象一下，一个国家收集了感染了导致传染性呼吸道疾病的“病原体 A”的患者预后数据。现有每位患者的 8 个特征以及预后结果：(a) 在家治疗并康复，(b) 住院并康复，或 (c) 死亡。事实证明，训练一个神经网络根据这 8 个特征以近乎完美的准确率预测这三种结果之一是非常简单的。

However, the health authorities would like to predict something that was not captured: From the patients who can be treated at home, who are the ones who are most at danger of having to go to hospital? And from the patients who are predicted to be hospitalized, who are the ones who are most at danger of not surviving the infection? Can we get a numeric score that represents how serious the infection will be? In this note I will cover a neural net with a bottleneck and a special head to learn a scoring system from a few categories, and cover some properties of small neural networks one is likely to encounter. The accompanying code can be found at https://codeberg.org/csirmaz/category-scoring.

然而，卫生部门希望预测一些未被捕捉到的信息：在可以居家治疗的患者中，哪些人最有可能需要住院？在被预测需要住院的患者中，哪些人最有可能无法从感染中存活下来？我们能否得出一个代表感染严重程度的数值评分？在本文中，我将介绍一种带有瓶颈层和特殊输出头的神经网络，用于从少量类别中学习评分系统，并探讨人们在处理小型神经网络时可能会遇到的一些特性。配套代码可在 https://codeberg.org/csirmaz/category-scoring 获取。

### The dataset
### 数据集

To be able to illustrate the work, I developed a toy example, which is a non-linear but deterministic piece of code calculating the outcome from the 8 features. The calculation is for illustration only — it is not supposed to be faithful to the science; the names of the features used were chosen merely to be in keeping with the medical example. The 8 features used in this note are: Previous infection with Pathogen A (boolean), Previous infection with Pathogen B (boolean), Acute / current infection with Pathogen B (boolean), Cancer diagnosis (boolean), Weight deviation from average (arbitrary unit), Age (years), Blood pressure deviation from average (arbitrary unit), Years smoked.

为了说明这项工作，我开发了一个玩具示例，这是一段非线性但确定性的代码，用于根据 8 个特征计算结果。该计算仅用于说明，并不代表真实的科学依据；所使用的特征名称仅为了符合医学示例。本文使用的 8 个特征包括：既往病原体 A 感染史（布尔值）、既往病原体 B 感染史（布尔值）、急性/当前病原体 B 感染（布尔值）、癌症诊断（布尔值）、体重偏离平均值（任意单位）、年龄（岁）、血压偏离平均值（任意单位）、吸烟年限。

### A classic classifier
### 经典分类器

The data is nonlinear but very neat, and so it is no surprise that a small classifier network can learn it to 98-99% validation accuracy. Launch train.py --classifier to train a simple neural network with 6 layers (each 8 wide) and ReLU activation, defined in ScoringModel.build_classifier_model(). But how to train a scoring system? Our aim is then to train a system that, given the 8 features as inputs, can produce a score corresponding to the danger the patient is in when infected with Pathogen A.

这些数据是非线性的但非常整洁，因此小型分类器网络能够以 98-99% 的验证准确率学习这些数据也就不足为奇了。运行 `train.py --classifier` 可以训练一个简单的神经网络，该网络包含 6 层（每层宽度为 8）并使用 ReLU 激活函数，定义在 `ScoringModel.build_classifier_model()` 中。但如何训练一个评分系统呢？我们的目标是训练一个系统，在给定 8 个特征作为输入时，能够产生一个分数，对应于患者感染病原体 A 时的危险程度。

The complication is that we have no scores available in our training data, only the three outcomes (categories). To ensure that the scoring system is meaningful, we would like certain score ranges to correspond to the three main outcomes. The first thing someone may try is to assign a numeric value to each category, like 0 to home treatment, 1 to hospitalization and 2 to death, and use it as the target. Then set up a neural network with a single output, and train it with e.g. MSE loss. The problem with this approach is that the model will learn to contort (condense and expand) the projection of the inputs around the three targets, so ultimately the model will always return a value close to 0, 1 or 2.

复杂之处在于，我们的训练数据中没有可用的分数，只有三个结果（类别）。为了确保评分系统有意义，我们希望特定的分数范围对应于这三个主要结果。人们首先可能会尝试为每个类别分配一个数值，例如居家治疗为 0，住院为 1，死亡为 2，并将其作为目标值。然后建立一个单输出的神经网络，并使用均方误差（MSE）损失进行训练。这种方法的问题在于，模型会学会围绕这三个目标值扭曲（压缩和扩展）输入的投影，因此最终模型总是会返回接近 0、1 或 2 的值。
