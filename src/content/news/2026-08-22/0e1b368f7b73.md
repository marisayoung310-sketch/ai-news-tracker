# CAViAR: A Causal Video Dataset for Fine-Grained Accident Reasoning in Real-World Scenarios
# CAViAR：用于现实场景中细粒度事故推理的因果视频数据集

**Abstract:** While modern autonomous driving systems excel at perception tasks such as object detection and trajectory prediction, they lack the high-level causal reasoning required to interpret traffic accidents. In particular, determining responsibility, such as identifying who is at fault and which traffic rule was violated, remains largely unexplored in current benchmarks.

**摘要：** 尽管现代自动驾驶系统在目标检测和轨迹预测等感知任务上表现出色，但它们缺乏解读交通事故所需的高级因果推理能力。特别是确定责任归属（例如识别谁有过错以及违反了哪条交通规则）在当前的基准测试中仍未得到充分探索。

To this end, we introduce CAViAR (Causal Accident Video and Incident Analysis Repository), a human-annotated dashcam benchmark comprising 2,249 real-world accident videos collected from CarCrashDataset (CCD) and Nexar. Each video is annotated with structured labels spanning environmental conditions, accident type, causal explanation, apparent At-Fault Agent, affected agent, and apparent rule-violation category.

为此，我们推出了 CAViAR（因果事故视频与事件分析库），这是一个由人工标注的行车记录仪基准测试集，包含从 CarCrashDataset (CCD) 和 Nexar 收集的 2,249 段真实事故视频。每段视频都附有结构化标签，涵盖环境条件、事故类型、因果解释、明显的过错方、受影响方以及明显的违规类别。

We benchmark state-of-the-art vision-language models (VLMs), including Cosmos-Reason2, Qwen3-VL, and InternVL3. Once class imbalance is accounted for with majority/random baselines and balanced metrics, perceptual competence is uneven--lighting is nearly solved, whereas weather and road-condition accuracy fall at or below the majority-class baseline---and all models degrade sharply on accident type and responsibility reasoning.

我们对最先进的视觉语言模型（VLM）进行了基准测试，包括 Cosmos-Reason2、Qwen3-VL 和 InternVL3。在通过多数/随机基准和平衡指标考虑类别不平衡后，我们发现模型的感知能力参差不齐——光照条件识别已基本解决，但天气和道路状况的准确率处于或低于多数类基准水平，且所有模型在事故类型和责任推理方面的表现均大幅下降。

Overall, CAViAR exposes a practical Perception--Reasoning Gap: current VLMs may recognize salient context, but do not reliably map visible agent actions to annotated rule-relevant responsibility categories in safety-critical driving scenarios.
