# Bayesian Guardrails for AI Decisions: Measuring Uncertainty Before Automating Decisions
# AI 决策的贝叶斯护栏：在自动化决策前衡量不确定性

Artificial Intelligence systems should not automate a decision simply because they can provide a prediction. A decision system should consider how uncertain the prediction is and defer if a mistake would be costly.
人工智能系统不应仅仅因为能够提供预测就自动执行决策。决策系统应当考虑预测的不确定性程度，如果错误代价高昂，则应选择推迟决策。

Most AI systems in production are optimized to provide an answer. A classifier identifies a high-risk customer. A forecasting model predicts demand. An analytics agent might recommend reallocating a marketing budget. The result usually appears as a number or category. Sometimes it becomes an action straight away.
大多数投入生产的 AI 系统都经过优化以提供一个答案。分类器识别高风险客户，预测模型预估需求，分析代理可能会建议重新分配营销预算。结果通常表现为一个数字或类别，有时会直接转化为行动。

This format can make the model seem more certain than it actually is. A growth forecast of 8 percent looks accurate, even when realistic outcomes range from a decline to a much larger increase. A high-risk customer may be just above an arbitrarily set threshold. A seemingly unprofitable campaign may still be waiting for delayed conversions. As soon as a prediction triggers an automated action, hidden uncertainty becomes operational risk.
这种格式可能会让模型看起来比实际更确定。8% 的增长预测看起来很准确，但实际结果可能从下滑到大幅增长不等。一个“高风险”客户可能仅仅是刚好超过了人为设定的阈值。一个看似无利可图的营销活动可能只是在等待延迟的转化。一旦预测触发了自动化操作，隐藏的不确定性就会转化为运营风险。

Bayesian methods, on the other hand, map uncertainty via probability distributions and not via a single estimated value. But a wider interval on a dashboard is not enough. The system should use the uncertainty to decide when it can act alone, when it needs scrutiny, and when it should abstain.
另一方面，贝叶斯方法通过概率分布而非单一估计值来映射不确定性。但仪表盘上显示一个更宽的区间是不够的。系统应当利用这种不确定性来决定何时可以独立行动、何时需要人工审查，以及何时应当放弃行动。

### Automation changes the cost of uncertainty
### 自动化改变了不确定性的代价

Uncertainty exists in every analytical system, whether it is measured or ignored. When a human analyst reviews a dashboard, they can pause and take a closer look at the data before taking action. They notice an unusual value and check whether the data is complete. They might compare it to historical patterns and get context from another team.
无论是否被衡量或忽略，不确定性存在于每一个分析系统中。当人类分析师查看仪表盘时，他们可以在采取行动前停下来仔细检查数据。他们会注意到异常值并检查数据是否完整，可能会将其与历史模式进行比较，并从其他团队获取背景信息。

Automation eliminates many of these pauses. An AI system can evaluate thousands of cases and act within seconds. This speed is useful if the model is reliable. But if it is confidently wrong, it multiplies the errors.
自动化消除了许多此类停顿。AI 系统可以在几秒钟内评估数千个案例并采取行动。如果模型可靠，这种速度很有用；但如果模型“自信地犯错”，它就会成倍地放大错误。

The NIST AI Risk Management Framework treats AI risks as contextual and recommends organizations capture and measure risks so that they remain manageable throughout the system lifecycle. This means that the risk of the same prediction can be different. A recommendation shown to an analyst may have little impact. The same recommendation can have a high impact if it directly changes prices, inventory, credit, marketing spending, or workforce planning. Accuracy alone is not enough. The remaining uncertainty must be low enough for the system to responsibly execute the proposed action.
NIST AI 风险管理框架将 AI 风险视为情境化的，并建议组织捕获和衡量风险，以便在整个系统生命周期内保持可控。这意味着同一预测带来的风险可能各不相同。展示给分析师的建议可能影响甚微，但如果同样的建议直接改变了价格、库存、信贷、营销支出或劳动力规划，其影响可能巨大。仅有准确性是不够的，剩余的不确定性必须足够低，系统才能负责任地执行建议的行动。

### Point estimates hide decision-relevant information
### 点估计掩盖了决策相关信息

Consider a composite scenario based on patterns I have encountered in multi-source marketing analytics. A company uses an AI model to recommend daily adjustments to campaign budgets. The model estimates that a budget increase for a given campaign will increase conversions by 7 percent. With a point estimate, the decision initially seems straightforward. The budget is increased.
考虑一个基于我在多源营销分析中遇到的模式所构建的综合场景。一家公司使用 AI 模型来建议每日营销预算调整。模型估计，增加某项活动的预算将使转化率提高 7%。有了这个点估计，决策起初看起来很简单：增加预算。

However, the model may assign substantial probability to several outcomes. Maybe it shows a small chance of a big improvement and at the same time a moderate chance of little change. Likewise, there may be a real possibility that profitability will decline. So the average forecast can remain positive, although the risk of loss is too high to automatically increase the budget.
然而，模型可能对多种结果赋予了相当大的概率。也许它显示出大幅改善的可能性很小，同时几乎没有变化的可能性适中。同样，盈利能力下降的可能性也确实存在。因此，尽管平均预测值可能保持正向，但亏损风险过高，不应自动增加预算。

The point estimate answers exactly one question: What is the central prediction of the model? This means that the plausible range of results and the probability of a negative outcome are not visible. Nor does it show whether the model has enough relevant data or what the company will lose if the recommendation is wrong. These are precisely the questions that are important when a prediction immediately triggers an action.
点估计只回答了一个问题：模型的中心预测是什么？这意味着结果的合理范围和负面结果的概率是不可见的。它也没有显示模型是否有足够的相关数据，或者如果建议错误，公司会损失什么。而这些正是当预测立即触发行动时至关重要的问题。

### Bayesian models ask a different question
### 贝叶斯模型提出了不同的问题

A Bayesian model combines a prior with the observed data to produce a posterior distribution over the values that the model estimates. For future values, the posterior predictive distribution is usually the crucial output. It combines the uncertainty about the model parameters with the variability that remains in future observations. A posterior predictive interval therefore describes the range of values that the model actually expects, and not just the uncertainty of a parameter.
贝叶斯模型将先验分布与观测数据相结合，产生模型估计值的后验分布。对于未来值，后验预测分布通常是关键输出。它将模型参数的不确定性与未来观测中残留的变异性结合起来。因此，后验预测区间描述的是模型实际预期的数值范围，而不仅仅是参数的不确定性。

In my research on economic policy uncertainty using Bayesian hierarchical modeling and Markov Chain Monte Carlo, the goal was not to produce one policy estimate. The model quantified uncertainty and showed how predictions change under different economic conditions. The same principle applies when companies automate decisions with AI. Demand models should provide posterior predictive intervals for future demand. Churn systems should show whether probability estimates remain stable across the customer segments that inform the decision. Analytics agents should disclose how robust the evidence is for each recommendation.
在我使用贝叶斯分层建模和马尔可夫链蒙特卡洛方法研究经济政策不确定性的过程中，目标并非产生单一的政策估计。模型量化了不确定性，并展示了预测在不同经济条件下如何变化。当公司使用 AI 自动化决策时，同样的原则也适用。需求模型应提供未来需求的后验预测区间；流失预测系统应显示概率估计在影响决策的客户群中是否保持稳定；分析代理应披露每项建议的证据稳健性。

### Aleatoric and epistemic uncertainty
### 偶然不确定性与认知不确定性

In practice, teams often divide prediction uncertainty into two types: aleatoric and epistemic. Aleatoric uncertainty reflects variation in the data itself or in the process that produces it. Customer behavior fluctuates, sensors provide noise, and conversion events arrive with a delay. Even similar users can react differently to the same ad. More samples from the same source often do not eliminate this variation. The prediction should therefore include them and the decision must take them into account.
在实践中，团队通常将预测不确定性分为两类：偶然不确定性（Aleatoric）和认知不确定性（Epistemic）。偶然不确定性反映了数据本身或产生数据的过程中的变异。客户行为会波动，传感器会产生噪声，转化事件会有延迟。即使相似的用户对同一广告的反应也可能不同。来自同一来源的更多样本通常无法消除这种变异。因此，预测应包含这些变异，决策也必须将其考虑在内。

Epistemic uncertainty represents limited knowledge about the model, its parameters, or the cases it is intended to assess. A model may have little evidence for a customer segment or new market. The same applies to a product or a particular economic regime. The influential Kendall & Gal framework uses this distinction in Bayesian deep learning. In practice, the two components cannot always be clearly separated from each other.
认知不确定性代表了对模型、其参数或其旨在评估的案例的知识有限。模型可能对某个客户群或新市场缺乏证据。产品或特定的经济体制也是如此。具有影响力的 Kendall & Gal 框架在贝叶斯深度学习中使用了这种区分。在实践中，这两个部分并不总是能被清晰地分开。
