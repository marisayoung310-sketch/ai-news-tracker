# VideoRun2D Demo: Markerless Body Tracking for Biomechanical Analysis of Running
# VideoRun2D 演示：用于跑步生物力学分析的无标记人体追踪技术

**Abstract:** Human pose estimation has advanced significantly due to the development of deep learning models, increased data availability, and improved computing resources. These developments have led to highly accurate body tracking systems with direct applications in sports analysis and performance evaluation.

**摘要：** 得益于深度学习模型的发展、数据可用性的增加以及计算资源的提升，人体姿态估计技术已取得了显著进步。这些进展催生了高精度的人体追踪系统，并已直接应用于运动分析和表现评估领域。

The VideoRun2D Demo performs a biomechanical analysis during sprints using different human pose estimators. The proposed framework was evaluated using human pose trackers and expert manual annotations. The tested framework uses 314 sprints from 44 professional runners, focusing on two key joint angles in sprint biomechanics: 1) hip flexion/extension and 2) knee flexion/extension.

VideoRun2D 演示利用不同的人体姿态估计器，在短跑过程中进行生物力学分析。该框架通过人体姿态追踪器和专家手动标注进行了评估。测试框架使用了来自 44 名职业跑步者的 314 次短跑数据，重点关注短跑生物力学中的两个关键关节角度：1) 髋关节屈曲/伸展，以及 2) 膝关节屈曲/伸展。

The framework also includes a post-processing module for outlier detection. The tested results demonstrate that the average root-mean-square errors range from 11.46° to 5.83° for the best trackers. When integrated with the post-processing modules, these errors can be reduced to 9.87° and 5.30°, respectively. The VideoRun2D Demo findings suggest that human pose-tracking approaches can be valuable resources for the biomechanical analysis of running.

该框架还包含一个用于异常值检测的后处理模块。测试结果表明，最佳追踪器的平均均方根误差范围在 11.46° 到 5.83° 之间。在集成后处理模块后，这些误差可分别降低至 9.87° 和 5.30°。VideoRun2D 演示的研究结果表明，人体姿态追踪方法可以成为跑步生物力学分析的宝贵资源。
