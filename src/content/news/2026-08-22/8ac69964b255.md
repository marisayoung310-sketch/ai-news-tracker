# SceneGTMM: A Conformal Mapping-based Scene-Aware Transferable GNN-Transformer Dual-Graph Interaction Framework for Map Matching

**SceneGTMM：一种基于共形映射的场景感知可迁移 GNN-Transformer 双图交互地图匹配框架**

---

Map matching is a key technology connecting positioning data with high precision road networks, but it faces challenges in noise robustness, cross-regional transfer, and interpretability. To address the limitations of existing methods in local-global fusion, dynamic road network adaptation, and reliance on black-box models, this paper proposes SceneGTMM, a transferable GNN-Transformer dual-graph interaction map matching framework based on a conformal mapping-based scene-relative strategy.

地图匹配是将定位数据与高精度道路网络连接的关键技术，但在噪声鲁棒性、跨区域迁移和可解释性方面面临挑战。为了解决现有方法在局部-全局融合、动态路网适应性以及对黑盒模型依赖方面的局限性，本文提出了 SceneGTMM，这是一种基于共形映射场景相对策略的可迁移 GNN-Transformer 双图交互地图匹配框架。

1) Conformal mapping-based scene-relative strategy: constructs trajectory-centric local coordinate systems to reduce dependence on the training road network, supporting cross-regional transfer and dynamic road network updates;

1) 基于共形映射的场景相对策略：构建以轨迹为中心的局部坐标系，以减少对训练路网的依赖，从而支持跨区域迁移和动态路网更新；

2) GNN-Transformer dual-graph interaction architecture: a GNN-modeled road graph captures local topological constraints, while a Transformer-modeled trajectory graph captures global temporal dependencies, and cross-graph attention achieves noise suppression and semantic alignment;

2) GNN-Transformer 双图交互架构：由 GNN 建模的道路图捕捉局部拓扑约束，由 Transformer 建模的轨迹图捕捉全局时间依赖，并通过跨图注意力机制实现噪声抑制和语义对齐；

3) CRF-enhanced structured prediction: combines the global context of the Transformer with the topological transition constraints of CRF to improve path connectivity and robustness.

3) CRF 增强的结构化预测：将 Transformer 的全局上下文与 CRF 的拓扑转换约束相结合，以提高路径的连通性和鲁棒性。

Experiments show that SceneGTMM achieves over 80% accuracy on multi-source trajectories with positioning errors of 16-50 meters, representing a 5.3% improvement over HMM. In cross-city transfer scenarios, it outperforms MTrajRec, GraphMM, and TMM, and enhances interpretability through attention and relative coordinate visualization. This study provides a new paradigm for high-precision, transferable map matching for real-time traffic perception and autonomous driving path planning.

实验表明，SceneGTMM 在定位误差为 16-50 米的多源轨迹上实现了超过 80% 的准确率，较 HMM 提升了 5.3%。在跨城市迁移场景中，其性能优于 MTrajRec、GraphMM 和 TMM，并通过注意力机制和相对坐标可视化增强了可解释性。本研究为实时交通感知和自动驾驶路径规划中的高精度、可迁移地图匹配提供了一种新范式。
