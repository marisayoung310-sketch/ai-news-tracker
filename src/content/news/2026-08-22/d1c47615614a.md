# Beyond Recognition: Compact Multi-Domain Arabic Manuscript HTR with Candidate-Selection Analysis and Evidence-Preserving Review
# 超越识别：具备候选选择分析与证据保存审查功能的紧凑型多领域阿拉伯手稿手写文本识别（HTR）

**Abstract:** Historical Arabic manuscript transcription is not only a recognition problem. A usable scholarly system must cope with shifting hands and layouts, preserve uncertain readings, distinguish visual evidence from linguistic plausibility, and record the researcher's final decision. 

**摘要：** 历史阿拉伯手稿的转录不仅仅是一个识别问题。一个可用的学术系统必须能够应对不断变化的书写笔迹和排版布局，保留不确定的解读结果，区分视觉证据与语言合理性，并记录研究人员的最终决策。

We present Phoenix, a 4.99-million-parameter CNN-BiLSTM-CTC recognizer, and Athar, an evidence-aware review workflow built around it. Phoenix is adapted across archival, Maghrebi, and historical manuscript domains using document-aware replay, an expanded 81-symbol codec, and forgetting guards that reject checkpoints that improve a new domain at unacceptable cost to previous domains. 

我们提出了 Phoenix，一个拥有 499 万参数的 CNN-BiLSTM-CTC 识别器，以及围绕它构建的证据感知审查工作流 Athar。Phoenix 通过文档感知重放（document-aware replay）、扩展的 81 符号编解码器以及“遗忘防护”（forgetting guards）机制，适配了档案、马格里布（Maghrebi）和历史手稿等多个领域；该机制能够拒绝那些以牺牲先前领域性能为代价来提升新领域性能的检查点。

In a pre-specified held-out comparison against the preceding checkpoint, frozen before evaluation and scored with greedy decoding and raw references, Phoenix reduced CER from 22.12% to 17.86% on 10,594 Agapet lines and from 17.72% to 11.84% on 11,684 Omar lines, while regressing from 10.39% to 10.72% on 164 TariMa lines. Across the two large held-out sets, character-weighted CER fell from 19.98% to 14.93%, a 25.3% relative error reduction. 

在针对先前检查点（在评估前冻结，并使用贪婪解码和原始参考进行评分）的预设留出集对比中，Phoenix 在 10,594 行 Agapet 手稿上的字符错误率（CER）从 22.12% 降低至 17.86%，在 11,684 行 Omar 手稿上从 17.72% 降低至 11.84%，而在 164 行 TariMa 手稿上则从 10.39% 轻微回退至 10.72%。在两个大型留出集上，字符加权 CER 从 19.98% 下降至 14.93%，相对错误率降低了 25.3%。

A separate same-protocol development diagnostic found the lowest CER for Phoenix on four of four comparable domains (9.59% unweighted macro CER). An N-best diagnostic revealed a 2.15-point oracle gap between beam decoding and Oracle@25, while neural text rerankers, consensus MBR, CTC-posterior quality estimation, and local pre-CTC hidden-state quality estimation recovered less than 4% of this gap. 

一项独立的同协议开发诊断发现，Phoenix 在所有四个可比领域中均达到了最低 CER（9.59% 的未加权宏观 CER）。N-best 诊断显示，束搜索解码（beam decoding）与 Oracle@25 之间存在 2.15 个百分点的 Oracle 差距，而神经文本重排序器、共识 MBR、CTC 后验质量估计以及局部预 CTC 隐藏状态质量估计对该差距的弥补不足 4%。

Athar therefore preserves the visual reading, exposes bounded alternatives, uses local language models conservatively, retrieves source parallels with unique, ambiguous, or abstain states, and exports auditable TEI and PAGE-XML records. The results support evaluating manuscript HTR as auditable evidence management rather than silent text replacement.

因此，Athar 能够保留视觉解读结果，展示有限的候选方案，保守地使用局部语言模型，通过唯一、模糊或弃权状态检索源平行文本，并导出可审计的 TEI 和 PAGE-XML 记录。这些结果支持将手稿 HTR 评估视为一种可审计的证据管理，而非简单的文本替换。
