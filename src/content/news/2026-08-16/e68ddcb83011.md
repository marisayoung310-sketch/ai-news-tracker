# SpaceX officially closes its Cursor acquisition
# SpaceX 正式完成对 Cursor 的收购

AI coding startup Cursor is now officially a part of SpaceX, according to an announcement on the Cursor blog.
根据 Cursor 官方博客发布的消息，AI 编程初创公司 Cursor 现已正式成为 SpaceX 的一部分。

Elon Musk’s SpaceX — which also acquired Musk’s xAI earlier this year — announced a deal in April for the companies to develop technology together; the deal also gave SpaceX the option to acquire Cursor for $60 billion.
埃隆·马斯克（Elon Musk）旗下的 SpaceX（该公司今年早些时候还收购了马斯克的 xAI）在四月份宣布了一项双方共同开发技术的协议；该协议还赋予了 SpaceX 以 600 亿美元收购 Cursor 的选择权。

Two months later, as SpaceX became a public company, the companies said they were moving forward with the acquisition.
两个月后，随着 SpaceX 成为一家上市公司，双方表示将推进此次收购。

In its announcement that the deal has closed, Cursor repeatedly referenced SpaceX’s computing infrastructure, which the company has been renting out to customers including Anthropic and Google. (SpaceX also faces a lawsuit over the pollution created by its data center gas turbines.)
在宣布交易完成的公告中，Cursor 反复提到了 SpaceX 的计算基础设施，该公司一直将这些设施出租给包括 Anthropic 和 Google 在内的客户。（SpaceX 目前还因其数据中心燃气轮机造成的污染而面临诉讼。）

Cursor said that by becoming part of SpaceX, it will have “access to the largest fleet of GPUs in the world.”
Cursor 表示，通过成为 SpaceX 的一部分，它将能够“接入全球规模最大的 GPU 集群”。

“SpaceX is building the computing capacity needed to scale intelligence far beyond what exists today,” the company added. “Cursor will be one place where that intelligence becomes useful.”
该公司补充道：“SpaceX 正在构建将智能扩展到远超当前水平所需的计算能力。而 Cursor 将成为让这种智能发挥作用的平台之一。”
