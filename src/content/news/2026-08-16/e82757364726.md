# 6 Best Apple Watch Accessories to Upgrade Your Watch (2026)
# 2026年升级 Apple Watch 的 6 款最佳配件

If you have an iPhone, you've probably considered buying an Apple Watch—and with good reason. It's the best smartwatch and best fitness tracker for iPhone owners, and it allows you to make calls, send texts, pay for purchases, and more without reaching for your phone. That said, I’m a firm believer that your Apple Watch is only as good as the accessories that support it.

如果你拥有 iPhone，你可能已经考虑过购买 Apple Watch——这是有充分理由的。它是 iPhone 用户能买到的最好的智能手表和健身追踪器，让你无需掏出手机就能拨打电话、发送短信、支付购物费用等。话虽如此，我坚信 Apple Watch 的体验好坏取决于支撑它的配件。

The right band can make it comfortable enough to wear all day and night for sleep tracking. A charging stand will remind you to keep it topped up, and a protective case could save you from a shattered screen. To help upgrade your Apple Watch setup, I, along with a few of my WIRED colleagues, tested chargers, bands, cases, and more to find the accessories worth the money. I’d also recommend getting AppleCare, which is the company's extended warranty plan, given that service pricing for watch repairs is exorbitant.

合适的表带能让你全天候佩戴（包括夜间睡眠追踪）都倍感舒适。充电支架能提醒你及时充电，而保护壳则能防止屏幕碎裂。为了帮助你升级 Apple Watch 的配置，我和几位 WIRED 的同事测试了充电器、表带、保护壳等产品，以找出最值得购买的配件。此外，鉴于手表维修的服务价格极其昂贵，我也建议购买 AppleCare（苹果的延长保修计划）。

***

### Best Apple Watch Band: Nike Sport Loop
### 最佳 Apple Watch 表带：Nike Sport Loop

When you buy your Apple Watch, you get to choose a band at checkout. I find textile and metal (stainless steel or titanium) bands to be more comfortable and fashionable than fluoroelastomer bands, which tend to be rigid and can irritate your skin. I love the Nike Sport Loop so much that it's been my everyday band for six years, and I ended up buying it in two colors: navy blue and black. Made from recycled polyester, Spandex, and nylon, it's soft, breathable, and easy to hand-wash. The Velcro pull tab makes it as adjustable as a band can get, and it's easy to fasten with one hand.

购买 Apple Watch 时，你可以在结账时选择表带。我发现织物和金属（不锈钢或钛金属）表带比氟橡胶表带更舒适、更时尚，因为氟橡胶表带往往较硬，可能会刺激皮肤。我非常喜欢 Nike Sport Loop，它已经成为我六年来每天佩戴的表带，我最终买了海军蓝和黑色两种颜色。它由再生聚酯纤维、氨纶和尼龙制成，柔软、透气且易于手洗。魔术贴拉环使其具有极高的可调节性，并且单手即可轻松佩戴。

### Best Charging Stand: Nomad Stand One Max
### 最佳充电支架：Nomad Stand One Max

The Nomad Stand One Max is a splurge, costing even more than reviewer Simon Hill’s top pick (the Belkin 3-in-1 Qi2 Charging Stand), but its minimalist design is arguably worth the extra spend. It’s compact enough that it doesn’t overcrowd a nightstand, and with the cable tucked away, it looks sleek. It’s also one of our favorites for fast Apple Watch charging, along with Qi2 25-watt charging for iPhones and MagSafe devices. The angled charging pad supports your phone in either portrait or landscape orientation, and the Apple Watch puck allows for Nightstand mode.

Nomad Stand One Max 是一款昂贵的选择，价格甚至超过了评测员 Simon Hill 的首选（Belkin 3-in-1 Qi2 充电支架），但其极简设计绝对物有所值。它非常紧凑，不会占用床头柜太多空间，且电缆隐藏得很好，外观十分优雅。它也是我们最喜欢的 Apple Watch 快充设备之一，同时支持 iPhone 和 MagSafe 设备的 Qi2 25 瓦充电。倾斜的充电板支持手机横屏或竖屏放置，Apple Watch 充电座则支持“床头钟模式”。

### Best Portable Charger: Anker MagGo Power Bank for Apple Watch
### 最佳便携充电器：Anker MagGo Power Bank for Apple Watch

A fully charged Apple Watch should last all day, but since I don't always remember to top it up, I always carry a power bank for backup when I'm out. WIRED reviewer Simon Hill—who specializes in smartphone accessories—says the compact Anker MagGo Power Bank is the best portable Apple Watch charger. It can deliver 5 watts and supports Nightstand mode. It has a 10,000-mAh battery capacity, and its built-in USB-C cable and USB-C port offer up to 30 watts to your phone or small device.

充满电的 Apple Watch 理应能使用一整天，但由于我并不总是记得充电，所以出门时我总会带一个充电宝作为备用。WIRED 评测员 Simon Hill（专门研究智能手机配件）认为，小巧的 Anker MagGo 充电宝是最好的 Apple Watch 便携充电器。它能提供 5 瓦功率并支持“床头钟模式”。它拥有 10,000 mAh 的电池容量，内置的 USB-C 线缆和 USB-C 接口可为手机或小型设备提供最高 30 瓦的充电功率。

### Best Apple Watch Case: Hontao Ultra Thin Case
### 最佳 Apple Watch 保护壳：Hontao 超薄保护壳

Unlike a phone, a watch is worn on your wrist, so every millimeter of real estate counts. If all you want is a thin, affordable, and attractive bumper that will protect your watch from bangs and scratches throughout the day, this is my favorite. It's made from a matte thermoplastic urethane (TPU) that is extremely thin and fits a 41-mm watch well. I have and like the dark green color, but reviewers say the lighter colors show dirt very easily, so I would stick with darker shades.

与手机不同，手表是佩戴在手腕上的，所以每一毫米的空间都很重要。如果你只需要一个轻薄、实惠且美观的保护框来保护手表免受日常磕碰和划伤，这是我的首选。它由哑光热塑性聚氨酯 (TPU) 制成，非常薄，能很好地贴合 41 毫米的手表。我个人喜欢深绿色，但据其他用户反馈，浅色款非常容易显脏，所以我建议选择深色系。

***

### Best Apple Watch Screen Protector: LK Apple Watch Screen Protector
### 最佳 Apple Watch 屏幕保护膜：LK 屏幕保护膜

Like bands, the best screen protector is not always the most expensive one. I tried pricier options from Spigen and Zagg, but when I pushed or pulled a case on over them, the thin films peeled off or bubbled. This screen protector (also made from TPU) was easy to apply and looks invisible once it's on. I particularly like the small rubber installation pad, which held the watch much more securely than other installation plates.

和表带一样，最好的屏幕保护膜并不总是最贵的。我尝试过 Spigen 和 Zagg 等更昂贵的选择，但当我套上保护壳时，这些薄膜往往会翘起或产生气泡。这款（同样由 TPU 制成）保护膜贴合简单，贴上后几乎隐形。我特别喜欢它的小型橡胶安装垫，它比其他安装板能更稳固地固定手表。
