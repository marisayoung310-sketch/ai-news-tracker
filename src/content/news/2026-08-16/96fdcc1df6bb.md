# Have a laugh at AI’s expense by roleplaying as a chatbot
# 通过扮演聊天机器人，在 AI 身上找点乐子

Scribble some nonsense, offer wooden jokes, or make your own requests of a real human playing a fake AI.
随手涂鸦些胡言乱语，讲几个生硬的笑话，或者向一个扮演“伪 AI”的真人提出你的请求。

*by Terrence O'Brien, Aug 15, 2026, 8:45 PM UTC*
*作者：Terrence O'Brien，2026年8月15日，UTC时间晚上8:45*

Your AI Slop Bores Me is brilliant in its simplicity. There are two tabs: human and LARP as an AI. On one side you enter a request. On the other, you submit an answer. But the important thing is that there’s a human on both sides of the equation. Prompts can request a response as text or image, and then whoever is roleplaying as the AI gets 150 seconds to respond.
《Your AI Slop Bores Me》以其简洁而令人惊叹。网站设有“人类”和“扮演 AI”两个标签页。在一侧，你可以输入请求；在另一侧，你可以提交回答。但最关键的是，这个过程的两端都是真人。提示词可以要求以文本或图像形式回复，而扮演 AI 的人有 150 秒的时间来做出回应。

Just like a real LLM, Your AI Slop Bores Me works on a sort of token system. Requests cost credits, and to earn credits you have to jump onto the AI side and answer some of your own. You can also just wait as you get one free request every two minutes. But, as fun as it is to drop a question or an image request into the chatbox and see what comes back, the real joy is in pretending to be a chatbot.
就像真正的大语言模型（LLM）一样，《Your AI Slop Bores Me》采用了一种类似代币的系统。发送请求需要消耗积分，而要赚取积分，你就必须切换到“AI”端去回答别人的问题。你也可以选择等待，每两分钟会获得一次免费请求的机会。不过，虽然在聊天框里丢出一个问题或图像请求并看看会得到什么回复很有趣，但真正的乐趣在于假装自己是一个聊天机器人。

Sure, there are way too many people asking for drawings of obscure anime characters that are nearly impossible to pull off with the bare-bones MS-Paint-like tool in two-and-a-half minutes. But that’s part of the fun, trying to work within the extreme limitations presented while trying to maintain the illusion that you’re an AI. If a request doesn’t appeal to you, you can always skip it.
当然，确实有太多人要求画一些冷门的动漫角色，要在两分半钟内用那个简陋得像“画图”软件一样的工具完成这些画作几乎是不可能的。但这正是乐趣所在——在极端的限制条件下工作，同时还要努力维持你是一个 AI 的假象。如果某个请求你不感兴趣，你随时可以跳过它。

There’s even a Discord server and a Hall of Fame. The latter of which includes an alarmingly good Starry Night rendition. Yes, the ads are a little overboard and obtrusive.
该项目甚至还有一个 Discord 服务器和名人堂。名人堂里甚至包含了一幅令人惊叹的《星夜》临摹作品。是的，广告确实有点多且碍眼。
