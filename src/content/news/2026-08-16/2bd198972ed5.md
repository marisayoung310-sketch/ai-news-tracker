# 5 Best Apple Watch Bands: Nike, Nomad, and Hermés (2026)
# 2026年五款最佳 Apple Watch 表带：Nike、Nomad 与 Hermés

Apple Watches are smart little gadgets, but they don't always fit the vibe. There were plenty of nights when I left mine at home because, as much as I wanted to check my notifications discreetly, the watch just didn't match my outfit. I've also had my fair share of faux pas and just let the neon orange sport band clash with my neutral ensemble.
Apple Watch 是精巧的智能设备，但它们并不总是能与你的风格相得益彰。曾有许多个夜晚，我把手表留在家里，因为尽管我想低调地查看通知，但手表却与我的穿搭格格不入。我也曾有过不少尴尬时刻，让荧光橙色的运动表带与我中性色调的服装产生冲突。

For example, I don't want a heavy stainless steel or high-quality leather band weighing me down during a sweaty workout, just as I wouldn't wear a flimsy silicone band to a dinner party.
幸运的是，Apple Watch 表带是一种简单且相对实惠的方式，可以提升你穿戴设备的时尚感。除了美观之外，不同的款式还会影响性能和功能。例如，我不想在挥汗如雨的锻炼时佩戴沉重的不锈钢或优质皮革表带，就像我不会戴着廉价的硅胶表带去参加晚宴一样。

### How I Test Apple Watch Bands
### 我是如何测试 Apple Watch 表带的

**How I Choose What to Test**
**我如何选择测试对象**
I research Apple Watch bands across major retailers, including Apple, Amazon, and Nike. I also consulted former WIRED editor Adrienne So, who had covered fitness trackers and Apple Watch bands for a decade. I look for bestsellers, affordable dupes, and niche finds.
我会在 Apple、Amazon 和 Nike 等主要零售商处调研 Apple Watch 表带。我还咨询了前 WIRED 编辑 Adrienne So，她曾报道健身追踪器和 Apple Watch 表带长达十年之久。我寻找的目标包括畅销品、平价替代品以及小众精品。

**How Long I Test**
**我的测试时长**
I test Apple Watch bands all day, every day, across workouts, commutes, and overnight. Each band is worn for at least two weeks and subjected to sweat, water, and everyday friction. That's typically enough time to evaluate comfort, quality, and durability. If it's severely uncomfortable or triggers skin irritation on the first day of testing, it is ruled out. My top picks remain in my rotation for months so I can compare them against newer models and assess how they hold up over time.
我全天候测试 Apple Watch 表带，涵盖锻炼、通勤和睡眠时间。每款表带至少佩戴两周，并经受汗水、水渍和日常摩擦的考验。这通常足以评估其舒适度、质量和耐用性。如果第一天测试就感到极度不适或引起皮肤过敏，它就会被淘汰。我的首选表带会留在我的轮换名单中数月，以便我将其与新款进行对比，并评估其长期表现。

**Criteria**
**评估标准**
*   **Comfort:** Apple Watch bands shouldn't pinch, trap moisture, or feel insecure. I also pay attention to breathability and potential irritation, especially with silicone and metal bands, and factor in how different skin types might react over time.
    **舒适度：** Apple Watch 表带不应夹皮肤、积聚水分或让人感觉不稳固。我还会关注透气性和潜在的刺激性，尤其是对于硅胶和金属表带，并考虑不同肤质随时间推移可能产生的反应。
*   **Quality:** Closures are fastened, unfastened, and adjusted repeatedly to see how they hold up. A good band should lock in place, adjust easily with one hand, and resist loosening over time. I also examine materials for flexibility, finish, and overall craftsmanship.
    **质量：** 我会反复扣合、解开和调节表扣，观察其耐用性。一款好的表带应该能锁定到位，单手即可轻松调节，且不易随时间推移而松动。我还会检查材料的柔韧性、表面处理和整体工艺。
*   **Durability:** To test claims of water resistance, I wear each band while I wash my hands, trek through the Chicago rain, and shower (if the brand says it's safe). Whenever relevant, I revisit bands after extended wear. I track discoloration, odor retention, and hardware fatigue.
    **耐用性：** 为了测试防水性能，我会在洗手、在芝加哥雨中行走以及淋浴（如果品牌说明支持）时佩戴每款表带。在适当的情况下，我会对长期佩戴后的表带进行复测，追踪其变色、异味残留和五金件的磨损情况。

### Best Band for Everyday Wear: Nike Sport Loop
### 日常佩戴最佳选择：Nike Sport Loop

The Nike Sport Loop has been my everyday band since 2020. I bought another in 2023 because I wanted to swap colors, and I figured after three years, I should buy a brand-new one, but it has held up remarkably well. Made from a blend of recycled polyester, spandex, and nylon, it's breathable and lightweight. The double-layered nylon weave is designed with dense loops that help prevent moisture from getting trapped against your skin. As long as you regularly hand-wash it to rid it of sweat buildup and dirt, it stays odor-free.
自 2020 年以来，Nike Sport Loop 一直是我日常佩戴的表带。我在 2023 年又买了一条，因为我想换个颜色，而且我觉得用了三年也该买条新的了，但旧的那条表现依然非常出色。它由再生聚酯纤维、氨纶和尼龙混纺而成，透气且轻便。双层尼龙编织设计有密集的环圈，有助于防止水分积聚在皮肤上。只要定期手洗以清除汗渍和污垢，它就能保持无异味。

### Best Titanium Apple Watch Band: Nomad Stratos Band
### 最佳钛金属 Apple Watch 表带：Nomad Stratos Band

Nomad originally designed the Stratos Band for the Apple Watch Ultra, but it fits just as well with the SE and Series 1 through 11. It's easy to adjust, refined enough for the office, and backed by Grade 4 titanium hardware with a scratch-resistant DLC coating. FKM rubber links (PFA-free and antimicrobial, according to Nomad) add flexibility, durability.
Nomad 最初是为 Apple Watch Ultra 设计了 Stratos 表带，但它同样适用于 SE 和 Series 1 到 11。它易于调节，外观精致，适合办公室佩戴，并配有带有防刮 DLC 涂层的 4 级钛金属五金件。FKM 橡胶链节（据 Nomad 称不含 PFA 且具有抗菌性）增加了灵活性和耐用性。
