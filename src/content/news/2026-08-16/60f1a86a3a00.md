# Disney D23 2026: Everything announced for Star Wars, Marvel, and more
# 迪士尼 D23 2026：星球大战、漫威等重磅发布内容汇总

The annual Disney fan event showed off the cast of Marvel’s X-Men movie, plus a new trailer for Avengers: Doomsday, and our first look at the VisionQuest TV show for Disney Plus. For Star Wars fans, there was a teaser trailer for season two of Ahsoka, plus a special look at Star Wars: Starfighter with an appearance from Ryan Gosling.
一年一度的迪士尼粉丝盛会展示了漫威《X战警》电影的演员阵容，发布了《复仇者联盟：末日》（Avengers: Doomsday）的新预告片，并首次展示了 Disney Plus 剧集《幻视探寻》（VisionQuest）。对于《星球大战》粉丝来说，现场不仅有《阿索卡》（Ahsoka）第二季的预告片，还特别展示了由瑞恩·高斯林（Ryan Gosling）参演的《星球大战：星际战斗机》（Star Wars: Starfighter）。

Other new announcements included a few updates about Pixar, The Simpsons, Bluey, and others you can find below.
其他新公告还包括关于皮克斯、辛普森一家、布鲁伊（Bluey）等内容的更新，详情如下。

### Highlights
### 亮点摘要

A new Avengers: Doomsday trailer focuses on Robert Downey Jr.’s Doom.
《复仇者联盟：末日》新预告片聚焦小罗伯特·唐尼饰演的“末日博士”。

The first trailer for VisionQuest reunites Paul Bettany and James Spader.
《幻视探寻》首支预告片让保罗·贝坦尼与詹姆斯·斯派德重聚。

Marvel reveals the new X-Men cast, including Inde Navarrette and Adam Driver.
漫威公布了新的《X战警》演员阵容，包括 Inde Navarrette 和亚当·德赖弗（Adam Driver）。

***

**Pixar is getting spooky in Ghost Market.**
**皮克斯新作《幽灵市场》（Ghost Market）走惊悚路线。**

It’s only the briefest of glimpses that we get of the upcoming Pixar feature, set for release in March of 2028. But we know it centers on a lunch shop on Oʻahu that serves lost spirits and Kyle, who manages to stumble his way into a supernatural adventure.
我们仅能从这部定于 2028 年 3 月上映的皮克斯长片中窥见一斑。但已知该片讲述了欧胡岛上一家为迷失灵魂提供餐食的午餐店，以及主角凯尔（Kyle）如何误打误撞进入一场超自然冒险的故事。

**Disney is making a Kingdom Hearts anime.**
**迪士尼将制作《王国之心》动画剧集。**

Square Enix and Disney Kids have teamed up to bring Sora’s adventures to the small screen with an all-new storyline as part of an anime series. Unfortunately, there are few details beyond that. Though, we do know that the game series’ creator, Tetsuya Nomura, is on board.
史克威尔艾尼克斯（Square Enix）与迪士尼儿童频道合作，将索拉（Sora）的冒险故事以全新剧情搬上荧幕，制作成动画系列。遗憾的是，目前除了这些信息外细节寥寥，但我们已知游戏系列创作者野村哲也（Tetsuya Nomura）已参与其中。

**The Bluey Movie teaser shows Keepy Uppy in Disneyfied CG.**
**《布鲁伊》大电影预告片展示了“迪士尼化”的 3D CG 风格。**

The good news: The beloved children’s series is coming to the big screen! The bad news: The charming 2D animation has been replaced with some bland 3D computer animation. And knowing Disney, we’ll probably get a soulless “live action” remake in about a decade.
好消息是：这部备受喜爱的儿童剧集即将登上大银幕！坏消息是：迷人的 2D 动画被平庸的 3D 电脑动画所取代。按照迪士尼的一贯作风，我们可能在十年内就会看到一部毫无灵魂的“真人版”翻拍。

**Star Wars: Ahsoka season 2 and Starfighter get teased at D23.**
**《星球大战：阿索卡》第二季与《星际战斗机》在 D23 亮相。**

Season two of Star Wars: Ahsoka is still months away, but Lucasfilm still took the opportunity to tease it a bit at D23. The company dropped the first trailer for the new season ahead of its January 20th, 2027 debut. The clip shows a darker, witchcraft-filled take on the Star Wars universe, with Grand Admiral Thrawn threatening to thrust the galaxy back into large-scale war after the fall of the Empire.
《星球大战：阿索卡》第二季距离播出还有数月，但卢卡斯影业仍借 D23 之机进行了预热。在 2027 年 1 月 20 日首播前，官方发布了新一季的首支预告片。片段展示了一个更黑暗、充满巫术色彩的星战宇宙，索龙元帅（Grand Admiral Thrawn）威胁要在帝国陨落后将银河系再次推向大规模战争。

Ryan Gosling also took the stage at D23 to tease the upcoming Star Wars: Starfighter movie. Unfortunately, while some footage was shown, it was exclusive to those in the room, and Lucasfilm hasn’t officially released it online. Star Wars: Starfighter is scheduled to hit theaters on May 28th, 2027, starring Matt Smith, Aaron Pierre, Mia Goth, and more alongside Gosling.
瑞恩·高斯林也登上了 D23 舞台，为即将上映的《星球大战：星际战斗机》电影造势。遗憾的是，现场展示的片段仅供参会者观看，卢卡斯影业尚未在网上正式发布。《星球大战：星际战斗机》定于 2027 年 5 月 28 日上映，除高斯林外，主演还包括马特·史密斯（Matt Smith）、亚伦·皮埃尔（Aaron Pierre）和米娅·高斯（Mia Goth）等。

**A new Avengers: Doomsday trailer focuses on Robert Downey Jr.’s Doom.**
**《复仇者联盟：末日》新预告片聚焦小罗伯特·唐尼饰演的“末日博士”。**

With the next chapter of the MCU continuing on after the well-received Spider-Man: Brand New Day, Disney brought Kevin Feige, Robert Downey Jr., Chris Evans, and Hayley Atwell out at D23 on Friday to show off this new trailer for the next Avengers movie, which is scheduled for release on December 18th.
随着漫威电影宇宙（MCU）在广受好评的《蜘蛛侠：全新的一天》（Spider-Man: Brand New Day）之后继续推进，迪士尼周五在 D23 现场邀请了凯文·费奇（Kevin Feige）、小罗伯特·唐尼、克里斯·埃文斯（Chris Evans）和海莉·阿特维尔（Hayley Atwell）登台，展示了这部定于 12 月 18 日上映的下一部《复仇者联盟》电影的新预告片。

**The first trailer for VisionQuest reunites Paul Bettany and James Spader.**
**《幻视探寻》首支预告片让保罗·贝坦尼与詹姆斯·斯派德重聚。**

The Disney+ show premieres on October 14th, closing out a trilogy of series that began with WandaVision. VisionQuest sees the titular hero wrestle with his identity, chat with Ultron (in human avatar form), and search for his potential son.
这部 Disney+ 剧集将于 10 月 14 日首播，为始于《旺达幻视》（WandaVision）的三部曲画上句号。《幻视探寻》讲述了这位同名英雄在身份认同中挣扎，与奥创（以人类化身形式出现）交谈，并寻找他潜在儿子的故事。

**Marvel reveals the new X-Men cast, including Inde Navarrette and Adam Driver.**
**漫威公布了新的《X战警》演员阵容，包括 Inde Navarrette 和亚当·德赖弗。**

With Spider-Man: Brand New Day behind us, and its reveal of Sadie Sink as Jean Grey, Marvel is finally ready to officially welcome the X-Men into the MCU. At the D23 event in Anaheim yesterday, the company revealed the cast for its upcoming, untitled X-Men film, which is scheduled to hit theaters on May 5th of 2028.
随着《蜘蛛侠：全新的一天》落下帷幕，以及萨迪·辛克（Sadie Sink）饰演琴·葛蕾（Jean Grey）的揭晓，漫威终于准备好正式将《X战警》引入 MCU。在昨天阿纳海姆举行的 D23 活动上，公司公布了即将上映的未定名《X战警》电影的演员阵容，该片定于 2028 年 5 月 5 日上映。
