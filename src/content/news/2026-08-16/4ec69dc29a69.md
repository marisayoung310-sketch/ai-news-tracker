# Marvel reveals the new X-Men cast, including Inde Navarrette and Adam Driver
# 漫威公布全新《X战警》演员阵容，Inde Navarrette 与 Adam Driver 加盟

With Spider-Man: Brand New Day behind us, and its reveal of Sadie Sink as Jean Grey, Marvel is finally ready to officially welcome the X-Men into the MCU. At the D23 event in Anaheim yesterday, the company revealed the cast for its upcoming, untitled X-Men film, which is scheduled to hit theaters on May 5th of 2028. Though, seeing as that’s quite far into the future, we wouldn’t be surprised if that date shifted slightly before then.

随着《蜘蛛侠：全新的一天》（Spider-Man: Brand New Day）落下帷幕，以及 Sadie Sink 饰演琴·葛雷（Jean Grey）的消息公布，漫威终于准备好正式将《X战警》引入漫威电影宇宙（MCU）。在昨天于阿纳海姆举办的 D23 活动上，漫威公布了即将上映的未命名《X战警》电影的演员阵容，该片定于 2028 年 5 月 5 日上映。不过，考虑到这个时间点还很遥远，如果上映日期在此之前有所变动，我们也不会感到惊讶。

In addition to Sink, the film will star Kit Connor as Scott Summers (Cyclops), Christopher Abbott as Charles Xavier (Professor X), Samara Weaving as Emma Frost, Maya Boyd as Storm, and Inde Navarrette as Rogue. Navarrette is fresh off a potentially star-making turn in the surprise critical and commercial hit Obsession. The team will be facing off against Nathaniel Milbury, AKA Mister Sinister, played by Adam Driver.

除了 Sink 之外，该片还将由 Kit Connor 饰演斯科特·萨默斯（镭射眼），Christopher Abbott 饰演查尔斯·泽维尔（X教授），Samara Weaving 饰演艾玛·弗罗斯特（白皇后），Maya Boyd 饰演暴风女，以及 Inde Navarrette 饰演小淘气（Rogue）。Navarrette 刚刚在口碑与票房双丰收的黑马影片《Obsession》中贡献了极具明星潜质的表演。该团队将对抗由 Adam Driver 饰演的纳撒尼尔·米尔伯里（Nathaniel Milbury），即惊恶先生（Mister Sinister）。

The addition of the X-Men to the MCU has been highly anticipated and teased several times over the years, but it was only in the latest Spider-Man that the Marvel mutants were finally brought into the fold.

《X战警》加入漫威电影宇宙一直备受期待，多年来也曾多次被预告，但直到最近的一部《蜘蛛侠》电影，漫威变种人才终于被正式纳入其中。
