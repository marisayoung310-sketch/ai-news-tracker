# A fantastical journey unfolds in gorgeous Wildwood trailer
# 华丽的《荒野》（Wildwood）预告片开启奇幻旅程

In June, we highlighted a behind-the-scenes featurette on the making of *Wildwood*, director Travis Knight’s forthcoming stop-motion animated fantasy film. Laika Studios released an extended teaser in May, which racked up over 90 million views. And now we’ve got the gorgeous full trailer, set to a haunting cover of Avicii’s “Wake Me Up.”

今年六月，我们曾重点介绍过导演特拉维斯·奈特（Travis Knight）即将推出的定格动画奇幻电影《荒野》（Wildwood）的幕后花絮。莱卡工作室（Laika Studios）在五月发布了一段加长版预告片，获得了超过 9000 万次的观看量。现在，我们终于迎来了这部华丽的完整预告片，背景音乐采用了艾维奇（Avicii）名曲《Wake Me Up》的空灵翻唱版。

The film is an adaptation of the 2011 novel by Colin Meloy—frontman of indie folk-rock band The Decemberists—and his wife, illustrator Carson Ellis. Meloy and Ellis live in Portland, Oregon, near Forest Park, where they often hike the trails. In *Wildwood*, they transformed that local landmark into a magical hidden world filled with talking animals and lurking threats, only accessible to people of The Wood—and select Outsider “half-breeds.”

这部电影改编自科林·梅洛伊（Colin Meloy，独立民谣摇滚乐队 The Decemberists 的主唱）及其妻子、插画家卡森·埃利斯（Carson Ellis）于 2011 年创作的同名小说。梅洛伊和埃利斯居住在俄勒冈州的波特兰，靠近森林公园（Forest Park），他们经常在那里徒步。在《荒野》中，他们将这一当地地标转化为一个充满会说话的动物和潜伏威胁的魔法隐秘世界，只有“森林人”以及少数“混血”外来者才能进入。

Twelve-year-old Prue McKee discovers the forest while out walking with her baby brother, Mac. A murder of crows swoops down and carries him away, and Prue discovers the Wilderness while hunting for him. Her schoolmate, Curtis Mehlberg, follows her, and both are able to enter that magical world. Fantastical adventures ensue as the pair encounter a mysterious woman, Alexandra, who has lost her son, along with coyotes, bandits, and some pretty big secrets.

12 岁的普鲁·麦基（Prue McKee）在带着弟弟麦克（Mac）散步时发现了这片森林。一群乌鸦俯冲而下将弟弟叼走，普鲁在寻找弟弟的过程中发现了这片“荒野”。她的同学柯蒂斯·梅尔伯格（Curtis Mehlberg）跟随她而来，两人得以进入那个魔法世界。随着两人遇到了一位失去儿子的神秘女子亚历山德拉（Alexandra），以及土狼、强盗和一些惊天秘密，奇幻的冒险随之展开。

Per the official logline, “*Wildwood* is a sweeping, darkly magical adventure about a young girl who ventures into a dangerous hidden forest to rescue her brother—an odyssey shaped by love, power, and the courage to confront the unknown.”

根据官方剧情简介：“《荒野》是一场宏大且带有暗黑魔法色彩的冒险故事，讲述了一位年轻女孩冒险进入危险的隐秘森林去营救弟弟——这是一段由爱、力量以及面对未知的勇气所塑造的奥德赛之旅。”

Knight has said *Wildwood* is Laika’s most ambitious project to date, featuring pitched battles and several aerial sequences. “We’ve had to build up the creative tools, the technology and our storytelling muscles to do this book justice,” he told *Empire* in 2024. The film required 136 sets and 231 practical puppets, augmented with some VFX.

奈特曾表示，《荒野》是莱卡工作室迄今为止最雄心勃勃的项目，其中包含了激烈的战斗和多个空中场景。他在 2024 年接受《帝国》杂志采访时说：“为了不辜负原著，我们必须建立起相应的创意工具、技术以及叙事能力。”这部电影共搭建了 136 个场景，制作了 231 个实体木偶，并辅以部分视觉特效。

Peyton Elizabeth Lee voices Prue, while Jacob Tremblay voices Curtis, and Carey Mulligan voices Alexandra. The cast also includes Angela Bassett as a giant golden eagle called the General, Jake Johnson and Maya Erskine as Prue’s parents, and Arthur Knight as her baby brother, Mac.

佩顿·伊丽莎白·李（Peyton Elizabeth Lee）为普鲁配音，雅各布·特伦布莱（Jacob Tremblay）为柯蒂斯配音，凯瑞·穆里根（Carey Mulligan）为亚历山德拉配音。演员阵容还包括：安吉拉·贝塞特（Angela Bassett）饰演被称为“将军”的巨型金雕，杰克·约翰逊（Jake Johnson）和玛雅·厄斯金（Maya Erskine）饰演普鲁的父母，亚瑟·奈特（Arthur Knight）饰演弟弟麦克。

In addition, the film will feature Mahershala Ali as Brendan the Bandit King, Jemaine Clement as Roger Swindon, Awkwafina as a rat named Septima, Charlie Day as a coyote named Dmitri, Amandla Stenberg as Niamh, Tom Waits as Maxim, Tantoo Cardinal as Iphigenia, Richard E. Grant as the wise old Owl Rex, Marc Evan Jackson as Lars Svik, and Len Cariou as Richard. Rob Delaney, Ólafur Darri Ólafsson, and Blythe Danner will voice as-yet-undisclosed roles.

此外，马赫沙拉·阿里（Mahershala Ali）将饰演强盗王布伦丹（Brendan），杰梅奈·克莱门特（Jemaine Clement）饰演罗杰·斯温登（Roger Swindon），奥卡菲娜（Awkwafina）饰演一只名叫塞普蒂玛（Septima）的老鼠，查理·戴（Charlie Day）饰演一只名叫德米特里（Dmitri）的土狼，阿曼德拉·斯坦伯格（Amandla Stenberg）饰演尼芙（Niamh），汤姆·威茨（Tom Waits）饰演马克西姆（Maxim），坦图·卡丁诺（Tantoo Cardinal）饰演伊菲革涅亚（Iphigenia），理查德·E·格兰特（Richard E. Grant）饰演睿智的老猫头鹰雷克斯（Rex），马克·埃文·杰克逊（Marc Evan Jackson）饰演拉尔斯·斯维克（Lars Svik），莱恩·卡琉（Len Cariou）饰演理查德（Richard）。罗伯·德兰尼（Rob Delaney）、奥拉维尔·达里·奥拉夫松（Ólafur Darri Ólafsson）和布莱丝·丹纳（Blythe Danner）将为尚未公布的角色配音。

*Wildwood* opens in theaters on October 23, 2026.

《荒野》将于 2026 年 10 月 23 日在影院上映。
