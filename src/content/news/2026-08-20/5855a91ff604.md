# Google Pixel 11 series review: Is the magic fading?
# Google Pixel 11 系列评测：魔力正在消退吗？

Google’s approach to making smartphones has changed a lot over the years. It started with Nexus phones aimed at the tinkerer and enthusiast niche back when there were countless companies making smartphones. Today, there are far fewer, and Google’s hyper-polished Pixel lineup is seeing less competition and higher sales. So maybe Google thinks it can just keep doing what works.

多年来，谷歌制造智能手机的策略发生了巨大变化。最初，谷歌推出了面向极客和发烧友群体的 Nexus 手机，那时市场上还有无数的智能手机厂商。如今，厂商数量已大幅减少，而谷歌精心打磨的 Pixel 系列面临的竞争更少，销量也更高。或许谷歌认为，只要坚持现有的成功模式即可。

The new Pixel 11 series is Google’s most refined yet, featuring sleek designs, smart software, and incredible screens. They’re also rather boring. Google made precious few hardware changes in its 2026 phones, shaving off a fraction of a millimeter here and dropping a gram there. The design language has been the same now for three generations, all while prices continue to inch upward. And this year, Google’s hardware has actually regressed in a few key areas. Despite that, the Pixel 11 phones aren’t bad—they’re some of the best 2026 has to offer. But it feels like Google is forgetting that it’s not just competing against Apple and Samsung. It also has to compete against the Pixels of yesteryear.

全新的 Pixel 11 系列是谷歌迄今为止最精致的产品，拥有时尚的设计、智能的软件和出色的屏幕。但它们也相当乏味。谷歌在 2026 款手机上的硬件改动微乎其微，只是这里削减了零点几毫米，那里减轻了一克重量。设计语言已经沿用了三代，而价格却在不断攀升。今年，谷歌的硬件在几个关键领域甚至出现了倒退。尽管如此，Pixel 11 手机并不差——它们依然是 2026 年市面上最好的手机之一。但谷歌似乎忘记了，它不仅是在与苹果和三星竞争，还要与往年的 Pixel 前辈们竞争。

### The same old (but great) design
### 依旧如故（但依然出色）的设计

Just like last year’s Pixel 10 series, Google’s 2026 lineup includes three flat phones and one foldable. The flat Pixels are the same size, and even side-by-side, it’s hard to tell them apart from their predecessors. We’ve got almost identical aluminum and glass sandwich designs, which do admittedly look very nice. They still have Pixelsnap, too.

就像去年的 Pixel 10 系列一样，谷歌 2026 年的产品阵容包括三款直板手机和一款折叠屏手机。直板 Pixel 的尺寸保持不变，即使放在一起，也很难将它们与前代产品区分开来。我们看到的依然是几乎相同的铝合金加玻璃三明治结构设计，不得不承认，外观确实非常漂亮。它们也都保留了 Pixelsnap 功能。

The Pixel 11s feel extremely solid, and Google has finally managed to make the buttons less wobbly. The base model Pixel also has a flatter camera bar, which makes it a little more pocket-friendly. The three flat phones are all still eSIM-only in the US—the international versions and the Pixel 11 Pro Fold retain their SIM card slots.

Pixel 11 系列手感极其扎实，谷歌终于解决了按键松动的问题。基础款 Pixel 的摄像头模组也变得更平整，这让它放进兜里时更方便。在美国，这三款直板手机依然仅支持 eSIM——国际版和 Pixel 11 Pro Fold 则保留了实体 SIM 卡槽。

The Pixel 11 Pro and Pro XL keep the taller camera bar on the back, but it’s nice that the cameras are centered on the back, unlike Samsung phones. You can set a Pixel down on a table, and it’s stable enough to poke at. The Pixel 11 Pro Fold still has that off-center camera module, which makes it less stable.

Pixel 11 Pro 和 Pro XL 保留了背部较高的摄像头模组，但好在摄像头位于背部中心，不像三星手机那样偏置。你可以把 Pixel 平放在桌面上，它足够稳定，不会晃动。而 Pixel 11 Pro Fold 的摄像头模组依然偏置，导致其稳定性较差。

The back of the phone is also home to the Pixel 11’s new HiLight module. This ring of LEDs is inside the camera flash of the Pro phones to act a bit like an old-fashioned notification LED. However, it’s mostly tied to Gemini to indicate when the AI is listening or responding. It also lights up to tell you when one of your favorite contacts calls. And that’s it.

手机背面还搭载了 Pixel 11 新增的 HiLight 模块。这个 LED 灯环位于 Pro 机型的摄像头闪光灯内，作用类似于老式的通知 LED 灯。然而，它目前主要与 Gemini 绑定，用于指示 AI 何时在监听或响应。当你的常用联系人来电时，它也会亮起。仅此而已。

HiLight only works when the phone is face down—if the accelerometer detects any other orientation, it won’t light up. So it really only makes sense if you’re using “OK Google” hotword detection and leaving your phone face down a lot. Annoyingly, you can’t even have the LEDs light up for text messages from your starred contacts. It’s really a baffling addition to Google’s phones. It would be questionable even if it had all the functionality of a classic notification LED, but it barely does anything as it currently stands. Honestly, give me the thermometer back. That at least had a theoretical use, even if it didn’t come up very often. HiLight has been so hobbled that it might as well not exist. Google says more features will be added later, but why the slow drip? We had notification LEDs figured out 20 years ago.

HiLight 只有在手机正面朝下时才会工作——如果加速度计检测到其他方向，它就不会亮起。所以，只有当你经常使用“OK Google”语音唤醒并习惯将手机正面朝下放置时，它才有意义。令人恼火的是，你甚至无法让它在收到星标联系人的短信时亮起。这确实是谷歌手机上一项令人费解的增加。即便它具备了经典通知 LED 的所有功能，其存在本身也值得商榷，更何况它目前几乎什么都做不了。老实说，把温度计功能还给我吧。那个功能至少还有理论上的用途，尽管使用频率不高。HiLight 被阉割得几乎形同虚设。谷歌表示未来会增加更多功能，但为什么要挤牙膏呢？我们 20 年前就已经搞定通知 LED 了。
