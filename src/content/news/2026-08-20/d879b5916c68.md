### What’s a Digital Wall Calendar? / 什么是数字挂历？

You might have heard these gadgets called by various names. Maybe “digital wall calendar” or “smart calendar device.” Maybe you've just heard the name Skylight Calendar, which is a notably popular model right now. No matter what you call them, these devices all do the same thing: replace a paper calendar with a digitized one that connects to the calendar tools you're already using, like Google Calendar or Apple Calendar. The devices let you see your entire family’s schedule in one view, with the ability to update or add to it without needing a phone or a separate account. It also erases issues like unreadable handwriting and forgetting to write down an event you've already scheduled digitally, and its color-coding system makes it easy to see who's on the hook for which activities.

你可能听过这些小工具的各种名称。也许是“数字挂历”或“智能日历设备”。也许你只听过 Skylight Calendar 这个名字，它是目前非常受欢迎的型号。无论你怎么称呼它们，这些设备的作用都是一样的：用数字日历取代纸质日历，并连接到你已经在使用的日历工具（如 Google 日历或 Apple 日历）。这些设备让你能一眼看到全家人的日程安排，并且无需手机或单独的账户即可更新或添加内容。它还消除了字迹潦草或忘记记录已在数字端安排的活动等问题，其颜色编码系统也能让你轻松分辨谁负责哪些活动。

While these are often called wall calendars, you can usually choose to place them on a tabletop. I preferred the models that included a tabletop stand, but I was still able to prop up the ones that only came with wall-mounting hardware during my testing.

虽然它们通常被称为挂历，但你通常也可以选择将它们放在桌面上。我更喜欢那些自带桌面支架的型号，但在测试过程中，我也能将那些仅配备壁挂硬件的设备立起来使用。

### What Else Can a Digital Wall Calendar Do? / 数字挂历还能做什么？

These devices aren't just calendars; they’re also designed for larger family management. You'll find features to help you plan meals, track to-do lists for multiple people, and earn rewards on a digital chore chart. The Skylight Calendar 2 is a favorite of mine because it includes a photo screensaver, but you'll need to be a Plus subscriber to get that and some of the other bonus features. Basically, you can use these calendars to manage your family's full schedule, chores, meals, and more in a single device.

这些设备不仅仅是日历，它们还专为更广泛的家庭管理而设计。你会发现一些功能可以帮助你规划膳食、跟踪多人的待办事项清单，以及在数字家务表上赚取奖励。Skylight Calendar 2 是我的最爱，因为它包含照片屏保功能，但你需要订阅 Plus 会员才能使用该功能及其他一些额外功能。基本上，你可以通过这些日历在单一设备上管理家庭的全部日程、家务、膳食等。

### Is a Digital Wall Calendar Worth It? / 数字挂历值得买吗？

I didn't expect to feel this way, but having a physical version of my hyper-detailed Google Calendar in the center of my home has been a delight. My husband and I both check our calendar at a glance while we're chatting in the kitchen, and at the start of the week, we'll camp out in front of it for a few minutes to plan the week's dinners and grocery needs. My top pick, Skylight Calendar 2, actually prompts us to add items to our grocery list based on what meals we picked (if it's a stored recipe, that is).

我没想到自己会有这种感觉，但家里中心位置能有一块显示我极其详细的 Google 日历的实体屏幕，确实是一种享受。我和丈夫在厨房聊天时，可以随时扫一眼日历。每周初，我们都会在它面前待上几分钟，规划这一周的晚餐和购物需求。我的首选产品 Skylight Calendar 2 甚至会根据我们选择的餐点（如果是存储的食谱）提示我们将食材添加到购物清单中。

My husband and I have found it so useful in part because we're both good about adding events to our individual calendars, so now this device can reflect that work. It lets us skip pulling out our phones when discussing plans for the next day or the upcoming weekend.

我和丈夫觉得它非常有用，部分原因是我们都习惯在各自的日历中添加活动，而现在这个设备能完美呈现这些信息。这让我们在讨论第二天或即将到来的周末计划时，无需再掏出手机。
