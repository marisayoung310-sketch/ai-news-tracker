# Meta AI is getting a Mac app
# Meta AI 即将推出 Mac 应用程序

You can share your window with the new Meta AI app, as well as connect it to Google Workspace.
你可以将窗口共享给全新的 Meta AI 应用程序，并将其连接到 Google Workspace。

Meta is launching a new Mac app dedicated to its AI chatbot. In an announcement on Wednesday, Meta says you can share your window with its AI chatbot, which can provide suggestions, answer questions, or create content based on what’s on your screen. Meta AI on the Mac also supports dictation across all apps.
Meta 正在为其 AI 聊天机器人推出一款专属的 Mac 应用程序。Meta 在周三的公告中表示，你可以将窗口共享给其 AI 聊天机器人，它能够根据你屏幕上的内容提供建议、回答问题或创建内容。Mac 版 Meta AI 还支持在所有应用程序中使用听写功能。

The launch comes as Meta makes its AI chatbot more like a productivity-focused assistant to better compete with its AI rivals — many of which already have desktop apps. You can share your window with Google’s Gemini AI app, while OpenAI’s ChatGPT and Anthropic’s Claude apps take things a step further by allowing the chatbots to take control of your computer.
此次发布正值 Meta 将其 AI 聊天机器人转型为更侧重生产力的助手之际，旨在更好地与已拥有桌面端应用的 AI 竞争对手抗衡。目前，你可以将窗口共享给 Google 的 Gemini AI 应用，而 OpenAI 的 ChatGPT 和 Anthropic 的 Claude 应用则更进一步，允许聊天机器人直接控制你的电脑。

Along with the new app, Meta is introducing new AI capabilities for businesses and creators. The company says its app across the web, on mobile, and on Mac can now work directly with Instagram and Facebook accounts, Meta ad campaigns, and Google Workspace.
除了这款新应用，Meta 还为企业和创作者引入了新的 AI 功能。该公司表示，其网页端、移动端和 Mac 端应用现在可以直接与 Instagram 和 Facebook 账户、Meta 广告活动以及 Google Workspace 进行联动。

As an example, Meta says its AI chatbot can analyze the reach of posts, along with their likes, shares, and saves, to make suggestions about what to post next. The app can also pull information from a business’s account and the web to create decks, docs, and spreadsheets, as well as perform recurring tasks, like providing weekly performance updates.
以企业应用为例，Meta 表示其 AI 聊天机器人可以分析帖子的覆盖面、点赞数、分享数和收藏数，从而就下一篇发布内容提供建议。该应用还可以从企业账户和网络中提取信息，以创建演示文稿、文档和电子表格，并执行重复性任务，例如提供每周的绩效更新。
