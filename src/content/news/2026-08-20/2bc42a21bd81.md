# OpenRouter is joining Stripe
# OpenRouter 加入 Stripe

Today, we are excited to announce that we are joining forces with Stripe, to power the next wave of GDP growth globally. OpenRouter is the first and largest model marketplace and gateway. We are the best way to discover and use any AI model, with one interface, broad provider choice, model-agnostic observability, cost management, and routing that improves price, performance, and uptime. We now process 10+ trillion tokens per day from 400+ AI models for a community of over 10 million developers and companies. Since our founding, we have seen at least 10x growth in inference volume every volume every year. We want to explain why we made the decision to join forces with Stripe and what it means for the millions of developers and companies that build on us.

今天，我们很高兴地宣布，我们将与 Stripe 携手合作，共同推动全球 GDP 的下一波增长。OpenRouter 是首个也是最大的模型市场和网关。我们提供发现和使用任何 AI 模型的最佳途径，通过统一的接口、广泛的供应商选择、模型无关的可观测性、成本管理以及能够优化价格、性能和正常运行时间的路由功能，为用户提供服务。目前，我们每天为超过 1000 万开发者和企业处理来自 400 多个 AI 模型的超过 10 万亿个 Token。自成立以来，我们的推理量每年至少增长 10 倍。我们希望借此机会解释为何做出加入 Stripe 的决定，以及这对数百万基于我们构建产品的开发者和企业意味着什么。

### What this means for our users
### 这对我们的用户意味着什么

OpenRouter will continue to operate as it is: same mission, same name, same product, same roadmap. If you build on OpenRouter today, nothing about your integration changes. OpenRouter exists to give users every model on equal footing, provide open signals about how they’re used in the market, help developers orchestrate them together, and make them observable and manageable at scale. That commitment is core to how we operate, and it doesn’t bend to any model, any provider, or any parent company. It also extends to a growing ecosystem of inference-adjacent services, including AI-native web search, context management, and more to come. Routing decisions will remain driven by one thing: what’s best for you, the user.

OpenRouter 将继续照常运营：使命不变、名称不变、产品不变、路线图不变。如果您目前正在使用 OpenRouter 构建产品，您的集成方式不会有任何改变。OpenRouter 的存在是为了让用户在平等的地位上使用每一个模型，提供关于它们在市场上如何被使用的公开信号，帮助开发者将它们协同编排，并使其在大规模应用下具备可观测性和可管理性。这一承诺是我们运营的核心，它不会向任何模型、任何供应商或任何母公司妥协。这也延伸到了不断增长的推理相关服务生态系统中，包括 AI 原生网络搜索、上下文管理以及更多即将推出的功能。路由决策将始终由一件事驱动：什么对您（用户）最有利。

### Our mission
### 我们的使命

We started OpenRouter in early 2023 on a simple belief: intelligence will be multi-model. No single model will win every task, and the frontier will move rapidly. That freedom is critical infrastructure for the industry. AI is too important for its future to be decided by whichever single model gets embedded first. AI has become the single largest driver of economic growth in the US, and inference is quickly becoming the largest line item for every company. We envision a healthy AI ecosystem where many models thrive, where AI neurodiversity is a strength, where a lab or an inference provider with a breakthrough can reach millions of developers, and where no single model becomes the default by inertia. Our mission is to realize this future, and it’s more important than anything else. The opportunity with Stripe allows us to accelerate it together.

我们在 2023 年初创立 OpenRouter 时基于一个简单的信念：智能将是多模型的。没有任何单一模型能胜任所有任务，技术前沿也将快速演进。这种自由是行业至关重要的基础设施。AI 的未来太过重要，不能仅由哪个模型先被嵌入来决定。AI 已成为美国经济增长的最大驱动力，而推理正迅速成为每家公司的最大支出项目。我们设想一个健康的 AI 生态系统，其中许多模型蓬勃发展，AI 的“神经多样性”成为一种优势，拥有突破性技术的实验室或推理提供商能够触达数百万开发者，且没有任何单一模型会因惯性而成为默认选择。我们的使命是实现这一未来，这比任何事情都重要。与 Stripe 的合作机会使我们能够共同加速这一进程。

### Why Stripe?
### 为什么选择 Stripe？

There are few companies on earth we would have considered selling to; our mission, our neutrality, and our lead in the market make the story for independence strong. We would only join a company if we thought we could do more together, faster, without compromising any of them. Stripe is that company. They are the best financial infrastructure platform in the world. Their API set the standard that developer products, including ours, have been measured against ever since. This is a combination of two platforms that developers choose on merit, with cultures focused on quality, scale, and commitment to builders, and that will remain essential in a post-AGI economy.

在地球上，我们愿意考虑被收购的公司屈指可数；我们的使命、中立性以及在市场中的领先地位，使得保持独立有着充分的理由。只有当我们认为双方携手能做得更多、更快，且不损害上述任何原则时，我们才会加入一家公司。Stripe 就是这样的公司。他们是世界上最好的金融基础设施平台。他们的 API 设定了行业标准，包括我们在内的开发者产品此后一直以此为标杆。这是两个开发者基于实力而选择的平台的结合，双方的文化都专注于质量、规模和对构建者的承诺，这在 AGI 后的经济中将依然至关重要。

For years, OpenRouter has been called “Stripe for LLMs.” Both companies share common DNA: we abstract complex infrastructure and market dynamics into delightful APIs, and we obsess over the developer and user on the other side of it. Businesses trust Stripe to optimize every part of their revenue stack, across payment methods, authorization, fraud, and more. Builders, customers, model labs, and providers trust OpenRouter to run a neutral, reliable layer across a fast-moving ecosystem. Stripe brings a large customer network, data on how internet businesses grow, and years of experience running trusted global infrastructure. There is also no one better at managing fraud and abuse, something we believe will only become more challenging for AI companies to address. We can now serve developers at a pace we couldn’t reach alone.

多年来，OpenRouter 一直被称为“大语言模型界的 Stripe”。两家公司拥有共同的基因：我们将复杂的架构和市场动态抽象为令人愉悦的 API，并始终关注 API 背后的开发者和用户。企业信任 Stripe 来优化其收入堆栈的每一个环节，涵盖支付方式、授权、欺诈防范等。构建者、客户、模型实验室和提供商信任 OpenRouter，在快速发展的生态系统中运行一个中立、可靠的层。Stripe 带来了庞大的客户网络、关于互联网企业如何增长的数据，以及多年运营可信全球基础设施的经验。此外，在管理欺诈和滥用方面，没有人比他们做得更好，我们认为这对 AI 公司来说将是一个日益严峻的挑战。现在，我们能够以单打独斗无法企及的速度为开发者提供服务。
