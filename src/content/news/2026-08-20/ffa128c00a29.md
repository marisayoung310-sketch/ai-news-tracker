# 5 new ways to level up your learning with Search
# 通过 Google 搜索提升学习效率的 5 种新方法

We’re introducing tools to help you learn new concepts, tackle tricky subjects, and organize your study sessions with AI in Search.
我们正在引入一系列工具，旨在通过搜索中的人工智能（AI）功能，帮助你学习新概念、攻克难题并高效组织学习。

A new school year is the ultimate chance to upgrade your routine — whether you’re navigating tough new subjects, prepping for exams, or trying to stay organized across your classes. Having the right tools makes all the difference. To help you start the semester with confidence, we’re introducing new ways to level up your learning through AI in Search, with education tools that are built to be safe by design.
新学年是优化学习习惯的最佳时机——无论你是要应对艰深的学科、准备考试，还是试图理清各门课程的进度。拥有合适的工具至关重要。为了让你自信地开启新学期，我们通过搜索中的 AI 功能引入了多种提升学习效率的新方法，这些教育工具在设计之初就充分考虑了安全性。

### 1. Understand concepts with interactive visuals.
### 1. 通过交互式视觉效果理解概念。

Sometimes, reading a static textbook page isn't enough to make a concept click. Search can now generate custom tools and simulations to help you see complex topics in action. Let's say you're learning about pH levels. You can do a search like "ph scale" and quickly get an interactive visual in an AI Overview to help you understand the basics.
有时，仅阅读静态的教科书页面不足以让你真正掌握某个概念。现在，搜索功能可以生成自定义工具和模拟演示，帮助你直观地观察复杂课题的运作过程。假设你正在学习 pH 值，你可以搜索“pH scale”（pH 量表），并在 AI 概览（AI Overview）中快速获得一个交互式视觉图表，帮助你理解基础知识。

To go even further, you can follow up and ask to see something more specialized, like plotting citrus fruits on the pH scale, and AI Mode will create a customized experience just for your question. Or, if you already have a hyper-specific question in mind, head straight to AI Mode and describe the tool or visual you need. Generative UI capabilities have launched globally in English in AI Mode and have started rolling out as well in AI Overviews.
如果你想深入了解，可以进一步提问，例如要求在 pH 量表上标注柑橘类水果，AI 模式（AI Mode）将根据你的问题创建定制化的体验。或者，如果你已经有了非常具体的问题，可以直接进入 AI 模式并描述你所需的工具或视觉效果。生成式 UI 功能已在全球范围内以英语上线 AI 模式，并已开始在 AI 概览中逐步推出。

### 2. Test your knowledge with practice quizzes.
### 2. 通过练习测验检验知识掌握程度。

Want to make sure you actually retain what you studied? You can now get customized practice quizzes directly in Search for any subject, from science and math to humanities, foreign languages and more. You can also ask for a quiz to help you prep for the following standardized tests: ACT, AP, ENEM, GRE, JEE, LSAT, MCAT, NEET, and SAT.
想确保自己真正记住了所学内容吗？现在，你可以直接在搜索中获取针对任何学科（从科学、数学到人文、外语等）的定制化练习测验。你还可以要求生成测验来帮助你准备以下标准化考试：ACT、AP、ENEM、GRE、JEE、LSAT、MCAT、NEET 和 SAT。

Thanks to our partnership with leading education companies The Princeton Review, Careers360, PhysicsWallah, and Akira Enem, these quizzes tap into trusted, authoritative content from test-prep experts, so you know you’re studying material that’s relevant to the exam. Just search for something like “create a quiz with the most commonly tested vocab words to help me prep for the SAT.” Search will give you an interactive quiz that includes explanations for each response and links to learn more, so you can truly master the material. Practice quizzes are now globally available in English free of charge, in both AI Overviews and AI Mode.
得益于我们与 The Princeton Review、Careers360、PhysicsWallah 和 Akira Enem 等领先教育公司的合作，这些测验采用了来自备考专家的权威可信内容，确保你所学的内容与考试高度相关。只需搜索类似“创建一个包含 SAT 最常考词汇的测验”的内容，搜索功能就会为你提供一个交互式测验，其中包含每个答案的解释和深入学习的链接，助你真正掌握知识点。练习测验功能现已在全球范围内以英语免费提供，涵盖 AI 概览和 AI 模式。

### 3. Learn each step with Lens.
### 3. 使用 Lens 学习每一个步骤。

Stuck on a tricky step or want to double-check your work? In the coming weeks, Lens in Search will offer a new interactive learning experience that helps you break down tough concepts and confirm if you’re on the right track. Just tap the Lens camera icon in the Google app (Android and iOS) and snap a photo of what you’re working on. You'll get an AI Overview that provides helpful explanations, pinpoints where you may have made mistakes, and coaches you when you’re stumped on a problem.
在某个棘手的步骤卡住了，或者想检查一下作业？在接下来的几周内，搜索中的 Lens 将提供一种全新的交互式学习体验，帮助你拆解难题并确认思路是否正确。只需点击 Google App（Android 和 iOS）中的 Lens 相机图标，拍下你正在处理的内容，你就会获得一份 AI 概览，它能提供有用的解释、指出你可能出错的地方，并在你遇到难题时为你提供指导。

Need extra clarification? Ask a follow-up question and keep the conversation going in AI Mode, while you explore relevant sites on the web. This new experience with Lens will roll out globally in English over the coming weeks.
需要进一步说明？你可以提出后续问题，并在 AI 模式中继续对话，同时探索网页上的相关资源。这项全新的 Lens 体验将在未来几周内以英语在全球范围内推出。

### 4. Stay organized with notebooks.
### 4. 使用笔记本保持条理。

Gemini Notebook (formerly NotebookLM) is one of our most popular tools for learning, and this week, we’re bringing notebooks to AI Mode — giving you an easy way to organize your studies and quickly find insights right in Search. You can set up a dedicated notebook for each class or project and add a range of sources like class slides, syllabi, or relevant web articles. You can even add your previous AI Mode threads on related topics.
Gemini Notebook（前身为 NotebookLM）是我们最受欢迎的学习工具之一。本周，我们将笔记本功能引入了 AI 模式，让你能够轻松整理学习资料，并直接在搜索中快速找到见解。你可以为每门课程或项目建立专属笔记本，并添加各种来源，如课堂幻灯片、教学大纲或相关的网络文章。你甚至可以添加之前在 AI 模式中关于相关主题的对话记录。

Your notebook brings it all together in one place, letting you ask questions and analyze topics based on your reference materials, while you build on your ideas over time. Your existing notebooks from Gemini Notebook or the Gemini app will appear automatically in Search, syncing the sources and instructions for each notebook across Google products. Notebooks in AI Mode are rolling out over the coming days across more than 180 countries in English, with local language support to follow in the weeks ahead.
笔记本将所有资料汇集于一处，让你能够基于参考资料提问并分析课题，同时随着时间的推移不断完善你的想法。你现有的 Gemini Notebook 或 Gemini App 中的笔记本将自动出现在搜索中，并在 Google 各产品间同步每个笔记本的来源和指令。AI 模式下的笔记本功能将在未来几天内向全球 180 多个国家和地区推出（英语版），其他语言支持将在未来几周内跟进。

### 5. Create custom files to streamline your studies.
### 5. 创建自定义文件以简化学习流程。

Instead of manually copying and pasting scattered course materials, you can now ask Search to create ready-to-use study documents based on your uploaded files or AI Mode threads. For example, you can add a photo of your handwritten notes along with lecture slides and ask Search for a one-pager that outlines the key concepts.
无需再手动复制粘贴零散的课程资料，你现在可以让搜索功能根据你上传的文件或 AI 模式对话记录，创建即用型的学习文档。例如，你可以上传手写笔记的照片和讲座幻灯片，并要求搜索功能生成一份概述核心概念的单页文档。

Custom file creation in AI Mode has rolled out globally in English and will come soon as well to AI Overviews. To start, it will be available for documents, slide presentations, spreadsheets, and text files across a range of formats, with more to come.
AI 模式下的自定义文件创建功能已在全球范围内以英语上线，并将很快应用于 AI 概览。初期，该功能将支持文档、幻灯片演示、电子表格和多种格式的文本文件，未来还将支持更多类型。

We hope these new tools help you learn with confidence this school year.
我们希望这些新工具能帮助你在本学年自信地学习。
