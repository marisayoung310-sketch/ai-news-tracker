# We reviewed the new Pixel lineup, ask us anything
# 我们评测了全新的 Pixel 系列，欢迎向我们提问

The reviews are live, and now you have a chance to ask us questions about the devices and our experiences testing them.
评测现已发布，现在你们有机会就这些设备以及我们的测试体验向我们提问。

The embargo has lifted on Google’s Pixel 11 series, as well as for its Pixel Watch 5. Now we get to talk smack — just kidding, the new hardware is good. We have four reviews live on the site that you can peruse at your leisure.
Google Pixel 11 系列以及 Pixel Watch 5 的评测禁令已经解除。现在我们可以开始“吐槽”了——开个玩笑，新硬件表现不错。我们已经在网站上发布了四篇评测，供大家随时查阅。

We’re giving subscribers a chance to engage with us directly in an AMA later today. I’ll be there (I reviewed the standard Pixel 11) to answer your questions, and so will David “11 Pro” Imel, Dominic “11 Pro Fold” Preston, and Victoria “Pixel Watch 5” Song.
我们将在今天晚些时候为订阅用户提供一个直接与我们互动的 AMA（问答）环节。我（负责评测标准版 Pixel 11）会出席回答你们的问题，David “11 Pro” Imel、Dominic “11 Pro Fold” Preston 以及 Victoria “Pixel Watch 5” Song 也将一同参与。

Subscribers, drop in your questions and we’ll begin answering them at 3PM ET. We can’t stop you from asking irrelevant questions, but we’ll be prioritizing ones that are focused on Google hardware and the new features (Camera Looks, Creator Suite, Magic Capture, Proactive Assistance, and so on), our experiences with the products during testing, who they’re for, and who might be able to skip on this year’s models. If you ask us to try Proactive Assistance with a specific example, we might just comply.
各位订阅用户，请留下你们的问题，我们将于美东时间下午 3 点开始回答。我们无法阻止你们提出无关的问题，但我们会优先回答那些关注 Google 硬件及新功能（如 Camera Looks、Creator Suite、Magic Capture、Proactive Assistance 等）、我们在测试过程中的体验、产品定位，以及哪些用户可以跳过今年机型的问题。如果你要求我们用具体的例子来测试 Proactive Assistance，我们或许会照做。
