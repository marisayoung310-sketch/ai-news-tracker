# Gwyneth Paltrow allegedly set to throw dinner in honor of Sam Altman
# 据称格温妮丝·帕特洛将为萨姆·奥特曼举办晚宴

Sam Altman is headed to the Hamptons, according to Puck. Gwyneth Paltrow is reportedly sending invites for a “private, off-the-record” dinner honoring the tech mogul on August 29 at her home out East.
据《Puck》报道，萨姆·奥特曼（Sam Altman）即将前往汉普顿。据称，格温妮丝·帕特洛（Gwyneth Paltrow）正在发出邀请，计划于8月29日在她位于东部的家中为这位科技大亨举办一场“私人、非公开”的晚宴。

Paltrow is an actress and founder of the wellness brand Goop. But she’s also a noted investor through her Kinship Ventures, which she launched in 2021 alongside Moj Mahdara. In 2023, reports said the firm was raising a $75 million fund, though it remains unclear whether it has closed.
帕特洛不仅是一位演员，也是健康品牌 Goop 的创始人。同时，她还是一位知名的投资者，通过她与 Moj Mahdara 于2021年共同创立的 Kinship Ventures 进行投资。2023年的报道称，该公司当时正在筹集7500万美元的基金，但目前尚不清楚该基金是否已完成募集。

Kinship is also an investor in OpenAI (per SEC filings, her investment in OpenAI dates back to 2023) and other AI companies, including the vibe-coding tool Lovable and the AI-powered marketing platform Nectar Social. We’ve reached out to Goop and OpenAI for comment.
Kinship 也是 OpenAI 的投资者（根据美国证券交易委员会的文件，她对 OpenAI 的投资始于2023年），此外还投资了其他人工智能公司，包括“氛围编程”（vibe-coding）工具 Lovable 和人工智能驱动的营销平台 Nectar Social。我们已联系 Goop 和 OpenAI 请求置评。

Over the past few years, Paltrow has become more outspoken about AI — she’s spoken about how she uses the tools to run her company, and her Goop Kitchen has signed a lease for the ground floor of Anthropic’s San Francisco HQ. Earlier this year, OpenAI hired Charles Porch, whom Vanity Fair called the “celebrity whisperer.”
在过去几年里，帕特洛对人工智能的态度变得更加直言不讳——她曾谈到自己如何利用这些工具来运营公司，而她的 Goop Kitchen 也已签署租约，入驻了 Anthropic 旧金山总部的底层。今年早些时候，OpenAI 聘请了查尔斯·波奇（Charles Porch），《名利场》杂志称他为“名人耳语者”（celebrity whisperer）。
