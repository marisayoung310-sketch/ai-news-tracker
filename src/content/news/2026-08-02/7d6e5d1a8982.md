# 15 Best Office Chairs of 2026—We Tested 70 to Pick Them
# 2026年15款最佳办公椅——我们测试了70款产品后的精选

There are three things you don't skimp on if you want a happy life: a good mattress, a good pair of shoes, and the best office chair you can find. Each one is key to a world free of unnecessary pain. Finding the right ergonomic chair can be the difference between a satisfying and focused home office life and a squirmy day filled with cricks and small dissatisfactions.
如果你想过上幸福的生活，有三样东西是绝对不能省钱的：一张好床垫、一双好鞋，以及你能找到的最好的办公椅。每一件都是让你远离不必要痛苦的关键。找到合适的工学椅，决定了你是能享受专注的居家办公生活，还是要在浑身酸痛和各种小烦恼中度过坐立难安的一天。

The best desk chair is almost always an adjustable chair—one that can be tailored to your body’s unique shape and that offers enough lumbar support to avoid back problems even after long hours at the grind. Many chairs also utilize materials that can dissipate body heat, so you’re not stuck in Zoom meetings with a sweaty back. But the chair options out there can be bewildering. Luckily, the WIRED Reviews team has spent more than seven years sorting them out. WIRED gear editor Julian Chokkattu has tested more than 60 chairs at his last tally. Reviewer Mathew Korfhage and other staffers have tried a dozen more, and most of our top picks have been tested by multiple people with different heights and body types.
最好的办公椅几乎总是可调节的——它能根据你独特的体型进行定制，并提供足够的腰部支撑，即使长时间工作也能避免背部问题。许多椅子还采用了能散热的材料，这样你就不会在参加 Zoom 会议时背部大汗淋漓。但市面上的椅子种类繁多，令人眼花缭乱。幸运的是，WIRED 评测团队花了七年多的时间对它们进行了梳理。据统计，WIRED 装备编辑 Julian Chokkattu 已经测试了超过 60 款椅子。评测员 Mathew Korfhage 和其他工作人员又试用了十几款，我们的大多数首选产品都经过了不同身高和体型的人员测试。

WIRED’s favorite office chair for most people is the Branch Ergonomic Chair Pro ($499) because of its balance between value, features, comfort, and adjustability. That chair, and its lower-budget Ergonomic Chair ($359) cousin, have been tested and attested to by multiple WIRED reviewers. But we also offer chairs for every budget, from the preternaturally comfortable Steelcase Gesture ($1,510) to a $169 chair from Staples that leaves cheap Amazon brands in the dust.
WIRED 为大多数人推荐的首选办公椅是 Branch Ergonomic Chair Pro（499 美元），因为它在性价比、功能、舒适度和可调节性之间取得了平衡。这款椅子及其预算更低的“兄弟款”Ergonomic Chair（359 美元）都经过了多位 WIRED 评测员的测试和认可。同时，我们也为各种预算提供了选择，从极其舒适的 Steelcase Gesture（1,510 美元）到 Staples 出品的一款 169 美元的椅子，后者足以秒杀亚马逊上的廉价品牌。

### Best Home Office Chair for Most: Branch Ergonomic Chair Pro
### 大多数人的居家办公首选：Branch Ergonomic Chair Pro

The price of a good office chair often inspires actual gasps, easily cresting $1,000. This is typically justified. Most cheaper chairs reveal their shortcomings quickly and uncomfortably. Branch’s Ergonomic Chair Pro is WIRED’s top pick for most people in part because it’s available at an attainable price while still offering comfort, ergonomics, and build quality. At $499, it’s not egregiously expensive and it has a decent warranty period.
一把好办公椅的价格往往让人倒吸一口凉气，轻松突破 1,000 美元。这通常是有道理的。大多数廉价椅子很快就会暴露出它们在舒适度上的短板。Branch 的 Ergonomic Chair Pro 成为 WIRED 大多数人首选的原因之一，在于它以可负担的价格提供了舒适性、人体工学设计和出色的制造质量。499 美元的价格并不算离谱，而且它还拥有不错的保修期。

One of the main markers of a good office chair is how well it can be adjusted to fit your particular shape and size. This is where Branch’s Ergonomic Chair Pro excels. The armrests can move up and down, forward and back, and angle inward or outward. But unlike most comparable chairs, the arms of the armrests can also move in and out to accommodate wider or narrower bodies, and lock into place.
衡量一把好办公椅的主要标准之一，是它能在多大程度上调节以适应你的特定体型。这正是 Branch Ergonomic Chair Pro 的优势所在。扶手可以上下、前后移动，并能向内或向外倾斜。但与大多数同类椅子不同的是，它的扶手还可以左右平移，以适应更宽或更窄的体型，并能锁定位置。

The seat depth can also be pushed out if you’re tall, or pulled in if you're short. The Pro can lock into the upright position, or at a few different reclining angles. This seat tilt allows for a more active seating position when you’re grinding away—a vanishingly rare feature. The high-density foam cushion is decently soft, noted tester Julian Chokkattu, who appreciated how the mesh backrest was able to follow the curve of his back. The lumbar support can also be adjusted deeper or shallower.
如果你个子高，可以将座深调大；如果个子矮，则可以调小。Pro 款可以锁定在直立位置，或几个不同的后仰角度。这种座垫倾斜功能让你在埋头苦干时能保持更活跃的坐姿——这在同类产品中极为罕见。评测员 Julian Chokkattu 指出，高密度泡沫坐垫足够柔软，他很欣赏网状靠背能够贴合他背部曲线的设计。腰部支撑也可以调节深浅。

The stock Branch seat might be a bit short for those above 6-feet tall, but this is fixable with a $29 add-on Tall Cylinder. You can also add a headrest, though Chokkattu did not find it was needed. Other options include personalized picks on fabric, vegan leather, or leather-leather.
对于身高超过 6 英尺（约 1.83 米）的人来说，Branch 的原装座椅可能有点短，但可以通过加购 29 美元的加高气压棒来解决。你也可以添加头枕，尽管 Chokkattu 认为没必要。其他选项还包括个性化的面料、纯素皮革或真皮材质选择。

It’s sturdy and well-built, with a powder-coated aluminum base and casters that roll easily on hardwood. Drawbacks are few, though Chokkattu found that the recline tension knob didn’t do much in particular. —Matthew Korfhage
它坚固耐用，配有粉末涂层铝合金底座和在硬木地板上滚动顺滑的脚轮。缺点很少，不过 Chokkattu 发现后仰张力调节旋钮的作用并不明显。——Matthew Korfhage
