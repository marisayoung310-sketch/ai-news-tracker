### Google has abandoned Google News?
### 谷歌已经放弃了 Google News？

You know that scene in Forrest Gump where Forrest sees Lieutenant Dan on the shore and is so distracted by him that he jumps off and swims to shore, allowing the boat to crash into the dock? Yeah, that's Google News now.
你还记得《阿甘正传》里那个场景吗？阿甘在岸上看到了丹中尉，因为太分心，直接跳下船游向岸边，结果导致船撞上了码头。没错，现在的 Google News 就是这样。

In this metaphor, Forrest is Google and Lieutenant Dan is AI. As a journalist, I have used Google News for many years to catch up on topics in which I am interested. I am always looking for news stories from U.S. news organizations published within a specific period of time, usually in the past week, but sometimes in the past day.
在这个比喻中，阿甘是谷歌，而丹中尉是人工智能。作为一名记者，我多年来一直使用 Google News 来追踪我感兴趣的话题。我通常会寻找美国新闻机构在特定时间内发布的新闻报道，通常是过去一周内，有时是过去一天内。

Google News has a Tools menu, where you can specify the range. You can also specify the language and location of the publication. Bit by bit, all of this just stopped working.
Google News 有一个“工具”菜单，你可以在其中指定时间范围。你还可以指定出版物的语言和地点。但一点一点地，所有这些功能都失效了。

Now, when I do my traditional searches, a huge majority of some of my searches are from Instagram or other social media sites, not news publishing sites. Many of my results are in foreign languages I cannot even identify. Many of my results are published in foreign countries, not the U.S. as I specified.
现在，当我进行常规搜索时，绝大多数搜索结果都来自 Instagram 或其他社交媒体网站，而不是新闻发布网站。许多结果是我甚至无法辨认的外语。许多结果是在国外发布的，而不是我指定的美国。

Now, the most recent failure is that when I specify news published in, say, the past week, it just ignores that setting and gives me news published weeks, months or years in the past. Google has abandoned Google News and let it crash into the dock.
现在，最近出现的问题是，当我指定搜索过去一周发布的新闻时，它直接忽略了该设置，给我推送的是几周、几个月甚至几年前发布的新闻。谷歌已经放弃了 Google News，任由它撞向码头。
