# Spider-Man: Brand New Day leak racks up millions of views
# 《蜘蛛侠：全新的一天》泄露版收获数百万观看量

A bootleg of Spider-Man: Brand New Day was up on X for over seven hours before eventually being pulled. During that time, it reached over 5.9 million accounts and accumulated over 143,000 likes. Other accounts have reposted the leaked film, but none have lasted particularly long, as Disney now seems to be on top of issuing takedown requests.

《蜘蛛侠：全新的一天》（Spider-Man: Brand New Day）的盗版视频在 X（原推特）上发布后，存活了超过七个小时才最终被删除。在此期间，该视频触达了超过 590 万个账户，并积累了超过 14.3 万个点赞。尽管其他账户也转发了这部泄露的电影，但由于迪士尼目前似乎正积极采取下架措施，这些转发内容大多没能存活太久。

The leak is unlikely to slow down the box office juggernaut, which is already shattering records. It raked in $72 million in previews this week. The previous record holder was Avengers: Endgame, which grossed $60 million during its previews way back in 2019. Endgame went on to set staggering records for opening weekend, grossing over $1.2 billion worldwide and over $350 million in the US. Spider-Man: No Way Home currently holds the number two spot on the all-time US opening weekend list with $260 million.

这次泄露事件不太可能阻挡这部票房巨作的势头，它目前正在打破各项纪录。本周，该片在点映阶段就狂揽了 7200 万美元票房。此前的纪录保持者是 2019 年上映的《复仇者联盟 4：终局之战》，其点映票房为 6000 万美元。《终局之战》随后创下了惊人的首周末票房纪录，全球总票房超过 12 亿美元，美国本土票房超过 3.5 亿美元。《蜘蛛侠：英雄无归》目前以 2.6 亿美元位列美国影史首周末票房榜第二名。

Brand New Day could potentially usurp Endgame, though it certainly faces an uphill battle. Between previews and opening day, it’s already grossed $168 million, which puts it ahead of Endgame’s $157.4 million. Sony is keeping its estimates somewhat conservative, saying it expects the latest Spider-Man to pull in $325 million.

《全新的一天》或许有潜力超越《终局之战》，尽管这无疑是一场硬仗。算上点映和首映日，该片目前已入账 1.68 亿美元，超过了《终局之战》同期的 1.574 亿美元。索尼影业目前的预估相对保守，称预计这部最新的蜘蛛侠电影最终票房将达到 3.25 亿美元。
