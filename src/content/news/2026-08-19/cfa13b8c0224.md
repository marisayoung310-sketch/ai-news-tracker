# Forward Pass Domain Adaptation (Without Cross-Layer Backpropagation)
# 前向传播领域自适应（无需跨层反向传播）

**Abstract:** Forward-Pass-Only MLP training (FPO) adapts large language models without a backward pass through the model body, achieving 2.7--3.2x the throughput of standard fine-tuning at ~40% less peak training memory, while leaving off-domain benchmarks within seed-noise of baseline, a property that full-network fine-tuning does not reliably reproduce.

**摘要：** “仅前向传播 MLP 训练”（Forward-Pass-Only MLP training, FPO）能够在无需对模型主体进行反向传播的情况下实现大语言模型的自适应。该方法在训练峰值内存降低约 40% 的同时，实现了标准微调 2.7 到 3.2 倍的吞吐量，且能将领域外基准测试结果保持在基准模型的种子噪声范围内——这是全网络微调难以稳定复现的特性。

FPO rests on a single empirical observation: at late layers of a transformer, the output-layer prediction error approximates the true gradient with cosine similarity 0.47--0.59 across six public models we survey. We introduce a two-minute diagnostic that quantifies this approximation per layer for any model, identifying where late-layer adaptation is viable.

FPO 基于一个单一的经验观察：在 Transformer 的深层中，输出层的预测误差可以近似于真实梯度，在我们调查的六个公开模型中，其余弦相似度在 0.47 到 0.59 之间。我们引入了一种两分钟的诊断方法，可以量化任何模型中每一层的这种近似程度，从而识别出哪些层适合进行深层自适应。

Informed by the diagnostic, FPO computes a single error signal at the output and applies it to each target layer. No signal is propagated between layers, and no autograd graph is constructed at any point. We evaluate FPO on three model families (OLMo-2-7B, Qwen3-8B, Falcon3-7B). Across all three, FPO produces in-domain perplexity improvement and leaves MMLU, ARC-Challenge, HellaSwag, and Winogrande within seed-noise of baseline. Localizing SFT to FPO's target layers to enter this regime is also feasible, but at 2.2x the wall-clock cost of FPO.

在诊断结果的指导下，FPO 在输出端计算单一误差信号，并将其应用于每个目标层。层与层之间不传播任何信号，且在整个过程中无需构建自动微分图（autograd graph）。我们在三个模型系列（OLMo-2-7B、Qwen3-8B、Falcon3-7B）上评估了 FPO。在这三个模型中，FPO 均提升了领域内困惑度（perplexity），并将 MMLU、ARC-Challenge、HellaSwag 和 Winogrande 的表现保持在基准模型的种子噪声范围内。将监督微调（SFT）局限在 FPO 的目标层以进入该状态也是可行的，但其耗时是 FPO 的 2.2 倍。
