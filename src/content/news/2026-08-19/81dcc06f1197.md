- The Fairphone (Gen. 6+) is powered by the Snapdragon® 7s Gen 4 processor, going up a generation compared to its predecessor.

When we introduce a new color, it’s never just a design decision. The reason we zeroed in on Cobalt Blue is because it serves as a nod to our origin story as a conflict mineral awareness campaign and our commitment to fairly sourced raw materials.
新版本，新配色：新款 Fairphone 提供地平线黑（Horizon Black）和森林绿（Forest Green），以及独家的钴蓝色（Cobalt Blue），这不仅仅是一种风格宣言。当我们推出新颜色时，绝不仅仅是出于设计考虑。我们选择钴蓝色是因为它致敬了我们的起源——作为一场冲突矿产意识运动，以及我们对公平采购原材料的承诺。

Having said that, it’s quite the bold look that’s sure to make heads turn and get conversations going. And even better, you can make sure your accessories match as well, with the card holder, finger loop, and protective cover available in the new color as well.
