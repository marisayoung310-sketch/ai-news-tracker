# On Cross-Validation for Hyperparameter Optimization of Deep Learning Image Classifiers
# 关于深度学习图像分类器超参数优化的交叉验证研究

**Abstract:** Hyperparameter optimization (HPO) can materially affect the performance of deep learning (DL) image classifiers, but there is little empirical guidance on how to derive the validation signal that drives it, especially for the small sample sizes common in fields such as medical imaging. 

**摘要：** 超参数优化（HPO）会实质性地影响深度学习（DL）图像分类器的性能，但目前关于如何获取驱动该优化的验证信号，尤其是在医学影像等领域常见的小样本场景下，缺乏实证指导。

We compared three HPO protocols in terms of {\em absolute performance-estimation error} (AEE; the absolute difference between the winning configuration's validation AUROC and its test AUROC): fixed holdout (F), reshuffled holdout (R), and 5-fold cross-validation (C). The search space, sampler, training procedure, architecture, and test set were held identical across protocols. 

我们比较了三种 HPO 协议在“绝对性能估计误差”（AEE；即最优配置的验证集 AUROC 与测试集 AUROC 之间的绝对差值）方面的表现：固定留出法（F）、重洗留出法（R）和 5 折交叉验证（C）。在所有协议中，搜索空间、采样器、训练流程、模型架构和测试集均保持一致。

We evaluated the protocols on three public datasets spanning two regimes: binary medical imaging (RSNA pneumonia radiographs and binarized HAM10000 skin lesions) and 200-class natural imaging (Tiny ImageNet), across a range of development set sizes $n$ and two backbones (ResNet-18 on all datasets, Vision Transformer (ViT-S/16) on RSNA). 

我们在涵盖两个领域的三个公共数据集上评估了这些协议：二分类医学影像（RSNA 肺炎放射影像和二值化 HAM10000 皮肤病变）以及 200 类自然图像（Tiny ImageNet）。评估过程涵盖了多种开发集规模 $n$ 以及两种骨干网络（所有数据集使用 ResNet-18，RSNA 使用 Vision Transformer (ViT-S/16)）。

On the medical datasets, every point estimate favored cross-validation over both holdout protocols, with reductions in AEE largest at small sample sizes and diminishing as $n$ increased. This pattern remained robust under conservative family-wise adjustment. On Tiny ImageNet, AEE was negligible under all three protocols. Test AUROC was generally similar among protocols. 

在医学数据集上，所有点估计结果均显示交叉验证优于两种留出法协议；AEE 的降低在小样本规模下最为显著，并随着 $n$ 的增加而减小。这种模式在保守的族系误差调整（family-wise adjustment）下依然稳健。在 Tiny ImageNet 上，三种协议下的 AEE 均可忽略不计。各协议间的测试集 AUROC 大体相似。

Fixed holdout had lower mean AEE than reshuffled holdout in 11 of 12 medical conditions, although this secondary finding was less uniformly supported. For small-sample medical image classification, we recommend cross-validation-based HPO when computational resources permit because it trades additional computation for a more reliable development-time estimate of subsequent test performance.

在 12 种医学实验条件下，有 11 种显示固定留出法的平均 AEE 低于重洗留出法，尽管这一辅助发现的支持力度不够统一。对于小样本医学图像分类，在计算资源允许的情况下，我们建议采用基于交叉验证的 HPO，因为它通过增加计算成本，换取了对后续测试性能更可靠的开发阶段估计。
