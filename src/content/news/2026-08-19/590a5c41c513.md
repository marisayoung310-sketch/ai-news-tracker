# Ask HN: Does anyone else feel like nothing matters anymore?
# Ask HN：还有人觉得一切都变得毫无意义了吗？

New software releases? Who cares. Learning new CS concepts? Whatever. Studying for the next interview? Job probably won't even exist in a year. Working on that side project? No one will care because it's just another addition to the mountain of slop, and the guy bagging your groceries can do it. Everything is done and cooked with AI now.
新软件发布了？谁在乎呢。学习新的计算机科学概念？随便吧。为下一场面试做准备？那份工作一年后可能都不存在了。做那个副业项目？没人会关心，因为它只是又一堆垃圾的堆砌，连超市打包员都能做。现在一切都被 AI 搞定并“烹饪”好了。

It's exactly like that feeling once you enable cheats in a game; sure you can do anything, but everything is meaningless now. Our entire purpose and joy in life has been taken from us.
这感觉就像在游戏里开启了作弊码；没错，你可以做任何事，但一切都变得毫无意义。我们生活中所有的目标和乐趣都被剥夺了。

**bm3719:** Corollary to your complaints: Another downside that I haven't seen on anyone's AI hatelist yet is that it crowds out all other discussion. What happened to everyone talking about languages, protocols, algorithms, architecture, etc.? We can't have those conversations anymore because we're not even sure they matter now. In fact, we probably suspect they don't, hence the not bothering.
**bm3719：** 对你抱怨的补充：我还没在任何人的“AI 厌恶清单”上看到另一个负面影响，那就是它挤占了所有其他的讨论空间。大家都去哪儿了？那些讨论语言、协议、算法、架构的人呢？我们再也无法进行这些对话了，因为我们甚至不确定它们现在是否还有意义。事实上，我们可能怀疑它们已经没意义了，所以干脆懒得讨论。

Also, anything we do write will just get immediately slurped into AI embeddings, anonymizing it and detaching it from its human source, at most nudging some corner of its vectorized slurry. Maybe someone will ask a question that our content influences, but if that user bothers to thank anyone, he'll thank Claude. Perhaps this is what it feels like to be fully commoditized, fully alienated from one's labor.
此外，我们写的任何东西都会立即被吸入 AI 的嵌入模型中，将其匿名化并与人类源头剥离，最多只是对其向量化泥潭的某个角落产生一点点影响。也许有人会问一个受我们内容影响的问题，但如果那个用户愿意感谢谁，他感谢的也是 Claude。也许这就是被完全商品化、与自己的劳动完全异化的感觉。
