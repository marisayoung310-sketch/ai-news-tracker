# Periocular Soft Biometrics: A Survey and Applications to Multimedia Forensics and Disinformation Detection
# 眼周软生物特征：综述及其在多媒体取证与虚假信息检测中的应用

**Abstract:** Soft-biometric attributes such as gender, age, and ethnicity provide valuable ancillary evidence when full identity recognition is not feasible, supporting applications in forensic investigation, identity verification, surveillance, or detection of synthetic and manipulated media.

**摘要：** 当无法进行完整的身份识别时，性别、年龄和种族等软生物特征属性可提供宝贵的辅助证据，从而支持法医调查、身份验证、监控或合成与篡改媒体检测等应用。

Among biometric modalities, the periocular region is a robust source of soft-biometric cues, as it often remains visible when other parts of the face are occluded, a frequent condition in forensic evidence and surveillance footage, and can be captured across a wide range of acquisition conditions.

在各种生物特征模态中，眼周区域是软生物特征线索的稳健来源。这是因为当面部其他部位被遮挡时（法医证据和监控录像中常见的情况），眼周区域往往仍可见，且可以在多种采集条件下进行捕捉。

In this paper, we provide a survey of demographic attribute estimation from periocular images, covering publicly available datasets, methodological trends from handcrafted descriptors to deep learning architectures, and the state of the art in gender, age, and ethnicity prediction.

在本文中，我们对基于眼周图像的人口统计学属性估计进行了综述，涵盖了公开可用的数据集、从手工特征描述符到深度学习架构的方法论趋势，以及性别、年龄和种族预测领域的最新技术水平。

We discuss use cases relevant to multimedia forensics and disinformation-detection applications, including demographic filtering in surveillance footage, age verification, and the detection of demographic inconsistencies in synthetic data.

我们讨论了与多媒体取证和虚假信息检测应用相关的用例，包括监控录像中的人口统计学过滤、年龄验证以及合成数据中人口统计学不一致性的检测。

We also highlight open challenges, including dataset bias, cross-domain generalisation, fairness, ethical aspects, and the lack of forensic-oriented benchmarks.

我们还强调了当前面临的挑战，包括数据集偏差、跨域泛化、公平性、伦理问题以及缺乏面向取证的基准测试。
