# Calibrated Trust, Not Sharper Prediction: An Empirical Test of Uncertainty Fusion
# 校准信任而非提升预测：不确定性融合的实证研究

**Abstract:** A recurring proposal in legal AI is to improve case-outcome prediction by fusing uncertainty tools (evidence graphs with belief propagation, sequential Bayesian odds updating, Dempster-Shafer combination, and conformal prediction) into one pipeline. We test this on 1,000 real European Court of Human Rights cases from LexGLUE and FairLex, predicting whether the Court found a Convention violation from the case's fact paragraphs.

**摘要：** 法律人工智能领域中一个反复出现的提议是，通过将不确定性工具（包括带有信念传播的证据图、序贯贝叶斯赔率更新、Dempster-Shafer 组合以及共形预测）融合到一个流水线中，来改善案件结果的预测。我们利用来自 LexGLUE 和 FairLex 的 1,000 个真实的欧洲人权法院案件进行了测试，旨在根据案件的事实段落预测法院是否认定存在违反《公约》的行为。

We compare three families across two frontier LLMs (Claude Opus 4.8 and GPT-5.5) as per-fact evidence estimators: (A) the raw LLM, (B) the LLM routed through the fusion pipeline, and (C) a term-frequency baseline through the same pipeline.

我们比较了两个前沿大语言模型（Claude Opus 4.8 和 GPT-5.5）作为事实证据估计器的三个系列：(A) 原始大语言模型，(B) 经过融合流水线处理的大语言模型，以及 (C) 经过相同流水线处理的词频基准模型。

Across roughly 4,750 tests we find: (1) on discrimination (AUROC around 0.83) the pipeline yields no improvement over either the raw LLM or the baseline; a frontier LLM used directly is the strongest single discriminator. (2) Naively composing an LLM with Bayesian-odds and Dempster-Shafer fusion more than doubles calibration error (ECE from about 0.16 to 0.46) via a prior-mismatch mechanism that replicates across both models.

在约 4,750 次测试中，我们发现：(1) 在歧视性判断方面（AUROC 约为 0.83），该流水线相比原始大语言模型或基准模型均无提升；直接使用前沿大语言模型是效果最强的单一判别器。(2) 将大语言模型与贝叶斯赔率和 Dempster-Shafer 融合进行简单组合，会通过一种在两个模型中均存在的先验不匹配机制，使校准误差（ECE 从约 0.16 增加到 0.46）增加一倍以上。

(3) Dempster-Shafer fusion is actively unsafe on long chains, committing confidently to wrong labels at below-chance accuracy; we recommend removing it. (4) The pipeline's genuine value is operational: routed through a conformal selective-prediction layer, the system decides which cases to automate and which to escalate.

(3) Dempster-Shafer 融合在长链条处理中存在明显的安全性问题，它会以低于随机猜测的准确率自信地给出错误的标签；我们建议弃用它。(4) 该流水线的真正价值在于操作层面：通过共形选择性预测层，系统可以决定哪些案件进行自动化处理，哪些案件需要人工介入。

After removing Dempster-Shafer, recalibrating, and applying class-conditional risk control on the full 1,000-case set, the tuned engine auto-clears at 96.8 percent accuracy with 0.5 percent errors escaping and 96.3 percent caught for review, versus 85.9 / 3.8 / 72.1 for an untuned baseline. The contribution of such pipelines in law is calibrated trust, not sharper prediction.

在移除 Dempster-Shafer、重新校准并对全部 1,000 个案件集应用类条件风险控制后，优化后的引擎实现了 96.8% 的自动处理准确率，其中仅有 0.5% 的错误漏过，96.3% 的案件被识别并提交审核；相比之下，未经优化的基准模型数据为 85.9% / 3.8% / 72.1%。此类流水线在法律领域的贡献在于“校准信任”，而非“更精准的预测”。
