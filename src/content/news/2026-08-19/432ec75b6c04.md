# Geometry Is Not Robustness: A Trajectory-Level Study of PGD Evaluation
# 几何并非鲁棒性：PGD 评估的轨迹级研究

**Abstract:** Projected Gradient Descent (PGD) is widely used to evaluate adversarial robustness, typically via final adversarial accuracy, which does not capture model behaviour throughout the attack. Recent work proposes trajectory-level diagnostics, such as loss evolution, gradient alignment, and steps-to-failure, for deeper insight into adversarial optimisation dynamics. However, whether these diagnostics reliably indicate robustness strength remains unclear.

**摘要：** 投影梯度下降（PGD）被广泛用于评估对抗鲁棒性，通常通过最终的对抗准确率来衡量，但这无法捕捉模型在整个攻击过程中的行为。近期研究提出了轨迹级诊断方法，例如损失演变、梯度对齐和失败步数，旨在深入洞察对抗优化动力学。然而，这些诊断指标是否能可靠地反映鲁棒性强度尚不明确。

We conduct a trajectory-level investigation of PGD attacks on convolutional neural networks trained on Fashion-MNIST. We compare clean-trained and adversarially-trained models across multiple robustness regimes, using rigorous 20-step PGD evaluations with random initialisation and multiple restarts for robustness measurement, and single-initialisation trajectory recording for diagnostics.

我们对在 Fashion-MNIST 上训练的卷积神经网络进行了 PGD 攻击的轨迹级调查。我们比较了在多种鲁棒性机制下，经过正常训练和对抗训练的模型。我们使用严格的 20 步 PGD 评估（包含随机初始化和多次重启）来衡量鲁棒性，并使用单次初始化轨迹记录来进行诊断分析。

We record full PGD trajectories across 3000 clean-correct samples per model and analyse loss evolution, gradient alignment, and failure timing across attack iterations. Our results reveal a clear robustness hierarchy across models; however, trajectory metrics do not contribute equally to its identification. Mean loss trajectories and gradient alignment patterns appear quantitatively similar across adversarially-trained models with substantially different robust accuracies.

我们记录了每个模型在 3000 个分类正确的样本上的完整 PGD 轨迹，并分析了攻击迭代过程中的损失演变、梯度对齐和失败时间。研究结果揭示了模型间清晰的鲁棒性层级；然而，轨迹指标在识别这种层级时的贡献并不均等。对于鲁棒准确率差异显著的对抗训练模型，其平均损失轨迹和梯度对齐模式在定量上表现相似。

In contrast, steps-to-failure distributions provide a clearer separation of robustness regimes, directly reflecting functional resistance to adversarial perturbation. These findings indicate that trajectory-level diagnostics describe optimisation geometry but do not independently measure adversarial robustness. Their interpretability depends on robustness regime, attack strength, and multi-metric evaluation. Trajectory-level analysis should be a complementary diagnostic tool, interpreted in context, rather than a replacement for standard robustness measurements.

相比之下，失败步数分布能更清晰地划分鲁棒性机制，直接反映了模型对对抗扰动的函数抵抗力。这些发现表明，轨迹级诊断描述的是优化几何结构，而非独立衡量对抗鲁棒性。其可解释性取决于鲁棒性机制、攻击强度和多指标评估。轨迹级分析应作为一种辅助诊断工具，在特定语境下进行解读，而非取代标准的鲁棒性测量方法。
