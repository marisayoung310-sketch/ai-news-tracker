# ChatGPT Leads Top Google Destinations in Paid-Click Share, iPullRank Finds
# iPullRank 研究发现：ChatGPT 在谷歌付费点击份额中位居榜首

ChatGPT had the highest share of paid clicks among the leading Google destinations in iPullRank's Q3 2026 zero-click and paid-click analysis. The dataset found that about 4.75% of Google traffic landing on ChatGPT came from paid clicks, well above the corresponding shares reported for major destinations such as YouTube, Wikipedia, and Amazon.
在 iPullRank 2026 年第三季度的“零点击”与“付费点击”分析中，ChatGPT 在谷歌主要目的地中占据了最高的付费点击份额。数据显示，约 4.75% 进入 ChatGPT 的谷歌流量来自付费点击，这一比例远高于 YouTube、维基百科和亚马逊等主要平台。

The result does not reveal OpenAI's advertising budget, bids, or total advertising activity. It does, however, show that paid placements represented a notably larger portion of observed Google referrals to ChatGPT than for the other leading destinations studied. That makes paid search an important part of the discovery picture for a widely used AI platform, alongside organic search, direct visits, and other referral paths.
该结果并未揭示 OpenAI 的广告预算、出价或整体广告活动。然而，它确实表明，在观察到的谷歌引荐流量中，付费投放对 ChatGPT 的贡献比例明显高于其他被研究的头部平台。这使得付费搜索成为继自然搜索、直接访问和其他引荐路径之后，该主流 AI 平台获取用户的重要组成部分。

### What iPullRank's data shows
### iPullRank 的数据揭示了什么

In its Q3 2026 zero-click behavior analysis, iPullRank examined roughly 200 million events to understand where Google clicks go and how often those clicks are paid. ChatGPT ranked around sixth among the leading destinations by Google clicks, behind destinations including YouTube, Google's own pages, Reddit, Facebook, and Wikipedia. That overall ranking is important context.
在 2026 年第三季度的零点击行为分析中，iPullRank 考察了约 2 亿次事件，以了解谷歌点击的流向及其付费频率。按谷歌点击量计算，ChatGPT 在主要目的地中排名第六左右，落后于 YouTube、谷歌自有页面、Reddit、Facebook 和维基百科。这一整体排名提供了重要的背景信息。

ChatGPT is not the largest destination in the analysis by total Google clicks, but its paid-click proportion stands out. A 4.75% share means paid traffic accounted for a more visible portion of its observed Google arrivals than it did for the larger, more established web destinations used for comparison.
就谷歌总点击量而言，ChatGPT 并非分析中最大的目的地，但其付费点击比例却非常突出。4.75% 的份额意味着，在其观察到的谷歌流量中，付费流量所占的比例比用于对比的那些规模更大、更成熟的网站平台更为显著。

The measure is deliberately narrow. It counts paid Google clicks that land on ChatGPT, not every interaction a user may have with ChatGPT after searching, and not OpenAI's total ad spending. It should therefore be read as a traffic-distribution signal rather than a direct estimate of advertising investment or campaign performance.
该衡量指标范围刻意设定得很窄。它统计的是进入 ChatGPT 的谷歌付费点击，而非用户搜索后与 ChatGPT 产生的所有互动，也不代表 OpenAI 的总广告支出。因此，它应被视为一种流量分布信号，而非对广告投资或营销活动表现的直接评估。

### Why this changes the AI discovery discussion
### 为什么这改变了关于 AI 发现的讨论

AI tools are often discussed as alternatives to conventional search. The iPullRank findings show that the relationship is more layered: Google can also function as a paid acquisition and discovery channel for an AI destination. Users may encounter an AI tool while searching for a task, product category, or solution, then arrive through a paid result rather than through a direct visit or an organic listing.
人们常将 AI 工具视为传统搜索的替代品。iPullRank 的研究结果表明，两者之间的关系更为复杂：谷歌也可以作为 AI 平台的付费获客和发现渠道。用户在搜索任务、产品类别或解决方案时可能会遇到某个 AI 工具，并通过付费结果进入，而非通过直接访问或自然搜索列表。

For marketing and growth teams, that changes the measurement question. Tracking only organic rankings or direct traffic can miss a material part of how people discover AI products. For enterprises evaluating AI adoption, the same pattern is a reminder that employee and customer journeys may begin in search environments before they reach an AI interface.
对于市场和增长团队来说，这改变了衡量标准。仅追踪自然排名或直接流量可能会遗漏人们发现 AI 产品的重要途径。对于评估 AI 应用的企业而言，这一模式提醒我们，员工和客户的旅程可能在到达 AI 界面之前，就已经从搜索环境开始了。

The evidence also argues against treating traffic sources as interchangeable. A visit that arrives through a paid Google result has different attribution, cost, and optimization implications from a direct visit or an organic referral. Teams assessing AI product visibility should separate these channels, then evaluate whether paid discovery is complementing organic demand or substituting for it.
该证据还表明，不应将流量来源视为可互换的。通过谷歌付费结果获得的访问，其归因、成本和优化意义与直接访问或自然引荐截然不同。评估 AI 产品曝光度的团队应将这些渠道分开，并评估付费发现是在补充自然需求，还是在替代它。

### Important limits on the finding
### 研究结果的重要局限性

The iPullRank analysis offers a useful comparative view, but it is not a complete map of AI discovery. Its panel is predominantly desktop, at about 94.5%, and behavior can vary by geography and market. Mobile usage, regional search results, campaign targeting, and user preferences may all change the relative contribution of paid clicks.
iPullRank 的分析提供了一个有用的对比视角，但它并非 AI 发现的全貌。其样本主要集中在桌面端（约 94.5%），且行为会因地理位置和市场而异。移动端使用情况、区域搜索结果、广告投放定位以及用户偏好都可能改变付费点击的相对贡献。

Three boundaries are especially important:
三个界限尤为重要：
1. The data does not quantify OpenAI's total ad spend or bidding strategy.
1. 该数据未量化 OpenAI 的总广告支出或出价策略。
2. It measures paid Google clicks landing on ChatGPT, rather than every possible path through which users discover or use the service.
2. 它衡量的是进入 ChatGPT 的谷歌付费点击，而非用户发现或使用该服务的每一条可能路径。
3. Panel composition and geographic variation limit generalization, particularly where mobile behavior or local search dynamics differ.
3. 样本构成和地理差异限制了结论的普适性，特别是在移动端行为或本地搜索动态存在差异的情况下。

Those caveats do not weaken the core comparison. They define what the result can support: ChatGPT had the highest paid-click share among the leading destinations analyzed in this specific dataset. They do not support broader claims about overall advertising budgets, conversion quality, or the long-term profitability of paid acquisition.
这些注意事项并未削弱核心对比的价值。它们界定了结果的适用范围：在这一特定数据集中，ChatGPT 在所分析的头部目的地中拥有最高的付费点击份额。它们不支持关于整体广告预算、转化质量或付费获客长期盈利能力的更广泛推论。

For businesses, the practical implication is that AI visibility is becoming a multi-channel issue. Search visibility increasingly includes where a brand, product, or AI tool appears in paid results as well as organic results and AI-generated answers. A strategy built around a single channel can leave important discovery paths unmeasured.
对企业而言，实际意义在于 AI 曝光度正成为一个多渠道问题。搜索曝光度日益涵盖品牌、产品或 AI 工具在付费结果、自然结果以及 AI 生成答案中的呈现位置。仅围绕单一渠道制定的策略可能会导致重要的发现路径被忽视。
