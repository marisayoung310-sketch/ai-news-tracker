# Deep Analog: Open-Set Film Emulation with Reference-Conditioned 3D LUTs
# Deep Analog：基于参考条件 3D LUT 的开放集胶片模拟

**Abstract:** Film emulation reproduces the look of an analog film stock on a new digital photograph. We target its open-set form -- matching any reference film frame from a single example -- with a 3D lookup table (LUT) predicted from that reference. 

**摘要：** 胶片模拟旨在将模拟胶片的质感复刻到新的数字照片上。我们针对其“开放集”形式——即通过单个示例匹配任何参考胶片帧——提出了一种由该参考图像预测生成的 3D 查找表（LUT）。

Real-time image enhancement predicts per-image weights over a fixed bank of 3D LUTs and blends them. We show this is a gated mixture of experts and inherits its failure: trained end-to-end against reconstruction, the gate collapses onto a single expert, so a bank of K LUTs delivers the capacity of one. An entropy term, the enhancement-setting analogue of mixture-of-experts load balancing, restores utilization and recovers about 1 dB PSNR. 

实时图像增强技术通常通过预测一组固定 3D LUT 库的权重并进行混合来实现。我们指出，这本质上是一种门控专家混合模型（Gated Mixture of Experts），并继承了其缺陷：在端到端的重构训练中，门控机制会坍缩至单一专家，导致包含 K 个 LUT 的库仅能发挥出一个 LUT 的效能。我们引入了一个熵项（作为专家混合模型负载均衡在图像增强领域的类比），成功恢复了利用率并提升了约 1 dB 的峰值信噪比（PSNR）。

The deeper constraint survives: a fixed LUT basis is closed-set, freezing the achievable looks at training time. We therefore discard the basis and predict a single 3D LUT as a residual from a reference image (StyleLUTNet), trained by self-supervision on procedurally generated color transforms. The conditional design removes the gate and generalizes open-set to unseen film stocks without paired data or retraining. 

然而，更深层次的限制依然存在：固定的 LUT 基底属于“封闭集”，将可实现的视觉效果锁定在了训练阶段。因此，我们摒弃了这种基底，转而通过参考图像预测单个 3D LUT 作为残差（StyleLUTNet），并利用程序生成的色彩变换进行自监督训练。这种条件化设计去除了门控机制，实现了对未见过的胶片类型的开放集泛化，且无需配对数据或重新训练。

Around this color backbone we build Deep Analog, a film-emulation pipeline that adds histogram-based tone matching and a physics-informed optical renderer -- multi-scale grain and per-channel halation driven by parameters an inverse network regresses from the reference. 

围绕这一色彩核心，我们构建了 Deep Analog 胶片模拟流水线。该流水线增加了基于直方图的色调匹配，以及一个物理驱动的光学渲染器——通过逆向网络从参考图像中回归出的参数，来驱动多尺度颗粒感和各通道的光晕效果。

On 350 self-supervised pairs the color stage reaches 22.05 dB PSNR / 0.925 SSIM and the full pipeline 21.72 dB / 0.923; the color path runs in 5.2 ms at 1080p (192 FPS) and exports a portable .cube LUT for standard editing tools. A second degeneracy in conditional LUT training -- residual-scale collapse -- shares the root cause and yields a general principle: auxiliary regularization must stay subordinate to reconstruction.

在 350 对自监督数据上，色彩阶段达到了 22.05 dB PSNR / 0.925 SSIM，完整流水线达到了 21.72 dB / 0.923；色彩处理路径在 1080p 分辨率下运行速度为 5.2 毫秒（192 FPS），并可导出适用于标准编辑软件的便携式 .cube LUT 文件。我们在条件化 LUT 训练中发现的第二种退化现象——残差尺度坍缩——与上述问题有着共同的根源，并得出一个通用原则：辅助正则化必须从属于重构任务。
