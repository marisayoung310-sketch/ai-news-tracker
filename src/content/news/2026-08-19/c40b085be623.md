# DumpsterCluster: From Dumpster Diving to Serving LLaMA-70B on $60 GPUs

**Abstract:** As AI datacenters retire functional GPUs, vast quantities of still capable accelerators enter secondary markets. This paper investigates whether these retired GPUs can find a productive afterlife to form a DumpsterCluster that can serve modern LLM inference, and under what conditions such repurposing is economically viable and environmentally sustainable.

**摘要：** 随着人工智能数据中心淘汰功能完好的 GPU，大量仍具备处理能力的加速器流入二手市场。本文探讨了这些退役 GPU 是否能通过组建“垃圾堆集群”（DumpsterCluster）获得“第二次生命”，以支持现代大语言模型（LLM）的推理任务，并研究了在何种条件下，这种再利用在经济上可行且在环境上可持续。

We physically built a 128-GPU DumpsterCluster from scratch using only second-hand components and ran it for one year. At current market prices (\$22K for the DumpsterCluster vs. \$600K for an 8-GPU B200 system), the economic advantages are substantial. Through pipeline-parallel optimizations, our V100 based DumpsterCluster achieves competitive LLaMA-70B throughput, validating production viability.

我们完全使用二手组件从零构建了一个拥有 128 个 GPU 的“垃圾堆集群”，并运行了一年。按当前市场价格计算（该集群成本为 2.2 万美元，而 8 卡 B200 系统则需 60 万美元），其经济优势十分显著。通过流水线并行优化，我们基于 V100 的集群实现了极具竞争力的 LLaMA-70B 推理吞吐量，验证了其生产环境的可行性。

However, our deployment reveals critical context dependencies. Older GPUs consume significantly more energy per token, making total cost of ownership favorable only in regions with inexpensive electricity. Under grid-average carbon intensity, second-hand systems can produce approximately 4x higher total carbon emissions per token for 8B models, and over 40x for 70B models, compared to current-generation hardware.

然而，我们的部署也揭示了关键的制约因素。旧款 GPU 在处理每个 Token 时消耗的能量显著更高，这意味着只有在电价低廉的地区，其总拥有成本（TCO）才具有优势。在电网平均碳强度下，与当前一代硬件相比，二手系统在运行 8B 模型时产生的单位 Token 总碳排放量约高出 4 倍，而在运行 70B 模型时则高出 40 倍以上。

These findings show that GPU afterlife is not universally sustainable - hardware repurposing must be strategically coupled with low carbon energy sources. When deployed in regions with favourable energy economics and clean electricity, second-hand GPUs offer a viable pathway for expanding AI capacity while advancing affordability, energy security, and environmental responsibility.

这些发现表明，GPU 的“第二次生命”并非在任何情况下都可持续——硬件再利用必须与低碳能源战略性地结合。当部署在能源经济性良好且电力清洁的地区时，二手 GPU 为扩展 AI 算力提供了一条切实可行的途径，同时兼顾了经济性、能源安全和环境责任。
