# Path2ST: Hierarchical Cell-Tissue Grounded Cross-Modal Translation for Spatial Transcriptomics
# Path2ST：用于空间转录组学的分层细胞-组织基础跨模态翻译

**Abstract:** Predicting spatial gene expression from hematoxylin and eosin (H&E)-stained images offers a cost-effective alternative to spatial transcriptomics (ST). However, existing methods treat H&E images as generic visual inputs and ignore their intrinsic biological hierarchy, where spatially organized cell types collectively form functional tissue microenvironments that govern local gene expression programs.

**摘要：** 从苏木精-伊红（H&E）染色图像中预测空间基因表达，为空间转录组学（ST）提供了一种经济高效的替代方案。然而，现有方法将 H&E 图像视为通用的视觉输入，忽略了其内在的生物学层次结构——即空间组织的细胞类型共同构成了控制局部基因表达程序的功能性组织微环境。

To bridge this gap, we formulate H&E-to-ST prediction as a cross-modal semantic translation task and propose Path2ST, a hierarchically grounded autoregressive framework featuring three key components: (i) a Hierarchical Cell-Tissue Conditioning mechanism that fuses explicit and implicit cellular features with tissue-level semantic representations to construct hierarchical conditioning signals; (ii) a Scale-Adaptive Autoregressive Generation process over a hierarchical semantic vocabulary, enabling coarse-to-fine, biologically consistent expression synthesis; and (iii) SpectraLoss, a full-spectrum objective that jointly enforces ordinal fidelity, models transcriptional bursts, and aligns semantic structures with cell types.

为了弥补这一差距，我们将 H&E 到 ST 的预测建模为一项跨模态语义翻译任务，并提出了 Path2ST。这是一个具有分层基础的自回归框架，包含三个关键组件：(i) 分层细胞-组织调节机制，它将显式和隐式细胞特征与组织级语义表示相融合，以构建分层调节信号；(ii) 基于分层语义词汇表的尺度自适应自回归生成过程，实现了从粗到细、生物学一致的表达合成；以及 (iii) SpectraLoss，这是一种全谱目标函数，旨在共同强化序数保真度、模拟转录爆发，并将语义结构与细胞类型对齐。

Extensive experiments on three datasets demonstrate state-of-the-art performance, validating that Path2ST generates highly accurate and spatially coherent transcriptomic profiles. The related code is released at this https URL.

在三个数据集上的大量实验证明了该方法达到了最先进的性能，验证了 Path2ST 能够生成高度准确且空间连贯的转录组图谱。相关代码已发布于此链接。
