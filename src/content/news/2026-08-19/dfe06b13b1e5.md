# Fairphone 6 and PostmarketOS working main camera
# Fairphone 6 与 PostmarketOS 主摄像头适配进展

Today i bring the working main camera! Building on the work nondescriptpointer did on the wide lens camera i have written the driver for the main camera and now its working alongside auto focus and color correction.
今天我带来了主摄像头的适配成果！在 nondescriptpointer 对广角镜头所做工作的基础上，我编写了主摄像头的驱动程序，目前它已经可以正常工作，并支持自动对焦和色彩校正。

The color correction still a work in progress, but you can already see how much it improved the image.
色彩校正工作仍在进行中，但你已经可以看到它对图像质量的显著提升。

But the after is still very grainy, plasma camera storing in jpg isn't exactly helping either, ill be working to get rid of the grain. If we look at the same scene form my android galaxy A16 you see that there still is alot of work to be done.
不过处理后的图像仍然有很多噪点，Plasma 相机以 JPG 格式存储数据也无济于事，我接下来会致力于消除这些噪点。如果我们对比一下我那台安卓 Galaxy A16 拍摄的同一场景，就会发现还有很多工作要做。

I'll continue to work on the camera, but i wanted to get something out today (mainly so i could send to nondescriptpointer xd). I asked him what role he wanted to play in the upstreaming and we agreed he would send it and i would review and assist. So i successfully pulled down another soul into the linux phone kernel rabbit hole muhahahahaha.
我会继续优化相机，但今天我想先发布一些成果（主要是为了发给 nondescriptpointer，哈哈）。我询问了他想在代码上游化中扮演什么角色，我们商定由他提交代码，我负责审查和协助。就这样，我成功地把另一个人拉进了 Linux 手机内核的深坑里，哈哈哈。

### Shipping restrictions
### 运输限制

I'm sad to announce this but i don't think i can do worldwide shipping, earlier i said i would be shipping worldwide but i must sadly retract that statement for reasons out of my control. I will be shipping worldwide with the following exceptions:
很遗憾地通知大家，我恐怕无法进行全球发货。之前我说过可以全球发货，但由于一些我无法控制的原因，我不得不收回这一承诺。我将进行全球发货，但以下地区除外：

*   **US, CA:** It's seemingly impossible to get a Bedrijfsaansprakelijkheidsverzekering (corporate liability insurance) for the United States and Canada in the Netherlands, its all worldwide excluding US and CA. Getting coverage for those requires "contact us" with probably a very hefty premium, and selling without insurance is too risky as that would mean financial ruin if i get sued.
    **美国、加拿大：** 在荷兰似乎无法为美国和加拿大购买企业责任保险，保险范围通常是“全球除美加外”。要获得这两个地区的承保需要“联系我们”，且保费可能非常昂贵。在没有保险的情况下销售风险太大，一旦被起诉可能导致破产。
*   **RU, BY, KP:** Sanctions make it a criminal offense for me to ship to these countries.
    **俄罗斯、白俄罗斯、朝鲜：** 由于制裁，向这些国家发货对我来说属于刑事犯罪。

### Making a Linux phone
### 制造 Linux 手机

Ever since this project i've been dreaming about making my own linux phone, i've been looking into it here and there and while this might just be the sleep deprivation talking i think i can do it. Making a good linux phone however is the hard part, and i don't think i can make an better arm linux phone then the fairphone 6 as those qualcomm chips are impossible to buy. So im not going to, i'll make a RISCV one. will it be bad? yes, will it end up like the pinephone? most probably, will it run hot and have terrible battery life?, most like
自从开始这个项目，我就一直梦想着制造自己的 Linux 手机。我一直在各处研究，虽然这可能只是我睡眠不足时的胡言乱语，但我认为我能做到。然而，制造一部优秀的 Linux 手机很难，我不认为我能制造出比 Fairphone 6 更好的 ARM 架构 Linux 手机，因为那些高通芯片根本买不到。所以我不会走这条路，我打算制造一部 RISC-V 架构的手机。它会很烂吗？是的。它会像 PinePhone 那样吗？很有可能。它会发热严重且电池续航极差吗？很有可能。
