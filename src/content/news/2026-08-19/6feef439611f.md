# Claude Code 2026 年 5 月至 8 月每周使用限额促销活动

**We're offering a limited-time promotion that increases weekly usage limits in Claude Code by 50%. Note: We've extended this promotion. Increased weekly limits now run through August 31, 2026.**
我们正在推出一项限时促销活动，将 Claude Code 的每周使用限额提高 50%。注：我们已延长了此次促销活动，提高后的每周限额将持续至 2026 年 8 月 31 日。

**This promotion is available for Pro, Max, and Team plans, as well as legacy seat-based users on Enterprise plans. Free plans and consumption-based Enterprise seats are not included in this promotion.**
此促销活动适用于 Pro、Max 和 Team 计划，以及企业计划中的传统席位用户。免费计划和基于用量的企业席位不包含在此次促销范围内。

**What is the promotion? From May 13, 2026 through August 31, 2026, your weekly usage limit in Claude Code is 50% higher. 5-hour usage limits are not affected by this promotion.**
什么是促销活动？从 2026 年 5 月 13 日至 2026 年 8 月 31 日，您的 Claude Code 每周使用限额将提高 50%。5 小时使用限额不受此促销活动影响。

**Eligibility: No action is required to participate. If you're on an eligible plan, the increased weekly limit is automatically applied to your account.**
参与资格：无需进行任何操作即可参与。如果您使用的是符合条件的计划，提高后的每周限额将自动应用于您的账户。

**Where does this apply? The 50% increase applies to Claude Code only, everywhere you use it: the CLI, IDE extensions, desktop, and the web. Usage limits for other Claude products, like Claude (web, desktop, and mobile) and Claude Cowork, are unchanged.**
适用范围：此次 50% 的提升仅适用于 Claude Code，涵盖您使用它的所有平台：CLI、IDE 扩展、桌面端和网页端。其他 Claude 产品（如 Claude 网页版、桌面版、移动版以及 Claude Cowork）的使用限额保持不变。

**Frequently asked questions**
常见问题解答

**Do I need to do anything to get the extra usage? No. The promotion applies automatically. You'll see the higher weekly limit reflected in your Claude Code usage without any changes to your account settings. You can run /usage in the CLI to view your updated limits.**
我需要做些什么来获得额外的使用额度吗？不需要。促销活动会自动生效。您无需更改任何账户设置，即可在 Claude Code 的使用情况中看到提高后的每周限额。您可以在 CLI 中运行 `/usage` 命令来查看更新后的限额。

**Does this change my 5-hour usage limit? No. This promotion increases weekly usage limits only.**
这会改变我的 5 小时使用限额吗？不会。此促销活动仅提高每周使用限额。

**I'm on an Enterprise plan. Am I included? Legacy seat-based users on Enterprise plans are included. Consumption-based Enterprise seats are not included in this promotion.**
我使用的是企业计划，我包含在内吗？企业计划中的传统席位用户包含在内。基于用量的企业席位不包含在此次促销中。

**What happens when the promotion ends? After August 31, 2026, weekly usage limits in Claude Code return to their standard levels. There's no change to your plan or billing.**
促销活动结束后会怎样？2026 年 8 月 31 日之后，Claude Code 的每周使用限额将恢复至标准水平。您的计划或账单不会有任何变动。

**Terms and conditions: This offer is valid from May 13, 2026 through August 31, 2026 at 11:59 PM PT. It applies to Pro, Max, and Team plans and to legacy seat-based users on Enterprise plans only, and excludes Free plans and consumption-based Enterprise seats. This offer has no cash value and is not transferable. It may not be combined with other offers.**
条款与细则：此优惠有效期为 2026 年 5 月 13 日至 2026 年 8 月 31 日太平洋时间晚上 11:59。仅适用于 Pro、Max 和 Team 计划以及企业计划中的传统席位用户，不包括免费计划和基于用量的企业席位。此优惠无现金价值，不可转让，且不可与其他优惠同时使用。
