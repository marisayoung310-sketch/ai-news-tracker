# OpenAI GPT-5.6 Launch Reshapes Its Model Line With Sol, Terra and Luna
# OpenAI 发布 GPT-5.6 系列，通过 Sol、Terra 和 Luna 重塑模型产品线

OpenAI has rolled out the GPT-5.6 family, introducing three models intended to cover advanced professional work, balanced deployments and high-volume workloads. The July 9, 2026 general-availability launch of Sol, Terra and Luna marks a significant step in OpenAI's effort to consolidate its model portfolio across ChatGPT and its API, while moving customers away from older GPT-4-era offerings.

OpenAI 推出了 GPT-5.6 系列，引入了三款旨在覆盖高级专业工作、平衡部署和高容量工作负载的模型。2026 年 7 月 9 日，Sol、Terra 和 Luna 的正式发布，标志着 OpenAI 在整合其 ChatGPT 和 API 模型组合方面迈出了重要一步，同时也推动客户从旧的 GPT-4 时代产品转向新一代模型。

The company's official GPT-5.6 announcement positions the generation as a higher-performance foundation for the ChatGPT experience and API use cases involving agents and coding. Rather than presenting a single general-purpose release, OpenAI has divided the family into distinct options: Sol for advanced professional work, Terra for a balance of capability and cost, and Luna for cost-sensitive, high-volume tasks. That segmentation matters because model selection is becoming a deployment decision rather than simply a question of accessing the newest available system. Teams building production workflows need to weigh performance requirements, usage volume, migration work and the cost profile of each application.

OpenAI 的官方公告将这一代模型定位为 ChatGPT 体验以及涉及智能体（Agents）和编程的 API 用例的高性能基础。OpenAI 没有发布单一的通用模型，而是将该系列划分为不同的选项：Sol 用于高级专业工作，Terra 用于平衡能力与成本，Luna 则用于对成本敏感的高容量任务。这种细分至关重要，因为模型选择已成为一项部署决策，而不仅仅是访问最新系统的问题。构建生产工作流的团队需要权衡性能需求、使用量、迁移工作以及每个应用程序的成本状况。

### What the GPT-5.6 rollout changes
### GPT-5.6 的发布带来了哪些变化

The general-availability announcement was followed by a July 30, 2026 pricing update that reduced Luna pricing by around 80% and Terra pricing by around 20%. OpenAI also signaled the phase-out of older models, including GPT-4o and related GPT-4.x variants, as customers move toward GPT-5.x and GPT-5.6 offerings. Taken together, the launch and subsequent price adjustments show that the GPT-5.6 family is not only a model update. It is part of a broader product lifecycle shift. OpenAI's roadmap messaging has emphasized more unified experiences across ChatGPT and API surfaces, and the new family gives that strategy a clearer set of deployment tiers.

在正式发布公告后，OpenAI 于 2026 年 7 月 30 日更新了定价，将 Luna 的价格降低了约 80%，Terra 的价格降低了约 20%。OpenAI 还表示将逐步淘汰旧模型，包括 GPT-4o 及相关的 GPT-4.x 变体，引导客户转向 GPT-5.x 和 GPT-5.6 产品。综合来看，此次发布及随后的价格调整表明，GPT-5.6 系列不仅是一次模型更新，更是更广泛的产品生命周期转型的一部分。OpenAI 的路线图强调了在 ChatGPT 和 API 界面之间提供更统一的体验，而这一新系列为该战略提供了更清晰的部署层级。
