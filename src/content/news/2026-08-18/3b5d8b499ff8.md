# What to Preserve, Where to Adapt: A Depth-Wise Analysis of Forgetting in Continual Gynecological Image Segmentation
# 保留什么，适配哪里：妇科图像连续分割中遗忘现象的深度分析

**Abstract:** Medical image segmentation models are typically trained under the assumption that all data are available simultaneously. However, in clinical practice, datasets often arrive sequentially, requiring models to adapt continuously to evolving data distributions. We study this problem in gynecological image segmentation, where substantial heterogeneity across imaging modalities, anatomical structures, and annotation protocols creates a particularly challenging continual learning setting.

**摘要：** 医学图像分割模型通常在所有数据同时可用的假设下进行训练。然而，在临床实践中，数据集往往是按顺序到达的，这要求模型能够持续适应不断变化的数据分布。我们研究了妇科图像分割中的这一问题，由于成像模态、解剖结构和标注协议之间存在显著的异质性，这创造了一个极具挑战性的持续学习环境。

Under these large distribution shifts, existing continual learning methods struggle to preserve previously learned knowledge, leading to catastrophic forgetting. To better understand forgetting in this setting, we investigate how different encoder--decoder regions influence segmentation performance and forgetting during continual gynecological segmentation.

在这些巨大的分布偏移下，现有的持续学习方法难以保留先前学到的知识，从而导致灾难性遗忘。为了更好地理解该环境下的遗忘现象，我们研究了在妇科图像连续分割过程中，编码器-解码器的不同区域如何影响分割性能和遗忘程度。

Through block-wise ablation analysis, we observe that ablating early encoder and late decoder regions results in the largest performance degradation, indicating that segmentation performance depends unevenly across the network hierarchy. Using controlled adaptation experiments, we further show that forgetting remains limited when updates are restricted to bottleneck-adjacent regions, but increases sharply once shallower encoders and decoders become trainable, even when only a small subset of parameters is updated.

通过分块消融分析，我们观察到消融编码器早期和解码器后期的区域会导致最大的性能下降，这表明分割性能对网络层级的依赖是不均匀的。通过受控的适配实验，我们进一步证明，当更新仅限于瓶颈层附近的区域时，遗忘现象仍然有限；但一旦较浅层的编码器和解码器变得可训练，即使只更新一小部分参数，遗忘现象也会急剧增加。

These findings suggest that forgetting in the encoder-decoder architecture is strongly influenced by where updates occur across network depth during continual learning. Full code and analysis pipelines will be made publicly available upon acceptance.

这些发现表明，在持续学习过程中，编码器-解码器架构中的遗忘现象受到网络深度更新位置的强烈影响。完整的代码和分析流程将在论文被录用后公开。
