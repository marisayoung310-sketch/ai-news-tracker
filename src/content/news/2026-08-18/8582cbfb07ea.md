### Best Budget Laptop: HP OmniBook 5
### 最佳预算笔记本电脑：HP OmniBook 5

Thanks to Qualcomm's Snapdragon X chips, we finally have affordable Windows laptops that get great battery life. The HP OmniBook 5 14 is the best among those, so long as you can get it on discount. For starters, it has great specs, including 16 GB of RAM, 512 GB of storage, and a 14-inch OLED screen. That screen, in particular, is a big deal for laptops at this price, as OLED displays offer such a massive step up from standard IPS displays. Its display isn't as sharp or bright as the MacBook Neo or Dell 14 Plus, though.

得益于高通的骁龙 X 芯片，我们终于拥有了续航表现出色的平价 Windows 笔记本电脑。只要你能买到折扣价，HP OmniBook 5 14 就是其中的佼佼者。首先，它的配置非常出色，包括 16 GB 内存、512 GB 存储空间和 14 英寸 OLED 屏幕。特别是这块屏幕，对于这个价位的笔记本电脑来说意义重大，因为 OLED 显示屏比标准的 IPS 显示屏有了巨大的提升。不过，它的屏幕清晰度和亮度不如 MacBook Neo 或 Dell 14 Plus。

The HP OmniBook 5 is a highly portable little laptop, at just a half-inch thick. The touchpad is probably its biggest downside. Like many budget laptops, the touchpad feels sticky to use, making precise movements difficult. The keyboard is great, though, and the addition of a USB-A port is clutch.

HP OmniBook 5 是一款非常便携的小型笔记本电脑，厚度仅为半英寸。触控板可能是它最大的缺点。和许多预算型笔记本电脑一样，它的触控板使用起来感觉有些粘滞，难以进行精确操作。不过，它的键盘手感很好，而且增加了一个 USB-A 接口非常实用。

There's a big caveat with the OmniBook 5's price. It retails for $1,300, but it’s often discounted at HP.com. I can typically find a configuration that sells for less than $700, sometimes as low as $550.

关于 OmniBook 5 的价格有一个重要的提醒。它的零售价为 1300 美元，但在 HP.com 上经常有折扣。我通常能找到售价低于 700 美元的配置，有时甚至低至 550 美元。

### Best Budget MacBook: Apple MacBook Neo
### 最佳预算 MacBook：Apple MacBook Neo

This one's easy. If you've always dreamed of owning a MacBook, but it was never in your price range, the MacBook Neo (7/10, WIRED Recommends) is for you. It has plenty of shortcomings, mind you. Between the speed of USB-C ports and the lack of keyboard backlighting, Apple made lots of small compromises to get the price down to $699. Just 8 GB of RAM is the most serious detriment. You'll notice that every other laptop on this list starts at 16 GB, which has become the new standard. In my own testing, I was able to create a memory bottleneck without too much effort. But as a device for students, or just if you work at an office all day on a work computer and need something simple for weekends and evenings, the MacBook Neo’s performance should work well for you.

这款的选择很简单。如果你一直梦想拥有一台 MacBook，但价格总是不在你的预算范围内，那么 MacBook Neo（评分 7/10，WIRED 推荐）就是为你准备的。当然，它有很多缺点。从 USB-C 接口的速度到缺乏键盘背光，苹果为了将价格降至 699 美元做了许多小的妥协。仅 8 GB 的内存是最严重的短板。你会注意到这份名单上的其他所有笔记本电脑起步都是 16 GB，这已经成为新的标准。在我的测试中，我很容易就让内存出现了瓶颈。但作为学生设备，或者如果你整天在办公室使用工作电脑，只需要在周末和晚上用它处理简单任务，MacBook Neo 的性能应该足够满足你的需求。

Its real strength is in its front-of-screen experience. It's the feeling you get every time you open the laptop, and that's where the MacBook Neo excels. A lot of that has to do with build quality and design, which are no less refined than the MacBooks that cost thousands more—with a dash of playfulness, thanks to the white keyboard and bolder colors. But the display is also worthy of recognition. It's both sharper and brighter than all its competitors, except for the Dell XPS 13.

它真正的优势在于屏幕前的体验。每次打开笔记本电脑时，你都能感受到那种质感，这正是 MacBook Neo 的出色之处。这很大程度上归功于其做工质量和设计，它们与售价高出数千美元的 MacBook 相比毫不逊色——得益于白色键盘和更鲜艳的配色，它还带有一丝俏皮感。此外，它的显示屏也值得称赞。除了 Dell XPS 13 之外，它的清晰度和亮度都超过了所有竞争对手。

### The Best Laptop for Under $1,000: Dell XPS 13 (2026)
### 1000 美元以下最佳笔记本电脑：Dell XPS 13 (2026)

I never thought a Windows laptop could pull off the trick of blending performance and affordability as well as the MacBook Neo, but the new Dell XPS 13 does it even better. The XPS 13 makes very few compromises, whether that's the portable design, the excellent display, the smooth trackpad, or the high speeds of the USB-C ports.

我从未想过有一款 Windows 笔记本电脑能在性能和性价比的平衡上做得像 MacBook Neo 一样好，但新款 Dell XPS 13 做得甚至更好。XPS 13 几乎没有做出什么妥协，无论是便携的设计、出色的显示屏、流畅的触控板，还是高速的 USB-C 接口，都表现优异。

It has a couple of one-ups over the MacBook Neo, too. Some are quality-of-life improvements, like the backlit keyboard, the ability to charge from either side of the device, or the half-pound lighter weight. Other features are more substantial, such as the addition of a touchscreen and the 120-Hz refresh rate of the display. The 8 GB of RAM in the starting configuration is a severe limitation, but upgrading to the 16-GB model at $900 yields an incredible value. The 16-GB model is the one most people should buy, even if the $700 version with 8 GB is more tempting for budget-conscious buyers. Just be aware that 8 GB of memory in a modern Windows 11 laptop will start slowing down without too much effort.

它在几个方面也比 MacBook Neo 更胜一筹。有些是体验上的提升，比如背光键盘、支持两侧充电，或者轻了半磅的重量。其他功能则更为实质，例如增加了触摸屏和 120Hz 的显示刷新率。起步配置中的 8 GB 内存是一个严重的限制，但升级到 900 美元的 16 GB 型号则具有极高的性价比。大多数人应该购买 16 GB 的型号，尽管 700 美元的 8 GB 版本对预算敏感型买家来说更具诱惑力。但请注意，在现代 Windows 11 笔记本电脑中，8 GB 内存很容易就会出现运行缓慢的情况。
