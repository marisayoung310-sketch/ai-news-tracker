# CookUnity Prepared Meal Delivery Review (2026): Chef-Centric Meals
# CookUnity 预制餐配送测评（2026）：以厨师为核心的餐食

**WIRED 测评**

**TIRED**
**不足之处**

Flavorful, filling, actually delicious prepared meals delivered to your home. Meals designed by restaurant chefs. No microwave required. Wide variety of cuisines represented. Many meals delivered the same day they're packed.
美味、饱腹且真正好吃的预制餐直接送到您家。餐点由餐厅主厨设计。无需微波炉。涵盖多种菜系。许多餐点在包装当天即可送达。

Quality and selection may differ depending on region and chef. Not all areas served. Must enter contact information before seeing the week’s menu.

My lunch felt impossible. Was my fried chicken actually … crispy and moist?
我的午餐感觉有些不可思议。我的炸鸡真的……又酥脆又多汁吗？

This result was by no means likely. After all, I'd received this chicken in a sealed microwavable tray three days ago, by courier from Seattle to Portland, Oregon. It seemed unlikely that my Night Market Chicken from meal delivery service CookUnity would stand up to the harsh light of day when reheated.
这个结果绝非易事。毕竟，这份炸鸡是我三天前通过快递从西雅图运到俄勒冈州波特兰的，装在密封的微波炉餐盒里。我本以为来自 CookUnity 配送服务的“夜市炸鸡”在重新加热后，很难经得起考验。

And yet, here we were. When heated in an air fryer, the chicken's breading was nicely crisp. The meat was tender, juicy even. The Asian-influenced sesame-soy glaze was pleasantly sweet and tangy, paired with a heaping portion of egg fried rice. If this had been takeout food from a restaurant down the street, I would have accepted it gladly.
然而，事实证明它确实不错。用空气炸锅加热后，鸡肉的外皮非常酥脆，肉质鲜嫩，甚至还很多汁。带有亚洲风味的芝麻酱油釉汁酸甜适口，搭配上一大份蛋炒饭。如果这是街角餐厅的外卖，我会非常乐意接受。

In fact, a number of the meals I've tasted from CookUnity over the past two years have rivaled restaurant takeout—a genuine rarity among prepared delivery meals. Most of the big meal delivery services have branched out into ready-to-heat prepared meals, and I've tried most of them. CookUnity is the best I've tried by a pretty long margin, while maintaining similar prices: $12 to $13 a meal on the most common plans.
事实上，过去两年我品尝过的许多 CookUnity 餐点都足以媲美餐厅外卖——这在预制配送餐中确实罕见。大多数大型送餐服务都已涉足加热即食领域，我几乎都尝试过。CookUnity 是我试过最好的一家，优势非常明显，同时价格也保持在相近水平：最常见的套餐计划每餐仅需 12 到 13 美元。

CookUnity stands out for two reasons. One is that it's less reliant on the microwave—offering alternate instructions that still usually only take five or 10 minutes. The other is that the meals are prepared in kitchens run by actual restaurant chefs, who each put their name and face on the box.
CookUnity 脱颖而出有两个原因。一是它不那么依赖微波炉，提供了通常只需 5 到 10 分钟的替代加热说明。二是这些餐点是在由真正的餐厅主厨经营的厨房中准备的，每位厨师都会将自己的名字和头像印在包装盒上。

My tasty Night Market Chicken came from the Seattle commissary of Michelin-decorated LA chef Kevin Meehan. And while I doubt Michelin will come calling this time, it was a genuine prize winner among work lunches hoovered over my laptop. And while last year I worried that veggies were a rarity, this year CookUnity's meals have expanded in both quantity and variety.
我那份美味的“夜市炸鸡”出自洛杉矶米其林星级厨师 Kevin Meehan 的西雅图中央厨房。虽然我怀疑这次米其林不会因此上门，但它确实是我在笔记本电脑前匆忙解决的工作午餐中的佼佼者。去年我曾担心蔬菜种类稀少，但今年 CookUnity 的餐点在数量和种类上都有了显著增加。

### The Chef-Centric Delivery Model / 以厨师为核心的配送模式
The reason CookUnity is not like other prepared meal services is that, quite frankly, it's not like other prepared meal services. CookUnity is a singular model, something like a cross between a meal service and DoorDash.
CookUnity 与其他预制餐服务不同的原因在于，坦率地说，它的模式独树一帜。CookUnity 是一种独特的模式，类似于餐食服务与 DoorDash 的结合体。

Rather than make recipes in a top-down corporate kitchen, CookUnity is regional. The service enlists local and national chefs and promises to bring restaurant-quality meals to the home—prepared according to recipes from occasionally quite big names. (Hello, Jose Garces. Hello, Esther Choi.)
CookUnity 并非在自上而下的企业厨房中制作食谱，而是采取区域化运营。该服务招募当地和全国性的厨师，承诺将餐厅品质的餐点带回家中——这些餐点均根据有时甚至是名厨的食谱制作。（比如 Jose Garces 和 Esther Choi。）

Like a lot of meal kits, CookUnity is a subscription service. You choose the number of prepared meals that you want delivered each week—and then pick out your meals from that week's menu. It's around $12 a meal if you get 10 portions a week—or more than $15 if you only want four meals. This is more than scratch cooking or a frozen dinner, but less than restaurant delivery or takeout.
像许多餐包服务一样，CookUnity 是一种订阅服务。您选择每周配送的预制餐数量，然后从当周菜单中挑选餐点。如果每周订购 10 份，每餐约 12 美元；如果只订购 4 份，则每餐超过 15 美元。这比自己做饭或吃冷冻晚餐贵，但比餐厅配送或外卖便宜。

The meal service is run out of eight regional commissary hubs around the United States and Canada: Seattle, LA, Austin, Chicago, New York, Atlanta, Miami, and Toronto. Each hub has its own stable of chefs: Some are local. Some are kitchens operated by national chefs. This does have the side effect of leaving out most of West Virginia and a good swath of the Plains states.
该服务通过美国和加拿大的八个区域中央厨房运营：西雅图、洛杉矶、奥斯汀、芝加哥、纽约、亚特兰大、迈阿密和多伦多。每个中心都有自己的厨师团队：有些是当地厨师，有些是全国性名厨经营的厨房。这也导致西弗吉尼亚州大部分地区和平原州的大片区域无法享受该服务。

If your zip code is covered, it goes like this: You sign up for a certain number of single-serve meals per week, which arrive in heatable trays in a big, cold-packed box. A representative menu for each region is available here, but you won't see any given week's menu where you live till you've entered your zip code and handed out your email address. (I know, I don't love this either.)
如果您的邮政编码在覆盖范围内，流程如下：您注册每周配送一定数量的单人份餐点，它们会装在可加热的餐盒中，放在一个大型冷藏包装箱里送达。各地区的代表性菜单可以在此查看，但在输入邮政编码并提供电子邮件地址之前，您无法看到所在地区的当周菜单。（我知道，我也不喜欢这样。）

Generally, if you order by Thursday, you can have delivery of your week's meals by Monday. Meals can be filtered for meal type (salad, meats and twos, pasta, et al.), vegan, vegetarian, GLP-1, fiber-rich, precise calorie counts (whether high or low), and a whole host of allergens. They can also be sorted by chef, and range from dainty 500-calorie lunches to 1,200-calorie feasts like that Night Market Chicken.
通常，如果您在周四前下单，周一就能收到当周的餐点。餐点可以按类型（沙拉、肉类配菜、意面等）、纯素、素食、GLP-1、高纤维、精确卡路里（高或低）以及各种过敏原进行筛选。它们还可以按厨师排序，从精致的 500 卡路里午餐到像“夜市炸鸡”那样 1,200 卡路里的盛宴应有尽有。

Each meal arrives in a tray that looks like a TV dinner, and I can microwave it in three minutes if I want. I usually don't, because each meal also has “chef-recommended” heating methods for optimal results. Maybe this involves a 10-minute turn in a toaster oven or a five-minute sauté. (Often, I sub in an air fryer where toaster ovens are suggested, with excellent results.)
每份餐点都装在一个看起来像冷冻快餐的餐盒里，如果愿意，我可以用微波炉在三分钟内加热。但我通常不会这样做，因为每份餐点都有“厨师推荐”的加热方法以达到最佳口感。这可能涉及在小烤箱中加热 10 分钟或在平底锅中翻炒 5 分钟。（通常，在建议使用小烤箱的地方，我会改用空气炸锅，效果非常好。）

### Local Chefs, Local Menus / 当地厨师，当地菜单
One of the most interesting things about CookUnity is that chefs and menus are different for each region. My hub, in Seattle, is among the smallest with only a dozen chefs. But because each chef offers anywhere from eight to 20 meals, my weekly menu in Seattle still ranged above 200 dishes. This is a far greater variety than when I first tried it.
CookUnity 最有趣的一点是，每个地区的厨师和菜单都不同。我所在的西雅图中心规模较小，只有十几位厨师。但由于每位厨师提供 8 到 20 种餐点，我在西雅图的每周菜单仍有 200 多种选择。这比我第一次尝试时种类丰富得多。
