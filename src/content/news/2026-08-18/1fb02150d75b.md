# Robust Dual-Model Collaborative Random Vector Functional Link Network
# 鲁棒双模型协作随机向量函数链接网络

**Abstract:** Random vector functional link (RVFL) networks are lightweight and fast neural models that offer efficient training and strong generalization through randomized hidden-layer weights and direct input-output connections.
**摘要：** 随机向量函数链接（RVFL）网络是一种轻量级且快速的神经模型，通过随机化的隐藏层权重和直接的输入-输出连接，提供了高效的训练和强大的泛化能力。

However, conventional RVFL models are sensitive to noisy labels, outliers, and imbalanced data, which limits their performance in real-world applications.
然而，传统的 RVFL 模型对噪声标签、异常值和不平衡数据较为敏感，这限制了它们在实际应用中的表现。

To address these challenges, we propose the kernel risk-sensitive mean p-power based RVFL (KRPRVFL) model, which integrates the computational efficiency of RVFL with the robustness of the kernel risk-sensitive mean p-power (KRP) criterion.
为了应对这些挑战，我们提出了基于核风险敏感均值 p 次幂的 RVFL（KRPRVFL）模型，该模型将 RVFL 的计算效率与核风险敏感均值 p 次幂（KRP）准则的鲁棒性相结合。

By replacing the standard least-squares objective with a KRP-based loss, KRPRVFL adaptively reduces the influence of corrupted or unreliable samples during training, resulting in improved stability and generalization.
通过用基于 KRP 的损失函数取代标准的最小二乘目标，KRPRVFL 在训练过程中能够自适应地减少损坏或不可靠样本的影响，从而提高了模型的稳定性和泛化能力。

Additionally, a collaborative learning mechanism is introduced to enable adaptive interaction among model components, further enhancing robustness in complex and noisy environments.
此外，我们引入了一种协作学习机制，以实现模型组件之间的自适应交互，进一步增强了在复杂和噪声环境下的鲁棒性。

The proposed framework also leverages kernel-induced feature mapping to capture nonlinear relationships without requiring explicit hidden-layer selection, maintaining both efficiency and scalability.
该框架还利用核诱导特征映射来捕捉非线性关系，而无需显式的隐藏层选择，从而保持了效率和可扩展性。

Extensive experiments on UCI and KEEL benchmark datasets demonstrate that KRPRVFL consistently outperforms baseline models in terms of accuracy, robustness, and statistical significance, highlighting its effectiveness as a fast, scalable, and reliable solution for challenging classification tasks.
在 UCI 和 KEEL 基准数据集上的大量实验表明，KRPRVFL 在准确性、鲁棒性和统计显著性方面始终优于基准模型，凸显了其作为应对挑战性分类任务的一种快速、可扩展且可靠的解决方案的有效性。
