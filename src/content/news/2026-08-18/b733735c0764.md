# PROVE: Training-Free Prompt Recovery using Verifiable Evidence
# PROVE：基于可验证证据的免训练提示词恢复

**Abstract:** Modern text-to-image models can generate highly realistic images from natural-language prompts, while recent advances in prompt inversion have made it increasingly feasible to recover those prompts from generated outputs, raising new concerns for copyright protection and content ownership.

**摘要：** 现代文生图模型能够根据自然语言提示词生成高度逼真的图像，而近期提示词反转（prompt inversion）技术的进步使得从生成结果中恢复这些提示词变得越来越可行，这引发了关于版权保护和内容所有权的新担忧。

As prompt marketplaces emerge, recovered prompts can enable both the unauthorized reproduction and redistribution of copyrighted creative works, and the exposure of the prompts that encode an artist's creative recipe in AI-generated content.

随着提示词交易市场的兴起，恢复出的提示词可能导致受版权保护的创意作品被未经授权地复制和分发，同时也可能泄露艺术家在 AI 生成内容中隐藏的“创意秘方”。

Existing prompt inversion methods rely on gradient-based optimization, autoregressive captioning, or reinforcement learning. However, optimization-based methods often produce unreadable prompts, captioning methods hallucinate unverified details, and RL-based approaches frequently overfit to specific generators while introducing evaluation circularity.

现有的提示词反转方法主要依赖于基于梯度的优化、自回归图像描述（captioning）或强化学习。然而，基于优化的方法往往会产生不可读的提示词，基于描述的方法容易产生未经证实的幻觉细节，而基于强化学习的方法则经常对特定生成器过拟合，并引入评估循环性问题。

We introduce PROVE (Prompt Recovery with Verified Evidence), a training-free, black-box prompt inversion attack that reconstructs prompts by composing verifiable scene descriptions rather than optimizing token sequences, targeting both original copyrighted works and AI-generated content.

我们提出了 PROVE（基于可验证证据的提示词恢复），这是一种无需训练的黑盒提示词反转攻击。它通过组合可验证的场景描述来重构提示词，而非优化标记序列，其目标涵盖了原始版权作品和 AI 生成内容。

The resulting prompts are fully auditable, with every recovered claim grounded in explicit image evidence, and are formalized through a precision-constrained recall maximization objective.

由此生成的提示词完全可审计，每一个恢复出的声明都有明确的图像证据作为支撑，并通过精度约束下的召回率最大化目标进行形式化。

Across MS-COCO, Flickr30K, and Lexica, using state-of-the-art text-to-image generators, PROVE consistently outperforms optimization, captioning, and RL-based baselines on image similarity (DINO, LPIPS) and text-image alignment (CLIP), without any training, generator access, or fine-tuning, demonstrating a stronger and more practical prompt inversion attack.

在 MS-COCO、Flickr30K 和 Lexica 数据集上，使用最先进的文生图生成器进行测试，PROVE 在图像相似度（DINO, LPIPS）和图文对齐（CLIP）指标上始终优于基于优化、描述和强化学习的基准方法。且该方法无需任何训练、生成器访问权限或微调，证明了一种更强大、更实用的提示词反转攻击手段。
