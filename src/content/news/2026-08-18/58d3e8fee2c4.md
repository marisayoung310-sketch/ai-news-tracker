# The Analogue Pocket gets a Supreme makeover in red or gold
# Analogue Pocket 推出 Supreme 联名款，提供红金两色选择

Supreme is launching a few gadgets with its next collection, including metallic Analogue Pocket handhelds.
Supreme 即将在其最新系列中推出几款电子产品，其中包括金属质感的 Analogue Pocket 手持游戏机。

Analogue and Supreme are teaming up to release metallic versions of the Analogue Pocket handheld in red and gold as part of Supreme’s fall / winter 2026 collection.
Analogue 与 Supreme 达成合作，作为 Supreme 2026 秋冬系列的一部分，双方将推出红色和金色的金属版 Analogue Pocket 手持游戏机。

Here’s how they’re described on Supreme’s website: Metallic portable handheld multi-video-game-system. Unibody aluminum with 24K Gold-plated and custom red glossy finishes.
Supreme 官网对该产品的描述如下：金属便携式多功能视频游戏系统。采用一体成型铝合金机身，配有 24K 镀金和定制红色亮面涂层。

They also have a Supreme logo on the front and the back. I dig the colors, but not the logos. (But I’m not really a Supreme guy.)
机身正面和背面均印有 Supreme 的标志。我很喜欢这些配色，但不太喜欢上面的 Logo。（不过我本来就不是 Supreme 的粉丝。）

No price is currently listed on the website for the handhelds. We’ve asked Analogue and Supreme how much they’ll cost. A standard Pocket costs $239.99.
目前官网上尚未公布这款游戏机的售价。我们已向 Analogue 和 Supreme 询问具体价格。作为参考，标准版 Pocket 的售价为 239.99 美元。

Supreme’s fall / winter 2026 collection features a few other gadgety things, too, including a Kodak Super 8 camera, a Dyson V12 Detect vacuum, a Jamo Capsule light, a JBL Go 5 speaker, and a set of wireless microphones and a receiver. The collection will be available on August 20th, Supreme says.
Supreme 2026 秋冬系列还包含其他几款电子产品，包括柯达 Super 8 摄像机、戴森 V12 Detect 吸尘器、Jamo Capsule 灯具、JBL Go 5 扬声器，以及一套无线麦克风和接收器。Supreme 表示，该系列将于 8 月 20 日正式发售。
