# Hard Cases, Bad Labels: Testing Error Exposure and Error Location in Uncertainty Sampling Under Bounded Label Noise
# 困难样本与错误标签：在有界标签噪声下测试不确定性采样中的错误暴露与错误定位

**Abstract:** Active learning can reduce labeling cost by selecting informative examples, but the most uncertain examples may also be the hardest to label correctly. This study tests whether uncertainty sampling fails because it acquires more corrupted labels or because errors concentrated in difficult regions are especially harmful.
**摘要：** 主动学习可以通过选择信息量大的样本来降低标注成本，但最不确定的样本往往也是最难被正确标注的。本研究旨在测试不确定性采样失效的原因：是因为它获取了更多的错误标签，还是因为集中在困难区域的错误具有特别大的危害。

Margin-based uncertainty sampling is compared with random sampling under clean labels, random classification noise (RCN), and bounded difficulty-dependent noise on three public binary tabular datasets. The design uses 100 paired seeds, nine expected noise rates from 0 to 0.30, annotation budgets from 20 to 120, and logistic regression with regularization re-selected by cross-validation at every budget.
在三个公开的二元表格数据集上，研究将基于边界的不确定性采样与随机采样进行了对比，实验环境涵盖了干净标签、随机分类噪声（RCN）以及有界难度相关噪声。实验设计采用了 100 个配对种子、从 0 到 0.30 的九种预期噪声率、20 到 120 的标注预算，并对每个预算点使用通过交叉验证重新选择正则化参数的逻辑回归模型。

An exposure-matched RCN control aligns mean final acquired corruption, while a clean-label extension reaches budget 400. Under clean labels, uncertainty sampling improved normalized balanced-accuracy area under the learning curve by 1.09 to 1.77 percentage points on all datasets.
通过暴露匹配的 RCN 对照组来对齐最终获取的平均噪声水平，同时通过干净标签扩展将预算提升至 400。在干净标签条件下，不确定性采样在所有数据集上的学习曲线下归一化平衡准确率面积（AUC）提升了 1.09 到 1.77 个百分点。

Difficulty-dependent noise reduced this advantage more than RCN at six of eight rates on Breast Cancer Wisconsin, but at no tested rate on Banknote Authentication or MAGIC Gamma Telescope. Exposure-matched analyses found no corrected evidence for a universal additional penalty from structured error location.
在“威斯康星州乳腺癌”（Breast Cancer Wisconsin）数据集上，难度相关噪声在八个噪声率中的六个上比 RCN 更显著地削弱了这种优势，但在“纸币验证”（Banknote Authentication）或“MAGIC 伽马望远镜”（MAGIC Gamma Telescope）数据集上，这种削弱在任何测试噪声率下均未出现。暴露匹配分析未发现结构化错误定位会带来普遍额外惩罚的证据。

On clean MAGIC data, uncertainty sampling improved balanced accuracy while reducing average precision and true-positive rate at fixed false-positive rates. Thus, uncertainty sampling was label-efficient, but its apparent robustness depended on dataset, budget, noise structure, and evaluation metric.
在干净的 MAGIC 数据上，不确定性采样在提升平衡准确率的同时，降低了固定假阳性率下的平均精度和真阳性率。因此，不确定性采样虽然具有标签高效性，但其表现出的鲁棒性取决于数据集、预算、噪声结构以及评估指标。
