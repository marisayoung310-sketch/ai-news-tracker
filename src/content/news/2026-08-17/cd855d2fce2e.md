# Escape the Algorithm and Save Time: Build Your Own Feed with AI Automation
# 逃离算法，节省时间：利用 AI 自动化构建你自己的信息流

I have a problem with my social media feed: there are some people whose content I find really valuable, but on the other hand, there is too much trivial content, so I waste too much time watching low-value videos... BAD DEAL!!!
我的社交媒体信息流出了点问题：有些人的内容对我来说非常有价值，但另一方面，琐碎的内容实在太多了，导致我浪费了太多时间在观看低价值视频上……这太糟糕了！！！

So, let’s solve this issue using AI automation. We can solve this issue by using Telegram as our own social media platform and using automation to make our own feed, far from the algorithms of social media platforms.
那么，让我们用 AI 自动化来解决这个问题。我们可以通过将 Telegram 作为自己的社交媒体平台，并利用自动化工具来打造专属信息流，从而远离各大社交媒体平台的算法干扰。

Choose the people whose content you find really valuable. Use AI automation to monitor them whenever they publish a new video. Once a new video is published, AI sends it to a Telegram bot where you can download it.
挑选出那些你认为内容真正有价值的人。利用 AI 自动化工具监控他们的动态，一旦他们发布新视频，AI 就会将其发送到你的 Telegram 机器人中，方便你随时下载。

The person who gave me this idea is Abo Ziad (the Egyptian version of Ali Abdaal), and he is one of my favorite YouTubers. Personally, I think you can add some entertaining channels to make it more balanced.
给我这个灵感的人是 Abo Ziad（埃及版的 Ali Abdaal），他是我最喜欢的 YouTuber 之一。个人认为，你也可以加入一些娱乐频道，让信息流更加平衡。

I’ll try this solution for one month, and if you’re interested, I’ll share my experience with you, whether it succeeds or fails. In the end, it’s up to you. Tell us in the comments if you found the general idea useful or not, and whether AI automation is the best solution for this issue.
我打算尝试这个方案一个月，如果你感兴趣，无论成功与否，我都会分享我的体验。最终决定权在你。请在评论区告诉我们你是否觉得这个想法有用，以及 AI 自动化是否是解决此问题的最佳方案。
