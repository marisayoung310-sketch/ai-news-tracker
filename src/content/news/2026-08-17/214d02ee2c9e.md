# VisionQuest trailer kicks off Disney's D23 fan event
# 《幻视》预告片拉开迪士尼 D23 粉丝大会序幕

It was a busy first day at D23: The Ultimate Disney Fan Event, in Anaheim, California, with Marvel Studios releasing the first trailer for *VisionQuest*. We also got the first teaser for *Ahsoka* S2; the public release of the *Avengers: Doomsday* special footage featured last month for attendees only at San Diego Comic-Con; a special sneak peek (for attendees only) for *Star Wars: Starfighter*; casting news for the MCU’s X-Men movie; and a trailer for an animated special, *LEGO Star Wars: The Mandalorian*, coming to Disney+ next month.

在加利福尼亚州阿纳海姆举行的“D23：终极迪士尼粉丝大会”第一天行程满满，漫威影业发布了《幻视》（VisionQuest）的首支预告片。此外，我们还看到了《阿索卡》第二季的首个预告片段；上个月仅在圣地亚哥国际动漫展（SDCC）向参会者展示的《复仇者联盟：末日》特别片段现已向公众发布；《星球大战：星际战斗机》（Star Wars: Starfighter）的特别前瞻（仅限参会者）；漫威电影宇宙（MCU）《X战警》电影的选角消息；以及将于下个月登陆 Disney+ 的动画特辑《乐高星球大战：曼达洛人》的预告片。

### VisionQuest
### 《幻视》

Marvel’s streaming TV portfolio has kind of been all over the place: a handful of hits, a few misses, and a bunch of middling offerings. One of the standout hits was *WandaVision*, followed by an excellent sequel (of sorts), *Agatha All Along*. *VisionQuest* will complete the trilogy. Yes, Vision (Paul Bettany) died in *Infinity War*, but *WandaVision* resurrected an all-white version whose memories were restored in the finale. We finally get to learn what happened afterward.

漫威的流媒体电视作品表现一直起伏不定：有几部佳作，也有一些失误，还有不少平庸之作。《旺达幻视》是其中的佼佼者，随后是其优秀的“续作”《阿加莎》。《幻视》将为这一三部曲画上句号。没错，幻视（保罗·贝坦尼饰）在《无限战争》中已经牺牲，但《旺达幻视》复活了一个全白色的版本，并在大结局中恢复了他的记忆。我们终于可以了解在那之后发生了什么。

Per the official premise: Vision, rebooted and having escaped from those who sought to weaponize him, has been in hiding. Searching for new meaning, he consults the AI personas embedded in his programming, including F.R.I.D.A.Y., E.D.I.T.H., J.A.R.V.I.S., and the infamous Ultron. His discreet existence ends when a bounty placed on his head thrusts him on the run with Thomas Shepard, a mysterious boy who may be Vision’s son, reincarnate. As Vision evade capture, he must confront his nature, resist Ultron’s influence, and unravel the enigma that is his young companion if he’s to survive.

根据官方剧情简介：幻视在重启后逃离了那些试图将他武器化的人，一直处于隐居状态。为了寻找新的意义，他咨询了植入其程序中的人工智能人格，包括星期五（F.R.I.D.A.Y.）、伊迪丝（E.D.I.T.H.）、贾维斯（J.A.R.V.I.S.）以及臭名昭著的奥创。当有人对他发出悬赏令，迫使他与托马斯·谢泼德（Thomas Shepard）——一个可能是幻视转世儿子的神秘男孩——一起逃亡时，他低调的生活宣告结束。为了生存，幻视在躲避追捕的同时，必须直面自己的本质，抵御奥创的影响，并解开这位年轻同伴身上的谜团。

### Ahsoka S2
### 《阿索卡》第二季

The Jedi Padawan of Anakin Skywalker, Ahsoka was first introduced as a supporting character in 2008’s animated film *Star Wars: The Clone Wars* and the subsequent animated series. The character returned in the sequel series *Star Wars Rebels* (2014-2018) as a member of the Rebel Alliance, operating under the codename Fulcrum, and made numerous other cameo appearances in the extended Star Wars universe. Rosario Dawson’s live-action version made her debut in *The Mandalorian*’s second season. She also appeared briefly in *The Book of Boba Fett*. And then she got her own series, *Ahsoka*, set in the same timeframe as *The Mandalorian*.

作为安纳金·天行者的绝地学徒，阿索卡最初是 2008 年动画电影《星球大战：克隆人战争》及其后续动画剧集中的配角。该角色在续作《星球大战：义军崛起》（2014-2018）中回归，作为义军同盟的一员，以代号“支点”（Fulcrum）行动，并在星球大战扩展宇宙中多次客串。罗莎里奥·道森饰演的真人版阿索卡在《曼达洛人》第二季中首次亮相。她还在《波巴·费特之书》中短暂出现。随后，她拥有了自己的剧集《阿索卡》，故事背景与《曼达洛人》处于同一时期。

Per the official S2 premise: “Season 2 continues the story of Ahsoka Tano and her extraordinary journey beyond the known galaxy. As Ahsoka and her Jedi apprentice Sabine Wren travel across a mysterious and perilous world to find their way home, their friends Hera, Ezra, Zeb, and Chopper, must face the powerful Grand Admiral Thrawn and his sinister allies, who seek to plunge the New Republic into war.”

根据第二季的官方剧情简介：“第二季继续讲述阿索卡·塔诺的故事，以及她超越已知银河系的非凡旅程。当阿索卡和她的绝地学徒萨宾·雷恩穿越一个神秘而危险的世界寻找回家之路时，她们的朋友赫拉、埃兹拉、泽布和乔巴必须面对强大的索龙元帅及其邪恶盟友，这些人企图将新共和国拖入战争。”

### Avengers: Doomsday
### 《复仇者联盟：末日》

Marvel dropped the first full trailer for *Doomsday* a few days before San Diego Comic-Con last month, and unveiled an exclusive “special look” at additional footage for SDCC attendees. The rest of us are finally getting to see that footage. The film takes place 14 months after the events of *Thunderbolts**. Per the official logline: “Beloved heroes from three distinct universes will be set on a deadly collision course and face an existential threat unlike anything they’ve ever encountered.”

漫威在上个月圣地亚哥国际动漫展前几天发布了《末日》的首支完整预告片，并向 SDCC 参会者展示了额外的独家“特别片段”。现在，我们其他人终于也能看到这些画面了。这部电影的故事发生在《雷霆特工队*》事件 14 个月后。根据官方剧情梗概：“来自三个不同宇宙的挚爱英雄们将陷入一场致命的碰撞，并面临前所未有的生存威胁。”
