# Samsung Galaxy Z Fold8 and Galaxy Z Fold8 Ultra Review: The Right Shape
# 三星 Galaxy Z Fold8 与 Galaxy Z Fold8 Ultra 评测：恰到好处的形态

**WIRED**
**优点：** Fold8 更宽、更短的机身设计使其更易于使用。拥有顶级性能、强大的摄像头、令人满意的电池续航，以及不那么明显的折痕。
**缺点：** Fold8 没有变焦镜头。手机放在平坦表面上会晃动。价格依然非常昂贵。

It took Samsung nearly a decade to realize that maybe folding phones deserve a new shape. For too long, its Fold and Flip smartphones have been designed around the traditional candy bar shape we've all come to know. The Flip folds that candy bar in half to make it even more pocketable, and the Fold adds a second, similarly long and narrow slab to the first for a booklike experience.
三星花了近十年时间才意识到，折叠屏手机或许值得拥有全新的形态。长期以来，其 Fold 和 Flip 系列智能手机的设计都围绕着我们所熟知的传统直板机造型。Flip 将直板机对折，使其更便于携带；而 Fold 则在第一块屏幕的基础上增加了一块同样细长的屏幕，带来类似书本的体验。

The new Galaxy Z Fold8 uses new proportions that feel just right and purpose-built. The device is shorter and wider than Folds past, and yet when you open it up, it feels even more tablet-like than Folds that came before. The Galaxy Z Fold8 Ultra, however, is closer to the taller and slimmer shape most of us are familiar with on Samsung’s book-style foldables.
全新的 Galaxy Z Fold8 采用了全新的比例，感觉恰到好处且目标明确。该设备比以往的 Fold 机型更短、更宽，但当你展开它时，它比之前的 Fold 机型更具平板电脑的质感。然而，Galaxy Z Fold8 Ultra 则更接近我们大多数人所熟悉的、三星书本式折叠屏手机那种又高又窄的形态。

I've spent several weeks using both the Fold8 and the Ultra, and my hands are always reaching for the former, despite the Ultra packing the better camera.
我花了数周时间同时使用 Fold8 和 Ultra，尽管 Ultra 配备了更好的摄像头，但我的手总是更倾向于拿起前者。

### Shape Shift
### 形态转变

I spent time with the Z Fold8 first, and it's frankly a little shocking to me just how little I care for the Z Fold8 Ultra after I switched. The Ultra’s taller display makes it harder to reach parts of the screen without shimmying your grip, and more importantly, the Fold8's hinge is easier to open.
我先体验了 Z Fold8，坦白说，在切换到 Z Fold8 Ultra 后，我发现自己对 Ultra 的喜爱程度之低，这让我感到有些震惊。Ultra 更高的屏幕使得在不调整握持姿势的情况下很难触及屏幕的某些部分，更重要的是，Fold8 的铰链更容易打开。

That's a big deal because so often with the Ultra you get used to the narrow, tall cover screen, since it resembles a standard phone, and the extra effort needed to open the device is enough friction that—and maybe this is just me—I forget to do it. The Fold8's wider shape does have limitations; for one, you won't see as much content in your apps at any given moment, but I didn't mind that. I felt myself opening the device far more often to use the inner 7.6-inch screen, in portrait or landscape orientation. It's like switching between layout options in Gmail. Yes, you can squeeze the email threads as much as possible in Compact view to see more at a glance, but Comfortable is the way to go, man.
这非常关键，因为在使用 Ultra 时，你很容易习惯那块窄而高的外屏，因为它看起来像普通手机。而打开设备所需的额外努力构成了一种阻力——也许这只是我个人的情况——导致我经常忘记去展开它。Fold8 更宽的形态确实有局限性；首先，你在任何时候都无法在应用中看到那么多的内容，但我并不介意。我发现自己更频繁地打开设备来使用 7.6 英寸的内屏，无论是纵向还是横向模式。这就像在 Gmail 中切换布局选项一样。没错，你可以在“紧凑”视图中尽可能压缩邮件列表以一目了然地查看更多内容，但“舒适”视图才是王道，伙计。

One sacrifice of having a less stiff hinge is that the Fold8 can't stay half-open in as many angles as the Fold8 Ultra. Again, I didn't find this as much of a problem as I expected. I can still prop the phone open at a near-90-degree angle for easy tabletop viewing. And that front 5.5-inch screen has a nice 10:16 aspect ratio that doesn't cause ridiculous letterboxing like the screen on the Ultra. Yes, you'll still see some chunky black bars, but it's not as pronounced. That's true even on the inner screen with full-size apps like Instagram, when you're watching Reels.
铰链阻尼较小的一个代价是，Fold8 无法像 Fold8 Ultra 那样保持在尽可能多的角度半开。同样，我发现这并没有预想中那么严重。我仍然可以将手机以近 90 度的角度支撑在桌面上，方便观看。而且那块 5.5 英寸的前屏拥有 10:16 的舒适比例，不会像 Ultra 的屏幕那样产生夸张的黑边。是的，你仍然会看到一些明显的黑条，但没有那么突出。即使在内屏上使用 Instagram 等全尺寸应用观看 Reels 时也是如此。

Despite the shorter, wider outer screen compared to the taller Fold8 Ultra, you're not losing that much information. I compared a handful of messaging apps and websites in Chrome, and the extra text I could read on the Ultra was marginal. Yet the feel of the Fold8 is so much better—less weighty, easier to reach the corners of the screen, and much better for typing (though I'll add my usual disclaimer that Samsung's built-in keyboard is bad, and its voice typing feature is even worse).
尽管与更高的 Fold8 Ultra 相比，外屏更短更宽，但你并没有损失太多信息。我对比了一些消息应用和 Chrome 浏览器中的网页，Ultra 上能多读到的文本内容微乎其微。然而，Fold8 的手感要好得多——重量更轻，更容易触及屏幕角落，打字体验也更好（尽管我还是要照例声明：三星的内置键盘很烂，语音输入功能更糟）。

On both phones, the crease—the point in the display where it folds—looks significantly less obvious and feels better than on prior models, with less of a bump as your fingers swipe across the screen. That's great, but my biggest design complaint with these Folds is the camera jutting out on the back. It means that whenever you set the phone on any surface, it rocks so much that it's annoying. A case doesn't really fix this either. Google's figured this out with a horizontal camera bar for its Pixels (and so has Apple, apparently).
在两款手机上，折痕（屏幕折叠处）看起来都比以前的机型明显少得多，手感也更好，手指滑过屏幕时的凹凸感也更轻微。这很棒，但我对这些 Fold 机型最大的设计抱怨是背部凸起的摄像头。这意味着每当你把手机放在任何平面上时，它都会晃动得非常厉害，令人烦躁。戴上手机壳也无法真正解决这个问题。谷歌通过 Pixel 的横向摄像头条解决了这个问题（显然苹果也一样）。

The Fold8 is immensely nicer to use in every way. I even prefer the way it handles split-screen mode more than the Ultra because things don't feel super squished on the wide screen. There's just more breathing room. And it's nice to have the option to put three apps side by side.
Fold8 在各方面都好用得多。我甚至更喜欢它处理分屏模式的方式，而不是 Ultra，因为在宽屏幕上内容不会感觉被极度挤压。它有更多的呼吸空间。而且能够选择将三个应用程序并排显示也很不错。

### Ultra Power
### Ultra 的性能

There's a $200 difference between these two very expensive folding phones, a difference that doesn’t sting as much when you're dropping two grand. But if you opt for the cheaper Fold8, you will be making some sacrifices from a pure spec-to-spec comparison. Thankfully, there is no sacrifice in performance; the Qualcomm Snapdragon 8 Elite Gen 5 chip is the same on both and delivers a buttery-smooth experience in every app and game.
这两款昂贵的折叠屏手机之间有 200 美元的差价，当你已经花费两千美元时，这个差价并不算太痛。但如果你选择更便宜的 Fold8，在纯粹的规格对比上你确实会做出一些牺牲。值得庆幸的是，性能上没有任何妥协；两款手机都搭载了高通骁龙 8 Elite Gen 5 芯片，在所有应用和游戏中都能提供丝般顺滑的体验。

Battery life is stronger on the Ultra, which makes sense considering its larger 5,000-mAh cell, compared to the Fold8's 4,800-mAh capacity (marking Samsung's first devices with silicon-carbon battery technology). But I've still been satisfied with both. I've been able to hit more than six hours of screen-on time with the Fold8 Ultra, and five to six on the Fold8, though I'm using the larger display far less on the Ultra, so it's a bit of an unfair comparison. If you prefer the Ultra's shape and use that inner display more, it'll suck up more juice than mine did in my testing.
Ultra 的电池续航更强，考虑到它拥有 5,000 mAh 的大电池，而 Fold8 为 4,800 mAh（这是三星首批采用硅碳电池技术的设备），这在情理之中。但我对两者的表现都很满意。Fold8 Ultra 的亮屏时间能超过 6 小时，Fold8 则在 5 到 6 小时之间，不过我在 Ultra 上使用大屏的频率要低得多，所以这对比有点不公平。如果你更喜欢 Ultra 的形态并更多地使用内屏，它的耗电量会比我在测试中观察到的更多。

It's really the camera system where you're losing out. The Fold8 has a 50-megapixel primary sensor and a 50-megapixel ultrawide, with two 10-megapixel selfie cameras on the outer and inner screens, respectively. The Fold8 Ultra's 200-megapixel main sensor is stronger overall in a variety of lighting conditions, but it's the fact that you have access to the 10-megapixel 3X optical zoom that gives it a leg up. I wish Samsung offered a greater zoom option like the 5X optical on the Pixel 11 Pro Fold, and as a big zoom photographer, the lack of this on the Fold8—especially given its high price—is frustrating. Hopefully Samsung can remedy that in the Fold9.
摄像头系统确实是你做出牺牲的地方。Fold8 配备了 5000 万像素主摄和 5000 万像素超广角镜头，外屏和内屏各有一颗 1000 万像素自拍镜头。Fold8 Ultra 的 2 亿像素主传感器在各种光照条件下整体表现更强，但真正让它胜出的是那颗 1000 万像素的 3 倍光学变焦镜头。我希望三星能提供更大的变焦选项，比如 Pixel 11 Pro Fold 上的 5 倍光学变焦。作为一个喜欢变焦摄影的人，Fold8 缺失这一功能——尤其是考虑到它高昂的价格——确实令人沮丧。希望三星能在 Fold9 中解决这个问题。
