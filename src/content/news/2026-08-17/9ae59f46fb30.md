# Matt Groening lets slip that Simpsons: Hit & Run might be making a comeback
# 马特·格勒宁透露《辛普森一家：横冲直撞》可能即将回归

At D23, when asked about the potential for a sequel to the cult classic The Simpsons: Hit & Run game, Matt Groening replied, “I think the original game is coming back in some form,” before current showrunner Matt Selman added, “or not.” The tone in both of their voices and the body language suggests that Groening may have just revealed something he wasn’t supposed to.

在 D23 大会上，当被问及邪典经典游戏《辛普森一家：横冲直撞》（The Simpsons: Hit & Run）是否有望推出续作时，马特·格勒宁（Matt Groening）回答道：“我认为原作会以某种形式回归。”随后，现任剧集制作人马特·塞尔曼（Matt Selman）补充了一句：“或者不回归。”两人说话的语气和肢体语言暗示，格勒宁可能刚刚不小心泄露了什么不该说的事情。

The original title came out way back in 2003 for PlayStation 2, Xbox, GameCube, and Windows. While it’s possible to get the game up and running on modern computers, it’s not available on modern consoles. It’s currently not in the PlayStation Plus Classics Catalog or on the Xbox backward compatibility list. It’s possible that the game could simply return that way, as a direct port. But a more interesting possibility is a full HD remaster. They wouldn’t even need to rerecord the voice acting since the original cast absolutely nailed it the first time around.

这款原作早在 2003 年就已登陆 PlayStation 2、Xbox、GameCube 和 Windows 平台。虽然目前仍能在现代电脑上运行该游戏，但它并未在现代游戏主机上架。它目前既不在 PlayStation Plus 的经典游戏目录中，也不在 Xbox 的向下兼容列表中。这款游戏确实有可能仅仅以直接移植的方式回归，但一个更有趣的可能性是进行全高清重制。他们甚至不需要重新录制配音，因为原版配音阵容在当年就已经表现得完美无缺了。
