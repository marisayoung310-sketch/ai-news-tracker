### Heard

**Launching today**
今天正式发布

**Give Claude Code and Codex a voice**
为 Claude Code 和 Codex 赋予语音能力

**Heard is a macOS voice layer for your agentic workflows. It connects to Claude Code, Codex, and Cursor and turns their output into intelligent summaries you can hear.**
Heard 是一款专为你的智能体（Agentic）工作流设计的 macOS 语音层。它能够连接 Claude Code、Codex 和 Cursor，并将它们的输出转化为你可以直接听到的智能摘要。

**Full commentary when your eyes are elsewhere, or silence until something errors or needs a decision.**
当你无暇顾及屏幕时，它会提供完整的语音解说；或者保持静默，直到出现错误或需要你做出决策时再进行提醒。

**With agents run in parallel, Heard summarizes at the project level, so you hear where the work stands, not five terminals talking over each other.**
当多个智能体并行运行时，Heard 会在项目层面进行汇总，让你清晰地了解工作进度，而不是听到五个终端同时发出嘈杂的声音。

**Pair your phone and it comes with you. Open source, free for personal use.**
通过手机配对，它还能随身伴你左右。该项目开源，且个人使用免费。
