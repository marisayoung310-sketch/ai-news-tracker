# Cricut Explore 5 vs. Siser Romeo: Choosing the Right Smart Cutting Machine (2026)
# Cricut Explore 5 对决 Siser Romeo：如何选择适合你的智能切割机（2026年版）

Smart cutting machines are a black hole, and I say that as someone who fell in. I’ve dabbled with Cricut products, but it wasn’t until I started reviewing them for work that these cutting machines became a true obsession. These days, I spend weekends thrifting for blanks and experimenting with patchwork appliqué, and I even caved and bought a secondhand Brother sewing machine. My home office, which once screamed “corporate remote worker,” is now overrun with glitter vinyl and who knows how many types of scissors (all for different purposes, of course).

智能切割机就像一个深不见底的“黑洞”，我这么说是因为我本人就深陷其中。我曾浅尝辄止地接触过 Cricut 的产品，但直到我开始为工作进行评测，这些切割机才真正让我着迷。如今，我周末的时间都花在淘各种手工素材、尝试拼布贴花上，甚至还忍不住买了一台二手的 Brother 缝纫机。我的家庭办公室曾经充满了“远程办公”的商务气息，现在却堆满了闪光乙烯基材料和数不清的剪刀（当然，它们各有各的用途）。

All of which to say, I was an easy target when Cricut and Siser both landed new, smart cutting machines on my desk. I quickly realized that comparing them to each other wasn’t particularly useful. Yes, they both cut materials like vinyl and cardstock, but they solve different problems and are for different consumers. Cricut’s latest model, the Explore 5, costs $199 for the machine alone (or $250 for the bundle I tested with mats, tools, and vinyl) and is designed for the more casual crafter making things like bookmarks, banners, and the occasional T-shirt. The Siser Romeo ($600 as tested), on the other hand, is built for someone who might pump out 50 graphic tees before lunch.

总而言之，当 Cricut 和 Siser 同时将新款智能切割机送到我桌上时，我很容易就成了它们的“目标用户”。我很快意识到，将这两者进行直接对比并没有太大意义。没错，它们都能切割乙烯基和卡纸等材料，但它们解决的是不同的需求，面向的也是不同的消费群体。Cricut 的最新机型 Explore 5 单机售价为 199 美元（我测试的包含垫板、工具和乙烯基材料的套装售价为 250 美元），专为制作书签、横幅和偶尔制作 T 恤的休闲手工爱好者设计。而 Siser Romeo（测试款售价 600 美元）则是为那些可能在午饭前就要批量生产 50 件印花 T 恤的用户打造的。

Siser has always been upfront about this. It’s not really trying to compete with a machine like the Explore 5. So instead of forcing a winner-take-all comparison, I over several weeks cut layered cardstock bookmarks, HTV hoodies, vinyl-decaled cups for my brother-in-law’s 40th birthday, and even bonded cotton appliqué pieces on both machines (disclaimer: I had to buy new, aftermarket blades for this).

Siser 对此一直很坦诚。它并没有试图与 Explore 5 这类机器竞争。因此，我没有强行进行“赢家通吃”式的对比，而是在几周的时间里，用这两台机器分别切割了分层卡纸书签、热转印（HTV）连帽衫、为我姐夫 40 岁生日准备的乙烯基贴纸杯子，甚至还尝试了粘合棉布贴花（免责声明：为此我不得不购买了新的第三方刀片）。

My goal wasn’t to crown a winner but to evaluate where the tipping point was. When do you outgrow a hobby cutter? More specifically, when is it worth trading the Cricut Explore 5’s simplicity for the Siser Romeo’s added control?

我的目标不是选出一个赢家，而是评估两者的“分水岭”在哪里。你什么时候会觉得业余级的切割机不够用了？更具体地说，什么时候值得为了 Siser Romeo 更强的操控性而放弃 Cricut Explore 5 的简洁易用？

If you’re casually personalizing things on the fly, or making cards or decals, the Explore 5 is your machine. More than the machine itself, though, my favorite part of Cricut is the whole ecosystem. The Design Space software is packed with user-created projects, and I could easily lose track of time finding designers I liked, then browsing and bookmarking everything they’ve made. You can start with one of those templates, customize it to your liking, and have it ready to cut in a matter of minutes. Design Space holds your hand through every single step, and the learning curve is shallow.

如果你只是偶尔随手做些个性化定制，或者制作卡片和贴纸，Explore 5 就是你的不二之选。不过，比起机器本身，我最喜欢 Cricut 的地方在于它完整的生态系统。Design Space 软件里塞满了用户创作的项目，我经常在寻找喜欢的创作者、浏览并收藏他们作品的过程中不知不觉地消磨掉时间。你可以从这些模板开始，根据自己的喜好进行定制，几分钟内就能准备好进行切割。Design Space 会引导你完成每一个步骤，学习曲线非常平缓。

The Explore 5 is more of a background worker. Its new Load and Go system makes the act of cutting super simple: You put your material on the mat (which isn’t even needed if you’re using Cricut’s Smart Vinyl), feed it into the machine, and press the flashing Go button—the machine takes it from there. Combined with Design Space’s step-by-step prompts, it’s about as close to plug-and-play as a cutting machine gets.

Explore 5 更像是一个默默工作的助手。它全新的“Load and Go”系统让切割过程变得极其简单：你只需将材料放在垫板上（如果使用 Cricut 的 Smart Vinyl，甚至连垫板都不需要），送入机器，然后按下闪烁的“Go”按钮，剩下的就交给机器了。配合 Design Space 的分步提示，这几乎是切割机所能达到的“即插即用”体验。

At just under 20 inches wide, the Explore 5 doesn’t require a dedicated crafting studio space or permanent workstation. Mine lived happily on the corner of my desk for weeks, ready whenever inspiration struck. I also appreciated the clamshell-style opening, which keeps the blade and interior protected from dust and stray threads that inevitably exist in a busy craft space, and you can fold it closed when you’re done.

Explore 5 的宽度不到 20 英寸，不需要专门的手工工作室或固定的工作台。我的机器在书桌角落里愉快地待了好几周，随时准备迎接灵感的迸发。我也很欣赏它的翻盖式设计，这能保护刀片和内部结构免受忙碌手工空间里不可避免的灰尘和线头的侵扰，用完后还可以折叠收纳。

This machine isn’t flawless. During testing, I ran into inconsistent cuts on cardstock projects even with a fresh sticky mat. My first real project on the machine, an intricate floral bookmark, came out rough enough that I questioned whether paper crafting was for me. Sharpening the blade with a ball of foil and a swipe of rubbing alcohol helped, but I still had to break out an X-Acto knife to clean up some edges.

这台机器并非完美无缺。在测试过程中，即使使用了全新的粘性垫板，我在切割卡纸项目时也遇到了切割不均匀的情况。我用这台机器做的第一个正式项目是一个复杂的花卉书签，成品粗糙得让我怀疑自己是否适合做纸艺。用锡纸球打磨刀片并用酒精擦拭后有所改善，但我仍然不得不拿出美工刀来修整边缘。

I’ve also had the occasional Bluetooth drop between finishing a design in Design Space and sending it to the machine—luckily never midcut, but it has happened often enough that I’ve gotten into the habit of starting projects from the app on my phone as a workaround. If you get stuck, though, help is never far off. Since Cricut is such an established brand, TikTok and YouTube probably have a creator who has solved your exact problem.

我还偶尔遇到在 Design Space 完成设计并发送给机器时蓝牙连接中断的情况——幸运的是从未在切割过程中发生，但这种情况发生得足够频繁，以至于我养成了通过手机 App 启动项目的习惯作为变通方案。不过，如果你遇到困难，帮助永远触手可及。由于 Cricut 是一个非常成熟的品牌，TikTok 和 YouTube 上很可能已经有创作者解决了你遇到的具体问题。

Whether you’re cutting heat-transfer vinyl in large volumes, running a business, or filling repeat orders, the Romeo is built for a completely different workflow than the Explore 5. At more than twice its size, it’s designed less for the spontaneous weekend project and more for keeping production moving. It all starts with the hardware. Thanks to the adjustable pinch rollers, the Romeo doesn’t require a cutting mat for most materials, and instead of trimming vinyl into smaller pieces, you can feed and roll it through the back of the machine. A tiling feature also maximizes material by automatically filling the available.

无论你是要大批量切割热转印乙烯基、经营业务，还是处理重复订单，Romeo 的设计初衷都与 Explore 5 的工作流程截然不同。它的体积是后者的两倍多，与其说是为了周末的即兴创作，不如说是为了保持生产效率。这一切始于硬件。得益于可调节的压轮，Romeo 在处理大多数材料时不需要切割垫板，而且你无需将乙烯基材料裁剪成小块，可以直接从机器后部送入并卷动。平铺功能还可以通过自动填充可用空间来最大化材料利用率。
