# SDCC teaser gives us our first good look at Blade Runner 2099
# SDCC 预告片让我们首次一睹《银翼杀手 2099》的风采

It’s been nearly five years since Ridley Scott first announced his plans for a TV spinoff of his sci-fi masterpiece Blade Runner (1982) and the 2017 sequel Blade Runner 2049. It’s finally here—or almost, anyway. Prime Video debuted the first teaser for the new ten-episode series, Blade Runner 2099, at San Diego Comic-Con on Friday.

自雷德利·斯科特首次宣布计划为其科幻杰作《银翼杀手》（1982）及其 2017 年续集《银翼杀手 2049》制作电视剧衍生剧以来，已经过去了近五年。它终于来了——或者说，快要来了。周五，Prime Video 在圣地亚哥国际动漫展（SDCC）上发布了这部全新十集剧集《银翼杀手 2099》的首支预告片。

We also got a teaser for the third season of Prime Video’s Rings of Power and a new trailer for HBO’s Lanterns. Yeoh and Schafer joined show runner Silka Luisa (Shining Girls) onstage for the big reveal, which also included an exclusive clip shown to the audience. Luisa told Entertainment Weekly that the series stays true to the original’s “noir principles… and there’s a mystery at the heart of the show that looks both forwards and backwards in terms of mythology.”

我们还看到了 Prime Video《指环王：力量之戒》第三季的预告片，以及 HBO《绿灯侠》（Lanterns）的新预告片。杨紫琼和亨特·莎弗与剧集主创西尔卡·路易莎（《闪亮女孩》）一同登台揭晓了这一重磅消息，现场还向观众展示了一段独家片段。路易莎告诉《娱乐周刊》，该剧忠实于原作的“黑色电影原则……剧集核心存在一个谜团，在神话层面既有前瞻性也有回顾性。”

An uprising has occurred and the humans lost, which means they are now the hunted second-class citizens. “The show is really imagining what comes after us, and what does it mean to be human in a world where humanity is no longer relevant,” said Luisa.

一场起义已经发生，人类战败，这意味着他们现在成了被猎杀的二等公民。路易莎说：“这部剧真正构想的是我们之后的世界，以及在一个人类不再重要的世界里，身为人类意味着什么。”

Per the official synopsis: Fifty years after the events of Blade Runner 2049, Los Angeles has been reborn, just not by humanity. Cora, a fugitive in a final bid to stop running, takes on one last identity: a Blade Runner. Forced to partner with Olwen, a Replicant days from dying, she hunts down a runaway hiding a truth that could collapse their fragile city.

根据官方剧情简介：在《银翼杀手 2049》事件发生五十年后，洛杉矶重获新生，但并非由人类所造。科拉（Cora）是一名逃亡者，为了结束逃亡生涯，她接受了最后一个身份：银翼杀手。她被迫与一名生命垂危的复制人奥尔文（Olwen）搭档，追捕一名隐藏着足以摧毁这座脆弱城市真相的逃亡者。

The incomparable Michelle Yeoh stars as Olwen, the dying Replicant, along with Hunter Schafer (Euphoria) as Cora. The cast also includes Hugo Hamlet as Buster Friendly, as well as Dmitri Abold, Lewis Gribben, Katelyn Rose Downey, Daniel Rigby, Johnny Harris, Amy Lennox, Sheila Atim, Matthew Needham, Tom Burke, and Maurizio Lombardi—all in as-yet-undisclosed roles. Blade Runner 2099 will premiere on November 25, 2026, on Prime Video.

无可比拟的杨紫琼饰演垂死的复制人奥尔文，亨特·莎弗（《亢奋》）饰演科拉。演员阵容还包括饰演巴斯特·弗兰德利的雨果·哈姆雷特，以及德米特里·阿博尔德、刘易斯·格里本、凯特琳·罗斯·唐尼、丹尼尔·里格比、约翰尼·哈里斯、艾米·伦诺克斯、希拉·阿蒂姆、马修·尼达姆、汤姆·伯克和毛里齐奥·隆巴迪——他们饰演的角色尚未公开。《银翼杀手 2099》将于 2026 年 11 月 25 日在 Prime Video 首播。

### Rings of Power S3
### 《指环王：力量之戒》第三季

Prime Video made a major investment in The Rings of Power when it acquired the rights to the source material from the Tolkien estate, even committing to multiple seasons upfront. We’re now coming up on S3 and based on the teaser the streaming platform unveiled at SDCC, we’ll finally get to see Sauron (Charlie Vickers) forging the One Ring.

Prime Video 在从托尔金遗产管理委员会获得原著版权时，对《力量之戒》进行了巨额投资，甚至预先承诺了多季制作。现在我们即将迎来第三季，根据该流媒体平台在 SDCC 上发布的预告片，我们终于可以看到索伦（查理·维克斯饰）锻造至尊魔戒的场景。

Per the official synopsis: In season three, Middle-earth is at war. Five years have passed since the fall of Eregion. Sauron’s armies have marched across the whole of the world, conquering all in their path. Only a few isolated pockets stand between the Dark Lord and total victory—Khazad-dǔm, where the Dwarves are now sealed deep within their mountain halls, and the elven realms of Lindon and Rivendell, which are protected by their Three Rings.

根据官方剧情简介：第三季中，中土世界陷入战争。埃瑞吉安陷落已过去五年。索伦的军队横扫世界，征服了沿途的一切。只有少数孤立的据点阻挡着黑暗魔君取得全面胜利——卡扎督姆（矮人们现在被封锁在深山大厅中），以及由三枚戒指保护的精灵领地林顿和瑞文戴尔。

But deep in the land of Mordor, in his newly completed tower of Barad-dûr, the Dark Lord toils day and night, obsessed with harnessing a power that will bring the last of his enemies to their knees: One ring to rule them all… Now, all the free peoples of Middle-earth—Dwarves, Elves, Men, and Wizards alike—must find a way to come together, in a race against time to prevent Sauron from achieving his goal of utter domination of all life.

但在魔多深处，在他新建成的巴拉多塔中，黑暗魔君日夜操劳，痴迷于驾驭一种能让最后敌人屈服的力量：至尊魔戒，统御众生……现在，中土世界所有自由的子民——矮人、精灵、人类和巫师——必须找到团结一致的方法，与时间赛跑，阻止索伦实现他彻底统治所有生命的野心。

We’re also getting some new characters this season, most notably Jamie Campbell Bower as Galadriel’s (Morfydd Clark) long-lost husband Celeborn. Zubin Varla will play Khamul the Easterling of Rhun; Eddie Marson will play Thrain, older brother to King Durin IV (Owain Arthur); Andrew Richardson will play Anarion, younger son of Elendil (Lloyd Owen) and brother to Isildur (Maxim Baldry); and Adam Young will play an Orc named Marnukh who “might not be what he seems.” Oh, and none other than Simon Pegg will voice the fiery Balrog. The third season of Rings of Power premieres on November 11, 2026, on Prime Video.

本季还将迎来一些新角色，最引人注目的是杰米·坎贝尔·鲍尔饰演凯兰崔尔（莫菲德·克拉克饰）失散多年的丈夫凯勒鹏。祖宾·瓦拉将饰演卢恩的东方人卡穆尔；埃迪·马森将饰演都林四世国王（欧文·亚瑟饰）的哥哥索恩；安德鲁·理查森将饰演埃兰迪尔（劳埃德·欧文饰）的小儿子、伊熙尔杜（马克西姆·巴德利饰）的弟弟阿纳瑞安；亚当·杨将饰演一个名叫马努克的半兽人，他“可能并非表面看起来那样”。哦，还有西蒙·佩吉将为火炎魔配音。《力量之戒》第三季将于 2026 年 11 月 11 日在 Prime Video 首播。

### Lanterns
### 《绿灯侠》

We’ve seen a couple of teasers thus far for Lanterns, the new DC Universe series coming to HBO Max, both of which made it clear we’re in for a show that’s closer to a sci-fi True Detective than your standard superhero fare. HBO unveiled a full trailer at SDCC that is a little less Earthbound. It leans into the “space cop” vibe, reveals that shapeshifting Manhunters will be central to the plot, takes us into outer space, and also gives us our first look at Sinestro (Ulrich Thomsen), a former Corps member gone rogue.

到目前为止，我们已经看到了几支即将登陆 HBO Max 的 DC 宇宙新剧《绿灯侠》的预告片，它们都清楚地表明，这部剧更像是科幻版的《真探》，而不是标准的超级英雄剧。HBO 在 SDCC 上发布了一支完整预告片，其视角不再局限于地球。它倾向于“太空警察”的氛围，揭示了变形的“猎人”（Manhunters）将成为剧情的核心，带我们进入外太空，并让我们首次看到了塞尼斯托（乌尔里奇·汤姆森饰），一位叛变的绿灯军团前成员。

Per the official logline, “The series follows new recruit John Stewart (Aaron Pierre) and Lantern legend Hal Jordan (Kyle Chandler), two intergalactic cops drawn into a dark, earth-based mystery as they investigate a murder in the American heartland.” There will be two storylines: one set in 2016 about a murder in Nebraska, and the second set in 2026. Chandler’s Hal Jordan is a former test pilot nearing retirement from the Green Lantern Corps. He’s training Pierre’s John Stewart Jr., a new recruit, to replace him.

根据官方剧情梗概，“该剧讲述了新兵约翰·斯图尔特（亚伦·皮埃尔饰）和绿灯侠传奇哈尔·乔丹（凯尔·钱德勒饰）的故事，这两位星际警察在调查美国腹地的一起谋杀案时，被卷入了一个黑暗的地球谜团。”剧中将有两条故事线：一条设定在 2016 年，关于内布拉斯加州的一起谋杀案；另一条设定在 2026 年。钱德勒饰演的哈尔·乔丹是一位即将从绿灯军团退役的前试飞员。他正在训练皮埃尔饰演的新兵约翰·斯图尔特二世来接替他的位置。

Nathan Fillion reprises his Superman role as the obnoxious Guy Gardner; we get a brief glimpse of him in the new teaser. The cast also includes Kelly MacDonald as Kerry, a small-town family-oriented sheriff; Jason Ritter as Billy Macon, Kerry’s husband; Garret Dillahunt as William Macon, Kerry’s cowboy father-in-law; Poorna Jagannathan as a woman named Zoe; and Paul Ben-Victor as an extraterrestrial called Antaan. Sherman Augustus plays John Stewart Sr., with J. Alphonse Nicholson playing the younger version; Nicole Ari Parker plays Bernadette Stewart (mother to John Jr.), with Jasmine Cephas Jones playing the young version of the character. In addition, Chris Coy plays a suspiciously nervous truck driver, Waylon Sanders; Cary Christopher plays a gifted child named Noah; Laura Linney will play a senior Guardian; and Paula Patton will appear in an as-yet-undisclosed guest role. We also know that the show will feature the inter-dimensional prison and the Checkmate organization.

内森·菲利安将继续饰演那个令人讨厌的盖·加德纳；我们在新预告片中短暂地看到了他。演员阵容还包括饰演小镇顾家警长克里的凯莉·麦克唐纳；饰演克里丈夫比利·梅肯的杰森·里特；饰演克里牛仔公公威廉·梅肯的加瑞特·迪拉胡特；饰演佐伊的普娜·贾甘纳坦；以及饰演外星人安坦的保罗·本-维克托。谢尔曼·奥古斯都饰演约翰·斯图尔特老先生，J·阿方斯·尼科尔森饰演其年轻版本；妮可·阿丽·帕克饰演伯纳黛特·斯图尔特（约翰二世的母亲），贾斯敏·塞法斯·琼斯饰演该角色的年轻版本。此外，克里斯·科伊饰演一名神情可疑、紧张的卡车司机韦伦·桑德斯；卡里·克里斯托弗饰演一名叫诺亚的天才儿童；劳拉·琳妮将饰演一位资深守护者；宝拉·巴顿将以未公开的客串角色出现。我们还知道，该剧将涉及跨维度监狱和“棋局”（Checkmate）组织。
