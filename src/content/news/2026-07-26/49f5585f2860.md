# Hannah Fry Wins the Leelavati Prize in 2026 for Mathematics Outreach

**Hannah Fry Wins the Leelavati Prize in 2026 for Mathematics Outreach**
汉娜·弗莱（Hannah Fry）荣获2026年利拉瓦蒂奖（Leelavati Prize），以表彰其在数学普及方面的杰出贡献。

***

**Professor Hannah Fry wins Leelavati Prize**
汉娜·弗莱教授荣获利拉瓦蒂奖。

***

**The Faculty of Mathematics is delighted to announce that Professor Hannah Fry has been awarded the prestigious Leelavati Prize for 2026. This international recognition celebrates her exceptional contributions to the public understanding of mathematics and her tireless efforts to make complex mathematical concepts accessible and engaging for audiences worldwide.**
数学系非常高兴地宣布，汉娜·弗莱教授荣获了2026年度久负盛名的利拉瓦蒂奖。这一国际性奖项旨在表彰她在提升公众对数学的理解方面所做出的杰出贡献，以及她为使复杂的数学概念变得通俗易懂、引人入胜而付出的不懈努力。

***

**Known for her ability to bridge the gap between academia and the general public, Professor Fry has become a leading voice in mathematics communication. Through her books, documentaries, and public lectures, she has inspired a new generation of students and enthusiasts to explore the beauty and utility of mathematics in everyday life.**
弗莱教授以其在学术界与公众之间架起桥梁的能力而闻名，已成为数学传播领域的领军人物。通过她的书籍、纪录片和公开讲座，她激励了新一代的学生和爱好者去探索数学在日常生活中的美感与实用价值。
