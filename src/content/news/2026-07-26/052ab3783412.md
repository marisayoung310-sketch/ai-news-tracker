# MouthPad: A Tongue-Controlled Touchpad
# MouthPad：舌控触控板

MouthPad: The world’s first hands-free touchpad. MouthPad is a custom-fit mouthpiece that turns subtle tongue and head movements into precise control of your phone, tablet, and computer. The mouth is a new interface. MouthPad translates small, natural movements into cursor control, clicks, scrolling, and dragging. No hands. No wires. Worn discreetly inside your mouth.
MouthPad：全球首款免提触控板。MouthPad 是一款定制的口腔设备，它能将细微的舌头和头部动作转化为对手机、平板电脑和计算机的精准控制。口腔成为了一个全新的交互界面。MouthPad 将细小、自然的动作转化为光标控制、点击、滚动和拖拽操作。无需双手，无需线缆，隐蔽地佩戴在口腔内。

### How it works
### 工作原理

**01 Choose how you move.**
Move the cursor with your tongue or head. Tap, swipe, sip, and hold to click and drag.
**01 选择你的操作方式。**
通过舌头或头部移动光标。通过点击、滑动、吸气和长按来实现点击和拖拽。

**02 Pair it like a mouse.**
Connect MouthPad to your phone, tablet, and computer over Bluetooth. Switch instantly between devices.
**02 像鼠标一样配对。**
通过蓝牙将 MouthPad 连接到手机、平板电脑和计算机。可在设备间即时切换。

**03 Use it discreetly.**
MouthPad sits inside your mouth, out of sight, with minimal impact on speech.
**03 隐蔽使用。**
MouthPad 放置在口腔内，从外观上无法察觉，且对说话的影响极小。

**04 Made for your mouth.**
Each MouthPad is custom-fit from a dental scan, then 3D printed and encapsulated for comfort, safety, and everyday use.
**04 为你的口腔量身定制。**
每个 MouthPad 均根据牙齿扫描结果定制，通过 3D 打印并封装，确保佩戴舒适、安全，适合日常使用。
