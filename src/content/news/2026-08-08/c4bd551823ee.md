# Beyond Full-Model Rollback: AuroSFT for Adapter-State Multi-Task Fine-Tuning
# 超越全模型回滚：用于适配器状态多任务微调的 AuroSFT

**Abstract:** Multi-task supervised fine-tuning (SFT) often casts a heterogeneous data mixture as a single optimization problem, even though different tasks may reach their best generalization at different times. msft exposes this mismatch through task-wise roll-out, exclusion, and rollback, but its original formulation materializes the scheduler state as full-model checkpoints, making stage transitions costly to store, restore, and deploy.

**摘要：** 多任务监督微调（SFT）通常将异构数据混合视为单一优化问题，尽管不同任务可能在不同时间达到其最佳泛化效果。msft 通过任务级的展开、排除和回滚揭示了这种不匹配，但其原始方案将调度器状态具体化为全模型检查点，导致阶段转换在存储、恢复和部署方面成本高昂。

This paper introduces AuroSFT, a parameter-efficient framework that recasts the carried state of overfitting-aware multi-task SFT as a compact, mergeable adapter state. AuroSFT freezes the pretrained backbone, trains only injected adapters, rolls back adapter checkpoints at task-wise peaks, and continues on the remaining active mixture.

本文介绍了 AuroSFT，这是一个参数高效的框架，它将过拟合感知多任务 SFT 的携带状态重构为紧凑且可合并的适配器状态。AuroSFT 冻结预训练的主干网络，仅训练注入的适配器，在任务级峰值处回滚适配器检查点，并继续在剩余的活跃混合数据上进行训练。

At the layer level, each adapter applies an AuroRA-inspired adaptive nonlinear layer to a low-rank weight factor rather than to the sample representation. The resulting update remains linear in the input, rank-bounded, and exactly mergeable into the frozen projection.

在层级上，每个适配器将受 AuroRA 启发的自适应非线性层应用于低秩权重因子，而不是应用于样本表示。由此产生的更新在输入上保持线性、秩受限，并且可以精确地合并到冻结的投影中。

Under the retained-backbone comparison protocol, AuroSFT achieves 61.36% average accuracy, compared with 59.85% for the corresponding msft reference row, and obtains higher accuracy on all five backbones. Our code is available at the anonymous repository: this https URL.

在保留主干网络的对比协议下，AuroSFT 实现了 61.36% 的平均准确率，而相应的 msft 参考基准为 59.85%，并且在所有五个主干网络上均获得了更高的准确率。我们的代码可在匿名存储库中获取：[点击此处访问](https://this-https-url)。
