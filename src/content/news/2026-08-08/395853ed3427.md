# PPDL: LLM-Based Flows as Probabilistic Programs

**Abstract:** Building reliable applications that leverage large language models (LLMs) remains a significant challenge. While LLMs offer impressive capabilities across diverse tasks, their outputs often lack accuracy and provide no clear measure of confidence. This uncertainty compounds in flows of multiple calls to LLMs and other tools, making it difficult for developers and end-users to trust the results.

**摘要：** 构建利用大语言模型（LLM）的可靠应用程序仍然是一项重大挑战。尽管大语言模型在各种任务中展现出令人印象深刻的能力，但其输出往往缺乏准确性，且无法提供明确的置信度衡量标准。这种不确定性在涉及多次调用大语言模型及其他工具的流程中会不断累积，使得开发者和最终用户难以信任其结果。

This paper introduces a probabilistic language for programming LLM-based flows. It enables developers to quantify and propagate uncertainty throughout the application's flow, and experiment with different inference scaling techniques without adding a single line of code beyond the flow's logic. We present an experimental study to demonstrate this capability, and a case study building a theorem proving agent for the Rocq theorem prover.

本文介绍了一种用于编程大语言模型流程的概率语言。它使开发者能够在整个应用程序流程中量化并传播不确定性，并在无需在流程逻辑之外添加任何额外代码的情况下，尝试不同的推理扩展技术。我们通过一项实验研究展示了这一能力，并提供了一个为 Rocq 定理证明器构建定理证明代理的案例研究。
