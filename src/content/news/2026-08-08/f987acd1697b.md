### How ideas of a vast censorship network moved from the online fringe to Trump policy
### 关于庞大审查网络的构想如何从网络边缘走向特朗普的政策

For years, narratives about a “censorship-industrial complex” spread in right-wing circles. The theory claimed that, under the guise of combating disinformation, a sprawling constellation of government agencies, academics, civil society groups, and Big Tech platforms was suppressing conservative and populist speech online.
多年来，关于“审查-工业复合体”的叙事在右翼圈子中流传。该理论声称，在打击虚假信息的幌子下，一个由政府机构、学术界、民间社会团体和大型科技平台组成的庞大网络正在线上压制保守派和民粹主义者的言论。

What began as a relatively niche conspiracy theory from the far right was able to—and continues to—influence a wide range of actions by the second Trump administration. In myriad ways, the idea of the censorship-industrial complex, or CIC, explains the logic behind how the administration operates both at home and internationally.
这一最初源于极右翼的相对小众的阴谋论，已经并将继续影响特朗普第二任政府的广泛行动。在许多方面，“审查-工业复合体”（CIC）的概念解释了该政府在国内和国际上运作背后的逻辑。

Over the past nine months, MIT Technology Review and Type Investigations have traced how the theory came to influence the country’s most powerful individuals. We found that it took off in 2023, driven by a handful of individuals, with support from a close-knit group of right-wing organizations and media outlets—and an internet activist named Mike Benz at its center. Read the full story on how the CIC framework has shaped government action. —Eileen Guo, Gisela Perez de Acha, and Martin Sona
在过去的九个月里，《麻省理工科技评论》和 Type Investigations 追踪了这一理论是如何影响该国最有权势的人物的。我们发现，它在 2023 年开始兴起，由少数几个人推动，并得到了一个紧密的右翼组织和媒体群体的支持，而一位名叫迈克·本茨（Mike Benz）的网络活动家处于核心地位。阅读关于 CIC 框架如何塑造政府行动的完整报道。——Eileen Guo, Gisela Perez de Acha, and Martin Sona
