# Marginal Matching Does Not License Factorized Sampling: Auditing Conditional Style Leakage in Factorized Generative Models
# 边缘匹配并不能保证因子化采样：对因子化生成模型中条件风格泄露的审计

**Abstract:** Factorized generative models commonly regularize a latent style variable $z_s$ by matching its marginal distribution to a fixed Gaussian prior and interpret this as evidence that the style representation is independent of class information. We show that this interpretation is incorrect. Matching only the marginal distribution places no constraint on the class-conditional distributions, allowing the latent style to remain highly predictive of the label despite appearing perfectly Gaussian in aggregate.

**摘要：** 因子化生成模型通常通过将其潜在风格变量 $z_s$ 的边缘分布与固定的高斯先验进行匹配来对其进行正则化，并将此解释为风格表示与类别信息无关的证据。我们证明这种解释是不正确的。仅匹配边缘分布并不能对类条件分布施加约束，这使得潜在风格在总体上表现为完美高斯分布的同时，仍然能够高度预测标签。

We derive an exact decomposition showing that this mismatch is one of four conditions required for factorized sampling, and demonstrate that eliminating it is necessary but not sufficient to obtain the intended factorization. Empirically, our case-study model and four representative latent baselines achieve near-zero global MMD while still allowing a linear probe to recover class labels with 74%--100% accuracy (10% chance level).

我们推导了一个精确的分解，表明这种不匹配是实现因子化采样所需的四个条件之一，并证明消除这种不匹配是实现预期因子化的必要条件，但非充分条件。在实证方面，我们的案例研究模型和四个具有代表性的潜在基线模型在实现近乎零的全局 MMD（最大均值差异）的同时，仍然允许线性探测器以 74%--100% 的准确率（随机猜测为 10%）恢复类别标签。

Our model reaches 99.15% clustering accuracy, whereas externally evaluated class-conditional generation succeeds only 16% of the time. This leakage remains under six independent perturbations involving model capacity, curriculum, prior geometry, and supervision across two datasets. Four mitigation strategies reduce probe accuracy to 21%--46%, although they leave within-class dependence largely unchanged.

我们的模型达到了 99.15% 的聚类准确率，而外部评估的类条件生成成功率仅为 16%。这种泄露在涉及模型容量、课程学习、先验几何结构和监督学习的六种独立扰动下（跨两个数据集）依然存在。四种缓解策略将探测准确率降低到了 21%--46%，但它们在很大程度上未能改变类内依赖性。

A post-hoc conditional prior improves externally evaluated class generation to 0.97 on MNIST without retraining but reaches only 0.41 on CIFAR-10, while an empirical style bank achieves 0.88 on CIFAR-10. These results demonstrate that no divergence computed solely on the marginal distribution of the style latent can certify independence from class labels, and that reporting marginal statistics alone does not verify the property commonly claimed in factorized generative models.

事后条件先验（Post-hoc conditional prior）在无需重新训练的情况下，将 MNIST 上的外部评估类生成指标提升至 0.97，但在 CIFAR-10 上仅达到 0.41，而经验风格库（empirical style bank）在 CIFAR-10 上达到了 0.88。这些结果表明，仅根据风格潜在变量的边缘分布计算出的任何散度都无法证明其与类别标签的独立性，且仅报告边缘统计数据并不能验证因子化生成模型中通常所声称的属性。
