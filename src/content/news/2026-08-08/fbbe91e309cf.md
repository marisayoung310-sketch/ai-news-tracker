# The only instant cameras worth your money
# 值得你掏腰包的拍立得相机

Whether you’re after inexpensive film, vintage prints, or a camera with more creative control, these are our top picks.
无论你是追求低廉的相纸成本、复古的成像风格，还是需要更强的创作控制力，以下都是我们的首选推荐。

There’s something magical about using instant cameras that smartphones can’t match. You can capture a moment, print it out, and then give the photo as a gift or hold onto it. Image quality won’t be all that good, but imperfections are part of the allure.
使用拍立得相机有一种智能手机无法比拟的魔力。你可以捕捉瞬间、即刻打印，然后将照片作为礼物送人或珍藏起来。虽然画质可能并不完美，但这些瑕疵正是其魅力所在。

We tested instant camera models from popular brands and landed on a handful that we think are the best. Some aim to preserve the classic experience above all else, while others support companion apps and the ability to print stored photos.
我们测试了来自各大主流品牌的拍立得相机，并选出了我们认为最出色的几款。有些机型旨在保留最纯粹的经典体验，而另一些则支持配套应用程序，并具备打印已存储照片的功能。

Fujifilm’s latest Instax cameras are the best out there for most people. The company’s Instax Mini Evo is a bit pricey, but it produces great-looking images and offers a lot of creative control. If you’re shopping on a tighter budget, the Instax Mini 13 also takes good photos but has fewer features. The Polaroid Flip is the best retro-focused experience (be warned: prints are pricey!), while Kodak’s Mini Retro 3 might be a better purchase for some because its prints are quite affordable by comparison.
对于大多数人来说，富士（Fujifilm）最新的 Instax 系列相机是目前市面上最好的选择。Instax Mini Evo 虽然价格稍高，但成像效果极佳，并提供了丰富的创作控制选项。如果你预算有限，Instax Mini 13 也能拍出不错的照片，只是功能相对较少。Polaroid Flip 是追求复古体验的最佳选择（但要注意：相纸很贵！），而柯达（Kodak）的 Mini Retro 3 对某些人来说可能更划算，因为它的相纸成本相对便宜得多。

***

### The best instant camera: Fujifilm Instax Mini Evo
### 最佳拍立得相机：Fujifilm Instax Mini Evo

Fujifilm’s Instax Mini Evo is a hybrid instant camera that bears a resemblance to its premium X100 line of cameras that cost hundreds more. Its vintage dials and buttons look so stylish that they caught the attention of passersby as I walked around Los Angeles.
富士 Instax Mini Evo 是一款混合型拍立得相机，其外观与价格高出数百美元的高端 X100 系列相机颇为相似。它复古的拨盘和按钮设计非常时尚，我在洛杉矶街头使用时，甚至吸引了不少路人的目光。

It’s easy to balk at its price tag, but this camera offers a level of flexibility that sets it apart (and could save you from wasting a bunch of prints). The camera’s dials also let you apply lens options and effects, from retro filters to monochrome shades, and you can even remotely trigger shots from the app. Its 3-inch LCD screen can be used as a viewfinder, and it also lets you preview and select which images you want to print.
虽然它的价格可能会让人望而却步，但这款相机提供的灵活性使其脱颖而出（这也能帮你省下不少浪费的相纸）。相机的拨盘允许你应用各种镜头选项和效果，从复古滤镜到黑白影调应有尽有，你甚至可以通过配套 App 远程遥控拍摄。其 3 英寸 LCD 屏幕既可作为取景器，也能让你预览并选择想要打印的照片。

***

### The best instant camera if you’re on a budget: Fujifilm Instax Mini 13
### 预算有限的最佳选择：Fujifilm Instax Mini 13

Fujifilm’s Instax Mini 13 is a more budget-friendly option that can take great photos mostly on par with the Mini Evo. It’s easy to use (just twist its lens to turn the camera on or off), it outperforms other budget instant cameras we tested, and we like its bright, playful designs. Its biggest improvement over the Mini 12 is its self-timer, with both two- and 10-second countdown options.
富士 Instax Mini 13 是一款更经济实惠的选择，其成像质量与 Mini Evo 基本持平。它非常易于使用（只需旋转镜头即可开关机），性能优于我们测试过的其他入门级拍立得相机，而且我们很喜欢它明亮、俏皮的设计。相比 Mini 12，它最大的改进在于增加了自拍定时功能，提供 2 秒和 10 秒两种倒计时选项。

The Mini 13 produced more true-to-life photos than the non-Fujifilm instant cameras I tested. I also appreciated the Mini 13’s generously sized physical viewfinder and that it shows your remaining shots, which is useful given that each print costs about $1.
与我测试过的非富士品牌拍立得相机相比，Mini 13 拍出的照片色彩更真实。我也很喜欢 Mini 13 宽大的物理取景器，以及它能显示剩余拍摄张数的功能，考虑到每张相纸成本约 1 美元，这非常实用。

### A note on Kodak Mini Shot 3 Retro
### 关于柯达 Mini Shot 3 Retro 的说明

Fujifilm’s Instax cameras produce excellent-looking photos, but each print costs around $1. Kodak’s hybrid Mini Shot 3 Retro is pricier up front (it retails for $159.99 but it often on sale for much less) but much cheaper to buy film for, making it the better budget pick (or beginner-friendly pick). Replacement cartridges are often around $20 for a 60-print pack. And, because you can preview photos before printing, you’re less likely to waste prints.
富士 Instax 相机成像效果出色，但每张相纸成本约为 1 美元。柯达的混合型 Mini Shot 3 Retro 虽然初始购买价格较高（零售价 159.99 美元，但经常有大幅折扣），但相纸成本便宜得多，使其成为更好的预算选择（或适合初学者的选择）。替换装相纸通常 20 美元左右即可买到 60 张。此外，由于你可以在打印前预览照片，因此浪费相纸的可能性更小。
