# Fenix Flexin isn’t even denying using AI to make ‘Rubberz’ anymore
# Fenix Flexin 甚至不再否认使用 AI 制作《Rubberz》

Fenix now claims he’s ‘never said I didn’t use AI,’ to make his hit song.
Fenix 现在声称他“从未说过我没有使用 AI”来制作这首热门歌曲。

It took long enough, but now LA rapper Fenix Flexin appears to have admitted using AI for the 80s synth pop-themed song “Rubberz.” His comments follow the producer Medasin’s videos claiming that an AI tool called Treblo (formerly Sonauto) was used to make the song, and the company releasing an AI detector that identifies it as Treblo AI-created.
经过了漫长的过程，洛杉矶说唱歌手 Fenix Flexin 似乎终于承认在 80 年代合成器流行风格的歌曲《Rubberz》中使用了 AI。此前，制作人 Medasin 发布视频称该歌曲使用了名为 Treblo（前身为 Sonauto）的 AI 工具，随后该公司发布了一款 AI 检测器，将其识别为由 Treblo AI 生成。

In a reply to a comment pointing out that Tyga admitted to relying on AI for his own controversial ’80s-influenced album $tarface, on one of his Instagram posts, Fenix said: “never said I didn’t use AI , i said it had nun to do w recording process . I didn’t even know what da app was till we mixed it and erbody started commenting . ain’t nothing wrong w using new technology as a tool keep up w the times or get left behind”
在 Instagram 的一条帖子下，有评论指出 Tyga 曾承认其备受争议的 80 年代风格专辑《$tarface》依赖于 AI，Fenix 回复道：“我从没说过我没用 AI，我说的是它和录音过程没关系。在混音之前我甚至不知道那个应用是什么，直到大家开始评论。使用新技术作为工具没什么错，要么跟上时代，要么被淘汰。”

The comment insists he never denied using AI, but last month on Hot 97’s Mornings With Mero when asked directly about AI use by interviewer Kazeem Famuyide, he stated clearly that there’s “no AI on it.”
这番评论坚称他从未否认使用 AI，但上个月在 Hot 97 的《Mornings With Mero》节目中，当主持人 Kazeem Famuyide 直接询问 AI 使用情况时，他曾明确表示这首歌“没有使用 AI”。

He’s repeatedly gone out of his way to try and dispel the AI allegations publicly since the song was released, despite what he says now. Fenix and his team have posted what they claimed were clips of the recording session files, popped into the comments to insist the song was made by two humans recording in his living room with “pro tools auto tune and some reverb,” and said he’s actually singing in clips where it looks like he’s lip syncing.
尽管他现在这么说，但自歌曲发布以来，他曾多次公开试图消除有关 AI 的指控。Fenix 和他的团队曾发布过所谓的录音室文件片段，并在评论区坚称这首歌是由两个人在他客厅里录制的，使用了“Pro Tools、自动调音和一些混响”，并声称在那些看起来像是在对口型的视频片段中，他实际上是在真唱。

I suppose technically AI had nothing to do with the recording process if the song was generated.
我想，如果这首歌是（由 AI）生成的，那么从技术上讲，AI 确实与录音过程无关。
