# Decoupling Perception from Description: Computation-Grounded Representation Alignment between Multivariate Time Series and Language
# 解耦感知与描述：多变量时间序列与语言之间基于计算的表征对齐

**Abstract:** Training multimodal models to align time series with language runs into a self-supervision trap. The usual recipe asks an LLM to read a series and write a description, so label quality is capped by the perceptual skill the model is supposed to learn. The data can never teach more than the labeler already knows.
**摘要：** 训练多模态模型以实现时间序列与语言的对齐时，往往会陷入一种“自监督陷阱”。通常的做法是让大语言模型（LLM）读取序列并撰写描述，因此标签的质量上限被模型本身需要学习的感知能力所限制。数据所能传授的知识永远无法超越标注者已有的认知。

A second gap makes this worse: most datasets use a single variable, but the patterns that matter (cross-channel correlation, lead-lag structure, co-occurring anomalies) appear only with several variables, right where the labeling LLM's limits are most exposed. These two problems create a trilemma: existing methods are reliable, realistic, or scalable, but none achieves all three.
第二个差距加剧了这一问题：大多数数据集仅使用单一变量，但真正重要的模式（如跨通道相关性、超前-滞后结构、共现异常）仅在涉及多个变量时才会出现，而这恰恰是标注用大语言模型能力最受限的地方。这两个问题共同构成了一个“三难困境”：现有的方法要么可靠，要么真实，要么可扩展，但没有一种方法能同时兼顾这三者。

We resolve this by decoupling perception from description. Deterministic code computes a set of statistics from real, open-source multivariate series; the LLM verbalizes those precomputed facts. Perception, which LLMs do poorly, is handled by computation, while the LLM handles expression.
我们通过将“感知”与“描述”解耦来解决这一问题。确定性代码从真实的开源多变量序列中计算出一组统计数据；大语言模型则负责将这些预先计算好的事实转化为语言。大语言模型不擅长的感知任务由计算过程处理，而大语言模型则专注于表达。

This produces CGTime, our 4B-parameter computation-grounded time-series-language model. CGTime outperforms far larger general-purpose models on multivariate understanding tasks: it attains the best multivariate fact score on our held-out benchmark (0.283 vs. 0.173 for GPT-4o-mini and 0.203 for GPT-5.4-nano), a gap that survives Holm-corrected paired significance tests against every baseline. It also states verifiable numerical facts in generated captions more accurately and covers a broader range of statistical properties.
由此产生了 CGTime，即我们开发的 40 亿参数的“基于计算的时间序列-语言模型”。在多变量理解任务中，CGTime 的表现优于规模大得多的通用模型：它在我们预留的基准测试中获得了最高的多变量事实得分（0.283，而 GPT-4o-mini 为 0.173，GPT-5.4-nano 为 0.203），这一差距在针对所有基准模型的 Holm 校正配对显著性检验中依然成立。此外，它在生成的标题中能更准确地陈述可验证的数值事实，并涵盖了更广泛的统计特性。
