# 3 Best Cheap Gaming Laptops (2026): Lenovo, MSI, Alienware
# 2026年三款最佳平价游戏本：联想、微星、外星人

There was a time not so long ago when you could buy a decent gaming laptop for around $800. It wouldn't be pretty, but it could play modern games at decent frame rates and provided an entry point to PC gaming for a broader demographic.
曾几何时，你只需花 800 美元左右就能买到一台不错的主流游戏本。虽然外观可能不那么出众，但它足以以不错的帧率运行现代游戏，并为更广泛的群体提供了进入 PC 游戏世界的门槛。

These days, those same laptops cost $400 or $500 more. Meanwhile, the newer generation of entry-level gaming laptops has arrived, often offering similar performance for even more money—or by compromising the overall quality of the system. But there are a few standouts I've tested worth mentioning. And while it may not feel like the best time to buy a cheap gaming laptop, prices may only increase in the future.
如今，同样的笔记本电脑价格却上涨了 400 到 500 美元。与此同时，新一代入门级游戏本已经上市，它们往往以更高的价格提供相似的性能，或者通过牺牲系统的整体质量来降低成本。不过，我测试过几款值得一提的佼佼者。虽然现在可能不是购买平价游戏本的最佳时机，但未来价格恐怕只会更高。

### Best Cheap Gaming Laptop: Lenovo LOQ 15
### 最佳平价游戏本：联想 LOQ 15

The Lenovo LOQ 15 has been the best deal in gaming since it came out at the beginning of 2026, and that remains true today. It comes with an Nvidia RTX 5060 laptop graphics card, for one thing, meaning you get solid frame rates in games without needing to depend on image upscaling through Nvidia's DLSS technology or frame generation. The performance advantage over RTX 5050 laptops is substantial—anywhere between 11 and 40 percent depending on the benchmark. I was impressed by the performance across a suite of games, even though the system gets undeniably loud under heavy load.
联想 LOQ 15 自 2026 年初发布以来，一直是游戏本中性价比最高的选择，这一点至今未变。首先，它搭载了 Nvidia RTX 5060 移动显卡，这意味着你无需依赖 Nvidia 的 DLSS 技术或帧生成功能进行图像缩放，就能获得稳定的游戏帧率。与 RTX 5050 笔记本相比，它的性能优势非常显著——根据基准测试的不同，性能提升在 11% 到 40% 之间。尽管在高负载下系统噪音确实很大，但它在多款游戏中的表现依然令我印象深刻。

The display is even more surprising, though. So many gaming laptops around (or above) $1,000 use dim, crummy-looking screens with terrible colors. But the LOQ 15 looks fantastic by comparison. It's still only 1920 x 1080 resolution, but the colors are more vibrant and cover 100 percent of the sRGB color space. It can also be cranked up to 347 nits of brightness, which is bright enough as long as you're not gaming by a window. The significantly better screen makes the LOQ 15 a far better option as a laptop that can be used for school or work just as enjoyably as it is for gaming.
更令人惊喜的是它的显示屏。许多售价在 1000 美元左右（甚至更高）的游戏本，往往配备亮度低、色彩糟糕的屏幕。相比之下，LOQ 15 的屏幕表现非常出色。虽然分辨率仅为 1920 x 1080，但其色彩更加鲜艳，覆盖了 100% 的 sRGB 色域。其亮度最高可达 347 尼特，只要不是在窗边玩游戏，亮度完全足够。这块素质出众的屏幕使 LOQ 15 不仅是一款出色的游戏本，也同样适合学习和办公。

### The Cheapest Gaming Laptop Worth Buying: MSI Cyborg A15
### 最值得购买的超值游戏本：微星 Cyborg A15

If you want to go cheaper without giving up more performance, there's one clear option to consider: the MSI Cyborg A15. Despite only costing $999, it comes with the same RTX 5060 as the LOQ 15. That will be enough for many who are looking first and foremost for decent gaming performance at a discount.
如果你想在不牺牲性能的前提下进一步降低预算，有一个明确的选择：微星 Cyborg A15。尽管售价仅为 999 美元，但它搭载了与 LOQ 15 相同的 RTX 5060 显卡。对于那些首要追求高性价比游戏性能的用户来说，这已经足够了。

There are two significant compromises, though, when compared to the LOQ 15. The MSI doesn't have the high-quality IPS display, sporting the same desaturated color spectrum as other budget models. It also uses a lower-quality 720p webcam compared to the 1080p one on the LOQ 15. It even comes with one fewer USB-A port. Still, there's a lot to like. The touchpad and keyboard are both nice, and if you like a colorful machine, the Cyborg gives you three-zone RGB backlighting.
不过，与 LOQ 15 相比，它有两个明显的妥协之处。微星这款产品没有配备高质量的 IPS 显示屏，其色彩饱和度与其它入门机型一样偏低。此外，它配备的是 720p 网络摄像头，不及 LOQ 15 的 1080p 摄像头，且 USB-A 接口也少了一个。尽管如此，它依然有很多优点。触控板和键盘手感都不错，如果你喜欢炫酷的灯效，Cyborg 还提供了三区 RGB 背光。

### The Nicest Cheap Gaming Laptop: Alienware 15 Gaming Laptop
### 最精致的平价游戏本：外星人 15 游戏本

There's a lot to like about this new entry in Alienware's lineup. It's pretty and well built, with decent performance and a really nice keyboard. Of all the gaming laptops on this list, I like the design and simple aesthetic of this the best. But it has the same display quality problem as the MSI Cyborg A15. Colors are desaturated and ugly, and everything has a green tint. That would be more understandable if the overall cost was $1,000 or less like the Cyborg A15, but at this price, it's harder to accept.
外星人系列这款新机型有很多值得称道的地方。它外观漂亮、做工精良，性能表现不错，键盘手感也非常好。在榜单的所有游戏本中，我最喜欢它的设计和简约美学。但它和微星 Cyborg A15 一样存在显示屏质量问题：色彩饱和度低且观感不佳，整体画面带有绿色调。如果它的价格能像 Cyborg A15 那样控制在 1000 美元以内，这或许还能理解，但在目前这个价位上，这种屏幕素质确实难以接受。
