# Best Webcams (2026): My Honest Take After Testing the Best
# 2026年最佳网络摄像头：深度测评后的真实评价

It's difficult to measure the quality of the best webcams without evaluating them side by side. Every webcam I've ever tested handles light differently, and subtle variations in a room—like the location of your windows and the colors of your walls—can change your perception of a webcam’s capabilities.
如果不进行横向对比，很难衡量顶级网络摄像头的质量。我测试过的每一款摄像头对光线的处理方式都不同，房间里的细微差别（比如窗户的位置和墙壁的颜色）都会改变你对摄像头性能的感知。

So, when given the task of declaring one webcam winner, I realized I really needed to test all of them at the same time under the exact same lighting conditions. So, that's what I did. After many rounds of testing and using these webcams in my daily life, which just happens to include a very poorly lit home office, I came across a few gems that stood out from the rest.
因此，当任务要求我选出一款获胜产品时，我意识到必须在完全相同的光照条件下同时测试它们。于是我这样做了。经过多轮测试以及在日常生活中（恰好我的家庭办公室光线非常差）的使用，我发现了几款脱颖而出的精品。

### The Single Best Webcam
### 最佳网络摄像头

After testing more than 15 webcams, I found a clear winner for the best image quality, microphone, and design: the Insta360 Link 2C Pro ($200). It's remarkably compact and light, allowing it to work just as well on a laptop as on a monitor. That flexibility alone lends the webcam a uniquely practical design that most other webcams can't compete with. The Logitech MX Brio (my previous pick for best overall webcam) is almost three times heavier.
在测试了超过15款网络摄像头后，我找到了在图像质量、麦克风和设计方面当之无愧的赢家：Insta360 Link 2C Pro（售价200美元）。它非常小巧轻便，既适用于笔记本电脑，也适用于显示器。仅这种灵活性就赋予了它其他摄像头无法比拟的实用设计。罗技 MX Brio（我之前选出的最佳综合摄像头）的重量几乎是它的三倍。

The Insta360 Link 2C Pro magnetizes to its strong aluminum mount, and I never worried about it falling back, even with the weight of the USB-C cable pulling on it. This also makes its panning adjustments super-smooth. It's surprising how many webcams struggle with this very simple motion, but it’s perhaps the result of many mounts being plastic—including the Logitech MX Brio. Because the Insta360 is magnetic, it can also stick to any other metal surface, which can be convenient if you're looking to shoot from a different angle.
Insta360 Link 2C Pro 通过磁吸方式固定在坚固的铝制支架上，即使有 USB-C 线缆的重量拉扯，我也从不担心它会后仰。这也使得它的平移调节非常顺滑。令人惊讶的是，许多摄像头在处理这种简单的动作时都很吃力，这可能是因为许多支架（包括罗技 MX Brio）都是塑料材质的。由于 Insta360 采用磁吸设计，它还可以吸附在任何其他金属表面上，如果你想从不同角度拍摄，这会非常方便。

In terms of image quality, no webcam at this price delivers lowlight performance on par with the Insta360 2C Pro. Right out of the box, without even tweaking any settings, I was impressed by its ability to handle harsh light. Like all the cameras, I tested it in some poor conditions, including under overhead office lighting and being backlit. The webcam handled these scenarios better than its competitors. It avoided blowing out the highlights and could deliver as much detail in the image as possible, even compared to more expensive webcams, like the Obsbot Tiny 3 ($349).
在图像质量方面，同价位中没有哪款摄像头能达到 Insta360 2C Pro 的暗光表现。开箱即用，无需调整任何设置，它处理强光的能力就令我印象深刻。像测试所有摄像头一样，我在一些恶劣条件下进行了测试，包括办公室顶灯照射和背光环境。该摄像头在这些场景下的表现优于竞争对手。它避免了高光溢出，并能尽可能多地呈现图像细节，即使与更昂贵的摄像头（如售价349美元的 Obsbot Tiny 3）相比也是如此。

### Insta360 Link 2C
### Insta360 Link 2C

The standard Insta360 Link 2C ($150) is also a strong option. It's $50 cheaper, with a slightly smaller sensor and narrower aperture, resulting in worse low-light performance. I noticed that it introduced more noise, especially in areas of high contrast, such as along my hairline. But let's be real: Though you can hunt down the differences between these two webcams, the standard Link 2C still punches above its weight for the price.
标准版 Insta360 Link 2C（售价150美元）也是一个强有力的选择。它便宜了50美元，传感器稍小，光圈更窄，导致暗光性能较差。我注意到它引入了更多噪点，特别是在高对比度区域，比如我的发际线边缘。但说实话：虽然你可以找出这两款摄像头之间的差异，但标准版 Link 2C 的表现依然物超所值。

One of the biggest disadvantages of the non-Pro model, though, is in microphone quality. The Insta360 2C is more in line with other webcams I tested. It uses just a single microphone and lacks the ability to pick up the deeper frequencies in my voice, whereas the 2C Pro uses a dual-microphone beam-forming system that does a far better job capturing the fuller spectrum. I still think that the Insta360 Link 2C is one of the best options at around $150, though, handily beating out models like the Razer Kiyo V2 and Logitech Brio 4K.
不过，非 Pro 型号最大的缺点在于麦克风质量。Insta360 2C 的表现与其他我测试过的摄像头水平相当。它仅使用单个麦克风，无法拾取我声音中较低的频率，而 2C Pro 使用的双麦克风波束成形系统在捕捉更完整的声音频谱方面表现要好得多。尽管如此，我认为 Insta360 Link 2C 仍然是150美元左右价位中最好的选择之一，轻松击败了雷蛇 Kiyo V2 和罗技 Brio 4K 等型号。

### 能够胜任工作的廉价摄像头
