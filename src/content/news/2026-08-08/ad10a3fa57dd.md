# The 5 Best Laptop Power Banks I've Personally Tested (2026)
# 我亲测过的 5 款最佳笔记本电脑移动电源（2026 年版）

Toss the right laptop power bank in your bag, and you'll never need to do that panicked run to the nearest outlet again. A hefty portable charger frees you to sit anywhere with your laptop and keep it running. Power banks with USB-C ports can charge phones and headphones as well, and I’ve tested more than 100 of them over the past few years. These powerful, high-capacity portable batteries are the best ones when laptop charging is a priority. For this guide, I considered power banks that are 20,000 mAh and up, enough to charge most laptops twice.
在包里放一个合适的笔记本电脑移动电源，你就再也不用为了找插座而惊慌失措了。一个大容量的便携充电器能让你带着笔记本电脑随处办公，并保持续航。带有 USB-C 接口的移动电源还可以为手机和耳机充电。过去几年里，我测试了超过 100 款此类产品。当笔记本电脑充电成为首要需求时，这些功能强大、容量充足的便携电池是最佳选择。在本指南中，我挑选了容量在 20,000 mAh 及以上的移动电源，这足以让大多数笔记本电脑充满两次电。

Updated August 2026: I added power banks from ReelCharge and Pisen, removed some older devices, and updated prices.
2026 年 8 月更新：我增加了来自 ReelCharge 和 Pisen 的移动电源，移除了部分旧设备，并更新了价格。

### Best Laptop Power Bank
### 最佳笔记本电脑移动电源

**Ecoflow Rapid Pro Power Bank**

Power bank manufacturers have made strides in pushing up potential charging rates in their high-capacity portable chargers. EcoFlow’s Rapid Pro X is a prime example, just edging out Anker’s Prime series below. Capable of delivering 300 watts, this power bank is close to the maximum capacity allowed on flights, and can charge a couple of laptops and phones at the same time via the three USB-C ports (140W, 65W, 65W) and a retractable USB-C cable (140W). It has a wee display showing remaining power as a percentage and the charging rate for each connected device. You can also check temperature and battery health, tweak the brightness and screen timeout, and even connect to your phone via the Ecoflow app (though most folks won’t need to).
移动电源制造商在大容量便携充电器的充电速率提升方面取得了长足进步。EcoFlow 的 Rapid Pro X 就是一个典型的例子，它以微弱优势胜过了下文提到的 Anker Prime 系列。这款移动电源能够提供 300 瓦的功率，接近航班允许携带的最大容量上限。它可以通过三个 USB-C 接口（140W、65W、65W）和一个可伸缩 USB-C 线缆（140W）同时为多台笔记本电脑和手机充电。它配有一个小显示屏，以百分比显示剩余电量以及每个连接设备的充电速率。你还可以查看温度和电池健康状况、调节屏幕亮度和自动熄屏时间，甚至可以通过 Ecoflow App 连接手机（尽管大多数人可能用不上）。

The Rapid Pro offers a laundry list of charging capabilities (PD3.1, PPS, QC3.0, AFC, Apple2.4A, BC1.2, SCP, FCP, UFCS), but its greatest feature could be its super speedy recharge rate. Charge from a single USB-C input at 140 watts, and it takes an hour or so to fill, but you can plug in both at once to hit 80 percent from dead in under half an hour. Even better, Ecoflow’s dock can charge via the pogo pins on the bottom at 320 watts, enabling you to go from zero to 80 percent in just 20 minutes. You can fully charge it while you pack a bag.
Rapid Pro 支持极其丰富的充电协议（PD3.1、PPS、QC3.0、AFC、Apple2.4A、BC1.2、SCP、FCP、UFCS），但它最大的亮点或许是超快的自充速度。通过单个 USB-C 接口以 140 瓦功率充电，大约一小时即可充满；如果同时插入两个接口，不到半小时就能从零充至 80%。更棒的是，Ecoflow 的底座可以通过底部的触点以 320 瓦功率充电，让你在短短 20 分钟内从零充至 80%。你甚至可以在收拾行李的间隙就把它充满。

### Best Laptop Power Bank With a Dock
### 最佳带底座笔记本电脑移动电源

**Anker Prime Power Bank**

Anker introduced an innovative, high-wattage, high-capacity Prime Power Bank series a few years ago with an optional docking station for faster recharging via pogo pins on the bottom. This model is more compact and powerful than ever, offering 26,250 mAh at 300 watts across two USB-C ports and one USB-A, making it suitable for charging a couple of MacBooks and a smartphone at once. It also boasts a smart display and app control, and it comes with a 3.3-foot USB-C cable. Plug in both USB-C ports, and you can recharge at 250 watts to 80 percent from zero in 20 minutes.
几年前，Anker 推出了创新的高功率、大容量 Prime 系列移动电源，并配备了可选的充电底座，可通过底部的触点实现更快的自充。该型号比以往任何时候都更紧凑、更强大，提供 26,250 mAh 容量和 300 瓦总功率，配有两个 USB-C 接口和一个 USB-A 接口，适合同时为两台 MacBook 和一部智能手机充电。它还拥有智能显示屏和 App 控制功能，并附带一根 3.3 英尺长的 USB-C 线缆。同时插入两个 USB-C 接口，可以 250 瓦的功率在 20 分钟内从零充至 80%。

### Best Value Laptop Power Bank
### 最佳性价比笔记本电脑移动电源

**ReelCharge ReelPro Power Bank**

You get an impressive range of features for your money with this laptop power bank from newcomer ReelCharge. The ReelPro (Model PWB3001) offers a generous 27,000-mAh capacity, has a built-in braided USB-C cable, and sports an informative display that shows precise battery stats. It’s a little light on ports, with just one USB-C and one USB-A, but both are rated at 100 watts, and the cable can deliver up to 140 watts. The combined maximum output of 200 watts is enough to charge a couple of laptops or your laptop and other devices.
这款来自新晋品牌 ReelCharge 的笔记本电脑移动电源物超所值，功能令人印象深刻。ReelPro（型号 PWB3001）拥有 27,000 mAh 的大容量，内置编织 USB-C 线缆，并配有一个信息丰富的显示屏，可显示精确的电池状态。它的接口稍显不足，仅有一个 USB-C 和一个 USB-A，但两者均支持 100 瓦功率，且内置线缆最高可提供 140 瓦功率。200 瓦的组合最大输出功率足以同时为两台笔记本电脑，或你的笔记本电脑及其他设备充电。

### A Power Bank With Sci-Fi Styling
### 极具科幻风格的移动电源

**Aohi Starship Power Bank**

Long may this trend of sci-fi-styled portable chargers continue. The Aohi Starship looks like something you might find on the Nostromo, with a black and yellow design, three ports in one end, and a display to show remaining battery life as a percentage and wattage in and out.
希望这种科幻风格便携充电器的潮流能持续下去。Aohi Starship 看起来就像你在《诺斯罗莫号》（电影《异形》中的飞船）上能找到的设备，采用黑黄配色设计，一端设有三个接口，并配有一个显示屏，用于显示剩余电量百分比以及输入和输出功率。
