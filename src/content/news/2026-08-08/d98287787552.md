### Grad-CAM for Vision Transformers: A Systematic Taxonomy and Audit of Methodological Ambiguity in Explainable AI
### 针对视觉 Transformer 的 Grad-CAM：可解释人工智能中方法论模糊性的系统分类与审计

**Abstract:** Gradient-weighted Class Activation Mapping (Grad-CAM) is widely used to visualize model decisions, but it was originally formulated for convolutional neural networks, where spatial feature maps and channel dimensions have clear architectural meanings. Vision Transformers (ViTs) do not provide the same structure, instead representing images through tokens, attention, residual streams, and multimodal interactions.

**摘要：** 梯度加权类激活映射（Grad-CAM）被广泛用于可视化模型决策，但它最初是为卷积神经网络（CNN）设计的，在 CNN 中，空间特征图和通道维度具有明确的架构含义。视觉 Transformer（ViTs）并不具备相同的结构，而是通过标记（tokens）、注意力机制、残差流和多模态交互来表示图像。

This paper presents a systematic taxonomy and literature audit of how Grad-CAM and related methods are adapted, justified, and reported for ViT-based architectures. From an initial search of more than 550 papers, we identify 175 papers that apply Grad-CAM or Grad-CAM-adjacent methods to ViTs. We find that most papers do not provide a full mathematical or implementation-level account of how Grad-CAM is adapted to transformer representations.

本文对 Grad-CAM 及相关方法如何针对基于 ViT 的架构进行适配、论证和报告进行了系统性的分类和文献审计。通过对超过 550 篇论文的初步检索，我们确定了 175 篇将 Grad-CAM 或类似方法应用于 ViT 的论文。我们发现，大多数论文并未就 Grad-CAM 如何适配 Transformer 表示提供完整的数学或实现层面的说明。

To characterize this gap, we introduce a descriptive taxonomy of ViT Grad-CAM adaptations that makes explicit the feature locations, gradient targets, spatial reconstruction steps, and aggregation choices that are often left implicit. This taxonomy is not intended to prescribe a single correct adaptation, but to clarify the range of methodological choices being made.

为了描述这一差距，我们引入了一种针对 ViT Grad-CAM 适配的描述性分类法，明确了通常被隐含处理的特征位置、梯度目标、空间重建步骤和聚合选择。该分类法并非旨在规定唯一的正确适配方式，而是为了厘清当前所采用的方法论选择范围。

The study shows that Grad-CAM on ViTs is often treated as a trivial extension of CNN-based Grad-CAM, despite requiring nontrivial choices that affect rigor, reproducibility, and interpretation.

研究表明，尽管在 ViT 上应用 Grad-CAM 需要做出影响严谨性、可重复性和解释性的非平凡选择，但它往往被视为 CNN 版 Grad-CAM 的简单扩展。
