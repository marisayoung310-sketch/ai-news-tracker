# StyleComposer: Training-Free Multi-Reference Style Composition

**Abstract:** The style of a painting is not monolithic: color, texture, and structure may come from different sources. Existing reference-guided methods transfer them as one style signal, leaving each attribute's source and strength outside the user's control. 

**摘要：** 绘画的风格并非单一整体：颜色、纹理和结构可能源自不同的来源。现有的参考引导方法将它们作为一个统一的风格信号进行迁移，导致用户无法控制每个属性的来源和强度。

We ask where in a diffusion model one attribute can change while the others hold, and find that no single representation isolates all three. The proposed StyleComposer therefore routes each style attribute through the representation where it separates best and coordinates the routes over denoising time. 

我们探讨了在扩散模型中，如何在保持其他属性不变的情况下改变单一属性，并发现没有任何单一的表征能够将这三个属性完全分离。因此，本文提出的 StyleComposer 将每个风格属性引导至其分离效果最佳的表征空间，并在去噪过程中协调这些路径。

Without training or inversion, it satisfies three references and the prompt jointly more closely than prior methods, and exposes one strength slider per attribute.

无需训练或反演，该方法在满足三个参考图与提示词的联合约束方面，比以往方法表现得更为精准，并为每个属性提供了独立的强度调节滑块。
