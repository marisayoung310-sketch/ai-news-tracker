# Ranking the Best Smart Glasses: Meta, Viture, & More (2026)
# 2026年最佳智能眼镜排行榜：Meta、Viture 等品牌上榜

The idea of smart glasses has been around for decades, but the technology is finally catching up. The best smart glasses let you listen to music, answer texts hands-free, or teach you about the world, even as you're looking at it. And they're getting more popular: Meta and Ray-Ban sold more than 7 million pairs of smart glasses in 2025. Google, Samsung, and Apple are releasing their own versions soon. Even better than helpful smart glasses becoming more available is that these specs can also make you look fabulous.

智能眼镜的概念已经存在了几十年，但相关技术终于迎头赶上。最好的智能眼镜不仅能让你听音乐、免提回复短信，还能在你观察世界时为你提供相关信息。这类产品正变得越来越受欢迎：Meta 和 Ray-Ban 在 2025 年售出了超过 700 万副智能眼镜。谷歌、三星和苹果也即将发布各自的版本。比智能眼镜日益普及更棒的是，这些眼镜还能让你看起来非常时尚。

Before you pick your pair, you should know that smart glasses are hugely varied, even more so than many other product categories that we test. Some are glorified headphones for everyday wear; others have very specific use cases, like for sports, gaming, or working. They also come in a wide variety of designs, with varying specs and price points.

在挑选之前，你需要了解智能眼镜的种类非常繁多，甚至比我们测试过的许多其他产品类别还要复杂。有些只是日常佩戴的“高级版耳机”；另一些则有非常明确的用途，例如运动、游戏或办公。它们的设计、规格和价格也各不相同。

The WIRED team has tested just about every pair of smart glasses on the market. In addition to our recommendations, I also explain what you need to know about this category and give a quick history lesson about the space. Think you might need something else instead? Don't forget to check out the rest of our buying guides, including the Best Meta Glasses and the Best Virtual Reality Headsets.

WIRED 团队测试了市面上几乎所有的智能眼镜。除了我们的推荐之外，我还将解释你需要了解的关于该类别的知识，并简要回顾一下该领域的发展历史。如果你觉得可能需要其他产品？别忘了查看我们其他的购买指南，包括《最佳 Meta 眼镜》和《最佳虚拟现实头显》。

### Best Smart Glasses Overall: Ray-Ban Meta Glasses (Gen 2)
### 最佳综合智能眼镜：Ray-Ban Meta 眼镜（第二代）

Arguably, the success of the Ray-Ban Meta smart glasses is what spurred the current AI glasses boom. These are the updated version of the OG Ray-Ban Meta Wayfarers that were the category's first certified hit. As the sales numbers reinforce, these are the best and most popular smart glasses available.

可以说，Ray-Ban Meta 智能眼镜的成功正是当前 AI 眼镜热潮的催化剂。这是初代 Ray-Ban Meta Wayfarers 的升级版，而初代产品是该类别中首款获得市场认可的爆款。正如销售数据所证实的，这是目前市面上最好、最受欢迎的智能眼镜。

**Why WIRED recommends:** The second-generation Ray-Ban Meta glasses let you choose different frames, including the Wayfarer ones, and lenses (including prescription lenses), and they look the most like regular glasses. They don’t have any virtual screen—instead, you get a 12-MP camera, open-ear speakers, and five microphones. There’s 32 GB of storage, and they run for around eight hours when fully charged, with a case that can add another 48 hours. They have an IPX4 water resistance rating, so they'll be OK in light rain.

**WIRED 推荐理由：** 第二代 Ray-Ban Meta 眼镜提供多种镜框选择（包括 Wayfarer 款）和镜片选择（包括近视镜片），外观最接近普通眼镜。它们没有虚拟屏幕，但配备了 1200 万像素摄像头、开放式扬声器和五个麦克风。内置 32GB 存储空间，充满电后可续航约 8 小时，充电盒可额外提供 48 小时续航。它们具备 IPX4 防水等级，足以应对小雨。

The photo quality can’t match the latest phones, and videos are limited to one minute in length, but the quality is much better than what the originals offered, enabling you to record in up to 3K at 30 frames per second. You also get a host of AI features, including live language translation and the ability to ask about what you're seeing. But using these features requires you to connect to a smartphone, plus Meta AI also needs an internet connection.

虽然照片质量无法与最新款手机相比，视频时长也限制在 1 分钟以内，但其画质比初代产品有了显著提升，支持以 3K 分辨率、30 帧/秒的速度录制。你还可以使用一系列 AI 功能，包括实时语言翻译和询问你所看到的内容。但使用这些功能需要连接智能手机，且 Meta AI 还需要互联网连接。

### Best Wearable Displays: Viture Beast Glasses
### 最佳可穿戴显示设备：Viture Beast 眼镜

Viture Beast's 2026 model has a screen that's bigger, brighter, and with a wider field of view than our previous winner for best virtual screen, the Xreal One Pro. I spent a week using the Beast for work, watching movies with my 2024 MacBook Pro and my iPhone 16, and fighting with my family to take turns playing Tears of the Kingdom in 3D using my Nintendo Switch 2. I'm excited to take the Beast traveling with me this year and eliminate the need for a portable monitor.

Viture Beast 的 2026 款机型拥有比我们之前的最佳虚拟屏幕得主 Xreal One Pro 更大、更亮、视野更广的屏幕。我花了一周时间用 Beast 工作，用 2024 款 MacBook Pro 和 iPhone 16 看电影，还和家人争抢着轮流用 Nintendo Switch 2 以 3D 模式玩《塞尔达传说：王国之泪》。我很期待今年带着 Beast 去旅行，这样就不用再带便携显示器了。

**Why WIRED recommends:** It's easy to dial in a comfy fit, thanks to a wide variety of nose pads. You can buy prescription lenses, and my kids and I could switch lenses quickly enough to take turns using the glasses. The glasses now feature an onboard chip letting you control features like screen-width customization and three DoF (degrees of freedom), which makes your setting fixed around you as you turn and rotate. The chip eliminates the need to use cumbersome third-party apps (like SpaceWalker) to take advantage of the glasses' capabilities.

**WIRED 推荐理由：** 得益于多种鼻托选择，这款眼镜很容易调节到舒适的佩戴状态。你可以购买近视镜片，我和孩子们可以快速更换镜片轮流使用。这款眼镜现在内置了一颗芯片，让你能够控制屏幕宽度自定义和 3 DoF（自由度）等功能，当你转动头部时，画面会固定在你的周围。这颗芯片消除了使用繁琐第三方应用（如 SpaceWalker）来发挥眼镜功能的必要性。
