# Disentangling 3D Modeling from Spatial Reasoning
# 将 3D 建模与空间推理相解耦

**Abstract:** In this work, we explore an alternative paradigm for spatial reasoning by explicitly disentangling 3D perception from reasoning, rather than jointly acquiring implicit 3D perception and reasoning through large-scale training.
**摘要：** 在这项工作中，我们探索了一种空间推理的替代范式，即通过显式地将 3D 感知与推理进行解耦，而不是通过大规模训练来联合获取隐式的 3D 感知和推理能力。

Our key observation is that modern perception models excel at estimating continuous 3D geometry, whereas large language models (LLMs) are particularly effective at compositional and symbolic reasoning.
我们的核心观察是，现代感知模型擅长估计连续的 3D 几何结构，而大型语言模型（LLMs）在组合推理和符号推理方面表现尤为出色。

Motivated by these complementary strengths, we propose the Disentangled Spatial Reasoner (DiSR), a simple yet effective framework that reconstructs the physical world into structured 3D evidence using off-the-shelf expert perception models and fine-tunes an LLM with LoRA to perform reasoning solely over this explicit geometric evidence.
受这些互补优势的启发，我们提出了“解耦空间推理器”（Disentangled Spatial Reasoner, DiSR）。这是一个简单而有效的框架，它利用现成的专家感知模型将物理世界重构为结构化的 3D 证据，并使用 LoRA 对大语言模型进行微调，使其仅基于这些显式的几何证据进行推理。

Without large-scale 3D VQA training or complex tool-use policies, DiSR achieves competitive performance on popular spatial reasoning benchmarks.
无需大规模的 3D 视觉问答（VQA）训练或复杂的工具使用策略，DiSR 在主流空间推理基准测试中取得了极具竞争力的表现。

Beyond its strong performance, DiSR offers improved interpretability, modularity, and computational efficiency, demonstrating that explicit separation of perception and reasoning is a scalable and effective alternative paradigm to end-to-end modeling for spatial intelligence.
除了强大的性能外，DiSR 还提供了更好的可解释性、模块化和计算效率，证明了将感知与推理显式分离是实现空间智能的一种可扩展且有效的替代范式，优于传统的端到端建模方法。
