# Personalized Scorer Modeling: A Learning-Based Framework for Deriving Robust Sleep Stage Labels from Multiple Experts
# 个性化评分者建模：一种从多位专家中推导稳健睡眠分期标签的基于学习的框架

**Abstract:** Sleep stage classification is important for the diagnosis and management of sleep disorders, yet most automatic staging studies evaluate models against a single reference hypnogram despite known inter-scorer variability. This study investigates whether multi-scored datasets can be used to construct more reliable reference labels from the collective behavior of multiple experts.

**摘要：** 睡眠分期分类对于睡眠障碍的诊断和管理至关重要，然而尽管已知评分者之间存在差异，大多数自动分期研究仍仅针对单一参考睡眠图（hypnogram）来评估模型。本研究探讨了是否可以利用多评分数据集，通过多位专家的集体行为来构建更可靠的参考标签。

We use the publicly available DOD-H and DOD-O datasets. EEG (C3-M2) and chin EMG signals were segmented into 30-s epochs, and 30 features were extracted from each modality, yielding 60 features for EEG+EMG. We propose a learning-based hypnogram (LBH) that models the stage-specific behavior of each scorer using confusion matrices derived from machine-learning models.

我们使用了公开的 DOD-H 和 DOD-O 数据集。将脑电图（EEG, C3-M2）和下颌肌电图（EMG）信号分割为 30 秒的片段，并从每种模态中提取 30 个特征，从而为 EEG+EMG 组合生成 60 个特征。我们提出了一种基于学习的睡眠图（LBH），利用从机器学习模型中导出的混淆矩阵，对每位评分者的特定分期行为进行建模。

After column normalization, these matrices estimate the probability of each true sleep stage given each scorer's label; probabilities are aggregated across scorers to assign the final label for each epoch. LBH was evaluated with random forest, support vector machine, and multilayer perceptron classifiers under EEG-only and EEG+EMG settings, and compared with the dataset hypnogram (DH) and best-scorer hypnogram (BSH).

在进行列归一化后，这些矩阵可以根据每位评分者的标签估算每个真实睡眠分期的概率；通过汇总各评分者的概率，为每个片段分配最终标签。我们在仅使用 EEG 和 EEG+EMG 的设置下，利用随机森林、支持向量机和多层感知器分类器对 LBH 进行了评估，并将其与数据集睡眠图（DH）和最佳评分者睡眠图（BSH）进行了比较。

LBH consistently improved overall performance. The best results were obtained with random forest and EEG+EMG, reaching 86.07% accuracy, 85.46% precision, and 85.29% F1-score on DOD-H, and 86.04% accuracy, 85.21% precision, and 84.70% F1-score on DOD-O. These findings suggest that personalized scorer modeling can improve reference hypnogram construction without discarding information from individual experts.

LBH 在整体性能上均有提升。在使用随机森林和 EEG+EMG 时获得了最佳结果，在 DOD-H 数据集上达到了 86.07% 的准确率、85.46% 的精确率和 85.29% 的 F1 分数；在 DOD-O 数据集上达到了 86.04% 的准确率、85.21% 的精确率和 84.70% 的 F1 分数。这些发现表明，个性化评分者建模可以在不丢弃个体专家信息的前提下，改善参考睡眠图的构建。
