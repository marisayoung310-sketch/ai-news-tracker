# Can Vision-Language Models Assess Proxemic Risk from Egocentric Robot Images?
# 视觉-语言模型能否评估机器人第一视角下的空间距离风险？

**Abstract:** Assessing proxemic danger from a robot's egocentric perspective is critical for safe embodied navigation in human environments and requires both visual and contextual reasoning.
**摘要：** 从机器人第一视角（Egocentric perspective）评估空间距离风险（Proxemic danger），对于在人类环境中实现安全的具身导航至关重要，这既需要视觉感知能力，也需要上下文推理能力。

We evaluate three opensource vision-language models (VLMs) (*InternVL*, *Qwen-VL*, and *SmolVLM*) on the classification of egocentric robot images into four danger levels, comparing three prompting strategies and two rounds of QLoRA fine-tuning against a stratified random baseline.
我们评估了三种开源视觉-语言模型（VLM，分别为 *InternVL*、*Qwen-VL* 和 *SmolVLM*），旨在将机器人第一视角图像分类为四个危险等级。研究对比了三种提示词（Prompting）策略以及两轮 QLoRA 微调的效果，并与分层随机基准进行了比较。

Without fine-tuning, all models perform near the baseline, while fine-tuning yields only modest overall improvements. However, *Qwen-VL* with an advanced prompt achieves substantially higher recall for high-danger cases than the other models.
在未经微调的情况下，所有模型的表现均接近基准水平，而微调仅带来了适度的整体性能提升。然而，使用高级提示词的 *Qwen-VL* 在高风险案例的召回率上，显著优于其他模型。

An analysis of person localization further shows that correct danger classification does not correspond to better spatial grounding, indicating that a model may produce a useful safety label without attending to the relevant region of the scene.
对人员定位的分析进一步表明，正确的危险分类并不等同于更好的空间定位（Spatial grounding），这说明模型可能在未关注场景中相关区域的情况下，依然给出了有效的安全标签。

These results show that current VLMs remain limited in fine-grained proxemic reasoning and spatial grounding, although targeted prompting and fine-tuning can improve high-danger detection in selected models.
这些结果表明，当前的视觉-语言模型在细粒度的空间距离推理和空间定位方面仍存在局限性，尽管针对性的提示词工程和微调可以在特定模型中改善对高风险情况的检测能力。
