# Attribute-Conditioned Multimodal Slot Factorization for Controllable Fashion Retrieval
# 属性条件下的多模态槽位分解：可控时尚检索

**Abstract:** Fashion retrieval often requires satisfying multiple attributes at once, such as category, color, pattern, and demographic. Monolithic embeddings mix these signals into a single vector, making attribute-specific control difficult at retrieval time.
**摘要：** 时尚检索通常需要同时满足多种属性，例如类别、颜色、图案和人群特征。单体嵌入（Monolithic embeddings）将这些信号混合成一个单一向量，使得在检索时难以进行特定属性的控制。

Many existing semantic-ID methods provide discrete item codes, but these codes are typically optimized as item-level or residual addresses and do not expose named, independently controllable attribute slots.
许多现有的语义 ID 方法提供离散的物品代码，但这些代码通常被优化为物品级或残差地址，并未暴露可命名且可独立控制的属性槽位。

We introduce MM-slotgate, a multimodal slot encoder that factorizes Fashion-CLIP text and image embeddings into four named attribute slots. Each slot learns its own text-image gate, so visually grounded attributes such as color and pattern can rely more on image evidence, while taxonomy-oriented attributes such as category and demographic can remain more text-driven.
我们引入了 MM-slotgate，这是一种多模态槽位编码器，它将 Fashion-CLIP 的文本和图像嵌入分解为四个命名的属性槽位。每个槽位学习其独立的文本-图像门控机制，因此颜色和图案等视觉基础属性可以更多地依赖图像证据，而类别和人群特征等分类导向属性则可以保持更多地由文本驱动。

On H&M, using a combined slot-similarity and slot-logit retrieval score, MM-slotgate achieves 0.7566 macro ConstraintSatisfied@10, outperforming equal-weight multimodal fusion (0.7142) and fCLIP text-only retrieval (0.4755).
在 H&M 数据集上，通过结合槽位相似度和槽位逻辑检索得分，MM-slotgate 达到了 0.7566 的宏观 ConstraintSatisfied@10 指标，优于等权重多模态融合（0.7142）和 fCLIP 纯文本检索（0.4755）。

The largest gain is on color, which improves from 0.321 to 0.889 (+0.568 absolute), as the learned color gate assigns 57.4% weight to image evidence. The learned gates are interpretable without modality supervision: color is image-leaning, category is text-leaning, and pattern and demographic lie near the middle.
最大的提升出现在颜色属性上，从 0.321 提高到 0.889（绝对值增加 0.568），因为学习到的颜色门控为图像证据分配了 57.4% 的权重。学习到的门控在无需模态监督的情况下具有可解释性：颜色偏向图像，类别偏向文本，而图案和人群特征则处于中间位置。

The resulting slots also remain controllable: linear probes show no measured excess leakage beyond the label-correlation baseline, and quantized slot codes support targeted intervention, including a 15.3x lift for color.
由此产生的槽位也保持了可控性：线性探测显示，除了标签相关性基线外，没有测量到额外的泄露，且量化后的槽位代码支持针对性干预，包括将颜色属性的性能提升了 15.3 倍。

These results suggest that controllable fashion retrieval benefits from typed, attribute-conditioned multimodal slots rather than either a single global embedding or opaque item-level semantic IDs.
这些结果表明，可控时尚检索受益于类型化、属性条件下的多模态槽位，而不是单一的全局嵌入或不透明的物品级语义 ID。
