# HIMEC: Directional Change Representation and Fixed-Interface Decoding for Remote Sensing Image Change Captioning

**HIMEC：用于遥感图像变化描述的方向性变化表示与固定接口解码**

***

**Abstract:** Remote sensing image change captioning (RSICC) converts bitemporal imagery into a sentence describing semantic changes. Most RSICC methods condition caption decoders directly on fused visual features, leaving intermediate change structure and decoder-interface consistency less studied.

**摘要：** 遥感图像变化描述（RSICC）旨在将双时相影像转换为描述语义变化的句子。大多数 RSICC 方法直接基于融合后的视觉特征来调节描述解码器，而对中间变化结构和解码器接口一致性的研究较少。

***

We present HIMEC, combining Directional Change Representation (DCR) with fixed-interface decoding. DCR separates signed differences into appearance-oriented, disappearance-oriented, and shared-context streams before fusion. A learned-query encoder converts the fused representation into visually conditioned change-query tokens that form the scene decoder's only sample-dependent memory.

我们提出了 HIMEC，将方向性变化表示（DCR）与固定接口解码相结合。DCR 在融合前将符号差异分离为面向出现、面向消失和共享上下文三个流。学习型查询编码器将融合后的表示转换为视觉条件下的变化查询标记，这些标记构成了场景解码器中唯一的样本依赖内存。

***

A training-only auxiliary phrase decoder supplies caption-derived supervision. With a fixed zero input, the scene decoder maintains the same interface during training and inference. Separately, we evaluate a local-to-scene cascade conditioned on teacher-forced local states during training and autoregressive states at inference. On changed LEVIR-CC validation pairs, these states have a mean cosine distance of 0.69.

一个仅在训练阶段使用的辅助短语解码器提供了基于描述的监督信息。通过固定零输入，场景解码器在训练和推理过程中保持相同的接口。此外，我们评估了一种局部到场景的级联模型，该模型在训练时以教师强制（teacher-forced）的局部状态为条件，在推理时以自回归状态为条件。在 LEVIR-CC 验证集的变化对上，这些状态的平均余弦距离为 0.69。

***

Regime-matched conditioning recovers most of the associated deficit, whereas permuting state correspondence causes no detectable penalty. These findings are limited to the evaluated cascade. In a matched three-seed comparison, HIMEC reaches a Consensus-based Image Description Evaluation (CIDEr) score of $142.81\pm0.60$ on LEVIR-CC, versus $139.51\pm3.40$ for direct fused-feature memory.

匹配机制的调节恢复了大部分相关的性能损失，而置换状态对应关系则不会导致可检测到的性能下降。这些发现仅限于所评估的级联模型。在匹配的三种子（three-seed）对比实验中，HIMEC 在 LEVIR-CC 数据集上的 CIDEr 得分为 $142.81\pm0.60$，而直接使用融合特征内存的方法得分为 $139.51\pm3.40$。

***

On SECOND-CC, fixed-zero and regime-matched diagnostic conditioning reach 75.67 and 76.99 CIDEr, respectively, versus 60.77 for the mismatched cascade. The source code will be made publicly available at this https URL upon publication.

在 SECOND-CC 数据集上，固定零输入和匹配机制的诊断调节分别达到了 75.67 和 76.99 的 CIDEr 分数，而不匹配的级联模型仅为 60.77。源代码将在论文发表后通过指定链接公开。
