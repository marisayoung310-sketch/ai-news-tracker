# PseudoMapLabeler: Confidence-Aware Pseudo-Label Generation for Semi-Supervised Online Mapping

**PseudoMapLabeler：用于半监督在线建图的置信度感知伪标签生成技术**

A critical challenge in deploying online HD map construction systems to real-world scenarios is the scarcity of labeled training data, which limits model generalization in diverse environments. 

在将在线高精地图构建系统部署到现实场景时，一个关键挑战是标注训练数据的匮乏，这限制了模型在多样化环境中的泛化能力。

To address this limitation, we propose a teacher-student semi-supervised learning (SSL) framework that generates high-quality pseudo-labels from unlabeled data through confidence-aware map refinement. 

为了解决这一局限性，我们提出了一种教师-学生半监督学习（SSL）框架，通过置信度感知的地图优化，从未标注数据中生成高质量的伪标签。

Our approach first trains a teacher model on limited labeled data, then leverages Beta-distribution-based confidence maps to assess the reliability of predicted map elements across temporal observations. 

我们的方法首先在有限的标注数据上训练教师模型，然后利用基于 Beta 分布的置信度图来评估跨时间观测中预测地图元素的可靠性。

Unlike conventional filtering methods that discard entire elements, we introduce a spatial clipping technique that selectively preserves high-confidence regions while removing unreliable segments. 

与丢弃整个元素的传统过滤方法不同，我们引入了一种空间裁剪技术，能够有选择地保留高置信度区域，同时剔除不可靠的片段。

The refined map elements serve as map priors that improve the teacher model's prediction accuracy on unlabeled data in a second pass. 

优化后的地图元素作为地图先验，在第二轮处理中提高了教师模型对未标注数据的预测准确性。

These enhanced predictions become pseudo-labels for training a student model from scratch, followed by fine-tuning on the original labeled data. 

这些增强后的预测结果成为训练学生模型的伪标签，随后在原始标注数据上进行微调。

Experimental results on the nuScenes dataset demonstrate that our teacher-student framework with refined pseudo-labels improves performance by +6.1 mAP under a low-label regime compared to training on labeled data alone, offering a practical solution to the labeled data scarcity problem in online HD map construction.

在 nuScenes 数据集上的实验结果表明，与仅使用标注数据进行训练相比，我们采用优化伪标签的教师-学生框架在低标注数据环境下性能提升了 +6.1 mAP，为在线高精地图构建中的标注数据匮乏问题提供了一种实用的解决方案。
