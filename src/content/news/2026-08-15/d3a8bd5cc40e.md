# AI Referral Traffic Is Small but Growing: What the 1.08% Benchmark Means for Measurement
# AI 引流流量虽小但正在增长：1.08% 的基准对衡量指标意味着什么

AI referral traffic remains a small share of website visits, but it is becoming too important to dismiss. Conductor's 2026 AEO / GEO Benchmarks Report found that AI referrals accounted for 1.08% of total website traffic across 13,770 domains in 10 industries between May and September 2025. That is roughly one in every 100 visits, a modest channel today, but one growing at about 1% month over month during the study period.
AI 引流流量在网站访问量中占比依然很小，但其重要性已不容忽视。Conductor 的《2026 年 AEO/GEO 基准报告》显示，2025 年 5 月至 9 月期间，在 10 个行业的 13,770 个域名中，AI 引流占总网站流量的 1.08%。这大约相当于每 100 次访问中就有 1 次来自 AI，虽然目前是一个较小的渠道，但在研究期间，该渠道正以每月约 1% 的速度增长。

The more important lesson is not that AI has replaced search, social, or direct traffic. It has not. Rather, AI chatbots and AI answers and AI Overviews are creating an additional discovery layer where users can encounter brands, products, and publishers before they ever produce a measurable site visit. For marketing, editorial, and analytics teams, referral reporting alone can therefore understate AI's role in awareness and early research.
更重要的启示并非 AI 已经取代了搜索、社交或直接流量——事实并非如此。相反，AI 聊天机器人、AI 回答和 AI 概览（AI Overviews）正在创造一个额外的发现层，用户在产生可衡量的网站访问之前，就能在这里接触到品牌、产品和发布商。因此，对于营销、编辑和分析团队来说，仅依靠引流报告可能会低估 AI 在品牌认知和早期研究阶段所发挥的作用。

Conductor's 2026 AEO / GEO Benchmarks Report provides a useful macro-level benchmark for interpreting this shift. The data supports a measured conclusion: AI referrals are real, growing, and context-dependent, but they are not yet a substitute for conventional traffic channels or a complete proxy for AI-driven discovery.
Conductor 的《2026 年 AEO/GEO 基准报告》为解读这一转变提供了一个有用的宏观基准。数据支持了一个审慎的结论：AI 引流是真实存在且正在增长的，并具有情境依赖性，但它目前还不能替代传统的流量渠道，也不能完全代表 AI 驱动的发现过程。

### Why AI referral traffic needs broader interpretation
### 为什么 AI 引流流量需要更广泛的解读

A referrer records a visit that arrives from a traceable source. That makes it useful for understanding the traffic that actually reaches a website. It does not, however, capture every way an AI answer may influence a user's decision. A person may see a brand cited in an AI response, conduct a later branded search, visit directly, or choose not to click at all after receiving enough information in the answer itself.
引流（Referrer）记录的是来自可追踪来源的访问。这对于了解实际到达网站的流量很有用，但它无法捕捉 AI 回答影响用户决策的所有方式。用户可能会在 AI 回答中看到品牌引用，随后进行品牌搜索、直接访问，或者在从回答中获得足够信息后选择不点击。

This distinction matters because AI surfaces can shape visibility before the click. AI answers and AI Overviews may influence which companies, publications, or products users consider, even when conventional analytics attributes no visit to an AI source. Referral data should remain part of performance reporting, but it should not be treated as the entire measurement framework for AI discovery.
这种区别至关重要，因为 AI 界面可以在点击发生前塑造品牌可见度。即使传统分析工具未将访问归因于 AI 来源，AI 回答和 AI 概览也可能影响用户考虑哪些公司、出版物或产品。引流数据应继续作为绩效报告的一部分，但不应被视为 AI 发现过程的全部衡量框架。

Conductor's dataset also shows that the channel is concentrated. ChatGPT generated about 87.4% of AI referrals in the analysis, meaning broad AI referral figures can be heavily influenced by one platform's behavior and audience. Teams should avoid assuming that performance in one AI environment represents all AI search or chat experiences.
Conductor 的数据集还显示，该渠道具有高度集中性。在分析中，ChatGPT 贡献了约 87.4% 的 AI 引流，这意味着广泛的 AI 引流数据可能会受到单一平台行为和受众的严重影响。团队应避免假设在某一个 AI 环境中的表现就代表了所有的 AI 搜索或聊天体验。

### Measure Conductor benchmark Interpretation
### 衡量指标 Conductor 基准 解读

*   **AI referral share across analyzed domains:** 1.08% of total traffic. AI is a small but measurable referral channel.
    **各分析域名的 AI 引流占比：** 总流量的 1.08%。AI 是一个规模虽小但可衡量的引流渠道。
*   **ChatGPT share of AI referrals:** About 87.4%. AI referral activity in the sample was strongly concentrated in ChatGPT.
    **ChatGPT 在 AI 引流中的份额：** 约 87.4%。样本中的 AI 引流活动高度集中在 ChatGPT 上。
*   **Average monthly AI referral growth:** About 1% month over month. The channel was expanding during the May to September 2025 observation period.
    **AI 引流月均增长率：** 每月约 1%。该渠道在 2025 年 5 月至 9 月的观察期内处于扩张状态。
*   **IT industry AI referral share:** 2.8%. Industry context can materially change the benchmark.
    **IT 行业 AI 引流占比：** 2.8%。行业背景会显著改变基准数据。

The industry differences are especially relevant for planning. Conductor reported AI referral traffic of 2.8% for IT and around 1.9% for Consumer Staples, while Communications Services and Utilities showed lower shares. A single cross-industry percentage is useful as a directional reference, not as a target or forecast for every organization.
行业差异对于规划尤为重要。Conductor 报告称，IT 行业的 AI 引流流量为 2.8%，日常消费品行业约为 1.9%，而通信服务和公用事业行业的占比则较低。单一的跨行业百分比仅可用作方向性参考，而非每个组织的具体目标或预测。

### Publisher data requires a separate benchmark
### 出版商数据需要单独的基准

The 1.08% figure should not be read as a publisher-only result. Conductor studied brand domains across multiple industries, whereas publisher-specific reporting cited in the research indicates that AI sources accounted for less than 1% of publishers' pageviews. These findings can coexist because they examine different populations, metrics, and traffic patterns.
1.08% 的数据不应被解读为仅针对出版商的结果。Conductor 研究的是跨多个行业的品牌域名，而研究中引用的出版商特定报告显示，AI 来源占出版商页面浏览量的比例不到 1%。这些发现可以共存，因为它们考察的是不同的群体、指标和流量模式。

For journalism and media operators, the practical conclusion is similarly restrained. AI discovery is rising and deserves attention, but it does not currently dominate referral patterns or replace established channels. Publisher teams need their own baselines for AI-referred sessions, branded demand, citation visibility, and the effects of indexing and link-sharing policies.
对于新闻和媒体运营者来说，实际结论同样需要保持克制。AI 发现正在兴起并值得关注，但它目前并未主导引流模式，也没有取代既定渠道。出版商团队需要建立自己的基准，以衡量 AI 引流会话、品牌需求、引用可见度以及索引和链接共享政策的影响。

### Build AI visibility into analytics governance
### 将 AI 可见度纳入分析治理

A more complete approach combines click data with evidence of how a brand appears on AI surfaces. This does not mean assigning every future conversion to AI. It means treating AI as one potential influence in a longer customer or reader journey and documenting the limits of attribution.
一种更全面的方法是将点击数据与品牌在 AI 界面上的呈现证据相结合。这并不意味着将未来的每一次转化都归功于 AI，而是将 AI 视为更长客户或读者旅程中的一种潜在影响因素，并记录归因的局限性。

A practical governance model can include:
一个实用的治理模型可以包括：

*   Track AI-referred sessions separately from other referrals, while preserving conventional channel reporting.
    将 AI 引流会话与其他引流分开追踪，同时保留传统渠道报告。
*   Monitor citations, mentions, and brand inclusion in relevant AI answers to assess exposure that may not create a click.
    监控相关 AI 回答中的引用、提及和品牌包含情况，以评估那些可能不会产生点击的曝光度。
*   Compare AI signals with branded search and direct traffic trends as contextual indicators, not proof of causation.
    将 AI 信号与品牌搜索和直接流量趋势进行比较，作为情境指标，而非因果关系的证明。
*   Set clear ownership and policy rules for AI indexing, content access, link-sharing, and reporting definitions.
    为 AI 索引、内容访问、链接共享和报告定义设定明确的所有权和政策规则。
*   Review results by industry, market, and audience segment rather than applying a universal AI traffic expectation.
    按行业、市场和受众细分审查结果，而不是应用统一的 AI 流量预期。

This approach helps leaders avoid two opposite errors: dismissing a small channel that is growing, or overstating referral traffic as proof that AI has already transformed every acquisition funnel. The current evidence supports careful measurement, repeatable monitoring, and decisions grounded in an organization's own audience data.
这种方法有助于领导者避免两个相反的错误：忽视一个正在增长的小渠道，或者夸大引流流量并将其作为 AI 已经改变了每一个获客漏斗的证据。目前的证据支持进行谨慎的衡量、可重复的监控，并基于组织自身的受众数据做出决策。
