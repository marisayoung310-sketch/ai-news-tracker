### Will Pixel 9 Cases Fit on the Pixel 10 Series?
### Pixel 9 的手机壳能用在 Pixel 10 系列上吗？

Sometimes the stars align, and an updated phone model has the identical dimensions, shape, and design as the previous model, allowing you to use an older case. That is, unfortunately, not the case with the Pixel 10 series. Google updated the speakers on this line, so while you may be able to fit some soft Pixel 9 cases over the Pixel 10 phones, you'll run into other issues, namely blocking the speaker port. Pixel 9 cases without magnets will interrupt the Qi2 functionality on the Pixel 10 series. For the best experience, I recommend avoiding a Pixel 10 case altogether, whether it's for the Pixel 10 or Pixel 10 Pro.

有时运气好，新款手机的尺寸、形状和设计与前代完全一致，让你能继续使用旧手机壳。遗憾的是，Pixel 10 系列并非如此。Google 更新了该系列的扬声器，因此虽然某些柔软的 Pixel 9 手机壳可能勉强套在 Pixel 10 上，但你会遇到其他问题，主要是扬声器孔被遮挡。此外，没有磁吸功能的 Pixel 9 手机壳会干扰 Pixel 10 系列的 Qi2 功能。为了获得最佳体验，我建议不要在 Pixel 10 或 Pixel 10 Pro 上使用 Pixel 9 的手机壳。

### Will Pixel 10 Cases Fit on the Pixel 10 Pro?
### Pixel 10 的手机壳能用在 Pixel 10 Pro 上吗？

Most Pixel 10 cases will fit the Pixel 10 Pro, and vice versa. Several retailers market their cases (even Google) for both phones. I’ve tested several Pixel 10 Pro cases on the Pixel 10 and have had no issues. The cutouts are all accurate, the button placements are perfect, and the fit is snug. However, Pixel 10 and 10 Pro cases are not compatible with the Pixel 10 Pro XL.

大多数 Pixel 10 手机壳都适用于 Pixel 10 Pro，反之亦然。许多零售商（甚至包括 Google）都将他们的手机壳标注为两款手机通用。我已经在 Pixel 10 上测试了多款 Pixel 10 Pro 手机壳，没有发现任何问题。开孔位置精准，按键位置完美，贴合度也很紧密。不过，Pixel 10 和 10 Pro 的手机壳与 Pixel 10 Pro XL 不兼容。

### Pixel 10 的手机壳能用在 Pixel 10a 上吗？

### What Does ‘Qi2’ Mean for Pixel 10 Cases?
### “Qi2”对 Pixel 10 手机壳意味着什么？

The Pixel 10 phones are the first Android flagships with native Qi2 wireless charging. That means magnets are built into the backs of the phones, just like Apple's iPhones and MagSafe, allowing you to access faster, more efficient wireless charging. Magnets also align the coils perfectly, eliminating any worries about your phone not charging because you didn't place it just right on a charging pad.

Pixel 10 系列是首批原生支持 Qi2 无线充电的 Android 旗舰手机。这意味着手机背面内置了磁铁，就像苹果 iPhone 的 MagSafe 一样，让你能够享受更快、更高效的无线充电。磁铁还能使充电线圈完美对齐，无需担心因手机在充电板上放置位置不当而无法充电。

Not every Pixel 10 case has magnets baked in, though most of them do. (Almost all of the cases in this guide feature magnets.) However, I have asked nearly every case maker whether its cases are Qi2-certified by the Wireless Power Consortium (which manages the Qi2 standard), and the answer is almost universally no, which may point to less reliable and efficient charging. For the best experience, make sure to check the product listing to see if it mentions “Qi2,” “Made for Google,” or “Pixelsnap compatible,” which is Google's name for its magnetic accessory system. The latter two certifications at least ensure that the case was subject to a rigorous testing standard.

并非所有 Pixel 10 手机壳都内置了磁铁，尽管大多数都有（本指南中几乎所有的手机壳都具备磁吸功能）。然而，我询问了几乎每一家手机壳制造商，其产品是否通过了无线充电联盟（管理 Qi2 标准的机构）的 Qi2 认证，得到的回答几乎都是“没有”，这可能意味着充电的可靠性和效率会打折扣。为了获得最佳体验，请务必查看产品详情，确认是否标注了“Qi2”、“Made for Google”或“Pixelsnap compatible”（这是 Google 对其磁吸配件系统的命名）。后两种认证至少能确保该手机壳经过了严格的测试标准。

One neat feature to note: When you place your Pixel 10 on a Qi2 wireless charger, you should see an option to add a screensaver. This isn't a traditional screensaver, though you can totally just set a clock or photo and call it a day. There's also a Home Controls option, which lets you place your Google Home smart devices—like smart lights and smart shades—on the lock screen while the phone is charging for quick access. Head to Settings > Display & touch > Screen saver to set it up.

值得注意的一个巧妙功能是：当你将 Pixel 10 放在 Qi2 无线充电器上时，你应该会看到添加屏幕保护程序的选项。这不是传统的屏保，你可以直接设置时钟或照片。此外还有一个“家庭控制”选项，让你在手机充电时，将 Google Home 智能设备（如智能灯、智能窗帘）放置在锁屏界面以便快速访问。前往“设置”>“显示与触摸”>“屏幕保护程序”即可进行设置。

### Will My Old Wireless Charger Work for the Pixel 10?
### 我旧的无线充电器能给 Pixel 10 充电吗？

While Qi2 is backward compatible with Qi wireless charging, I have extensive evidence that not all Qi wireless chargers are fully compatible with Qi2 phones. The reason may come down to the placement of the wireless charging array on newer devices. By all means, try charging your new Pixel 10 with your preferred case on a wireless charger you already own. If it works, great! It won't be as fast or efficient, but at least it'll work. However, if it doesn't work, look for a Qi2 charger.

虽然 Qi2 向下兼容 Qi 无线充电，但我有大量证据表明，并非所有 Qi 无线充电器都能与 Qi2 手机完全兼容。原因可能在于新设备上无线充电阵列的位置有所不同。你当然可以尝试用你现有的无线充电器配合你喜欢的手机壳为新 Pixel 10 充电。如果能用，那就太好了！虽然速度和效率可能不如预期，但至少能用。不过，如果无法充电，请寻找一款 Qi2 充电器。

### How I Test Cases
### 我是如何测试手机壳的

I install every case I receive over the Pixel 10 series and take a look at the fit, whether or not ports or speakers are blocked, and ensure the buttons are clicky and responsive. I also monitor how much the case sticks out from the display—the taller the better, as it'll keep your display off the ground. To test Qi2 accessories, I gauge the strength of the magnetic connection. I also install every screen protector to test the ease of the application process.

我会将收到的每一款手机壳安装到 Pixel 10 系列手机上，检查其贴合度、接口或扬声器是否被遮挡，并确保按键手感清脆且灵敏。我还会观察手机壳边缘高出屏幕的程度——越高越好，因为这样可以防止屏幕直接接触地面。为了测试 Qi2 配件，我会评估磁吸连接的强度。我还会安装每一款屏幕保护膜，以测试其贴合的难易程度。
