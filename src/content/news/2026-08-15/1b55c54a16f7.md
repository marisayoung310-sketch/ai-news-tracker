# Unifying Generative Models with Path Integrals
# 通过路径积分统一生成模型

**Abstract:** We formulate generative modeling as a path integral in which flow-based, diffusion-based, variational, and adversarial models arise as different evaluation principles for a single master action.
**摘要：** 我们将生成建模表述为一种路径积分，其中基于流（flow-based）、基于扩散（diffusion-based）、变分（variational）和对抗（adversarial）模型，均可视为同一主作用量（master action）下不同的评估准则。

Its Martin-Siggia-Rose-Janssen-de~Dominicis (MSRJD) form separates free from interacting probability flows and opens them to diagrammatic perturbation theory.
其 Martin-Siggia-Rose-Janssen-de~Dominicis (MSRJD) 形式将自由概率流与相互作用概率流分离开来，并使其能够应用图解微扰理论（diagrammatic perturbation theory）。

The expansion yields a one-loop correction to deterministic samplers at no stochastic-sampling cost, which we validate on solvable and nonlinear drifts, where it reduces a 53 % tree-level error to 1.6 %.
该展开式为确定性采样器提供了单圈修正（one-loop correction），且无需额外的随机采样成本。我们在可解漂移和非线性漂移模型上验证了这一点，结果显示其将树级（tree-level）误差从 53% 降低到了 1.6%。

Imperfect learned scores enter as insertions and yield a response-weighted score-matching objective, and symmetry-equivariant drift design becomes an operator expansion with EFT power counting.
不完美的学习分数（learned scores）以插入项的形式引入，并导出了响应加权的分数匹配目标函数；同时，对称等变漂移设计转化为一种带有有效场论（EFT）幂次计数（power counting）的算子展开。
