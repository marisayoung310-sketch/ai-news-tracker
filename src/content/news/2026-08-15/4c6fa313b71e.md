# Surface-to-Skeleton 3D Cephalometry: Estimating Hidden Skeletal Landmarks from CT-Derived External Soft-Tissue Surfaces
# 基于表面到骨骼的 3D 头影测量：从 CT 导出的外部软组织表面估计隐藏的骨骼标志点

**Abstract:** Existing 3D facial-landmark methods localize points on visible skin, but whether CT-defined internal skeletal landmarks can be inferred from external soft-tissue geometry remains unclear. We formulate a coordinate-consistent surface-to-skeleton task using same-acquisition CT-derived surfaces, separating estimation from optical-to-CT registration, scanner-domain, and acquisition-state effects, with coverage analyzed separately.

**摘要：** 现有的 3D 面部标志点定位方法主要针对可见皮肤上的点，但能否通过外部软组织几何结构推断出 CT 定义的内部骨骼标志点，目前尚不明确。我们利用同一采集过程中导出的 CT 表面，制定了一项坐标一致的“表面到骨骼”任务，将估计过程与光学到 CT 的配准、扫描仪域以及采集状态的影响分离开来，并对覆盖范围进行了单独分析。

From 240 clinical CT scans from two hospitals, we construct a locked retrospective protocol pairing CT-derived external soft-tissue point clouds with 21 skeletal landmarks and three visible soft-tissue landmarks. An integrated hierarchical point-cloud model achieves 2.97 mm mean radial error on skeletal landmarks and 3.03 mm on deep or surface-invisible landmarks in 40 held-out patients.

通过来自两家医院的 240 例临床 CT 扫描，我们构建了一套锁定的回顾性协议，将 CT 导出的外部软组织点云与 21 个骨骼标志点及 3 个可见软组织标志点进行配对。在一个包含 40 名留出患者的测试集中，集成层级点云模型在骨骼标志点上实现了 2.97 毫米的平均径向误差，在深层或表面不可见的标志点上实现了 3.03 毫米的平均径向误差。

Patient-mismatch controls support patient-specific signal beyond a fixed population configuration or global similarity alone, while coverage ablations indicate dependence on non-anterior geometry. Optical-transfer diagnostics reveal substantial coverage-related and global-configuration components, although deployable optical inference remains unresolved. These results answer the controlled feasibility question affirmatively and provide a basis for hidden skeletal landmark inference.

患者失配对照实验证明，除了固定的群体配置或全局相似性外，模型还捕捉到了患者特有的信号；而覆盖范围消融实验则表明，该模型依赖于非前侧的几何结构。光学转换诊断揭示了大量与覆盖范围和全局配置相关的成分，尽管可部署的光学推断仍未解决。这些结果肯定地回答了受控可行性问题，并为隐藏骨骼标志点的推断提供了基础。
