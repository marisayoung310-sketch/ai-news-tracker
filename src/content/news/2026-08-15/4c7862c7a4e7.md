# MV2: Multi-View Multi-Vehicle Driving Dataset for Novel View Synthesis
# MV2：用于新视角合成的多视角多车辆驾驶数据集

**Abstract:** Differentiable rendering has advanced novel view synthesis (NVS), yet applying it to real-world driving remains difficult due to sparse capture viewpoints, dynamic objects, and limited multi-trajectory data. 

**摘要：** 可微渲染技术推动了新视角合成（NVS）的发展，然而由于采集视角稀疏、动态物体干扰以及多轨迹数据有限，将其应用于真实世界的驾驶场景仍然困难重重。

We introduce the Multi-View Multi-Vehicle (MV2) dataset and benchmark for evaluating NVS models under large viewpoint changes in dynamic urban scenes. 

我们推出了多视角多车辆（MV2）数据集和基准测试，旨在评估动态城市场景中大视角变化下的 NVS 模型。

MV2 features synchronized captures from a car, scooter, and drone, each following distinct yet synchronized trajectories. 

MV2 的特点是包含来自汽车、滑板车和无人机的同步采集数据，每种载具都遵循独特但同步的轨迹。

Training NVS methods on one vehicle's camera stream and testing on another enables evaluation under substantially larger viewpoint variations than existing single-trajectory datasets. 

通过在一种载具的摄像机流上训练 NVS 方法，并在另一种载具上进行测试，我们能够实现比现有单轨迹数据集大得多的视角变化评估。

All sequences are registered via Structure-from-Motion and camera poses verified using manual pixel-level correspondence annotations, yielding 50 high-quality scenes with 12,000 images. 

所有序列均通过运动恢复结构（Structure-from-Motion）进行配准，并使用手动像素级对应标注对摄像机位姿进行了验证，最终生成了包含 12,000 张图像的 50 个高质量场景。

Benchmarking recent NVS and camera pose estimation methods shows that NVS performance degrades with increasing viewpoint disparity, and that feed-forward pose estimators notably lag behind optimization-based approaches, highlighting MV2 as a rigorous testbed for NVS in driving. 

对近期 NVS 和摄像机位姿估计方法的基准测试表明，NVS 性能会随着视角差异的增大而下降，且前馈式位姿估计器明显落后于基于优化的方法，这凸显了 MV2 作为驾驶领域 NVS 严苛测试平台的价值。
