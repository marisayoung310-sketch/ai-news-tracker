### Best Weekly Planner: Roterunner Purpose Planner
### 最佳周计划手账：Roterunner Purpose Planner

If you need a single planner that can do it all and won't burn through your back-to-school budget, this is the one. The Roterunner Purpose Planner is an undated planner that has monthly spreads, weekly spreads, and two full open pages for each week, so there's plenty of room to write longer lists or a project brainstorm. Each monthly and weekly spread has a ton of premade checklist sections for you to use, too. There's even space to track your meals, water intake, and gratitude. It's a planner that actually helps me stay organized and gives me space to track and take notes without running out of room.

如果你需要一本功能全面且不会让你开学预算超支的手账，选它就对了。Roterunner Purpose Planner 是一款无日期手账，包含月计划、周计划，以及每周两整页的空白页，因此有充足的空间来书写长清单或进行项目头脑风暴。每个月度和周度页面都有大量预设的清单栏供你使用。它甚至还提供了记录饮食、饮水量和感恩事项的空间。这本手账确实帮助我保持了条理，并提供了足够的空间进行记录和笔记，完全不用担心空间不够。

The undated nature makes it easy to pick up anytime and use for whichever months you need. Fell off the boat for a few months? No problem. No pages will go to waste when you can fill them in as you go. It only covers six months, but I actually like that; it once again feels like a feasible commitment, especially if you're waffling on how much paper planner you want to promise to use. I love the massive B5 size, since it's finally enough space for me (almost too much, which is a rare find), but there's also a smaller (and cheaper!) A5 ($28) size.

无日期的设计让你随时都能开始使用，想用哪个月就用哪个月。中间断档了几个月？没问题。你可以随用随填，不会浪费任何页面。它仅涵盖六个月的量，但我反而很喜欢这一点；这让人感觉是一个容易坚持的目标，特别是当你还在犹豫自己到底能坚持用多久纸质手账时。我非常喜欢它巨大的B5尺寸，因为它终于给了我足够的空间（甚至多到有点用不完，这很难得），当然它也有更小（也更便宜，28美元）的A5版本。

### Best Daily Planner: Hobonichi Techo and Techo Cousin
### 最佳日计划手账：Hobonichi Techo 和 Techo Cousin

If you're getting into planners, there's no way you won't hear about Hobonichi at some point. There are a few different planners the brand makes, with the Techo being the most popular model. I tested the similar Hobonichi Cousin, which adds weekly spreads to the front of the planner after the monthly overviews. While I don't typically prefer a daily planner style, I was surprised by how much I liked using the Hobonichi. All of Hobonichi's planners use Tomoe River S paper, which is super thin but won't bleed through to the other side. It's also super smooth to the touch and to write on.

如果你开始接触手账，迟早会听说 Hobonichi。该品牌有几款不同的手账，其中 Techo 是最受欢迎的型号。我测试了类似的 Hobonichi Cousin，它在月度概览之后增加了周计划页面。虽然我通常不太喜欢日计划风格，但我很惊讶自己竟然如此喜欢使用 Hobonichi。Hobonichi 的所有手账都使用巴川纸（Tomoe River S），这种纸非常薄，但不会洇墨到背面。而且触感和书写体验都极其顺滑。

I liked adding my own structure to the daily pages; I sketched a box to separate my to-do lists and habit trackers from my hourly agenda for the day, and then would usually freeform the rest of the page based on what was on my mind. The Cousin I tested and photographed is an A5 size, while the standard Techo is the smaller A6. The 2026 Cousins are out of stock at most retailers, but you can still find them on Hobonichi's website.

我喜欢在每日页面上添加自己的结构；我会画一个方框，将待办事项和习惯追踪与当天的时程表分开，然后通常会根据当下的想法在页面的其余部分进行自由书写。我测试和拍摄的 Cousin 是 A5 尺寸，而标准的 Techo 是更小的 A6 尺寸。2026 款 Cousin 在大多数零售商处已售罄，但你仍然可以在 Hobonichi 官网上找到它们。
