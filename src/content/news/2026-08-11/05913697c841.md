# The Outlaw Chemist Teaching People How to Make Drugs From Scratch
# 教授人们如何从零开始制造毒品的“法外化学家”

“This is probably the most important video I have ever done,” William “Willy” Myco tells his nearly 2,500 paying Patreon subscribers. The video, posted on Halloween 2024, offers viewers step-by-step instructions on how to produce what he claimed is “100 percent pure LSD.”
“这可能是我做过的最重要的视频，”威廉·“威利”·迈科（William “Willy” Myco）对他近 2500 名付费 Patreon 订阅者说道。这段发布于 2024 年万圣节的视频，为观众提供了如何生产他所称的“100%纯度 LSD”的分步指南。

Wearing a baseball cap that said “Mycelium Familia” over his shaved head with his brown beard peeking out from beneath his mask, he declares his tutorial on synthesizing the psychedelic over the course of three days to be the “world’s first.”
他剃着光头，戴着一顶印有“Mycelium Familia”字样的棒球帽，棕色的胡须从口罩下方露出来。他宣称，这份关于在三天内合成这种迷幻剂的教程是“世界首创”。

“It has been a dream of mine to record this process so everybody has the scientific knowledge they need,” says Myco, which is short for mycologist, a scientist who studies fungi, in the video. (He doesn’t share his real last name, and he spoke to WIRED on the condition we wouldn’t use his last name due to the illegal nature of some of his work.)
“记录下这个过程一直是我的梦想，这样每个人都能获得他们所需的科学知识，”迈科在视频中说道。Myco 是“真菌学家”（mycologist，研究真菌的科学家）的缩写。（他没有透露自己的真实姓氏，并要求《连线》杂志在报道中隐去其姓氏，因为他的一些工作涉及非法性质。）

When the process is finished, the video shows his chemist collaborator holding out a lab flask of a greenish-blue liquid, but no test of the substance is conducted on camera to confirm its identity. Nonetheless, Myco issues a stark if hyperbolic warning about the final product, telling viewers, if it “touches your eyes, skin, mouth … you better cancel your plans for a month, ’cause you’re not going to be able to do anything.” (Most LSD trips wear off after a day, though there can be exceptions.)
当流程结束时，视频显示他的化学家合作者拿着一个装有蓝绿色液体的实验室烧瓶，但镜头中并未对该物质进行任何测试以确认其身份。尽管如此，迈科还是对最终产品发出了严厉甚至夸张的警告，他告诉观众，如果它“接触到你的眼睛、皮肤或嘴巴……你最好取消未来一个月的计划，因为你什么也做不了。”（大多数 LSD 的致幻效果在一天后就会消失，尽管也有例外情况。）

It was once unthinkable to post a video on the internet demonstrating how to synthesize a controlled psychoactive substance. But the masked psychedelic educator has released more than 120 how-to videos over the past decade which run the gamut from the attempted LSD synthesis to explainers on cultivating psilocybin mushrooms, extracting mescaline from mind-altering cacti, and preparing DMT vape cartridges.
在互联网上发布视频演示如何合成受管制的精神活性物质，这在过去是不可想象的。但这位戴着面具的迷幻剂教育者在过去十年中发布了 120 多部教学视频，内容涵盖了从尝试合成 LSD，到讲解如何培育迷幻蘑菇、从致幻仙人掌中提取麦司卡林，以及制作 DMT 电子烟弹等各个方面。

In addition to his Patreon, which had more than 14,500 subscribers, Myco sells mushroom genetics, merch (in the LSD video, he wore his own branded hoodie with the slogan “Love My Country But Fuck The Government”), and offers consultancy services for mushroom growers. His YouTube channel, called “Willy’s World,” had over 96,000 followers but was taken down after WIRED reached out to the platform for comment. YouTube spokesperson Boot Bullwinkle said it was removed “under our terms of service, which prohibit creating new channels after a termination.”
除了拥有超过 14,500 名订阅者的 Patreon 频道外，迈科还销售蘑菇基因产品、周边商品（在 LSD 视频中，他穿着印有“爱我的国家，但去他的政府”口号的品牌连帽衫），并为蘑菇种植者提供咨询服务。他的 YouTube 频道“Willy’s World”拥有超过 96,000 名粉丝，但在《连线》杂志联系该平台征求评论后被封禁。YouTube 发言人 Boot Bullwinkle 表示，该频道被移除是“根据我们的服务条款，该条款禁止在被终止后创建新频道”。

Myco responded on Instagram: “YouTube is back on their BS again. Channel had zero strikes whatsoever, I post super censored and safe content there … So if you’ve ever asked why I put all my energy into my patreon channel … this is why.”
迈科在 Instagram 上回应道：“YouTube 又在搞鬼了。我的频道没有任何违规记录，我在那里发布的内容经过了严格审查且非常安全……所以，如果你曾问我为什么把所有精力都投入到 Patreon 频道上……这就是原因。”

But shortly afterward, his Patreon account was taken down too. In an email statement, a spokesperson for the platform said: “Patreon prohibits works that provide instructions around the creation of controlled substances or other recreational drugs. We take violations of our policies seriously, and after an internal investigation, we have removed the creator’s account.”
但不久之后，他的 Patreon 账号也被封禁了。该平台发言人在一份电子邮件声明中表示：“Patreon 禁止发布提供制造受管制物质或其他娱乐性毒品指南的作品。我们严肃对待违反政策的行为，经过内部调查，我们已经移除了该创作者的账号。”

Myco hopes to successfully appeal the Patreon decision, as he pays his team through the funds accrued from his subscribers. “I know I’m a renegade, but I don’t promote the use [of drugs],” he says. “It’s all scientific. It’s all education, in the hopes to promote harm reduction.”
迈科希望能够成功申诉 Patreon 的决定，因为他依靠订阅者的资金来支付团队费用。“我知道我是一个叛逆者，但我并不鼓励（毒品）使用，”他说，“这一切都是科学的。这一切都是为了教育，希望能促进减少伤害。”

As hallucinogens edge closer to mainstream medicine, Myco is pushing in the opposite direction—away from clinics, regulators, and expensive trip-sitters, and toward radical self-sufficiency. To his many supporters, the outlaw chemist is democratizing psychedelic knowledge, out of the hands of academics, dealers, and erstwhile gatekeepers. To critics, his confidence occasionally outruns his science, and his commitment to secrecy raises questions about at least some of what he says.
随着致幻剂逐渐向主流医学靠拢，迈科却在向相反的方向推动——远离诊所、监管机构和昂贵的“致幻向导”，转向彻底的自给自足。对于他的许多支持者来说，这位法外化学家正在将迷幻知识民主化，使其脱离学术界、毒贩和昔日把关人的掌控。而对于批评者来说，他的自信有时超出了科学范畴，他对保密的执着也让人们对他所言的部分内容产生了质疑。
