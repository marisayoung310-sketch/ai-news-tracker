# SNI-GNN: SmartNIC-Assisted Full-Graph GNN Training with In-Network Embedding Prediction
# SNI-GNN：基于 SmartNIC 辅助的网内嵌入预测全图 GNN 训练

**Abstract:** Full-graph GNN training delivers high accuracy but scales poorly on multi-server clusters due to heavy, irregular inter-node embedding exchanges. We present SNI-GNN, a SmartNIC-assisted full-graph training system that reduces communication while preserving accuracy by predicting remote embeddings in-network.

**摘要：** 全图 GNN 训练虽然能提供高精度，但由于节点间存在大量且不规则的嵌入交换，导致其在多服务器集群上的扩展性较差。我们提出了 SNI-GNN，这是一个由 SmartNIC 辅助的全图训练系统，通过在网络内部预测远程嵌入，在保持精度的同时减少了通信量。

SNI-GNN deploys a lightweight linear-trend predictor on SmartNICs to refine cached historical embeddings, coupled with an importance-based boundary-node sampling policy and an asynchronous DPU--GPU data pipeline with intermediate-result reuse.

SNI-GNN 在 SmartNIC 上部署了一个轻量级的线性趋势预测器，用于优化缓存的历史嵌入，并结合了基于重要性的边界节点采样策略，以及支持中间结果复用的异步 DPU-GPU 数据流水线。

We provide error and convergence bounds showing that predictor bias remains controlled under bounded second-order dynamics and yields standard non-convex convergence with inexact gradients.

我们提供了误差和收敛性界限，证明了在有界的二阶动态下，预测器偏差保持在可控范围内，并能通过不精确梯度实现标准的非凸收敛。

Implemented on NVIDIA BlueField-3, SNI-GNN integrates with state-of-the-art full-graph systems, cuts communication by 21--45\%, achieves 1.3--3.6$\times$ end-to-end speedups over BNS-GCN and up to 1.29$\times$ over baseline SANCUS, with accuracy loss $\leq 0.01$, and scales efficiently to 16 GPUs on graphs with up to tens of millions of edges.

SNI-GNN 在 NVIDIA BlueField-3 上实现，并与最先进的全图系统集成。它将通信量减少了 21%–45%，相比 BNS-GCN 实现了 1.3–3.6 倍的端到端加速，相比基准系统 SANCUS 实现了高达 1.29 倍的加速，精度损失不超过 0.01，并且在处理拥有数千万条边的图时，能够高效扩展至 16 个 GPU。

These results indicate SmartNIC-based in-network prediction is a practical complement to partitioning and compression techniques for communication-efficient full-graph GNN training at scale.

这些结果表明，基于 SmartNIC 的网内预测是分区和压缩技术的实用补充，能够为大规模、通信高效的全图 GNN 训练提供支持。
