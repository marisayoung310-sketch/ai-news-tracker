# Fixed and Adaptive Topological DeepONets: Functional Measurements on Hausdorff Locally Convex Spaces
# 固定与自适应拓扑 DeepONet：豪斯多夫局部凸空间上的函数测量

Deep Operator Networks (DeepONets; arXiv:1910.03193) typically encode an input function through point values on a fixed discretization.
深度算子网络（DeepONets; arXiv:1910.03193）通常通过固定离散化上的点值来编码输入函数。

Building on the Topological DeepONet framework of Ismailov (arXiv:2603.11972), we replace point samples by continuous linear functionals drawn from the continuous dual of a Hausdorff locally convex space $(V,\{p_\alpha\}_{\alpha\in A})$, whose topology is generated by a point-separating family of seminorms rather than a single norm, and develop fixed and adaptive functional measurement systems.
基于 Ismailov 的拓扑 DeepONet 框架（arXiv:2603.11972），我们将点采样替换为从豪斯多夫局部凸空间 $(V,\{p_\alpha\}_{\alpha\in A})$ 的连续对偶中提取的连续线性泛函。该空间的拓扑结构由点分离半范数族而非单一范数生成，我们据此开发了固定和自适应的函数测量系统。

Measurements are combined with the coefficient-space Two-Step procedure of Lee and Shin (arXiv:2309.01020), while a training-only decoder and regularization stabilize the adaptive coordinates.
测量结果与 Lee 和 Shin 的系数空间两步法（arXiv:2309.01020）相结合，同时通过仅在训练阶段使用的解码器和正则化手段来稳定自适应坐标。

We derive a discrete error decomposition separating measurement, output-basis, and neural-approximation errors, together with a Barron-rate refinement.
我们推导了一种离散误差分解方法，将测量误差、输出基误差和神经近似误差分离开来，并结合了 Barron 速率细化。

The framework is evaluated on the antiderivative operator, a non-normable locally convex input space, heterogeneous Darcy flow, a controlled operator, and fixed-time and time-evolving Navier-Stokes vorticity operators.
该框架在反导数算子、不可赋范局部凸输入空间、非均匀达西流、受控算子以及固定时间和随时间演化的纳维-斯托克斯涡度算子上进行了评估。

In the heterogeneous Darcy problem, the functional models retain nearly resolution-independent errors of 5.5-5.6% on unseen grids, while in the controlled problem adaptive measurements reduce the mean error below 1.2%.
在非均匀达西问题中，函数模型在未见过的网格上保持了近乎与分辨率无关的 5.5-5.6% 的误差；而在受控问题中，自适应测量将平均误差降低到了 1.2% 以下。

For the fixed-time Navier-Stokes problem, the Adaptive Topological DeepONet is the most accurate DeepONet-based model, attaining a mean relative $L^2$ error of 1.685% +/- 0.017% using 128 functional coordinates.
对于固定时间的纳维-斯托克斯问题，自适应拓扑 DeepONet 是最精确的基于 DeepONet 的模型，在使用 128 个函数坐标时，其平均相对 $L^2$ 误差达到了 1.685% +/- 0.017%。

A comparably sized Fourier neural operator (FNO; arXiv:2010.08895) achieves the lower error 0.832% +/- 0.172%, but requires the full 64x64 input field, twice the training time, and 10.7x greater peak GPU memory.
规模相当的傅里叶神经算子（FNO; arXiv:2010.08895）实现了更低的 0.832% +/- 0.172% 的误差，但它需要完整的 64x64 输入场，训练时间是前者的两倍，且峰值 GPU 内存占用高出 10.7 倍。

The formulation provides compact, interpretable, and discretization-portable coordinates in the continuous dual $V'$, including for non-normable input spaces.
该公式在连续对偶空间 $V'$ 中提供了紧凑、可解释且具有离散化可移植性的坐标，即使对于不可赋范的输入空间也同样适用。
