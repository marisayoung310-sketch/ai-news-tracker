# Why I Chose NestJS and Never Looked Back
# 为什么我选择了 NestJS，并且从未后悔

A few years ago, I was just another developer trying to figure out how to build things that actually work, not just things that run once and fall apart the moment real users touch them. I tried a few paths. I read a lot. I broke a lot of things. And somewhere along that road, I found NestJS.

几年前，我只是众多开发者中的一员，试图弄清楚如何构建真正可用的东西，而不是那些只能运行一次，一旦真实用户接触就会崩溃的程序。我尝试过几条路，读过很多书，也搞砸过很多事。在这一路上的某个节点，我发现了 NestJS。

At first, it looked like just another tool. Another framework to learn, another thing to add to my resume. But the more I used it, the more I realized something. NestJS wasn't just teaching me how to build backend systems. It was teaching me how to think like someone who builds things meant to last.

起初，它看起来只是又一个工具。又一个需要学习的框架，又一个可以写进简历的东西。但随着使用深入，我意识到了一些事情。NestJS 不仅仅是在教我如何构建后端系统，它还在教我如何像一个构建长久产品的人那样去思考。

I did not choose NestJS because it was trendy. I chose it because it made me feel organized in a way nothing else had. It gave structure to ideas that used to feel messy in my head. It made me feel like a professional, not just someone typing code and hoping it works.

我选择 NestJS 并非因为它流行，而是因为它带给我一种前所未有的条理性。它为我脑中原本混乱的想法赋予了结构。它让我感觉自己像个专业人士，而不仅仅是一个敲着代码、祈祷程序能跑通的普通开发者。

Here is the lesson I want you to take from this, even if you never write a single line of NestJS code. Anything you build, whether it is software, a business, or even your own life, lasts longer when it has structure. Not rules for the sake of rules, but structure that makes room for growth without everything falling apart.

即便你从未写过一行 NestJS 代码，我也希望你能从中学到这一点：无论你构建什么——软件、事业，甚至是自己的人生——只要拥有结构，它就能持续得更久。这并非为了规则而设立规则，而是那种既能为成长留出空间，又不会让一切分崩离析的结构。

That is what NestJS taught me first, before it taught me anything technical. Organize your thinking, and the work becomes easier to carry. Today, when people ask me why I still use NestJS after all this time, my answer is simple. It is not just a tool I use. It is the reason I became confident in what I do. And once you experience that kind of confidence in your work, it is very hard to walk away from it.

在教授任何技术细节之前，这是 NestJS 教给我的第一课。整理好你的思维，工作就会变得更轻松。今天，当人们问我为什么这么久了还在使用 NestJS 时，我的回答很简单：它不仅仅是我使用的工具，更是我对自己所做之事充满信心的原因。一旦你在工作中体验过那种自信，就很难再放弃它了。

I write these thoughts as Peace Melodi, a backend software engineer who cares deeply about building things that hold up under real pressure, real users, and real growth. If any of this resonated with you, I would love to connect.
