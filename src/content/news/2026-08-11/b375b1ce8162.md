# Apple will stream Friday Night Baseball live in Vision Pro
# 苹果将在 Vision Pro 上直播“周五晚间棒球赛”

Starting on Friday, August 28th, Apple will begin streaming Friday Night Baseball in immersive video on Apple Vision Pro. The stream will feature commentary from various analysts and reporters, and live graphics will be anchored around the viewer's space during the game. After the games conclude, replays will be made available in the Apple TV app.

从 8 月 28 日（周五）开始，苹果将通过 Apple Vision Pro 以沉浸式视频形式直播“周五晚间棒球赛”（Friday Night Baseball）。直播将包含多位分析师和记者的解说，比赛期间的实时数据图表将固定在观众周围的空间中。比赛结束后，回放内容将在 Apple TV 应用中提供。

Last year, Apple streamed a selection of live Lakers NBA games to the headset, hoping to entice basketball fans. Baseball adds to its library of immersive content users are able to access on Vision Pro, which also includes immersive content like parkour across Paris and an Alicia Keys rehearsal.

去年，苹果曾向该头显设备直播了部分湖人队的 NBA 比赛，旨在吸引篮球迷。棒球赛的加入进一步丰富了 Vision Pro 用户的沉浸式内容库，该库目前还包括巴黎跑酷和艾莉西亚·凯斯（Alicia Keys）排练等沉浸式内容。

The first Friday Night Baseball matchup will be Boston Red Sox at New York Yankees, and the streams will span through September 18th.

首场“周五晚间棒球赛”的对阵双方将是波士顿红袜队对阵纽约洋基队，直播活动将持续至 9 月 18 日。
