# Improving Low-Resolution Face Recognition under Limited Data: How Synthetic Data Generation Can Close the Domain Gap
# 在数据受限的情况下改进低分辨率人脸识别：合成数据生成如何弥合领域差距

**Abstract:** Face Recognition (FR) systems in surveillance settings often encounter Low Resolution (LR) faces, those whose face region falls below the standard 112 $\times$ 112 input size. While labelled High Resolution (HR) training data is abundant, labelled native-LR data, and above all paired native LR/HR data, is scarce. One workaround is to synthesize LR data from the available HR faces, but how much synthesis effort is repaid in recognition accuracy remains unclear.

**摘要：** 监控环境中的人脸识别（FR）系统经常遇到低分辨率（LR）人脸，即人脸区域小于标准 112 $\times$ 112 输入尺寸的情况。虽然标注过的高分辨率（HR）训练数据非常丰富，但标注过的原生 LR 数据，尤其是成对的原生 LR/HR 数据却十分稀缺。一种变通方法是从现有的 HR 人脸中合成 LR 数据，但这种合成工作在识别准确率上能带来多少回报尚不明确。

We present a study of simple synthetic generation strategies for a compact, edge device-oriented face recognition system, spanning interpolation-based degradation, knowledge distillation, a Prepended Domain Transformer (PDT), Real ESRGAN-style degradation, and a learned Super Resolution (SR) front-end with an identity-aware loss. We evaluate these strategies on synthetic cross-resolution face benchmarks (LFW, CFP-FP, AgeDB-30) and on TinyFace, a real-world native LR dataset, and expose a synthetic-real gap: the degradation setting that is optimal on synthetic benchmarks is not the one that is optimal on real LR.

我们针对面向边缘设备的紧凑型人脸识别系统，研究了一系列简单的合成生成策略，涵盖了基于插值的退化、知识蒸馏、预置领域转换器（PDT）、Real ESRGAN 风格的退化，以及带有身份感知损失的学习型超分辨率（SR）前端。我们在合成的跨分辨率人脸基准（LFW、CFP-FP、AgeDB-30）和真实世界的原生 LR 数据集 TinyFace 上评估了这些策略，并揭示了一个“合成-真实”差距：在合成基准上表现最优的退化设置，在真实 LR 数据上并非最优。

We find that more synthesis effort does not help monotonically: the learned SR front-end does not surpass a direct feed of the aligned LR image into a strong backbone, while simple interpolation augmentation of a compact backbone is the only synthesis that improves over its own baseline. We conclude that generative methods for LR face recognition must be validated on real LR and against a direct-feed baseline, and release our pipeline at this URL.

我们发现，合成工作的投入并不总是与收益成正比：学习型 SR 前端的效果并未超过将对齐后的 LR 图像直接输入强力骨干网络（backbone）的效果，而对紧凑型骨干网络进行简单的插值增强是唯一能超越其自身基准的合成方法。我们得出结论，用于 LR 人脸识别的生成式方法必须在真实 LR 数据上进行验证，并与直接输入基准进行对比。我们已在此 URL 发布了我们的流水线。
