# Taylor Farms recalls jalapeño products for Salmonella amid Cyclospora outbreak
# Taylor Farms 因沙门氏菌污染召回墨西哥辣椒产品，同时深陷圆孢子虫疫情风波

Amid a massive Cyclospora outbreak linked to its iceberg lettuce, Taylor Farms is now recalling products containing jalapeños, which were linked last week to a large, multi-state Salmonella outbreak.
在因其冰山生菜引发大规模圆孢子虫（Cyclospora）疫情之际，Taylor Farms 公司目前正在召回含有墨西哥辣椒（jalapeños）的产品，这些辣椒在上周被证实与一起波及多州的大规模沙门氏菌疫情有关。

On Sunday, August 9, Taylor Farms recalled jalapeño-containing products, including diced jalapeños, dips, salsas, pico de gallo, guacamole, and pre-made salads, burritos, and sandwiches. The foods, which include both branded and unbranded products, were sold across 26 states at major retailers, including Hannaford, Kroger, Target, Trader Joe’s, Walmart, and Whole Foods.
8 月 9 日星期日，Taylor Farms 召回了含有墨西哥辣椒的产品，包括辣椒丁、蘸酱、莎莎酱、墨西哥番茄辣酱（pico de gallo）、牛油果酱，以及预制沙拉、墨西哥卷饼和三明治。这些食品（包括自有品牌和非品牌产品）在全美 26 个州的各大零售商处销售，包括 Hannaford、Kroger、Target、Trader Joe’s、Walmart 和 Whole Foods。

The recalled products contain recalled jalapeños from Coast Citrus Distributors, which was linked last week to a Salmonella outbreak that has infected at least 345 people across 27 states, sending at least 36 people to the hospital. No deaths have been reported.
此次召回的产品中含有来自 Coast Citrus Distributors 的墨西哥辣椒，该公司上周被证实与一起沙门氏菌疫情有关，该疫情已导致全美 27 个州至少 345 人感染，其中至少 36 人住院治疗。目前尚未有死亡报告。

According to the Food and Drug Administration’s outbreak notice, illnesses started being reported on June 19. Outbreak investigators interviewed 191 cases, and of those, 177 reported eating at Mexican-style restaurants, including Chipotle and Qdoba. Chipotle stopped using jalapeños from Coast Citrus on July 20, and Qdoba similarly ditched the peppers on July 28.
根据美国食品药品监督管理局（FDA）的疫情通报，病例最早于 6 月 19 日开始报告。疫情调查人员采访了 191 名病例，其中 177 人表示曾在墨西哥风味餐厅用餐，包括 Chipotle 和 Qdoba。Chipotle 于 7 月 20 日停止使用 Coast Citrus 的墨西哥辣椒，Qdoba 也于 7 月 28 日弃用了该供应商的辣椒。

On Wednesday, August 5, the FDA and Centers for Disease Control and Prevention announced the outbreak and identified Coast Citrus Distributors as the source of suspect jalapeños, which were grown in Sinaloa, Mexico. The FDA said that Coast Citrus had distributed the peppers to other food distributors, restaurants, and food service companies and that Coast Citrus was “in the process of identifying and contacting affected customers.”
8 月 5 日星期三，FDA 和美国疾病控制与预防中心（CDC）宣布了此次疫情，并确定 Coast Citrus Distributors 为问题墨西哥辣椒的来源，这些辣椒产自墨西哥锡那罗亚州。FDA 表示，Coast Citrus 已将这些辣椒分销给其他食品分销商、餐厅和餐饮服务公司，目前该公司“正在识别并联系受影响的客户”。

It’s unclear why it took Taylor Farms several days to identify that some of its products contained the recalled jalapeños. Ars Technica has asked Taylor Farms about when it realized it was affected by the recall and if it did so because Coast Citrus contacted the company. We will update this story if Taylor Farms responds.
目前尚不清楚为什么 Taylor Farms 花了几天时间才确认其部分产品含有被召回的墨西哥辣椒。Ars Technica 已向 Taylor Farms 询问其何时意识到受到召回影响，以及是否是因为 Coast Citrus 主动联系了该公司。如果 Taylor Farms 做出回应，我们将更新此报道。

### Taylor Farms’ record
### Taylor Farms 的过往记录

Taylor Farms, based in Salinas, California, is a major distributor of fresh produce in the US. It has recently been in headlines for its connection to an ongoing, 47-state outbreak of the parasite Cyclospora. Taylor Farms’ Mexican-grown iceberg lettuce, distributed to at least 27 states, has been linked to cases in at least 15 states so far.
总部位于加利福尼亚州萨利纳斯的 Taylor Farms 是美国主要的农产品分销商。最近，该公司因与一场波及 47 个州的圆孢子虫寄生虫疫情有关而登上头条。Taylor Farms 在墨西哥种植并分销到至少 27 个州的冰山生菜，目前已证实与至少 15 个州的感染病例有关。

In all, at least 22,723 cases of cyclosporiasis (confirmed and probable) have been reported in the US this year, according to the CDC. The total easily sets a record above standard yearly cases, which have been between 2,000 and 5,000 cases in recent years. Over 500 people have been hospitalized, and two people have died in the outbreak.
据 CDC 数据显示，今年美国共报告了至少 22,723 例圆孢子虫病（确诊及疑似病例）。这一总数远超往年通常每年 2,000 至 5,000 例的水平，创下历史新高。疫情已导致超过 500 人住院，2 人死亡。

Taylor Farms has drawn scrutiny for its handling of and communication about the Cyclospora outbreak. It has revised its statement about the outbreak several times, at one point claiming the FDA had apologized to the company, which the FDA denied. Its recall information has been vague; the company has not given the names of retailers and companies that received its lettuce, and it has not reported how much lettuce was recalled.
Taylor Farms 在处理圆孢子虫疫情及其沟通方式上受到了严格审查。该公司曾多次修改关于疫情的声明，一度声称 FDA 已向其道歉，但遭到 FDA 否认。其召回信息一直含糊不清；公司未公布接收其生菜的零售商和公司名称，也未报告召回生菜的具体数量。

Further, the company donated to a Trump-backing super PAC, MAGA, Inc, shortly after the administration delayed implementing an FDA rule that would have strengthened regulations for tracing foods through supply chains in the case of such outbreak events. The company also reportedly called the White House directly to try to delay issuing a recall for its lettuce.
此外，在政府推迟实施一项本可加强此类疫情中食品供应链溯源监管的 FDA 法规后不久，该公司便向支持特朗普的超级政治行动委员会“MAGA, Inc”进行了捐款。据报道，该公司还曾直接致电白宫，试图推迟对其生菜的召回。

Symptoms of cyclosporiasis usually begin about a week after consuming contaminated food or water, but can start as early as two days or take as long as two weeks. The most common symptom is watery, explosive diarrhea, as well as bloating, stomach cramps, gas, nausea, and fatigue.
圆孢子虫病的症状通常在食用受污染的食物或水后约一周出现，但也可能早在两天内或长达两周后出现。最常见的症状是水样喷射性腹泻，以及腹胀、胃痉挛、胀气、恶心和疲劳。

Symptoms of Salmonella infections begin between six hours and six days after consuming the bacteria. Common symptoms include fever, diarrhea that can be bloody, and stomach cramps. The infection also has a risk of spreading to the urine, blood, bones, joints, brain, and other organs. Salmonella bacteria are the leading cause of foodborne illnesses, hospitalizations, and deaths in the US. Children under age 5, the elderly, and people with weakened immune symptoms are most at risk.
沙门氏菌感染的症状通常在摄入细菌后 6 小时至 6 天内出现。常见症状包括发烧、可能带血的腹泻以及胃痉挛。该感染还有扩散至尿液、血液、骨骼、关节、大脑和其他器官的风险。沙门氏菌是美国食源性疾病、住院和死亡的主要原因。5 岁以下儿童、老年人和免疫系统较弱的人群风险最高。
