# Costco’s great Switch 2 console bundle includes over $100 in free stuff
# 好市多推出的 Switch 2 主机套装包含价值超过 100 美元的赠品

Costco’s deal on the Switch 2 includes a year of Nintendo Switch Online and a 256GB MicroSD Express card.
好市多（Costco）推出的 Switch 2 优惠套装包含了一年的 Nintendo Switch Online 会员资格以及一张 256GB 的 MicroSD Express 存储卡。

The cost of the Nintendo Switch 2 is going up by $50 on September 1st, 2026, resulting in a $499.99 price tag for just the console. We’re just weeks away, but Costco is offering a fantastic $499.99 bundle for its members that includes a Nintendo Switch 2, a 256GB microSD Express card (usually $59), and a one year subscription to Nintendo Switch Online’s Expansion Pack tier, which normally costs $49.99 per year. That’s over $100 in value, and a great way to kickstart your Switch 2 experience.
任天堂 Switch 2 的价格将于 2026 年 9 月 1 日上涨 50 美元，届时仅主机的售价将达到 499.99 美元。虽然距离涨价仅剩几周时间，但好市多目前为会员提供了一套超值的 499.99 美元套装，其中包括一台 Switch 2 主机、一张 256GB microSD Express 存储卡（通常售价 59 美元），以及一年期的 Nintendo Switch Online 扩展包会员（通常每年售价 49.99 美元）。这相当于超过 100 美元的额外价值，是开启你 Switch 2 游戏体验的绝佳方式。

The Switch 2 has 256GB of internal storage, so a microSD card is likely something you’ll need eventually for storing more games without having to constantly manage your library. With first-party Nintendo games typically running between 10 and 25GB, and larger third-party games reaching 50GB, a 256GB card should store anywhere from 5-20 additional games.
Switch 2 自带 256GB 的内置存储空间，因此你最终很可能需要一张 microSD 卡来存储更多游戏，以免频繁清理游戏库。考虑到任天堂第一方游戏通常占用 10 到 25GB，而大型第三方游戏可达 50GB，这张 256GB 的存储卡大约可以额外容纳 5 到 20 款游戏。

The Switch Online subscription includes a variety of features like cloud saving, online play, plus classic NES, SNES, and Game Boy titles. The Expansion Pack adds games from the GameCube, Game Boy Advance, N64, and Virtual Boy, and includes features for modern games, notably the Switch 2 upgrade packs for The Legend of Zelda: Breath of the Wild and Tears of the Kingdom.
Switch Online 会员订阅包含云存档、在线联机以及 NES、SNES 和 Game Boy 经典游戏等多种功能。扩展包则增加了来自 GameCube、Game Boy Advance、N64 和 Virtual Boy 的游戏库，并包含针对现代游戏的增强功能，特别是《塞尔达传说：旷野之息》和《王国之泪》的 Switch 2 升级包。
