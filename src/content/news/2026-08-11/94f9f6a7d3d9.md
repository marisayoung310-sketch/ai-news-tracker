### Title: Risk-Aware Decision Policies for Agents Under Noisy Perception
### 标题：噪声感知下智能体的风险感知决策策略

**Abstract:** Perception in biological systems is inherently noisy, requiring organisms to make decisions under uncertainty where misclassification can be costly or fatal.
**摘要：** 生物系统中的感知本质上是带有噪声的，这要求生物体必须在不确定性下做出决策，而错误的分类可能会带来高昂的代价甚至致命的后果。

We present an Artificial Life predator-prey model of foraging under noisy perception, and compare agent performance when using various policies that take into account their noisy predictions.
我们提出了一个在噪声感知下进行觅食的人工生命捕食者-猎物模型，并比较了智能体在采用各种考虑了噪声预测的策略时的表现。

Through controlled experiments under both symmetric and asymmetric perceptual noise, we show that blindly trusting perceptual labels leads to catastrophic failure as noise increases, while uncertainty-aware strategies significantly improve survival and reduce fatal errors.
通过在对称和非对称感知噪声下的对照实验，我们证明了随着噪声的增加，盲目信任感知标签会导致灾难性的失败，而具有不确定性意识的策略则能显著提高生存率并减少致命错误。

We further observe qualitative regime shifts in behaviour, with agents transitioning from exploratory to conservative strategies as uncertainty increases.
我们进一步观察到了行为上的定性机制转变，即随着不确定性的增加，智能体会从探索性策略转向保守型策略。

Our model links risk-sensitive foraging, ecological information use, and Artificial Life by showing that explicit information gathering can improve robustness when perception is unreliable.
我们的模型通过展示在感知不可靠时，显式的信息收集可以提高鲁棒性，从而将风险敏感型觅食、生态信息利用和人工生命联系了起来。

These results highlight the importance of uncertainty-aware decision-making and provide an interpretable artificial life analogue to robust learning with noisy labels.
这些结果强调了不确定性感知决策的重要性，并为带有噪声标签的鲁棒学习提供了一个可解释的人工生命模拟案例。
