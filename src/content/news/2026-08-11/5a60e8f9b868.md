# ED-CSP: Crystal Structure Prediction from Electron Diffraction
# ED-CSP：基于电子衍射的晶体结构预测

**Abstract:** Recovering a periodic 3D crystal structure from sparse, unindexed electron diffraction (ED) observations is a challenging generative inverse problem. Existing ED-based learning methods mainly predict crystallographic labels, reconstruct structures from indexed reflections, or retrieve candidates from finite structure libraries.

**摘要：** 从稀疏且未索引的电子衍射（ED）观测数据中恢复周期性 3D 晶体结构是一个具有挑战性的生成式反问题。现有的基于 ED 的学习方法主要用于预测晶体学标签、从已索引的反射中重建结构，或从有限的结构库中检索候选结构。

Here, we introduce ED-CSP, a machine learning framework that predicts crystal structures from chemical composition, atom count, and multiple detector-plane ED spot sets. ED-CSP combines a relational set encoder, permutation-invariant multi-view aggregation, and a periodic flow generator to jointly predict lattice parameters and fractional atomic coordinates.

在此，我们介绍了 ED-CSP，这是一个能够根据化学成分、原子计数和多个探测器平面的 ED 斑点集来预测晶体结构的机器学习框架。ED-CSP 结合了关系集编码器、置换不变的多视图聚合以及周期性流生成器，以联合预测晶格参数和分数原子坐标。

To train the model, we construct ED-CS, a dataset of 4.85 million simulated multi-view ED crystal structures, deduplicated across seven materials repositories and filtered to exclude CHILI-100K overlaps. On 2,075 held-out CHILI-100K materials, ED-CSP trained only on CHILI achieves a structural match rate of 57.49% MR@5, outperforming PXRDGen (52.92%), a state-of-the-art crystal structure prediction model conditioned on powder X-ray diffraction.

为了训练该模型，我们构建了 ED-CS 数据集，其中包含 485 万个模拟的多视图 ED 晶体结构。该数据集对七个材料存储库进行了去重，并过滤掉了与 CHILI-100K 重叠的部分。在 2,075 个留出的 CHILI-100K 材料上，仅在 CHILI 上训练的 ED-CSP 实现了 57.49% 的结构匹配率（MR@5），优于基于粉末 X 射线衍射的最先进晶体结构预测模型 PXRDGen（52.92%）。

Scaling training data further improves performance: initializing from a one-million-structure precursor raises MR@5 to 66.27%. On 1,024 compositions absent from the training retrieval library, the model still achieves 53.52% MR@5, demonstrating true generative capability beyond exact-formula retrieval.

扩展训练数据进一步提升了性能：从一百万个结构的预训练模型进行初始化，将 MR@5 提高到了 66.27%。在训练检索库中不存在的 1,024 种成分上，该模型仍达到了 53.52% 的 MR@5，证明了其超越精确公式检索的真正生成能力。

Replacing target ED observations with diffraction from non-isomorphic structures of identical composition decreases MR@5 by 22.09 percentage points, confirming that predictions depend on the input diffraction patterns rather than composition alone. ED-CSP and ED-CS establish a benchmark for generative crystal structure prediction from sparse ED observations and provide a foundation for future transfer to experimental data.

用相同成分的非同构结构的衍射数据替换目标 ED 观测数据后，MR@5 下降了 22.09 个百分点，这证实了预测结果依赖于输入的衍射图案，而不仅仅是化学成分。ED-CSP 和 ED-CS 为基于稀疏 ED 观测的生成式晶体结构预测建立了基准，并为未来向实验数据的迁移提供了基础。
