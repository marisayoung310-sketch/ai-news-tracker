# Bluesky now lets you hide reposts from that annoying person you follow
# Bluesky 现在允许你屏蔽所关注账号的转发内容

Bluesky is giving you another way to control your feeds.
Bluesky 正在为你提供另一种控制信息流的方式。

Bluesky has added a new feature that lets you hide reposts from a specific person in your feeds. The tool might be helpful if you want to stop seeing reposts from that one person who clogs up your feed with reposts of things you don’t care about. If you follow me, I may be that person, I’m sorry. I try not to be.
Bluesky 新增了一项功能，允许你屏蔽信息流中特定用户的转发内容。如果你不想再看到某个人频繁转发你并不感兴趣的内容，从而导致你的信息流被“刷屏”，这个工具将会非常有用。如果你关注了我，我可能就是那个让你困扰的人，对此我深表歉意，我尽量克制自己。

You can hide reposts from individual users by going to their profile, clicking the three dots button, and selecting the option to “Hide reposts in feeds.” (X offers a similar toggle.)
你可以通过访问特定用户的个人资料页面，点击三个点的菜单按钮，并选择“在信息流中隐藏转发”（Hide reposts in feeds）选项来屏蔽该用户的转发内容。（X 平台也提供了类似的开关。）

Bluesky also says that it’s launching a beta feature that will automatically add post numbers to threads. For long threads, it could be a useful way to get a sense of how much there might be to read, and it means you won’t have to manually add the numbers yourself. (Meta’s Threads already adds post counts to threads.) You can turn the feature on from Bluesky’s settings.
Bluesky 还表示，他们正在推出一项测试功能，可以自动为帖子串（threads）添加编号。对于长帖串来说，这有助于让你了解阅读量，也意味着你无需再手动添加编号。（Meta 的 Threads 平台已经具备了为帖子串添加计数的功能。）你可以在 Bluesky 的设置中开启此功能。
