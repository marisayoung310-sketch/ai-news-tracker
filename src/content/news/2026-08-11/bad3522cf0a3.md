# Best Wireless Earbuds We’d Buy Right Now (2026): Apple, Sony, Bose, and More
# 2026 年我们最推荐购买的无线耳机：Apple、Sony、Bose 等品牌

I use wireless earbuds constantly. I use them for work, running, working out, relaxing, traveling—whatever I am doing, chances are I have a pair of wireless earbuds on me, just in case. While some of the earliest models were huge and inconvenient, earbuds nowadays are slim, comfortable, and often affordable.
我经常使用无线耳机。无论是工作、跑步、健身、放松还是旅行——无论我在做什么，我身边大概率都会带着一副无线耳机，以备不时之需。虽然早期的一些型号体积庞大且不便，但如今的耳机已经变得轻薄、舒适，而且价格通常也很亲民。

The WIRED Reviews team doesn't just use earbuds—we test them. Hundreds of them, actually. And after weeks of music, calls, commutes, and rogue earbuds flying out of our ears, we've chosen these favorites that are the best for most people.
WIRED 评测团队不仅是耳机的使用者，更是测试者。事实上，我们测试过数百款耳机。经过数周的音乐播放、通话、通勤测试，以及应对耳机意外掉落的考验，我们挑选出了这些最适合大多数人的心仪之选。

***

### The Best Earbuds for Most People: Google Pixel Buds 2a
### 最适合大多数人的耳机：Google Pixel Buds 2a

The Pixel Buds 2a aren't the flashiest earbuds around, but they hit just the right balance of sound quality, useful features, comfortable fit, and low price, making them a great option for just about anyone.
Pixel Buds 2a 并不是市面上最花哨的耳机，但它们在音质、实用功能、佩戴舒适度和低廉价格之间取得了完美的平衡，这使它们成为几乎任何人的绝佳选择。

The smooth, pebblelike case has a replaceable battery, and the buds are very small and ergonomic, with ear loops that really help them stay in most ears. An IP54 rating, noise canceling, and seven hours of battery life make these fantastic for everyday wear. Reliable software support and excellent touch controls (though no volume controls directly on the earbuds) make these especially nice while working out, at the desk, or gardening. And instant pairing with Android phones and near-instant pairing with iPhones means both teams can enjoy these buds easily.
其光滑的鹅卵石状充电盒配有可更换电池，耳机本身非常小巧且符合人体工程学，耳挂设计能有效帮助它们稳固在大多数人的耳朵里。IP54 级防尘防水、主动降噪以及七小时的续航能力，使其成为日常佩戴的理想之选。可靠的软件支持和出色的触控操作（尽管耳机上没有直接的音量控制）让它们在健身、办公桌前工作或园艺时表现尤为出色。此外，与 Android 手机的即时配对以及与 iPhone 近乎即时的配对，意味着无论是 Android 还是 iOS 用户都能轻松使用。

***

### Best Earbuds for iPhone: Apple AirPods Pro 3
### 最适合 iPhone 的耳机：Apple AirPods Pro 3

Apple’s AirPods Pro 3 solidify their status as the best-selling headphones for good reason. They remain the easiest, most integrated buds for iOS users, now with crucial upgrades. The design is subtly refined for a more universal fit, aided by new foam-lined ear tips that improve comfort and passive noise isolation. Key improvements include a much-needed battery life boost to eight hours and an enhanced IP57 rating for greater durability.
Apple 的 AirPods Pro 3 巩固了其作为最畅销耳机的地位，这并非没有道理。它们依然是 iOS 用户最易用、集成度最高的耳机，且现在进行了关键升级。其设计经过细微改进以适应更广泛的耳型，配合新的泡沫衬里耳塞，提升了舒适度和被动降噪效果。主要改进包括续航提升至八小时，以及增强至 IP57 级的耐用性。

New features like real-time translation and surprisingly accurate heart rate monitoring, powered by robust software support, are very nice to have. The bass-heavy sound profile favors pop music, but the active noise canceling is class-leading, effectively silencing even high-pitched distractions.
得益于强大的软件支持，实时翻译和令人惊讶的精准心率监测等新功能非常实用。偏重低音的调音风格更适合流行音乐，而其主动降噪功能处于行业领先水平，能有效屏蔽包括高频噪音在内的干扰。

***

### Best Earbuds for Samsung Phones: Samsung Galaxy Buds4 Pro
### 最适合三星手机的耳机：Samsung Galaxy Buds4 Pro

The Samsung Galaxy Buds4 Pro are the best earbuds for anyone who already owns and uses a Samsung phone or Samsung smartwatch, similar to AirPods for Apple users. That comparison isn't just surface level: These are the most AirPods-like Samsung earbuds yet; the size, shape, fit, and feel are very AirPods Pro 3.
对于已经拥有并使用三星手机或三星智能手表的用户来说，Samsung Galaxy Buds4 Pro 是最佳选择，这类似于 Apple 用户选择 AirPods。这种对比不仅仅停留在表面：这是三星迄今为止最像 AirPods 的耳机；其尺寸、形状、贴合度和手感都非常接近 AirPods Pro 3。

These are fun, comfortable, and convenient earbuds for anyone with a Samsung phone. The noise-canceling works admirably, canceling out most low drones like plane and AC noise and lowering café and office chatter well. The Ambient Sound (aka transparency mode) is a real standout—it's clear and natural-sounding, so much so that we used it in place of open earbuds for walks and hikes on occasion.
对于任何拥有三星手机的人来说，这是一款有趣、舒适且便捷的耳机。其降噪效果令人赞叹，能消除大部分低频嗡嗡声（如飞机和空调噪音），并能很好地降低咖啡馆和办公室的嘈杂声。环境音模式（即通透模式）是一大亮点——声音清晰自然，以至于我们有时在散步和徒步时会用它来代替开放式耳机。
