# The Best Portable Solar Panels: My Take After Years of Testing
# 最佳便携式太阳能电池板：多年测试后的心得

A permanent solar panel installation can be expensive and tricky to navigate, and for some homes and renters it’s not a viable option. But the best portable solar panels can put the sun’s rays to use in any situation. Whether you want some backup power at home or want to collect some off-grid juice for a cabin, RV, or campsite, portable solar panels are a good way to get the job done.

固定式太阳能电池板安装费用高昂且操作复杂，对于某些家庭和租房者来说并非可行方案。但最好的便携式太阳能电池板可以在任何情况下利用太阳能。无论你是想在家中备用电源，还是想为小屋、房车或露营地收集离网电力，便携式太阳能电池板都是实现这一目标的理想选择。

I’ve been testing portable solar panels for a few years now, and these are the ones I recommend for different scenarios. They are best paired with one of the best solar generators or portable power stations. I’ll also offer some solar panel tips in an FAQ.

我已经测试便携式太阳能电池板好几年了，以下是我针对不同场景推荐的产品。它们最好与市面上顶级的太阳能发电机或便携式储能电源搭配使用。我也会在常见问题解答（FAQ）部分提供一些关于太阳能电池板的使用建议。

### The Absolute Best Portable Solar Panel
### 绝对最佳的便携式太阳能电池板

There are loads of great portable solar panels on the market now, and much depends on what you need. You’ll want to consider the maximum wattage they can deliver, connection types, portability, price, and more. I go into more depth in the FAQ below, but another important consideration is brand. If you already have a solar generator, it is often a good idea to pair solar panels from the same brand, especially if it has a proprietary solar charging port.

目前市面上有许多优秀的便携式太阳能电池板，具体选择很大程度上取决于你的需求。你需要考虑它们能提供的最大瓦数、连接类型、便携性、价格等因素。我将在下方的 FAQ 中深入探讨，但另一个重要的考量因素是品牌。如果你已经拥有太阳能发电机，通常建议搭配同一品牌的太阳能电池板，特别是当它配备了专有的太阳能充电接口时。

#### Jackery Solar Saga Portable Solar Panels
#### Jackery Solar Saga 便携式太阳能电池板

The models from Jackery were consistently the most effective solar panels I tested. Both the 100-watt Solar Saga 100 and the 200-watt Solar Saga 200 generated a relatively good amount of power in full sun and in overcast conditions. Neither folds down very small, but they can easily be carried one-handed thanks to strong molded-plastic handles at either end in Jackery’s signature bright orange. Both fold neatly away very easily with the help of magnetic pads, and the legs attach via Velcro, so they’re very quick to put up or away compared to panels that use metal fasteners.

Jackery 的型号是我测试过表现最稳定的太阳能电池板。无论是 100 瓦的 Solar Saga 100 还是 200 瓦的 Solar Saga 200，在阳光充足和阴天条件下都能产生相当可观的电力。虽然它们折叠后的体积不算很小，但得益于两端标志性的亮橙色坚固模塑塑料手柄，单手即可轻松携带。两者都借助磁吸垫可以非常轻松地折叠收纳，支架通过魔术贴固定，因此与使用金属扣件的电池板相比，它们的搭建和收纳速度非常快。

Sun permitting, the SolarSaga 200 consistently delivered around 140 watts, sometimes reaching up to 170 watts. I fully charged the 4,085-watt-hour-capacity Explorer 2000 Plus Portable Power Station from around 20 percent in two scorching days with my SolarSaga 200 panel.

在阳光允许的情况下，SolarSaga 200 持续输出功率约为 140 瓦，有时甚至能达到 170 瓦。我曾用 SolarSaga 200 电池板在两个炎热的日子里，将容量为 4,085 瓦时的 Explorer 2000 Plus 便携式储能电源从 20% 的电量充满。

It’s a shame Jackery insists on the proprietary DC8020 barrel connector designed for use with its own power stations; most panels come with MC4 connectors and an XT60 adapter (the most common solar charging port on solar generators). But Jackery solar generators often have two of these barrel ports, enabling you to plug in two panels. It’s vital to angle the panels correctly, but the fold-out legs make them easy to prop up.

遗憾的是，Jackery 坚持使用专为自家储能电源设计的 DC8020 圆口连接器；而大多数电池板都配备 MC4 连接器和 XT60 适配器（太阳能发电机上最常见的充电接口）。不过，Jackery 的太阳能发电机通常配有两个这种圆口，允许你同时接入两块电池板。正确调整电池板的角度至关重要，而折叠式支架使它们很容易支撑起来。

### Two Other Favorites
### 另外两个推荐产品

#### Bluetti Portable Solar Panels
#### Bluetti 便携式太阳能电池板

If you need more power in a single panel, I like both the Bluetti 350-W panels and the Bluetti 500-W panels, though they are expensive at the RRP. Make sure to look for discounts, as they commonly go on sale. The 350-W panel uses a familiar folding design with fold-out feet and a sliding strap with metal fasteners to prop it at various angles. It’s heavy at just over 30 pounds, but the molded handles make it easy to carry. I got a maximum of around 290 watts out of this, and the conversion efficiency is 23.4 percent.

如果你需要单块电池板提供更大的功率，我推荐 Bluetti 350W 和 500W 的型号，尽管它们的建议零售价较高。记得留意折扣，因为它们经常促销。350W 电池板采用了常见的折叠设计，配有折叠支脚和带金属扣件的滑动带，可以支撑不同的角度。它重量超过 30 磅（约 13.6 公斤），但模塑手柄使其易于携带。我测试出的最大功率约为 290 瓦，转换效率为 23.4%。

#### BougeRV Arch Flexible Solar Panel
#### BougeRV Arch 柔性太阳能电池板

Lightweight and flexible, this panel is ideal for mounting on top of your RV, van, or other vehicle. You could attach it to a cabin roof or a hardtop tent too, and it can bend up to 270 degrees. Though no fixings are included, it's possible using adhesive or strong double-sided tape, and it also has holes for bolting. It’s thin, weighs just shy of 9 pounds, and boasts an IP68 rating. Conversion efficiency is 25 percent, and I was able to get 165 watts out of it when the sun was at its highest.

这款电池板轻便且灵活，非常适合安装在房车、货车或其他车辆顶部。你也可以将其安装在小屋屋顶或硬顶帐篷上，它最多可弯曲 270 度。虽然不包含固定件，但可以使用粘合剂或强力双面胶固定，它也预留了螺栓孔。它很薄，重量不到 9 磅（约 4 公斤），并具备 IP68 防护等级。转换效率为 25%，在阳光最充足时，我能从中获得 165 瓦的功率。
