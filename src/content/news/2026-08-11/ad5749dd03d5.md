# InsertFuse: A Unified Framework for Multi-Category Reference-Guided Image Insertion
# InsertFuse：用于多类别参考引导图像插入的统一框架

We present InsertFuse, a unified framework for multi-category reference-guided image insertion. Its key idea is to decouple category-specific expertise learning from cross-category capability consolidation. InsertFuse first trains specialized experts for different insertion categories and then introduces Insertion On-Policy Distillation (IOPD) to consolidate their capabilities into a single student. By querying the matched expert at states visited by the student, IOPD preserves category-specific insertion behavior while mitigating the cross-category interference caused by direct joint training.

我们提出了 InsertFuse，这是一个用于多类别参考引导图像插入的统一框架。其核心思想是将特定类别的专业知识学习与跨类别能力整合分离开来。InsertFuse 首先针对不同的插入类别训练专门的专家模型，然后引入插入策略在线蒸馏（Insertion On-Policy Distillation, IOPD）技术，将这些能力整合到一个单一的学生模型中。通过在学生模型访问的状态下查询匹配的专家模型，IOPD 在保留特定类别插入行为的同时，减轻了直接联合训练所导致的跨类别干扰。

To improve spatial control, we propose Token-Aligned Geometry Conditioning (TAGC), which maps mask-derived geometric cues to the visual token grid, and Region-Balanced Flow Matching, which separately normalizes prediction errors inside and outside the insertion region to prevent background-dominated and scale-dependent supervision. We further introduce Reference CFG to isolate and strengthen the guidance induced by the visual reference under fixed scene and geometry conditions, with IOPD transferring this enhanced supervision into the unified student.

为了提高空间控制能力，我们提出了标记对齐几何调节（Token-Aligned Geometry Conditioning, TAGC），将掩码导出的几何线索映射到视觉标记网格上；同时提出了区域平衡流匹配（Region-Balanced Flow Matching），通过分别归一化插入区域内外的预测误差，防止出现背景主导和尺度依赖的监督偏差。此外，我们引入了参考无分类器引导（Reference CFG），旨在固定场景和几何条件下，隔离并增强视觉参考所带来的引导作用，并通过 IOPD 将这种增强后的监督信息传递给统一的学生模型。

Extensive experiments on the public AnyInsertion benchmark and our multi-category test set demonstrate state-of-the-art performance on most metrics, showing strong reference fidelity and generation quality across diverse insertion categories.

在公开的 AnyInsertion 基准测试和我们构建的多类别测试集上的大量实验表明，该方法在大多数指标上达到了最先进（SOTA）的性能，在各种插入类别中展现出强大的参考保真度和生成质量。
