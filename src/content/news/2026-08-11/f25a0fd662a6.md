# Adults don't want parental control apps. They want a wall they choose themselves
# 成年人不需要家长控制软件，他们需要的是自己亲手筑起的“墙”

I keep seeing the same mismatch in the screen-time category: a lot of apps are technically blockers, but they feel like parental control software. That is fine if a parent is the customer. It is a bad fit if the user is an adult trying to manage their own habits.
在屏幕使用时间这一领域，我不断看到同样的错位现象：许多应用在技术上确实是拦截工具，但给人的感觉却像是家长控制软件。如果客户是家长，这没问题；但如果用户是试图管理自己习惯的成年人，这种设计就非常不合适。

The difference is not cosmetic. When a blocker feels like surveillance, adults bounce. They do not want an account, a dashboard, or the feeling that their phone behavior is being watched somewhere else.
这种区别不仅仅是表面上的。当一个拦截工具让人感觉像是在被监视时，成年用户就会流失。他们不需要账号、不需要仪表盘，也不希望产生自己的手机行为正被远程监控的感觉。

That is the gap I built SproutGuard for: built for adults blocking themselves, not kids; runs on-device through Apple's Screen Time APIs; no account; no server; no usage data leaving the phone. App Store: [https://apps.apple.com/us/app/sproutguard-screen-time-detox/id6768664921?ct=devto-adults](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/sproutguard-screen-time-detox/id6768664921?ct=devto-adults)
这就是我开发 SproutGuard 的初衷：它是为成年人自我约束而设计的，而非针对儿童；它通过苹果的屏幕使用时间 API 在设备本地运行；无需账号、无需服务器、没有任何使用数据会离开手机。App Store 下载地址：[https://apps.apple.com/us/app/sproutguard-screen-time-detox/id6768664921?ct=devto-adults](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/sproutguard-screen-time-detox/id6768664921?ct=devto-adults)

I also put the positioning plainly on the product page: self-control, not parental control.
我还在产品页面上明确了定位：这是自我控制，而非家长控制。

The hard lesson from launching it is that being right about the problem is not the same thing as being shareable. Privacy architecture matters, but users rarely tell friends about architecture. What they do share is something emotional or visible: a streak, a mascot, a challenge, a before/after feeling.
产品发布后我学到的惨痛教训是：找准问题并不等同于产品具有传播力。隐私架构固然重要，但用户很少会向朋友谈论架构。他们真正愿意分享的是那些感性或直观的东西：比如连续打卡记录、吉祥物、挑战赛，或是使用前后的心理感受。
