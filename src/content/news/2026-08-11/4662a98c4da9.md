# Meta’s new Glimmer AI model offers a hint at Zuckerberg’s personal intelligence vision
# Meta 新推出的 Glimmer AI 模型揭示了扎克伯格对“个人智能”的愿景

Meta on Monday released Muse Glimmer, an open-weight model designed to power AI agents locally on consumer hardware, providing the clearest picture yet of what CEO Mark Zuckerberg’s vision of “personal superintelligence” could look like in practice.
Meta 周一发布了 Muse Glimmer，这是一个旨在为消费级硬件上的 AI 智能体提供本地驱动力的开源权重模型。这为 CEO 马克·扎克伯格（Mark Zuckerberg）关于“个人超级智能”的愿景在实践中可能呈现的样子，提供了迄今为止最清晰的蓝图。

The 30-billion parameter model is essentially an open version of Meta’s most powerful closed model, Muse Spark, which the company debuted in April. Glimmer’s weights are available under the permissive Apache 2.0 license, so developers can download them and modify as necessary.
该模型拥有 300 亿参数，本质上是 Meta 在 4 月份推出的最强大闭源模型 Muse Spark 的开源版本。Glimmer 的权重基于宽松的 Apache 2.0 许可证发布，因此开发者可以下载并根据需要进行修改。

Glimmer is designed to run AI agents that can perform multi-step tasks — like call tools, write and debug code, work with files and screenshots, and execute on a task over an extended workflow — locally on a Mac or PC with a single consumer GPU. It supports text and images, and was trained across more than 100 languages, the company said.
Glimmer 旨在运行能够执行多步骤任务的 AI 智能体，例如调用工具、编写和调试代码、处理文件和截图，以及在扩展的工作流中执行任务，且这一切均可在配备单块消费级 GPU 的 Mac 或 PC 上本地完成。Meta 表示，该模型支持文本和图像，并经过了 100 多种语言的训练。

Meta imagines Glimmer being used for things like managing schedules, drafting messages, and organizing files — tasks that would require large amounts of access to personal data. By processing the information on a user’s device instead of sending it to the cloud, Meta is laying the groundwork for a more privacy-sensitive personal agent.
Meta 设想 Glimmer 可用于管理日程、起草信息和整理文件等任务，这些任务通常需要大量访问个人数据。通过在用户设备上处理信息而非将其发送到云端，Meta 正在为打造更注重隐私的个人智能体奠定基础。

Glimmer is also designed to be “always-on” and able to operate “anywhere, anytime, with or without an internet connection.” That vision mirrors the future Mark Zuckerberg has previously laid out for Meta.
Glimmer 还被设计为“始终在线”，并能够“随时随地，无论是否有互联网连接”地运行。这一愿景反映了马克·扎克伯格此前为 Meta 规划的未来。

Last year, he argued that advanced AI should empower individuals rather than stay concentrated in the hands of a few companies, while also warning that Meta would have to be careful about which of its increasingly powerful models it released openly due to safety concerns.
去年，他曾主张先进的 AI 应该赋能个人，而不是集中在少数几家公司手中；同时他也警告称，出于安全考虑，Meta 必须谨慎对待其日益强大的模型中哪些可以公开。

In a new letter Monday, Zuckerberg reiterated that vision, arguing that distributing superintelligence widely “has the potential to begin a new era of personal empowerment where individuals can use this powerful new capability to reach their full potential, pursue their interests, and improve their lives and the world more than ever before.”
在周一的一封新信件中，扎克伯格重申了这一愿景，认为广泛分发超级智能“有可能开启一个个人赋能的新时代，个人可以利用这种强大的新能力充分发挥潜力、追求兴趣，并以前所未有的方式改善自己的生活和世界。”

Zuckerberg went on to list the ways Meta’s superintelligence can improve a person’s life, from providing a capable personal agent that “will work 24/7 on your behalf to improve your relationships, health, career, finances, home management, hobbies, and more” to giving people access to tools needed to create a new business or advance scientific progress. The biggest promise of all is that “everyone will have free or affordable access to these tools.”
扎克伯格随后列举了 Meta 的超级智能改善个人生活的方式，从提供一个能够“全天候为您工作，改善您的人际关系、健康、职业、财务、家庭管理、爱好等”的强大个人智能体，到让人们获得创建新业务或推动科学进步所需的工具。最核心的承诺是，“每个人都将能够免费或以负担得起的价格使用这些工具。”

But access isn’t the same as ownership. Zuckerberg’s promise to distribute superintelligence widely comes as Meta is increasingly distinguishing between models it will release openly and those it will keep under its control. Muse Spark, its more powerful mode, remains closed-weight, while the smaller Glimmer can be downloaded, fine-tuned, and run on a user’s hardware.
但“使用权”并不等同于“所有权”。扎克伯格关于广泛分发超级智能的承诺，正值 Meta 越来越明确地区分其将公开发布的模型与将保留在自己控制下的模型之际。其更强大的模型 Muse Spark 仍保持闭源权重，而较小的 Glimmer 则可以下载、微调并在用户的硬件上运行。

As a result, Glimmer offers an early indication of where Meta may draw the line between the AI it wants people to own themselves and the more powerful intelligence that remains under the company’s control.
因此，Glimmer 提供了一个早期的信号，表明 Meta 可能在“希望人们自己拥有的 AI”与“保留在公司控制下的更强大智能”之间划定界限。
