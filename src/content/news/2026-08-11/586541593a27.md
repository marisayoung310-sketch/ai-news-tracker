# Latent Fact-Checking: Detecting Misinformation through Activation Engineering
# 潜在事实核查：通过激活工程检测错误信息

**Abstract:** The proliferation of misinformation online has driven demand for scalable detection systems. While most existing approaches rely on surface-level linguistic features or external knowledge retrieval, we examine truthfulness as a geometric property of a language model's representation space. We introduce a misinformation detection framework grounded in activation engineering, which leverages the latent geometry of transformer models.

**摘要：** 网络错误信息的激增推动了对可扩展检测系统的需求。虽然大多数现有方法依赖于表层语言特征或外部知识检索，但我们将真实性视为语言模型表示空间的一种几何属性。我们引入了一种基于激活工程的错误信息检测框架，该框架利用了 Transformer 模型的潜在几何结构。

Our approach elicits a misinformation direction in the residual stream by contrasting activations from paired truthful and false statements, following the difference-in-means principle of Contrastive Activation Addition (CAA). At inference time, the last-token activation of an unseen claim is projected onto this direction, and the projected representation is fed to an Multilayer Perceptron (MLP) for classification. The procedure requires no fine-tuning of the backbone model, no external evidence retrieval, and no task-specific supervision beyond the contrastive pairs used to estimate the direction.

我们的方法遵循对比激活添加（CAA）的均值差原则，通过对比成对的真实陈述和虚假陈述的激活值，在残差流中引出一条“错误信息方向”。在推理时，将未见声明的最后一个 Token 的激活值投影到该方向上，并将投影后的表示输入到多层感知机（MLP）中进行分类。该过程无需对骨干模型进行微调，无需外部证据检索，除了用于估计方向的对比对之外，也不需要任何特定任务的监督。

We evaluate the method across 11 models from the Gemma, Llama, and Qwen families, ranging from 270M to 12B parameters, on three fact-checking benchmarks: AVeriTeC, LIAR, and FACTors. The falsehood direction is recoverable across model scales and architectural families, and last-token projection matches or surpasses zero-shot and few-shot prompting baselines on LIAR and FACTors, with the largest gains observed for smaller models. Performance on AVeriTeC is more limited, which we attribute to its evidence-grounded labeling scheme.

我们在 Gemma、Llama 和 Qwen 系列的 11 个模型上评估了该方法，参数规模从 2.7 亿到 120 亿不等，测试基准包括 AVeriTeC、LIAR 和 FACTors。研究发现，虚假方向在不同模型规模和架构系列中均可恢复；在 LIAR 和 FACTors 基准上，最后一个 Token 的投影方法匹配或超过了零样本（zero-shot）和少样本（few-shot）提示基准，且在较小模型上观察到的增益最为显著。在 AVeriTeC 上的表现较为有限，我们将其归因于该基准基于证据的标注方案。

These findings provide evidence that truthfulness is a structured, linearly separable concept in the latent space of pretrained language models, and point toward interpretability-driven misinformation detection as a practical complement to retrieval-based pipelines.

这些发现证明了真实性在预训练语言模型的潜在空间中是一个结构化的、线性可分的概念，并指出基于可解释性的错误信息检测可以作为基于检索的流程的一种实用补充。
