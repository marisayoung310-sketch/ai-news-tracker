# Review: Spider-Man: Brand New Day reminds us that superhero movies can be good
# 影评：《蜘蛛侠：全新的一天》提醒我们，超级英雄电影依然可以很精彩

Over the past few years, it has become a rather rare treat when I end up genuinely, unequivocally enjoying the latest superhero film. Mostly I find them to be middling entertainment, sometimes a bit dull—and for The Eternals, nearly unwatchable—with the notable exception of 2024’s Deadpool & Wolverine and last year’s Thunderbolts*. Add Spider-Man: Brand New Day to that list of exceptions.

在过去的几年里，能让我发自内心地、毫无保留地享受一部最新的超级英雄电影，已经成了一种难得的享受。大多数时候，我觉得它们只是平庸的娱乐，有时甚至有些乏味——对于《永恒族》来说，简直到了无法观看的地步——当然，2024年的《死侍与金刚狼》和去年的《雷霆特工队*》是值得注意的例外。现在，请将《蜘蛛侠：全新的一天》也加入到这个例外名单中。

Tom Holland’s latest outing as the titular web-slinger reminds us that superhero movies can still be very, very good—as much about well-drawn characters with interesting developmental arcs and well-timed emotional beats as CGI-filled action extravaganzas. There’s plenty of the latter in Brand New Day, of course, but it’s the grounded humanity at its heart that makes director Destin Daniel Cretton’s film work so well. (Spoilers below but no major reveals until after the gallery. We’ll give you a heads up when we get there)

汤姆·赫兰德（Tom Holland）作为这位标志性“蛛丝发射者”的最新演出提醒我们，超级英雄电影依然可以非常、非常出色——它们不仅是充满CGI特效的动作大片，更在于塑造了性格鲜明、拥有引人入胜成长弧线的人物，以及恰到好处的情感节奏。《全新的一天》中当然不乏动作场面，但正是其核心所蕴含的扎实人性，使得导演德斯汀·丹尼尔·克雷顿（Destin Daniel Cretton）的这部作品如此成功。（下文包含剧透，但在图集之后才会有重大揭秘。届时我们会提醒你。）

Spider-Man: No Way Home ended on a pretty bleak note, with Peter Parker (Holland) asking Doctor Strange (Benedict Cumberbatch) to erase him from everyone’s memory to protect the multiverse and everyone in it, including his girlfriend MJ (Zendaya) and his BFF Ned (Jacob Batalon). Brand New Day finds an isolated Peter focusing on work to the exclusion of all else, while secretly following MJ and Ned on social media as they make their way through four years of MIT.

《蜘蛛侠：英雄无归》的结局相当凄凉，彼得·帕克（赫兰德饰）请求奇异博士（本尼迪克特·康伯巴奇饰）抹除所有人关于他的记忆，以保护多元宇宙及其中的每一个人，包括他的女友MJ（赞达亚饰）和挚友内德（雅各布·巴特朗饰）。在《全新的一天》中，孤身一人的彼得将全部精力投入到工作中，同时在社交媒体上默默关注着MJ和内德在麻省理工学院度过的四年时光。

He’s living rough in a NYC tenement, monitoring the city, and keeping his suit and web slingers in good repair with the help of a homemade “fabricator” (described as part sewing machine, part 3D printer). His only friend is Det. Jean DeWolff (Liza Colon-Zayas), and even she is getting worried about Spider-Man working too hard. Even worse, when Peter does work up the nerve to attend a party incognito, he sees MJ kissing her new beau.

他住在纽约的一间破旧公寓里，监视着这座城市，并借助一台自制的“制造机”（描述为缝纫机与3D打印机的结合体）来维护他的战衣和蛛丝发射器。他唯一的朋友是吉恩·德沃尔夫警探（丽莎·科隆-扎亚斯饰），连她也开始担心蜘蛛侠工作过度了。更糟糕的是，当彼得鼓起勇气匿名参加一场派对时，他却看到MJ正在亲吻她的新男友。

The stress of it all triggers a disturbing mutation in his Spidey-self: His senses are ultra-heightened, he gets much more aggressive (and his eyes go black) when battling foes, and he develops organic web spinners (similar to the Tobey Maguire Spidey incarnation). Even worse, he’s facing an entirely new threat: an invisible psionic villain who can hijack people’s minds and bodies and is targeting the Department of Damage Control (DODC). Peter seems to be the only one who is immune, which is why DODC chief Bill Metzger (Tramell Tillman) seeks his help in capturing the mysterious being.

这一切压力引发了他蜘蛛体质的一种令人不安的变异：他的感官变得极度敏锐，在与敌人战斗时变得更加好斗（眼睛会变黑），并且长出了有机蛛丝发射器（类似于托比·马奎尔版的蜘蛛侠）。更糟糕的是，他正面临一个全新的威胁：一个隐形的灵能反派，能够劫持人们的思想和身体，并且正在针对损害控制部（DODC）。彼得似乎是唯一免疫的人，这就是为什么DODC主管比尔·梅茨格（特拉梅尔·蒂尔曼饰）寻求他的帮助来抓捕这个神秘生物。

Cretton devised a clever non-CGI means to visualize the mysterious villain’s body-hopping: He brought in a dance choreographer to map out how human bodies would respond biomechanically and coordinate the movements of actors playing affected passersby accordingly. The identity of the foreboding cloaked figure is the film’s Big Reveal: it’s none other than a young Jean Grey (Sadie Sink). Peter does eventually defeat her and turn her over to the DODC, only to learn that Jean is looking for her sister, Sara, who was kidnapped by the DODC for experimentation.

克雷顿设计了一种巧妙的非CGI手段来呈现反派的“附身”能力：他请来了一位舞蹈编导，规划人体在生物力学上会如何反应，并据此协调扮演受影响路人的演员们的动作。那个令人不安的披风身影的身份是电影的重大揭秘：她正是年轻的琴·葛蕾（萨迪·辛克饰）。彼得最终击败了她并将其交给DODC，却得知琴是在寻找她的妹妹萨拉，而萨拉被DODC绑架去进行实验了。

It’s a neat twist that meshes nicely with Peter’s overall emotional arc. Brand New Day shows Peter leaving his more naive teen years behind and truly coming of age. That includes Peter’s realization that perhaps the DODC aren’t the uncomplicated “good guys” he thought they were, just like Jean is as much victim as villain. Peter must come to terms with his physical changes as well. His first response is to squelch his mutation, much like Bruce Banner keeps the Hulk in check with a handy inhibitor. But Banner asks a pointed question: “How would you decide what parts of nature are good or bad?” Ultimately, Peter must learn to embrace those changes to become a fully mature Spider-Man.

这是一个巧妙的转折，与彼得整体的情感弧线完美契合。《全新的一天》展示了彼得告别了天真的少年时代，真正走向成熟。这包括彼得意识到DODC或许并非他所认为的那种单纯的“好人”，正如琴既是受害者也是反派一样。彼得也必须接受自己身体上的变化。他的第一反应是压制这种变异，就像布鲁斯·班纳用抑制器控制浩克一样。但班纳提出了一个尖锐的问题：“你如何决定自然的哪些部分是好的，哪些是坏的？”最终，彼得必须学会拥抱这些变化，成为一个完全成熟的蜘蛛侠。

Cretton also doesn’t take the easy way out when it comes to Peter’s relationship with MJ. When she accidentally finds the letter he wrote and never delivered at the end of No Way Home, she confronts Peter about the revelation that they were once a couple. He reaffirms his love, only for MJ to gently respond that she doesn’t love him in return. How could she? This MJ has no memory of him, or their time together. Holland’s initially hopeful, then heartbroken expression as he absorbs this painful truth is why Holland will have the role of Spider-Man for pretty much as long as he wants it.

在处理彼得与MJ的关系时，克雷顿也没有选择简单的处理方式。当MJ无意中发现了彼得在《英雄无归》结尾写下却从未寄出的信时，她质问彼得关于他们曾经是情侣的真相。他重申了自己的爱，但MJ温柔地回应说她并不爱他。她怎么可能爱他呢？这个MJ对他以及他们在一起的时光毫无记忆。赫兰德在吸收这一痛苦事实时，从最初的充满希望到心碎的表情，这就是为什么只要他愿意，他就能一直担任蜘蛛侠这个角色。

Alas, the film is inconsistent about how the memory wipe spell works, since Ned, at the very end, suddenly remembers Peter after they both perform their special handshake. If Peter needs to rebuild what he lost with MJ with new experiences to create new memories, why doesn’t he have to do the same with his friendship with Ned?

遗憾的是，电影对于记忆抹除咒语如何运作的设定并不一致，因为在影片最后，内德在两人完成他们特殊的击掌动作后，突然记起了彼得。如果彼得需要通过新的经历来重建他与MJ失去的一切以创造新的记忆，为什么他不需要对内德的友谊也这样做呢？
