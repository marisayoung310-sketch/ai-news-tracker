# Building Document Structure with Loop Engineering: Recovering a PDF’s Outline from Body Typography for RAG
# 利用循环工程构建文档结构：为 RAG 从正文排版中恢复 PDF 大纲

Large Language Model Building Document Structure with Loop Engineering: Recovering a PDF’s Outline from Body Typography for RAG Enterprise Document Intelligence [Vol.1 #5octies] – Rules propose, LLM validates: six deterministic signals on span-level typography surface heading candidates, one bounded loop keeps the real ones, and the same toc_df drops back into the RAG pipeline.
大型语言模型构建文档结构与循环工程：为 RAG 从正文排版中恢复 PDF 大纲。企业文档智能 [第1卷 #5octies] —— 规则提出，LLM 验证：基于跨度级（span-level）排版的六个确定性信号浮现出标题候选，一个有界循环保留真实标题，最终生成的 toc_df（目录数据框）被送回 RAG 流水线。

A structured RAG pipeline needs the document’s table of contents: retrieval scopes by section, and the chunker cuts on heading boundaries instead of mid-sentence. But some documents don’t have one. A paper exported straight from LaTeX has no native outline and no printed contents page, so there is no toc_df for the pipeline to read. The headings are still right there on the body, section by section, bigger and bolder than the text around them.
一个结构化的 RAG 流水线需要文档的目录：按章节划分检索范围，并让分块器（chunker）在标题边界处进行切割，而不是在句子中间。但有些文档没有目录。直接从 LaTeX 导出的论文没有原生大纲，也没有打印的目录页，因此流水线无法读取到 toc_df。然而，标题依然存在于正文中，逐节排列，比周围的文本更大、更粗。

Article 5septies (TOC reconstruction from a table of content) handled the document that at least prints a contents page; this one prints none. So the pipeline rebuilds the toc_df from the one signal left: the way the headings look. This article is a document parsing companion in Enterprise Document Intelligence, the series that builds an enterprise RAG system from four bricks.
第 5septies 篇文章（从目录页重建 TOC）处理了至少打印了目录页的文档；而本文处理的是完全没有目录页的情况。因此，流水线通过仅存的一个信号重建 toc_df：标题的外观。本文是“企业文档智能”系列的文档解析指南，该系列旨在通过四个模块构建企业级 RAG 系统。

It sits in brick 1 (document parsing) and closes the TOC-reconstruction thread that Article 5 (document parsing), Article 5B (the relational data model), and Article 5septies (TOC reconstruction from a table of content) opened.
它属于模块 1（文档解析），并结束了由第 5 篇（文档解析）、第 5B 篇（关系数据模型）和第 5septies 篇（从目录页重建 TOC）开启的 TOC 重建系列讨论。

### 1. Where this article sits and what it does
### 1. 本文的定位与作用

Article 5septies (TOC reconstruction from a table of content) drew a line: TOC detection reads a page that already exists (a native outline or a printed table of content); recovering structure from body typography belonged to summarisation. That line was pragmatic. It kept the table of content cascade small and testable.
第 5septies 篇划定了一条界限：TOC 检测应读取已存在的页面（原生大纲或打印的目录）；而从正文排版中恢复结构则属于摘要范畴。这条界限是务实的，它使 TOC 级联（cascade）保持精简且易于测试。

But the summarisation intent has different failure modes (long-context, prompt-first) and different guarantees (no fixed shape, no source column). For a document that has clear typographic headings on its body, we do not need a summarisation loop. We need the same toc_df shape as everywhere else, filled from the signals the body carries. So the frontier moves: body-typography reconstruction is a fourth detection case, not a summarisation fallback.
但摘要意图存在不同的故障模式（长上下文、提示词优先）和不同的保证（无固定形状、无源列）。对于正文中有清晰排版标题的文档，我们不需要摘要循环。我们需要与其他地方相同的 toc_df 形状，并由正文携带的信号填充。因此，边界发生了移动：正文排版重建成为第四种检测情况，而不是摘要的备选方案。

In: a line_df (plus a span_df when the parser exposes typography) whose native TOC and printed table of content both came up empty. Out: a toc_df in the standard Article 5B shape, so retrieval, chunking, and summarisation keep working unchanged.
输入：一个 line_df（当解析器暴露排版信息时还包括 span_df），其原生 TOC 和打印目录均为空。输出：符合第 5B 篇标准形状的 toc_df，从而确保检索、分块和摘要功能保持不变。

Once you have that fourth case, three neighbouring situations come along for free:
一旦有了这第四种情况，三种相邻的情况便迎刃而解：

*   **The PDF has no structure at all**, the case just described.
*   **PDF 完全没有结构**，即上述情况。
*   **The PDF has a partial native outline** that stops at level 2 while the body clearly has a level 3 (3.2.1 ..., 3.2.2 ...). The body-typography pass deepens what the outline gives you.
*   **PDF 具有部分原生大纲**，仅停留在第 2 级，而正文显然有第 3 级（3.2.1 ..., 3.2.2 ...）。正文排版处理过程可以深化大纲提供的信息。
*   **The PDF is composite**: two or more documents concatenated. Section numbering re-inits mid-file, and the native outline (if any) is flat. The same pass, with a merge step, reconciles the streams.
*   **PDF 是复合文档**：由两个或多个文档拼接而成。章节编号在文件中途重置，且原生大纲（如果有）是扁平的。通过相同的处理过程加上合并步骤，可以协调这些数据流。

The rest of the article walks each in turn, in the same order the cascade tries them.
文章的其余部分将依次介绍每种情况，顺序与级联尝试的顺序一致。

### 2. Four cases, one cascade
### 2. 四种情况，一个级联

The cascade grew by one. Same order: cheapest first, drop through to the next when a case does not fire or returns too little. Case 4 is the new one; the earlier three come from Article 5septies (TOC reconstruction from a table of content) unchanged.
级联增加了一项。顺序相同：成本最低者优先，当某种情况未触发或返回结果过少时，进入下一种情况。第 4 种是新增的；前三种来自第 5septies 篇，保持不变。

Case 1, native outline, and Case 2, contents page with links. Article 5septies (TOC reconstruction from a table of content) covers both. Deterministic, cheap, exact when they work.
情况 1（原生大纲）和情况 2（带链接的目录页）。第 5septies 篇涵盖了这两者。它们是确定性的、低成本的，且在有效时非常精确。

Case 3, printed contents page without links. Same article, text patterns plus label-to-page alignment.
情况 3（无链接的打印目录页）。同样在第 5septies 篇中讨论，涉及文本模式和标签到页码的对齐。

Case 4, no contents page at all. This article. Body typography surfaces heading candidates; an LLM loop keeps the real ones.
情况 4（完全没有目录页）。即本文内容。正文排版浮现出标题候选；LLM 循环保留真实的标题。

Case 4 is opt-in in the cascade because it is genuinely the most expensive: several passes of an LLM in the worst case, one pass when the deterministic signals are clean enough. Enable it by passing `methods=("links", "contents_text", "llm", "body_structure")` to `reconstruct_toc_df`, or call `reconstruct_toc_from_body` directly when you know upfront that the file has no contents page.
情况 4 在级联中是可选的，因为它确实成本最高：最坏情况下需要多次 LLM 调用，在确定性信号足够清晰时则只需一次。通过向 `reconstruct_toc_df` 传递 `methods=("links", "contents_text", "llm", "body_structure")` 来启用它，或者当你预先知道文件没有目录页时，直接调用 `reconstruct_toc_from_body`。

### 3. How the cascade reconstructs a TOC
### 3. 级联如何重建 TOC

With the four cases mapped, here is how the cascade actually turns a document’s pages into a toc_df, one stage at a time, from the signals it reads off the page to the LLM check at the end.
在确定了四种情况后，以下是级联如何将文档页面转化为 toc_df 的过程，从读取页面信号到最后的 LLM 检查，分阶段进行。

#### 3.1 The parser matrix: what each parsing method gives us
#### 3.1 解析器矩阵：每种解析方法提供什么

Every rule the body-typography detector reads is a column of the extracted table. So the question is not “what are the six signals” first, it is “which columns does the parser give me at all”. The answer depends on how the PDF was parsed.
正文排版检测器读取的每一条规则都是提取表中的一列。因此，问题不在于“六个信号是什么”，而在于“解析器到底给了我哪些列”。答案取决于 PDF 是如何被解析的。

Two practical points that shape the code: PyMuPDF exposes typography per span, not per line. A span is one contiguous run of characters in one font, size, weight, italic and color. A line that mixes bold and non-bold text expands to multiple spans. So the module provides a helper `enrich_line_df_with_style(line_df, span_df)` that aggregates the spans of each line down to one row and appends font_size (character-weighted), bold_ratio, is_bold, is_italic, dominant_font_name.
影响代码的两个实际点：PyMuPDF 按跨度（span）而非按行暴露排版信息。跨度是指具有相同字体、大小、字重、斜体和颜色的连续字符序列。混合了粗体和非粗体文本的行会扩展为多个跨度。因此，该模块提供了一个辅助函数 `enrich_line_df_with_style(line_df, span_df)`，将每一行的跨度聚合为一行，并附加字体大小（按字符加权）、粗体比例、是否粗体、是否斜体以及主字体名称。
