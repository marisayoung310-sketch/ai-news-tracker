# Apple’s selling refurbished MacBook Neos with a $100 discount
# 苹果正在以 100 美元的折扣销售官翻版 MacBook Neo

Apple’s most affordable laptop, the MacBook Neo, is available once again at its pre-price hike price. All four colors are currently discounted and available refurbished through the company, with the base 256GB model listed for $599 (usually $699 new), and the upgraded version with 512GB of storage and TouchID selling for $679 (usually $799). The laptops have a one-year manufacturer warranty, with the option to purchase an extended AppleCare plan.

苹果最实惠的笔记本电脑 MacBook Neo 再次以涨价前的价格回归。目前，苹果官网提供四种颜色的官翻版机型，均享有折扣。其中，256GB 基础款售价为 599 美元（新品原价 699 美元），配备 512GB 存储空间和 TouchID 的升级版售价为 679 美元（新品原价 799 美元）。这些笔记本电脑均提供一年原厂保修，并可选择购买延保服务 AppleCare。

My colleague Antonio G. Di Benedetto reviewed the MacBook Neo, and was impressed with its performance given that it’s running on the A18 Pro - an iPhone 16 Pro processor. While power users might feel a bit limited by the 8GB of memory and relatively slow storage, the excellent screen, keyboard and speakers make this a great choice for people who primarily use browsers instead of system-intensive apps. It’s also a fantastic laptop for high school or college students.

我的同事 Antonio G. Di Benedetto 在评测 MacBook Neo 后，对其搭载 iPhone 16 Pro 处理器（A18 Pro）的表现印象深刻。虽然 8GB 内存和相对较慢的存储速度可能会让重度用户感到受限，但其出色的屏幕、键盘和扬声器，使其成为那些主要使用浏览器而非高负载应用程序用户的绝佳选择。对于高中生或大学生来说，这也是一台非常棒的笔记本电脑。
