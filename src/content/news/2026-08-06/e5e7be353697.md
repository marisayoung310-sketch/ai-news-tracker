# Helsinki Hacker News Meetup
# 赫尔辛基 Hacker News 聚会

Helsinki Hacker News Meetup This is a coffee-morning meetup for Hacker News posters in Helsinki. It is completely unofficial - and nothing to at all do with the Y Combinator startup accelerator.
这是为赫尔辛基的 Hacker News 发帖用户举办的晨间咖啡聚会。该活动完全是非官方的，与 Y Combinator 创业加速器没有任何关系。

Membership To keep the group focused on posters (and not just readers!) anyone can join so long as: they have a non-greenname account they have 64 karma or more Often this is waived if you're known personally to someone in the group already. To join, fill out the Google Form.
会员资格：为了确保小组专注于发帖用户（而非仅仅是读者），任何人只要满足以下条件即可加入：拥有非“绿名”（non-greenname）账号，且拥有 64 点或以上的 Karma 值。如果你已与组内成员相识，此要求通常可以豁免。如需加入，请填写 Google 表单。

Organisation, meeting time, location We use a WhatsApp group for co-ordination. The time and location of the meetup is announced there. We meet approximately every 6-8 weeks. The meetings are usually on a Sunday, 10am-midday, in a coffee shop in central Helsinki - but occasionally we meet for beers too. The organisers are Cal Paterson and Oleg Podsechin.
组织、时间与地点：我们使用 WhatsApp 群组进行协调，聚会的时间和地点会在群内公布。我们大约每 6 到 8 周聚会一次。聚会通常安排在周日上午 10 点至中午，地点位于赫尔辛基市中心的一家咖啡馆，但偶尔我们也会聚在一起喝啤酒。本次活动的组织者是 Cal Paterson 和 Oleg Podsechin。
