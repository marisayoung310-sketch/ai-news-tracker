# Sunbird relaunched its iMessage app for Android users after three years away
# Sunbird 在时隔三年后重新为 Android 用户推出 iMessage 应用

Sunbird Messaging is back on the Google Play Store, offering Android users blue bubble privileges in iMessage complete with reactions and high quality videos for $2.99 a month. Apple and Google have made cross platform messaging better in recent years with support for RCS, but Android users can still cause issues in iMessage group chats, like breaking reactions and replies.

Sunbird Messaging 已重返 Google Play 商店，为 Android 用户提供 iMessage 的“蓝色气泡”特权，包括表情回应和高质量视频发送功能，每月收费 2.99 美元。尽管近年来苹果和谷歌通过支持 RCS 改善了跨平台消息传递体验，但 Android 用户在 iMessage 群聊中仍可能引发问题，例如导致表情回应和回复功能失效。

Android Authority reports that after Sunbird shut down nearly three years ago due to security issues, the company says its new setup addresses those, and that “Any message moving through our infrastructure is protected in transit and released once it’s delivered,” while media you send or receive is deleted “typically within 48 hours and never longer than 72.”

据 Android Authority 报道，在 Sunbird 因安全问题关闭近三年后，该公司表示其新的架构已经解决了这些隐患，并称“任何通过我们基础设施传输的消息在传输过程中都会受到保护，并在送达后立即释放”，而用户发送或接收的媒体文件则会“通常在 48 小时内、最长不超过 72 小时内”被删除。

Sunbird: Your messages are encrypted on your phone before they leave it, encrypted in transit over certificate-pinned connections, and encrypted at rest on your device, using strong AES-256 encryption. On the final leg to an iPhone, your message is protected by Apple’s iMessage end-to-end encryption, which Sunbird never modifies, intercepts, or weakens.

Sunbird 表示：您的消息在离开手机前即已加密，在通过证书锁定连接传输时也经过加密，并使用强大的 AES-256 加密技术在您的设备上进行静态存储。在到达 iPhone 的最后阶段，您的消息将受到苹果 iMessage 端到端加密的保护，Sunbird 绝不会修改、拦截或削弱该加密机制。

Sunbird’s CEO Danny Mizrahi told Android Authority that “an independent security auditing firm tested its rebuilt Sunbird app,” and reported “no critical vulnerabilities.” Mizrahi also added that, “When a user connects iMessage, their Apple ID password is used once to set up the session and then destroyed. We do not store the password, and we do not keep an authentication token that would let us sign in as them later.”

Sunbird 首席执行官 Danny Mizrahi 告诉 Android Authority，“一家独立的第三方安全审计公司测试了其重建后的 Sunbird 应用”，并报告称“没有发现关键漏洞”。Mizrahi 还补充道：“当用户连接 iMessage 时，他们的 Apple ID 密码仅在建立会话时使用一次，随后即被销毁。我们不会存储密码，也不会保留任何允许我们在之后以用户身份登录的身份验证令牌。”

You can also access Google Messages and SMS from Sunbird, and support for integrating WhatsApp and Facebook Messenger is coming later this year, along with an AI assistant that can summarize chats and draft replies.

您还可以通过 Sunbird 访问 Google Messages 和短信。此外，针对 WhatsApp 和 Facebook Messenger 的集成支持将于今年晚些时候推出，同时还将引入一个能够总结聊天内容并起草回复的 AI 助手。
