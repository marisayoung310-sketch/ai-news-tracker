### Low precision, high recall
### 低精度，高召回率

The problem with the current backlash against LLM-generated content is that the automatic tools to detect AI are often low precision and high recall: classifying everything as AI written for the maximum amount of “gotchas.” To demonstrate, here’s an excerpt of my writing (pre chatbot days):

目前针对 LLM 生成内容的抵制浪潮存在一个问题：自动检测 AI 的工具往往具有低精度和高召回率的特点：为了尽可能多地“抓现行”，它们把所有东西都归类为 AI 撰写。为了证明这一点，以下是我（在聊天机器人时代之前）写的一段文字：

*Each idea will need to be prioritized on two factors as mentioned, ease of execution and likelihood of success. There is no easy way to determine ease of execution or likelihood unfortunately, as each researcher and team is different. Some considerations:*
*如前所述，每个想法都需要根据两个因素进行优先级排序：执行难度和成功可能性。遗憾的是，没有简单的方法来确定执行难度或可能性，因为每个研究人员和团队的情况都不同。一些考量因素：*

*- How complex is the method or idea?*
*- 方法或想法有多复杂？*

### Vocabulary tells
### 词汇特征

"Delve"
“Delve”（钻研/深入探讨）

It wasn’t always suspicious. It was a slightly formal word that showed up every now and again. Once ChatGPT came out, it exploded. Now, everyone’s delving into something. There are a couple interesting bodies of research behind what’s called “excess vocabulary.” Words that rarely show up in human writing but frequently in AI generated content.

它并不总是可疑的。它曾经只是一个偶尔出现的略显正式的词。ChatGPT 问世后，它就爆发了。现在，每个人都在“delving into”（钻研）某事。在所谓的“过度词汇”（excess vocabulary）背后，有几项有趣的研究。这些词在人类写作中很少出现，但在 AI 生成的内容中却频繁出现。

In “Delving into ChatGPT usage in academic writing through excess vocabulary” by Kobak et al., researchers reviewed ~15 million PubMed abstracts from 2010–2024, and demonstrate that the appearance of LLMs led to an abrupt increase in “certain style words.” Running it on scientific abstracts, the researchers found “delves” showed a noticeable increase in the two years after ChatGPT launched, with “intricate” and “meticulous” climbing right alongside it.

在 Kobak 等人撰写的《通过过度词汇深入研究 ChatGPT 在学术写作中的使用》一文中，研究人员回顾了 2010 年至 2024 年间约 1500 万篇 PubMed 摘要，并证明 LLM 的出现导致了“某些风格词汇”的突然增加。研究人员在科学摘要上运行该分析发现，在 ChatGPT 发布后的两年里，“delves”的使用量显著增加，“intricate”（错综复杂的）和“meticulous”（一丝不苟的）也随之攀升。

We didn’t just start “delving.” LLMs started “delving” for us. Researchers ran another version of the same test. In “Why Does ChatGPT ‘Delve’ So Much? Exploring the Sources of Lexical Overrepresentation in Large Language Models” by Juzek and Ward, the researchers identified “focal words” by comparing two corpora. The first was PubMed abstracts written from 2020-2024. The second was generated by having ChatGPT rewrite the same body of abstracts. The exact same words showed abrupt increases in usage in both of the corpora, suggesting that the patterns were a result of ChatGPT usage.

并不是我们开始频繁使用“delving”，而是 LLM 开始替我们“delving”。研究人员进行了另一项相同的测试。在 Juzek 和 Ward 撰写的《为什么 ChatGPT 如此频繁地“钻研”？探索大语言模型中词汇过度代表的来源》一文中，研究人员通过比较两个语料库确定了“焦点词”。第一个是 2020-2024 年间撰写的 PubMed 摘要。第二个是通过让 ChatGPT 重写同一批摘要生成的。完全相同的词在两个语料库中都出现了使用量的突然增加，这表明这些模式是使用 ChatGPT 的结果。

What this boils down to is: LLMs prefer certain words. This is most likely a result of human preferences in LLM post-training. “Ooh, delve sounds nice, I like this answer better.” These token probabilities differ from human usage probabilities in measurable ways. Seeing them could be a useful indicator of LLM use.

归根结底：LLM 偏好某些词汇。这很可能是 LLM 后训练中人类偏好的结果。“噢，delve 听起来不错，我更喜欢这个答案。”这些 Token（词元）概率与人类使用概率在可测量的方式上存在差异。观察这些差异可以作为判断 LLM 使用情况的有效指标。

**简而言之：需要留意的词汇**

In isolation, not worrisome, but in combination or frequently, definitely a warning flag.
单独出现时不必担心，但如果组合出现或频繁出现，绝对是一个警示信号。

### Name preference
### 姓名偏好

There’s also some research highlighting the preference for names, specifically related to co-occurring names. In “The Ghost Couple: Correlated LLM Name Priors and Their Haunting of the Web and Academic Publishing”, Michał Brzozowski and Neo Christopher Chung found that models develop “name priors,” repeatedly generating the same fictional identities. These fictional identities are cited as co-authors in ghost written research papers which have real DOIs, among other AI generated content, like website templates. The authors argue that these name pairs can act as signatures for the underlying model.

还有一些研究强调了模型对姓名的偏好，特别是与共同出现的姓名相关的偏好。在《幽灵夫妇：相关的 LLM 姓名先验及其对网络和学术出版的困扰》一文中，Michał Brzozowski 和 Neo Christopher Chung 发现模型会形成“姓名先验”，反复生成相同的虚构身份。这些虚构身份被引用为代笔研究论文的合著者（这些论文拥有真实的 DOI），以及其他 AI 生成的内容（如网站模板）。作者认为，这些姓名对可以作为底层模型的签名。
