# Google just announced a major shakeup of its top AI leadership
# 谷歌刚刚宣布对其顶级人工智能领导层进行重大改组

Google is making some significant AI leadership changes, including a major shift for Google DeepMind leader Demis Hassabis.
谷歌正在对其人工智能领导层进行重大调整，其中包括对 Google DeepMind 负责人德米斯·哈萨比斯（Demis Hassabis）职位的重大变动。

Hassabis will become the chair of Google DeepMind and the chief scientist at Alphabet, CEO Sundar Pichai announced on Wednesday. Hassabis will continue to lead Alphabet’s Isomorphic Labs, which aims to use AI to develop drugs. Koray Kavukcuoglu, formerly DeepMind’s CTO, is stepping up to become Google’s SVP of DeepMind and will report to Pichai. Kavukcuoglu will remain Google’s chief AI architect as well.
谷歌首席执行官桑达尔·皮查伊（Sundar Pichai）周三宣布，哈萨比斯将出任 Google DeepMind 董事长兼 Alphabet 首席科学家。哈萨比斯将继续领导 Alphabet 旗下的 Isomorphic Labs，该公司旨在利用人工智能开发药物。原 DeepMind 首席技术官科雷·卡武克奥卢（Koray Kavukcuoglu）将晋升为谷歌 DeepMind 高级副总裁，并直接向皮查伊汇报。卡武克奥卢还将继续担任谷歌首席人工智能架构师。

“​​As you’ve heard me say many times, I’ve always believed the No.1 application of AI should be to improve human health,” Hassabis says in a message to staff. “It’s time for AI to prove its unequivocal value to the world, and what better way to demonstrate that than to help finally cure diseases like cancer.” Hassabis says he will “continue to work closely with Sundar on strategic and global AGI matters” and to advise Kavukcuoglu and other DeepMind leads.
“正如你们多次听我所说的那样，我一直认为人工智能的首要应用应该是改善人类健康，”哈萨比斯在给员工的信息中表示。“现在是人工智能向世界证明其明确价值的时候了，而帮助最终治愈癌症等疾病，还有什么比这更好的证明方式呢？”哈萨比斯表示，他将“继续在战略和全球通用人工智能（AGI）事务上与桑达尔密切合作”，并为卡武克奥卢及其他 DeepMind 领导层提供建议。

In another big change, veteran Googler Jeff Dean — the chief scientist of Google DeepMind and Google’s 30th employee — is leaving with Google Fellow Sanjay Ghemawat to form an AI-focused public benefit corporation called Discovery Loop. Google will be a “founding investor” in the new company, Pichai says.
另一项重大变动是，谷歌资深员工、Google DeepMind 首席科学家兼谷歌第 30 号员工杰夫·迪恩（Jeff Dean）将与谷歌研究员桑杰·格玛瓦特（Sanjay Ghemawat）一同离职，成立一家名为 Discovery Loop 的专注于人工智能的公益公司。皮查伊表示，谷歌将成为这家新公司的“创始投资者”。

“Our mission is straightforward: we are building AI solutions that can automatically solve important problems in machine learning, science, and engineering,” Discovery Loop says on its website. “By advancing the pace at which we conduct engineering and scientific discovery, we can bring the benefits of science and technology to the world much faster. Ultimately, our goal is to build AI systems that act as a deeply positive, empowering force for humanity, delivering technology solutions that improve people’s lives on a global scale.”
“我们的使命很简单：我们正在构建能够自动解决机器学习、科学和工程领域重要问题的人工智能解决方案，”Discovery Loop 在其网站上写道。“通过加快我们进行工程和科学发现的步伐，我们可以更快地将科学技术的成果带给世界。最终，我们的目标是构建人工智能系统，使其成为人类一股深具积极意义的赋能力量，提供能够改善全球人民生活的技术解决方案。”
