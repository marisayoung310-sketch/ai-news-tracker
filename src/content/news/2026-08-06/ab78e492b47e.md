**Most Popular / 最受欢迎文章**

A startup claims it broke through a bottleneck that’s holding back LLMs. Subquadratic has now shared more details about its new model. But some are still skeptical. By Will Douglas Heaven.
一家初创公司声称突破了阻碍大语言模型（LLM）发展的瓶颈。Subquadratic 公司现已分享了其新模型的更多细节，但仍有人持怀疑态度。（作者：Will Douglas Heaven）

The “steroid olympics” were a circus—and a window into our culture. Dozens of athletes on performance-enhancing drugs competed in the Enhanced Games. Organizers hope it'll sell a lot of peptides and other supplements. By Amit Katwala.
“类固醇奥运会”是一场闹剧，也是洞察我们文化的一扇窗口。数十名使用违禁药物的运动员参加了“增强运动会”（Enhanced Games）。组织者希望借此推销大量的肽类及其他补剂。（作者：Amit Katwala）

A fundamental flaw leaves LLMs strikingly vulnerable to attack. It makes it easy to trick them into doing things they shouldn’t, such as telling you how to sabotage an aircraft’s navigation system. By Will Douglas Heaven.
一个根本性的缺陷使大语言模型极易受到攻击。这使得诱导它们执行不当操作变得轻而易举，例如教你如何破坏飞机的导航系统。（作者：Will Douglas Heaven）

Sperm donors need limits, says a European fertility group. Some donor-conceived people are finding hundreds of siblings. An international cap on donations could help prevent that. By Jessica Hamzelou.
欧洲一个生育组织表示，精子捐献需要限制。一些通过捐献受孕的人发现自己有数百名兄弟姐妹。设定国际捐献上限有助于防止这种情况发生。（作者：Jessica Hamzelou）
