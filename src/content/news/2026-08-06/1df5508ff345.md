# Two of Ring’s latest video doorbells are a lot cheaper than usual
# Ring 两款最新的可视门铃价格大幅下调

Ring’s Wired Doorbell Pro and Battery Doorbell Plus are two of the brand’s most well-rounded video doorbells, whether you’re looking for a hardwired model or one that runs on a battery. Right now, its Wired Doorbell Pro is down to $199 ($50 off) at Amazon and Best Buy, while the Battery Doorbell Plus is on sale for $119.99 ($60 off) at Amazon and Best Buy. Both deals are the second-best prices we’ve seen, just $20 shy of their Prime Day lows.

无论您是在寻找有线供电型号还是电池供电型号，Ring 的 Wired Doorbell Pro 和 Battery Doorbell Plus 都是该品牌功能最全面的两款可视门铃。目前，Wired Doorbell Pro 在亚马逊和百思买（Best Buy）的售价已降至 199 美元（优惠 50 美元），而 Battery Doorbell Plus 的售价则为 119.99 美元（优惠 60 美元）。这两项优惠均为我们所见过的第二低价，仅比 Prime Day 的历史最低价高出 20 美元。

Aside from how they’re powered, the main difference between the two comes down to video quality. The Wired Doorbell Pro records sharper 4K video, and it can automatically respond to visitors with prerecorded messages. The Battery Doorbell Plus has fewer features and can capture video in slightly less sharp 2K resolution. Beyond that, the two doorbells share many of the same core features.

除了供电方式不同外，这两款产品的主要区别在于视频质量。Wired Doorbell Pro 可以录制更清晰的 4K 视频，并能通过预录信息自动回复访客。Battery Doorbell Plus 的功能较少，视频分辨率为 2K，清晰度略逊一筹。除此之外，这两款门铃共享许多相同的核心功能。

Both can provide a head-to-toe view of your doorstep and support motion detection, two-way talk, and support for color night vision. If you’re willing to pay $4.99 for a monthly subscription, you also get more specific alerts for people, packages, and vehicles. Upgrading to the $19.99 Ring Pro plan, meanwhile, grants access to extra AI features like facial recognition and text-based activity summaries, all of which are free for the first month. Both also work well with Amazon Alexa, allowing you to view a live feed on a compatible Echo Show smart display and receive audible announcements when someone rings your doorbell.

两者都能提供从头到脚的门前视野，并支持移动侦测、双向通话和彩色夜视功能。如果您愿意每月支付 4.99 美元的订阅费，还可以获得针对人员、包裹和车辆的更精准提醒。升级到 19.99 美元的 Ring Pro 套餐，则可以使用人脸识别和基于文本的活动摘要等额外 AI 功能，且首月免费。此外，这两款门铃都能与 Amazon Alexa 良好兼容，让您可以在兼容的 Echo Show 智能显示屏上查看实时画面，并在有人按门铃时收到语音提示。
