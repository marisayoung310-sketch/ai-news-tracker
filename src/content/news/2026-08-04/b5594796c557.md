# MPP-GNN: Subject-Adaptive Community Detection for fMRI-Based Alzheimer's Disease Classification
# MPP-GNN：基于 fMRI 的阿尔茨海默病分类的主体自适应社区检测

**Abstract:** Functional magnetic resonance imaging (fMRI) is a widely used technique for studying the brain. Recent methods that utilize graph neural networks (GNNs) for analysis of brain functional connectivity have shown great potential for the classification of brain disorders, such as Alzheimer's disease (AD). 

**摘要：** 功能性磁共振成像（fMRI）是一种广泛用于脑部研究的技术。近期利用图神经网络（GNN）分析脑功能连接的方法，在阿尔茨海默病（AD）等脑部疾病的分类方面展现出巨大潜力。

However, these methods often assume a preset number of functional modules across all subjects, which overlooks inter-subject variability. In addition, the discovered modules are rarely used to directly guide the learned connectivity patterns. 

然而，这些方法通常假设所有受试者具有预设数量的功能模块，这忽略了受试者之间的个体差异。此外，所发现的模块很少被直接用于指导学习到的连接模式。

Here, to address these issues, we propose a Meta Probabilistic Pooling GNN (MPP-GNN). We frame the model's task as a coupled, bilevel optimization that performs adaptive graph partitioning hierarchically to discover subject-specific modules and then uses the discovered brain modules as an explicit prior to guide edge refinement and representation learning. 

为了解决这些问题，我们提出了一种元概率池化图神经网络（MPP-GNN）。我们将模型的任务构建为一个耦合的双层优化问题，通过分层执行自适应图划分来发现特定于受试者的模块，然后将发现的脑模块作为显式先验，用于指导边缘细化和表征学习。

We validate MPP-GNN on two public datasets for AD classification, achieving the highest AUC in comparison to established baselines for both datasets. Furthermore, our analysis demonstrates that MPP-GNN shows significant alignment with the canonical functional-network organization defined by the Yeo brain atlas and reveals a network-level dedifferentiation pattern for AD.

我们在两个用于 AD 分类的公共数据集上验证了 MPP-GNN，在两个数据集上均取得了优于现有基准模型的最高 AUC。此外，我们的分析表明，MPP-GNN 与 Yeo 脑图谱定义的典型功能网络组织高度一致，并揭示了 AD 的网络级去分化模式。
