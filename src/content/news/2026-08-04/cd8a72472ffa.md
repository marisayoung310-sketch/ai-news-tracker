# LAWFUL: Law-Aligned Witness for Faithful Use of Latents
# LAWFUL：用于潜变量忠实使用的定律对齐见证框架

When a neural network predicts a physical system accurately, has it learned the governing law as formal, structured knowledge, and if so, does the network's internal computation actually use that representation throughout the law's domain of validity? 

当神经网络准确预测一个物理系统时，它是否已经将支配该系统的定律作为正式的、结构化的知识学习到了？如果是，网络的内部计算是否在整个定律的有效域内真正使用了这种表示？

We identify four interpretability gaps that limit answering these questions for {\em physics laws over continuous variables}: the absence of a coverage-aware causal-consistency measure over continuous counterfactuals; of a domain-of-validity test for the identified circuit; of a verification of the law's invariants and forbidden behaviors; and of a quantification of how a derived physical quantity flows through the circuit. 

我们确定了四个限制回答上述问题的可解释性差距，特别是在“连续变量上的物理定律”方面：缺乏针对连续反事实的覆盖感知因果一致性度量；缺乏对已识别电路的有效域测试；缺乏对定律不变量和禁止行为的验证；以及缺乏对派生物理量如何在电路中流动的量化。

We develop a foundational framework, LAWFUL, that closes the first two and lays groundwork for the remaining two, and illustrate it on the Mocap2Radar transformer, validating whether it learns and internally uses the Doppler frequency law $f(t) = \frac{2 v(t)}{\lambda}$ from motion-capture and radar data in which neither $f(t)$ nor $v(t)$ appears.

我们开发了一个基础框架 LAWFUL，它弥补了前两个差距，并为后两个差距奠定了基础。我们通过 Mocap2Radar Transformer 模型展示了该框架，验证了它是否能从既不包含 $f(t)$ 也不包含 $v(t)$ 的动作捕捉和雷达数据中，学习并内部使用多普勒频率定律 $f(t) = \frac{2 v(t)}{\lambda}$。
