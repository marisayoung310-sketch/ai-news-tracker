# FocusGS: Spatial Delta Layers for Local Repair and Deterministic Editing of Trained 3D Gaussian Assets
# FocusGS：用于已训练 3D 高斯资产局部修复与确定性编辑的空间增量层

**Abstract:** 3D Gaussian Splatting (3DGS) is evolving from one-time reconstruction into deliverable, inspectable, and maintainable visual assets. Existing workflows focus on global reconstruction, training-time density control, or open-ended generative editing, leaving trained assets without precise local maintenance. 

**摘要：** 3D 高斯泼溅（3DGS）正从一次性重建演变为可交付、可检查且可维护的视觉资产。现有的工作流程主要集中在全局重建、训练阶段的密度控制或开放式生成编辑上，导致已训练的资产缺乏精确的局部维护手段。

We propose FocusGS, which unifies local repair and deterministic editing as composite spatial deltas. Repair is the purely additive special case: its base-manipulation term is empty, and it adds only local Gaussian bases; deterministic editing uses erase-insert factorization (EIF) to combine old-carrier erasure with new-content insertion. 

我们提出了 FocusGS，它将局部修复和确定性编辑统一为复合空间增量（composite spatial deltas）。修复是纯加法的一种特殊情况：其基础操作项为空，仅添加局部高斯基；确定性编辑则使用擦除-插入分解（EIF）技术，将旧载体的擦除与新内容的插入相结合。

FocusGS addresses spatial gradient starvation: local repair raises target-region PSNR by 7.91 dB over 93 evaluation views. Across all 83 deterministic editing trials, the target ROI improves, with a trial-averaged mean edited ROI PSNR of 21.97 dB and a mean gain of +11.05 dB; across five public editing cases, FocusGS-EIF reaches 33.17 dB Target-mask PSNR and 0.994 Target-delta Correlation, while both text-driven baselines fail to complete the prescribed updates. FocusGS provides a lightweight, verifiable 3DGS maintenance operator.

FocusGS 解决了空间梯度匮乏问题：在 93 个评估视角下，局部修复将目标区域的 PSNR 提升了 7.91 dB。在全部 83 次确定性编辑试验中，目标感兴趣区域（ROI）均得到改善，试验平均编辑 ROI PSNR 达到 21.97 dB，平均增益为 +11.05 dB；在五个公开编辑案例中，FocusGS-EIF 达到了 33.17 dB 的目标掩码 PSNR 和 0.994 的目标增量相关性，而两种基于文本驱动的基准方法均未能完成预定的更新任务。FocusGS 提供了一种轻量级、可验证的 3DGS 维护算子。
