# WhatsApp says it is fixing an issue that disabled several accounts
# WhatsApp 表示正在修复导致多个账户被禁用的问题

In the last few hours, several people across social media have reported that their WhatsApp accounts have been blocked and put “under review” by Meta. The company, in response to TechCrunch’s inquiries about the matter, said that it is working on restoring access to these accounts.
在过去几个小时里，社交媒体上的多名用户反映，他们的 WhatsApp 账户被 Meta 封锁并处于“审核中”状态。针对此事，Meta 在回应 TechCrunch 的询问时表示，公司正在努力恢复这些账户的访问权限。

According to posts from those impacted on X and Threads, many were suddenly unable to use their accounts. When they tried to access their accounts, WhatsApp displayed a screen that said, “Your account activity and device info is being checked to make sure it follows our Terms of Service. We’ll notify you of the result typically within 24 hours.”
根据受影响用户在 X 和 Threads 上发布的帖子，许多人突然无法使用自己的账户。当他们尝试登录时，WhatsApp 显示屏上写道：“您的账户活动和设备信息正在接受检查，以确保其符合我们的服务条款。我们通常会在 24 小时内通知您结果。”

Meta’s popular chat app has more than 3 billion monthly active users across the world, making even a proportionally small disruption one that can impact thousands of users.
Meta 这款热门聊天应用在全球拥有超过 30 亿月活跃用户，这意味着即使是比例很小的故障，也可能影响到数千名用户。

Meta acknowledged the issue and told TechCrunch the company is working on resolving it. However, it didn’t specify if users in specific regions were impacted, or what caused the problem.
Meta 承认了这一问题，并告诉 TechCrunch 公司正在努力解决。不过，该公司并未说明是否有特定地区的用户受到影响，也未透露导致该问题的原因。

“We are always working to stay ahead of those trying to abuse our service and ban accounts to help keep other users safe. Sometimes we get this wrong, and if we do, we try to fix it as quickly as possible to get people back to chatting. We’ve taken action to restore affected accounts,” a company spokesperson said.
“我们一直在努力防范那些试图滥用我们服务的人，并封禁相关账户以保障其他用户的安全。有时我们会出错，如果发生这种情况，我们会尽快修复，让用户恢复正常聊天。我们已经采取行动恢复了受影响的账户，”公司发言人表示。

The company didn’t provide details around what caused a bunch of accounts to go under review.
该公司并未提供关于是什么原因导致大量账户进入“审核中”状态的详细信息。
