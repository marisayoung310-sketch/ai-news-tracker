# Uncertainty-Aware Deepfake Detection via Multi-View Structural Learning
# 基于多视图结构学习的深度伪造不确定性感知检测

**Abstract:** Security-critical biometric and forensic applications require accurate predictions and reliable confidence estimates, particularly under distribution shift. This challenge is especially acute for deepfake detection, where foundation-model-based detectors often exhibit overconfident predictions on out-of-distribution manipulations, which limits their suitability for operational deployment.

**摘要：** 在安全关键的生物识别和取证应用中，系统需要准确的预测和可靠的置信度估计，尤其是在数据分布发生偏移的情况下。这一挑战在深度伪造检测领域尤为突出，因为基于基础模型的检测器往往会对分布外（out-of-distribution）的伪造操作表现出过度自信的预测，从而限制了其在实际部署中的适用性。

We propose an uncertainty-aware deepfake detection framework that identifies manipulations through inconsistencies across complementary evidence sources. The framework integrates three streams: a visual stream based on an adapted CLIP encoder, a semantic stream that models consistency among facial attributes through differentiable constraints, and a structural stream that captures class-dependent dependency patterns between semantic and forensic features.

我们提出了一种不确定性感知的深度伪造检测框架，通过跨互补证据源的不一致性来识别伪造操作。该框架集成了三个分支：基于改进版 CLIP 编码器的视觉分支；通过可微约束对人脸属性间一致性进行建模的语义分支；以及捕捉语义特征与取证特征之间类依赖模式的结构分支。

To effectively combine these signals, we introduce Inter-Branch Disagreement Calibration (IBDC), a disagreement-aware uncertainty modeling mechanism that links predictive uncertainty to conflicts among evidence streams.

为了有效地整合这些信号，我们引入了分支间分歧校准（Inter-Branch Disagreement Calibration, IBDC）机制。这是一种感知分歧的不确定性建模机制，它将预测的不确定性与各证据流之间的冲突关联起来。

Extensive cross-dataset experiments using FaceForensics++ as the training source demonstrate that the proposed framework achieves state-of-the-art generalization across multiple out-of-distribution benchmarks while consistently improving calibration and selective prediction performance. These results show that combining complementary evidence with disagreement-aware uncertainty provides a robust foundation for trustworthy and well-calibrated deepfake detection under distribution shift.

使用 FaceForensics++ 作为训练源进行的广泛跨数据集实验表明，该框架在多个分布外基准测试中实现了最先进的泛化能力，同时持续提升了校准效果和选择性预测性能。这些结果表明，将互补证据与分歧感知不确定性相结合，为在分布偏移下实现可信且校准良好的深度伪造检测提供了坚实的基础。
