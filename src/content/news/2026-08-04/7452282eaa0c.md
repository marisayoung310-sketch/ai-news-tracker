# How Claude Helped Me Build My $200k+ ML Resume
# Claude 如何助我打造年薪 20 万美元以上的机器学习简历

In this article, I want to breakdown exactly how I used Claude to help me craft my resume that landed me a $200k+ Machine Learning Engineer offer. Let’s get into it!
在本文中，我想详细拆解我是如何利用 Claude 来优化简历，并最终获得一份年薪超过 20 万美元的机器学习工程师录用通知的。让我们开始吧！

### Resume Mindset
### 简历思维

Your resume is the single most important document in your career. I spent over 10 hours drafting mine. That’s a lot of time, but it’s exactly why it got results. Something I tell all my coaching clients: Resumes are more of an art than a science. This is not to sound “smart”, but rather to tell you that there is no “definitive” guide on what your resume needs to look like.
简历是你职业生涯中最重要的一份文件。我花了超过 10 个小时来起草它。这虽然耗时，但正是它能带来成果的原因。我常告诉我的辅导客户：简历更像是一门艺术，而非科学。这并不是为了显得“聪明”，而是想告诉你，关于简历应该长什么样，并没有所谓的“标准答案”。

Tailoring your resume is the ultimate cheat code and the easiest way to tailor a resume is through having a “master” resume that has everything on it. Don’t worry about the length, how many bullet points you have or the number of technologies you list. Then, you create a resume tailored to the job description and role by removing unnecessary or irrelevant information. It’s much easier to subtract than add, and it’s a much quicker method to tailor your resume to every application.
量身定制简历是终极的“作弊码”，而定制简历最简单的方法就是先准备一份包含所有内容的“母版”简历。不要担心篇幅、要点数量或列出的技术栈有多少。之后，你可以通过删除不必要或无关的信息，针对具体的职位描述和岗位来定制简历。做减法远比做加法容易，这也是针对每次申请快速定制简历的最快方法。

### Fundamental Structure
### 基础结构

Before letting Claude go wild on your resume, you need a solid underlying structure. We need rough sections and raw content that give the AI enough to work with so it actually “gets” the tone, achievements, and overall vibe you’re going for. I’ve tried generating a resume from scratch with Claude, and it doesn’t work well. You still need to feed it your technical skills, experience, and education for it to draft something decent.
在让 Claude 大展身手之前，你需要一个扎实的底层结构。我们需要粗略的板块和原始内容，让 AI 有足够的信息来理解你想要的语气、成就和整体风格。我曾尝试让 Claude 从零开始生成简历，效果并不理想。你仍然需要提供你的技术技能、工作经验和教育背景，它才能起草出一份像样的简历。

### Design & Layout
### 设计与排版

People overcomplicate resumes so much. Your resume needs to be clean, simple, and, to some extent, boring! No fancy formatting, colours, and definitely no multi-columns. All we need is black text, consistent readable fonts and bullet points.
人们总是把简历搞得太复杂。你的简历需要整洁、简单，甚至在某种程度上要“枯燥”！不要花哨的格式、颜色，绝对不要用多栏排版。我们只需要黑色文字、统一易读的字体和项目符号。

### Structure
### 结构

The goal is to get the most important information showcasing your fit for the job into the top third of the page. However, in general the following works well for the majority of cases: Header, Summary Statement, Skills, Experience, Projects, Education.
目标是将展示你与职位匹配度的最重要信息放在页面上方的三分之一处。通常情况下，以下结构适用于大多数情况：页眉、个人总结、技能、工作经验、项目经历、教育背景。

### Header
### 页眉

All you need is: Your name, Job title (put data scientist if in doubt, don’t say “aspiring”), Contact details, Location, and Relevant links like LinkedIn, GitHub, Medium, Kaggle etc. (No more than 4). One thing to ensure is that the links work.
你只需要包含：姓名、职位（不确定时写数据科学家，不要写“有志于成为……”）、联系方式、所在地，以及相关的链接（如 LinkedIn、GitHub、Medium、Kaggle 等，不超过 4 个）。确保所有链接都能正常打开。

### Summary Statement
### 个人总结

A summary section used correctly can instantly tell the recruiter that you are fit for the job. You realistically should only have a summary section after 2+ years of experience in the field. Avoid fluffy language such as “passionate,” “determined,” or “hard-working.” No cliches or vague language.
如果使用得当，个人总结部分可以立即告诉招聘人员你非常适合该职位。实际上，只有在拥有 2 年以上相关领域经验后，才建议添加此部分。避免使用“充满激情”、“意志坚定”或“勤奋努力”等空洞的词汇。不要使用陈词滥调或模糊的语言。

### Technical Skills
### 技术技能

For data science jobs, technical skills are so important and you will immediately be filtered on these. You should only have three rows for this section:
对于数据科学工作，技术技能至关重要，你会被直接根据这些技能进行筛选。此部分建议只分三行：
*   **Languages (Proficient/Familiar):** Programming languages you feel comfortable working in vs. those you have worked in the past but aren't overly confident in.
*   **语言（精通/熟悉）：** 你能熟练使用的编程语言，以及过去使用过但目前不太自信的语言。
*   **Technologies:** List tools you have worked with or feel comfortable working in e.g. AWS, Git, Docker, GCP, Linux, Bash.
*   **技术栈：** 列出你使用过或能熟练操作的工具，例如 AWS、Git、Docker、GCP、Linux、Bash。

### Experience
### 工作经验

This is the most essential part of your resume. Start by drafting your experience bullet points and then we will refine them with Claude.
这是简历中最核心的部分。先起草你的工作经历要点，然后我们会用 Claude 对其进行润色。
*   3–5 bullet points max per experience.
*   每段经历最多 3-5 个要点。
*   Use active action verbs: “developed,” “led,” “optimised,” “generated,” and “improved.”
*   使用主动动词：如“开发”、“领导”、“优化”、“生成”和“改进”。
*   Use numbers, metrics and especially financial impact in every bullet point.
*   在每个要点中加入数字、指标，尤其是财务影响。

### Projects
### 项目经历

Projects are very similar to experience, but it’s often hard to measure business impact because the work isn’t for the business. Either way, you need to think of them as experience because that’s the closest “experience” you have to the job role if you are adding this section in.
项目经历与工作经验非常相似，但由于这些工作并非直接服务于商业，往往很难衡量其商业影响。无论如何，你都需要将其视为工作经验，因为如果你添加了这一部分，这就是你与目标职位最接近的“经验”。
