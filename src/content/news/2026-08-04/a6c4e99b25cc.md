# Your Fitbit data can now connect directly to Apple Health
# 你的 Fitbit 数据现在可以直接连接到 Apple Health

Google is rolling out an update that will finally allow you to connect your Fitbit workouts, steps, vitals, and other data to Apple Health, as reported earlier by 9to5Mac. With Google Health’s 5.05 update, you can now tap your profile icon, select “Partner apps,” and choose Apple Health to link your data directly.

据 9to5Mac 此前报道，谷歌正在推出一项更新，终于允许用户将 Fitbit 的锻炼、步数、生命体征及其他数据连接到 Apple Health。随着 Google Health 5.05 版本的更新，你现在只需点击个人资料图标，选择“合作伙伴应用”（Partner apps），然后选择 Apple Health，即可直接关联你的数据。

Previously, you could only import your Apple Health data into Google Health, but not the other way around. That meant using third-party apps — like Power Sync for Fitbit or Fitbit to Apple Health Sync — or some other kind of workaround to connect your Google Health information to Apple Health.

此前，你只能将 Apple Health 的数据导入 Google Health，而无法反向操作。这意味着用户必须使用第三方应用（如 Power Sync for Fitbit 或 Fitbit to Apple Health Sync）或其他变通方法，才能将 Google Health 的信息连接到 Apple Health。

Google Health replaced the Fitbit app in May. The new app had a bit of a rocky rollout after users raised concerns over the new AI health coach, user interface, and other issues, though Google has since addressed some of these complaints.

Google Health 于今年 5 月取代了 Fitbit 应用。这款新应用在推出初期经历了一些波折，用户对其新的 AI 健康教练、用户界面及其他问题表示担忧，不过谷歌此后已经解决了一些投诉。

Along with this update, Google announced that users in the US can use Smart Health Links to share medical records with a provider or family member. You can create a link by heading to the Health tab, choosing the “Medical” section, selecting “Shareable records,” and tapping the “plus” icon. From there, you can choose which data you want to include in the link and share it via a URL or QR code.

伴随此次更新，谷歌还宣布美国用户可以使用“智能健康链接”（Smart Health Links）与医疗服务提供商或家庭成员共享医疗记录。你可以通过前往“健康”（Health）选项卡，选择“医疗”（Medical）部分，点击“可共享记录”（Shareable records），然后点击“加号”图标来创建链接。之后，你可以选择要包含在链接中的数据，并通过 URL 或二维码进行分享。
