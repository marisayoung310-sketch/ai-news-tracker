# SCMA: Structure-Conditioned and Metal-Aware Flow Matching for CT Metal Artifact Reduction
# SCMA：用于CT金属伪影去除的结构条件化与金属感知流匹配模型

**Abstract:** In X-ray CT, metallic objects cause beam hardening, photon starvation, and scattering, leading to projection inconsistency, streaks, dark bands, and structural distortions that compromise clinical diagnosis and quantitative analysis.
**摘要：** 在X射线CT成像中，金属物体会导致射束硬化、光子匮乏和散射，从而引发投影不一致、条纹、暗带以及结构畸变，这些问题会严重影响临床诊断和定量分析。

Existing metal artifact reduction (MAR) methods remain limited: optimization-based methods may leave residual artifacts or blur structures, regression networks may generalize poorly across scenarios, and generative models without sample-specific structural guidance and physical constraints may produce anatomically inconsistent structures.
现有的金属伪影去除（MAR）方法仍存在局限性：基于优化的方法可能会留下残留伪影或导致结构模糊；回归网络在不同场景下的泛化能力较差；而缺乏样本特定结构引导和物理约束的生成模型，则可能产生解剖学上不一致的结构。

Flow Matching learns a continuous-time velocity field that deterministically transports a source distribution to a target distribution, providing a flexible MAR prior. However, standard unconditional Flow Matching does not exploit sample-specific structure, spatially nonuniform metal-induced degradation, or measured projections.
流匹配（Flow Matching）通过学习连续时间速度场，将源分布确定性地传输到目标分布，为MAR提供了一种灵活的先验。然而，标准的无条件流匹配并未利用样本特定的结构、空间非均匀的金属诱导退化，或测量得到的投影数据。

To address these limitations, we propose SCMA, a structure-conditioned and metal-aware Flow Matching framework. First, a linear-interpolation-corrected image is fed into the velocity network with the intermediate state as a sample-specific structural condition, guiding inference toward artifact-free CT images while preserving anatomy.
为了解决这些局限性，我们提出了SCMA，一种结构条件化与金属感知的流匹配框架。首先，将线性插值校正后的图像输入速度网络，并将中间状态作为样本特定的结构条件，在引导推理生成无伪影CT图像的同时，保留解剖结构。

Second, time-varying spatial weights from the metal mask and its distance transform are incorporated into the Flow Matching loss to emphasize severe degradation within and around metal regions.
其次，将来自金属掩模及其距离变换的时变空间权重纳入流匹配损失函数中，以重点处理金属区域内部及其周围的严重退化。

Finally, conditional Flow Matching updates alternate with projection-consistency correction during inference, allowing reliable measurements outside metal traces to constrain predictions.
最后，在推理过程中，条件流匹配更新与投影一致性校正交替进行，从而利用金属轨迹之外的可靠测量数据来约束预测结果。

Experiments on simulated and real CT data demonstrate that SCMA more effectively suppresses metal artifacts, preserves local anatomical structures, and reduces hallucination-like structures inconsistent with projection measurements than representative MAR methods.
在模拟和真实CT数据上的实验表明，与代表性的MAR方法相比，SCMA能更有效地抑制金属伪影，保留局部解剖结构，并减少与投影测量不一致的幻觉结构。
