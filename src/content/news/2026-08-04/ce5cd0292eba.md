# Spider-Man and The Odyssey are splitting up IMAX screens after a record-breaking weekend
# 《蜘蛛侠》与《奥德赛》在打破票房纪录的周末后，将共同瓜分 IMAX 银幕

Spider-Man: Brand New Day is joining The Odyssey in IMAX theaters after both movies led the biggest weekend in box office history. On Monday, IMAX announced that Spider-Man: Brand New Day will be available in select digital IMAX locations across North America starting Thursday, August 6th.

在两部电影共同引领了影史上最强劲的周末票房表现后，《蜘蛛侠：全新的一天》（Spider-Man: Brand New Day）即将进驻 IMAX 影院，与《奥德赛》（The Odyssey）同台放映。周一，IMAX 宣布《蜘蛛侠：全新的一天》将于 8 月 6 日（周四）起，在北美部分数字 IMAX 影院上映。

The news comes as The Odyssey’s exclusive three-weekend run in IMAX theaters comes to an end, allowing Spider-Man: Brand New Day to play alongside it, as reported by Variety. Spider-Man: Brand New Day raked in $355 million following its July 31st release, making it the second-biggest box office debut in North America since Avengers: Endgame in 2019.

据《综艺》（Variety）报道，随着《奥德赛》在 IMAX 影院为期三周的独家放映期结束，IMAX 银幕将腾出空间让《蜘蛛侠：全新的一天》同步上映。《蜘蛛侠：全新的一天》自 7 月 31 日上映以来已狂揽 3.55 亿美元票房，成为自 2019 年《复仇者联盟：终局之战》以来北美票房首映成绩第二高的电影。

Christopher Nolan’s The Odyssey had a similarly big weekend, pulling in $51 million weeks after its premiere. Both films — in addition to Toy Story 5, Moana, Minions & Monsters — led to a record-breaking $430 million weekend at the box office, according to Variety.

克里斯托弗·诺兰执导的《奥德赛》同样表现不俗，在首映数周后仍斩获了 5100 万美元的周末票房。据《综艺》报道，这两部电影，加上《玩具总动员 5》、《海洋奇缘》、《小黄人与怪物》等影片，共同推动了周末票房达到创纪录的 4.3 亿美元。

The Odyssey will continue playing in theaters across the global IMAX network. The film has already generated $221 million at the global IMAX box office, helping to make July IMAX’s highest-grossing month in history. IMAX says The Odyssey is the only film that has driven “multiple $40 million-plus weekends” in its global box office.

《奥德赛》将继续在全球 IMAX 影院网络中放映。该片已在全球 IMAX 院线贡献了 2.21 亿美元的票房，助力 7 月成为 IMAX 历史上票房收入最高的月份。IMAX 表示，《奥德赛》是唯一一部在全球 IMAX 票房中实现“多个周末票房突破 4000 万美元”的电影。
