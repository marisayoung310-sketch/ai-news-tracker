# Samsung’s discounted smart monitor is $349.99, its lowest price yet
# 三星智能显示器降价至 349.99 美元，创历史新低

This 4K smart monitor is loaded with features like streaming apps, cloud gaming apps, and a webcam.
这款 4K 智能显示器功能丰富，内置了流媒体应用、云游戏应用以及网络摄像头。

Samsung makes a variety of TVs and computer monitors, but if you want both and you’re limited on space, you might want to consider the M80F. This capable 32-inch 4K panel combines features commonly found on both types of screens, and Amazon and B&H Photo both have it discounted to $349.99, where it’s usually $400 or much higher.
三星生产各种电视和电脑显示器，但如果你既想要电视又想要显示器，且空间有限，那么不妨考虑一下 M80F。这款性能出色的 32 英寸 4K 面板结合了这两种屏幕的常见功能。目前亚马逊（Amazon）和 B&H Photo 均将其价格降至 349.99 美元，而其通常售价为 400 美元甚至更高。

In addition to acting as a standard 4K screen for your computer or console, the M80F has a full suite of video streaming apps you’d expect to find on a television, with picture-in-picture support for multitasking, plus a removable webcam for video chats.
除了作为电脑或游戏主机的标准 4K 屏幕外，M80F 还配备了电视上常见的全套视频流媒体应用，支持画中画多任务处理，并附带一个可拆卸的网络摄像头用于视频聊天。

The M80F has above-average connectivity for a monitor. In addition to the ports like HDMI 2.0, USB-C for charging devices up to 65W, and USB-A for your keyboard and mouse, you can also connect the Tizen interface to Wi-Fi to directly communicate with your network (it also supports Bluetooth headphones).
M80F 的连接性能在显示器中表现优于平均水平。除了 HDMI 2.0 接口、支持最高 65W 充电的 USB-C 接口以及用于连接键盘鼠标的 USB-A 接口外，你还可以将 Tizen 系统连接至 Wi-Fi，直接与网络通信（它还支持蓝牙耳机）。

Speaking of gaming, the M80F supports apps like Xbox and Nvidia GeForce Now, letting you pair a controller to the display to play games over the cloud. The VA panel is better suited for work than play, but HDR10 Plus support will help your movies and TV shows pop.
说到游戏，M80F 支持 Xbox 和 Nvidia GeForce Now 等应用，让你能够将手柄与显示器配对，通过云端畅玩游戏。虽然 VA 面板更适合办公而非游戏，但 HDR10 Plus 的支持将使你的电影和电视节目画面更加生动。

My colleague Cameron Faulkner reviewed a previous version of the M80F and appreciated its versatility, but the $699.99 launch price was a bit off-putting, so a discount on a newer, improved model is definitely worth calling out.
我的同事 Cameron Faulkner 曾评测过 M80F 的前代版本，并对其多功能性表示赞赏，但 699.99 美元的首发价格让人望而却步。因此，这款更新、更出色的型号能够降价，绝对值得关注。
