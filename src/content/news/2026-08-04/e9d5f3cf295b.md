# Palworld’s expanding to mobile with a new MMORPG
# 《幻兽帕鲁》将推出全新 MMORPG 手游

After its 1.0 launch last month, Palworld is coming to iOS and Android with a new open-world MMORPG launching later this year, Polygon reports. Garena, the developer behind the new game, says in its announcement that Palworld Online “reimagines the depth, freedom, and scale of the original PC survival creature-collecting title for mobile.”

据 Polygon 报道，继上个月 1.0 版本发布后，《幻兽帕鲁》（Palworld）即将登陆 iOS 和 Android 平台，并于今年晚些时候推出一款全新的开放世界 MMORPG。负责开发这款新游戏的 Garena 在公告中表示，《幻兽帕鲁 Online》“为移动端重新构想了原版 PC 生存及生物收集游戏的深度、自由度和规模。”

The core features of the new mobile game sound a lot like the original Palworld, with crafting, creature collecting, and multiplayer options for co-op, PvP, and PvE. Garena says it will introduce a “new narrative setting with original story elements,” and “expand the narrative with new side stories created exclusively for this version.” It will also have a “streamlined, touch-friendly interface with optional one-tap controls” to handle base management on different sized mobile screens.

这款新手游的核心玩法与原版《幻兽帕鲁》非常相似，包含制作、生物收集以及支持合作、PvP 和 PvE 的多人游戏选项。Garena 表示，游戏将引入“带有原创剧情要素的全新叙事背景”，并“通过专为此版本创作的全新支线故事来扩展叙事”。此外，它还将配备“精简且适配触屏的界面，并提供可选的一键式操作”，以便在不同尺寸的移动设备屏幕上进行基地管理。

Palworld Online doesn’t have a specific release date yet, but if it does come out this year, it could arrive before the Palfarm spin-off Pocketpair announced last year, which also doesn’t have a release date yet.

目前《幻兽帕鲁 Online》尚未确定具体的发布日期，但如果它能在今年内推出，可能会早于 Pocketpair 去年宣布的《Palfarm》衍生作，后者目前也尚未公布发布日期。
