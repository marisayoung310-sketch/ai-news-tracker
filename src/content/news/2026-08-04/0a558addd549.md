# Can Synthetic Data Overcome the Generalization Limits of AI-Based Flower and Pod Detection Across Cowpea Breeding Genotypes and Environments?
### 合成数据能否克服基于人工智能的豇豆育种基因型与环境花荚检测的泛化限制？

**Abstract:** High-throughput phenotyping requires AI-enabled computer vision models that generalize across genotypes, locations, and growing seasons, yet such models often lose accuracy under new conditions. Annotating real imagery for every genotype-by-environment (G x E) combination a breeding program encounters is prohibitively expensive.

**摘要：** 高通量表型分析需要能够在不同基因型、地点和生长季节之间进行泛化的人工智能计算机视觉模型，然而此类模型在面对新条件时往往会丧失准确性。为育种项目中遇到的每一种基因型与环境（G x E）组合标注真实图像，其成本高得令人望而却步。

We quantify how G x E shifts affect AI-based detection of cowpea flowers and pods across two California locations and two growing seasons. Flower detection mAP@50 fell from 76.3% to as low as 50.6% under unseen shifts, and pod detection was more sensitive. Feature-space and image-quality diagnostics confirmed these losses track measurable distributional shifts.

我们量化了 G x E 变化如何影响加利福尼亚州两个地点和两个生长季节中基于人工智能的豇豆花和荚检测。在未见过的变化条件下，花朵检测的 mAP@50 从 76.3% 降至最低 50.6%，而荚果检测则更为敏感。特征空间和图像质量诊断证实，这些性能损失与可测量的分布偏移密切相关。

Because closing this gap with real data alone is not practical, we test whether synthetic imagery, rendered from a procedural 3D cowpea model, can substitute for that annotation burden. Synthetic supervision alone improved over pretraining but remained limited by a domain gap driven by camera image formation, not scene content.

由于仅靠真实数据来弥补这一差距并不现实，我们测试了通过程序化 3D 豇豆模型渲染的合成图像是否可以替代标注工作。仅使用合成监督虽然比预训练有所改进，但仍受限于由相机成像过程（而非场景内容）引起的领域差异。

A domain-gap-aware camera-realism augmentation strategy, optimized against measured real-image statistics via Wasserstein distance, narrowed this gap, and a linear HDR representation converted a smaller measured gap into a larger detection gain than an 8-bit representation.

一种感知领域差异的相机真实感增强策略，通过 Wasserstein 距离针对测量的真实图像统计数据进行了优化，从而缩小了这一差距。此外，线性 HDR 表示法比 8 位表示法能将更小的测量差距转化为更大的检测增益。

Optimized HDR synthetic data combined with as few as five real images matched or exceeded the real-data baseline for spatial generalization, and pod detection benefited most at the lowest shot counts, with more modest gains under temporal shift. These results show that synthetic data can overcome the generalization limits of AI-based flower and pod detection, but only when the domain gap is measured and optimized rather than assumed away.

优化后的 HDR 合成数据仅需结合五张真实图像，即可在空间泛化方面达到或超过真实数据基准；荚果检测在样本量最少时获益最大，而在时间偏移下的增益则较为温和。这些结果表明，合成数据可以克服基于人工智能的花荚检测的泛化限制，但前提是必须对领域差异进行测量和优化，而不是将其忽略。
