# Lenovo Googlebook leaks reveal a laptop and 2-in-1 tablet
# 联想 Googlebook 泄露图曝光：包含笔记本电脑与二合一平板

Lenovo is expected to release some of the first Googlebook models later this year, and leaked images have now given us a good idea of what they might look like. Leaked press images shared by Digital Citizen and Android Headlines include a laptop and a 2-in-1 tablet, all of which feature Googlebook branding on the keyboard.

联想预计将于今年晚些时候发布首批 Googlebook 机型，而泄露的图片让我们对其外观有了初步了解。Digital Citizen 和 Android Headlines 分享的泄露新闻图片展示了一款笔记本电脑和一款二合一平板电脑，它们的键盘上均印有 Googlebook 的品牌标识。

This is the best look we’ve had at Google’s Chromebook successor since the Android-based Googlebook platform was announced in May. Details about the initiative remain sparse, but we know that Acer, Asus, Dell, HP, and Lenovo are lined up to release the first models sometime in Fall 2026. Googlebooks are also expected to ditch ChromeOS in favor of a new operating system, and Gemini AI will reportedly be baked into pretty much everything.

这是自 5 月份基于 Android 的 Googlebook 平台发布以来，我们对这款谷歌 Chromebook 继任者最清晰的一次观察。关于该计划的细节仍然很少，但我们已知宏碁 (Acer)、华硕 (Asus)、戴尔 (Dell)、惠普 (HP) 和联想 (Lenovo) 都计划在 2026 年秋季的某个时间点发布首批机型。预计 Googlebook 将放弃 ChromeOS，转而采用全新的操作系统，据报道，Gemini AI 将被深度集成到系统的各个角落。

The Lenovo leaks look fairly similar to Lenovo’s existing Chromebook lineup, with a few exceptions. The Lenovo Googlebook laptop appears to be available in two sizes, and features a light strip on the back that resembles the glowing bar that appears on the official Googlebook renders that Google released earlier this year. The 2-in-1 tablet also includes a light bar on the rear camera housing — similar to the glowing orb that will reportedly feature on the upcoming Pixel 11 lineup.

联想的泄露机型看起来与其现有的 Chromebook 产品线非常相似，但也有一些例外。联想 Googlebook 笔记本电脑似乎有两种尺寸可选，其背面配有一条灯带，类似于谷歌今年早些时候发布的官方 Googlebook 渲染图中的发光条。二合一平板电脑的后置摄像头外壳上也包含一个灯条，这与据传将出现在即将推出的 Pixel 11 系列上的发光圆环类似。

The keyboards also include a new key on the bottom row that displays Google’s “G” branding. Digital Citizen reports that this is a dedicated hardware key to surface Gemini, but it’s worth noting that the keyboards also include a Quick Insert button where the cap lock key is — something that Chromebooks introduced to call up Gemini back in 2024.

键盘底行还增加了一个显示谷歌“G”标志的新按键。据 Digital Citizen 报道，这是一个用于唤起 Gemini 的专用硬件按键。但值得注意的是，键盘上原本大写锁定键 (Caps Lock) 的位置现在是一个“快速插入”(Quick Insert) 按钮——这是 Chromebook 在 2024 年引入的用于调用 Gemini 的功能。
