# The AI Was the Easy Part: What Is a Forward-Deployed Engineer in a Supply Chain?
# AI 只是小菜一碟：供应链中的“前线部署工程师”究竟是什么？

In 2012, the Harvard Business Review called the data scientist “the sexiest job of the 21st century.” Every company suddenly wanted one. More than a decade later, the title everyone is chasing now is the Forward Deployed Engineer. This engineer is embedded inside a company’s operation to make AI models or optimisation algorithms actually work in their messy reality. Unfortunately, few operations are messier than a supply chain. I learned it the hard way when I became Supply Chain Data Scientist.
2012 年，《哈佛商业评论》将数据科学家称为“21 世纪最性感的工作”。一时间，每家公司都想招募一名。十多年后的今天，每个人都在追逐的头衔变成了“前线部署工程师”（Forward Deployed Engineer，简称 FDE）。这类工程师被嵌入到公司的运营一线，旨在让 AI 模型或优化算法在混乱的现实中真正发挥作用。不幸的是，很少有业务比供应链更混乱。当我成为一名供应链数据科学家时，我深刻地体会到了这一点。

For example, imagine a shipment of luxury bags leaving a warehouse in Milan, passing through trucks, airports, and customs clearance before arriving at a store in Shanghai. This chain involves several teams in multiple countries that use different systems (ERP, WMS, TMS) that were not designed to provide harmonised data. For example, I worked on a project where it took us 18 months just to build a pipeline to compare two dates (requested and actual delivery dates) to measure on-time delivery.
例如，想象一下一批奢侈品包从米兰的仓库出发，经过卡车运输、机场中转和海关清关，最终抵达上海的门店。这条链条涉及多个国家的多个团队，他们使用的不同系统（ERP、WMS、TMS）在设计之初并未考虑提供统一的数据。例如，我曾参与过一个项目，仅仅为了建立一个能够对比两个日期（要求交付日期与实际交付日期）以衡量准时交付率的数据管道，我们就花了 18 个月的时间。

Therefore, in most AI supply chain projects, the biggest challenge is implementing agents in this chaotic environment. This was the case in the past for business intelligence and data science, and now even more with generative AI. Why companies are now paying so much to have Forward Deployed Engineer implementing AI for Supply Chain operations? In this article, I will answer this question using a project I conducted for a fashion retailer, illustrating the challenges FDEs face in this environment and the qualities they need to manifest.
因此，在大多数 AI 供应链项目中，最大的挑战在于如何在如此混乱的环境中部署智能体（Agents）。过去在商业智能和数据科学领域如此，如今在生成式 AI 领域更是如此。为什么公司现在愿意花高价聘请前线部署工程师来为供应链运营实施 AI？在本文中，我将通过我为一家时尚零售商开展的项目来回答这个问题，阐述 FDE 在这种环境下所面临的挑战以及他们需要具备的素质。

This is a deployment of an AI-powered distribution chain monitoring tool that helps planners find the root cause of late store deliveries. I have implemented this agentic orchestration in the logistics department of a retailer based in Milan. Their problem was their limited capacity to detect and analyse failures in the distribution chain. Logistics Director: “More than 35% of late deliveries are not explained.” They wanted an agent to conduct root cause analyses from the data alone, without biases, like the analysis below.
这是一个 AI 驱动的配送链监控工具的部署案例，旨在帮助规划人员找出门店延迟交付的根本原因。我在一家位于米兰的零售商的物流部门实施了这种智能体编排。他们的问题在于检测和分析配送链故障的能力有限。物流总监曾说：“超过 35% 的延迟交付无法解释。”他们希望有一个智能体能够仅凭数据进行无偏见的根本原因分析，就像下方的分析一样。

The interesting part of this implementation is not the agentic orchestration itself, but everything I had to build around it. I will use this example to go through what the job actually requires: Reading systems with no documentation, getting teams to agree on a definition, and letting the users test the tool before you trust it. None of it is technical, and all of it is the job of a Forward Deployed Engineer. If you are a data scientist looking at where the highest-paid roles are right now, this is the work behind the title, and the skills you would need to do it.
这次实施中最有趣的部分并非智能体编排本身，而是我必须围绕它构建的一切。我将通过这个例子来探讨这份工作真正需要什么：阅读没有文档的系统、让各团队就定义达成一致、以及在信任工具之前让用户进行测试。这些都不是技术工作，但它们全是前线部署工程师的工作内容。如果你是一名正在寻找高薪职位的数据科学家，这就是该头衔背后的工作实质，也是你完成这些工作所必需的技能。

It started with a request from the logistics director: “I want AI to help my planners find the root cause of failures in my distribution chain.” They have a team of 12 planners who manage store inventory. They send orders to the warehouse, each with a requested delivery date, and monitor shipments until they reach the store. Because of delays in the warehouse, a routing change, or a hold at customs, more than 25% of orders arrive late. Store managers complain to the director because a late delivery means lost sales.
一切始于物流总监的一个请求：“我希望 AI 能帮助我的规划人员找出配送链中故障的根本原因。”他们有一个由 12 名规划人员组成的团队负责管理门店库存。他们向仓库发送订单，每个订单都有要求的交付日期，并监控货物直到其到达门店。由于仓库延迟、路线变更或海关扣留，超过 25% 的订单会延迟到达。门店经理会向总监投诉，因为延迟交付意味着销售损失。

Fortunately, each step is recorded in a system: Warehouse operational teams that will prepare and load the goods in a truck, using a Warehouse Management System (WMS); Transportation teams organise the pickup at the warehouse, air freight and the last-mile delivery, using a Transportation Management System (TMS). These systems generate the timestamps used to track orders across the entire distribution chain. Planners manually crunch data in Excel to explain past delays and flag future ones. However, this is slow and inefficient. The analyses usually arrive too late, and operational teams do not have enough time to pinpoint the root cause. So the discussion turns into an argument, each team pointing at the other. As I like to say, the most complicated task in supply chain management is dealing with humans.
幸运的是，每一步都被记录在系统中：仓库运营团队使用仓库管理系统（WMS）准备货物并装车；运输团队使用运输管理系统（TMS）组织仓库提货、空运和最后一公里配送。这些系统生成的时间戳用于跟踪整个配送链中的订单。规划人员过去手动在 Excel 中处理数据来解释过去的延迟并标记未来的延迟。然而，这种方式缓慢且低效。分析结果通常来得太晚，运营团队没有足够的时间来查明根本原因。于是讨论变成了争吵，每个团队都在指责对方。正如我常说的，供应链管理中最复杂的任务就是与人打交道。

That is why we built a Claude agent, connected to their data through an MCP server, to run this analysis regularly and help analysts act on it. The agent can query the data to answer any request in natural language, helping planners find the root causes of failures in the distribution chain. Reports with interactive visuals can be generated by planners in seconds without writing a single line of code. Planners now produce more root cause analyses than before, and they arrive in time to act on them. Analysts can now automatically schedule flash reports to each team (warehouse, road freight, air freight), conduct deep root cause analyses in less than 5 minutes.
这就是为什么我们构建了一个 Claude 智能体，通过 MCP 服务器连接到他们的数据，定期运行分析并帮助分析师采取行动。该智能体可以用自然语言查询数据以回答任何请求，帮助规划人员找出配送链中故障的根本原因。规划人员可以在几秒钟内生成带有交互式视觉效果的报告，而无需编写一行代码。现在，规划人员产出的根本原因分析比以前更多，而且这些分析能及时到位，以便采取行动。分析师现在可以自动向每个团队（仓库、公路运输、空运）安排快报，并在 5 分钟内完成深入的根本原因分析。
