# ReLoop-UME: Recurrent Depth with Learnable Retrieval Registers for Universal Multimodal Embedding

**ReLoop-UME：用于通用多模态嵌入的循环深度与可学习检索寄存器**

Universal multimodal embedding (UME) maps heterogeneous multimodal inputs into a shared embedding space. Existing UME models either form embeddings through single forward encoding or add computation through explicit rationale tokens and latent autoregressive states. Although token expansion can improve complex matching, serial generation increases retrieval latency and makes the final embedding depend on generated intermediate states.

通用多模态嵌入（UME）将异构的多模态输入映射到一个共享的嵌入空间中。现有的 UME 模型要么通过单次前向编码形成嵌入，要么通过显式的推理标记（rationale tokens）和潜在自回归状态来增加计算量。尽管标记扩展可以改善复杂的匹配效果，但串行生成增加了检索延迟，并使最终的嵌入依赖于生成的中间状态。

This raises a different question: can useful computation be expanded along model depth while keeping the token workspace fixed? We analyze positive-negative similarity separation at every layer of independently trained UME models and observe a shared progression: early layers contextualize multimodal inputs, a contiguous middle-to-late stage forms retrieval-discriminative features, and the final layers map them into the embedding space.

这提出了一个不同的问题：能否在保持标记工作空间固定的同时，沿模型深度扩展有效的计算？我们分析了独立训练的 UME 模型每一层的正负相似度分离情况，并观察到一个共同的演进过程：早期层对多模态输入进行语境化，连续的中后期阶段形成具有检索判别力的特征，而最终层将其映射到嵌入空间中。

Based on this finding, we propose ReLoop-UME, which executes the early layers once, recurrently reuses a parameter-shared retrieval-forming block, and applies the final mapping layers after the last loop. Learnable Retrieval Registers provide persistent retrieval-specific states that accumulate and exchange evidence across loops, with the final register serving as the embedding readout.

基于这一发现，我们提出了 ReLoop-UME。该模型仅执行一次早期层，循环复用一个参数共享的检索形成模块，并在最后一次循环后应用最终的映射层。可学习检索寄存器（Learnable Retrieval Registers）提供了持久的、特定于检索的状态，这些状态在循环中积累并交换证据，最终的寄存器作为嵌入的读取输出。

On MMEB-V2 and MRMR, ReLoop-UME consistently improves retrieval across different backbones while running 44.9x faster than UME-R1 and 1.5x faster than PLUME.

在 MMEB-V2 和 MRMR 基准测试中，ReLoop-UME 在不同的骨干网络上均持续提升了检索性能，同时运行速度比 UME-R1 快 44.9 倍，比 PLUME 快 1.5 倍。
