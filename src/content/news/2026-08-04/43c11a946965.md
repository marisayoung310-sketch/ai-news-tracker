# Hierarchical Copula-Gumbel-Top-$K$ Routing: Two-Sided Dependence Control for Frozen Mixture-of-Experts at Fixed Per-Token Routing Laws

**Hierarchical Copula-Gumbel-Top-$K$ 路由：在固定单 Token 路由规则下对冻结混合专家模型进行双向依赖控制**

***

**Abstract:** A stochastic Gumbel-Top-$K$ router defines, for every token of a mixture-of-experts (MoE) model, a \emph{routing law}: a distribution over ordered expert lists and mixture weights. We ask which \emph{joint} distributions over the routing choices of different tokens are reachable while every individual token's complete routing law is held exactly fixed.

**摘要：** 随机 Gumbel-Top-$K$ 路由器为混合专家（MoE）模型的每个 Token 定义了一种“路由规则”（routing law）：即关于有序专家列表和混合权重的分布。我们探讨了在保持每个 Token 的完整路由规则完全不变的前提下，不同 Token 路由选择的哪些“联合”分布是可实现的。

***

We give a two-sided construction, \emph{Hierarchical Copula-Gumbel-Top-$K$} (\CGA{}). Within a group of related tokens, an exchangeable Gaussian copula positively correlates the Gumbel perturbations at each expert coordinate, which can increase within-group expert-set coherence. Across disjoint pairs of groups, a tunable antithetic construction introduces a selectable amount of negative dependence.

我们提出了一种双向构建方法，即“分层 Copula-Gumbel-Top-$K$”（CGA）。在相关 Token 组内，可交换高斯 Copula 对每个专家坐标处的 Gumbel 扰动进行正相关处理，这可以增强组内专家集合的一致性。在不相交的组对之间，一种可调的对偶构建引入了可选择的负相关量。

***

We prove that both operations leave each token's ordered Top-$K$ sample, mixture weights, and inclusion probabilities identical in distribution to independent routing \emph{at a routing layer conditioned on its pre-routing logits}; conditional expected expert traffic is preserved as a consequence.

我们证明，这两种操作使得每个 Token 的有序 Top-$K$ 采样、混合权重和包含概率，在“以预路由 Logits 为条件的路由层”上，其分布与独立路由完全相同；因此，条件期望的专家流量得以保持。

***

We characterize the resulting trade-off: positive within-group coupling can only inflate the variance of realized expert loads relative to independent routing, while nonnegative cross-group opposition can only reduce it relative to flat coupling at the same within-group strength. Coherence and load dispersion are thus controlled by two complementary dependence dials on the invariance constraint surface.

我们刻画了由此产生的权衡：与独立路由相比，组内正耦合只会增加已实现专家负载的方差，而与相同组内强度的平坦耦合相比，非负的跨组对立只会降低该方差。因此，一致性和负载离散度可以通过不变性约束面上的两个互补依赖调节旋钮来控制。

***

Because the base model is untouched, the dials can be driven by a small controller over frozen features, trainable with a score-function estimator: the frozen network is evaluated only in the forward direction, and gradients are confined to the controller. An initial small-scale pilot validates the mechanism and the training route, but does not establish task-level fine-tuning gains.

由于基础模型未被触动，这些旋钮可以由一个基于冻结特征的小型控制器驱动，并可通过分数函数估计器进行训练：冻结网络仅在正向传播中进行评估，梯度仅限于控制器。初步的小规模试验验证了该机制和训练路径，但尚未确立任务级的微调增益。
