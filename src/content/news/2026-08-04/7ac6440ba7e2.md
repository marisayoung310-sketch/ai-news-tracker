# Samsung’s 2TB 9100 Pro SSD is actually somewhat reasonably priced
# 三星 2TB 9100 Pro SSD 价格终于变得合理了一些

Next to buying RAM, finding a fast, high-capacity NVMe SSD at a reasonable price has been a challenge during RAMageddon. I don’t want to say it’s getting easier, but one of Samsung’s latest SSDs is cheaper than it has sold for since February 2026. That’s something, right? Amazon has the heatsink-less version of the Samsung 9100 Pro with 2TB of storage for $302.99, which is about $100 less than the usual price. Granted, it sold for a low of $169.99 back in October 2025, but it’s a smart purchase right now compared to buying the 1TB model for $250.

在“内存危机”（RAMageddon）期间，除了购买内存条之外，以合理的价格找到一款高速、大容量的 NVMe SSD 一直是个难题。我不敢说情况正在好转，但三星最新款 SSD 之一的价格已降至 2026 年 2 月以来的最低点。这总算是个好消息，对吧？亚马逊目前正在销售不带散热片的 2TB 三星 9100 Pro，售价为 302.99 美元，比通常价格便宜了约 100 美元。诚然，它在 2025 年 10 月曾创下 169.99 美元的历史低价，但与目前 250 美元的 1TB 型号相比，现在入手 2TB 版本显然更划算。

The 9100 Pro is Samsung’s first SSD to support PCIe Gen 5, making it a great fit if you have the right motherboard and other parts to get as much speed from it as possible. Its theoretical maximum random read and write speeds are at least twice as fast as the 990 Pro SSD that it replaced. However, it’s good (albeit slower than advertised) in a PCIe Gen 4 setup, as well.

9100 Pro 是三星首款支持 PCIe Gen 5 的 SSD。如果你拥有合适的母板和其他硬件，能够充分发挥其速度潜力，那么它将是一个绝佳的选择。其理论最大随机读写速度至少是它所取代的 990 Pro SSD 的两倍。当然，即使在 PCIe Gen 4 环境下，它的表现依然出色（尽管速度会低于标称值）。

It’ll work fine without a heatsink in a PC or laptop (though, it’s never a bad idea to use one that fits in your system). However, you’ll need to install a heatsink in order to use this in a PlayStation 5, due to potential heat issues that could damage the SSD if used without one. Thankfully, heatsinks are super affordable, and come in a range of designs and price points.

在台式机或笔记本电脑中，即使不加装散热片，它也能正常工作（不过，为系统配备一个合适的散热片总归不是坏事）。但如果你打算将其用于 PlayStation 5，则必须安装散热片，因为潜在的过热问题可能会在没有散热保护的情况下损坏 SSD。好在散热片非常便宜，且有多种设计和价位可供选择。
