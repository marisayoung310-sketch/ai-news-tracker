# Histopathological Spectrum-Guided Prostate Stratification via Segmentation-Assisted Diagnostic Transformer
# 基于组织病理学谱引导与分割辅助诊断 Transformer 的前列腺分层研究

**Abstract:** Prostate cancer diagnosis with multiparametric MRI (mpMRI) is commonly based on PI-RADS assessment or binary classification, which suffer from subjectivity and fail to capture clinically relevant pathological heterogeneity. 

**摘要：** 基于多参数磁共振成像（mpMRI）的前列腺癌诊断通常依赖于 PI-RADS 评估或二分类方法，这些方法往往存在主观性，且无法捕捉到具有临床意义的病理异质性。

To address this limitation, we construct a Prostate Cancer Histopathology Spectrum Dataset (PCa-HSD) and formulate a clinically meaningful four-class classification task, addressing the underrepresentation of benign lesions that are easily confounded with prostate cancer in existing datasets. 

为了解决这一局限性，我们构建了前列腺癌组织病理学谱数据集（PCa-HSD），并制定了一项具有临床意义的四分类任务，旨在解决现有数据集中容易与前列腺癌混淆的良性病变样本代表性不足的问题。

We propose Language-guided Segmentation-assisted Diagnostic Transformer model (LSDT), which leverages zero-shot segmentation to provide anatomical priors and performs effective multi-modal slice fusion for classification. 

我们提出了语言引导的分割辅助诊断 Transformer 模型（LSDT），该模型利用零样本分割提供解剖学先验，并执行有效的多模态切片融合以进行分类。

Our proposed method consistently improves accuracy across backbones, achieving the best average accuracy of 0.633 and JointRecall of 0.768 in five-fold cross-validation on a cohort of 344 patients. 

我们提出的方法在不同骨干网络上均能持续提升准确率，在 344 名患者的队列中，五折交叉验证达到了 0.633 的最佳平均准确率和 0.768 的联合召回率（JointRecall）。

These results demonstrate that integrating pathology supervision and anatomical priors significantly enhances fine-grained prostate MRI classification and provides a more clinically relevant paradigm for risk stratification. Code will be made publicly available in a future revision.

这些结果表明，整合病理监督和解剖学先验能够显著增强前列腺 MRI 的细粒度分类效果，并为风险分层提供了一种更具临床相关性的范式。相关代码将在未来的修订版本中公开。
