# LowAux-RDNet: Low-Pass Residual Supervision with Scene-Balanced Real-World Training for Single-Image Reflection Removal

**LowAux-RDNet：用于单图像去反射的低通残差监督与场景平衡真实世界训练**

***

**Abstract:** Single-image reflection removal aims to recover a clean transmission layer from one image captured through glass. We study an explicit decomposition pipeline built on RDNet and introduce LowAux, a training-only low-pass reflection auxiliary objective. The original residual target remains the main reflection supervision, while symmetrically filtered prediction and target provide a stable low-frequency constraint.

**摘要：** 单图像去反射旨在从透过玻璃拍摄的图像中恢复出清晰的透射层。我们研究了一种基于 RDNet 构建的显式分解流水线，并引入了 LowAux——一种仅用于训练阶段的低通反射辅助目标。原始残差目标依然作为主要的反射监督，同时通过对称滤波后的预测结果与目标，提供了一种稳定的低频约束。

***

We further incorporate scene-balanced real pairs from RRW to broaden real-scene coverage and improve cross-dataset generalization. To avoid evaluation discrepancies caused by model-specific resizing, padding, output quantization, and metric code, we build a unified public benchmark over CEILNet, Real20, Postcard, Objects, and Wild.

我们进一步整合了来自 RRW 的场景平衡真实图像对，以扩大真实场景的覆盖范围并提升跨数据集的泛化能力。为了避免因模型特定的缩放、填充、输出量化及度量代码差异导致的评估偏差，我们基于 CEILNet、Real20、Postcard、Objects 和 Wild 构建了一个统一的公共基准。

***

Under the same evaluator, the proposed system obtains a five-dataset macro average of 27.546 dB PSNR, 0.9220 SSIM, 0.9751 NCC, and 0.004760 LMSE, achieving the highest macro-average PSNR, SSIM, and NCC and the lowest LMSE among the compared public checkpoints and internal variants. Per-dataset and qualitative analyses show that the main benefit is a more balanced performance across diverse reflection distributions, while clear semantic reflections in Postcard remain challenging.

在相同的评估器下，该系统在五个数据集上的宏平均指标达到了 27.546 dB PSNR、0.9220 SSIM、0.9751 NCC 和 0.004760 LMSE，在所有对比的公共检查点和内部变体中，实现了最高的宏平均 PSNR、SSIM 和 NCC，以及最低的 LMSE。分数据集分析和定性分析表明，其主要优势在于对不同反射分布表现出更均衡的性能，但 Postcard 数据集中清晰的语义反射仍具有挑战性。
