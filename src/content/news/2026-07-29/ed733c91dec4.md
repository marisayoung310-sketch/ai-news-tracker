# 5 ways AI Mode in Search helps you enjoy the real world
# 搜索中的 AI 模式助你享受现实世界的 5 种方式

Here’s how Search's AI tools can help you make the most of your time offline, whether you want to book concert tickets or find the perfect hiking boots.
无论你是想预订演唱会门票，还是寻找完美的登山靴，搜索（Search）的 AI 工具都能帮助你充分利用线下时光。

Google’s AI Mode helps you spend more time offline by simplifying how you plan activities and events. You can use it to find local classes that fit your schedule, shop for specific gear, practice new hobbies, and book tickets. Try connecting your apps to generate custom designs or organize your calendar so you can focus on enjoying the real world.
谷歌的 AI 模式通过简化活动和事件的规划流程，帮助你花更多时间在线下。你可以利用它寻找符合你日程的本地课程、选购特定装备、练习新爱好以及预订门票。尝试连接你的应用程序来生成自定义设计或整理日历，这样你就能专注于享受现实世界。

People are searching for more and more ways to get offline, experience the real world, and “touch grass.” This year, people are searching for “adult tennis lessons,” “trail running,” “hiking,” and “run club” more than ever before. People are also searching for questions like “how to digital detox” (+110% since the beginning of the year) and “how to meet people my age near me.”
人们正越来越多地寻找离线、体验现实世界和“回归自然”（touch grass）的方式。今年，人们对“成人网球课”、“越野跑”、“徒步旅行”和“跑步俱乐部”的搜索量达到了前所未有的高度。人们还在搜索诸如“如何进行数字排毒”（自年初以来增长了 110%）以及“如何结识附近同龄人”等问题。

To make the most of your IRL fun, AI Mode in Google Search has the tools to help you find the information you need. Here's how:
为了让你在线下的乐趣中获得最大收获，谷歌搜索中的 AI 模式提供了多种工具来帮助你找到所需信息。具体方法如下：

**1. Find local classes that fit into your schedule.**
**1. 寻找符合你日程的本地课程。**

Ready to try your hand at something new but don’t know where to begin? Use AI Mode to kick things off by asking something like, "How can I get started playing tennis? Are there beginner clinics or lessons nearby that I could easily join?" Personal Intelligence allows you to securely connect the dots across your Google apps so that AI Mode’s responses are even more personalized to you. For example, say you’re looking to get better at cooking and want to find classes that’d easily fit into your schedule next month. If you’ve chosen to connect Google Calendar, you’ll get suggestions that fit your existing schedule.
准备尝试新事物却不知从何下手？使用 AI 模式开启你的第一步，试着问：“我该如何开始打网球？附近有什么我可以轻松参加的初学者训练营或课程吗？”个人智能（Personal Intelligence）功能允许你安全地关联各个谷歌应用，使 AI 模式的回复更加个性化。例如，假设你想提高厨艺，并希望找到下个月能轻松安排进日程的课程。如果你选择了关联谷歌日历，你将获得符合你现有日程的建议。

**2. Find the exact gear you need.**
**2. 寻找你所需的精准装备。**

Getting outdoors is a great way to disconnect, but for activities like hiking and trail running, you want to make sure you’re fully prepared before you head out. You can easily shop within AI Mode to make sure you have the items to exactly fit your needs. Try asking, "Can you help me find some affordable hiking boots with good ankle support?" AI Mode will help you choose between products that match what you’re looking for. You can also see what’s in stock in the area by including “nearby” or “near me” in your search, and even ask Google to call nearby stores to get product availability for you.
户外活动是放松身心的好方法，但对于徒步和越野跑等活动，出发前务必做好充分准备。你可以轻松在 AI 模式内购物，确保买到完全符合你需求的装备。试着问：“你能帮我找一些价格实惠且脚踝支撑性好的登山靴吗？”AI 模式将帮助你在符合要求的商品中进行选择。你还可以通过在搜索中加入“附近”或“near me”来查看当地库存，甚至可以让谷歌代你致电附近的商店查询产品是否有货。

**3. Perfect your strategy.**
**3. 完善你的策略。**

If you’re getting into tabletop games this year, you can use Canvas in AI Mode to create a guide to help you memorize some of the core playing strategies. Head to AI Mode, tap the plus menu, select Canvas, then ask it something like, “I know the basics of playing chess and what the different pieces do, but I want to up my game. Make a guide with a few easy to remember strategies I can start using immediately to get an edge when I play my friends.” Once you’ve mastered key moves, you can even use Canvas to practice by building a game simulation where you play against a computer.
如果你今年开始接触桌面游戏，可以使用 AI 模式中的 Canvas 功能创建一个指南，帮助你记忆一些核心玩法策略。前往 AI 模式，点击加号菜单，选择 Canvas，然后问它：“我了解国际象棋的基础知识和棋子的走法，但我想提升水平。请制作一份指南，包含一些我能立即上手、在与朋友对弈时占据优势的简单策略。”一旦掌握了关键招式，你甚至可以使用 Canvas 构建游戏模拟，通过与电脑对弈来进行练习。

**4. Find and book event tickets.**
**4. 查找并预订活动门票。**

If you like spending your time offline by attending live events, use AI Mode to help you find and book tickets for nearby events, like concerts and sports. Share details like your budget, how many tickets you want, and what event you’d like to go to. AI Mode will curate a list of options, so you can finalize your booking at your preferred platform to secure your spot.
如果你喜欢通过参加现场活动来度过线下时光，可以使用 AI 模式来查找并预订附近的活动门票，例如演唱会和体育赛事。提供你的预算、所需票数以及想参加的活动等详细信息，AI 模式将为你整理出一份选项清单，以便你在首选平台上完成预订并锁定席位。

**5. Design a dinner party invite.**
**5. 设计晚餐派对邀请函。**

Hosting a dinner party is a great way to put your phone away and spend quality time with friends. By connecting some of your go-to apps, like Canva, to Search, you can easily turn any inspiration into an editable design all in one space. Simply try a prompt like, "Create a flyer in Canva for the party I’m hosting in two weeks. Make it modern minimalistic style." Canva will generate a custom design right in your Search results. You can add finishing design touches in Canva, or easily download it, and send it out. Then you can get back to planning your tablescape design.
举办晚餐派对是放下手机、与朋友共度美好时光的好方法。通过将 Canva 等常用应用连接到搜索，你可以轻松地将任何灵感转化为可编辑的设计，一切都在同一个界面完成。只需尝试输入提示词，例如：“在 Canva 中为我两周后举办的派对创建一个传单，风格要现代简约。”Canva 会直接在搜索结果中生成自定义设计。你可以在 Canva 中进行最后的润色，或者直接下载并发送。然后，你就可以继续规划你的餐桌布置了。
