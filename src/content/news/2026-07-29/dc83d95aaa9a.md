# Dyson V16 Piston Animal Submarine Review: Powerful but Pricey
# 戴森 V16 Piston Animal Submarine 评测：性能强劲但价格昂贵

**WIRED Rating: 7/10**
**WIRED 评分：7/10**

**TIRED:** Higher suction doesn't make a crazy impact on regular vacuuming. Submarine head is also an add-on accessory, and it doesn't come with Dyson's cleaning solution.
**不足之处：** 更高的吸力对日常清洁并没有产生质的飞跃。Submarine 湿拖头属于附加配件，且包装内不包含戴森专用的清洁液。

**WIRED:** Comes with two cleaner heads, or three if you upgrade to the Submarine variant. Has crevice tools built into the wand and handheld motor. Packs 315 AW of suction.
**优点：** 配备两个清洁吸头，升级至 Submarine 版本则有三个。手持电机和延长杆上均内置了缝隙吸头。拥有 315 AW 的强劲吸力。

Dyson's newest headliner is here: the Dyson V16 Piston Animal, along with a Submarine version that includes a wet cleaner head. The V16 is now Dyson’s most powerful stick vacuum, unseating the Gen5Detect ($840), which held that title since 2023. This new model reports 315 air-watts (AW) of power, which is a big step up from the Gen5's 262 AW. The latest update also includes a new style of cleaner head designed for vacuuming both soft and hard floors—the All Floor Cones Sense cleaner head—which expertly tackled all floor types and debris during my testing. But is newer and more powerful always better? While the Dyson V16 is an all-around great vacuum, I’m not sold on that being the case.
戴森最新的旗舰产品已经登场：Dyson V16 Piston Animal，以及包含湿拖吸头的 Submarine 版本。V16 目前是戴森吸力最强的无线吸尘器，取代了自 2023 年以来一直占据该头衔的 Gen5Detect（售价 840 美元）。这款新机型的吸力达到 315 空气瓦特（AW），较 Gen5 的 262 AW 有了显著提升。此次更新还包括一款专为软硬地板设计的全新清洁吸头——All Floor Cones Sense 吸头，在我的测试中，它能出色地处理各种地面类型和碎屑。但更新、更强就一定更好吗？虽然 Dyson V16 是一款全能且出色的吸尘器，但我对此持保留意见。

With the new cleaner head, the Submarine variant for mopping, plus the Fluffy Optic head for hard floors, the V16 has better cleaning options than any other vacuum from the brand. But the older Gen5Detect still holds up against the V16—and will cost you much less. Ultimately, the V16 Piston Animal is the vacuum to get if you really want all of the add-ons and power. But if you just want a great Dyson vacuum, there are plenty of cheaper options.
凭借全新的清洁吸头、用于拖地的 Submarine 版本以及针对硬地板的 Fluffy Optic 吸头，V16 提供了戴森品牌中最为丰富的清洁方案。然而，老款的 Gen5Detect 在面对 V16 时依然表现不俗，且价格要便宜得多。归根结底，如果你追求所有的附加功能和极致性能，V16 Piston Animal 是你的不二之选；但如果你只是想要一台好用的戴森吸尘器，市面上还有许多更具性价比的选择。

### New Vac on the Block
### 吸尘器界的新成员

The Dyson V16 Piston Animal (and its Submarine variant, the model I'm testing) arrives five years after the previous model in its line, the V15 Detect. The V16’s “Animal” designation—a term long associated with Dyson—refers to the Piston’s specialized hair-screw attachment for cleaning pet hair. Its biggest change, though, is its new All Floor Cones Sense cleaner head that adapts to different types of flooring, responding in about a second when you switch from hard floors to carpet or a rug.
Dyson V16 Piston Animal（以及我正在测试的 Submarine 版本）是在其系列前作 V15 Detect 发布五年后推出的。V16 的“Animal”命名——这是戴森长期沿用的术语——指的是 Piston 配备的专门用于清理宠物毛发的防缠绕螺旋吸头。不过，它最大的变化在于全新的 All Floor Cones Sense 清洁吸头，它能适应不同类型的地面，当你从硬地板切换到地毯或垫子时，它能在约一秒钟内做出响应。

You’ll know it recognizes the surface correctly because on hard floors a green dust light turns on, and on soft floors, you’ll hear the suction power increase. This function worked well for me as I moved around between rugs and hard floors in my home. I also tested how well the vacuum could handle various debris I put in its path, but the All Floor Cones Sense head didn't always outperform the older Motorbar head.
当它正确识别地面时，你会发现硬地板上会亮起绿色灰尘探测灯，而在软地板上，你会听到吸力自动增强。在我家中往返于地毯和硬地板之间时，这项功能表现良好。我还测试了该吸尘器处理各种碎屑的能力，但 All Floor Cones Sense 吸头的表现并不总是优于老款的 Motorbar 吸头。

The Piston has Eco, Auto, and Boost modes. During testing, Auto mode adjusted based on the debris it sensed for either hard or soft flooring. You can control these modes using the silver button on the built-in LCD screen. Notably, Dyson released three new stick vacuum models this summer (the V10 Konical and the V8 Cyclone also dropped in June), and the Piston is the only one with a screen.
Piston 拥有节能（Eco）、自动（Auto）和强效（Boost）模式。在测试中，自动模式会根据感应到的碎屑情况，针对硬地板或软地板进行调节。你可以通过内置 LCD 屏幕上的银色按钮来控制这些模式。值得注意的是，戴森今年夏天发布了三款新的无线吸尘器（V10 Konical 和 V8 Cyclone 也在六月推出），而 Piston 是其中唯一配备屏幕的机型。

Among its other highlights, the V16 also has two built-in crevice tools: one at the end of the handheld motor and another at the end of the detachable wand. These tools are shorter and wider than the crevice attachment head that you’ll also get with this vacuum, but the built-in capability is handy for addressing a specific spot quickly, without needing to hunt down all of your accessories.
V16 的其他亮点还包括两个内置的缝隙吸头：一个位于手持电机末端，另一个位于可拆卸延长杆末端。这些工具比你随机器附赠的缝隙吸头更短、更宽，但这种内置设计非常方便，可以快速清理特定区域，无需到处翻找配件。

There's a dust-compactor tool built into the dust bin, which aims to help you get out the debris. But I still had to grab and yank off fluffy debris from the dustbin. There's also a handy little red tube you can tug on to release the vacuum head without needing to bend down or lift the entire vacuum, which is a feature I really like.
集尘桶内内置了一个灰尘压缩装置，旨在帮助你清理碎屑。但我仍然需要动手去抠掉桶内蓬松的灰尘。此外，还有一个非常实用的小红管，你可以拉动它来释放吸头，而无需弯腰或抬起整个吸尘器，这是我非常喜欢的一个功能。

### Head to Head
### 性能对决

The V16 Piston Animal’s 315 AW makes it inarguably powerful, but I didn't find that its higher suction capability necessarily outperformed the rest of Dyson's lineup. The initial, early-release V16 model I tested had some clogging issues, but I've since retested a current version, and I didn't have the same problem. The V16 and its All Floor Cones Sense cleaner head had nice suction on my thick rug and bedroom carpets. Despite my having vacuumed the week prior, the powerful V16 still filled itself with dust and hair after covering two small bedrooms and a couple of stairs.
V16 Piston Animal 的 315 AW 吸力无疑非常强劲，但我并不认为其更高的吸力一定能超越戴森的其他产品线。我最初测试的早期版本 V16 存在一些堵塞问题，但后来我重新测试了当前版本，并没有出现同样的情况。V16 及其 All Floor Cones Sense 吸头在我的厚地毯和卧室地毯上表现出了出色的吸力。尽管我前一周刚吸过尘，但 V16 在清理完两个小卧室和几级楼梯后，依然吸满了灰尘和毛发。

The V16 did a great job overall, but testing it alongside the older (and cheaper) Gen5Detect proved the two models mostly worked equally. In some cases, the Gen5Detect even outpaced the V16, which had mixed results vacuuming sand, struggling to catch more than half on a thinner rug, and pushing some sand into a pile in front of itself on a thicker one. It did a similar thing with cat litter on my vinyl plank floors. A second pass with the vacuum caught everything, but I'd prefer to see such an expensive machine get it right on the first try. That’s especially true considering cheaper vacuums, like the Shark PowerDetect Speed ($430), did so with no problem.
总体而言，V16 表现出色，但将其与更老（也更便宜）的 Gen5Detect 进行对比测试后发现，两者的表现基本持平。在某些情况下，Gen5Detect 的表现甚至超过了 V16。V16 在清理沙子时表现参差不齐，在较薄的地毯上难以吸走一半以上的沙子，而在较厚的地毯上则会将部分沙子推成一堆。在清理乙烯基板地板上的猫砂时，它也出现了类似的情况。虽然第二遍清理可以吸干净，但我更希望这样昂贵的机器能一次搞定。考虑到像 Shark PowerDetect Speed（430 美元）这样更便宜的吸尘器都能轻松做到这一点，这一点尤为重要。

While I like the included Fluffy Optic cleaner head, you don't necessarily need it, since the All Floor Cones Sense head also works on hard floors. Whichever cleaning attachment you prefer, the built-in release on the vacuum's tube makes it easy to switch cleaner heads without bending over. Still, when I'm vacuuming on my top floor, where there are carpeted bedrooms and hard flooring in the hallways and bathrooms, I just stick to the All Floor Cones Sense head for ease. It's nice having a single head that can do a good job with both.
虽然我很喜欢附赠的 Fluffy Optic 清洁吸头，但你并不一定需要它，因为 All Floor Cones Sense 吸头同样适用于硬地板。无论你偏好哪种清洁吸头，吸尘器管上的内置释放装置都能让你轻松切换，无需弯腰。不过，当我在顶楼吸尘时（那里有铺地毯的卧室，走廊和浴室则是硬地板），我还是倾向于只用 All Floor Cones Sense 吸头，因为这样更省事。能有一个既能搞定地毯又能搞定硬地板的吸头，感觉确实不错。

The Submarine version of the V16 that comes with the wet cleaning head costs an additional $120, and if you need to mop, it's worth the upgrade. The Submarine version of the V16 Piston Animal impressed me much more than I expected. When you use the wet head, the LCD screen switches to give you two modes—Basic and Max—and I found the Basic option to be plenty powerful for scrubbing dirty.
配备湿拖吸头的 V16 Submarine 版本需要额外支付 120 美元，如果你有拖地需求，这个升级是值得的。V16 Piston Animal 的 Submarine 版本给我的印象远超预期。当你使用湿拖吸头时，LCD 屏幕会切换为两种模式——基础（Basic）和强效（Max），我发现基础模式在清理污垢时已经足够强劲。
