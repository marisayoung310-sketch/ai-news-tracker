# MCP startup Runlayer accuses Rippling of stealing its product idea
# MCP 初创公司 Runlayer 指控 Rippling 窃取其产品创意

Runlayer, a startup that offers a secure Model Context Protocol gateway — a standard for letting AI models and agents securely pull in outside data and tools — has filed a lawsuit against HR software startup Rippling, according to the complaint seen by TechCrunch.
据 TechCrunch 查阅的诉状显示，提供安全模型上下文协议（Model Context Protocol，简称 MCP）网关的初创公司 Runlayer 已对人力资源软件初创公司 Rippling 提起诉讼。MCP 是一项旨在让 AI 模型和智能体安全获取外部数据和工具的标准。

The lawsuit is a cautionary tale for anyone selling AI infrastructure to enterprise customers, especially to other tech companies, that increasingly have the engineering muscle to just build the thing themselves.
这起诉讼为所有向企业客户（尤其是那些日益具备自主研发能力的科技公司）销售 AI 基础设施的厂商敲响了警钟。

In the suit, Runlayer describes an extensive product trial conducted by Rippling as a prospective customer, during which the MCP startup shared everything from its product roadmap to its actual source code. The parties signed a mutual non-disclosure agreement and Rippling signed a product trial agreement with a clause that forbade it from copying Runlayer’s intellectual property or making derivative works, which is standard boilerplate in enterprise software trials.
在诉状中，Runlayer 描述了 Rippling 作为潜在客户进行的一次深度产品试用。在此期间，这家 MCP 初创公司分享了从产品路线图到实际源代码的所有内容。双方签署了互不泄露协议（NDA），Rippling 还签署了一份产品试用协议，其中包含禁止复制 Runlayer 知识产权或制作衍生作品的条款，这在企业软件试用中属于标准格式条款。

Runlayer says in the complaint that Rippling’s evaluation involved “nearly a year of intensive engineering collaboration.” But in the end, the two could not agree on a price, so Runlayer ended the product trial. Shortly after that, Runlayer alleges that a “Rippling insider” texted Runlayer founder and CEO Andrew Berman to inform him of “a project internally to build essentially a clone o[f] Runlayer … it’s almost a 1 to 1 copy of Runlayer.”
Runlayer 在诉状中称，Rippling 的评估过程涉及“近一年的密集工程协作”。但最终双方未能就价格达成一致，Runlayer 因此终止了产品试用。此后不久，Runlayer 声称一名“Rippling 内部人士”给 Runlayer 创始人兼首席执行官 Andrew Berman 发短信，告知他公司内部有一个项目，“本质上是在构建一个 Runlayer 的克隆版……几乎是 1 比 1 的复制品。”

Runlayer claims in the suit that Rippling’s product must have been based on the startup’s intellectual property and therefore constitutes trade secret misappropriation, unfair competition, and breach of contract.
Runlayer 在诉讼中主张，Rippling 的产品必然是基于该初创公司的知识产权构建的，因此构成了商业秘密盗用、不正当竞争和违约。

Rippling has confirmed to TechCrunch that it is indeed launching its own MCP gateway, though a spokesperson denies Runlayer’s allegations about misusing its IP. “Runlayer’s panicked effort to avoid competition by fabricating claims is not an effective way to deal with its business failures. Rippling is launching a superior product for connecting AI tools to business data using only our proprietary information — we have every reason to win in this market,” a Rippling spokesperson tells TechCrunch.
Rippling 向 TechCrunch 证实，其确实正在推出自己的 MCP 网关，但发言人否认了 Runlayer 关于滥用其知识产权的指控。Rippling 发言人告诉 TechCrunch：“Runlayer 为了逃避竞争而捏造指控，这种恐慌行为并非应对业务失败的有效方式。Rippling 正在推出一款更出色的产品，用于将 AI 工具连接到业务数据，且仅使用我们自己的专有信息——我们完全有理由在这个市场上胜出。”

Runlayer has retained white-shoe law firm Sullivan & Cromwell. That doesn’t mean Runlayer will, or even should, win this suit, but the same way a marquee VC lends a startup some credibility, a marquee law firm lends a lawsuit some credibility, at least optically.
Runlayer 已聘请了顶级律所 Sullivan & Cromwell。这并不意味着 Runlayer 一定会（或应该）赢得这场诉讼，但正如知名风投能为初创公司带来信誉一样，知名律所也能为诉讼带来一定的可信度，至少在观感上是如此。

The more interesting part about this suit is really the inside peek it provides at the trials and tribulations of selling complex AI infrastructure into the enterprise, particularly to other tech companies. Enterprise sales notoriously take a long time to close, often because they hinge on this kind of deep, hands-on trial.
这起诉讼更有趣的地方在于，它让我们得以一窥向企业（尤其是其他科技公司）销售复杂 AI 基础设施所面临的种种磨难。众所周知，企业销售的成交周期很长，往往是因为它们依赖于这种深入的、亲身实践的试用过程。

MCP gateways in particular are getting crowded. Anthropic launched MCP as an open source protocol in November 2024. It’s now one of the basic building blocks of AI interoperability, giving models and agents a secure way to access external data sources and services. MCP gateway products add control, security, and other features, especially for managing agents, and the field has grown considerably more competitive since Runlayer launched its product in the middle of last year and raised a total of $42 million, including from Khosla Ventures and Felicis.
MCP 网关领域正变得日益拥挤。Anthropic 于 2024 年 11 月发布了作为开源协议的 MCP。它现在已成为 AI 互操作性的基本构建模块之一，为模型和智能体提供了一种安全访问外部数据源和服务的方式。MCP 网关产品增加了控制、安全和其他功能，特别是在管理智能体方面。自 Runlayer 在去年年中推出产品并筹集了总计 4200 万美元（包括来自 Khosla Ventures 和 Felicis 的投资）以来，该领域的竞争已显著加剧。

Even after an intense trial, an enterprise may simply opt to build the tool in-house. Both sides are stuck between a rock and a hard place.
即使经过了激烈的试用，企业也可能最终选择自主研发该工具。双方目前都陷入了进退两难的境地。
