### Vehicle Motion Cues

**Vehicle Motion Cues can help reduce motion sickness for passengers in moving vehicles. When this feature is on, small dots appear on the edges of the screen that move with the vehicle to represent changes in motion, helping to reduce sensory conflict without interfering with the content on the screen.**

车辆运动提示（Vehicle Motion Cues）功能可以帮助减轻乘客在行驶车辆中的晕车感。当此功能开启时，屏幕边缘会出现小圆点，这些圆点会随车辆运动而移动，以呈现运动的变化，从而在不干扰屏幕内容的情况下帮助减少感官冲突。

**Go to Settings > Accessibility > Motion, then tap Vehicle Motion Cues.**

前往“设置” > “辅助功能” > “动态效果”，然后点击“车辆运动提示”。

**Choose from the following options:**
*   **Off:** Vehicle Motion Cues are hidden.
*   **Automatic:** Vehicle Motion Cues appear automatically when the iPhone detects you are in a moving vehicle.
*   **On:** Vehicle Motion Cues are always shown.

**您可以从以下选项中进行选择：**
*   **关闭：** 隐藏车辆运动提示。
*   **自动：** 当 iPhone 检测到您处于行驶中的车辆时，车辆运动提示会自动显示。
*   **开启：** 始终显示车辆运动提示。
