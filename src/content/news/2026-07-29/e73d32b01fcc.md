# The union drive at the Wikimedia Foundation is expanding
# 维基媒体基金会的工会运动正在扩大

In June, UK staff at the nonprofit that runs Wikipedia became the first to announce their intentions to form a union. Now, US-based employees at the Wikimedia Foundation are joining the union drive, but management at the nonprofit have declined to voluntarily recognize their union.
今年 6 月，运营维基百科的这家非营利组织的英国员工率先宣布了成立工会的意向。如今，维基媒体基金会（Wikimedia Foundation）的美国员工也加入了这场工会运动，但该非营利组织的管理层已拒绝自愿承认其工会地位。

“While the Wikimedia Foundation touts to the public that they plan to take a neutral stance in our organizing efforts, they have already proven that not to be the case behind the scenes,” a press release sent by Communications Workers of America, with which Wiki Workers United is affiliated, reads in part. “When the Foundation rejected our request for voluntary recognition this week, they immediately sent out to employees and the public statements couched in classic union-busting rhetoric, rhetoric undoubtedly provided by outrageously expensive union-avoidance law firms. This insulting and wasteful use of Foundation resources is both an insult to Foundation employees and to the Free Knowledge Movement as a whole.”
“尽管维基媒体基金会向公众宣称他们计划在我们的组织工作中保持中立，但他们已经在幕后证明事实并非如此，”与“维基工会联合会”（Wiki Workers United）有关联的美国通信工人工会（Communications Workers of America）发布的一份新闻稿中写道，“本周，当基金会拒绝了我们自愿承认工会的请求时，他们立即向员工和公众发布了充斥着典型反工会言论的声明，这些言论无疑是由极其昂贵的反工会律师事务所提供的。这种对基金会资源的侮辱性和浪费性使用，既是对基金会员工的侮辱，也是对整个‘自由知识运动’的侮辱。”

In a public statement, the foundation said it believes a secret ballot election with the NLRB is the “appropriate path forward.”
在一份公开声明中，基金会表示，他们认为通过美国国家劳资关系委员会（NLRB）进行无记名投票选举是“合适的推进方式”。

The unionization efforts come at a time when tensions are high between the foundation and the thousands of unpaid volunteers who edit, write, and maintain Wikipedia. In May, hundreds of Wikipedia editors threatened to go on strike after the foundation dissolved an engineering team that worked closely with volunteers. Some volunteers questioned whether the layoffs were related to union efforts, which the foundation denied.
此次工会化努力正值基金会与数千名负责编辑、撰写和维护维基百科的无薪志愿者之间关系紧张之际。今年 5 月，在基金会解散了一个与志愿者密切合作的工程团队后，数百名维基百科编辑威胁要举行罢工。一些志愿者质疑裁员是否与工会活动有关，但基金会对此予以否认。
