### The Best Knife for Fancy Hiking / 徒步“精致生活”必备刀具

Opinel's classic folding knife practically begs to be taken on a hike with some good hard chorizo, soft Spanish cheese, and a nice baguette. It's the first step to hiking European style. Or at least how I imagine Europeans hike (always with delicious cheese, cured meats, and cheap wine—please don't ruin my vision, European readers). I got my Opinel as a gift from a friend, and I love it. And I am an outdoor person, so there is your living proof that this is a good gift.

Opinel 的经典折叠刀简直就是为了徒步时享用硬质西班牙香肠、软奶酪和法棍面包而生的。这是迈向“欧式徒步”的第一步。或者至少是我想象中欧洲人的徒步方式（总是带着美味的奶酪、腌肉和廉价葡萄酒——欧洲读者们，请别打破我的幻想）。我收到的第一把 Opinel 是朋友送的礼物，我非常喜欢。作为一个户外爱好者，这就是它作为礼物的最佳证明。

Opinel makes dozens of variations on this knife that you can pick from when gifting. Grab them a kit with a bento box, or a nature lover friend might like the version with handles designed by different artists. The one version I don't recommend is the carbon steel version. Carbon steel is great, but in this case, when you're throwing it in your backpack and possibly forgetting about it for long periods of time, carbon steel will rust. Stick with stainless steel when gifting this.

Opinel 为这款刀提供了几十种变体供你选择。你可以买一套带便当盒的套装，或者送给热爱自然的朋友那种由不同艺术家设计手柄的版本。唯一不推荐的是碳钢版本。碳钢虽然好，但在这种情况下，当你把它扔进背包并可能长时间遗忘时，碳钢会生锈。送礼时，请选择不锈钢版本。

### Gift Sun Protection / 防晒好礼

No matter what I’m doing outdoors, whether it’s hiking, running, kayaking, or even just sitting in a pool float, I somehow end up sweating off my sunscreen. Maybe that's just a me problem, but if you’ll be in the sun for an extended period of time, a wide-brimmed, packable hat is always a good idea. This lightweight yet sturdy one from Solbari is made of UPF 50+ nylon and cotton fabric that’s reversible.

无论我在户外做什么，是徒步、跑步、皮划艇，还是仅仅坐在泳池浮排上，我总会把防晒霜汗掉。也许这只是我个人的问题，但如果你要在阳光下待很长时间，一顶宽檐且可折叠的帽子永远是明智之选。这款来自 Solbari 的帽子轻便又结实，采用 UPF 50+ 的尼龙和棉质面料制成，而且是双面可戴的。

There’s a colorful side (navy, pink, ice blue, or green) and a cream side—I’ve found that turning it to the cream side when the sun is extra strong helps to deflect light and cool things down. It’s also got a handy hole in the back for ponytails and braids, and the 5-inch brim in the front and 6-inch brim in the rear are longer than most hats of this style, to ensure you’re well-covered. It’s adjustable for different head sizes via a toggle in the back, and there’s a detachable chin strap, though for some reason this addition makes me feel like an elderly river-rafting guide, so I leave it off. (Though if it’s windy, it’s going back on—just call me Class Five Cloris.) —Kat Merck

它有一面是彩色的（海军蓝、粉色、冰蓝色或绿色），另一面是奶油色——我发现当阳光特别强烈时，换到奶油色那一面有助于反射光线并降温。它后面还有一个方便扎马尾或辫子的孔，前檐 5 英寸、后檐 6 英寸的设计比大多数同类帽子更长，确保了良好的遮阳效果。它可以通过后部的调节扣适应不同头围，还有一个可拆卸的下巴带，虽然不知为何戴上它让我感觉像个上了年纪的漂流向导，所以我通常不戴。（不过如果风大，我还是会戴上的——叫我“五级激流克洛里斯”吧。）——Kat Merck

### Laws Guide to Nature Journaling / 自然笔记指南

This book changed the way I, and my kids, look at the world. I originally bought it because I thought my kids might like it (which they did), but then I stole it, read it cover to cover, bought a sketchbook, and, following along with the book, started to see the world in much more detail than I ever had before. Don't worry if your giftee is not an artist. I am the opposite of skilled when it comes to drawing, and I still love this book. More than drawing, what this book is teaching you is to look closely, observing and recording the world around you, and to form your own understanding of what you've witnessed.

这本书改变了我以及我的孩子们看待世界的方式。我最初买它是因为觉得孩子们可能会喜欢（他们确实喜欢），但后来我把它“据为己有”，从头到尾读了一遍，买了一本素描本，并跟着书中的指导，开始以比以往任何时候都更细致的眼光观察世界。别担心你的受赠者不是艺术家。我在绘画方面毫无天赋，但我依然热爱这本书。比起绘画，这本书教给你的是如何仔细观察、记录周围的世界，并对你所见之物形成自己的理解。
