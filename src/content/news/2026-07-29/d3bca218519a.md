### MIME: Multimodal Interactive Motion Encoder

**Abstract:**
Text-motion representation learning has advanced rapidly, with growing interest in multi-person interactions for animation, AR/VR, and embodied AI. These settings require representations that align language with both individual actor dynamics and the relationships between actors.

文本-动作表征学习发展迅速，人们对动画、AR/VR 和具身智能中的多人交互兴趣日益浓厚。这些场景要求表征能够将语言与个体动作动态以及参与者之间的关系进行对齐。

We introduce the Multimodal Interactive Motion Encoder (MIME), which, to our knowledge, represents the first dedicated multimodal encoder designed specifically for two-person interactive motion. MIME captures individual and shared structure using stream-based co-attention with explicit interaction features and curriculum-based contrastive training.

我们引入了多模态交互式动作编码器（MIME），据我们所知，这是首个专门为双人交互动作设计的多模态编码器。MIME 通过基于流的协同注意力机制（stream-based co-attention）、显式交互特征以及基于课程的对比学习（curriculum-based contrastive training）来捕捉个体和共享结构。

On Inter-X text-motion retrieval, MIME consistently outperforms early and late fusion baselines across gallery sizes, achieving a 12.8% relative improvement in text-to-motion R@1 at a 2,000-sample gallery. We further evaluate MIME as a frozen auxiliary prior within TIMotion and InterMask on the unseen InterHuman dataset.

在 Inter-X 文本-动作检索任务中，MIME 在不同规模的图库中均持续优于早期和晚期融合基线模型，在 2,000 个样本的图库中，文本到动作的 R@1 指标实现了 12.8% 的相对提升。我们进一步将 MIME 作为冻结的辅助先验，在未见过的 InterHuman 数据集上对 TIMotion 和 InterMask 进行了评估。

MIME improves semantic alignment metrics while maintaining comparable FID in TIMotion. These results show that interaction-aware multimodal encoding improves multi-person motion retrieval and transfers across datasets to support downstream motion generation.

MIME 在提升语义对齐指标的同时，在 TIMotion 中保持了相当的 FID（弗雷歇初始距离）。这些结果表明，具备交互感知能力的多模态编码能够改善多人动作检索，并可在不同数据集间迁移，从而支持下游的动作生成任务。
