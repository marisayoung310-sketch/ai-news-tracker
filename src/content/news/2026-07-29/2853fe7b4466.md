# Bot-detection startup Spur nabs $200M from Insight
# 机器人检测初创公司 Spur 获得 Insight 2 亿美元融资

Spur Intelligence, a cybersecurity startup based in Lake Mary, Florida, has raised a $200 million round led by Insight Partners. Spur, founded by two former Defense Department engineers in 2017 — five years before ChatGPT’s public launch — was prescient.
总部位于佛罗里达州玛丽湖（Lake Mary）的网络安全初创公司 Spur Intelligence 已完成 2 亿美元融资，由 Insight Partners 领投。Spur 由两名前国防部工程师于 2017 年创立——比 ChatGPT 公开发布早了五年——具有极高的前瞻性。

The startup’s tech helps enterprises distinguish legitimate human users from increasingly well-hidden bot traffic to help identify fake users and threats.
该初创公司的技术旨在帮助企业区分合法的真实用户与日益隐蔽的机器人流量，从而识别虚假用户和潜在威胁。

“As sophisticated criminal VPNs, residential proxy networks, and anonymization infrastructure proliferate, organizations are increasingly operating with a critical blind spot: they can see the activity, but not the infrastructure behind it,” Insight’s Thomas Krane said in a written statement.
Insight 的 Thomas Krane 在一份书面声明中表示：“随着复杂的犯罪 VPN、住宅代理网络和匿名化基础设施的激增，企业正面临一个关键的盲点：他们能看到活动，却无法洞察其背后的基础设施。”

Detecting malicious traffic has, of course, been a hill corporate security teams have been climbing for eons. But nothing compares to the onslaught facing them today. As of mid-2026, bots are now more active on the internet than humans are, Cloudflare reported last month.
当然，检测恶意流量一直是企业安全团队长期以来面临的挑战。但如今他们所面对的冲击是前所未有的。据 Cloudflare 上个月发布的报告显示，截至 2026 年年中，互联网上的机器人活跃度已超过人类。

“Thought it would be end of 2027, then early 2027, but agentic traffic growing so fast that bots have now passed human traffic online for the first time in the Internet’s history,” Cloudflare founder and CEO Matthew Prince posted on X last month, pointing to his company’s latest traffic report.
“原以为会是在 2027 年底，后来觉得是 2027 年初，但代理流量（agentic traffic）增长如此之快，以至于机器人流量在互联网历史上首次超过了人类流量，” Cloudflare 创始人兼首席执行官 Matthew Prince 上个月在 X 上发文称，并引用了公司最新的流量报告。
