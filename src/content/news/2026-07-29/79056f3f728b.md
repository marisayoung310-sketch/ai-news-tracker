# This comfy gaming headset that can play audio from two sources is $25
# 这款舒适的游戏耳机现价仅需 25 美元，且支持双音源同时播放

The EPOS H3 Hybrid combines wired and Bluetooth audio sources, all for less than the cost of a new game.
EPOS H3 Hybrid 结合了有线和蓝牙音频源，其价格甚至低于一款新游戏。

While most gaming headsets have moved towards low-latency wireless connections, there’s something to be said for a budget-friendly, capable wired headset. The EPOS H3 Hybrid is on sale for just $24.99 at Woot, a whopping 75 percent off its original price of $99.99.
虽然大多数游戏耳机已转向低延迟无线连接，但一款经济实惠且功能强大的有线耳机依然有其独特价值。EPOS H3 Hybrid 目前在 Woot 网站上仅售 24.99 美元，较 99.99 美元的原价有着高达 75% 的折扣。

It has an unorthodox batch of features: USB-A and 3.5mm options for connecting to a controller or a PC, and Bluetooth support for listening to music and taking calls. Notably, it can play audio from wired and wireless sources concurrently, and it can last up to 37 hours per charge.
它拥有一系列非同寻常的功能：提供 USB-A 和 3.5mm 接口以连接手柄或电脑，并支持蓝牙功能用于听音乐和接听电话。值得注意的是，它可以同时播放有线和无线音源，且单次充电续航可达 37 小时。

My colleague Cameron Faulkner spent some time with the wired-only version of this headset five years ago, and was impressed by its balanced sound profile, comfortable fit, and generously-sized volume knob. He had some qualms about USB connectivity and lack of sidetone, both of which seem to have been addressed in the Hybrid version of the headset that’s available at a steep discount right now.
我的同事 Cameron Faulkner 五年前曾体验过该耳机的纯有线版本，并对其均衡的音质表现、舒适的佩戴感以及宽大的音量旋钮印象深刻。他当时对 USB 连接和侧音（sidetone）缺失存有疑虑，而这些问题在目前大幅打折的 Hybrid 版本中似乎都已得到解决。

This version also has two microphones — a removable boom mic and a built-in microphone — so you can still take calls via Bluetooth without looking like an airline pilot.
该版本还配备了两个麦克风——一个可拆卸的悬臂麦克风和一个内置麦克风——因此你可以在通过蓝牙接听电话时，不必看起来像个飞行员。
