# Carrie is just trying to make a friend in the new trailer
# 在新预告片中，嘉莉只是想交个朋友

Mike Flanagan’s latest Stephen King adaptation, *Carrie* (this will be his fourth), is slated to make its debut on Amazon Prime on October 7th. The new trailer dropped at Comic-Con 2026 and doesn’t contain many surprises. If you’re familiar with *Carrie* at all, it hits all the expected notes. Though it’s clearly been updated for the modern era.

迈克·弗拉纳根（Mike Flanagan）执导的最新斯蒂芬·金改编作品《嘉莉》（这将是他的第四部相关作品）定于 10 月 7 日在 Amazon Prime 上映。新预告片在 2026 年圣地亚哥国际动漫展（Comic-Con）上发布，并没有带来太多惊喜。如果你对《嘉莉》的故事有所了解，它涵盖了所有预期的情节要点，尽管它显然已经针对现代背景进行了更新。

According to Deadline, Flanagan’s take on the classic King novel will tackle very modern teen challenges like active shooter drills and bullying on social media. The director apparently invited high schoolers into the writers’ room, using their real-life traumas to inform the script:

据《Deadline》报道，弗拉纳根对这部金氏经典小说的改编将探讨非常现代的青少年挑战，例如校园枪击演习和社交媒体霸凌。据称，这位导演邀请了高中生进入编剧室，利用他们的真实生活创伤来充实剧本：

“They shared shocking one-off situations or constant treatment and microaggressions and things that are not necessarily present themselves as targeted harassment. [We will dive into] the cruelty in today’s world and how the internet has encouraged cruelty, rewards and celebrates and amplifies it. This is awful for adults to deal with, and for teenagers even more so.”

“他们分享了令人震惊的突发事件，或是持续遭受的对待和微歧视，以及那些未必表现为针对性骚扰的事情。[我们将深入探讨]当今世界的残酷，以及互联网如何鼓励、奖励、颂扬并放大这种残酷。这对成年人来说已经难以应对，对青少年而言更是如此。”

Brian De Palma’s iconic 1976 adaptation of *Carrie* is iconic and features an unimpeachable performance from Sissy Spacek. But it’s undeniable that Summer H. Howell, who was 21 at the time of shooting, is much more believable as a high school student than Spacek, who was 26 at the time.

布莱恩·德·帕尔马（Brian De Palma）1976 年执导的《嘉莉》堪称经典，西席·斯派塞克（Sissy Spacek）在片中的表演无可挑剔。但不可否认的是，拍摄时年仅 21 岁的萨默·H·豪威尔（Summer H. Howell）在饰演高中生时，比当年 26 岁的斯派塞克更具说服力。
