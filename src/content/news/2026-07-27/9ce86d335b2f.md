# The Best Motorola Phones, From Razr to Moto G (2026)
# 最佳摩托罗拉手机：从 Razr 到 Moto G (2026)

Motorola phones often play third (or fourth) fiddle to Samsung and Google, but the winds are changing. Motorola is among the fastest-growing mobile companies in the world, and the company’s Razr handsets are the most popular folding smartphones in the US. Motorola has also been experimenting with colorful Android phones and fun textures, making its devices stand out from the sea of boring glass handsets.

摩托罗拉手机在三星和谷歌面前往往只能排在第三（甚至第四）位，但风向正在转变。摩托罗拉是全球增长最快的移动通信公司之一，其 Razr 系列手机是美国最受欢迎的折叠屏智能手机。摩托罗拉还一直在尝试色彩鲜艳的安卓手机和有趣的机身材质，使其设备在千篇一律的玻璃机身手机中脱颖而出。

If you’re an interested buyer, picking the best Motorola phone can be tough because the company launches several models each year. I’ve tested almost all of them, and in this guide, I break down the pros and cons and steer you toward my favorite Moto models—from the Razr Ultra to the Moto G Power 2026.

如果你有购买意向，挑选最适合的摩托罗拉手机可能会很困难，因为该公司每年都会发布多款机型。我测试了几乎所有这些手机，在本指南中，我将分析它们的优缺点，并为你推荐我最喜欢的摩托罗拉机型——从 Razr Ultra 到 Moto G Power 2026。

### What’s WIRED About Motorola Phones?
### 摩托罗拉手机的“亮点” (WIRED)

Motorola phones have a simple Android interface. This means the company hasn’t done much to gunk up the software. It’s simple and easy to use. There’s increasingly a lot of bloatware, but you can easily uninstall almost all of it.

摩托罗拉手机拥有简洁的安卓界面。这意味着该公司没有在软件中添加太多冗余内容。它简单易用。虽然预装软件（bloatware）越来越多，但你几乎可以轻松卸载所有这些应用。

They come with lots of storage. The company stuffs 256 GB as standard into many of its smartphones, even some of the cheaper models. Most Android flagship devices still come with just 128 GB of storage. Motorola is also one of the only phone brands to still include a microSD card slot to expand storage on many of its devices, and the Moto G phones retain the headphone jack.

它们配备了大容量存储空间。该公司在许多智能手机中将 256 GB 作为标配，即使在一些廉价机型中也是如此。大多数安卓旗舰设备仍然只提供 128 GB 的存储空间。摩托罗拉也是为数不多仍在许多设备上保留 microSD 卡槽以扩展存储空间的手机品牌之一，且 Moto G 系列手机还保留了耳机插孔。

There’s broad carrier support. While some Android phone brands have inconsistent carrier support in the US, like Nothing, Motorola phones often work on most networks, if not all. They all work on the big three—T-Mobile, AT&T, and Verizon. Make sure you buy them unlocked.

它们拥有广泛的运营商支持。虽然一些安卓手机品牌（如 Nothing）在美国的运营商支持方面表现不稳定，但摩托罗拉手机通常可以在大多数（如果不是全部）网络上运行。它们都能在三大运营商——T-Mobile、AT&T 和 Verizon 上使用。请确保你购买的是无锁版。

The prices dip quite often. Never buy a Motorola phone at its MSRP. They almost always go on sale a few months (or weeks) after launch, sometimes at steep discounts.

价格经常下调。千万不要按建议零售价购买摩托罗拉手机。它们几乎总是在发布后的几个月（或几周）内打折，有时折扣力度非常大。

### What’s TIRED About Motorola Phones?
### 摩托罗拉手机的“槽点” (TIRED)

Motorola’s software updates are lackluster. The company is slowly improving here—it promises to deliver three Android OS updates and five years of bimonthly security updates for its high-end smartphones—the ultra-expensive Razr Fold gets seven years—but overall, it still lags behind its peers. Most Moto G phones receive only two Android updates along with three years of security updates. Fewer updates mean you miss out on new Android features quickly. Even if a phone is promised updates, they take a long time to arrive.

摩托罗拉的软件更新表现平平。该公司正在缓慢改进——承诺为其高端智能手机提供三次安卓系统更新和五年的双月安全更新（超昂贵的 Razr Fold 可获得七年更新）——但总体而言，它仍落后于同行。大多数 Moto G 手机仅能获得两次安卓更新和三年的安全更新。更新次数少意味着你无法快速体验到新的安卓功能。即使手机承诺了更新，推送时间也往往很长。

The cameras are lackluster. Among the things putting Motorola behind the likes of Samsung, Apple, and Google are the cameras. Moto phones can take fine photos, but the competition eclipses them. Motorola made some improvements with its high-end phones, but it’s still behind.

摄像头表现平平。导致摩托罗拉落后于三星、苹果和谷歌的原因之一就是摄像头。摩托罗拉手机可以拍出不错的照片，但竞争对手的表现更胜一筹。摩托罗拉在其高端手机上做了一些改进，但仍处于落后地位。

There’s no always-on display. Most Android phones have a setting you can toggle on if you want an always-on display that will show a clock on your screen even when the screen is “off.” Motorola has Peek Display, which requires you to move the phone or tap the screen to see anything. It’s not a big deal, but it’s a feature you might miss if you’re coming from another phone.

没有全天候显示（Always-on Display）功能。大多数安卓手机都有一个开关，如果你想要全天候显示，即使在屏幕“关闭”时也能显示时钟。摩托罗拉使用的是“Peek Display”，需要你移动手机或点击屏幕才能看到信息。这虽然不是什么大问题，但如果你是从其他手机换过来的，可能会怀念这个功能。

### The Best Motorola Phone: Motorola Razr Ultra (2026)
### 最佳摩托罗拉手机：Motorola Razr Ultra (2026)

The Razr Ultra is the best reason to get a Motorola phone over the likes of Samsung, Apple, and Google. It’s a statement piece, not just because it’s a compact folding flip phone, but because it’s stylish, whether you opt for the Alcantara finish or the elegant wood.

Razr Ultra 是你选择摩托罗拉而非三星、苹果或谷歌手机的最佳理由。它不仅是一款紧凑的折叠翻盖手机，更是一款彰显个性的产品，无论你选择 Alcantara 材质还是优雅的木质纹理，它都非常时尚。

The spacious 4-inch OLED screen is quite customizable—you can add widgets for glanceable information, so you don’t need to open the phone constantly. Once you do, you’re treated to a large 7-inch AMOLED display that’s sharp, bright, and smooth thanks to the 165-Hz refresh rate.

宽敞的 4 英寸 OLED 外屏可定制性很高——你可以添加小组件以快速查看信息，无需频繁打开手机。当你打开手机后，会看到一块 7 英寸的大型 AMOLED 显示屏，得益于 165Hz 的刷新率，画面清晰、明亮且流畅。
