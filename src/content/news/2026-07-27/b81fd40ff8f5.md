### The Best GoPro You Can Buy: Mission 1 Pro Camera
### 你能买到的最佳 GoPro：Mission 1 Pro 相机

The Mission 1 Pro isn’t just the best GoPro you can buy, it’s the best action camera you can buy. It has the largest sensor found in an action camera, paired with GoPro’s new GP3 processor to churn out up to 8K 60 fps video that’s sharp with great natural color, and looks more cinematic than any other camera in its class.
Mission 1 Pro 不仅仅是你目前能买到的最好的 GoPro，它也是市面上最好的运动相机。它搭载了运动相机中最大的传感器，配合 GoPro 全新的 GP3 处理器，能够输出高达 8K 60fps 的视频。画面清晰、色彩自然，且比同类产品更具电影感。

There are actually three Mission 1 cameras: the entry-level Mission 1, the slightly better Mission 1 Pro, and the forthcoming Mission 1 Pro ILS, which uses interchangeable lenses based on the Micro Four Thirds mount. The main difference between the Mission 1 and the Pro model is the higher framerates available in the Pro.
Mission 1 系列实际上有三款机型：入门级的 Mission 1、性能稍强的 Mission 1 Pro，以及即将推出的 Mission 1 Pro ILS（采用基于 M4/3 卡口的可更换镜头系统）。Mission 1 与 Pro 版的主要区别在于 Pro 版支持更高的帧率。

Despite the significant upgrade in specs, physically, the Mission 1 Pro is nearly the same size as the Hero 13. It’s wider due to the enlarged lens housing, and weighs more, but otherwise, it’s close to the Hero 13 and still very much feels like a GoPro. The larger, raised buttons are easier to use and part of the reason that the waterproof rating is now 20 meters (66 feet) without a case.
尽管规格大幅提升，但在物理尺寸上，Mission 1 Pro 与 Hero 13 几乎相同。由于镜头模组增大，机身略宽且重量增加，但除此之外，它与 Hero 13 非常接近，依然保留了浓厚的 GoPro 手感。更大、更凸起的按键更易于操作，这也是其裸机防水深度提升至 20 米（66 英尺）的原因之一。

### The Best for Most People: GoPro Hero 13 Black
### 大多数人的首选：GoPro Hero 13 Black

The Mission 1 series has stolen some of the Hero 13 Black’s thunder, but it’s still a great camera and the interchangeable lens system gives you some shooting options you won’t find yet in the Mission 1 Pro. The Hero 13 Black is also $200 cheaper and will be plenty of camera for most of us.
Mission 1 系列虽然抢走了 Hero 13 Black 的一些风头，但它依然是一款出色的相机。其可更换镜头系统提供了 Mission 1 Pro 目前尚不具备的拍摄灵活性。此外，Hero 13 Black 的价格便宜了 200 美元，对于大多数用户来说，其性能绰绰有余。

In addition to the standard wide-angle action camera lens, you can also invest in an Ultra Wide ($120), Macro ($110), and Anamorphic ($150) lens. All of these are automatically detected by the Hero 13 the minute you attach them. The camera will automatically adjust settings accordingly, which is a very slick feature that makes switching lenses on the fly a much smoother experience.
除了标准的广角镜头外，你还可以选购超广角（120 美元）、微距（110 美元）和变形镜头（150 美元）。Hero 13 在安装这些镜头的瞬间即可自动识别，并自动调整相应设置。这一非常巧妙的功能让实时更换镜头变得更加顺畅。

Even if you never buy any of the additional lenses, the Hero 13 is a great action camera with improved battery life, a 3-way mounting system, great Log video support, and a good bit more. Video specs top out at 5.3K 30 (8:7 ratio) or 5.3K 60 (16:9 ratio).
即使你不购买任何额外镜头，Hero 13 本身也是一款出色的运动相机，拥有更长的续航、三向安装系统、优秀的 Log 视频支持等诸多亮点。视频规格最高支持 5.3K 30fps（8:7 比例）或 5.3K 60fps（16:9 比例）。
