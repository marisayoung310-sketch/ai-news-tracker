Several questions are especially important when assessing an enterprise AI workspace:
*   **Workflow boundaries:** Which steps can the system assist with, and which actions remain with employees or approved business systems?
*   **Data handling:** What information can be provided to the service, retained, shared or used in downstream workflows?
*   **Access and accountability:** How are user roles, permissions, reviews and ownership handled across teams?
*   **Deployment fit:** Does the product's rollout approach align with the organization's geography, procurement process and existing technology stack?

在评估企业级 AI 工作空间时，以下几个问题尤为重要：
*   **工作流边界：** 系统可以协助哪些步骤，哪些操作仍需由员工或经批准的业务系统完成？
*   **数据处理：** 哪些信息可以提供给该服务，以及这些信息如何被保留、共享或用于下游工作流？
*   **访问与问责：** 团队间的用户角色、权限、审核和所有权是如何管理的？
*   **部署适配性：** 产品的部署方式是否符合组织的地理位置、采购流程和现有技术栈？

These questions are not obstacles to innovation. They are the difference between a useful pilot and an automation project that creates unmanaged risk.

这些问题并非创新的障碍，而是区分“有用的试点项目”与“产生失控风险的自动化项目”的关键。

### Why governance should shape the evaluation
### 为什么治理应主导评估过程

Collaborative AI changes the stakes because outputs may influence group decisions, project plans and business processes. A tool that is used across a team needs more than strong answers. It needs clear operating boundaries. Governance is therefore not a separate compliance exercise to address after deployment. It is part of determining whether a workflow is suitable for AI support in the first place.

协作式 AI 改变了风险格局，因为其产出可能会影响团队决策、项目计划和业务流程。一个在团队中使用的工具需要的不仅仅是强大的回答能力，还需要清晰的操作边界。因此，治理并非部署后才需处理的独立合规工作，而是决定某项工作流是否适合 AI 支持的前提。

For example, an organization might test ChatGPT Work with internal planning, drafting or research workflows before considering processes that involve confidential customer data, regulated information or external publication. The appropriate sequence depends on the controls described in OpenAI's current documentation and on the organization's own policies.

例如，组织可以在考虑涉及机密客户数据、受监管信息或对外发布流程之前，先在内部规划、起草或研究工作流中测试 ChatGPT Work。合适的实施顺序取决于 OpenAI 当前文档中描述的控制措施以及组织自身的政策。

The supplied research confirms that governance is part of the official positioning, but does not provide sufficient detail to assess how any individual control works. A sound evaluation should also distinguish assistance from execution. Generating a project outline, summarizing inputs or proposing options can be valuable even if an employee must validate the result and carry out the final action. Claims that a workplace AI can independently produce full applications or complete complex collaborative work require product-specific evidence, defined permissions and testing in a real operating environment.

现有研究证实了治理是官方定位的一部分，但并未提供足够的细节来评估各项控制措施的具体运作方式。合理的评估还应区分“协助”与“执行”。生成项目大纲、总结输入内容或提出选项即使需要员工验证结果并执行最终操作，也具有价值。关于办公 AI 能独立生成完整应用程序或完成复杂协作工作的说法，需要产品特定的证据、明确的权限定义以及在真实运营环境中的测试。
