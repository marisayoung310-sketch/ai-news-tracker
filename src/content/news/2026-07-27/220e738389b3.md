# Marvel announces Ryan Gosling as Ghost Rider, a new Black Panther, and more
# 漫威宣布瑞恩·高斯林将饰演“恶灵骑士”，并公布新任“黑豹”人选等重磅消息

While perhaps less dramatic than in years past, Marvel nonetheless had some big reveals lined up for Comic-Con. The highest profile was certainly the announcement that Ryan Gosling would be joining the MCU as Ghost Rider, with the Shawn Levy-directed (Deadpool & Wolverine) standalone feature set to drop sometime in 2028.

尽管今年的漫威在圣迭戈国际动漫展（Comic-Con）上的表现或许不如往年那般戏剧化，但依然带来了一些重磅消息。其中最受瞩目的无疑是瑞恩·高斯林（Ryan Gosling）将加入漫威电影宇宙（MCU）饰演“恶灵骑士”（Ghost Rider），这部由肖恩·利维（《死侍与金刚狼》导演）执导的独立电影预计将于2028年上映。

The studio also brought out Ryan Coogler, who officially confirmed Black Panther III is slated for release on December 15th, 2028. He also introduced David Jonsson (The Long Walk, Alien Romulus) as T’Chala’s son and the new Black Panther in the franchise.

漫威影业还邀请了导演瑞恩·库格勒（Ryan Coogler）登台，他正式确认《黑豹3》定档于2028年12月15日上映。此外，他还介绍了大卫·琼森（David Jonsson，《漫漫长路》、《异形：夺命舰》演员）将饰演特查拉（T’Chala）的儿子，并成为该系列电影中的新任“黑豹”。

Marvel also showed off a new Avengers: Doomsday trailer and seemed to reveal yet another cast member. Ryan Reynolds made an appearance in the crowd as Deadpool and stood up to ask the film’s directors, Joe and Anthony Russo, if there was room for him in the movie. They said the film was basically done, but they might be able to work something out. It was also confirmed that Hayley Atwell is returning as Peggy Carter.

漫威还展示了《复仇者联盟：末日》（Avengers: Doomsday）的最新预告片，并似乎揭晓了另一位演员阵容。瑞恩·雷诺兹（Ryan Reynolds）以“死侍”的装扮现身观众席，并起身询问该片导演乔·罗素和安东尼·罗素（Joe and Anthony Russo），电影中是否还有他的戏份。导演们表示电影基本已经制作完成，但或许可以再商量一下。此外，官方也确认海莉·阿特维尔（Hayley Atwell）将回归饰演佩吉·卡特（Peggy Carter）。
